title: No fire
---

None of your ten planets sit in Aries, Leo, or Sagittarius. Fire is the element of spontaneous confidence, enthusiasm, and the willingness to act before the plan is finished, and without it in your chart you may not feel that spark arrive on its own. Motivation tends to come from duty, care, or reason rather than from simple appetite for the next thing.

The absence often works as a hunger. You may be drawn to bold, warm, dramatic people who seem to light up a room without effort, or to work that demands courage and visibility. Some people with no fire overcompensate, pushing themselves into big gestures that feel borrowed and then crashing afterward.

Your steadiness is real; fire is something you can kindle rather than own. Notice what reliably warms you up — movement, sunlight, a certain friend, a creative risk — and build one of those into the start of your week on purpose.
