title: No water
---

None of your ten planets sit in Cancer, Scorpio, or Pisces. Water is the element of emotional attunement, intimacy, and the quiet reading of moods that are never spoken aloud. Without it in your chart, you may meet life through action, ideas, or practical sense first, and feelings can arrive late, show up in the body, or seem slightly foreign when they do.

This often works as a hunger rather than an absence. You may be drawn to emotionally expressive people, to music or art that moves you, or to caring work that lets you feel through others. Some people with no water overcompensate, becoming intensely sentimental in one area or feeling flooded when emotion finally breaks through.

Your clarity under pressure is a gift others lean on. To keep your inner tide moving, give feelings a regular outlet: a journal entry, a long bath, an honest check-in with someone close. Ask yourself once a day what you are feeling, and wait for an answer.
