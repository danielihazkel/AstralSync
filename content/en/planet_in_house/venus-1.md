title: Venus in the 1st House
---

Charm reaches the door before you knock. Venus in the 1st wears grace on the surface — people warm to you quickly, forgive you easily, and remember your presence as pleasant even when they forget your points. Style and likability are part of how you move through the world, not accessories to it.

That welcome is real currency: doors open on first impressions, and you soften rooms just by entering them. The friction is that being liked can quietly become the job — you sand your edges smooth, agree in the room and resent in the car, or let the mirror's mood decide your own.

Once a day, say the slightly less charming true thing, and count how few doors actually close.
