title: Neptune in the 8th House
---

Intimacy, for you, is dissolution. Where others guard the deep end, you swim toward it: merging emotionally, sexually, even psychically with the people you trust. You sense undercurrents in a room, sit with the dying and the grieving without flinching, and find crisis strangely clarifying. This is a genuine depth few people can offer.

The hazards are financial and energetic. Shared money — loans, inheritances, joint accounts, a partner's debts — tends to blur exactly where it should be explicit, and your openness can leave you carrying someone else's darkness home. Total merging can also quietly erase your own edges.

Keep the mystical and the material in separate drawers. Put every shared financial arrangement in writing, and after deep encounters, do something ordinary and physical to return to yourself.
