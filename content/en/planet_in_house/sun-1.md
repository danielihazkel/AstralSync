title: Sun in the 1st House
---

You lead with yourself. Identity is not something you discover in private and then reveal — it forms in real time, in front of people, through how you move, speak, and carry your body. First impressions of you tend to be accurate because there is little gap between who you are and what you show.

The strength here is presence: rooms register your arrival, and self-direction comes naturally. The friction is that everything feels personal. Feedback on your work can land as a verdict on your existence, and you may crowd out quieter people without meaning to.

Vitality tracks your physical state closely, so neglecting the body dims the whole chart. Practice asking one genuine question before offering your own take, and notice how much larger the room becomes.
