title: Pluto in the 7th House
---

Partnership is your crucible. You are not built for lukewarm alliances: the people you bind yourself to — spouses, business partners, even declared rivals — tend to be powerful, magnetic, and catalytic, and your closest relationships change you at the studs. That is the gift. Few placements can match your loyalty once trust is genuinely earned, or your willingness to go through fire beside someone.

The battleground is control. Power in your partnerships rarely sits level: one of you holds the money, the decisions, or the emotional trigger, and jealousy, ultimatums, and scorched-earth endings are the classic failure modes. You may also meet your own disowned intensity wearing your partner's face.

In your next conflict, ask one question first: what am I afraid of losing? Name that instead of maneuvering around it.
