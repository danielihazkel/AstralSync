title: Moon in the 10th House
---

Your inner weather is public record. Career is not just what you do — it is where your feelings live, so a good review can carry you for a week and a stalled project can follow you home like fog. Colleagues read you easily, and your reputation includes your moods whether you like it or not.

The strength is authentic authority. People trust leaders who visibly care, and your instinct for what the public needs — timing, tone, appetite — is a professional asset. Nurture scales in your hands: you build workplaces that feel like functioning families. The friction is the barometer problem: when self-worth pegs to public standing, every reorg becomes an identity event, and rest starts to feel like falling behind.

Keep one emotional anchor that has nothing to do with achievement, and check weekly that it is still getting fed.
