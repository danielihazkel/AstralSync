title: Uranus in the 2nd House
---

Your money refuses a salary's shape. Uranus in the 2nd House tends to make income arrive in surges, side doors, and inventions — windfalls, dry spells, revenue streams nobody around you quite understands. The strength is genuine financial originality: you can earn from things that did not exist five years ago, and your self-worth stays refreshingly detached from conventional status symbols.

The friction is volatility. Feast-or-famine cycles punish anyone depending on you for steadiness, and boredom can tempt you to blow up a perfectly working arrangement just to feel the fresh air of risk again.

Automate one boring anchor — a fixed transfer to savings on every payment, however irregular — so the experiments rest on a floor instead of a tightrope.
