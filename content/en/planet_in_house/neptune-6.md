title: Neptune in the 6th House
---

You bring devotion to daily work. Whatever the job title says, you treat tasks as offerings — service, craft, care — and colleagues quickly learn you are the one who notices when someone is struggling. Work that helps, heals, or beautifies suits you far better than work that merely pays.

The friction lives in boundaries and the body. You say yes past your capacity, absorb the office mood, and cover for people until resentment leaks out sideways. Health signals tend to be diffuse — tiredness, sensitivities, symptoms that resist tidy diagnosis — and often track your stress more honestly than your calendar does.

Give your compassion a container. Fixed hours, a real lunch break, and a written list of what is actually your job will protect the service you love giving.
