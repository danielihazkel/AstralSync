title: Pluto in the 1st House
---

You enter rooms like weather. Even silent, you register as intensity — people either lean in, fascinated, or brace without knowing why. Behind that presence sits real power: you survive what flattens others, reinvent yourself in eras, and your gaze tends to find what people are hiding.

The friction is that your armor reads as threat. Control is your default self-protection — of your image, your privacy, your reactions — and others may experience your composure as domination or your directness as attack. You can spend years being handled carefully by people you only wanted to know.

Practice deliberate transparency in small doses: say what you actually feel before anyone has to excavate it. Notice, too, the moments you grip a conversation — then experiment with letting one go.
