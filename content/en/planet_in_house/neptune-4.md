title: Neptune in the 4th House
---

Home, for you, is less an address than an atmosphere. You have a rare gift for making any space feel like sanctuary — candles, music, an open door for the lost — and people exhale the moment they cross your threshold. Somewhere underneath runs a longing for an ideal home you may never quite locate on a map.

The fog gathers around origins. Family history tends toward myth: a parent who was hard to see clearly, stories that shift with each telling, sacrifices nobody names. You may keep chasing the family you wished you had, or dissolve your boundaries to keep peace under your own roof.

Get curious about the record. Ask relatives for specifics, keep the documents, and notice when nostalgia is editing the past into something it wasn't.
