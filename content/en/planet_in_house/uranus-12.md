title: Uranus in the 12th House
---

Your rebellions happen backstage. Uranus in the 12th House puts the lightning in the unconscious: breakthroughs arrive in dreams, in the shower, in solitude — sudden knowings you cannot show your work for, but which keep proving right. You also have a gift for freeing others from invisible prisons — old grief, institutional ruts — often without ever advertising your role.

The friction is what stays hidden from you. Suppressed restlessness leaks out as anxiety, insomnia, or a life quietly detonated "out of nowhere," and genuine intuition can be hard to tell from static.

Give the underground a scheduled exit — journaling, long walks, deliberate idleness — so insight can surface on purpose instead of by ambush.
