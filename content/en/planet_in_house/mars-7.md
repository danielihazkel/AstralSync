title: Mars in the 7th House
---

Other people wake up your fight. With Mars in the 7th you are drawn to strong-willed partners — in love and in business — and your own assertiveness often switches on only when someone stands across from you. Alone you may drift; opposed, you focus instantly.

The gift is that you do conflict in relationship rather than avoiding it, which keeps your closest bonds honest and alive. The hazard is outsourcing your anger: choosing fiery partners and then blaming them for the heat, or turning every negotiation into a duel where someone must lose.

Open rivals teach you more than admirers do, and the pattern in who provokes you is a map of what you disown. Next time a partner ignites you, ask which desire of your own you have been too polite to claim.
