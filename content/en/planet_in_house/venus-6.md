title: Venus in the 6th House
---

Your love language is the fixed hinge and the packed lunch. Venus in the 6th expresses affection through service — you show devotion by making someone's day run better, and you take real pleasure in craft: the tidy spreadsheet, well-kept tools, a routine polished until it shines. Workplaces like having you around; you make them kinder.

The strength is love people can verify — your care shows up in practical, daily, countable ways. The friction is invisibility: quiet service gets taken for granted, so you keep doing more while resenting that nobody names it, and being needed can start to feel identical to being loved.

Ask directly, this week, for the appreciation you have been earning silently.
