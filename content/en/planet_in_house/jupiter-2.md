title: Jupiter in the 2nd House
---

Money finds you more easily than it finds most people — and leaves just as fluently. Jupiter in the 2nd gives a talent for earning through enthusiasm: opportunities appear, raises materialize, and you rarely doubt that more is coming. That faith is usually vindicated, which is exactly the trap.

Your generosity is real wealth: you pick up checks, fund friends, and give without keeping score, and people remember. The friction is the outflow matching the inflow. Budgets feel like pessimism to you, luxury upgrades feel like necessities, and a lucky streak can convince you that the safety net is optional.

Self-worth here is naturally sturdy — you know your value without needing the numbers to prove it. Automate savings before the money reaches your hands, so your optimism spends what is left instead of what was needed.
