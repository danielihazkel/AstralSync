title: Venus in the 4th House
---

Home is where your beauty lives. Venus in the 4th builds sanctuaries — the good lamp, the full table, the room arranged so people exhale when they enter it. Your affection is domestic and loyal: you love by hosting, feeding, and keeping the family peace, and your private life is far warmer than strangers would guess.

The strength is roots. People feel safe around you, and the home you make restores everyone in it, including you. The friction is retreat — comfort this good can argue against every party, risk, and difficult conversation, and keeping the peace can mean burying a live conflict under a nice tablecloth.

Invite the hard conversation into the comfortable room; the setting you built is strong enough to hold it.
