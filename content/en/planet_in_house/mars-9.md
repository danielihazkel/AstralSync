title: Mars in the 9th House
---

Beliefs are worth fighting for — that sentence could be your signature. Mars in the 9th turns conviction into fuel: you defend your worldview vigorously, chase education like a competition, and travel the way some people enlist, seeking distance and difficulty on purpose.

The strength is crusading stamina. When a cause or a course of study captures you, you will out-argue, out-read, and outlast nearly anyone, and your enthusiasm genuinely recruits people. The friction is the pulpit: debates about religion, politics, or philosophy can turn scorched-earth, and you may mistake winning the argument for changing the mind.

Foreign places and unfamiliar frameworks are where your certainty gets useful resistance. Once a month, argue the strongest version of a position you oppose — out loud, fairly — and notice what survives.
