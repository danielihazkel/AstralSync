title: Saturn in the 11th House
---

You count friends on one hand, and every finger is load-bearing. Saturn in the 11th House trades wide social circles for few, durable alliances: friendships that survive decades, groups you serve as the responsible one, causes you keep showing up for long after the enthusiasts drift off. If a community around you works, odds are you built or maintain part of its scaffolding.

The friction is standing at the edge of the party. Groups can feel like exams, belonging like a status you have not yet earned, and hope for the future like something other people are issued at birth.

When you next feel outside the circle, contribute something concrete — you bond through usefulness, so give the group a job you do well.
