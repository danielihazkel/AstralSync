title: Sun in the 10th House
---

You are wired for the summit. Career, reputation, and public responsibility are not vanity projects for you — they are the arena where your character gets forged and tested. You take the long view, accept visibility as the cost of impact, and tend to be given authority earlier than your peers because you visibly want to be accountable.

The strength is ambition with structure: you can hold a decade-long aim without flinching. The friction is that the role can eat the person. Titles start to feel like skin, failure feels public even when it isn't, and family or private joy can become items on a delayed to-do list. You may also inherit an ambition — often a parent's — and run it for years before checking whose it was.

Write your own definition of "enough," in numbers, and revisit it every birthday.
