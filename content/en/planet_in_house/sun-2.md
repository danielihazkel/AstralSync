title: Sun in the 2nd House
---

Your sense of self is built, not borrowed. You shine by making things tangible — earning, saving, crafting, owning outright. Where others chase visibility, you chase solidity, and people learn to trust you because your word behaves like collateral.

The gift is durable self-worth: you know what you value and you rarely rent your opinions. The hazard is that the ledger becomes the mirror. A lean season can read as a personal failure, and you may cling to possessions, positions, or habits long after they have stopped serving you, simply because letting go feels like shrinking.

Once a month, name one thing you would still respect about yourself if your income vanished — and one thing you are keeping only because releasing it feels like loss.
