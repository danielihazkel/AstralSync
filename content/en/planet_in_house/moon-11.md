title: Moon in the 11th House
---

Friendship is your safety net, woven deliberately. Where others refuel with family or solitude, you refuel in the group text, the weekly gathering, the shared project with people who get it. Belonging to a circle — and to a cause pointed at the future — is not social garnish for you; it is emotional load-bearing wall.

The strength is that you make community feel like kin. You remember birthdays and burdens, fold newcomers in, and your hope for what could be steadies everyone in the room. The friction is diffusion: needing the whole network can keep every single bond conveniently shallow, and the group's mood can override your own until you cannot tell whether you are sad or the movement is.

When you feel unmoored, resist the urge to add more people; instead, take one existing friendship a level deeper this month.
