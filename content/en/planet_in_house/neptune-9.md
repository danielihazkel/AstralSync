title: Neptune in the 9th House
---

You are built for pilgrimage. Belief is not an argument you win but an ocean you enter, and you are drawn to whatever promises transcendence — distant coastlines, mysticism, philosophies that dissolve the ordinary. Your faith is genuine and flexible, and it lets you find meaning in places more rigid minds walk straight past.

The undertow is credulity. Teachers can become gurus, systems can become answers-to-everything, and a beautiful worldview can excuse you from checking whether it is true. Travel plans and long-term visions share the same fuzziness: gorgeous in outline, vague in logistics.

Keep the seeking, add a filter. Test any teaching against how its loudest advocates actually live, and before the next leap of faith, ask what evidence would change your mind.
