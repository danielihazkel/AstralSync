title: Neptune in the 7th House
---

You fall in love with potential. In partnership you offer something rare: devotion without a ledger, empathy that meets people exactly where they are, and faith in who your partner could become. At best this is soul-level companionship — two people dissolving the usual armor.

At worst, you are dating a dream with a person standing behind it. You may idealize partners until the first crack appears, then feel betrayed by facts that were visible all along. Rescuing is the other classic pattern: choosing people who need saving, so the relationship becomes a mission instead of a meeting of equals. Even rivals get the soft-focus treatment, making conflict hard to see coming.

Before committing to anything major, list what this person actually does, not what they promise. Love the evidence.
