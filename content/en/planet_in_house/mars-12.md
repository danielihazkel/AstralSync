title: Mars in the 12th House
---

Your anger works the night shift. With Mars in the 12th, drive and aggression operate below the waterline: you rarely feel the flash others describe, yet the energy is there — surfacing as insomnia, vague guilt, sudden exhaustion, or campaigns of avoidance that look like calm.

The hidden strength is remarkable: stamina for solitary work, an instinct for helping the overlooked, and courage that appears precisely in crises when self-consciousness dissolves. The cost is leakage. Unclaimed anger recruits proxies — you may attract people who act out the temper you deny, or sabotage your own plans right before they succeed.

Behind-the-scenes arenas — studios, hospitals, retreats, long solo projects — are where your force flows cleanest. Keep a written log of what irritated you each day; naming the small angers keeps them from pooling into the invisible kind.
