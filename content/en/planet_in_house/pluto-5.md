title: Pluto in the 5th House
---

You do not dabble. Whatever you love — a craft, a person, a child, a game — you love with your whole engine, and what you make carries a charge audiences can feel from the back row. Your creativity works like alchemy: pain goes in, something potent comes out. Romance arrives the same way, as obsession, transformation, all or nothing.

The friction is stakes. When every poem is your soul and every date is destiny, exposure feels like mortal risk, so you may hide your best work or turn love affairs into power struggles over who wants whom more. Play, the whole point of this house, quietly dies under that pressure.

Make something small and unserious every week — and show it before it is ready. Lower stakes are how the volcano learns to sing.
