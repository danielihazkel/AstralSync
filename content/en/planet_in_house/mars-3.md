title: Mars in the 3rd House
---

Words are your weapons of choice. Mars in the 3rd gives a fast, combative mind: you learn by arguing, sharpen ideas by testing them out loud, and can dismantle a weak claim before its owner finishes the sentence. Debate feels less like conflict to you than like exercise.

That verbal speed makes you a formidable advocate and a quick study — new skills, new routes, new tools, all absorbed on the move. The cost is friendly fire. Sarcasm lands harder than you intend, emails written in heat read as attacks, and siblings or neighbors often know your temper better than anyone.

Your daily environment absorbs your static: traffic, errands, small talk. Draft the sharp message, then wait one hour before sending — the point you keep is worth more than the point you score.
