title: Pluto in the 8th House
---

Pluto sits in its own territory here, and you feel it as depth without a floor. Crisis is strangely native ground: when lives fall apart — a death, a divorce, a financial collapse — you become calm, capable, and honest while everyone else is still in shock. Intimacy, psychology, and the machinery of shared money hold no terror for you; you would rather have one devastatingly real conversation than a year of pleasantries.

The friction is trust and its enforcement. You may test people covertly, hold vetoes over joint resources, or keep an exit strategy from every merger you enter. Betrayal, once experienced, can calcify into a worldview.

Let one person in a half-step further than feels safe, and say so out loud. Depth shared is power; depth guarded is just a bunker.
