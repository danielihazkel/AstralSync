title: Sun in the 11th House
---

You come into focus inside a crowd with a purpose. Friendship networks, teams, movements, and long-range causes are where your identity finds traction — you would rather build something that outlives you with fifty allies than win alone. People instinctively hand you the connector role: you see who belongs together and what the group could become.

The strength is genuine social vision and loyalty that scales beyond one-on-one bonds. The friction is dilution. Belonging can shade into conformity, and you may sand down your sharpest opinions to keep the group comfortable, or scatter your energy across so many circles and causes that none receives your best work.

The future can also become a hiding place from the present. Choose the one cause and the three friendships that matter most, and give them disproportionate time this year.
