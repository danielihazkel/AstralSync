title: Uranus in the 7th House
---

You want a partner and a co-conspirator, not a warden. Uranus in the 7th House writes freedom into every close bond: unusual partners, unconventional arrangements, relationships that run on space and honesty rather than script. The strength is that nothing about your commitments is automatic — whoever stays, stays by ongoing choice, which is its own kind of romance.

The friction is the exit reflex. Closeness can trip an alarm that reads intimacy as capture, producing sudden coolings or abrupt endings that blindside partners who thought everything was fine.

Say the restlessness out loud early — "I need a weekend to myself" costs far less than the disappearing act it prevents.
