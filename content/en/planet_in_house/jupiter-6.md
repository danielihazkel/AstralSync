title: Jupiter in the 6th House
---

Meaning, for you, hides inside the workday. Jupiter in the 6th finds abundance in craft, service, and routine: you genuinely enjoy being useful, your workplaces tend to treat you well, and helpers, mentors, and good colleagues appear whenever your load gets heavy.

The strength is morale — you make ordinary work feel purposeful, and teams function better with your generosity in the room. The friction is volunteering yourself into the ground. You say yes to every task because each one seems meaningful, inflate small jobs into philosophies, and can treat rest as a moral failure. Health follows the same expansive logic: vitality is strong, but excess — rich food, overwork, skipped checkups on faith — is the pattern to watch.

Your best service is sustainable service. Cap your commitments at the number you can name from memory, and let one request this week find a different volunteer.
