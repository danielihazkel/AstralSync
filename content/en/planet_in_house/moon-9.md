title: Moon in the 9th House
---

Meaning is your comfort food. When you are hurting, you reach for the big frame — a book, a pilgrimage, a philosophy that explains the pain — and distance itself is medicine: a long drive or a foreign city can reset your mood the way sleep resets others. You feel at home in places you were not born.

The strength is emotional resilience through perspective. You genuinely recover by learning, and your enthusiasm for ideas warms whole rooms. The friction is escape wearing wisdom's coat. The next country, course, or belief system can become a way to leave feelings unfelt, and moods can swing with your convictions — crisis of faith and crisis of heart arriving as the same storm.

When restlessness hits, ask whether you need a wider horizon or just an honest hour with what you are avoiding; book the trip only after answering.
