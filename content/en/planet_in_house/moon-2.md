title: Moon in the 2nd House
---

Security has a number and a texture for you. A funded account, a stocked pantry, familiar objects in their places — these are not materialism, they are how your nervous system settles. When resources feel stable you are generous and unhurried; when they wobble, your mood wobbles with them, sometimes before you consciously notice the bank balance.

The strength is a reliable instinct for value: you sense what things, people, and hours are actually worth, and you build comfort wherever you land. The friction is emotional spending in both directions — soothing bad days with purchases, or white-knuckle hoarding that starves the present to feed an imagined famine.

Your self-worth can also ride the same elevator as your net worth. When anxiety spikes, check whether the real deficit is money or reassurance, and address the one that is actually empty.
