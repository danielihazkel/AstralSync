title: Mars in the 6th House
---

You attack the to-do list like it insulted you. Mars in the 6th pours raw drive into work, craft, and routine: you outpace colleagues, fix what others tolerate, and treat competence as a moral position. Bodies with this placement usually need hard exercise the way others need sleep.

The strength is relentless productivity and genuine skill, honed by repetition most people cannot sustain. The friction is irritation at anyone slower or sloppier — coworkers feel graded — and a tendency to work through warning signs until your body files a formal complaint through inflammation, tension, or injury.

Service is where this fire matures: effort spent on someone else's problem burns cleaner than effort spent proving yourself. Schedule recovery as strictly as you schedule output, and let one imperfect thing stay imperfect this week.
