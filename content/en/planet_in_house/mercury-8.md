title: Mercury in the 8th House
---

Your mind is drawn to what others avoid. Mercury in the 8th reads the fine print — the will, the prenup, the motive underneath the polite sentence. Small talk starves you; you want to know what people fear, owe, and hide, and they often tell you, because you handle secrets like a professional.

That depth makes you a formidable researcher, analyst, or confidant for anything buried — debt, grief, desire, power. The friction is suspicion: a mind trained on hidden motives will find them where none exist, and your own thoughts stay so guarded that even intimates can feel shut out of your interior.

Once a week, say one guarded thought aloud to someone safe — disclosure is the toll your depth owes.
