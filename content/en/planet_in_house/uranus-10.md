title: Uranus in the 10th House
---

Your résumé refuses a straight line. Uranus in the 10th House makes the career zigzag structural: sudden pivots, self-invented job titles, an allergy to bosses that flares whenever authority stops making sense. The strength is that your reputation eventually becomes the innovation itself — you are known precisely for doing the thing differently, and fields in upheaval pay well for exactly that.

The friction is the burned bridge and the restart penalty. Quitting on principle feels clean in the moment but compounds over a career; each dramatic exit resets capital you spent years building.

When the urge to detonate a role arrives, negotiate for autonomy first — you usually want freedom inside the structure more than freedom from it.
