title: Venus in the 7th House
---

Partnership is your natural habitat. Venus in the 7th sits in its own house: you are built for the duet — attentive, fair, gifted at making the other person feel like the most interesting one in the room. Contracts, collaborations, and marriages tend to go well for you because you genuinely enjoy meeting someone halfway.

The strength is harmony that is not faked; people relax into agreements with you. The friction is over-accommodation: you can bend so gracefully toward a partner's preferences that your own go undeclared for years, and being unpartnered can feel like being unfinished rather than simply solo.

Practice stating a preference before asking for theirs — "I'd like Thai tonight" is a complete sentence.
