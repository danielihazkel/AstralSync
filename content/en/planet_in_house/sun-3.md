title: Sun in the 3rd House
---

Words are your arena. You come alive in conversation, in the quick trade of questions and answers, in the daily circuit of neighbors, siblings, messages, and errands. Your identity forms through articulation: you often do not know what you think until you have said it out loud or written it down.

The strength is genuine curiosity paired with the ability to make ideas portable — you translate, summarize, and connect people who would never otherwise meet. The friction is depth. Skimming ten subjects feels more vital than mastering one, and restlessness can turn learning into channel-surfing.

You may also talk past the point where listening would serve you better. Pick one topic this season and stay with it past the point of boredom; that is where your voice acquires weight.
