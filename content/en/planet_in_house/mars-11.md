title: Mars in the 11th House
---

Groups move faster with you in them. Mars in the 11th makes you the friend who books the trip, the member who forces the vote, the volunteer who turns a cause's talk into a campaign. Your fire is social: shared goals ignite you more reliably than personal ones.

That makes you a genuine catalyst — teams, scenes, and movements get their momentum from people wired like you. The friction is that comrades can become opponents overnight. You compete with friends without admitting it, push agendas through groups that wanted consensus, and may burn alliances over strategy disputes that outsiders can barely see.

Your vision of the future is vivid enough to fight for, which is rare. Before your next group push, recruit one dissenter as a co-planner instead of steamrolling them — allies you argued with hold longest.
