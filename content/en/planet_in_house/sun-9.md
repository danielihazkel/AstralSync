title: Sun in the 9th House
---

Your identity is a horizon, not an address. Travel, study, philosophy, publishing, faith — anything that widens the frame makes you feel most alive. You are built to leave the home valley, and the question "what does it all mean?" is not idle for you; it is the pulse.

The strength is vision. You see patterns others miss, teach naturally, and give people permission to think bigger. The friction is distance from the ground. Grand meaning can excuse you from Tuesday's obligations, and conviction can harden into preaching — you may notice you enjoy being right more than being understood. The next trip or degree can also function as an escape hatch from a life that needs tending.

When you catch yourself explaining a belief, stop and ask the other person what they have seen; let one conversation change your mind this month.
