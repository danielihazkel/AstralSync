title: Mercury in the 2nd House
---

Your mind works best with something to count. Mercury in the 2nd concentrates thought on value — budgets, pricing, what things are worth and whether they are worth it to you. You learn by asking what an idea is *for*, and you can turn words themselves into income: writing, negotiating, selling, advising.

The gift is pragmatism; abstractions have to pay rent before you will house them, which keeps you honest and your plans solvent. The catch is that self-worth can hitch itself to being useful, so a rejected idea feels like a personal devaluation — and you may dismiss knowledge that does not obviously earn its keep.

Watch the moment you ask "but what's the point?" — sometimes the pointless question is the one that pays off later.
