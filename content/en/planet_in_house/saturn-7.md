title: Saturn in the 7th House
---

You read the fine print on love. Saturn in the 7th House treats partnership as a contract in the noblest sense — vows meant literally, loyalty as policy, commitment arriving late but built to weather decades. Partners often skew older, steadier, or more serious than you, and your relationships tend to improve with time rather than burn out.

The friction is the wall before the vow. Fear of choosing wrongly can keep you auditing candidates for years, or turn a living relationship into a permanent performance review. Rivals and critics, too, get taken more seriously here than they deserve.

Notice when you are grading a partner instead of meeting them, and say one appreciative thing before the next correction.
