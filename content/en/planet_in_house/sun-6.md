title: Sun in the 6th House
---

You shine in the doing, not the announcing. Craft, routine, and useful work are where your identity takes shape: the well-run system, the skill honed over years, the colleague who actually fixes the thing. Being genuinely good at something matters more to you than being seen as good at it.

That humility is a strength — your competence compounds while flashier people plateau. The friction is self-erasure. Service can slide into servitude, and you may hide inside busyness, perfecting details because finishing and being judged feels riskier. Your body tends to file the complaints your mouth won't; recurring symptoms often track unspoken resentment.

Watch for the moment you volunteer for a task nobody asked you to do, and ask whether you are helping or hiding.
