title: Pluto in the 6th House
---

Work is where your intensity concentrates. You approach craft the way surgeons approach surgery: total focus, zero tolerance for sloppiness, mastery or nothing. Broken systems seem to find you, and you genuinely can rebuild them — a chaotic office, a failing process, a body that needs an overhaul. Colleagues learn that if it must be done right, it goes to you.

The compulsion is the catch. Routines can harden into rituals you cannot skip, control over your schedule can become control over coworkers, and you tend to run yourself past empty because stopping feels like weakness. Your body then negotiates in the only language you respect: crisis.

Treat rest as part of the craft, not a betrayal of it. Schedule recovery like a deadline, and watch for the week you cancel it twice.
