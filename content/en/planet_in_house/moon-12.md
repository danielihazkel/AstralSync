title: Moon in the 12th House
---

Your feelings live one room away from you. They arrive as atmosphere first — a heaviness, a dream, a sudden need to be alone — and only later, sometimes much later, as nameable emotion. Solitude is not a preference; it is where you finally hear yourself. Retreats, quiet mornings, and time near water or sleep restore you like food.

The strength is profound empathy: you register what others feel before they say it, and your compassion extends to people the world has filed away — the sick, the confined, the forgotten. The friction is the lag and the leak. You may not know you are hurt until you are exhausted, and you absorb strangers' moods so readily that crowds cost you double.

Needs you never voice cannot be met. Keep a nightly two-line log of what you actually felt today, and tell one person one item from it each week.
