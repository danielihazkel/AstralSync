title: Jupiter in the 4th House
---

Somewhere behind you stands a big table with room for one more chair. Jupiter in the 4th expands the private life: homes that grow, families that absorb strays, holidays that run loud and long. Whatever your childhood was, you carry a baseline belief that home should be abundant — and you build toward it.

That instinct makes you a natural host and anchor; people heal in the spaces you keep, and property often works out in your favor. The friction is overextension at the root: the bigger mortgage than needed, the household that swells until privacy vanishes, the family generosity that quietly becomes obligation.

Your inner foundation is genuinely optimistic, which steadies everyone around you in hard seasons. Once a year, audit what your home actually needs versus what it accumulated — abundance you curate feels rich; abundance you ignore feels like clutter.
