title: Neptune in the 12th House
---

Neptune sits in its own territory here, and the effect is oceanic. Your inner life is vast — dreams that feel like dispatches, compassion for people you have never met, an instinct for silence, prayer, or art made with the door closed. Solitude does not deplete you; it is where you refuel and where your best material comes from.

The friction is that the same door lets things leak. Moods arrive without labels, old sorrows surface without appointments, and escapism — sleep, screens, substances, fantasy — can masquerade as the retreat you legitimately need. What stays unexamined in you tends to run the show from backstage.

Schedule your solitude instead of falling into it. Twenty deliberate minutes of quiet, journaling, or meditation daily keeps the ocean a resource rather than an undertow.
