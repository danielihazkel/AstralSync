title: Pluto in the 11th House
---

Friendship carries voltage for you. You are not built for fifty acquaintances; you bond ferociously with a few, and the groups you join tend to be ones with a mission — movements, guilds, circles trying to change something real. Within them you are the strategist who sees the actual power map, and your loyalty, once given, is close to unbreakable.

The shadow is the purge. Betrayal by a friend cuts you deeper than romance gone wrong, and your instinct is exile: whole chapters of your social life ended with a closed door. Inside groups, you may maneuver for influence while insisting you have none, or quietly resent leaders you could simply challenge openly.

When a friendship strains, try repair before removal — one direct conversation. Not everyone who disappoints you is a traitor.
