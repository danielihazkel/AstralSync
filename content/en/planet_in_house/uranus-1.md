title: Uranus in the 1st House
---

You are your own experiment. Uranus in the 1st House puts the rebel in the front window: your look, manner, or timing marks you as different before you say a word, and periodic self-reinventions are not phases — they are the operating system. The strength is authenticity with voltage: you give others permission to be strange, and you spot dead conventions a decade before they officially die.

The friction is unpredictability as a first impression. People who need steadiness may find you hard to plan around, and contrariness can quietly hijack your choices — *different from* becomes a compass that only points away.

Before your next reinvention, check whether you are moving toward something or merely against something.
