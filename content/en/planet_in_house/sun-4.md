title: Sun in the 4th House
---

Your center of gravity sits at home. While the world measures achievement in public, your real work happens in private: building a household, tending family bonds, making a physical place that feels unmistakably yours. Roots are not background for you — they are the project.

This gives you rare depth and staying power; people near you feel anchored, and you can turn any dwelling into a stronghold. The trade-off is visibility. Your best efforts happen where no one applauds, and you may resent that colleagues with half your substance collect twice your credit. Family history also runs strong in you, for better and worse — inherited patterns can pass as personality.

Ask yourself which of your daily instincts is truly yours and which is a parent's echo, and keep only the ones you would choose.
