title: Mercury in the 10th House
---

Your words are part of your résumé. Mercury in the 10th makes communication a public act — you become known for what you say and how you say it, and careers built on writing, strategy, analysis, or teaching fit you like tailored clothes. People in authority tend to notice your ideas early.

The strength is professional clarity: you can brief a room, name the plan in one sentence, and be quoted accurately afterward. The friction is self-censorship — when every sentence feels ranked, you may polish opinions until they are safe and hollow, or feel exposed when an offhand remark travels further than you intended.

Keep one channel — a journal or a trusted friend — where the unpolished version gets said first.
