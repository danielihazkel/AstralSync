title: Pluto in the 10th House
---

Ambition runs deep and quiet in you. You are drawn to positions of real consequence — leadership, expertise, influence over outcomes — and you have the stamina to rebuild a career from ash, sometimes more than once. Institutions sense it: you get handed the crises, the turnarounds, the things nobody else can hold.

Authority is also where your oldest battles replay. Bosses can become adversaries, scrutiny can feel like attack, and the fear of public downfall may push you toward either overcontrol or self-sabotage right at the threshold of success. Power gathered in shadow — favors, leverage, information — has a way of detonating in this house.

Use power in daylight: say plainly what you want, who decided, and why. A reputation for transparency is the one asset no rival can burn down.
