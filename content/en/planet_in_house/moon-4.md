title: Moon in the 4th House
---

Home is your recharging dock. Wherever you live becomes the emotional headquarters of your life — you feel your feelings there, hours or days after the event, once the door is shut and the world is muffled. Guests notice it too: your space holds people the way some voices do.

This placement gives deep reserves. You know how to tend, feed, and shelter, and your loyalty to family — chosen or given — is structural, not sentimental. The friction is that your moods anchor to family history. Old kitchen arguments replay in new relationships, and a disrupted home life — a move, a renovation, tension under your roof — can destabilize everything else, because the dock itself is rocking.

You may also retreat when you most need reinforcement. When you feel the pull to disappear inward, invite one trusted person into the sanctuary instead of sealing it.
