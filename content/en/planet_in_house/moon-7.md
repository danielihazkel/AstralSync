title: Moon in the 7th House
---

Your feelings seek a witness. Safety, for you, is fundamentally relational — the person across the table, the partner who answers at 2 a.m., the colleague you debrief with after every meeting. Alone, your emotions can feel theoretical; with someone, they come into focus.

The strength is real attunement. You read the other side of any table with unusual accuracy, adjust without being asked, and make people feel met. The friction is the trade you may not notice making: tuning yourself to a partner's needs so automatically that your own go unfiled, then surfacing later as resentment or a mood neither of you can explain. Solitude can register as mild emergency, which makes staying in an ill-fitting relationship tempting.

Practice sitting with one uncomfortable feeling for a day before sharing it — not to hide it, but to learn what it says when only you are listening.
