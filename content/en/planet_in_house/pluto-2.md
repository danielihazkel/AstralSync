title: Pluto in the 2nd House
---

Security is not casual for you. Money, property, and skills are survival equipment, and you pursue them with a focus that can rebuild a bankrupt life from rubble — more than once if necessary. You have a nose for undervalued assets and hidden worth, in markets and in yourself, and your resourcefulness under pressure is genuinely formidable.

The grip is the problem. Possessions can become bunkers, budgets can become control systems, and your sense of self-worth can fuse with your net worth until any financial dip feels like annihilation. Sharing resources — even with people you love — may trigger a quiet, fierce mine.

Loosen one finger at a time. Give something away each month, on purpose, and watch what the fear says. You are the asset; everything else is replaceable.
