title: Neptune in the 2nd House
---

Money behaves like water in your hands. It arrives from unexpected directions, funds beautiful things, and evaporates before you can say where it went. The strength here is real non-attachment: you can earn through imagination — art, healing, atmosphere — and you give without keeping score, which makes you generous in ways people never forget.

The trouble is that vagueness about numbers becomes vagueness about worth. Unchecked balances, loans you were too kind to refuse, and pricing your work on faith all quietly teach you that your value is negotiable.

Idealism and accounting can coexist. Automate the boring parts — savings, bills, invoices — so the dreaming happens on top of a floor. Once a month, look at the actual figures without flinching.
