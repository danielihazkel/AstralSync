title: Saturn in the 2nd House
---

Money arrives in your life carrying homework. Saturn in the 2nd House often installs an early scarcity script — a sense that resources are hard-won, easily lost, and never quite enough — regardless of what the bank statement says. The gift inside the anxiety is competence: you budget when others improvise, and wealth you build tends to stay built, brick by patient brick.

The trap is letting caution calcify into self-denial, or measuring your worth by the ledger. Underselling your work is this placement's signature leak — you charge for the hours and forget to charge for the mastery.

Once a year, price something you offer slightly above your comfort level, and watch whether the world actually objects.
