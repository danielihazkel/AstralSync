title: Mars in the 10th House
---

Ambition is not one of your traits; it is your operating system. Mars in the 10th aims the whole fight upward — toward the title, the summit, the visible win — and you tend to measure a week by what it did for the mission. Others notice: your drive is public, and so are your battles.

Because your effort happens where everyone can see it, you build reputation fast and doors open for the sheer force of your work. The exposure cuts both ways. Clashes with bosses and authorities become part of your record, and a temper lost in a meeting travels further than one lost at home.

Career is your arena; family and rest can quietly become the sacrifice. Choose which fights are worth your name being attached to them — then decline one public battle this month.
