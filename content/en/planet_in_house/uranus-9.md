title: Uranus in the 9th House
---

Every dogma you meet, you stress-test. Uranus in the 9th House makes you a heretic in the honest sense — beliefs assembled from unexpected sources, education pursued sideways, travel that rearranges you rather than decorates you. Your worldview updates when the evidence does, which is rarer than it sounds, and you often see where a culture's story is heading before its official narrators do.

The friction is contrarian orthodoxy: rejecting an idea because it is mainstream is still letting the mainstream steer. Serial conversion — a new framework every eighteen months — can keep your philosophy wide but rootless.

Keep one conviction long enough to live by it for a full year; test depth the way you already test breadth.
