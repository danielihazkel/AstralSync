title: Moon in the 6th House
---

Order soothes you. A cleared desk, a finished list, a body that has been fed and moved on schedule — these are your emotional infrastructure, and when routine collapses, mood follows within days. You feel safest when you are useful, and small acts of service are your native dialect of care.

The strength is steadiness others can lean on: you notice what needs doing and quietly do it, and your habits genuinely protect your health. The friction runs two ways. Anxiety disguises itself as productivity, so you may scrub the kitchen at midnight instead of feeling the feeling — and your body, in turn, files unprocessed emotion as headaches, gut trouble, or fatigue.

Caring through chores can also replace saying anything real. When a symptom flares or the tidying turns compulsive, stop and ask what you needed this week that you never requested.
