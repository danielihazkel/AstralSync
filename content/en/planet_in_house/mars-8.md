title: Mars in the 8th House
---

Your courage lives in the deep end. Crisis, intimacy, debt, grief, other people's secrets — the situations most people flee are where Mars in the 8th steadies and sharpens. You would rather have one devastating honest conversation than ten pleasant shallow ones.

That composure under pressure makes you the person others call when everything breaks, and it gives your closeness real intensity. The friction gathers around shared resources and control: joint finances, inheritances, and intimate power balances can become slow-burning battlegrounds, and your anger tends to go covert — strategic, patient, and harder to resolve than an open flare.

Desire here is transformative but rarely casual, which can exhaust partners who wanted lightness. When you feel yourself maneuvering instead of speaking, surface the grievance within a day — buried Mars digs tunnels.
