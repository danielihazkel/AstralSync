title: Moon in the 1st House
---

Your feelings arrive before your words do — and everyone can see them. Mood lives on your face, in your posture, in the weather you bring through the door. This transparency is a strength: people trust you quickly because nothing about you feels staged, and your instincts about rooms and strangers are usually right.

The cost is porousness. You absorb the emotional charge of whatever space you enter, and it can take hours to work out which feelings were originally yours. Safety, for you, means being allowed to respond honestly in the moment; being told to "keep it professional" can feel like suffocation.

Because your needs broadcast themselves, others may react to your mood before you have even registered it. Build a two-minute check-in with yourself before entering charged rooms, and name the feeling silently before anyone else names it for you.
