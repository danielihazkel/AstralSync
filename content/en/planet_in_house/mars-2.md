title: Mars in the 2nd House
---

Money is where your fight shows up. You pursue income the way athletes pursue records — hustling, negotiating, taking on the extra job — and you would rather earn something twice over than be handed it once. Ownership matters: your things, your rates, your terms.

The strength is real earning power and the nerve to ask for what your work is worth. The friction is that money also becomes the battlefield. Spending can be as impulsive as earning is fierce, and a challenge to your possessions or your value can flip you from calm to combative in seconds.

Self-worth is the quieter stake underneath every invoice. When a financial disagreement heats up, pause and ask whether you are defending the number or defending your dignity — then negotiate only the number.
