title: Uranus in the 4th House
---

Your roots grow in portable soil. Uranus in the 4th House rewrites the script for home: an unconventional family, sudden relocations in childhood, or an adulthood of re-potting — new cities, chosen kin, living arrangements the neighbors cannot categorize. The strength is liberation from inherited molds; you get to design belonging from scratch, and the household culture you create can be genuinely freer than the one you left.

The friction is restlessness at the foundation. Just as a place starts to feel settled, something in you reaches for the doorknob — and the people nesting with you can feel the draft.

Learn to tell the urge to grow from the urge to bolt: before the next move, name what you are actually escaping.
