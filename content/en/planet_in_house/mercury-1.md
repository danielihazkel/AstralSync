title: Mercury in the 1st House
---

People meet your intellect first. Mercury in the 1st puts thinking at the front of your identity — you talk with your hands, narrate your reasoning out loud, and make first impressions through questions, wit, and sheer speed of response.

The strength is agility: you read a room fast, adapt your language to whoever is in it, and rarely get stuck for words. The friction is that your identity can fuse with your opinions, so being corrected feels like being erased — and your quickness can steamroll people who process slowly, leaving them agreeing without having actually been heard.

Practice one deliberate pause per conversation: let someone finish their entire thought before you improve on it.
