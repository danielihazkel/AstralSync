title: Sun in the 8th House
---

Surfaces bore you. Your vitality lives in the deep end — intimacy that costs something, money that is shared and therefore political, crises that strip people to what they actually are. You are the one who stays calm when the situation gets real, and others sense they can bring you what they hide from everyone else.

That depth is your strength: you transform, repeatedly, and you help others do the same. The friction is trust and control. Merging resources or hearts means surrendering some sovereignty, and you may test partners covertly instead of asking plainly, or keep an exit fund — emotional or financial — that quietly caps every commitment.

Intensity can also become a habit, manufacturing storms when life goes flat. Notice when you provoke depth rather than wait for it, and try naming one secret need out loud instead.
