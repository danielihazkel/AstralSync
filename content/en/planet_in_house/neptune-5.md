title: Neptune in the 5th House
---

When you create, something seems to move through you rather than from you. Songs, images, and stories arrive nearly whole, and your play has a quality of enchantment that draws people in — audiences, children, lovers. Romance especially glows here: falling for someone can feel like stepping into a film scored just for you.

That glow is also the trap. You tend to love the movie more than the person, casting ordinary humans in transcendent roles they never auditioned for. Creative projects can suffer the same fate, staying perfect and imaginary because finishing would make them real and flawed.

Choose completion over perfection once a month: finish one small piece and show it to someone. In love, keep dating the actual person after the soundtrack fades.
