title: Jupiter in the 7th House
---

Your luck travels in pairs. Jupiter in the 7th brings growth through the other person: partners who open doors, marriages that expand your world, business alliances that outperform anything you build alone. Even your rivals tend to be generous ones who teach more than they take.

The strength is that you partner well — you give people the benefit of the doubt, negotiate in good faith, and attract others who are established, wise, or simply bigger-hearted than average. The friction is inflation of the other. You can idealize partners into saviors, expect one relationship to supply meaning that belongs to a whole life, or stay too long because your optimism keeps redrafting who they might become.

Committed bonds are your genuine growth engine, not a limit on your freedom. When you next assess a partnership, list what is actually happening beside what you hope will happen — and weigh only the first column.
