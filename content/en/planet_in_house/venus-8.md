title: Venus in the 8th House
---

You do not want pleasant; you want profound. Venus in the 8th loves at depth — merged finances, merged histories, the kind of intimacy that requires showing what you would rather hide. Casual connection leaves you hungry: you would trade ten nice evenings for one conversation that changes both people.

The gift is transformative closeness. People trust you with their darkest material, and love with you leaves no one unchanged. The friction is entanglement — desire, money, and power braid together easily in your relationships, and jealousy or quiet testing can creep in when you cannot tell whether you love someone or possess them.

Notice when you withhold warmth to see what the other person will do — intensity earned by testing is not intimacy.
