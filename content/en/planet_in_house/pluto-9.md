title: Pluto in the 9th House
---

Belief, for you, is load-bearing. You do not collect opinions; you excavate worldviews, studying a philosophy, faith, or field until you have touched bedrock. When you teach or argue from that depth, you are compelling in a way casual thinkers never manage, and a single book or journey really can demolish and rebuild your entire understanding of life.

The danger is the crusade. Conviction can shade into dogma, debate into conversion, and you may write off entire traditions — or people — after one disillusionment with a teacher who turned out to be human. Certainty feels like strength but often marks the exact spot you have stopped digging.

Once a year, read the best steelman of a view you despise, written by someone who holds it. Keep your convictions strong and your borders open.
