title: Uranus in the 11th House
---

You collect people the way others collect ideas — for the spark. Uranus in the 11th House fills your circle with originals: friends from incompatible worlds, groups organized around causes rather than convention, a network that keeps handing you the future early. You are often the one who introduces strangers who become collaborators, names the coming thing, or drags a community into its next decade.

The friction is loyalty to the vision over the people. You may drop groups abruptly when they ossify, or champion humanity in the abstract while an individual friend waits for a returned call.

Anchor one friendship with boring maintenance — a standing call, a remembered birthday — and let the rest stay gloriously irregular.
