title: Pluto in the 3rd House
---

Words are instruments of power in your hands. You do not chat — you investigate. Small talk bores you, but give you a real question and you will dig past every polite answer to the root. As a researcher, writer, or interviewer you are relentless, and people confess things to you they have told no one.

The shadow side is verbal combat. You can weaponize what you know, win arguments so thoroughly that relationships lose, and replay conversations obsessively, drafting the retort you should have delivered. Early dynamics with siblings or classmates may have taught you that information is leverage — a lesson worth outgrowing.

Once a day, swap an assertion for a question you do not know the answer to. Curiosity disarms better than the perfect comeback ever will.
