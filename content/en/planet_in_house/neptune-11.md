title: Neptune in the 11th House
---

You dream in the plural. Causes, communities, and visions of a better collective future light you up, and you offer groups something priceless: faith that people can be more than their incentives. Friends experience you as the one who sees their best self, and your circles often include artists, mystics, and strays.

Disillusionment is the recurring tax. Movements reveal their politics, charismatic organizers reveal their egos, and friendships built on projection dissolve when reality arrives. You may also give time and money to collective dreams that were never structured to succeed, then feel foolish for having believed.

Vet groups by behavior, not rhetoric: watch how they treat their least useful member. Keep two or three friendships grounded in mutual, specific, reciprocated care — not just shared dreams.
