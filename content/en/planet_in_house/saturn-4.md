title: Saturn in the 4th House
---

Home is where your work is — the inner work. Saturn in the 4th House often points to a childhood that ran on duty more than warmth: early responsibility, a strict or absent parent, a house with rules where softness should have been. The strength this builds is foundational. You know how to hold a family together, and the home you eventually make is solid precisely because you constructed it deliberately instead of inheriting it.

The friction is a lingering sense that you must earn belonging, even at your own kitchen table. Rest can feel unearned; roots can feel like weight rather than nourishment.

Practice receiving care at home without immediately repaying it — let one favor stand uninvoiced this week.
