title: Sun in the 5th House
---

Play is not a break from your real life — it is the engine of it. You are vitalized by making things and showing them: art, performance, games, flirtation, children, any arena where self-expression carries risk and gets a response. When you create, you feel like yourself; when you cannot, the lights dim.

Your strength is warmth with wattage. People feel more alive around you, and you give courage by example, doing the vulnerable thing in public first. The catch is the applause. Praise can become the fuel gauge, so you may abandon good work that draws no audience, or keep performing a version of yourself the crowd already likes.

Once in a while, make something you show no one — and watch what happens to the quality of everything else.
