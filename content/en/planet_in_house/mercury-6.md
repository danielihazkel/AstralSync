title: Mercury in the 6th House
---

Your mind is a workshop. Mercury in the 6th concentrates thought on process — routines, checklists, the tool that shaves ten minutes off the task. You notice what others miss: the typo, the bottleneck, the small daily habit quietly wrecking someone's health. Colleagues bring you tangled problems because you actually read the fine print.

That precision is a real craft, and it makes you indispensable wherever quality matters. Its shadow is worry with a clipboard: the same eye that catches errors can turn on your own body and schedule, converting every twinge and imperfect day into a diagnostic that needs running.

Give improvement a time slot of its own, so optimizing stops colonizing all of them.
