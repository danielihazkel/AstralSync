title: Sun in the 12th House
---

Your light works behind the scenes. Solitude, imagination, dreams, and quiet service are where you feel most yourself — often more real in an empty room than at your own party. You absorb atmospheres others miss, and your best contributions may be anonymous: the hospital shift, the unsigned draft, the prayer, the help no one traces back to you.

That interior depth is the strength: compassion, creative wells that never quite empty, comfort with the endings other people flee. The friction is visibility and boundaries. You can vanish inside institutions, relationships, or fantasy, letting others take the credit and the oxygen, then wonder why you feel unseen. What you refuse to acknowledge in yourself tends to run the show from backstage.

Protect one hour of true solitude daily — and once a week, deliberately let someone watch you do the thing you do best.
