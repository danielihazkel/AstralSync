title: Moon in the 3rd House
---

Talking is how you digest feeling. A worry stays raw until you have said it to someone, texted it, or journaled it into shape; conversation is not a distraction from your inner life, it is the plumbing. Your emotional world is wired to the everyday circuit — siblings, neighbors, group chats, the barista who knows your order.

The strength is emotional fluency: you name feelings quickly, comfort others with exactly-right words, and learn best when a subject touches you personally. The friction is churn. A restless mind can narrate every mood into a crisis, and you may talk about a feeling so thoroughly that you never actually feel it — or vent to five people and act on none of it.

Notice when re-telling a story stops relieving you and starts rehearsing it; that is your cue to stop describing and decide.
