title: Saturn in the 6th House
---

You take work personally, in the literal sense: it lives in your body. Saturn in the 6th House makes you the reliable one — the colleague who documents, maintains, and finishes; the craftsperson whose standards raise everyone's game. Routines, once built, become load-bearing, and your health improves dramatically under structure you chose yourself.

The friction is overwork disguised as virtue. Duty expands to fill every hour, rest reads as laziness, and the body eventually files a complaint — often through the very stress symptoms you were too busy to prevent.

Treat one recovery habit — sleep, a walk, an actual lunch away from the desk — with the same seriousness you give deadlines, and defend it on your calendar.
