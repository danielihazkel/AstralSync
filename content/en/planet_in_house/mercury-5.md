title: Mercury in the 5th House
---

You think best when you are playing. Mercury in the 5th makes wit a form of self-expression — puns, banter, storytelling, the perfectly timed caption. Romance starts as conversation for you; a partner who cannot volley words rarely holds your attention, and children draw out your most inventive teaching.

The gift is that ideas come alive in your hands: you make dry material entertaining, and your enthusiasm is contagious in classrooms, brainstorms, and group chats. The friction is the audience — you can start performing insight instead of having it, and criticism of your creative work can sting like heckling in a room you thought was friendly.

Make something regularly with no plan to show anyone, and notice how differently your mind moves.
