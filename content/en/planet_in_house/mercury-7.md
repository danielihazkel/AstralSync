title: Mercury in the 7th House
---

You find out what you think by saying it to someone. Mercury in the 7th makes the mind dialogical — ideas stay half-formed until a partner, client, or worthy opponent pushes back on them. You are a natural negotiator, interviewer, and devil's advocate, and you tend to bond with people you can talk to for hours.

The strength is genuine fairness: you can hold the other side's argument as skillfully as your own, which makes you a superb mediator. The friction is dependency — left alone with a decision, you may poll everyone you know, or quietly let the most articulate person in the room do your thinking for you.

Before you ask anyone's opinion, write down your own first, so the conversation sharpens it instead of replacing it.
