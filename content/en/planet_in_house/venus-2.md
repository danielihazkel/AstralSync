title: Venus in the 2nd House
---

Pleasure, for you, is tangible. Venus in the 2nd loves what can be touched, tasted, saved, and owned — good fabric, a good meal, a bank balance that lets you sleep. You have a knack for attracting resources and an instinct for quality: the things you buy tend to be worth it, and people trust your taste enough to borrow it.

The friction is the loop between comfort and worth. Spending can become self-soothing, a lean season can feel like a verdict on your value rather than your ledger, and generosity sometimes arrives with a quiet price tag attached.

Notice what you reach for when you feel unloved — often the purchase is a message you could deliver more cheaply in words.
