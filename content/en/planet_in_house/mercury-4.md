title: Mercury in the 4th House
---

You do your best thinking at home. Mercury in the 4th turns the mind inward and backward — toward family stories, old photographs, the question of where you come from and why the household runs the way it does. You may be the family's historian, its translator, or the one who finally says the unsaid thing at dinner.

The strength is depth: your thoughts have roots, and your private reflections often outclass your public remarks. The friction is rumination — the same walls that shelter you can become an echo chamber where one worry loops long after midnight, gaining volume instead of resolution.

When a thought circles a third time, take it out of the house: say it aloud to someone, or walk it around the block.
