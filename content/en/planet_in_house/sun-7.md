title: Sun in the 7th House
---

You find yourself in the mirror of another person. Partnership — marriage, close collaboration, even a worthy rival — is where your identity sharpens. You think better in dialogue, commit harder with a counterpart, and often only learn what you want by watching yourself respond to someone else.

The gift is genuine skill with people: diplomacy, timing, the ability to make one-on-one bonds that reshape both lives. The risk is outsourcing selfhood. You can become whoever the relationship needs, and drift when single or unpartnered at work, waiting for someone to reflect you back. Conflict avoidance is the quiet version of the same problem — peace purchased with your own preferences.

Before your next joint decision, write down your position while alone, then compare it with what you actually say in the room.
