title: Mercury in the 12th House
---

Your clearest thoughts arrive when nobody is watching. Mercury in the 12th does its work behind the scenes — in dreams, long showers, and the half-hour before sleep when the real answer finally surfaces. You absorb the unspoken mood of a room, often knowing what someone means before they finish saying it.

That receptive imagination is a genuine research instrument: it hands you conclusions that faster minds walk past. The friction sits at the border of speech — what is vivid inside can arrive garbled out loud, so you get misquoted or underestimated in fast meetings, and noise scrambles a signal that solitude would have restored.

Protect a daily window of quiet, and write your points down before meetings — your mind deserves a translator, and it can be you.
