title: Saturn in the 10th House
---

Your career is a mountain, and you know it. Saturn in the 10th House — its home territory — points ambition at a long, visible climb: authority earned rung by rung, reputation compounding slowly, mastery arriving in the second half of life and then proving unshakeable. People instinctively hand you responsibility because you visibly do not drop it.

The friction is the fear of public failure that shadows every step. Success can become a debt you are perpetually servicing, and titles can substitute for the harder question of what you actually want to build with your one working life.

Every year or so, ask whether you are climbing your mountain or one that was assigned to you — and adjust the route while you still can.
