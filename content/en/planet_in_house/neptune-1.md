title: Neptune in the 1st House
---

People meet you through a soft-focus lens. With Neptune rising over your identity, your face and manner act like a screen: others project their longings onto you and swear you are exactly what they needed. That porousness is a genuine gift — you read rooms instantly, adapt without effort, and put strangers at ease.

The friction is that the fog works both ways. You absorb moods that are not yours, and your sense of who you are can shift to match whoever is watching. Unexplained fatigue, vagueness about your own wants, and a drifting self-image are the usual symptoms.

Treat your body as the anchor your identity needs — sleep, food, movement, all of it. When you feel yourself blurring, ask one grounding question: is this feeling mine, or did I just pick it up?
