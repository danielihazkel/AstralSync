title: Mercury in the 11th House
---

Ideas are how you make friends. Mercury in the 11th thinks in networks — group chats, communities, the future tense. You are the one who knows who is working on what, introduces strangers with shared obsessions, and treats a good cause as a puzzle worth arguing about. Your best thinking happens in company and aims years ahead.

The strength is reach: your ideas travel because you have already built them an audience, and collective brainstorming genuinely improves your work. The friction is consensus — a mind tuned to the group can start outsourcing its conclusions to it, mistaking what your circle believes for what you have verified.

Every so often, argue a position none of your friends hold, just to confirm the muscle still works.
