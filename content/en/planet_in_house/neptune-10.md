title: Neptune in the 10th House
---

Your calling is more felt than named. Job titles sit awkwardly on you, and the standard ladder can look like someone else's dream, yet you carry a persistent sense of being meant for something — often work with an artistic, healing, or spiritual thread. When your career aligns with that current, you work with unusual devotion and grace.

The friction is definition. Your public image is unusually easy to project onto: audiences, bosses, and strangers will decide who you are, credit you with saintliness or blame you for fog, and drift can pass for openness while years slip by without a chosen direction.

Author your own reputation before someone else drafts it. Every quarter, name one concrete professional step in plain language — a title, a portfolio piece, a number — and take it.
