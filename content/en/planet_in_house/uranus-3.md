title: Uranus in the 3rd House
---

Your mind changes lanes without signaling. Uranus in the 3rd House wires perception for leaps: you connect fields nobody thought adjacent, ask the question that reroutes the meeting, and learn best by dismantling things rather than memorizing them. Siblings, neighbors, or the daily commute often carry a streak of the unusual too.

The gift is real originality of thought; the friction is transmission. Insights arrive faster than syntax, attention scatters across eleven open tabs, and listeners three steps behind may hear arrogance where you meant electricity.

When you present a breakthrough, build the staircase — say the two obvious steps you skipped — so others can climb to where you already are.
