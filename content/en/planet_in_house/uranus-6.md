title: Uranus in the 6th House
---

Routine is the one thing you cannot do routinely. Uranus in the 6th House brings an inventor's eye to work and health: you automate the tedious, redesign broken workflows, and do your best work on schedules you chose — freelance rhythms, odd hours, sprints instead of shifts. Teams profit enormously from your improvements, right up until you improve something nobody asked you to touch.

The friction is rebellion against maintenance itself. Health and craft both run on repetition, and abandoning every regimen in week three keeps you permanently starting over.

Build routines with escape hatches — the same daily practice in a rotating format — so consistency and novelty stop being enemies.
