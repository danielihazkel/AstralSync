title: Jupiter in the 11th House
---

Your net worth is your network. Jupiter in the 11th multiplies friends, allies, and lucky introductions: the right person tends to know the right person, and somehow both of them already like you. Groups expand when you join them, and causes gain momentum from your belief that the future can actually be better.

The strength is genuine social wealth — you connect people generously, without keeping ledgers, and it returns to you at the strangest, best moments. The friction is dilution. A hundred warm acquaintances can crowd out three deep friendships, commitments to groups multiply past what your calendar survives, and big collective dreams can stay dreams because the crowd's enthusiasm feels like progress.

Your hopes are one of your real instruments; aim them. Choose the one cause or circle that matters most this year and give it your depth — breadth will take care of itself.
