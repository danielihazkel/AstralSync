title: Venus in the 11th House
---

Friendship is your first romance. Venus in the 11th loves the group — the found family, the shared cause, the long table of people you collected one by one over the years. Your partners often begin as friends, and your friends often stay for decades; you have a gift for making community feel like a place rather than a schedule.

The strength is breadth: warmth that scales, and networks that actually catch you when you fall. The friction is dilution — affection spread across forty people can leave the closest few underfed, and romance can stall in friendship because escalating it risks the whole ecosystem.

Name your inner circle to yourself, and give those few the disproportionate share they have earned.
