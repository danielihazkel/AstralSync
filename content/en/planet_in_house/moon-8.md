title: Moon in the 8th House
---

You feel in currents, not ripples. Emotional life runs deep and mostly underground: you sense the unsaid thing in every room, bond through shared vulnerability rather than shared hobbies, and feel safest with people who have shown you their wreckage. Half-open is not a setting you have.

The strength is that you are unshockable. Friends bring you their worst chapters because you neither flinch nor gossip, and you metabolize crisis into growth better than almost anyone. The friction is the vault. You test loyalty silently, remember slights in high resolution, and can mistake intensity for intimacy — mistrusting calm affection precisely because it asks for no proof.

Entanglement with others' resources or secrets can also keep old wounds employed. Once a season, tell a trusted person one thing you decided they could not handle — and let the evidence update you.
