title: Mars in the 1st House
---

Your energy arrives before your words do. With Mars in the 1st, drive lives in the body — you walk fast, decide fast, and treat hesitation as a problem to be solved. People read you as direct because you are: there is no committee between impulse and action.

That immediacy is the strength. You start things others only discuss, and you defend yourself without waiting for permission. The friction is collateral heat: impatience leaks into your tone, small delays register as insults, and you can turn a conversation into a contest without noticing you drew the line.

Your body is the pressure valve — unspent energy curdles into irritability within a day. Before pushing back in any exchange, name what you are actually fighting for, and burn the rest off with movement.
