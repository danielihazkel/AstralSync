title: Mercury in the 3rd House
---

Curiosity is your resting state. Mercury in the 3rd is the messenger in its own house: you collect facts, errands, open tabs, and conversations the way other people collect quiet. Neighborhood news, sibling banter, the odd article at midnight — everything local and immediate feeds you.

Your strength is fluency. You explain complicated things clearly, learn new systems fast, and connect people who should already know each other. The friction is scatter: ten open threads can mean none finished, and constant input leaves little room for digestion, so you can end up quoting everyone and concluding nothing of your own.

Pick one subject a season to go genuinely deep on, and let everything else remain delightful shallows.
