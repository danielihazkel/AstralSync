title: Jupiter in the 3rd House
---

Curiosity is your natural resource, and the vein never runs dry. Jupiter in the 3rd makes the everyday world a classroom: you collect facts, courses, podcasts, and conversations the way others collect souvenirs, and you can make a trip to the corner store feel like an expedition worth retelling.

The strength is connective intelligence — you explain things so they land, tell stories people repeat, and often become the informal teacher of any circle you join. Siblings and neighbors frequently turn out to be luck-bearers. The friction is sprawl: ten open books, five half-finished courses, and a talent for talking past the point where listening would have served you.

Breadth is your gift; depth is your discipline. Pick one subject this season and follow it three layers deeper than usual — your best insights live just past where your curiosity normally moves on.
