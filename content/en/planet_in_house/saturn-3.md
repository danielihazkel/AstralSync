title: Saturn in the 3rd House
---

You distrust easy answers, including your own. Saturn in the 3rd House makes speech and learning deliberate: you may have felt slow in early classrooms, careful in conversation, or responsible for a sibling before your time. What looks like hesitation is quality control — when you finally speak or write, the result carries weight, and people quote you.

The friction is silence at the wrong moments. Waiting for the perfect sentence, you can lose the meeting, the argument, or the chance to be known. Small talk feels like a test you didn't study for, so you skip it and read as distant.

Say the eighty-percent version of your thought out loud today; precision can arrive in the second sentence.
