title: Neptune in the 3rd House
---

Your mind works in watercolor. Where others think in bullet points, you think in images, associations, and half-heard music, which makes you a natural poet, storyteller, or translator of moods into words. You often understand what someone means before they finish the sentence, and your writing can carry an atmosphere no outline could produce.

The cost is precision. Dates slip, directions blur, and you sometimes remember the feeling of a conversation rather than what was actually said. Siblings and neighbors may know a slightly different version of shared history than you do, and misunderstandings can snowball from a single fuzzy detail.

Trust the intuition, verify the facts. Repeat agreements back in plain words, write things down at the moment they happen, and let the poetry live everywhere else.
