title: Pluto in the 4th House
---

Your taproot goes into charged soil. Somewhere in the family story there is buried material — secrets, ruptures, a parent whose power shaped the household — and you grew up reading the emotional currents under the floorboards. That early training left you with a gift: you see the truth of families, yours and everyone's, and you have the strength to end cycles that ran for generations.

The friction is that home can stay a defended fortress. You may control your domestic world tightly, keep even intimates outside certain doors, and find the past resurfacing exactly when life gets stable — as if calm itself were suspicious.

Name one inherited pattern out loud this year, to a therapist, a partner, or a page. What is spoken loses its power to run you.
