title: Mars in the 4th House
---

Home is not your resting place — it is your engine room. You renovate, reorganize, defend, and improve wherever you live, and you fight hardest for the people under your roof. Family history likely includes conflict you either inherited or had to push against to become yourself.

The strength is fierce protectiveness and the stamina to build a foundation from scratch. The friction is where the temper goes: anger you suppress in public tends to detonate in private, so the people closest to you meet a sharper version than colleagues ever see.

Old family battles can keep running in you long after the house is quiet. When irritation spikes at home, check whether it belongs to this moment or an older one — then give the energy a project, not a person.
