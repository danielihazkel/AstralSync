title: Part of Spirit in Sagittarius
---

You act toward meaning. Your will engages when the work connects to something larger — a belief, a body of knowledge, a horizon — and disengages quickly from anything you cannot justify at that scale. When you are convinced you are extremely hard to stop.

- Where it works: teaching, travel and study in the demanding sense, publishing, advocacy, work requiring genuine conviction rather than mere competence.
- What wastes it: the next horizon. Conviction that moves on before the work lands leaves a trail of promising beginnings, and what feels like breadth from inside reads as unreliability from outside.

Finish one thing you started because you believed in it, especially now the belief has cooled. Completion is the part that teaches.
