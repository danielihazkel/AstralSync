title: Lilith in Leo
---

You were taught that wanting to be seen was vanity, so you learned to shine by accident and shrug off the credit. Lilith in Leo is the unapologetic performer underneath — the one who wants the stage, the applause, and the byline, and is done pretending otherwise. Owned, this is magnetic: your open delight in being witnessed gives everyone around you permission to stop shrinking.

Denied, it curdles. You orbit charismatic people and quietly resent them, dismiss attention you secretly crave, or leak the hunger through fishing and false modesty — and every unclaimed spotlight confirms the old story that wanting one was shameful.

Say it out loud once this week: name something you made, and let the compliment land without deflecting it.
