title: Lilith in Cancer
---

Your wildness is need itself. Lilith in Cancer was told early that wanting comfort, closeness, and care made you too much — so you learned to feed everyone else and call your own hunger weakness. Reclaimed, this placement is fierce tenderness: you protect what you love without apology and ask to be held without a cover story.

Suppressed, the need doesn't vanish; it goes underground. You give with invisible strings, keep score in silence, or test people by withdrawing and waiting to be missed. When no one passes the test, the old story — that your need drives people away — feels proven again.

- Your strength: when you name a need plainly, your care for others turns clean and unresentful.
- Your friction: hinting instead of asking, then grieving the answer to a question you never voiced.

This week, make one direct request for comfort with no justification attached.
