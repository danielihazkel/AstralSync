title: North Node in Taurus
---

Your growth points toward the slow, the solid, and the sufficient. Life keeps nudging you to build things that don't require a crisis to feel real: savings that accumulate, a body that gets fed and rested, a garden-pace project that rewards patience over intensity. Calm is the unfamiliar country you're being asked to settle.

When you lean in, you become the steady one — the person whose word, table, and budget can be leaned on, and that reliability turns out to be magnetic rather than boring. The Scorpio undertow is the friction: a taste for emotional weather, for merging with other people's resources and dramas, for burning it all down because rebuilding feels more alive than maintaining.

Peace will initially read as flatness. Sit with that misreading instead of fixing it.

Today, finish one unglamorous maintenance task — and let the quiet afterward count as an achievement.
