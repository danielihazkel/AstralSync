title: North Node in Leo
---

Your growth points toward the center of the stage you keep declining. Life wants you to make something with your name on it — art, a family, a project that couldn't have come from anyone else — and to enjoy the applause instead of deflecting it toward the team, the cause, the collective.

- Your strength, when you lean in: warmth becomes leadership. Your delight is genuinely contagious, and claiming it gives everyone around you permission to shine too.
- Your friction: the Aquarius undertow. You hide inside the group, theorize about life instead of starring in yours, and call the detachment "objectivity" when it is mostly stage fright.

Special is not a dirty word here. Wanting to be loved for who you are, specifically, is the whole assignment.

Share one thing you made this week and stay present for the reaction — no disclaimer, no *it's just a little thing*.
