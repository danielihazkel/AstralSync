title: Part of Spirit in Capricorn
---

You act by taking responsibility. Your will works through structure, sequence and a willingness to be accountable for the result — and you will carry weight for years if the endpoint is real. Authority, when it reaches you, tends to be earned rather than granted.

- Where it works: building institutions and careers, work with a long horizon, stewardship, any situation where somebody has to be answerable and nobody is.
- What wastes it: duty without examination. You shoulder a burden so automatically that you never ask whether it is yours, and a decade can go to a goal you inherited rather than chose.

Name the ambition you are currently serving and ask plainly whose it is. Better to know now than at the summit.
