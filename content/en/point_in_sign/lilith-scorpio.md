title: Lilith in Scorpio
---

Here your untamed part is intimacy itself — desire, power, and the will to know what is actually going on beneath the performance. You were taught this intensity was dangerous, so you learned to meter it out carefully. Owned, it becomes your gift: people trust you with what they have never said aloud, because you don't flinch.

Suppressed, the intensity goes covert. You test loyalty instead of asking for it, hold secrets as quiet leverage, or starve your own desire until it resurfaces as obsession with someone else's. Control becomes the acceptable costume that hunger wears.

- Your strength: fearless honesty about the undercurrents everyone else politely ignores.
- Your friction: strategizing your vulnerability instead of risking it.

This week, tell one trusted person a want you would normally make them guess.
