title: Part of Spirit in Virgo
---

You act by improving. Your will expresses itself as craft — the thing made more accurate, more useful, more nearly right — and you are most yourself in service to a standard rather than an audience. Competence, for you, is close to a moral position.

- Where it works: skilled work with a real criterion, analysis, repair, systems that make other people effective, apprenticeship in either direction.
- What wastes it: correction turned inward. The discernment that sharpens your work will grind you down when aimed at yourself, and perfectionism is the most efficient method you have ever found for avoiding a finish.

Ship one thing this week at eighty per cent, then note what actually went wrong. It will be less than you predicted.
