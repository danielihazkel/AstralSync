title: North Node in Libra
---

Your growth points toward the art of *with*. Life keeps placing you in duets you can't win alone — partnerships, negotiations, marriages of every kind — and asking you to stay at the table past the point where going solo would feel simpler. Considering another person's reality as thoroughly as your own is the muscle being built.

- Your strength, when you lean in: you become a genuine diplomat, someone whose fairness people trust with their conflicts, and your relationships stop being battlegrounds and start being resources.
- Your friction: the Aries undertow. At the first whiff of compromise you bolt for independence, frame every negotiation as a fight to survive, and call the loneliness afterward "freedom."

Other people are not obstacles to your path here. They are the path.

Next time you're about to decide unilaterally, ask the person it affects what they'd prefer — and let their answer actually move you.
