title: South Node in Cancer
---

Caretaking is your native craft. You read a room's emotional weather before anyone speaks, you remember how people take their coffee and their bad news, and you can turn any four walls into a nest. Families, teams, and wounded friends orbit you because being near you feels like being held.

- Your fluency: nourishment. You keep people emotionally fed with an ease that looks supernatural to those who had to learn it.
- The undertow: the nest becomes the excuse. You hide ambitions inside other people's needs, feel indispensable instead of fulfilled, and let moods make decisions your goals should be making. Protecting can shade into managing; being needed can substitute for being known.

The unlived territory is public: authority, achievement, a name earned in the world beyond your kitchen. The people you tend don't need more tending — they need to watch you climb.

This month, spend one evening a week on your own ambition, guarded as fiercely as you'd guard anyone else's dinner.
