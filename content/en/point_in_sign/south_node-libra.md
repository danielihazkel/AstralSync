title: South Node in Libra
---

Harmony is your first language. You can find the middle of any argument, make opposite people feel equally heard, and smooth a tense dinner with one well-placed sentence. This diplomacy is genuine skill — mediators train for years to do what you do on instinct.

The undertow is that the peacemaker forgets to have a position. You mirror so fluently that your own preferences blur; you defer, then simmer; you stay in partnerships past their honesty because ending them would mean being the one who disturbed the water. Other people's approval becomes the compass, and the compass spins.

The muscle now being built is selfhood: a want stated before it's been focus-grouped, a conflict entered on purpose, a life that has your fingerprints on the decisions. Discord survived is worth more to you now than accord maintained.

Next time someone asks where you want to eat, answer in five seconds with a real preference — and hold it even if they frown.
