title: Lilith in Aries
---

Somewhere early, someone decided your anger was too much — too fast, too loud, too first. Lilith in Aries is the part of you that wants before it apologizes, that knows what it knows in the first half-second and says so. When you own that heat, you become the person who names the thing everyone else is circling.

- Your strength: clean, immediate honesty. Your anger, expressed on time, is information — it ends a bad arrangement in one sentence instead of three resentful years.
- Your friction: suppressed, the fire goes sideways. You swallow the flash, act agreeable, then erupt over something small — or pick fights on behalf of a want you never admitted having.

This week, when the flash comes, say the want plainly within a minute — no apology attached.
