title: Part of Fortune in Scorpio
---

Your fortune lives below the surface. Ease, for you, is not lightness — it's depth: the conversation that finally gets honest, the crisis that clears away a false life, the research rabbit hole that turns into expertise. You are replenished by intensity that would exhaust other people, and your luck often arrives disguised as an ending.

- Where it flows: transformation work, shared resources handled shrewdly, intimacy without pretense, finding value in what others discard.
- What blocks it: control as comfort. When you grip — monitoring, testing, refusing to need anyone — you seal the well shut, and everything becomes effortful precisely because nothing is allowed to move.

This week, deliberately release one small thing you've been managing — a grudge, a secret, an outcome — and pay attention to the energy that comes back to you.
