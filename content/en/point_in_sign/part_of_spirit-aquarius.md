title: Part of Spirit in Aquarius
---

You act from principle, and often against the grain. Your will is roused by what could be arranged better — the rule that makes no sense, the group excluded for no reason — and you are unusually able to hold a position without needing the room to ratify it.

- Where it works: reform, systems and technology, organising, research that ignores fashion, speaking for people who are not present.
- What wastes it: opposition as identity. Defining yourself against things is a reliable way to let other people set your agenda, and detachment practised too long stops being freedom and becomes distance.

Put this month's effort into something you are *for*, concretely and with your name attached, rather than something you object to.
