title: Lilith in Taurus
---

Your untamed thing is appetite — for touch, food, rest, beauty, and the sheer animal comfort of having enough. Somewhere along the way you learned to ration it, to earn pleasure instead of simply taking it, and Lilith in Taurus is the low steady growl that refuses that bargain. When you claim your appetite openly, you become immovable in the best sense: a person whose yes and no are made of granite.

Suppressed, the appetite leaks. You overspend or overeat in secret, hoard what you won't let yourself enjoy, or cling to people and things you'd never admit to wanting. Then comes the shame, then the new austerity, then the leak again.

Notice one pleasure you routinely defer, and take it this week in the open — unhurried, and without earning it first.
