title: Part of Fortune in Aries
---

Ease finds you in motion. Your luck concentrates around beginnings — the first hour of a project, the decision made on the spot, the body moving fast enough that thought can't interfere. When you act directly, doors open with suspicious convenience; hesitation, for you, is where good fortune goes to expire.

- Where it flows: solo starts, physical effort, honest confrontation, anything where you get to go first.
- What blocks it: waiting for consensus. Every committee you convene around a decision drains the charge out of it, and over-planning converts your natural momentum into dread.

Pick one thing you've been circling and take the first concrete step today — not the whole plan, just the opening move — and notice how much lighter you feel afterward.
