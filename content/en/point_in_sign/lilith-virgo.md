title: Lilith in Virgo
---

Your untamed part refuses to be useful on demand. Lilith in Virgo was praised into service early — the capable one, the fixer, the body that behaves — and somewhere in there you learned to mistake your worth for your function. The reclaimed version is discernment with teeth: you serve what deserves you, and your no is as precise as your work.

- Your strength: when you choose your labor freely, your craft is immaculate and nobody can guilt an hour out of you.
- Your friction: suppressed, you overwork to stay beyond criticism, then resent the people you volunteered to save. The body keeps score — tension, symptoms, sudden strikes of exhaustion that force the rest you wouldn't take.

This week, decline one task you'd normally absorb, without offering a replacement or an excuse.
