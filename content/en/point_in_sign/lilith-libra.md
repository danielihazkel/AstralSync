title: Lilith in Libra
---

You were trained to be agreeable, and your wildness is the truth you keep swallowing to stay lovely. Lilith in Libra knows exactly what is unfair in the room and the moment the balance tipped — and owned, that knowing makes you a formidable advocate: graceful, exact, and impossible to wave off.

Suppressed, it turns to vapor. You agree in the meeting and dissent in the hallway, keep a private ledger of concessions, and one day the accumulated fairness debt comes out cold — a door quietly closed on someone who never heard a single complaint.

- Your strength: naming an imbalance early, beautifully, while it can still be fixed.
- Your friction: mistaking your silence for peace and your exit for a boundary.

Next time you disagree, say so in the room within the hour — kindly, and once.
