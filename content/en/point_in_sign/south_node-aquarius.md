title: South Node in Aquarius
---

The wide view is your birthright. You think in systems, spot the trend before the trend, and belong effortlessly to networks, scenes, and causes. Detachment is a genuine skill in you: you can analyze a heated situation from orbit and stay friends with people no one else can stand, because you accept humans as data and don't take their weirdness personally.

The undertow is that orbit becomes exile. You theorize your own life instead of living it, dissolve into the group's identity to avoid the exposure of a personal one, and greet your own strongest feelings like unverified reports. *Everyone* gets your loyalty; *someone* — one particular, watching, hoping someone — rarely gets your whole heart.

The frontier now is the spotlight you've been deconstructing: making something unmistakably yours and standing beside it while it's judged.

This week, tell one person something you feel about them specifically — not the group, not the idea of them — and stay for the response.
