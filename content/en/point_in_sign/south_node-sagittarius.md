title: South Node in Sagittarius
---

Meaning-making is your old magic. You arrived with a philosopher's reflex: any event, however messy, gets a framework by nightfall, and your storytelling can turn a delayed flight into a parable. Optimism, wanderlust, the teacher's fire — these are authentic gifts, and rooms are warmer for your big-sky talk.

The undertow is that the sermon writes itself before the facts arrive. Certainty is so comfortable that you round complicated people up to archetypes, skip the follow-up question, and travel far to avoid the near — the neighbor, the sibling, the detail that would complicate the doctrine. Being interesting can quietly replace being accurate.

The growth now runs through small data: listening without composing your response, learning the local instead of preaching the universal, letting *I don't know yet* finish a sentence.

In your next three conversations, count how many questions you ask — and try to make it more than the number of conclusions you offer.
