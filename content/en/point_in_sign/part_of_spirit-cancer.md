title: Part of Spirit in Cancer
---

You act on behalf of what you care for. Your will engages fully when something — a person, a place, a body of work — is in your keeping; asked to act for abstract reasons, you stall. This makes you formidable in defence of your own and oddly passive without a stake.

- Where it works: building and protecting a base, caring roles held with skill rather than martyrdom, work that has someone specific at the end of it.
- What wastes it: indirectness. When you need something you tend to create conditions in which it might be offered rather than asking, and then resent the result.

Identify one thing you want and ask for it plainly this week — no groundwork, no hinting. The discomfort is the price of being answerable.
