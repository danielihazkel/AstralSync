title: North Node in Capricorn
---

Your growth points toward the long climb: goals with deadlines, authority worn without apology, a public life you build brick by deliberate brick. Life keeps handing you situations where feelings alone won't finish the job — where someone has to be the adult, and the someone is you.

When you lean in, you discover ambition is not a betrayal of tenderness; it's a container for it. Your word becomes structural — people build on it — and the discipline you feared would harden you actually steadies everyone you love. The Cancer undertow is the friction: retreating into moods and family caves, over-nurturing others to avoid your own summit, letting *I'm not ready yet* echo through another year.

The mountain doesn't need you to stop feeling. It needs you to keep walking while you feel.

Set one deadline this week for something you've been incubating, and tell a person who will ask you about it.
