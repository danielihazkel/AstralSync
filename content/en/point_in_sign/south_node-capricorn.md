title: South Node in Capricorn
---

Responsibility fits you like an old coat. You were the child adults treated as staff, and the adult everyone assumes will handle it — the plan, the bill, the hard call. Structure is instinctive: you can run an organization, a household, a funeral, without visibly breaking stride.

- Your fluency: execution under load. Deadlines, hierarchies, worst-case logistics — you carry weight the way others carry keys.
- The undertow: the coat won't come off. You manage feelings instead of having them, parent your peers until intimacy suffocates, and measure your worth in output so strictly that a resting version of you feels like an impostor. Control becomes the tax on every tender moment.

What's undergrown is the soft interior: being fed rather than feeding the machine, belonging that isn't earned, tears that don't require a business case.

Once this week, when someone offers help, say only *thank you, yes* — no counter-offer, no reciprocal task, no supervising how they do it.
