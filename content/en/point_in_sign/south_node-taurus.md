title: South Node in Taurus
---

Comfort-building is effortless for you. You know how to make money hold still, how to cook a room into calm, how to keep a life stocked, soft, and predictable. People underestimate this gift until they need it — then your pantry, your steadiness, and your refusal to panic look like genius.

- Your fluency: you can stabilize anything. Given a mess, you produce a routine; given scarcity, you produce reserves.
- The undertow: stability curdles into entrenchment. You keep the job, the couch, and the opinion long past their expiry because change registers in your body as danger, and you'd rather be comfortable than transformed.

The deeper invitation is intimacy with what can't be owned — other people's inner worlds, shared risk, your own buried intensity. Those don't come with receipts.

Say yes this month to one experience that costs you comfort rather than money, and stay through the awkward middle of it.
