title: Part of Fortune in Pisces
---

Your fortune arrives on the tide, not the timetable. Ease finds you when you loosen your grip — the answer that surfaces in the shower, the stranger's kindness at the exact low point, the creative work that makes itself once you stop supervising. Solitude, water, music, and sleep are not escapes for you; they are where your luck does its filing.

- Where it flows: imagination, compassion, art, rest, helping without keeping score, trusting the current when the map runs out.
- What blocks it: forcing. Rigid schedules and white-knuckled effort scatter exactly the sensitivity your fortune depends on — and so does numbing out, which mimics surrender but delivers none of it.

Once this week, leave a real gap in your day — no input, no task — and let whatever has been waiting for the silence come find you.
