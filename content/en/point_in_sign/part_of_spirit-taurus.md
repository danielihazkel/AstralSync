title: Part of Spirit in Taurus
---

You exert yourself through steadiness. Your will is not dramatic — it is cumulative, and it wins by refusing to stop. What you decide to build tends to get built, provided you are allowed to do it at your own tempo and with materials you can actually touch.

- Where it works: long projects, craft and skill, building something that holds its value, patient negotiation, staying put while others churn.
- What wastes it: comfort mistaken for purpose. The same steadiness that finishes a decade-long project will keep you in a situation years past its usefulness, because leaving costs more effort than staying.

Ask of one commitment you are maintaining: am I building this, or only holding it? If it is the second, set a date to put it down.
