title: Part of Spirit in Aries
---

Your deliberate power is initiative. Where Fortune describes what happens to you, Spirit describes what you *do* about it — and you do it by starting. Intention becomes real for you at the moment of commitment, not before; you think best once you are already moving, and plans made in the abstract tend to die there.

- Where it works: declaring intent out loud, taking the first risk, work that rewards nerve, competing against your own previous best.
- What wastes it: deliberation as a substitute for choosing. You mistake the discomfort of an unmade decision for evidence that you need more information, when you almost always need less.

Name one thing you intend this year, tell one person, and do the smallest irreversible piece of it this week.
