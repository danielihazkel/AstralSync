title: North Node in Aries
---

Your growth points toward wanting things out loud. Life keeps engineering situations where no committee will form, no partner will decide for you, and the only way through is to say *I want this* and move. It feels rude at first. It is actually the curriculum.

- Your strength, when you lean in: decisions get faster and cleaner, and people discover they can trust you precisely because you stop managing their reactions.
- Your friction: the Libra undertow. Under stress you poll the room, split the difference, and call the resulting mush "compromise" — then quietly resent everyone involved.

The stretch is not aggression; it is authorship. Anger, here, is often just a want that waited too long to be spoken.

This week, make one decision that affects only you without consulting anyone — and notice that the sky holds.
