title: Part of Fortune in Sagittarius
---

Your luck has a longer stride than you do. Fortune reaches you through distance — the trip that rearranges your assumptions, the teacher from another tradition, the big question you follow past the edge of the familiar. Meaning is your fuel: when life feels like it's *about* something, everything else gets easier, including the hard parts.

- Where it flows: travel, study, teaching, publishing your thinking, betting on possibility while others hedge.
- What blocks it: the shrunken map. When fear or routine keeps your world small — same commute, same opinions, same five people — your natural buoyancy deflates and restlessness turns sour.

This month, put one genuinely unfamiliar thing on the calendar — a place, a class, a cuisine, a viewpoint you'd normally argue with — and go toward it on purpose.
