title: Lilith in Sagittarius
---

Your heresy is the point. Lilith in Sagittarius refuses inherited doctrine — the family religion, the company line, the culture's script for a meaningful life — and insists on beliefs you have road-tested yourself. Owned, this makes you the honest traveler: you would rather hold one true paragraph than a borrowed philosophy, and your candor gives other people the nerve to question theirs.

Suppressed, the refusal goes sour. You defer to authorities you privately mock, or swing the other way — turning your escape route into a new dogma and preaching freedom at people who didn't ask. Restlessness stands in for conviction: another trip, another framework, anything but committing to what you actually believe.

Write down one belief you hold because someone handed it to you, and test it against your own experience this month.
