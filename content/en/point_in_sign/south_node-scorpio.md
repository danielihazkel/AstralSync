title: South Node in Scorpio
---

Depth is your default. You read subtext like large print, sense the secret in the room, and stay functional in crises that flatten everyone else. Intensity doesn't scare you — it orients you, and people bring you their darkest material because you demonstrably don't flinch.

- Your fluency: transformation. You know how endings work, how power moves, how to survive the loss of everything but your nerve.
- The undertow: you keep booking the storm. Calm reads as suspicious, so you probe for hidden agendas that aren't there, test loyalties nobody knew they were taking, and tie your fortunes to other people's resources and dramas because self-generated stability feels almost naive.

The unglamorous frontier is peace: your own money, your own garden, pleasure that doesn't require a crisis to justify it. Building slowly is now braver than burning down.

This week, when nothing is wrong, practice believing it — take the quiet evening at face value instead of interrogating it.
