title: Part of Spirit in Pisces
---

Your agency is subtle and rarely direct. You act by attunement — sensing where something wants to go and moving with it — and force applied head-on yields less for you than patience and indirection. This looks like passivity from outside and very often is not.

- Where it works: creative and contemplative work, healing roles, anything requiring you to feel a situation before analysing it, service given by choice rather than reflex.
- What wastes it: boundarylessness. Without a clear line between your intention and everyone else's needs your effort disperses into other people's lives, and you end the year exhausted with nothing of your own to show.

Protect one block of time this week for something that is yours alone, and treat it as no more negotiable than an appointment with someone else.
