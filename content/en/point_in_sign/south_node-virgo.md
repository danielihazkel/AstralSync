title: South Node in Virgo
---

Precision is your inheritance. You see the flaw in the plan, the typo in the contract, the vitamin missing from the diet — and you fix things with a quiet competence that whole workplaces come to depend on. Analysis, routine, service: these run in you like breathing.

- Your fluency: you make systems work. Chaos, handed to you, comes back labeled and functional.
- The undertow: the audit never ends. You proofread your own life until spontaneity is edited out, treat rest as a scheduling error, and offer critique when what was asked for was company. Worry masquerades as diligence, and being useful becomes the toll you pay to feel allowed at the table.

What's calling now is the unfixable: awe, flow, trust in a current larger than your checklist. You can't earn grace by improving; you can only let it in.

Once this week, do something purely because it moves you — music, water, wandering — and refuse to make it productive.
