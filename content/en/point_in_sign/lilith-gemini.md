title: Lilith in Gemini
---

The thing you were taught to suppress is your tongue. Lilith in Gemini carries the questions polite rooms don't want asked and the observations that name what everyone is pretending not to see. When you own it, you're the person whose one sentence reframes the whole meeting — curious, quick, and unafraid of the awkward follow-up.

- Your strength: heretical curiosity. You can say the unsayable with enough lightness that it lands as truth instead of attack.
- Your friction: silenced, the voice goes crooked — gossip, sarcasm, the barbed joke that says sideways what you wouldn't say straight. Afterward you feel exposed and vow to be quieter, which only builds pressure for the next barb.

Once this week, ask the direct question out loud in the room instead of speculating about it afterward.
