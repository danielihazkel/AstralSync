title: North Node in Sagittarius
---

Your growth points toward the horizon and the hunch. Life keeps inviting you to commit to a direction before you've read every review — a journey, a belief, a teacher, a leap of meaning — and to trust the pattern instead of drowning in the data points.

- Your strength, when you lean in: conviction. You become someone with an actual worldview, capable of inspiring others because you finally picked a road and walked it far enough to report back.
- Your friction: the Gemini undertow. You gather one more opinion, keep every option open, stay clever and noncommittal at the surface of ten subjects — and mistake the buzz of information for the nourishment of understanding.

Not every voice deserves a vote. Your gut has been outvoted by your inbox for too long.

Next time you catch yourself researching past the point of usefulness, close the tabs and act on what you already believe.
