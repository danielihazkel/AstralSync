title: Part of Fortune in Gemini
---

Luck reaches you through language. The chance conversation, the forwarded article, the acquaintance of an acquaintance — your fortune travels along wires of talk and curiosity, and the more lines you keep open, the more often it rings. Variety isn't a distraction for you; it's the delivery system.

- Where it flows: asking questions, writing things down, short trips, learning two things at once, introducing people who should know each other.
- What blocks it: the fantasy of the one perfect focus. When you force yourself into monkish single-tasking because someone said you should, your energy flattens and the lucky coincidences stop arriving.

This week, follow one tangent on purpose — the odd link, the unfamiliar topic, the stranger's recommendation — and see where the thread actually leads.
