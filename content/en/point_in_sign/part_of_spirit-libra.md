title: Part of Spirit in Libra
---

You act through relationship. Your deliberate power is in the alliance, the agreement, the fair arrangement — and decisions taken alone tend to feel provisional until someone has been consulted. Done well this is real diplomatic skill; done badly it is outsourcing.

- Where it works: partnership of every kind, negotiation, design, arbitration, work needing someone who can hold two positions without collapsing either.
- What wastes it: deferral dressed as fairness. Waiting to see what the other person wants is not balance, and the resentment that follows is the bill for a choice you declined to make.

Take one decision this week entirely on your own judgement, and tell the relevant person what you decided rather than asking what they think.
