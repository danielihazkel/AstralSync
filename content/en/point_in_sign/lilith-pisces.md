title: Lilith in Pisces
---

Your untamed part is longing itself — for merger, for meaning, for the oceanic thing ordinary life keeps promising and withholding. Lilith in Pisces was told that sensitivity this deep was impractical, so you learned to numb it or route it through fantasy and rescue. Owned, it is a clean mystic streak: you feel what a room won't admit, and your compassion carries no small print.

Suppressed, the longing goes looking for containers. Escapes that blur more than they soothe, lovers cast as saviors, causes you dissolve into until there is no one left to have wanted anything. Then the shame of drifting, then a stricter numbness, then the undertow again.

- Your strength: unarmored empathy that makes suffering people feel accurately seen.
- Your friction: choosing oblivion over asking, in plain words, for what would actually move you.

Once this week, name the longing directly to one person — what you miss, what you want — instead of drifting it away.
