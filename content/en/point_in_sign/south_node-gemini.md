title: South Node in Gemini
---

Words obey you. You were the kid who could talk their way out of anything, and the adult version can reframe, banter, and explain in real time — a broker of information who always knows a little about whatever the table is discussing. Genuine gift: you make ideas travel.

The undertow is that fluency lets you skate. Two clever sentences can end a conversation your heart needed to go deeper; a well-timed joke can deflect a question you've been dodging for years. You collect facts the way some people collect souvenirs — proof of places your convictions never actually lived. Busy-mindedness becomes its own traffic jam, and the signal drowns in your own chatter.

What's underdeveloped is the long arc: one philosophy tested by one journey, meaning that costs something. Your next chapter is written in commitments, not comments.

Choose one question you keep dancing around and give it a whole quiet hour — no browsing, no wordplay, just you and the answer forming slowly.
