title: Part of Spirit in Scorpio
---

Your will is concentrated and strategic. You act by committing completely to a small number of things and seeing them through long past the point where interest alone would carry anyone. You are also willing to look at what a situation actually is, which is rarer than it sounds and is most of your advantage.

- Where it works: work requiring depth and discretion, research, crisis, anything where the official story and the real one differ, long campaigns.
- What wastes it: secrecy as reflex. Withholding is a genuine instrument, but used by default it isolates you — and a strategy nobody else can see is a strategy nobody else can help with.

Tell one person what you are actually working toward, not the version you usually give, and let them make it better.
