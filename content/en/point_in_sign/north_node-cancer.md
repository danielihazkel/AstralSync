title: North Node in Cancer
---

Your growth points toward the undefended life: feeling things in front of witnesses, tending a home like it matters, letting people take care of you without an exchange rate. Life keeps arranging moments where competence won't help and only presence will — a grieving friend, a sick parent, your own tiredness refusing to be scheduled away.

When you lean in, you discover a strength the resume never listed: people heal in your kitchen. Your attention, unarmored, is nourishing in a way your achievements never were. The Capricorn undertow is the friction — under pressure you revert to management mode, turning feelings into projects, family into logistics, and rest into a reward you haven't earned yet.

Needing people is the skill being trained here. It will feel like failure long before it feels like intimacy.

Tell one person this week how you actually feel, before you've turned it into a plan.
