title: South Node in Pisces
---

The invisible world leaks into you naturally. Empathy, imagery, atmosphere — you absorb them without trying, dream vividly, and comfort suffering people simply by not requiring them to explain. Artists and mystics work decades for the porousness you were issued at birth.

- Your fluency: dissolving boundaries. You merge with music, moods, and other people's pain, and your compassion has no entry fee.
- The undertow: the fog rolls inward. Deadlines blur, help arrives everywhere but toward yourself, and escape — sleep, screens, romanticizing, one more rescue mission — keeps outbidding the concrete task on your desk. Feeling everything can become a sophisticated way of doing nothing in particular.

The growth is granular now: a schedule kept, a body maintained, one skill sharpened until your compassion has competent hands. Precision won't shrink your soul; it will give it a spine.

Tonight, write tomorrow's three-item list — small, physical, finishable — and let completing it count as a spiritual practice.
