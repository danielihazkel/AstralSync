title: Part of Spirit in Gemini
---

Your agency runs through language and connection. You act by finding out, explaining, introducing, translating — and your most effective moves are usually conversations. Where others need a plan, you need the right two facts and someone to think alongside.

- Where it works: learning in public, writing, teaching, brokering between people who cannot hear each other, work with genuine variety in it.
- What wastes it: motion mistaken for progress. You can spend a year extremely busy across nine interests and arrive nowhere, because nothing was pursued long enough to compound.

Choose one current interest and give it a month of exclusive attention. Notice whether the constraint feels like a cage or a relief.
