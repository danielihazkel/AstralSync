title: Lilith in Aquarius
---

Your wildness is your otherness. Lilith in Aquarius was taught that fitting in was the price of belonging, and never quite managed to pay it — the odd angle in your thinking kept showing through. Owned, that angle is your value: you see the group's blind spot precisely because you were never fully absorbed into the group.

Rejected, it becomes exile as identity. You leave before you can be left, dismiss communities as conformist from a safe distance, or perform an approved eccentricity while hiding the genuinely strange thing you actually think. The loneliness gets renamed independence.

- Your strength: dissent that improves the room — you say the unpopular, accurate thing.
- Your friction: confusing detachment with freedom, and pre-rejecting people who might have taken you whole.

Share one unedited opinion with a group you care about this week, and stay in the room afterward.
