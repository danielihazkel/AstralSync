title: North Node in Scorpio
---

Your growth points toward the depths you've been decorating around. Life keeps presenting situations that can't be handled by acquiring more — more savings, more comfort, more of the same — and can only be handled by merging: shared finances, real intimacy, grief metabolized instead of managed, help accepted from another person's hands.

When you lean in, you develop a strength few people have: you can sit in the burning room. Crises stop scattering you; you become the one others trust with the unsayable, and transformation stops feeling like theft. The Taurus undertow is the friction — clinging to possessions, routines, and a version of security that is really just stasis, choosing the known discomfort over the unknown depth every single time.

What you own can start owning you. Letting go is the wealth this node pays in.

This month, release one thing you keep for safety rather than love — an object, a grudge, a routine — and watch what fills the space.
