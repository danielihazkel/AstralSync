title: Lilith in Capricorn
---

Your untamed part wants authority — real say over your own life and work — and somewhere you learned that ambition in your hands was presumptuous. Lilith in Capricorn spots illegitimate power instantly: the boss who rules by fear, the rule that exists to protect the rule-maker. Owned, that radar joined to your discipline makes you the one who builds better structures instead of merely surviving bad ones.

Suppressed, it splits you. You over-comply, becoming the most reliable person in a system you resent, or you quietly undermine leaders you could simply outgrow. Either way you postpone your own claim, waiting for a permission slip no institution will ever issue.

Take one decision this week that you usually route through someone else's approval, and make it yourself — on the record.
