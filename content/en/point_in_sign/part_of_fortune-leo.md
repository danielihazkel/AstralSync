title: Part of Fortune in Leo
---

Ease arrives when you stop hiding. Your luck is heliotropic — it turns toward whatever lets you create, perform, play, or put your name on the work. Opportunities have a way of appearing right after you've been visibly, unapologetically yourself, as if the world had been waiting for a clear signal to know what to send you.

- Where it flows: making things, leading warmly, games and celebrations, generous acts done in the open rather than anonymously.
- What blocks it: pre-emptive modesty. When you shrink to seem humble, declining credit and muting your delight, you also mute the beacon your fortune navigates by.

Sometime this week, share one thing you made — a sentence, a meal, a song, a fix — with an audience of at least one, and let yourself enjoy their enjoyment.
