title: Part of Fortune in Capricorn
---

Ease, for you, is earned altitude. Your fortune compounds through structure — the long apprenticeship, the reputation built brick by brick, the mountain climbed at a pace you can sustain for decades. Where others need luck to strike, yours accrues: responsibility taken on early tends to pay out later with interest.

- Where it flows: mastery over time, leadership you grew into, institutions and traditions you've made your own, finishing what you start.
- What blocks it: grimness. When you decide that anything enjoyable must be unearned, you turn your own reward system against yourself and work becomes a debt that can never be paid down.

At the end of this week, write down one thing you completed — however small — and let yourself feel finished for a full minute before planning what's next.
