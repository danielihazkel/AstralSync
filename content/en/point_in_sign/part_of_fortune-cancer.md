title: Part of Fortune in Cancer
---

Your fortune has a home address. Ease pools wherever you feel safe enough to soften — the kitchen full of people you love, the familiar chair, the small circle that knows your history and keeps it kindly. Caring for someone or something is not a drain on you; done freely, it is the very activity that refills you.

- Where it flows: tending, feeding, hosting, remembering; work that lets feeling count as information.
- What blocks it: armoring up. When you decide that needing comfort is weakness, you exile yourself from your own supply line, and the world starts to feel unaccountably hostile.

This week, invest one hour in your actual nest — cook something slow, fix the corner that bothers you, invite the friend who feels like family — and treat it as strategy, not indulgence.
