title: Part of Fortune in Libra
---

Luck comes to you in pairs. Your best outcomes are co-authored — the collaborator who supplies what you lack, the introduction that changes everything, the negotiation where both sides leave lighter. Beauty is functional for you as well: a harmonious room or a well-set table genuinely restores your capacity to think.

- Where it flows: partnership, diplomacy, matchmaking of every kind, making environments and agreements more graceful.
- What blocks it: peace bought with silence. When you agree in order to avoid friction, you poison the very relationships that carry your fortune, and resentment starts to redecorate everything.

The next time you feel yourself about to say *whatever you prefer*, pause and state an actual preference — kindly, plainly — and notice that the relationship survives, and usually improves.
