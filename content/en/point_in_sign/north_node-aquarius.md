title: North Node in Aquarius
---

Your growth points toward the wide shot: the team, the cause, the future that needs your gifts more than your ego does. Life keeps pulling you out of the spotlight and into the ensemble — collaborations where the outcome matters more than the credit, friendships among equals rather than audiences.

- Your strength, when you lean in: vision. You see what a group could become and you free people, including yourself, from the exhausting job of being impressive.
- Your friction: the Leo undertow. When attention thins, you make the meeting about you, take the cause's setbacks as personal insults, and confuse being adored with being useful.

Your originality doesn't shrink in a crowd — it finally gets amplified. The applause you're weaning off was always smaller than the impact you're headed toward.

Contribute to something this week where your name won't appear, and notice how it lands in your body.
