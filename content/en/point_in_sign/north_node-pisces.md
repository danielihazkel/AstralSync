title: North Node in Pisces
---

Your growth points toward surrender — the competent person's least favorite word. Life keeps arranging outcomes your systems can't control: illnesses that ignore the protocol, griefs without checklists, moments of beauty that arrive only when you stop optimizing and simply attend.

When you lean in, you gain access to a strength efficiency never offered: trust. Imagination, rest, compassion without an improvement plan — these begin to solve problems your analysis only tightened. The Virgo undertow is the friction: perfectionism as anxiety management, criticizing yourself and others down to the molecule, redoing the list at midnight because uncertainty feels like a personal failing.

Not everything broken is yours to fix, and not everything unfixed is broken. Some things ripen; they don't get corrected.

Once this week, leave a small imperfection alone on purpose — the typo, the crooked frame — and let the discomfort pass through instead of acting on it.
