title: North Node in Gemini
---

Your growth points toward curiosity without a verdict. Life keeps handing you conversations, neighbors, siblings, small facts — invitations to ask questions you don't already know the answers to. The stretch is staying in the inquiry: gathering details, hearing the other side, letting a topic stay open past the point where certainty would be comfortable.

- Your strength, when you lean in: you become a translator — someone who can meet people inside their own vocabulary, and whose questions make others smarter.
- Your friction: the Sagittarius undertow. You leap to the big conclusion, preach it with conviction, and stop collecting evidence the moment a philosophy feels sturdy enough to stand on.

The local, specific, and unfinished is where your luck lives now — not the distant summit of truth.

In your next disagreement, ask two genuine questions before offering any opinion at all.
