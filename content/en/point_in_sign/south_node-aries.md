title: South Node in Aries
---

You arrive already knowing how to fight. Initiative is your mother tongue: you can start from zero, survive alone, and make a decision in the time it takes others to open the discussion. This is real mastery — nobody has to teach you courage, and in an emergency you are worth three committees.

The undertow is that the warrior reflex fires everywhere. Every negotiation becomes a contest, every request for patience feels like a leash, and going it alone stops being a capability and becomes a policy. You win rooms you didn't need to win and stand solitary in the wreckage of collaborations that only wanted a little yielding from you.

Your fluency isn't the problem; its monopoly is. Speed and self-reliance are savings to spend on partnership, not walls to live behind.

Once this week, let someone else's slower plan lead — fully, without a rescue — and study what their pace produces.
