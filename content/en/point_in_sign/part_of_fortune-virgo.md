title: Part of Fortune in Virgo
---

Your good fortune wears work clothes. Ease comes to you through usefulness — the schedule that finally works, the process refined until it hums, the small daily practice that quietly transforms a year. You feel best not after grand events but after a morning of absorbed, competent effort where every tool was where you left it.

- Where it flows: fixing, sorting, tending health, mastering craft details, being genuinely helpful in specific ways.
- What blocks it: perfection as a precondition. When nothing may begin until conditions are flawless, your favorite medicine — steady improvement — becomes unreachable, and worry moves into the space where satisfaction should live.

Choose one small system in your life that irritates you daily and spend twenty minutes improving it, not perfecting it — then let *better* be the win.
