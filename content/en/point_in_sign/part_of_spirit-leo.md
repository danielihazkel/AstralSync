title: Part of Spirit in Leo
---

Your intention becomes real when you put your name on it. You act wholeheartedly or not at all, and the work you sign — visibly, personally, at risk of looking foolish — is the work that carries your force. Anonymous effort quietly drains you.

- Where it works: creative authorship, leading from the front, performance in any sense, mentoring someone who is watching you closely.
- What wastes it: needing the response before you begin. When approval becomes the point rather than the by-product you start making the safer, smaller thing, and the generosity that is your real strength curdles into performance.

Make one thing this month purely to your own standard and show it to exactly one person. Notice how much of your investment was in the making and how much in the reception.
