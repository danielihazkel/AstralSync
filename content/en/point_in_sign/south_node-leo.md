title: South Node in Leo
---

Charisma came pre-installed. You know how to hold a room, tell the story, be the birthday around which the year rotates. Performance is not vanity in you — it's craft, and your instinct for drama, timing, and generosity of presence is the real thing.

The undertow is that the throne gets heavy. When every gathering needs your sparkle to succeed, rest starts to feel like disappearing, and you begin auditioning even for the people who already love you. Criticism lands like exile; the group's project quietly becomes a set for your scenes. The applause you can always earn crowds out the belonging you haven't yet learned to trust.

The frontier is peership: mattering as one voice among many, useful to a future that won't know your name. Your creativity is meant to pollinate causes now, not just performances.

At your next gathering, ask three questions for every story you tell, and let someone else's moment stay theirs.
