title: Part of Fortune in Aquarius
---

Your luck is networked. Fortune reaches you sideways, through the group — the community that funds the idea, the friend-of-a-friend with exactly the missing piece, the collective project that lifts everyone including you. You are also replenished by the future itself: imagining what could exist recharges you the way nostalgia recharges others.

- Where it flows: collaboration among equals, causes bigger than your own outcome, experiments, technology, the odd angle nobody else tried.
- What blocks it: standing apart on principle. When detachment hardens into superiority — *no one gets it, so why join* — you cut yourself off from the very web your good fortune travels through.

This week, contribute one idea or hour to a group effort with no personal payoff attached, and watch what circles back to you from an unexpected direction.
