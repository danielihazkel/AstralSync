title: Mutable Dominant
essence: a talent for bending with events until they reveal their use
---

With most of your planets in mutable signs, your chart is tuned to transitions. You read shifting conditions early and adjust without ceremony — new plan, new city, new framing of the same old problem. Where others need the ground to stop moving before they can act, you treat motion itself as workable terrain.

That flexibility makes you invaluable in any situation that refuses to go according to plan, which is most of them. The friction is shapelessness: endlessly adaptable people can defer so often — to the room, to the moment, to whoever spoke last — that their own position becomes hard to find, even for themselves. Options multiply while commitments starve.

Adaptation is your gift; declaration is your practice. Once a day, state a preference out loud before hearing anyone else's, and let it stand unedited for an hour.
