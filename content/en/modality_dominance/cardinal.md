title: Cardinal Dominant
essence: an instinct to open new ground before anyone asks
---

With most of your planets in cardinal signs, your chart is tilted toward beginnings. You see the gap before others admit it exists, and your first response to any stuck situation is to move — start the conversation, draft the plan, book the ticket. Waiting for permission feels less like patience and more like decay.

That initiative makes you the person a room reorganizes around. Projects, trips, and reconciliations often exist because you pushed the first domino. The friction is follow-through: once a thing is truly launched and merely needs tending, your attention starts scanning the horizon for the next opening, leaving half-built structures behind you for other people to finish or mourn.

Starting is your gift; finishing is your practice. Pick one stalled thing you began, and give it a real ending — completed or formally closed — before you open anything new.
