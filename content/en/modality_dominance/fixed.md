title: Fixed Dominant
essence: a loyalty to what you have built that outlasts every mood
---

With most of your planets in fixed signs, your chart is built for the middle of the story — the long, unglamorous stretch where things either get finished or quietly abandoned. You commit late but completely. Once a person, project, or belief has earned its place, you hold it through weather that would scatter most people.

That constancy is why others build on you: your yes still means yes in month eleven. The friction is that holding can become gripping. You may defend a routine, a grudge, or a plan long after the reasons for it have expired, treating any change as a personal defeat rather than new information.

Endurance is your gift; release is your practice. Name one thing you are still holding purely because letting go feels like losing, and test what actually happens when you loosen your hand.
