title: An 11th-house year
---

This year runs on allies. Friends, communities, professional networks, patrons, and shared causes come forward, along with the 11th house's older name: the place of good fortune and of hopes. Help arrives through people who like you, groups you join now can shape years ahead, and long-held aspirations get a genuine window to advance.

The strength is support — this is a year when asking for introductions, joining the association, or showing up for your people pays off visibly. The caution is the crowd's gravity: drifting with a group's agenda instead of your own, collecting contacts instead of friendships, or waiting for luck that only moves when you engage it. Audit who actually shares your hopes.

The year lord colors the quality of these connections, so weigh its condition and notice its transits when alliances form or fray. Name one aspiration plainly, tell the right three people about it, and let the network do what networks do.
