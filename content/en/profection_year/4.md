title: A 4th-house year
---

This year pulls you toward the foundations: home, family, land, lineage, and the private life that everything else stands on. Moves, renovations, family negotiations, and questions of where you actually belong tend to surface now. Even if nothing outward changes, the inner work of settling — making peace with parents, ancestors, or your own history — belongs to this year.

The gift is rootedness. Time spent securing your base, literally or emotionally, repays you for years. The caution is retreat that curdles into avoidance: hiding at home while public matters stall, or letting old family patterns replay unexamined. Career may feel quieter this year; that is the cycle, not a failure.

The year lord describes how the home front develops, so weigh its condition and watch its transits around moves and family turning points. Fix one thing about your foundation — a lease, a room, a relationship with kin — and let that be the year's achievement.
