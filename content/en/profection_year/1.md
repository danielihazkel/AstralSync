title: A 1st-house year
---

This year turns the spotlight back onto you — your body, your appearance, your name, your sense of who you are becoming. The twelve-year cycle begins again here, so the choices you make now tend to echo through the years that follow. It is a strong year for reinventing how you show up: new habits, a new look, a clearer story about yourself.

The gift is momentum and self-definition. The friction is self-absorption: with so much energy pointed inward, it is easy to take everything personally or to overhaul your life faster than your relationships can adjust. Health and vitality also come into focus, for better or worse, so the body deserves real attention rather than lip service.

Watch the year lord closely — its condition in your chart and its transits through the year describe how this fresh start actually unfolds. Begin one thing that genuinely represents you, and tend it all year.
