title: A 2nd-house year
---

This year foregrounds livelihood: money earned, resources gathered, and the quieter question of what you believe you are worth. Financial matters that drifted along on autopilot now ask for real decisions — pricing your work, renegotiating pay, building savings, or finally looking at the numbers honestly.

The strength of a year like this is consolidation. Steady effort compounds, and practical skills you sharpen now keep paying you long after the year ends. The caution is scarcity thinking: clutching too tightly, undercharging out of doubt, or measuring yourself purely by income. Self-worth is the deeper subject here, and money is just its most visible mirror.

The year lord shows how the income story develops, so note its condition natally and follow its transits for the timing of gains and squeezes. Pick one concrete financial goal, put a number and a date on it, and review it each season.
