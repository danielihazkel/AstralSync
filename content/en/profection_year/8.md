title: An 8th-house year
---

This year takes you into shared depths: other people's money and what it costs to accept it. Loans, taxes, inheritance, a partner's finances, debts owed and collected — the entanglements of resources come due for attention. So do the heavier themes the 8th house holds: loss, grief, mortality, and the psychological material most years let you ignore.

The strength of this year is depth and honest accounting. Debts restructured, estates settled, therapy begun, or intimacy deepened past politeness — these are its real victories, and they change you. The caution is dread and entanglement: catastrophizing normal fears, or signing financial agreements that quietly trade away your independence. Read every document twice.

The year lord shows how these exchanges unfold, so check its condition and follow its transits around settlements and turning points. Face one buried thing deliberately — a debt, a fear, a conversation about endings — and let facing it be enough.
