title: A 3rd-house year
---

This year runs on words, errands, and the people just next door. Siblings, cousins, neighbors, and daily contacts move toward the center of your story, and so does everything you write, say, study, and sign. Short trips multiply. A course, a certification, or a writing project begun now tends to fit the year's grain.

The emphasis is connection and quick learning: your mind is busier, your calendar fuller, your messages more consequential than usual. The friction is scatter — a hundred small commitments can shred your attention, and careless speech or an unread contract can cost more than it should this year. Say less, mean more, and read the fine print.

Since the year lord colors how all this communication flows, check its condition and track its transits for the weeks when words carry extra weight. Choose one skill or conversation that matters and give it consistent, scheduled attention.
