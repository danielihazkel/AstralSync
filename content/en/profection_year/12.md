title: A 12th-house year
---

This year closes the twelve-year cycle, and it works best when treated as an ending on purpose. The 12th house governs retreat, solitude, hidden matters, institutions, and the quiet unwinding that precedes renewal. Energy often runs lower; life pulls inward toward rest, reflection, archives, dreams, and the unfinished business of the past decade.

The gift is release: this is an excellent year for finishing what lingers, forgiving old debts of every kind, working behind the scenes, and letting obsolete identities dissolve before next year's fresh start. The friction is self-undoing — isolation that turns bleak, escapist habits, or sabotaging yourself just as a chapter tries to close cleanly. Hidden costs and quiet adversaries reward a calm, honest look rather than avoidance.

The year lord shows how this dissolving proceeds, so mind its condition and give yourself slack during its harder transits. Schedule real rest, end one thing completely, and leave room for what begins next year.
