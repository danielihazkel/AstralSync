title: A 7th-house year
---

This year happens in the mirror of other people. Partnership takes the lead — marriage and committed relationships, business partners, close collaborators, and also open rivals and anyone sitting across a negotiating table. Significant meetings, engagements, contracts, and reckonings within existing bonds all fit the year's shape.

The gift is relatedness: you learn faster about yourself through others now than through solitary effort, and alliances formed this year can be durable. The friction is the loss of your own center — over-accommodating, outsourcing decisions, or discovering that a conflict you avoided has organized itself into an opponent. Disagreement handled directly is part of the work, not a failure of it.

The year lord describes how these encounters tend to go, so weigh its condition and track its transits around commitments and disputes. Show up fully in one key relationship — negotiate honestly, name what you need — and let that be this year's project.
