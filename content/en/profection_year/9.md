title: A 9th-house year
---

This year widens the horizon. Long journeys, higher education, publishing, teaching, law, and questions of belief and meaning all belong to the 9th house, and one or more of them will likely knock. Travel that changes you, a degree or serious course of study, a book or body of ideas put into the world — these fit the year's current.

The emphasis is growth through distance: physically, intellectually, or spiritually, you see your life from further away and understand it better. The friction is overreach and dogma — promising more than you can deliver, romanticizing the far-off while neglecting the nearby, or mistaking a new opinion for a settled truth. Legal matters, if they arise, reward preparation over bravado.

The year lord shapes how these ventures land, so consider its condition and time major departures and submissions by its transits. Pick one horizon — a place, a subject, a question of faith — and travel toward it deliberately all year.
