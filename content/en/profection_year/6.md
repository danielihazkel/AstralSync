title: A 6th-house year
---

This year is about maintenance: the daily work, the body's upkeep, the systems that keep your life running. Workload often increases, health asks for honest attention, and routines — sleep, food, exercise, the shape of an ordinary Tuesday — become the real levers of change. Pets and the people you serve or employ can also feature.

The strength here is repair. Small, repeated corrections made this year fix problems that grand gestures never touch, and disciplined service builds a reputation for reliability. The friction is grind: overwork, anxiety about imperfections, or ignoring the body's signals until they get loud. This is a year to respect limits, not to test them.

Because the year lord colors both work and health, note its condition and watch its transits when scheduling demanding stretches or medical care. Choose one routine to rebuild from the ground up, and let consistency, not intensity, be your measure of success.
