title: A 10th-house year
---

This year puts your public life on stage. Career, reputation, rank, and your dealings with authority — bosses, institutions, the audience that judges your work — become the organizing themes. Promotions, launches, title changes, and moments of visibility tend to cluster in a year profected here, and effort invested in your professional standing compounds unusually well.

The gift is traction: doors that were stuck can open, and ambition finds a legitimate outlet. The friction is exposure — the same visibility that rewards good work magnifies mistakes, and overreaching for status can strain health, home, and the people who hold your private life together. What you neglect at the base shows up at the summit.

The year lord tells you how the climb goes, so study its condition and schedule big professional moves around its supportive transits. Define what advancement actually means to you, name one public goal, and pursue it steadily rather than loudly.
