title: A 5th-house year
---

This year invites you to make something and enjoy it. Creative projects, romance, children, games, performance, and honest pleasure all belong to the 5th house, and a year profected here tends to warm up whichever of those is live for you. Art started now carries the year's vitality; matters involving children, your own or the idea of them, often move as well.

The emphasis is joy that produces something — the play that turns into a body of work, the fling that turns serious, the hobby that becomes a signature. The caution is indulgence and drama: speculation, overspending on fun, or chasing attention instead of expression. Pleasure is the theme, not the excuse.

The year lord shows how freely this creative current runs, so check its condition and follow its transits around openings and romances. Commit to one creative practice on a schedule, and let delight be a discipline this year.
