title: Yod
---

Two planets in comfortable contact both strain toward a distant third, meeting at an awkward angle that never quite settles. The apex planet lives with a persistent itch — a sense of being aimed at something it cannot see, adjusted and readjusted by forces that do not speak its language. Tradition calls this the finger of fate, and it does tend to mark a life theme that chooses you rather than the reverse.

- Your strength: uncommon calibration. All that adjusting makes the apex part of you unusually precise, capable of work that requires constant fine-tuning where blunter instruments fail.
- Your friction: restlessness without a map. Because the tension resolves through timing rather than effort, you can spend years forcing doors that only open on their own schedule, and the apex never feels finished.

When the familiar itch returns this week, write down what it seems to be pointing at instead of immediately acting — the yod rewards observation before movement.
