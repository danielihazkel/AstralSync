title: T-Square
---

Two planets pull against each other, and a third — the apex — takes the strain of both. This is an engine, not an ornament. The tension never fully resolves, so it converts into drive: you get more done, push harder, and recover faster than people whose charts let them rest. Most visible achievement carries a T-square somewhere behind it.

- Your strength: productive restlessness. Pressure that would flatten others becomes your fuel, and you instinctively move toward problems instead of away from them.
- Your friction: the apex overworks. One part of your life absorbs every conflict the other two generate, and it can burn out, act out, or harden into compulsion when you never let it off duty.

The open side of the pattern — the empty space opposite the apex — is where relief lives. Once this week, when the familiar pressure spikes, try doing the opposite of your reflex and see what the strain actually wanted.
