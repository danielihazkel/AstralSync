title: Grand Trine
---

Three planets in easy, flowing contact form a closed circuit of talent. Something in you simply works — a gift that runs on its own current, needing no push, no crisis, no permission. Others struggle to build what you were handed assembled, and in a good year this pattern feels like being carried.

The catch is that ease teaches nothing. Because the circuit is closed, the gift can idle for decades, entertaining you privately while producing nothing the world ever sees. You may not even name it as a talent, since it never cost you anything, and what costs nothing is easy to leave unspent.

- Your strength: natural fluency where others grind — a resource you can draw on under pressure without depleting.
- Your friction: complacency. Without friction, the gift stays a hobby, and you drift toward whatever demands the least.

Pick one thing that comes easily to you and give it a deadline this month, because effort is the only ingredient this pattern cannot supply.
