title: Stellium
---

Three or more planets gathered in one place act like a chorus singing the same note. Whatever this cluster touches — a life area, a style of feeling, a way of working — receives an enormous share of your attention, and you develop depth there that scattered charts rarely reach. People come to you for this one thing because you have lived inside it.

- Your strength: concentration. When your interest locks on, you outlast almost everyone, and mastery arrives as a side effect of obsession.
- Your friction: the chorus drowns out the soloists. Planets outside the cluster get ignored, so whole capacities in you stay underdeveloped, and every problem gets solved with the same tool.

This week, spend one deliberate hour on something that has nothing to do with your favorite subject, and notice how loudly the pull back registers.
