title: Progressed Sun in Gemini
---
After the Taurus years of settling and securing, this chapter opens the windows. The progressed Sun in Gemini schools you in curiosity: new skills, new circles, new vocabulary. Identity becomes less about what you own and more about what you can learn, say, and connect. People from earlier chapters may barely recognize how talkative and mobile you have become.

The strength is range — you gather ideas and contacts fast, and you discover you can reinvent your competence at any age. The friction is scatter: five projects at thirty percent, conversations replacing commitments, and a restlessness that can unsettle the very foundations the last chapter built. Early in the chapter the hunger for novelty is indiscriminate; later you learn to curate it.

Pursue every curiosity, but pick two threads this season and follow them all the way to fluency.
