title: Progressed Sun in Sagittarius
---
The Scorpio years took you into the depths; this chapter walks you back out under open sky. The progressed Sun in Sagittarius schools you in meaning: after transformation, you need a philosophy, a horizon, a reason. These decades pull you toward travel, teaching, study, publishing, faith — anything that turns survived experience into a worldview worth sharing.

The strength is expansion with conviction. You become more generous, more mobile, and braver about betting on the future, and your hard-won honesty now comes with humor instead of sting. The friction is overreach: promising more than a calendar can hold, preaching where nobody asked, and skipping town — literally or emotionally — whenever life gets specific. Early on the search is restless; later it settles into genuine wisdom.

Book the journey or the course you keep deferring, and commit to finishing it.
