title: Progressed Sun in Virgo
---
After the Leo years of performance and heart, this chapter trades the stage for the workshop. The progressed Sun in Virgo schools you in refinement and service: skill over spectacle, usefulness over applause. These decades ask you to master details — health, routines, craft, the unglamorous systems that make a life actually work — and to measure yourself by what you improve rather than by who is watching.

The strength is competence that compounds. You become the person others trust with the hard, precise thing, and your body and schedule finally get honest attention. The friction is the inner critic promoted to management: perfectionism that stalls delivery, nitpicking that corrodes warmth, service that curdles into martyrdom. Early on the editing is anxious; later it becomes quiet mastery.

Ship one improved, imperfect version of something this week instead of polishing it privately.
