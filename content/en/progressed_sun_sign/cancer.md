title: Progressed Sun in Cancer
---
After the Gemini years of circulation and talk, this chapter turns you toward home. The progressed Sun in Cancer schools you in feeling and belonging: what you actually need, who counts as family, where you can put roots down. Career and cleverness do not disappear, but questions of care start deciding your choices — a move, a child, a parent, a household that finally matters more than the next conversation.

The strength is emotional depth. You learn to read your own weather honestly and to protect what is tender, in yourself and in others. The friction is the shell: moodiness that others must decode, retreating when you should ask directly, clinging to safety that has become confinement. Early on the tides feel like weakness; later they become navigation.

Name one need out loud this week instead of hinting at it.
