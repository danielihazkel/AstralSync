title: Progressed Sun in Libra
---
The Virgo years perfected your solo craft; this chapter puts another chair at the table. The progressed Sun in Libra schools you in relation: partnership, negotiation, and the art of building something with a person you cannot control. Marriages, business alliances, and serious collaborations tend to define these decades — identity develops through the mirror others hold up.

The strength is a growing gift for fairness and design: you learn to weigh sides honestly, to make peace that lasts, and to bring beauty into rooms that lacked it. The friction is the vanishing self — agreeing to keep the temperature down, outsourcing decisions to whoever is nearest, resenting compromises no one forced you to make. Early on harmony is appeasement; later it becomes real diplomacy.

State your actual preference in the next negotiation before asking for anyone else's.
