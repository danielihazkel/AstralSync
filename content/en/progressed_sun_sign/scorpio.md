title: Progressed Sun in Scorpio
---
After the Libra years of pairing and pleasing, this chapter takes you below the waterline. The progressed Sun in Scorpio schools you in depth and transformation: shared money, sexuality, grief, power — the material polite partnership avoided. These decades tend to include at least one ending that remakes you, and they ask you to stop performing agreeableness and find out what you actually run on.

The strength is X-ray honesty. You develop stamina for crisis, loyalty that survives ugliness, and the capacity to let dead things die so something real can grow. The friction is control: suspicion as a default setting, grudges kept like pets, intensity aimed at people who only wanted lunch. Early in the chapter everything feels like betrayal or fusion; later it matures into discernment.

Choose one thing you have outgrown and end it cleanly this season.
