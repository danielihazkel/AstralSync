title: Progressed Sun in Aries
---
These years school you in beginning. After a long Pisces chapter of dissolving, drifting, and absorbing, the progressed Sun in Aries hands you a match and asks you to strike it. Whatever your natal Sun is, this chapter layers urgency over it: decisions come faster, appetite returns, and waiting starts to feel like a kind of lying.

The strength here is courage on demand — you learn to act before you feel ready, and to trust that identity is built by doing, not deliberating. The friction is collateral: impatience with slower people, quarrels you did not need, projects abandoned at the first plateau. Early in the chapter the fire is raw and reactive; later it steadies into reliable nerve.

Pick one initiative that is genuinely yours and start it this month, before the plan is perfect.
