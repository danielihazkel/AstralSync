title: Progressed Sun in Leo
---
The Cancer years taught you where you belong; this chapter asks what you will make from that security. The progressed Sun in Leo schools you in self-expression: stepping out of the household and into visibility, signing your name to your work, letting yourself be seen wanting applause. Whatever your natal Sun, these decades add warmth, drama, and a hunger to create something unmistakably yours.

The strength is heart — generosity, loyalty, and the nerve to perform your life rather than apologize for it. The friction is the ego's sunburn: taking neutral events personally, needing praise before you can begin, mistaking attention for love. Early in the chapter the roar can be compensating; later it relaxes into genuine radiance.

Make one piece of work public this month and let it carry your name plainly.
