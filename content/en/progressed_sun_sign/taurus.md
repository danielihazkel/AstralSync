title: Progressed Sun in Taurus
---
This chapter is consolidation after initiation. The Aries years taught you to start; the Taurus decades teach you to keep, tend, and build slowly enough that things last. Your natal Sun still runs underneath, but these years pull your attention toward the body, money, land, craft — whatever can be touched and improved by patience.

The gift is staying power. You learn what you actually value, as opposed to what merely excited you, and you develop the stubborn calm that finishes what the last chapter began. The friction is inertia dressed up as loyalty: holding a job, habit, or grudge long after it stopped feeding you, and mistaking comfort for peace. Early on the slowdown can feel like loss of momentum; later it reads as rootedness.

Choose one thing worth a decade of care and invest in it deliberately this year.
