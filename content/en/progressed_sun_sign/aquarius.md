title: Progressed Sun in Aquarius
---
The Capricorn years built the mountain; this chapter asks whether you still want to live on it. The progressed Sun in Aquarius schools you in individuation: separating who you are from the roles, titles, and approval you accumulated. These decades often bring a swerve — leaving an establishment you helped run, adopting causes or communities that share your ideals rather than your resume.

The strength is honest originality. You get braver about being the odd one, clearer about the future you want, and better at finding your people instead of just your colleagues. The friction is exile by reflex: contrarianism as identity, detachment that reads as coldness, ideals defended more fiercely than the humans nearby. Early on the rebellion is jagged; later it becomes principled reinvention.

Tell one truth this month that risks your standing but matches your convictions.
