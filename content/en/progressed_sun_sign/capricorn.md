title: Progressed Sun in Capricorn
---
After the Sagittarius years of wandering and believing, this chapter asks you to build something the world can stand on. The progressed Sun in Capricorn schools you in mastery and structure: the philosophy you found now needs institutions, deadlines, and a reputation. These decades reward long climbs — a career made serious, a body of work, authority earned rather than borrowed.

The strength is gravity. You stop explaining yourself, deliver on time, and discover ambition can be a form of integrity. Others start bringing you the decisions that matter. The friction is the armor: workaholism that starves your relationships, pessimism marketed as realism, and a fear of looking foolish that vetoes anything new. Early on the climb feels grim and dutiful; later the competence becomes quietly enjoyable.

Define one summit worth ten years, then schedule this month's portion of the climb.
