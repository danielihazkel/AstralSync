essence: an elder's wide-angle compassion for the whole story

You see endings coming before other people admit anything is ending, and you feel strangers' troubles as if they were relatives'. Nine closes the single-digit cycle, and its lesson keeps arriving in your life as release: finishing, forgiving, handing things on.

That breadth makes you generous in a way that changes rooms. You give time to causes, patience to difficult people, and perspective to friends stuck inside their own week. The friction is abstraction. Loving humanity at large is easier than answering the message from the one specific human who needed you Tuesday, and your instinct to rise above conflict can shade into simply leaving whenever things get grubby and particular.

Idealism earns its keep when it lands somewhere small. Pick one unresolved goodbye — a project, a grudge, a half-ended friendship — and close it properly this week.
