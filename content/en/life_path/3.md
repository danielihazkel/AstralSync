title: Life Path 3
essence: an entertainer's urge to turn feeling into expression
---

Whatever moves through you wants out — as a joke, a sketch, a story told twice as well the second time. Your birth date encodes a lesson about voice: you are here to say things, and to discover that saying them halfway costs more than staying silent.

People warm to you quickly. You make dull rooms bearable and hard news survivable, and that social ease is genuine skill, not luck. The catch is scatter. Three starts a dozen projects and finishes the two that stayed fun, and when a feeling turns heavy you may reach for charm instead of honesty, performing lightness you do not feel.

Depth is what turns your talent into a body of work. Choose the one unfinished thing that still tugs at you and give it thirty minutes today, before you entertain anyone.
