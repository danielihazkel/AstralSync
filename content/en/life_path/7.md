title: Life Path 7
essence: an analyst's insistence on depth over easy answers
---

Surface explanations do not satisfy you. Where others accept the summary, you want the source, the mechanism, the exception that breaks the rule — and your recurring lesson is trusting what you find enough to live by it.

Solitude is fuel, not punishment. You do your clearest thinking alone, and colleagues learn that your quiet weeks precede your best conclusions. In close relationships this same wiring causes friction: retreating to think can look like withdrawal to a partner, and your habit of testing everything can make people feel audited rather than loved. Skepticism protects you, but left unchecked it becomes a moat nobody bothers crossing anymore.

Knowledge that never touches another person stays half-alive. When you finish your next stretch of private research, tell one person what you learned — including the part you are still unsure about.
