title: Life Path 2
essence: a diplomat's ear for what goes unsaid
---

You register the temperature of a room before anyone speaks. Where others hear an agenda, you hear the tension underneath it, and your recurring lesson is learning what to do with that sensitivity instead of being run by it.

Partnership is your native format. Colleagues bring you the disputes they cannot untangle, and friends trust you with the versions of themselves they hide elsewhere. The friction arrives when peacekeeping slides into self-erasure: you agree to keep things smooth, then resent the agreement, then say nothing about the resentment either. Conflict avoided is rarely conflict resolved — it is conflict postponed with interest.

Your growth edge is stating a preference plainly while the stakes are still small. Next time someone asks where you want to eat, answer with a place, not with "wherever works for you."
