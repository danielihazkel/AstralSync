title: Life Path 6
essence: a caretaker's pull toward repairing what others drop
---

Broken things find you — the friend mid-crisis, the team without a plan, the household running on fumes. Your birth date encodes a lesson in responsibility, and you tend to learn it by taking on more of it than any one person should carry.

The strength is real: you make places feel held. People do their best work and their best resting around you because someone is clearly minding the whole. The shadow arrives in two forms. One is martyrdom, where you give until you are empty and then present the invoice as resentment. The other is quiet control, where "helping" becomes deciding how other adults ought to live.

Care that respects other people's competence is the graduate-level version of your path. Today, notice one problem you are itching to fix and ask its owner what they actually want first.
