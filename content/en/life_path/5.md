title: Life Path 5
essence: a traveler's hunger for the unscripted next thing
---

Routine sits on you like a coat two sizes too small. Your recurring lesson is freedom — not just chasing it, which comes naturally, but learning that freedom without a spine turns into drift.

You adapt faster than almost anyone around you. New city, new tool, new social circle: you find the door handles while others are still reading the manual, which makes you valuable in any situation that keeps changing. The friction shows up at the six-month mark of anything — a job, a relationship, a fitness plan — when novelty fades and your instinct whispers that the interesting part is over. Leaving at that moment is sometimes wisdom and often just reflex.

The skill to build is telling those two apart. Before your next exit, write down what you would be leaving toward, not just what you are leaving from.
