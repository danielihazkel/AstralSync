title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Midheaven
---

For a year or more, through multiple exact passes, your public life and your inner life stop arguing. Work acquires an atmosphere — colleagues trust you more readily, your name attracts goodwill you did not engineer, and openings appear in creative, spiritual, or service-oriented directions that once seemed impractical. Reputation grows by resonance now, not by push.

The opening is rare: you can steer your career toward what actually moves you while the current is helping — launching the studio practice, the counseling training, the mission-driven role — and have it read as a natural evolution rather than a midlife lurch. The risk is drifting on the goodwill: because nothing hurts, nothing gets decided, and the inspired direction remains a lovely feeling you almost pursued.

Convert atmosphere into structure — pick the one meaningful professional direction this transit keeps illuminating and give it a deadline, a budget, and a first public artifact.
