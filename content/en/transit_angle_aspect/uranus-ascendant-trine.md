title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Ascendant
---

For a year or so, with multiple exact passes, change flows toward you instead of crashing into you. Reinvention that would normally take nerve — a new look, a new field, a franker way of speaking — meets almost no resistance now. People read your experiments as authenticity rather than crisis, and the version of you that has been waiting in the wings gets a friendly stage.

The opening is genuine self-authorship with the audience on your side; chance meetings and odd opportunities tend to reward whatever is most original in you. The risk is the classic trine problem: because nothing forces the issue, you can coast, enjoying the feeling of being about to change while the window quietly closes. Ease is permission, not action.

Choose the boldest visible change you have been postponing and make it during this transit, while the world is inclined to applaud.
