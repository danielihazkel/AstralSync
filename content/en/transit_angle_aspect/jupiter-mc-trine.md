title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Midheaven
---

For a few weeks to a few months, with a possible retrograde encore, your professional life runs with the current instead of against it. Work you did quietly months ago starts paying public dividends; superiors are approachable, reviews lean generous, and the bureaucratic machinery that usually stalls your plans seems oddly cooperative. Reputation grows without you forcing it.

The hazard of a trine is complacency. Because nothing hurts, you may bank the goodwill instead of spending it, skip the negotiation, shelve the application, assume next quarter will be just as friendly. Windows this smooth usually close without ceremony, and the same request costs far more effort later.

Choose the single career move you would attempt if you knew you would be received warmly, and make it before this window ends.
