title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Midheaven
---

For about a year, with multiple exact passes, innovation and your reputation are on speaking terms. Ideas that once sounded eccentric now read as visionary; decision-makers are unusually receptive to your unconventional angle, and lateral moves into newer fields tend to work out. Advancement during this transit rarely comes from climbing harder — it comes from being visibly, usefully different.

The opening is a career upgrade with the friction dialed down: you can modernize your role, change specialties, or step into a more public position while circumstances cooperate. The risk is mistaking flow for permanence. Because nothing is on fire, it is easy to bank the compliments, defer the actual move, and let a rare alignment expire as a pleasant season in the old job.

Name the professional reinvention you would attempt if failure were unlikely, and take its first public step while this support holds.
