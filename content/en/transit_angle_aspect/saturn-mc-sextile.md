title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Midheaven
---

For several months, including retrograde revisits, Saturn offers your career the kind of help that never makes headlines: processes that finally stick, a senior figure willing to vouch for you, credentials or systems you can assemble now at a fraction of their later cost. Steady work is visibly noticed by the people who control your next step.

The opportunity is quiet consolidation, document what you know, formalize your role, apprentice yourself to someone whose position you want in ten years. The risk is drift: because nothing is wrong, professional groundwork keeps losing to whatever is urgent, and this cooperative stretch expires with the scaffolding still unbuilt. Sextiles do not send reminders.

Pick one structural career investment, a certification, a mentor relationship, a portfolio, and put its first working session on the calendar this week.
