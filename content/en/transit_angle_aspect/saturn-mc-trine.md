title: Transiting Saturn trine natal Midheaven
---

For several months, with retrograde revisits, your professional life rewards method over flash. Long efforts mature quietly: the system you built starts running without you, decision-makers treat your judgment as settled fact, and advancement arrives not as a lightning strike but as the obvious next step. This is one of the best transits for negotiating from strength, because your track record speaks in a calm voice.

The opportunity is to lock in gains, convert reputation into title, salary, equity, or autonomy, and to take on the long project that needs a patient hand. The risk is under-asking: trines feel so stable that you may accept the default offer, defer the leap, and let a season built for consolidation pass as mere routine.

Negotiate one concrete upgrade to your position while your standing is this solid, and get the outcome in writing.
