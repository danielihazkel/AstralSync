title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Midheaven
---

For a few weeks to a few months, with a possible retrograde revisit, Jupiter crowns the career point of your chart. This is a classic promotion window: recognition arrives, senior people notice your work, and doors that stayed shut for years may swing open with strange ease. Ask for the raise, publish the thing, apply above your grade; visibility is unusually kind to you now.

The shadow side is inflation. It is easy to promise delivery dates you cannot meet, accept a title without asking about the workload, or let a run of applause convince you the fundamentals no longer apply. What you claim publicly this season, you will be expected to back up long after Jupiter moves on.

Pursue the biggest opportunity in front of you, but commit in writing only to what your calendar can actually hold.
