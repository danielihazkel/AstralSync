title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Midheaven
---

Once in a lifetime, Neptune crosses the top of your chart, and for a year or several — through repeated exact passes — your career stops being a ladder and becomes a question. Titles that used to motivate you go strangely flat; a pull toward meaningful, creative, or service-oriented work grows insistent; colleagues may see you less clearly, crediting or blaming you for things you did not do.

The opening is vocational, in the oldest sense: this transit can dissolve a merely successful path so a calling can surface, and imaginative or compassionate work begun now often defines the decades after. The risk is career fog — quitting with no plan, trusting a glamorous promise that evaporates, or letting your professional reputation blur while you wait for a sign.

Follow the pull in small, funded steps: keep an income anchor, test the calling as a side practice, and write down every agreement.
