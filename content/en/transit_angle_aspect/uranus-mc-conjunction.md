title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Midheaven
---

Once in a lifetime, Uranus crosses the top of your chart, and for a year or more — through direct and retrograde passes — your career refuses to stay on script. Restlessness with your title, your industry, or the whole ladder becomes impossible to ignore. Layoffs, reorganizations, viral visibility, or an out-of-left-field offer can all serve the same purpose: prying your public life loose from a trajectory you outgrew.

The opening is a genuine vocational rebirth. Work that expresses your originality — the thing colleagues call your weird streak — can become your actual profession now, and pivots that would look reckless at other times tend to land. The risk is scorched earth: resigning in a flourish, alienating allies, and discovering you demolished infrastructure you still needed.

Draft the wildest honest version of your next chapter, then dismantle the current one deliberately, keeping every bridge you can.
