title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Ascendant
---

Uranus is crossing your Descendant, the angle of partnership, and for a year or more — with several exact passes — the shake-up arrives through other people. A steady partner suddenly wants space, an electric stranger appears out of nowhere, a business alliance flips overnight. Whatever you have been suppressing about your own need for freedom tends to show up wearing someone else's face and making demands.

The opening is a wholesale renovation of how you relate: stale contracts can be renegotiated into something honest, and relationships built on genuine choice rather than habit become possible, sometimes with the same person. The risk is projection — casting the other as the villain or the liberator and detonating a workable bond to avoid admitting the restlessness is yours.

Before ending or beginning anything, ask what freedom you are hoping this person will hand you, and start granting it to yourself.
