title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Midheaven
---

For several months, with retrograde revisits, Saturn stands at the summit of your chart, and your career meets its accounting. Work built on real foundations tends to culminate now: the senior title, the completed body of work, the authority you have quietly earned finally becoming official. Work built on shortcuts, borrowed credibility, or a direction your heart left years ago tends to be restructured, sometimes abruptly.

The opportunity is to step into genuine authority and accept its weight on purpose; responsibility taken now compounds for the better part of a decade. The risk is the classic Saturn pair, overwork that hollows out everything below the job, or a public reckoning for corners cut when no one was watching.

Audit your professional position honestly this season, then either claim the responsibility you are ready for or begin the disciplined exit from the path that is not yours.
