title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Ascendant
---

Over a span of two to three years, with several exact passes as Pluto stations and retrogrades across your Ascendant, the person you present to the world is dismantled and rebuilt from the ground up. This crossing happens once in a lifetime, if at all, and it does not negotiate. Old mannerisms, old looks, old introductions stop fitting; people who knew you before may say you seem like someone else entirely.

The empowerment is real: you carry a new gravity, and rooms register it. The risk is equally real — the urge to control how you are perceived, to win every encounter, to fix people with intensity they did not ask for. Others will mirror your force back at you, sometimes as fascination, sometimes as resistance.

Choose one honest way to show yourself each day and let the performance die on schedule.
