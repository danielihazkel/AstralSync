title: Transiting Neptune square natal Midheaven
---

For a year or more, across several exact hits, the question of where your life is going loses its crisp outline. The career that made sense on paper feels oddly hollow; goals you recite start sounding like someone else's; and at work you may be misjudged — idealized one quarter, doubted the next — while home and ambition tug you in incompatible directions.

This dissolution has a purpose. The square is washing out a direction you adopted for approval or security so that a truer one can be felt, and people often emerge from it with work that finally means something. The risk is acting on fog: resigning impulsively, chasing a rescuer or a glamorous scheme, or quietly checking out until your reputation erodes on its own.

Navigate by instruments, not visibility — keep commitments small and reversible, get financial facts in writing, and let a daily grounding routine steady the season.
