title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Midheaven
---

Neptune is crossing the base of your chart, the IC, and for a year or several — with repeated exact passes — the fog gathers under your foundations rather than over your career. Home may feel provisional: a move that never quite settles, family stories revising themselves, a longing for a sanctuary you cannot name. Ambition can keep functioning on the surface while the ground it stands on quietly turns to water.

The opening is a deep re-rooting of the spirit. Old family myths lose their grip, compassion for where you came from becomes possible, and a home life organized around peace rather than performance starts to seem worth building. The risk is dissolving the base carelessly — idealized relocations, murky property or family finances, or retreating so far inward that outward life dangles unattended.

Tend the root deliberately: make home decisions slowly, verify every practical detail twice, and keep one steady domestic ritual as ballast.
