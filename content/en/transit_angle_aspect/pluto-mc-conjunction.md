title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Midheaven
---

Over two to three years, with several exact passes across your Midheaven, Pluto puts your vocation through total transformation. This is a once-in-a-lifetime crossing of the career angle, and it rarely leaves the resume intact: a field outgrown, a company imploding, a calling that surfaces with the force of an ultimatum. Your reputation passes through the crucible too — scrutiny, rumor, or sudden prominence can all arrive uninvited.

Power dynamics with authority become the recurring test. Bosses, boards, institutions, and gatekeepers either hand you real leverage or provoke you into taking it, and every shortcut in your public record tends to surface now. The empowerment is authentic authority — work that carries your full weight. The risk is ambition turned compulsive: ruthlessness, empire-building, winning at costs you refuse to count.

Write down what you would still pursue with no audience, and steer the next career decision by that.
