title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Ascendant
---

Pluto opposing your Ascendant means Pluto is crossing your Descendant — the angle of the other. For two to three years, with several exact passes, partnership becomes the crucible. A marriage, a business alliance, a rivalry, or a magnetic new arrival carries transformative force into your life, and every close bond is tested for honesty about power: who decides, who yields, who has been quietly running things.

Once in a lifetime, relationship stops being a comfort and becomes an initiation. The empowerment is learning to meet another person at full strength without domination or collapse. The risk is obsession — with a partner, an enemy, or the question of who controls whom — and the slow-motion power struggles that follow.

Some bonds will end because they can only exist between the people you both used to be. Name one power imbalance in a close relationship this week and address it out loud.
