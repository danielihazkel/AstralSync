title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Ascendant
---

Neptune is crossing your Descendant, and for a year or several — with repeated exact passes — the mist settles over partnership. You may meet someone who seems fated, watch a familiar partner turn unfamiliar, or find that what you always believed about a relationship was partly your own projection. Idealization and disillusionment are the twin weathers of this transit, and most people get both.

The opening is a more soulful way of relating: compassion where there was scorekeeping, forgiveness that is actually possible, and bonds chosen with open eyes rather than out of need. The risk is sacrifice without consent — rescuing, being rescued, tolerating deception, or dissolving your own boundaries to keep a beautiful story intact.

Reality-test the story regularly: compare what your partner or prospect actually does with what you tell yourself it means, and keep one trusted outside friend in the loop.
