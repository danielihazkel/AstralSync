title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Midheaven
---

For several months, with retrograde revisits, Saturn crosses the base of your chart and the weight in your life shifts homeward. Family responsibilities become impossible to delegate, an aging parent, a house that needs real repair, a household that needs an adult in the room, and career expansion politely waits while you tend the foundation everything else stands on.

The opportunity is groundwork that pays for decades: settle where you actually intend to live, fix the structural problems, resolve old family business, and rebuild the private base from which your next public climb will launch. The risk is resenting the descent, neglecting home duties to chase visibility, or sinking into heaviness about how far from the summit this season feels.

Choose the one domestic obligation you have been deferring, and give it a schedule and a budget as if it were a professional project, because right now it is.
