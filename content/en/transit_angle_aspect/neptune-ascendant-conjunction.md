title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Ascendant
---

Once in a lifetime, Neptune drifts across your Ascendant, and for a year or several — with repeated exact passes — the outline of who you are goes soft. You become a screen: strangers confide in you, project onto you, mistake you for whatever they need. Old self-descriptions stop fitting, and you may feel more porous, more tired, more moved by everything than you can easily explain.

The opening is a subtler, more compassionate self coming through — artists, healers, and anyone who works with imagination often date their real work to this crossing. The risk is dissolution without direction: drifting through commitments, absorbing other people's moods, escaping into fantasy or substances, or being chronically misread because you no longer defend an edge.

Anchor the fog in the body: keep one daily physical ritual — a walk, a swim, a practice — and let it be the shoreline while the tide does its work.
