title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Midheaven
---

Uranus is crossing the base of your chart, the IC, and for a year or more — including retrograde returns — the earthquake happens at home rather than at work. Moves, renovations, family upheavals, a parent's sudden change, or a plain refusal to keep living the way you have been: the foundations under your public life are being rewired. Career questions during this time usually trace back to a deeper one about where and how you belong.

The opening is a liberating re-rooting. You can relocate, restructure a household, or break a long family pattern and discover that your ambitions breathe easier on honest ground. The risk is treating home as the enemy — serial moves, abrupt exits from family obligations, and a rootlessness that eventually destabilizes the career you were protecting.

Change your foundations on purpose: choose one structural shift at home and plan it before circumstances choose for you.
