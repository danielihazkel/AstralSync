title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Ascendant
---

For a few weeks to a few months, and possibly again on a retrograde pass, your appetite for more collides with the person you usually present. You may feel restless inside your own routines, tempted to say yes to everything, upgrade everything, or announce plans before they are ready. Growth is genuinely available here; it just arrives pushing against the grain of your image and schedule.

The opportunity is a real stretch: taking on something slightly too big for your current self-description and growing into it. The risk is classic Jupiter overreach, overbooked weeks, promises made in enthusiasm and paid for in scramble, spending that outruns the plan, or a confident tone that others read as presumption.

Before accepting any new commitment this season, name out loud what you will drop to make room for it.
