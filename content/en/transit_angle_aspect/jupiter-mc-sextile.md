title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Midheaven
---

For several weeks, sometimes with a retrograde return, professional growth becomes quietly easier to arrange. A colleague mentions your name in the right meeting, a small course or certification suddenly looks affordable, a manager is receptive to the idea you have been sitting on. Nothing dramatic forces the issue; the terrain simply tilts gently in your favor.

Sextiles reward initiative and ignore passivity. The risk is not disaster but waste: you stay heads-down, assume the good mood at work is permanent, and let a modest but real opening close unnoticed. Opportunities under this transit tend to look ordinary, an email, a coffee, a form, until you see where they led.

Send one career-advancing message this week, to a mentor, a hiring manager, or the person whose project you want to join.
