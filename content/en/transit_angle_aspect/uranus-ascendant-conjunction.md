title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Ascendant
---

Over a year or more, with several exact crossings as Uranus stations and retraces its steps, the person you show the world stops fitting. This is a once-in-a-lifetime crossing of your Ascendant, and it tends to announce itself physically: a sudden urge to change your hair, your city, your name, the way you walk into a room. People who have known you for years start saying you seem different, because you are.

The opening is real freedom — you get to rebuild your persona around who you have actually become rather than who you trained yourself to appear to be. The risk is indiscriminate demolition: quitting, leaving, or shaving your head in a single week and torching relationships that could have flexed with you.

Before each irreversible move, write down what you are escaping and what you are building, and act only where the second answer is longer.
