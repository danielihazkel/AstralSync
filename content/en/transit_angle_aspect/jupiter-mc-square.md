title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Midheaven
---

For a few weeks to a few months, possibly twice if a retrograde brings it back, your ambitions grow faster than your current role can contain. You may feel an itch to escalate, to pitch the grand version, demand the bigger territory, or publicly outgrow a boss or a job description. Real advancement is available, but it comes at an angle: the path that opens is rarely the one you announced.

The opportunity is momentum; restlessness like this can finally push you out of a plateau. The risk is overreach in front of an audience: overpromising to leadership, picking a visible fight, or expanding into work that dazzles but does not pay. Squares from Jupiter punish grandiosity more than they punish ambition.

Channel the restlessness into one concrete proposal with numbers attached, and let the numbers do the boasting.
