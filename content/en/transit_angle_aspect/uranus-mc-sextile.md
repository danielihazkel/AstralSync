title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Midheaven
---

Over roughly a year, with a retrograde revisit or two, your professional world quietly loosens. New tools, side projects, and unconventional contacts keep drifting within reach, and experiments you float at work meet less resistance than usual. This is the transit under which people modernize a stale role, pick up a technology that later defines their career, or discover that a hobby has a market.

The opening is progressive, low-drama: you can update your public direction one deliberate step at a time, without staking your reputation on a leap. The risk is passivity. Sextiles whisper rather than shove, and if you treat every invitation as something to consider later, this window closes with your trajectory untouched and the next Uranus contact arriving as a harder aspect.

Commit to one forward-looking professional experiment — a course, a pitch, a prototype — and ship something visible before the transit ends.
