title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Midheaven
---

For roughly two years, across several exact contacts, Pluto sextiles your Midheaven and offers steady regenerative pressure on your working life. Nothing detonates; instead, openings appear — a mentor with real influence, a niche where depth is rewarded, a chance to research, restructure, or lead where others would rather not look. The offer is quiet and strictly use-it-or-lose-it.

Taken seriously, this window lets you rebuild your professional foundation while the walls are still standing: renegotiate your role, retrain, claim work that actually compels you. Authority figures are unusually willing to share power with you if you ask directly. Ignored, the same current turns inward as career brooding — plotting moves you never make, resenting those who made theirs.

The empowerment is influence earned in increments; the risk is scheming as a substitute for acting. Make one concrete, slightly intimidating professional ask before the next exact pass.
