title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Midheaven
---

For a few weeks to a few months, sometimes revisited during retrograde, Jupiter crosses the base of your chart and growth moves indoors. This is a season of household expansion in every sense: moving somewhere larger, renovating, welcoming a new family member or housemate, or simply feeling your inner life widen while the office recedes in importance. Home becomes generous, and so can you.

The opportunity is real roots: buy, build, host, reconcile with family, restore the private reserves your public life draws on. The risk is inflation at the foundation, stretching for more house than the budget bears, letting domestic projects sprawl, or drifting from career visibility just when it needed minimal tending.

Invest deliberately in one improvement to your home or family life, and set its budget before enthusiasm sets it for you.
