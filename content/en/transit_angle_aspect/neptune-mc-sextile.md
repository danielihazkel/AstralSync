title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Midheaven
---

Over a year or more of quiet exact passes, meaning seeps into your working life without demanding you blow anything up. Projects with a creative, healing, or idealistic dimension come looking for you; your public image gains warmth; and you can sense which parts of your career feed something deeper and which merely pay. It is a season of gentle vocational listening.

The opening is integration: you can fold imagination and service into the structure you already have — a humane management style, an artistic side practice, pro bono work that slowly becomes a specialty — and your reputation absorbs it as depth rather than drift. The risk is letting the whisper stay a whisper, filing the sense of calling under someday while the window closes.

Give the inkling one concrete outlet this year — a portfolio, a volunteer role, a course — and review each quarter whether it deserves more room.
