title: Transiting Saturn square natal Midheaven
---

For several months, with retrograde revisits, friction builds between your ambitions and the structures that contain them. Authority figures grow more exacting; a reorganization, a demanding new boss, or a stalled promotion can make you feel professionally tested from all sides, while obligations at home tug against the hours the job demands. Progress is possible, but nothing is handed over.

The opportunity hides inside the scrutiny: standards applied to you now show exactly which skills, credentials, or boundaries your next level requires, and closing those gaps under pressure builds a reputation for reliability that outlasts the transit. The risk is fighting the referee, quitting in frustration, feuding with management, or working to exhaustion to prove a point nobody asked you to prove.

Ask directly what would earn the next step, write down the answer, and work that list instead of your grievances.
