title: Transiting Neptune square natal Ascendant
---

For a year or more, across several exact hits and retrograde returns, the gap between how you appear and what you feel becomes disorienting. You may be widely misread — cast as savior, flake, or mystery — while your own sense of direction mists over. Energy can dip, decisions blur, and identities that once felt solid start to seem like roles you were assigned rather than chose.

Inside the confusion is a genuine invitation: the personas dissolving now were too small, and the sensitivity flooding in is raw material for a truer, more imaginative way of meeting the world. The risk is filling the blur with anesthetics — overwork, screens, substances, romantic fantasy — or making binding commitments mid-fog that a clearer year will have to unwind.

Delay irreversible choices where you can, verify what people are actually asking of you, and hold one grounding routine — sleep, movement, honest friends — without exception.
