title: Transiting Uranus square natal Midheaven
---

For a year or more, across several exact hits, the tension between your public direction and your private truth becomes hard to sit with. The job may still be fine on paper while feeling like a costume; or external shocks — restructuring, a new boss, an industry lurch — keep destabilizing a path you thought was settled. Either way, the square insists that your life direction and your actual self stop pretending not to know each other.

Used well, this friction funds a course correction you would never have made voluntarily: renegotiating your role, relocating, or naming ambitions you have hidden to keep the peace at home or at work. Used badly, it produces sudden resignations, public clashes with authority, and pivots chosen mostly to feel the jolt.

List what your current path still gives you before you break anything, and change the smallest structure that would end the suffocation.
