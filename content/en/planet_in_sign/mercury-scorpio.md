title: Mercury in Scorpio
---

Surface answers do not satisfy you. Your mind is an investigator: it notices the pause before the reply, the number that will not reconcile, the motive underneath the stated reason. Fixed water gives you a focus that borders on X-ray — you research one thing to the bottom rather than ten things to the middle.

That depth makes you formidable at diagnosis, strategy, and keeping confidences. The cost is suspicion as a default setting: you can find hidden agendas that are not there, hold your own cards so close that trust starves, and win arguments with an accuracy that leaves marks.

Remember that some people are simply vague, not concealing. When your gut flags deception, verify once before acting on it — and volunteer one true thing about yourself this week, unprompted.
