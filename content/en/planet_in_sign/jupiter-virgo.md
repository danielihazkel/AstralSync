title: Jupiter in Virgo
---

Improvement is your religion. You do not trust grand visions until they survive contact with a checklist, and your version of faith is quietly radical: the belief that anything — a process, a habit, a body of work — can be made better by careful, incremental attention. Growth comes through craft, apprenticeship, and being genuinely useful; your luck tends to arrive as a reputation for competence that precedes you.

The strength is expansion that actually works — no vaporware, no broken promises. The friction is a big planet in a small-tolerance sign: perfectionism can cramp Jupiter's appetite, shrinking every opportunity to the size of its flaws, and service can slide into servitude.

When the next imperfect chance appears, ask what is right with it first — then say yes before the audit is finished.
