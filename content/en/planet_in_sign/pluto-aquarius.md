title: Pluto in Aquarius
---

Your cohort inherits the reckoning with collective power. Pluto in Aquarius transforms networks, technology, and the very idea of the group — who gets counted, how crowds decide, what machines are allowed to know. You likely feel this as an instinct that systems, not just individuals, need remaking, and that your true peers are wherever your values are.

The strength is genuine reformist nerve; you can imagine structures that do not yet exist and organize strangers around them. The friction is abstraction — loving humanity in aggregate while going cold on the person in front of you, or dissolving into a group identity until your own voice thins out.

In every collective you join, keep one opinion the group does not share, and voice it occasionally.
