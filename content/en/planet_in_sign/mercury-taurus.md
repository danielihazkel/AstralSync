title: Mercury in Taurus
---

You think the way soil absorbs rain: slowly, completely, without waste. New information gets tested against experience before it earns a place, and once you have decided, the decision holds — friends learn that arguing with you is a weather event, not a negotiation.

The strength is reliability. Your conclusions are load-bearing; people build on what you say because you actually did the checking. The trade-off is inertia: a changed fact can knock politely for months before you let it in, and you sometimes mistake familiarity for truth.

You explain things in concrete, sensory terms, which makes you a natural teacher of practical skills. Once a season, deliberately reopen one settled opinion and audit the evidence like a stranger would.
