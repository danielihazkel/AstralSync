title: Sun in Aries
---

You come alive at the starting line. Beginnings feed you — the blank page, the cold plunge, the problem nobody has touched yet. When you act first, you feel most like yourself, and that instinct makes you the person who breaks a group's paralysis.

The cost of that fire is patience. You burn hottest in the opening minutes and can go cold once the work turns into maintenance. People sometimes read your directness as aggression when you meant it as honesty.

Courage on demand is your strength; finishing what stopped being exciting is your friction. This week, pick one stalled project and carry it one boring step further before you start anything new.
