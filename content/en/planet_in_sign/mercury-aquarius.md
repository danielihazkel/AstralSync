title: Mercury in Aquarius
---

Your first instinct with any consensus is to test it. Fixed air makes you a systems thinker: you zoom out to the pattern, the incentive structure, the rule generating the behavior everyone else is arguing about case by case. It makes you original — and occasionally right years too early.

Detachment is the strength: you can reason about charged topics without catching fire, which makes you the clearest head in a room full of opinions. The catch is that contrarianism can calcify — the unconventional view, once adopted, becomes as fixed as any tradition — and your cool analysis of someone's problem can feel like a diagnosis when they wanted company.

When someone brings you a feeling, ask whether they want solutions before you unroll the schematic.
