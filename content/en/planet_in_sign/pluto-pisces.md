title: Pluto in Pisces
---

Yours is a generation that transforms the invisible. Pluto in Pisces excavates the collective unconscious itself — faith, compassion, imagery, the dissolving line between minds — and forces a reckoning with what humanity dreams, worships, and refuses to feel. You carry this as uncanny sensitivity: undercurrents reach you before facts do, and suffering anywhere registers as somehow yours.

The gift is regenerative compassion; you can go into grief, addiction, or despair alongside someone and come back holding a live thread of meaning. The friction is dissolution — martyrdom, savior fantasies, losing your outline in oceanic causes or in private escapes.

Anchor your empathy in one bounded commitment with a schedule, so the boundless has somewhere concrete to land.
