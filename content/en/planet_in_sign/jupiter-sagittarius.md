title: Jupiter in Sagittarius
---

This is Jupiter at home, and it behaves like it: your appetite for more is not for money or status but for meaning itself. You grow through distance — literal travel, foreign ideas, teachers, and the moments a bigger map suddenly replaces your old one. Luck rides your optimism; you say yes at long odds, and the odds keep humoring you often enough to make the faith rational.

Your strength is vision plus morale — you can lift a whole room's ceiling just by describing what is possible.

The excess is Jupiter squared: promises outrunning calendars, opinions delivered as sermons, the next horizon devaluing the ground underfoot. Twice a year, finish something completely before booking the flight — and ask one real question for every truth you broadcast.
