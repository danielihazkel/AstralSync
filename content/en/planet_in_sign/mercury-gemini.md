title: Mercury in Gemini
---

Curiosity runs your operating system. Mercury is at home in Gemini, and it shows: you collect ideas the way others collect keys, make connections mid-sentence, and can talk fluently about ten subjects — often within the same conversation.

Speed and range are the gift. You are the person who finds the workaround, the reference, the perfect phrasing. Depth is the tax: attention slides off anything that stops being novel, and you can end up knowing a little about everything while finishing books about nothing.

Your arguments win on agility rather than force, which sometimes leaves slower minds convinced yet unpersuaded. Pick one thread this month that deserves a second and third sitting, and stay past the point where it stops being shiny.
