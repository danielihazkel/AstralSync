title: Saturn in Scorpio
---

Trust is your slow build. Merging with another person — emotionally, sexually, financially — is exactly where you feel least safe, so you keep the vault locked and study people at length before letting them in.

The gift is depth with discipline. You can sit with crisis, secrets, grief, and shared debts that make others flee, and you never betray a confidence. What you commit to at that level, you commit to completely.

The shadow is control — testing loyalty instead of asking for it, managing intimacy like risk exposure, ending things pre-emptively so you can't be left. Vulnerability on your own terms is still armor. Tell one trusted person a true thing you'd normally withhold, before you feel ready.
