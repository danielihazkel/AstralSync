title: Moon in Libra
---

You steady yourself through connection. Harmony isn't a preference for you — it's oxygen, and you can feel a room tilt out of balance the moment someone's tone sharpens. Instinctively you smooth, translate, and mediate, often before anyone else realizes there was friction to manage.

This makes you a gifted peacemaker and a genuinely fair listener; people trust you with both sides of a story. The trouble is that keeping the peace and telling the truth sometimes point in opposite directions, and you tend to choose peace. Your own preferences blur — you say "either is fine" so often you stop knowing which one you wanted.

Alone time actually restores you more than you expect, because there's no one to calibrate to. This week, when someone asks what you want, answer in under five seconds and let the answer be uneven.
