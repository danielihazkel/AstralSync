title: Mars in Pisces
---

Your engine runs on atmosphere. When the work feels meaningful — creative, compassionate, connected to something larger — you can produce in a weekend what others grind out in a month. When it does not, no amount of willpower reliably starts the motor, and you drift toward whatever does glow. Motivation, for you, is weather: real, powerful, and not fully under your command.

Under attack you go liquid rather than rigid — deflecting, dissolving, disappearing — which spares you many pointless battles and wins some by sheer elusiveness. The friction is that important battles get evaded too, and anger with nowhere to go tends to sink inward as guilt or fog.

Track your tides for two weeks; schedule hard tasks at proven high water. And when something genuinely matters, say the direct sentence you have been swimming around.
