title: Saturn in Taurus
---

Security is your classroom. You build money, comfort, and stability the slow way — and often start out feeling you have less than everyone else, whether or not the ledger agrees. Scarcity thinking can hum quietly under even your good years.

Your strength is endurance. You can save, maintain, and compound long after quicker people have spent their gains and wandered off. What you own, you earned, and it tends to last.

The trade-off is rigidity. Holding on becomes reflex — to money, routines, possessions, grudges — until security itself turns into a cage. Once a season, deliberately spend on or give away something you could technically keep, and notice that the ground still holds.
