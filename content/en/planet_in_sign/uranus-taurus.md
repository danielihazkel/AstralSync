title: Uranus in Taurus
---

Your generation is rewriting what "enough" means. Uranus in Taurus shakes the ground itself — money, food, land, ownership — so your cohort came of age watching supposedly solid things behave like weather.

Personally, this shows up as unconventional instincts about security: you may earn in ways older relatives can't parse, distrust institutions with your assets, or crave a stability you also keep disrupting. Your strength is inventing new forms of value where the old ones failed.

The tension is that Taurus wants slow and solid while Uranus keeps flipping the table, leaving you oscillating between hoarding and torching. Build one boring, durable financial habit first, and let the experiments happen on top of it.
