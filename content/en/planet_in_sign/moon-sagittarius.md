title: Moon in Sagittarius
---

Your instinct when a feeling gets heavy is to widen the frame. You reach for the joke, the road trip, the bigger philosophical picture — anything with a horizon in it. Safety means room to move: emotionally, physically, intellectually. Lock the exits and even a good situation starts to feel like a trap.

Optimism is your genuine superpower, not a pose; you metabolize setbacks faster than almost anyone and pull others up with you. But there's a difference between perspective and escape, and you sometimes book the flight — literal or mental — right when staying put would have healed something.

When you're drained, you get cynical and blunt, mocking what you used to find meaningful. That's the warning light. Watch for the urge to leave mid-difficulty, and try staying in one hard conversation all the way to its end.
