title: Sun in Scorpio
---

Intensity is your natural resting state. You want the real conversation, the actual motive, the truth under the performance — and your willingness to go where others flinch makes you a formidable ally in a crisis. When you commit, you commit whole; half-measures feel like lies to you.

Control is the shadow side of that depth. You test people quietly, reveal yourself slowly, and sometimes hold power in a room simply by withholding. Trust extended too late can arrive too late.

Your power to transform is real, and so is your habit of guarding the door. Watch for the moment you answer a sincere question with strategy instead of truth — then try answering straight once.
