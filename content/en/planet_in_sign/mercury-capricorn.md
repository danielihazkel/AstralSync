title: Mercury in Capricorn
---

Before an idea earns your attention, it has to answer one question: what does this build? Saturn disciplines your thinking into structures — steps, budgets, timelines — and you speak the way you plan, sparingly and to the point. People bring you their tangled situations because you return them as ordered lists.

That pragmatism is rare and valuable; your skepticism has saved more projects than most people's enthusiasm has started. It also runs cold: risk assessment can shade into pessimism, and your economy with words may read as dismissal to those who think by talking.

Play is not the enemy of rigor — some of your best solutions arrive off the clock. Once in a while, entertain an impractical idea for ten full minutes before pricing it.
