title: Neptune in Taurus
---

Your cohort's dreams are made of earth. Neptune in Taurus casts a collective glamour over land, food, craft, and simplicity — the fantasy of the off-grid cabin, the honest loaf, wealth that feels solid and clean. Personally, you sense the sacred through your body and your surroundings: certain objects, meals, and landscapes carry genuine spiritual weight for you.

That groundedness is a gift; you make the spiritual tangible where others leave it abstract. The fog gathers around money and comfort — idealizing security until it becomes a religion, or trusting that whatever feels pleasant must also be true.

Before a purchase or investment that promises peace of mind, ask what specifically it will change — and whether calm can be bought at all.
