title: Moon in Capricorn
---

You treat feelings the way you treat everything else: as something to handle competently. In a crisis you go calm and practical, sorting logistics while others fall apart, and being useful is honestly easier for you than being comforted. Safety means self-sufficiency — knowing that whatever happens, you can carry it.

That composure is real strength; people build their lives on your reliability. The friction is that "I can carry it" quietly becomes "I must carry it alone." You minimize your own needs until they exit through the side door as exhaustion, back pain, or a dry, cutting mood you aim at whoever's nearest.

Rest feels unearned to you, which is exactly why you need it scheduled like a meeting. Once this week, let someone do something for you without repaying it within the hour — and notice how hard that is.
