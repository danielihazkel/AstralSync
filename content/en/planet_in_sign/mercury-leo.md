title: Mercury in Leo
---

You do not just explain ideas — you stage them. Fixed fire gives your mind conviction and warmth: you think out loud, commit to a viewpoint, and deliver it with a storyteller's timing that makes people actually listen.

That performance instinct is a genuine asset. Meetings drift until you name the point, and your summary becomes the version everyone remembers. The shadow is authorship pride: an idea can fuse with your identity, so amending it feels like a demotion, and quieter contributors may stop offering thoughts they suspect you will outshine.

Practice revising positions in public; it reads as strength on you. Once a week, restate someone else's idea better than they did — then hand it back with their name on it.
