title: Sun in Virgo
---

You are animated by the gap between how things are and how they could work. Where others see mess, you see a fixable system, and your quiet competence is often the reason the whole operation runs. Craft is your form of devotion — the well-edited sentence, the debugged process, the ordered shelf.

The same lens turns inward and gets cruel. Your inner critic files reports nobody asked for, and *not good enough yet* can postpone your life indefinitely. Helpfulness sometimes slides into correcting people who only wanted company.

Precision is the strength; perfectionism is the tax on it. Before your next piece of feedback, ask whether it was requested — and let one imperfect thing ship this week.
