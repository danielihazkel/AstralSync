title: Uranus in Scorpio
---

You were born into a generation that dragged taboos into the light. Uranus in Scorpio marks a cohort unafraid of sex, death, power, and psychology as public conversation — what earlier generations whispered, yours investigates out loud.

Personally, the current runs deep and sudden. You're drawn to intensity, allergic to polite surfaces, and capable of surgical honesty about motives, including your own. Your strength is transformation: you can burn down a life stage and rebuild without flinching.

The friction is that voltage plus depth can go scorched-earth — cutting people off in one stroke, treating every power dynamic as a battlefield. Not every ending needs to be total. Before the next irreversible severing, wait one week and notice what the intensity was protecting.
