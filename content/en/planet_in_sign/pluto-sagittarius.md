title: Pluto in Sagittarius
---

Your generation set fire to the belief systems it inherited. Pluto in Sagittarius transforms truth itself — religions scrutinized, borders crossed, worldviews colliding at broadband speed until no single story could claim everyone. You carry this as a lie detector for dogma: the moment a belief demands blind loyalty, something in you starts digging.

The strength is genuine philosophical courage; you will follow a question across cultures, disciplines, and comfort zones until it answers. The friction is crusading — replacing one absolutism with another, arguing to convert rather than to learn, and a cynicism about meaning that is really unprocessed grief.

When you demolish someone's belief, ask what you are offering them to live in instead.
