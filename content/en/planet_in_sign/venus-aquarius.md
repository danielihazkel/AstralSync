title: Venus in Aquarius
---

Friendship is your gateway to love. You are drawn to the interesting before the beautiful — the odd mind, the unusual life, the person nobody else quite decodes — and your affection comes with a rare feature: genuine acceptance, free of any urge to renovate anyone.

You need air in a relationship the way others need reassurance, and you extend the same liberty back; partners are never smothered. The friction is the thermostat. Your cool, amused steadiness can leave someone starving for ordinary heat — longing, jealousy, the irrational proofs of mattering — and you can analyze a relationship brilliantly while forgetting to be in it.

Detachment is a talent, not an identity. Say the warm thing out loud sometimes, even when it is obvious — especially because it is.
