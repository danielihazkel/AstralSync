title: Pluto in Leo
---

Yours is the generation that made the self a superpower. Pluto in Leo turns self-expression into a matter of destiny — a cohort convinced that individual will, charisma, and creative fire can and should remake the world. You likely carry a non-negotiable need to matter: recognition is not vanity to you, it is oxygen.

The gift is sovereign creative force; when you commit to being fully seen, you authorize everyone around you to shine too. The friction is the throne — pride that cannot apologize, drama that must escalate, and a fear of ordinariness that quietly mistakes attention for love.

Every so often, hand someone else the spotlight deliberately, and watch what it does to your sense of power.
