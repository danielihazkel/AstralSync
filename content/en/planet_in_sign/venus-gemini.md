title: Venus in Gemini
---

You fall in love with minds first. Banter is your courtship, curiosity your love language, and the surest way to lose you is to become predictable. Mutable air keeps affection light, playful, and verbal — you would rather trade jokes at one in the morning than gaze silently into anyone's eyes.

The gift is freshness: you keep long relationships interesting by staying interested, in your partner and in everything else. The friction is depth and dosage. Heavy emotional weather sends you reaching for wit or the exits, and a partner may wonder whether your delightful attention is intimacy or just excellent conversation.

Variety is a need, not a flaw — but name it out loud. When things get serious, practice staying in the hard conversation two minutes past the first joke.
