title: Uranus in Libra
---

Your cohort renegotiated the relationship contract. Uranus in Libra marks a generation that took marriage, partnership, and fairness apart to see how they work — redefining commitment and insisting that love justify itself instead of merely persisting.

You carry that as a live wire in your partnerships: a demand for equality and breathing room, sudden clarity about what isn't working, and real talent for designing relationships that fit the actual people in them rather than the template.

The shadow is the escape hatch — leaving abruptly when intimacy gets ordinary, mistaking friction for oppression. Every bond eventually asks for a boring Tuesday. When the urge to bolt arrives, name the specific unfairness first; if you can't, stay and look closer.
