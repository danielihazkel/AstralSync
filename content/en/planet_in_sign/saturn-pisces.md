title: Saturn in Pisces
---

Your task is giving form to fog. Imagination, compassion, and spiritual longing run strong in you — and so does the fear of dissolving into them, of being too porous, too easily flooded by other people's pain.

The mature version of this placement is rare and valuable: a helper or artist with boundaries, someone who can structure the intangible — turning empathy into practice, dream into finished work, faith into daily discipline.

The struggle is leakage: guilt that says every need is your job, escapism when structure feels cruel, deadlines that dissolve like everything else. Compassion without a container drains you dry. Decide in advance how much you'll give this week — time, money, worry — and stop at the line.
