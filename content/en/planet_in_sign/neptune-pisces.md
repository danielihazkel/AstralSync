title: Neptune in Pisces
---

You were born while Neptune moved through its own sign, and in your generation the veil is thin everywhere. Collective imagination floods its banks: mysticism goes mainstream, and so do conspiracies, epidemics of empathy and of escapism. You likely feel this as porousness — art, moods, and other people's pain soak straight into you.

The gift is enormous: compassion without borders, creativity that seems channeled rather than made, an unforced sense that everything connects. The friction is boundary collapse — absorbing feelings that were never yours, numbing out through screens, sleep, or substances when the world gets loud.

Treat discernment as your spiritual practice: each time you feel overwhelmed, name which part of the weight actually belongs to you.
