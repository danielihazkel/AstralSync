title: Uranus in Aquarius
---

Uranus was in its home sign when your generation arrived — a cohort of natives, not visitors, to the networked age. Collective idealism, technology, and the rights of the outsider are your generation's default settings, not its discoveries.

For you personally, the group mind is where the electricity flows: online communities, causes, collaborations, ideas that only work at scale. You spot the future early and feel a genuine duty to the many.

The friction is abstraction — loving humanity while ghosting humans, endless connectivity with thin intimacy, contrarian identity worn as a uniform. Utopia is not a substitute for Tuesday's phone call. Choose one cause and one actual person to show up for consistently this month.
