title: Pluto in Cancer
---

You were born into a generation that had home remade by force. Pluto in Cancer drags the deepest privacies — family, homeland, mother herself — into upheaval, and the cohort carries stories of displacement, sacrifice, and loyalty tested to the bone. In you this lives as ferocious protectiveness: family, chosen or given, is where your real power concentrates.

The strength is root-deep resilience; you can hold a household together through events that should have shattered it. The friction is control disguised as care — emotional debts quietly tallied, grievances preserved like heirlooms, belonging offered with conditions attached.

Notice when you protect people from truths they could handle; that is the moment care becomes management.
