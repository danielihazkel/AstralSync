title: Mercury in Sagittarius
---

You think in horizons. Details are scaffolding; what your mind wants is the pattern, the principle, the punchline that makes the whole mess make sense. Mutable fire keeps you learning in leaps — one podcast and you have a worldview, one trip and you have a philosophy.

The strength is synthesis and candor: you connect distant fields and say the true thing everyone was politely avoiding. The friction is precision. Jupiter's territory is generous with rounding errors, so dates blur, caveats vanish, and "basically" does a lot of unpaid labor in your sentences. Bluntness, meanwhile, can land as a verdict when you meant it as weather.

Keep making the big claims — they are your gift — but let one detail-lover check your math before the idea goes public.
