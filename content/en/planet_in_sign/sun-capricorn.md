title: Sun in Capricorn
---

The long game is where you live. You measure yourself by what you have built that stands — the career, the reputation, the people kept safe — and your discipline lets you keep walking when others sit down. Duty is not a burden you resent; it is a language you speak fluently.

Here is the friction: fluency in duty can crowd out every other tongue. Rest starts to feel like theft, play like a category error, and the people close to you may experience your provision without ever feeling your presence.

Your endurance builds real things; your grimness rations the joy of having built them. Schedule one hour this week with no output attached, and defend it like a deadline.
