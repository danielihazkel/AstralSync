title: Moon in Gemini
---

You process feelings by talking about them — or texting, journaling, narrating them to yourself on a walk. An emotion doesn't feel real until it has words attached, so your first move in distress is to find someone to think out loud with. Safety is having a person who will let you circle a topic five times without telling you to get to the point.

This makes you quick to name what others can't, and genuinely fun to be upset around. But narrating a feeling is not the same as feeling it, and you can spend a week analyzing sadness you never actually let land.

When you're running on empty, you scatter: twelve tabs open, three conversations going, nothing finished. That's your cue. Put the phone down for twenty minutes and ask what you feel *before* you describe it.
