title: Pluto in Taurus
---

You come from a generation that transforms what owning means. Pluto in Taurus excavates the ground itself — money, land, food, security — and forces a reckoning with who holds resources and at what cost. In your own life, questions of survival and worth carry unusual voltage: possessions, savings, and even your body can feel like concentrated power.

Your strength is titanic endurance; when material foundations that others assumed were permanent crumble, you rebuild them. The friction is grip — hoarding, resisting necessary losses, equating net worth with self-worth until letting go of anything feels like a small death.

Practice one voluntary release each season — an object, a grudge, a revenue stream — before life chooses one for you.
