title: Pluto in Capricorn
---

You were born while the pillars were being stress-tested. Pluto in Capricorn dismantles institutions — banks, governments, corporations, careers — and your cohort has never known authority with automatic credibility. In you this lives as a double instinct: deep skepticism of hierarchies, paired with a quiet ambition to build something that would actually deserve trust.

Your strength is structural X-ray vision; you see where an organization is rotten long before the collapse, and you are unusually willing to do the unglamorous rebuilding afterward. The friction is either-or thinking about power — total cynicism on one side, or grinding yourself down trying to be the single incorruptible pillar on the other.

Pick one institution you have written off and contribute one concrete improvement to it this month.
