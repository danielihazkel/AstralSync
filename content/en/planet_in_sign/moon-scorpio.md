title: Moon in Scorpio
---

Nothing about your emotional life is casual. You feel in depths, not ripples, and you'd rather share nothing than share halfway. Trust is built slowly, tested quietly, and once given, it's total — you are the friend who stays through the worst chapter without flinching.

That depth is your power: you read undercurrents other people miss and you can sit with pain, yours or anyone's, without looking away. The friction is control. Because being exposed feels dangerous, you probe others while revealing little, hold grudges like evidence, and sometimes test people you never told they were being tested.

Depleted, you don't get loud — you go subterranean, cold, and surveillance-minded. Recharging requires privacy and one person you don't have to be strategic with. Notice when you're gathering intel instead of asking a question, and just ask the question.
