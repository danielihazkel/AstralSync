title: Sun in Leo
---

Warmth is your engine. You do not just enter rooms — you raise their temperature, and the loyalty, humor, and sheer color you bring make people feel more alive around you. Creating something with your name on it is not vanity; it is how you metabolize being here.

But applause can become the meal instead of the seasoning. When recognition runs thin, you may dim or dramatize, performing your feelings rather than stating them plainly. The strong heart that lifts others can also refuse to be seen struggling.

Your generosity is the strength; your dependence on the audience is the friction. This week, do one genuinely good piece of work and tell no one — watch what that stirs up.
