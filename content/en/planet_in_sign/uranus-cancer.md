title: Uranus in Cancer
---

Yours is a cohort that unsettled the meaning of home. Uranus in Cancer runs electricity through family, roots, and belonging — a generation for whom the traditional household stopped being the default and became one option among many.

You likely feel it as restlessness at the root: chosen family alongside given family, homes that keep changing shape, a need for closeness that coexists with a need to keep the exit visible. Your gift is emotional innovation — you can build genuinely new kinds of belonging.

The friction is that part of you still wants the old warm harbor you keep rewiring. When domestic restlessness spikes, check whether you need a truly new situation or just a renovation of the current one.
