title: Mars in Gemini
---

You fight with sentences. Mercury steers your Mars, so your first weapon is the sharper question, the faster comeback, the reframe that leaves an opponent arguing with themselves. Desire follows curiosity: you chase what you have not figured out yet, and lose appetite the moment you have.

That mental agility makes you dangerous in any negotiation and quick to find the third option nobody saw. The trade-off is scattered force. Mutable air splits your energy across five open fronts, so each gets a fifth of your fire — and a cutting remark can wound deeper than you intended long after you have forgotten the argument.

When motivation dips, do not push; switch tasks and come back. And once a day, choose the fight you will not take.
