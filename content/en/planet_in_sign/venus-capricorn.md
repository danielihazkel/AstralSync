title: Venus in Capricorn
---

You love the way you build: deliberately, for the long term, with attention to the foundation. Words are cheap in your economy — devotion is showing up, paying the bill, keeping the promise made in a year nobody else remembers. You would rather be reliable than romantic, though the reliability is the romance.

The gift is that your commitment, once given, is weatherproof; people plan lives around it. The friction is the drawbridge: you test people for seasons before softening, ration tenderness as if it might run out, and can weigh a partner's prospects so soberly that warmth starts to look like an acquisition review.

Vulnerability is not a structural weakness; it is the mortar. Let someone see you unaccomplished this month — tired, uncertain, mid-failure — and notice that they stay.
