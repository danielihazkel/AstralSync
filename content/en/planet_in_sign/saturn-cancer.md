title: Saturn in Cancer
---

You guard the soft parts. Home, family, or emotional safety was where structure felt shakiest, so you built walls — competence on the outside, a moat around the feelings. Needing people can register as risk.

The strength inside this placement is that you become the shelter you didn't reliably have: steady in a crisis, serious about protecting your people, capable of building a home that actually holds.

The friction is the drawbridge. You care deeply but confess it rarely, and loved ones can read your armor as distance. Notice when someone offers comfort and you deflect it with a joke or a chore — once this week, let the comfort land instead.
