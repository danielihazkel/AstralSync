title: Mars in Scorpio
---

Your drive runs underground. In its nocturnal home, Mars trades the open charge for the long game: you study the terrain, wait for the moment of maximum leverage, and act once — decisively. People rarely see you sweat, which is exactly how you like it. Desire has the same depth; you want fully or not at all, and half-interest is not in your vocabulary.

The strength is relentlessness. Fixed water does not evaporate: no setback, boredom, or humiliation makes you drop a goal you have truly claimed.

The friction is what you do with injury. Grudges become long-term residents, and strategic silence can shade into control. Once a season, audit your enemies list — and formally retire one grievance that is costing you more than it protects.
