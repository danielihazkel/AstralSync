title: Neptune in Scorpio
---

You were born into a generation that dreams in the dark. Neptune in Scorpio glamorizes depth — sex, death, the occult, the buried truth — and treats intensity itself as proof of meaning. You likely inherited an instinct that surfaces lie, along with a pull toward experiences that strip people down to their core.

Your strength is fearlessness about what others euphemize; you can sit with someone's worst night without flinching. The fog is intensity addiction — mistaking obsession for intimacy, chasing catharsis through substances, crisis, or dangerous company because calm feels like death.

When a bond or habit feels fated and consuming, ask what it would mean about you if you simply set it down.
