title: Mars in Leo
---

Effort, for you, is a performance — and that is not an insult. Fixed fire burns steadiest with an audience: you work harder, fight braver, and endure longer when someone is watching whose opinion you value. Your drive has warmth in it; you compete to be magnificent, not to make others small.

The strength is sustained, generous intensity. You can carry a team's morale on sheer conviction, and you rarely quit anything you have publicly claimed.

The friction is pride. A challenge to your competence can hijack a whole week, and you may keep pouring energy into a doomed effort because walking away looks like defeat. Notice when you are fighting for the outcome versus fighting for the image — and let the image lose once in a while.
