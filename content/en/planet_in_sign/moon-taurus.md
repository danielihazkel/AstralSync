title: Moon in Taurus
---

Comfort is your emotional language. A familiar meal, a warm room, a predictable evening — these aren't luxuries to you, they are how you metabolize a hard day. Your feelings move slowly and settle deep, so you're often the calmest person in a crisis and the last to be rattled by drama.

The strength here is steadiness: people lean on you because your mood doesn't swing with the weather. The catch is inertia. Once you've decided how you feel about someone or something, revising that verdict feels like moving furniture, and you can stay too long in situations simply because leaving is uncomfortable.

Depleted, you go quiet and stubborn, reaching for food or spending instead of words. Watch for the moment "I'm fine" really means "I've stopped updating" — and say one true sentence out loud instead.
