title: Mars in Cancer
---

Your anger moves sideways. Direct confrontation feels dangerous to a Moon-ruled Mars, so frustration tends to surface as withdrawal, a colder tone, help that stops arriving — signals others may miss until resentment has been simmering for weeks. But threaten someone you love and the hesitation vanishes: you fight hardest, and cleanest, in defense of your own.

That protective ferocity is the gift. People inside your walls are genuinely safer for it, and your drive is tidal — enormous when your heart is in the work, absent when it is not.

The friction is indirectness: unnamed anger leaks out at the wrong targets. When you notice yourself going quiet and busy, stop and say the actual grievance out loud within a day.
