title: Neptune in Virgo
---

Yours is a generation that spiritualizes usefulness. Neptune in Virgo turns the collective dream toward service, health, and the perfectible routine — the fantasy that if every detail were handled, life would finally be clean. You may feel closest to meaning while working: helping, fixing, refining something until it hums.

That devotion to craft is a quiet strength; you sanctify the mundane and make care concrete. The fog is anxiety wearing an apron — purity spirals around food and health, guilt about rest, the suspicion that you are only as lovable as you are useful.

When you catch yourself improving something no one asked you to fix, stop and ask whose peace you are actually chasing.
