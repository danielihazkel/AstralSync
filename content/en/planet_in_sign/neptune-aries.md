title: Neptune in Aries
---

You belong to a generation that dreams in verbs. With Neptune in Aries, the collective imagination idealizes the pioneer: the founder, the activist, the person who simply starts. In you this shows up as a longing for causes worth fighting for and a low tolerance for spirituality that just sits still.

The strength is real: you can make idealism kinetic, turning vague hopes into first moves while others are still lighting candles. The fog is impatience — mistaking adrenaline for meaning, joining crusades before checking who leads them, burning out on visions that needed a decade rather than a weekend.

When a cause sets your blood on fire, take one deliberate week to study it before you enlist.
