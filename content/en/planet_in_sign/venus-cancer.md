title: Venus in Cancer
---

You love like a lighthouse keeper: devotedly, protectively, with one eye always on the weather. Affection for you means shelter — the meal made, the blanket brought, the memory kept of what they said in April. Home is not where the relationship happens; it is what the relationship is.

That depth of care is the gift; people feel genuinely kept by you. The friction is indirectness and tide. You rarely ask outright for what you need, hinting instead and hurting when hints go unread, and a small slight can pull you into a shell no apology seems to reach.

Your moods carry real information, but your partner cannot read them unaided. Once a week, trade one hint for one plain sentence about what you actually want.
