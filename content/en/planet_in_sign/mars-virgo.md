title: Mars in Virgo
---

You attack problems by taking them apart. Where others swing hammers, you find the one screw holding the whole mess together. Mercury-ruled earth gives your drive precision: energy flows into refining, debugging, and quietly doing the thing correctly while louder people take the credit.

Anger, too, gets analyzed before it gets expressed — which usually means it comes out as critique. A Mars in Virgo rarely explodes; it edits. The people close to you may feel graded rather than fought with, and that can sting longer than a shout.

Your strength is effectiveness per unit of effort; your friction is the stalling that perfectionism causes, in your work and in your judgments of others. Ship the eighty-percent version this week, and say one appreciation for every correction.
