title: Jupiter in Libra
---

Your luck is other people. Doors open for you through partnership — the collaborator who doubles your reach, the introduction that changes your decade, the marriage or alliance that becomes an economy of two. You grow by pairing, and you have a gift for it: Venus-ruled air makes you generous with credit, graceful in negotiation, and genuinely enlarged rather than threatened by other people's success.

Fairness is more than manners to you; it is philosophy. You believe balanced arrangements outperform exploitative ones, and you are usually right.

The excess is agreeableness at scale: too many yeses, contracts softened to keep the peace, your own appetite lost in the average of everyone else's. Before your next joint decision, write your honest preference down first — then negotiate.
