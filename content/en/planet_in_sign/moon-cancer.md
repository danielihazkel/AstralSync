title: Moon in Cancer
---

You feel everything, including things that aren't yours. A friend's bad day, the mood of a room, an unanswered text — all of it registers in your body before your mind catches up. Home is not a place so much as a nervous-system setting, and you guard the people inside your circle with real ferocity.

Your strength is memory and care: you notice what people need before they ask, and you rarely forget a kindness. The friction is the shell. When hurt, you don't argue — you withdraw, go quiet, and expect the other person to decode the silence, which reads as distance rather than pain.

Recharging means retreating somewhere soft with no one to perform for. The next time you pull back, tell one person plainly, "I'm hurt, not gone" — it saves them the guessing and you the resentment.
