title: Uranus in Aries
---

You belong to a cohort wired for the unilateral move. Uranus in Aries breeds pioneers who don't ask permission — a generation whose instinct in any stuck system is to walk out and start a rival one by Friday.

In your own life, that current shows up as electric impatience: sudden pivots, projects launched overnight, an allergy to being told to wait your turn. Your genius is ignition — you make the future feel startable.

The friction is aftermath. Revolutions need administrators, and your cohort's fights can be reflexive — rebellion for its own voltage. Before your next dramatic exit or launch, ask one question: am I moving toward something, or just away? Then move anyway, on purpose.
