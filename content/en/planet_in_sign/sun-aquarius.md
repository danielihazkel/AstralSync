title: Sun in Aquarius
---

You are most fully yourself at a slight angle to the group. You see the system where others see the scene — the assumptions, the rules nobody voted on — and your detachment lets you say the useful thing everyone else is too embedded to notice. Friends collect around you because you grant them the same freedom you require.

Distance, though, can calcify into a position. Contrarianism can replace thinking, and the people who love you may feel studied rather than held. Being *interesting* comes easier to you than being reachable.

Original mind, cool hands — that is the trade. The next time someone brings you a feeling, respond with warmth first and analysis only if they ask.
