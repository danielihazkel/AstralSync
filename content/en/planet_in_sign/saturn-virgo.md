title: Saturn in Virgo
---

The details are where your discipline lives — and where it turns on you. You hold your work, health, and daily habits to a standard most people reserve for surgery, and you feel every deviation as a personal failing.

Done well, this is mastery. Your systems work, your craft is trustworthy, and you catch the error everyone else shipped. People hand you the parts that cannot be allowed to go wrong.

Done anxiously, it is paralysis — endless refinement, vigilant scanning of your own body, projects unfinished because imperfect. Usefulness, not flawlessness, is the actual assignment. When you catch yourself polishing a detail no one will ever see, ship the thing and log what happens.
