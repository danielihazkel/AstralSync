title: Jupiter in Cancer
---

Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, and the logic is old and simple: generosity grows best where it is rooted. You expand by nurturing — feeding people, remembering their hard weeks, building the home or team that others treat as harbor. Belonging is your luck engine; the family you make, by blood or choice, keeps returning opportunities you never solicited.

The strength is abundance that actually lands. Your care is concrete — soup, shelter, a made-up bed — not sentiment. The Jupiterian excess is care without an off switch: overfeeding, over-rescuing, and a nest so comfortable that neither you nor your people ever test their wings.

Notice where your generosity has become someone's ceiling. This month, help one person you love do a hard thing without doing it for them.
