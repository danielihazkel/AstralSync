title: Neptune in Aquarius
---

Your generation dreamed the network into being. Neptune in Aquarius idealizes connectivity and collective intelligence — the belief that enough linked minds can solve anything, that community can be summoned at planetary scale. You carry this as a real instinct for finding your people across any distance and imagining futures most adults dismissed.

That visionary sociability is your strength; you organize hope. The fog is the substitution trick — parasocial closeness standing in for friendship, ideology standing in for intimacy, the cause of humanity easier to love than the roommate in the kitchen. Digital utopias crash too, and disillusionment with them can curdle into detachment.

Once a day, move one online connection an inch toward the physical world — a call, a letter, a plan.
