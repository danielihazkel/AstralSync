title: Uranus in Pisces
---

Your generation dissolved the line between the real and the imagined. Uranus in Pisces marks a cohort raised as boundaries liquefied — digital and physical, waking and dreaming, one belief blurring into another in a shared ambient stream.

In you, the current is intuitive and strange: inspiration arrives sideways, spirituality resists membership, and you can feel collective moods shift before the news does. Your gift is visionary empathy — imagining futures that have feelings in them.

The friction is drift — escapism with better technology, sudden enthusiasms that evaporate, porousness to whatever the crowd is feeling this week. Vision needs a riverbank. Give one intuition a deadline and a concrete first step within a day of receiving it.
