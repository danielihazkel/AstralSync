title: Venus in Leo
---

You love in full color. Grand gestures, loyal fire, generosity that borders on theater — when you adore someone, they know it, and so does everyone within earshot. Fixed fire makes your heart both warm and constant: you do not do casual well, and you do not pretend to.

The gift is that you make people feel chosen, radiant, upgraded by your attention. The friction is the applause meter. You need to be admired, not merely loved, so a partner's quiet or distracted season can feel like betrayal when it is only weather. Pride can also keep you performing fineness long after you are hurt.

Learn the difference between attention drying up and attention resting. Ask directly for appreciation once before assuming it is gone.
