title: Venus in Sagittarius
---

For you, love should feel like a departure gate. You are drawn to people who expand your map — the foreigner, the philosopher, the friend with the motorcycle and the reading list — and your affection is generous, honest, and allergic to cages.

The gift is buoyancy: you keep romance adventurous and you forgive quickly, because grudges are luggage. The friction is the fine print. Commitment can feel like a border closing, so you may keep one foot in the doorway of even a good relationship, and your cheerful honesty sometimes tramples feelings you genuinely never meant to hurt.

Freedom and devotion are not opposites; the right structure is a base camp, not a cell. Tell partners plainly what room you need — vagueness, not wanderlust, is what breaks your relationships.
