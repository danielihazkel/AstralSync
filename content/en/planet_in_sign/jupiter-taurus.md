title: Jupiter in Taurus
---

Your faith is in the tangible. You grow by accumulation — skills, savings, gardens, relationships tended over years — and your luck tends to arrive as steady appreciation rather than lottery moments. Venus-ruled earth gives you a genius for making abundance feel like abundance: the good meal, the well-made tool, generosity that shows up as an actual full plate rather than a pep talk.

That patient building is the strength; almost everything you grow, holds. The Jupiterian excess here is appetite and attachment. Comfort can quietly become the goal instead of the fuel, more can become hoarding, and a sure thing can hold you long after a better risk appeared.

Once a quarter, trade a little security for a little growth — one deliberate, slightly uncomfortable bet on your own expansion.
