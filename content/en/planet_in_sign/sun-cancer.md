title: Sun in Cancer
---

You assert yourself sideways, like the tide — not by force but by return. Your identity is rooted in what you protect: the people, places, and memories that form your inner shoreline. When someone in your circle is struggling, you become the safe room, and that instinct to shelter is real strength, not softness.

The cost is the shell. Hurt sends you inward, and from outside your silence can look like rejection when it is actually self-repair. You remember slights with archival precision, and old grievances can quietly steer present choices.

Notice the next time you withdraw mid-conflict, and tell the other person when you will be back instead of simply disappearing.
