title: Moon in Pisces
---

Your boundaries are watercolor. Other people's moods soak into you so easily that by evening you often can't tell which feelings started as yours. This porousness makes you extraordinarily kind — you comfort without being asked, forgive without being begged — and it gives you an imagination that turns ordinary life luminous.

The friction is drift. When reality gets harsh, you slip sideways into daydream, sleep, screens, or someone else's crisis, and problems that needed a decision quietly grow. You can also confuse being needed with being loved, staying devoted to people who mostly consume you.

To recharge you need solitude near something vast or beautiful: water, music, an empty afternoon. Twice a day, pause and ask, "Is this feeling mine?" If it isn't, picture handing it back — then do one small concrete task that belongs entirely to you.
