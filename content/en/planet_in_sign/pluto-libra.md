title: Pluto in Libra
---

You belong to the generation that put partnership on trial. Pluto in Libra digs into marriage, contracts, and fairness itself — a cohort that watched unions dissolve at scale and remade the rules of commitment from the rubble. In your own life, relationships are where the deepest transformations arrive: you rarely do casual bonds so much as slow-motion metamorphoses.

Your strength is a hard-won understanding that fairness must be renegotiated, not assumed; you can rebuild a relationship mid-flight without abandoning it. The friction is power politics in soft focus — passive maneuvering, quiet scorekeeping, losing yourself in the merger and then detonating to get free.

Say the uncomfortable thing at ten percent intensity now, instead of a hundred percent later.
