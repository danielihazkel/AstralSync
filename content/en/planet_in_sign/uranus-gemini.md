title: Uranus in Gemini
---

You come from a generation that rewires how information moves. Uranus in Gemini electrifies language, media, learning, and connection — your cohort treats communication channels as things to invent, not inherit.

In you, the current runs as a fast, lateral mind: ideas arriving like static shocks, boredom with any fixed curriculum, a gift for linking fields that officially have nothing to do with each other. You learn by rerouting.

The cost is scatter — twelve open loops, brilliant threads dropped mid-sentence, novelty mistaken for progress. Your generation's noise problem becomes your personal attention problem. Pick one idea per month that gets follow-through instead of fireworks, and write down where the others are parked.
