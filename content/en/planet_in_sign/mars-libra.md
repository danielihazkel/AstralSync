title: Mars in Libra
---

You would rather win the peace than win the fight. Venus rules this Mars, so your combat style is diplomacy: you disarm through charm, build coalitions before the vote, and argue the other side's case better than they can — right before dismantling it. Striking first feels almost physically wrong to you.

That makes you a superb negotiator and the person who keeps a group from splintering. The cost is delayed self-defense. You can spend so long weighing fairness that the moment to act passes, and unexpressed anger curdles into polite, exhausting resentment.

Desire follows partnership; you push hardest with an ally beside you. Practice one small, unilateral no each week — chosen by you, unexplained, before consensus forms — and notice that nothing collapses.
