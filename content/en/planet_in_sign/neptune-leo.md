title: Neptune in Leo
---

You come from a cohort that dreams in spotlight. Neptune in Leo bathes a whole generation in glamour — romance, performance, the golden screen, the conviction that a life should be dramatic to be real. In you, this current shows up as a hunger for creative expression that feels almost devotional, and a soft spot for charismatic people.

The gift is warmth at scale: you can make ordinary moments feel mythic, and your enthusiasm gives others permission to shine. The fog is the gap between radiance and image — idolizing stars, romances, or your own persona until the applause matters more than the art.

Once a week, make something with no audience in mind, and notice whether it still feels worth doing.
