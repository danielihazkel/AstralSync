title: Mercury in Libra
---

You think in dialogue. Every position summons its counterargument, every plan its alternative, and you genuinely cannot evaluate an idea until you have walked around it and seen the other side. Cardinal air makes you a superb negotiator, editor, and explainer of one person to another.

Fairness is the strength: people trust your judgment because you visibly weighed theirs first. Deciding is the friction. The scales keep moving with each new input, menus become ordeals, and you can soften a true statement until it offends no one and informs no one either.

You are at your sharpest with a thinking partner, so borrow one for big calls. For small choices, set a two-minute limit — pick, move, and save full deliberation for decisions that deserve it.
