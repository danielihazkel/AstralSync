title: Pluto in Gemini
---

Your generation rewires the word. Pluto in Gemini drags power struggles into language, media, and the traffic of ideas — what may be said, who controls the channels, which facts get buried. You inherit a penetrating mind: you sense the message beneath the message, and small talk rarely stays small around you.

That forensic curiosity is your strength; you can name what a room is actually negotiating while everyone else discusses the weather. The friction is weaponized speech — gossip as surveillance, debate as domination, and a scattered obsessiveness that opens forty investigations and closes none.

When you learn something damaging about someone, sit with it for a day before deciding whether sharing it serves truth or just power.
