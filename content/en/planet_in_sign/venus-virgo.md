title: Venus in Virgo
---

Your love letters are folded laundry, fixed printers, and a calendar that quietly knows their dentist appointment. You express devotion through usefulness — noticing what a person needs and handling it, often before they ask. It is unglamorous, and it is profound.

The gift: people you love are genuinely better maintained, in body and logistics. The friction: the same eye that spots needs spots flaws, and a steady drip of small improvements — offered for their own good — can make a partner feel like a project. You also tend to deflect romance aimed at you, auditing compliments for accuracy instead of receiving them.

Care that only flows outward eventually curdles into resentment. This week, accept one act of service and one compliment without correcting either.
