title: Mercury in Virgo
---

Your mind runs quality control. Where others see a finished plan, you see the two unhandled edge cases, the ambiguous sentence, the step that will fail on a Tuesday. Mercury is powerful here, and it makes you precise, useful, and quietly indispensable.

The gift is craftsmanship: your work rarely needs a second pass, and your questions improve everyone else's. The friction is that analysis has no natural off switch — it turns on drafts, dinners, and people, and a steady stream of small corrections can read as disapproval even when you mean it as care.

Perfecting also delays shipping; the flawless version competes with the finished one. Before offering a fix, ask whether it changes the outcome or just the polish — and let the polish go two times out of three.
