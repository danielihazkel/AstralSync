title: Saturn in Gemini
---

Your voice took work. Saturn in Gemini often shows up as early self-consciousness about speaking, writing, or seeming smart — the sense that everyone else got the words faster. So you checked facts, chose sentences carefully, and learned instead of guessed.

That discipline pays out as precision. When you do speak, people take notes; your explanations hold up under questioning because you built them to.

The cost is the silence before the sentence — self-editing so thorough that half your ideas never leave your head, and small talk that feels like an oral exam. Practice saying the unpolished version out loud once a day; a rough draft spoken beats a perfect one withheld.
