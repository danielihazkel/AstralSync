title: Jupiter in Gemini
---

You expand sideways. Where others dig one deep well, you drill a dozen test holes — a language here, a podcast rabbit hole there, three tabs of something unrelated — and your luck lives in the connections between them. Opportunities reach you through conversation: the offhand remark at a party, the friend of a friend, the newsletter nobody else read.

Breadth is the genuine strength. You can talk to anyone about anything, and cross-pollination makes you original where specialists are predictable. The friction is that Jupiter here inflates input: more books than can be read, more courses than can be finished, a philosophy that changes with the last clever thing you heard.

Pick one thread this season and follow it past the point of easy interest — depth is your acquired taste.
