title: Uranus in Sagittarius
---

Your generation broke belief systems open. Uranus in Sagittarius marks a cohort of border-crossers — spiritually eclectic, globally minded, instinctively suspicious of any single church, party, or curriculum claiming the whole truth.

In you it shows as hunger for the far side of the map: sudden travels, philosophies swapped mid-life, an electric certainty that meaning exists but not where you were told to look. You free people from small stories, and that is a real gift.

The friction is perpetual seeking as a way to avoid landing — a new framework every year, commitment mistaken for confinement. Freedom without conclusions gets thin. Pick one conviction you have actually tested and live by it for a full season before shopping again.
