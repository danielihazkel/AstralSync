title: Moon in Aquarius
---

You experience emotion at a slight remove, as if watching the weather through good glass. Step one is always understanding the feeling — naming it, contextualizing it, comparing it to the general human condition — and only later, sometimes much later, actually having it. Safety is space: friendships without clinging, love without surveillance.

This cool head makes you superb in other people's emergencies, offering clarity where everyone else offers panic. It's also the friction point. Partners and friends can feel analyzed rather than held, and your calm "that's interesting" in a tender moment can land like a closed door.

You recharge among ideas and loose company — a long solo think, a group that demands nothing. When someone you love is upset, resist explaining their feeling back to them; sit closer, say less, and let the moment stay unsolved for five minutes.
