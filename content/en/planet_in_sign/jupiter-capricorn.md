title: Jupiter in Capricorn
---

Your optimism is earned, never issued. Saturn's sign puts Jupiter on a budget, so you distrust windfalls and believe in compounding: growth that comes from structure, reputation, and a decade of showing up. This makes your expansions unusually durable — when you finally scale something, it holds weight, and people extend you trust because your promises have a track record instead of a glow.

Strategic patience is the strength; the friction is that Jupiter is said to fall here, and it can feel like it. Hope gets means-tested. You may defer joy until some threshold of achievement that keeps moving, and read other people's easy luck as unseriousness.

Practice one act of unearned optimism this month — accept a good thing you did not build, without auditing it for the catch.
