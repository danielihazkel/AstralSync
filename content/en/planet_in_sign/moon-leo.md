title: Moon in Leo
---

Being seen is how you feel safe. Not applause, exactly — recognition. When someone notices your effort, remembers your story, laughs at your joke, your whole system settles. You give warmth generously and theatrically, and the people you love tend to feel genuinely chosen.

Your strength is loyalty with flair: you show up big, celebrate hard, and make ordinary days feel like occasions. The friction is that hurt pride and hurt feelings are tangled together for you. A small slight — being interrupted, overlooked, teased in the wrong tone — can land as rejection, and you'll perform being fine while privately keeping score.

Depleted, you get dramatic or weirdly flat, fishing for reassurance you won't request outright. Practice asking directly: "I need some appreciation today" costs less than a week of sulking for it.
