title: Jupiter in Aquarius
---

Your faith is in the future and the many. You grow through networks, movements, and ideas ahead of their season — the open-source project, the odd community that becomes a career, the cause that seemed fringe until suddenly it was not. Luck reaches you laterally: not from the boss, but from some node three connections out who saw your strange idea and got it.

The strength is principled breadth. Fixed air lets you hold a humane vision steadily for decades, and your generosity scales — you would rather fix the system than rescue one person from it.

The excess is abstraction: loving humanity in concept while the actual human at your table waits, and collecting ideals faster than commitments. Pick one cause and one person, and this week give each an hour that costs you something.
