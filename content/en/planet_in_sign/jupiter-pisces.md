title: Jupiter in Pisces
---

You believe in what cannot be measured, and it keeps paying off in ways no spreadsheet predicts. Jupiter has ancient rulership here, and it expands through compassion, imagination, and surrender — the hunch you followed, the stranger you helped who reappeared years later as a door, the creative work that arrived more than it was made. Your generosity has no gatekeeping; you give to the undeserving on principle, because mercy is the point.

That boundless faith is the strength, and it is real: people heal a little in your vicinity.

The excess is porousness. Without edges, compassion floods into rescue patterns, finances blur, and escapism can wear spirituality's clothing. Choose one boundary — money, hours, or one specific person — and hold it kindly for thirty days.
