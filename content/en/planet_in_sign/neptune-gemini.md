title: Neptune in Gemini
---

You were born into a generation enchanted by words. Neptune in Gemini pours the collective dream into language, media, and the sheer romance of information — the belief that if we could just say it right, everything would connect. In your own life, ideas can feel numinous: a book, a conversation, a clever turn of phrase can move you the way scripture moves others.

Your strength is translation. You give shimmering, half-formed things a voice, and you make strangers feel understood. The fog is credulity dressed as curiosity — rumors that feel true because they are interesting, and a habit of talking about the dream instead of living it.

When a claim delights you, treat the delight as a cue to verify, not to repeat it.
