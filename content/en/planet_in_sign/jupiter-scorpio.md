title: Jupiter in Scorpio
---

You grow in the deep end. Surfaces bore your faith; you expand through intensity — the crisis that remade you, the intimacy that required everything, the research obsession that went three layers past sensible. Where others find luck in networking, you find it in trust: the few people who have seen your depths hand you opportunities no acquaintance ever could. Shared resources, inheritance, other people's money and secrets — these are classic Jupiter-in-Scorpio channels.

The strength is transformative resilience: every collapse in your history became compost, and you know it, which makes you unusually brave about endings.

The excess is intensity as a requirement — dismissing calm goodness as shallow, and turning insight into leverage. Let one relationship stay easy and unexamined this year, and watch it hold anyway.
