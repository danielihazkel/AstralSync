title: Sun in Sagittarius
---

You are fueled by the horizon. Meaning matters more to you than comfort, and your instinct to zoom out — to ask what the story is *for* — gives everyone around you oxygen when the details have gone stale. Your honesty lands like weather: sudden, cleansing, occasionally destructive.

- **Strength:** you make people believe bigger things are possible.
- **Friction:** you promise arrival before checking the distance.

Blunt truth without timing wounds people you like, and enthusiasm without follow-through erodes the trust your vision depends on. Before your next big claim, write down the first three steps — if you cannot, say you are exploring, not committing.
