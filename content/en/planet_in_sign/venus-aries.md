title: Venus in Aries
---

Desire, for you, is a verb. You fall fast, pursue openly, and find the early days of attraction — the dare, the chase, the first spark of yes — more intoxicating than most people find the whole relationship. There is no guessing game with your affection: people know.

That directness is the gift; you spare everyone the months of ambiguity. The friction arrives after the win. Comfort can read as boredom, ease as an ending, and you may pick a fight just to feel the temperature rise again.

You love best alongside a partner who stays slightly wild, and you wilt under possessiveness. When a good relationship starts feeling flat, aim your hunger at a shared challenge before concluding the love is gone.
