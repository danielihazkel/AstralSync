title: Mars in Capricorn
---

Mars is exalted here, and the reason is simple: Saturn teaches fire to hold formation. Your drive is a campaign, not a charge — you pick goals measured in years, break them into ledgers and load-bearing steps, and keep showing up on the days motivation does not. Anger runs cold rather than hot: controlled, deliberate, and often expressed as consequences instead of words.

That discipline is your edge; you outlast nearly everyone. It comes with two frictions. Efficiency can flatten play — even rest becomes a scheduled deliverable — and cold anger can read as contempt to people who needed a human argument instead of a verdict.

Keep the ambition; loosen the grip. Put one pointless, unproductive pleasure on this week's calendar and defend it like a deadline.
