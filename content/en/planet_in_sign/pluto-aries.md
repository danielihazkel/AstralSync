---

Your cohort digs up the buried question of raw will. Pluto in Aries transforms what it means to begin, to fight, to survive as an individual — a generation compelled to tear down inherited notions of courage and rebuild identity from first instinct. In you this runs as an almost volcanic need for self-determination: being told what to do can feel like being erased.

The strength is regenerative nerve; you restart from zero the way others reheat leftovers, and setbacks tend to sharpen rather than shrink you. The friction is combustion — power struggles picked mainly to feel your own pulse, and bridges burned for an independence you later needed help to use.

Before your next declaration of war, check whether the real opponent is boredom.
