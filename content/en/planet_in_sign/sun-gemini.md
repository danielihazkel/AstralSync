title: Sun in Gemini
---

Your vitality runs on questions. A new idea does for you what sunlight does for other people: you get sharper, funnier, more awake. You translate between worlds — technical and casual, old friends and new rooms — and that agility is a genuine gift, not a party trick.

The friction shows up as scatter. Three open loops feel energizing; nine feel like static, and the people waiting on you cannot tell the difference from the outside.

- Keep a visible list of what you have promised.
- Close one loop before opening the next.

Today, finish the smallest unfinished thing on that list and notice how much cleaner your thinking gets.
