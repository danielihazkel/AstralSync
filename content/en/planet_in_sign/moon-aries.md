title: Moon in Aries
---

Your feelings arrive fast, hot, and unfiltered. Anger, excitement, and hurt all hit at full volume, and you rarely need time to figure out what you feel — you need somewhere to put it. Safety, for you, is motion: the freedom to act on an emotion the moment it appears.

That speed is a real gift. You recover quickly, you don't nurse grudges, and people always know where they stand with you. The friction is that you can torch a conversation in the thirty seconds before your feeling finishes forming, then wonder why everyone else is still upset an hour later.

When you're depleted, you get snappish and restless rather than sad. Next time a surge hits, do one physical thing — walk a block, do ten push-ups — before you send the message.
