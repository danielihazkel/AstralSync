title: Neptune in Sagittarius
---

Your cohort carries a dream of the far horizon. Neptune in Sagittarius pours collective faith into journeys, teachers, and grand unifying meaning — the pilgrimage, the guru, the philosophy that will finally explain everything. In you it lives as restlessness of the soul: a sense that truth is out there, one more trip or book or teacher away.

The gift is genuine breadth; your optimism is contagious and your searching keeps whole rooms from going stale. The fog is the guru problem — outsourcing discernment to charismatic voices, and using the next horizon to dodge the lesson sitting right in front of you.

Before adopting a new teacher or worldview, list what it conveniently excuses you from.
