title: Sun in Pisces
---

Boundaries are thinner where you stand. Other people's moods reach you before their words do, and your imagination gives form to what most can only vaguely feel — in art, in kindness, in the uncanny accuracy of your hunches. Compassion is not something you practice; it is the medium you live in.

That permeability is also the leak. You absorb what is not yours, drift when structure is required, and escape — into fantasy, screens, or self-sacrifice — can masquerade as flow. Saying no feels like cruelty even when it is only clarity.

Empathy is your strength and dissolution is its price. At the end of today, name one feeling you carried that belonged to someone else, and set it down.
