title: Mercury in Pisces
---

You think in images, echoes, and atmospheres. Where linear minds walk from A to B, yours dissolves the alphabet and arrives somewhere true by a route you cannot retrace. Mutable water makes you porous: you absorb moods, finish other people's sentences internally, and know things before you can defend them.

That intuition is real — your first read on a person or a room is often years ahead of the evidence. The friction is precision: dates, names, and step two of five slide out of memory, and in arguments you may feel the right answer while losing to someone who merely organized theirs.

Write intuitions down the moment they arrive, then verify one detail before you act. Your hunches deserve better bookkeeping than your memory provides.
