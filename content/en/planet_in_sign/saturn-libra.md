title: Saturn in Libra
---

Commitment is serious business to you. Saturn works well in Libra: partnership, contracts, and fairness are where you do your deepest work — and where you feel the most weight. You would rather be alone than in something halfway honest.

Your strength is judgment. You weigh both sides for real, keep your agreements, and become the person others trust to arbitrate. Bonds you build slowly, sometimes late, tend to outlast everyone else's.

The friction is formality and fear of tipping the scales — avoiding conflict to preserve balance until the imbalance is you, over-giving quietly and calling it fairness. Next time resentment surfaces in a relationship, treat it as data and renegotiate the terms out loud.
