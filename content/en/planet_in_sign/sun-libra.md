title: Sun in Libra
---

Fairness is not a policy for you; it is a reflex. You instinctively weigh the other side, smooth the sharp edge, and find the phrasing everyone can live with — rooms are genuinely better with you in them because of it. Partnership sharpens your identity: you often discover what you think by thinking alongside someone.

The friction is that constant weighing has a cost of its own. Decisions stall while you audit every angle, and keeping the peace can mean quietly abandoning your own position. Resentment builds in the gap between what you agreed to and what you actually wanted.

State one plain preference today — restaurant, plan, opinion — before asking anyone else what they would like.
