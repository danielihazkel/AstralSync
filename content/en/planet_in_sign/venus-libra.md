title: Venus in Libra
---

Venus rules here, and it gives you a genius for the duet. You instinctively harmonize — matching energy, smoothing edges, making the other person feel like the best version of themselves. Beauty matters to you the way air matters: proportion, courtesy, the rightness of a well-set table.

Partnership is your natural habitat, and that is the gift; you make relating feel elegant. The friction is your allergy to discord. You will agree to restaurants you hate and shelve grievances for months, then wonder why resentment leaks out sideways. Merging so smoothly, you can lose track of what you wanted before you started accommodating.

A voiced preference is not an act of war. State one honest "actually, I'd rather..." this week and let the small friction happen.
