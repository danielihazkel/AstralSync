title: Venus in Taurus
---

Venus is at home here, and it shows in everything you touch: the good blanket, the long dinner, the playlist unchanged for five years because it is simply correct. You love through presence and appetite — slowly won, deeply loyal, physically affectionate.

Steadiness is your gift. Partners relax around you because your affection does not flicker with mood; what you promised on Tuesday is still true in March. The shadow is grip: love can shade into ownership, comfort into rut, and letting go — of a person, a grudge, a couch — costs you more than it costs most people.

You show love in matter, not manifesto, and that is fine. Just watch for the moment security becomes stasis, and book something unfamiliar together before the routine chooses for you.
