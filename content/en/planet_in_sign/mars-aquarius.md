title: Mars in Aquarius
---

Tell you the rule and you will test it. Your drive activates against systems more than against people: the outdated process, the unjust policy, the assumption everyone stopped questioning. Fixed air fights with strategy and stubborn principle — you can hold an unpopular position for years without needing anyone's applause, and anger comes out analytical, almost chilly, aimed at the argument rather than the person.

That detachment is a real strength: you stay operational in conflicts that make others lose their heads, and you fight fair. The friction is contrarianism as reflex — opposing a thing simply because the majority likes it — and a distance that leaves allies unsure you care.

Next time you dig in, check whether you are defending a principle or just a posture, and tell one ally, plainly, that the fight matters to you.
