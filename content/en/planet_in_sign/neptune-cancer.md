title: Neptune in Cancer
---

Your generation dreams of home. Neptune in Cancer soaks the collective imagination in belonging — the perfect family, the motherland, the kitchen where everyone is finally safe. You likely carry this as a tender radar for emotional atmosphere and a longing for places and people that feel like origin.

At its best this makes you a sanctuary: you create rooms, meals, and rituals where others let their guard down, and your empathy is nearly physical. The fog is nostalgia — grieving a golden past that partly never existed, or clinging to family myths that keep everyone small.

When homesickness hits, ask whether you miss a real place or a feeling — and if it is a feeling, build one small piece of it where you live now.
