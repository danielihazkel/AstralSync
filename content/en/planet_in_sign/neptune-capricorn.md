title: Neptune in Capricorn
---

You belong to a generation whose dreams wear suits. Neptune in Capricorn filters the collective imagination through institutions and ambition: the meaningful career, the company with a mission, success as a kind of salvation. You probably absorbed both the longing — that work should mean something — and the disillusionment when structures reveal their hollowness.

Your strength is that you can give ideals a skeleton: budgets, timelines, roles. Where others only dream, you build. The fog is mistaking status for substance — climbing ladders that lean on nothing, or turning cynical when institutions fail, as if disappointment were the same thing as depth.

When a role loses its meaning, revise the mission before you conclude that meaning itself was the illusion.
