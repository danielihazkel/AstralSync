title: Mercury in Aries
---

Your mind moves like a sprinter out of the blocks. Conclusions arrive fast — sometimes before the question has finished being asked — and you trust the first clear thought more than the fifth careful one. In debate you are direct, competitive, and refreshingly free of hedging.

That decisiveness is a real edge in a crisis, when someone has to make the call. The friction shows up in deliberation: slow talkers exhaust you, caveats feel like cowardice, and you can end up defending a hasty position simply because retreating stings.

You learn by doing, not by reading the manual twice. Before you fire off the verdict, try asking one more question — the pause costs seconds and saves arguments.
