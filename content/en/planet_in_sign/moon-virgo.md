title: Moon in Virgo
---

When something hurts, you reach for a system. You tidy the kitchen, make a list, research the problem — anxiety converts itself into useful motion almost before you notice it's anxiety. Feeling helpful is your version of feeling safe, and small acts of service are how you say what you can't quite say.

That translation is a strength: you're the person who actually brings soup, fixes the spreadsheet, remembers the appointment. The cost is that worry runs constantly under the surface, and you can turn it on yourself — auditing your own words hours after a conversation, or on others as criticism that's really just fear wearing glasses.

You recharge through order and quiet, not pep talks. When you catch yourself improving everything in sight, stop and check whether the real sentence is "I'm scared" — then say that instead of reorganizing the closet.
