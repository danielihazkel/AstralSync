title: Saturn in Sagittarius
---

You take meaning seriously — too seriously to accept it secondhand. Easy answers, inherited religion, motivational certainty: all of it triggers your inner auditor. A belief has to survive cross-examination before you will live by it.

That rigor makes you a genuine teacher. Your worldview was built brick by brick and tested against experience, so it carries weight that borrowed philosophies can't.

The cost is the long stretch of doubt in the middle — seasons where nothing feels provable and cynicism starts impersonating wisdom. Skepticism is your tool, not your home. When you catch yourself dismissing an idea simply for being hopeful, give it the same trial you would give a grim one.
