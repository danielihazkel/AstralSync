title: Venus in Scorpio
---

You do not date; you merge. Fixed water gives your affections a depth and totality that casual culture has no vocabulary for — when you choose someone, you want the unedited version: their history, their shadows, the password to the whole vault.

The gift is a loyalty that survives what would end other relationships; people you love are seen to the bone and defended to the end. The friction is that same intensity turned anxious: jealousy, loyalty tests you never announce, the habit of requiring everyone's secrets while withholding your own. And betrayal, once registered, gets carved in stone.

Intimacy grows from risks you take, not proofs you demand. Reveal something first — and when the fear spikes, look for evidence before you look through their phone.
