title: Neptune in Libra
---

You share a generational dream of the perfect partner. Neptune in Libra idealizes harmony itself — soulmates, peace treaties, the beautiful union that will redeem everything. In your personal life this can make relationship feel like a spiritual project: you sense what connection could be, and you reach for it in marriages, friendships, and collaborations.

The strength is genuine grace; you soften conflict and help mismatched people find a shared key. The fog is projection — falling for your image of someone, keeping peace by dissolving your own edges, staying in almost-right bonds because the ideal glows brighter than the person.

In any close bond, practice naming one honest disagreement out loud; real harmony survives it.
