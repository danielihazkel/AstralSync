title: Mercury in Cancer
---

You remember conversations the way other people record them. Facts arrive wrapped in feeling: who said it, in what tone, and how the room felt at the time. That emotional metadata makes you an uncanny reader of subtext and a listener people confide in without quite knowing why.

The same sensitivity blurs the line between disagreement and rejection. A blunt critique of your idea can land as a critique of you, and hurt can make you go quiet exactly when speaking up would help.

You persuade best through story and memory, not bullet points — use that. When feedback stings, write down the literal words that were said and read them tomorrow; the content is often kinder than the tone you stored.
