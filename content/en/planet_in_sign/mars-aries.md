title: Mars in Aries
---

Your drive comes in one flavor: straight ahead. Mars is at home in Aries, and it shows — you want the obstacle in front of you, named, so you can hit it. Anger flares fast, burns clean, and leaves little residue; you are often the first person ready to forgive because you already had the fight.

The strength is honest, immediate courage — nobody has to guess where you stand. The friction is stamina and collateral. Long campaigns bore you, and people with slower tempers can still be smoldering days after you have moved on.

Before your next confrontation, ask one question: is this a sprint or a siege? If it is a siege, recruit help before you charge.
