title: Pluto in Scorpio
---

You were born with Pluto in its own sign, and nothing about your generation is casual. This cohort drags the taboos into the open — sex, death, abuse, debt, contamination — and insists on looking directly at them. You personally tend to trust only what has been tested: intensity feels honest, and small talk feels like lying politely.

The gift is transformative depth; you can accompany people through experiences that break others, and you regenerate from losses that should have ended you. The friction is scorched earth — suspicion as a default setting, control as safety, and cutting people off cleanly where repair was still possible.

Once you have decided someone is untrustworthy, gather one more piece of evidence before acting on the verdict.
