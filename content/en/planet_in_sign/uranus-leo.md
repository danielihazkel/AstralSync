title: Uranus in Leo
---

Your generation electrified self-expression. Uranus in Leo produced a cohort that treats identity as performance art — originals and showstoppers who refuse to shine on anyone else's stage or schedule.

That current gives you a flair for reinvention: you can rebrand a life, a look, or a creative voice overnight, and you're at your best when the work is unmistakably yours. Ordinary applause bores you; you want recognition for the strange thing only you do.

The trap is rebellion aimed at attention — contrarianism as costume, drama as proof of specialness. Originality that requires an audience isn't quite free yet. Make your next creative move before announcing it anywhere, and see if it still excites you.
