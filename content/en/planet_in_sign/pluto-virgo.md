title: Pluto in Virgo
---

Your cohort took apart the machinery of daily life. Pluto in Virgo transforms work, health, and service — a generation that dissected routines, medicine, diets, and job descriptions, then rebuilt them under intense scrutiny. In you this shows as a compulsion to optimize: the flawed detail glows in your field of vision like a hot coal.

The strength is surgical usefulness; you fix systems others merely complain about, and your standards genuinely raise the floor for everyone. The friction is the purge that never ends — health anxiety, workaholism, criticism turned inward until no version of you passes inspection.

When you finish something, name three things it does well before you list its defects, and say them out loud.
