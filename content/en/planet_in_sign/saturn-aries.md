title: Saturn in Aries
---

Asserting yourself is the skill life keeps grading you on. Where others charge, you hesitate — anger swallowed, wants understated, the first move rehearsed until the moment passes. Saturn here often marks someone who learned early that boldness gets punished.

The reward for working this edge is formidable: when you finally act, you act with a deliberateness raw impulsives never develop. Your courage, once built, is load-bearing and doesn't crumble under pushback.

The friction is delay and self-blame — mistaking caution for virtue while resentment collects underneath. Watch for the moment you draft the assertive message and then don't send it. This month, make one direct request without softening it first.
