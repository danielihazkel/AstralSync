title: Saturn in Leo
---

Being seen is the test. Somewhere early, expressing yourself — performing, creating, taking the spotlight — got graded harshly, and you concluded that shine must be earned, never simply enjoyed. So you rehearse while others improvise.

That severity produces real craft. When you finally step forward, the work under you is solid, and your confidence, once built, doesn't wobble with the applause meter.

The trap is outsourcing your worth to the audience — over-preparing, postponing the debut, dismissing the praise you privately crave. Warmth withheld from yourself eventually gets withheld from your work. Make something this month that no one will grade, and let it be visibly, unapologetically yours.
