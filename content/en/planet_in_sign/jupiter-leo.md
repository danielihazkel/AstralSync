title: Jupiter in Leo
---

You grow in the light. Visibility is not vanity for you — it is photosynthesis. The promotion, the stage, the room where your name is known: these expand you, and you repay attention with real warmth, championing other people's talents as loudly as your own. Your luck arrives through being seen; the right person notices your work because you were unembarrassed to show it.

Fixed fire makes your faith durable and theatrical — big gestures, big loyalty, celebrations that people remember for years. The excess is scale. Jupiter here can inflate self-image faster than substance, spend on splendor before rent, and hear feedback as insult when it arrives without applause first.

Keep performing — but once a month, do something generous that no one will ever find out about, and notice how it feels.
