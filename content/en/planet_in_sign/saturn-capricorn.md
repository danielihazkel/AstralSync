title: Saturn in Capricorn
---

Saturn is at home here, and so is the workload. Responsibility found you early; you may have felt like the adult in the room before you were one. Ambition, for you, is less a dream than a duty roster.

The payoff is real authority. You understand structures — time, hierarchy, consequence — and you build careers and reputations that compound for decades. People with this placement tend to age into power.

The tax is heaviness: workaholism, an inner critic with a corner office, and the suspicion that rest must be earned but somehow never is. Achievement can quietly replace living. Put one non-negotiable block of unproductive time on this week's calendar and defend it like a deadline.
