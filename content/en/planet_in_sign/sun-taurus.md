title: Sun in Taurus
---

Slow is not lazy in your hands — it is method. You build a life the way soil builds itself, layer by layer, and what you finish tends to last. Comfort, beauty, and steady work are not distractions from your purpose; they *are* your purpose, and people relax around you because your presence promises that things will hold.

Watch the line where steadiness hardens into refusal. You can mistake familiarity for correctness and defend a habit long after it stops serving you. Change rarely announces itself politely, and you sometimes pay extra for ignoring the early notices.

Name one routine you keep purely because it is old, and test a small variation on it this month.
