title: Mars in Sagittarius
---

You do not just want to win; you want the win to mean something. Jupiter aims this Mars at horizons, so your energy surges when a goal doubles as a cause — the race that raises money, the argument that defends a principle, the project that takes you somewhere new. Deprived of meaning or movement, your drive simply leaves the building.

Enthusiasm is your renewable fuel and your best recruiting tool; people sign up for things because you believe in them out loud. The friction is aim. Mutable fire overshoots: you promise more than a calendar can hold, and blunt honesty fired from the hip hits bystanders.

Before committing to the next crusade, do one unglamorous act of arithmetic — hours, money, distance — and let the numbers vote.
