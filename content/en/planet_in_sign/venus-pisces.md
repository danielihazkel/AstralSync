title: Venus in Pisces
---

Venus is exalted in Pisces, and it gives you a heart with no fences. You love inclusively, imaginatively, and slightly beyond the visible — falling for potential, for the wounded, for the soul you can sense behind an unpromising Tuesday self.

The gift is a compassion that makes people feel accepted at depths they hide from everyone else. The friction is the missing boundary: you idealize freely, forgive on credit, and can spend years devoted to who someone could become while their actual behavior drains you. Saying no feels like cruelty, so you say maybe and quietly suffer.

Romance, music, water, art — you need enchantment like a nutrient; keep it supplied. But keep one dry ledger too: write down what a person does, not what you feel they meant, and reread it before forgiving the same thing twice.
