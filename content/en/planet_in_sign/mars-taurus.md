title: Mars in Taurus
---

Nothing about your effort is quick, and nothing about it is fragile. Fixed earth gives your drive a diesel quality: slow ignition, enormous torque. You may take weeks to commit to a goal, but once your weight shifts, deadlines, discouragement, and other people's doubt mostly bounce off.

Anger works the same way. You absorb provocation calmly — until a line is crossed and the response arrives with surprising force, often over something that looks small to outsiders because they missed the forty provocations before it.

Endurance is your strength; inertia is your friction, in both directions. Starting is hard, and so is stopping what no longer serves you. Watch for the moment persistence quietly becomes refusal, and let one stalled commitment go this month.
