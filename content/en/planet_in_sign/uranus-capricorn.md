title: Uranus in Capricorn
---

You belong to a cohort born to renovate institutions. Uranus in Capricorn marks a generation that watched governments, corporations, and career ladders wobble — and concluded that structures are not sacred, merely software that can be rewritten.

In your own life, this reads as ambitious rebellion: you want authority, but on rebuilt terms — flatter teams, newer industries, success defined sideways from your parents' version. You are at your best modernizing something old that still matters.

The tension is between the ladder-climber and the ladder-burner in you; you can sabotage your own advancement the moment it starts smelling like conformity. Before quitting a system in disgust, ask whether you could redesign your corner of it first.
