title: Uranus in Virgo
---

You belong to a generation that revolutionized work itself. Uranus in Virgo puts electricity into the mundane — health, labor, systems, service — producing a cohort that automates, optimizes, and questions how daily life is supposed to run.

In your own life, that manifests as an inventor's eye for broken routines: you can't watch an inefficient process without redesigning it, and your best ideas arrive while fixing something small. Unconventional approaches to health and craft come naturally to you.

The friction is perfectionist restlessness — endlessly overhauling systems that mostly worked, treating your own body and schedule as a permanent beta test. Not everything needs disruption. Before your next optimization, name what the current system does well, and keep that part.
