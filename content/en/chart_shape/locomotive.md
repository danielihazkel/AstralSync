title: A Locomotive chart
---

Your planets spread across about two-thirds of the wheel and leave a clean third empty. That empty arc works like a missing piece you keep reaching for, and the reaching becomes momentum. This shape tends to show self-driven people who generate their own motivation and keep going long after the initial spark has faded.

The leading planet, the first one after the empty space, acts as the engine pulling the rest of the train. Its sign and house describe the style of your drive and the arena where your efforts most often start. When you are not sure what to do next, that planet's themes usually point the way.

The strength is sustained power: you can carry long projects through to the end. The friction is that drive can become compulsion, a sense that stopping means falling behind. Practical power needs a rest stop. This week, schedule one real pause in a project you are pushing hard, and notice whether the engine runs better after it.
