title: A Bucket chart
---

Nine of your planets gather in one half of the wheel while a single planet sits alone on the far side. That lone planet is the handle, and it carries a disproportionate share of your chart's weight. Everything the other nine build up looks for an exit, and the handle is the outlet it finds — a channel through which your talents, frustrations, and ambitions pour into the world.

Read the handle planet's sign and house closely, because that is where your life tends to concentrate its drive. People with this shape often have one cause, craft, or relationship that organises everything else, and they can pursue it with startling intensity.

The gift is direction: a whole chart funnelled toward a point gets things done. The friction is lopsidedness. When the handle is blocked or overused, the rest of you can feel stranded with nowhere to go. Notice this week where you push everything through one channel, and give the bowl side of your life some attention of its own.
