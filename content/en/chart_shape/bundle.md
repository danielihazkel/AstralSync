title: A Bundle chart
---

All ten of your planets sit within a third of the wheel, leaving two-thirds of the zodiac empty. Your energy is packed into a narrow band of signs and houses, so you tend to know early what you care about and to pour yourself into it with unusual completeness. You are self-contained: you build a world from a small set of materials and live inside it thoroughly.

This concentration is a real asset. You specialise, you go deep, and you rarely waste effort on things that do not speak to you. The friction is that the empty part of the wheel can feel like foreign territory. Interests, people, or life areas outside your band may seem irrelevant until circumstance forces them on you, and then you can feel strangely unequipped.

The empty two-thirds is not a lack so much as an open frontier. Pick one house you have no planets in, and this month do one small, deliberate thing in its territory just to see what it teaches you.
