title: A Splash chart
---

Your planets spread evenly around the whole wheel with no large empty stretch. Every quarter of life has something in it, and you tend to have a genuine interest in a little of everything: people, ideas, places, crafts. You are wide-ranging and many-sided, and you rarely feel that some part of the world is closed to you.

That breadth makes you adaptable and well informed, able to talk to almost anyone and to connect fields that specialists keep apart. The friction is scattering. With every door open, you can spread your energy so thinly that nothing gets your full weight, and you may start far more than you finish.

The aim is not to narrow yourself but to choose a season's focus. Pick one thread that matters most right now, give it a protected hour this week, and let the other interests wait without guilt.
