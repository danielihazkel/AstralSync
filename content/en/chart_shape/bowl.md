title: A Bowl chart
---

Every planet you have sits within one half of the wheel, and the other half lies open. You live from one side of experience — holding, gathering, containing — and you are always faintly aware of the empty half you face. That awareness gives your life a sense of mission: something out there seems to need what you carry, and you feel pulled to supply it.

The leading planet, the first one after the empty space, is where that sense of purpose tends to break out into action. Its sign and house show how you step toward the unoccupied half and what you bring when you do.

The strength here is focus with a direction; you rarely drift. The friction is a feeling of incompleteness, as if the other half of life belongs to someone else, which can make you over-rely on partners or causes to fill it. Watch what you hand over to others by default, and try carrying one of those things yourself this week.
