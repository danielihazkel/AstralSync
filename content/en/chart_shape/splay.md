title: A Splay chart
---

Your planets gather in three or more distinct clumps scattered around the wheel, separated by open stretches. Each clump is its own centre of gravity, and together they describe a person with several strong, individual interests that refuse to be filed under one heading. You are hard to categorise, and you tend to like it that way.

The strength is originality. You draw on separate reservoirs of skill and feeling, and you combine them in ways nobody else would think of. You also resist being told how your life should fit together, which keeps you independent. The friction is that others may find you hard to read, and you may struggle to explain why these different parts of you belong in one life.

You do not need a single line to be whole. Try naming each of your clusters by what it does for you, then notice this week which one you neglect when the others get busy.
