title: A Seesaw chart
---

Your planets fall into two groups facing each other across the wheel, with empty space between them on both sides. Your life tends to be organised around balancing acts: two careers, two homes, two loyalties, or simply two strong temperaments in one person. You see both sides of almost any question because both sides live in you.

This makes you a natural mediator, negotiator, and fair judge. You weigh things others would decide on impulse, and you rarely mistake one perspective for the whole truth. The friction is oscillation. You can swing between the two groups, committing fully to one side and then the other, or stall in indecision while you keep re-weighing a choice already made.

The work is not to pick a winner but to build a bridge. Look for the planets that link your two groups by aspect, since they often hold the key to integration, and this week find one action that serves both sides of a dilemma at once.
