title: Solar arc directions
---

In solar arc directions, every point of your birth chart is moved forward at the Sun's progressed pace — roughly one degree for every year you have lived — and read against the chart you were born with. Nothing in the sky actually moves this way; it is a symbolic clock, and that is its strength. Because all points advance by the same arc, the technique speaks with one voice: when a directed planet reaches an exact contact with a natal planet or angle, it names a year, not a mood.

Read it sparingly. A directed contact within one degree is "in effect" for roughly a year either side of exactness, and the events it describes tend to feel structural — a change of chapter rather than a change of weather. Directed contacts to the Ascendant, Midheaven, Sun, or Moon carry the most weight; contacts between slow planets repeat themes you will recognize from the natal aspect they echo.

When a contact is listed here, look up the natal pairing it activates and reread it as a development due now. If nothing is within orb, the arc is between appointments — a useful quiet in which the last contact's changes settle.
