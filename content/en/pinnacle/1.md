title: Pinnacle 1
---

This chapter asks you to stand on your own two feet. In whichever stretch of life it arrives, it tends to hand you situations where waiting for permission costs more than acting: a job where you have to make the call, a move you make alone, a relationship that only works if you keep a self apart from it. Initiative is the curriculum, and the tests are rarely polite.

The opportunity is a self you actually chose. Years like these build confidence through evidence — you started the thing, you survived the mistakes, you can name what you want without checking the room first. The shadow is independence hardening into isolation: refusing help as a point of pride, steamrolling people who move slower, or mistaking stubbornness for leadership.

Treat this chapter as an apprenticeship in self-direction. Once a month, make one decision that is fully yours — small or large — without polling anyone, then write down what happened. The record becomes the confidence.
