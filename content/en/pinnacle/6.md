title: Pinnacle 6
---

This chapter centers on love and the responsibilities that come with it. Marriage, children, caring for parents, making a home, or taking on a role in which others depend on you tend to define the period. Work may lean toward service, teaching, healing, or anything that improves the places people live. The question underneath is what it means to look after something well.

The opportunity is deep belonging. You can become the steady presence a family or community gathers around, and the bonds you invest in now often outlast the chapter itself. The shadow is caretaking without limits — fixing people who did not ask, carrying burdens out of guilt, or quietly keeping score of all you have given.

Responsibility includes your own life. Once a week, do one thing for yourself with the same seriousness you bring to others, and say a clear no to one request that is not yours to carry.
