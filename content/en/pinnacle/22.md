---

This chapter asks you to build at scale. It carries the 4's discipline and love of structure, but points it toward projects larger than one life — organizations, systems, institutions, or work whose results reach many people. In whichever stretch of life it arrives, ambitions can grow, responsibilities can multiply, and you may find yourself trusted with plans other people would find intimidating.

The opportunity is turning vision into something concrete and lasting: a business that employs people, a program that serves a community, a body of work that outlives the effort. The shadow is the weight of it. Pressure can harden into control, perfectionism can stall the start, and the gap between the blueprint and the slow reality can tempt you back into playing small.

Big structures are built one load-bearing piece at a time. Write down the large aim, then name the single next task you can complete this week. Repeat that, and let the scale accumulate rather than arrive.
