title: Pinnacle 2
---

This is a chapter about other people, and about the patience it takes to work well with them. Partnerships of every kind come forward — a marriage or close relationship, a business alliance, a team where your role is to hold things together rather than lead. Progress tends to come through cooperation and timing, not force, and much of the real work happens quietly, behind someone else's name.

The opportunity is mastery of the subtle arts: listening, mediating, reading a room, knowing when to wait. People will trust you with delicate situations because you handle them gently. The shadow is disappearing into the role — absorbing everyone's moods, avoiding conflict until resentment does the talking, or measuring your worth only by how useful you are to others.

Diplomacy needs a spine to stand on. Practice one honest request a week, phrased kindly and left unapologized-for, and notice that the partnership survives your having needs.
