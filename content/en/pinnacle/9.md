title: Pinnacle 9
---

This chapter widens the circle and closes old loops. It tends to bring work or causes that serve more than your own household — teaching, the arts, healing, community or humanitarian effort — along with endings: relationships that have run their course, roles you have outgrown, places it is time to leave. In whichever stretch of life it arrives, the lesson is generosity, including the generosity of letting go.

The opportunity is perspective. You can become wiser, more forgiving, and more useful to people beyond your immediate world, and what you release clears room for what matters. The shadow is clinging to what is already finished, or giving so broadly that you neglect the people closest to you and end up feeling unappreciated.

Practice completion deliberately. Each season, finish or formally end one thing — a project, an obligation, a grudge — and give away something you no longer need. Lightness is part of the point.
