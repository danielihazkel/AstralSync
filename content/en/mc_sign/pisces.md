title: Midheaven in Pisces
---

Your vocation resists a job title. You are pulled toward work that channels something — art, healing, service, imagery, the unquantifiable — and your career path tends to arrive by tide rather than by plan: the right person appears, a side interest becomes the main story, a door opens that you never knocked on. Your reputation is atmospheric; people struggle to say exactly what you do, but they feel your work.

- Your strength: permeability. You absorb what an audience, client, or era needs before it is spoken, and your work carries an emotional accuracy that cannot be taught.
- Your friction: boundaries and business. You undercharge, overgive, drift when structure is absent, and let others define your role because defining it yourself feels like shrinking it.

This week, write a one-sentence description of what you do and quote a real price to the next person who asks.
