title: Midheaven in Libra
---

Your public life runs on partnership. You rise through alliances rather than solo climbs — the co-founder, the negotiator, the one brought in when two sides have stopped hearing each other. Your reputation carries an aesthetic signature too: whatever you produce is composed, and people associate your name with things done gracefully. Authority experiences you as easy to work with, which opens doors force never would.

- Your strength: diplomacy that produces outcomes. You can hold opposing stakeholders at one table and leave with an agreement, a rarer professional skill than any technical one.
- Your friction: your position blurs. Framing everything to keep the peace, you become hard to pin down, and decision-makers eventually discount the counsel of someone who never plants a flag.

In your next meeting that matters, state your actual recommendation in one sentence before you present the balanced options.
