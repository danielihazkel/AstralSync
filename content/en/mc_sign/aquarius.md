title: Midheaven in Aquarius
---

You are known for being ahead of the curve, or outside it entirely. Your public arc bends toward the unconventional: emerging fields, reform of stale systems, collectives and networks rather than ladders. You often do your best work slightly outside the org chart — the consultant, the founder, the in-house heretic — and your reputation is for saying the thing the room was not yet ready to hear, two years before it becomes policy.

- Your strength: perspective. You see the system itself, not just your desk in it, which makes you the natural architect of what comes next.
- Your friction: contrariness can become the brand. You dismiss workable conventions just for being conventional, and detachment from office politics reads to decision-makers as detachment from the team.

This month, adopt one boring best practice without improving it first, and let people see you do it.
