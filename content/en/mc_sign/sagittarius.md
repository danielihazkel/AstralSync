title: Midheaven in Sagittarius
---

Your career needs a horizon. You are known for the big picture — teaching, publishing, travel, strategy, anything that lets you carry meaning from one world into another — and your reputation grows in direct proportion to the size of the questions you are willing to answer in public. People above you tend to enjoy you: your optimism is contagious, and your candor, though occasionally alarming, reads as integrity.

- Your strength: vision plus faith. You see where a field is going before the data confirms it, and you can make other people want to go there.
- Your friction: details are somebody else's job until they are suddenly, publicly yours. Overpromising is the tax on your enthusiasm, and a few broken grand claims can erode trust faster than years of small accuracy rebuilds it.

Before your next bold public commitment, subtract twenty percent from the promise and deliver the difference as a surprise.
