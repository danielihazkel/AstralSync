title: Midheaven in Cancer
---

Your public role tends to be a caretaking one, whatever the job title says. Teams feel like households around you: you notice who is struggling, feed what is growing, and defend your people against the weather of the organization. Your reputation travels by loyalty rather than headline — the people you have protected become your references, and they are fierce ones.

- Your strength: you build belonging, and belonging retains talent, clients, and trust long after cleverer competitors have churned through theirs.
- Your friction: you take professional weather personally. Criticism of the work lands as criticism of you, and when you feel unappreciated you retreat into your shell mid-negotiation, going quiet exactly when your position needed stating.

The next time feedback stings at work, wait one full day, then answer only the sentence about the work — and notice how much of the sting was never about you.
