title: Midheaven in Scorpio
---

You are drawn to professions with depth and stakes — crisis, research, other people's money or secrets, anything where the surface is not the story. Your reputation is intensity: colleagues sense you see more than you say, and authority learns you can be trusted with what cannot be public. Your career tends to move in transformations rather than steps, shedding whole identities and emerging in new ones.

- Your strength: penetration. You find the real issue under the stated one, hold your nerve when situations turn serious, and your loyalty, once given, is absolute.
- Your friction: strategic opacity curdles into isolation. You hold cards so close that allies cannot help you play them, and workplaces can read your privacy as scheming even when you are simply guarded.

This month, tell one trusted colleague what you are actually working toward, and let them be useful to it.
