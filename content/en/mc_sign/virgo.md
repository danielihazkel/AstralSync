title: Midheaven in Virgo
---

You are known for the quality of your work, not the volume of your voice. Your public arc bends toward craft: the person whose analysis holds up, whose systems actually run, whose corrections turn out to be right. Reputations like yours build slowly and quietly, then become load-bearing — organizations discover they cannot function without the standard you keep.

- Your strength: precision under real conditions. You improve whatever you touch, and serious people learn to route the important work through you.
- Your friction: modesty becomes camouflage. You let the work speak for itself in rooms where nobody is listening to work, and watch less careful people narrate their way past you. Perfectionism adds a second tax: nothing feels finished enough to show.

Once this month, present something at ninety percent to someone above you, and say plainly what problem it solves — the sentence, not the polish, is your growth edge.
