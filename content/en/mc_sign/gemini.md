title: Midheaven in Gemini
---

You are known for what you can explain. Your public life runs on words, connections, and the ability to translate between rooms that do not speak each other's language — sales and engineering, experts and beginners, one industry and the next. Careers with your Midheaven rarely form a single straight line; they braid, and the braid itself becomes the credential.

- Your strength: range. You learn fast, pivot cleanly, and can hold two professional identities at once without either seeming diluted, which makes you invaluable wherever fields intersect.
- Your friction: depth is your reputation's weak flank. Skeptical authority reads your versatility as dabbling, and if every answer is fluent, people begin to wonder which subjects you have actually lived in.
