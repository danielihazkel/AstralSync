title: Midheaven in Capricorn
---

The Midheaven is at home here, and it shows: your life has a summit in it, visible from early on. You treat career as a long mountain rather than a lottery, accepting cold stretches and slow years that would send others back down, and your reputation compounds into something close to institutional — people assume you are in charge even when you are not yet.

- Your strength: gravity. You take responsibility so naturally that authority accrues to you, and your late-blooming arc means your forties and fifties often outshine everyone's thirties.
- Your friction: the climb can eat the climber. You measure yourself by altitude alone, defer rest and relationships to some future basecamp that keeps moving, and risk arriving at the summit efficient, respected, and hollow.

Schedule one non-negotiable block this week that produces nothing for the mountain, and defend it like a board meeting.
