title: Midheaven in Taurus
---

You build a reputation the way a wall gets built — course by course, checked for level, meant to outlast you. The world comes to know you as reliable, substantial, worth paying for: your work has weight, your word holds, and clients or employers relax when your name is attached. Ambition, for you, is less about altitude than about permanence and comfort honestly earned.

- Your strength: compounding. Skills, savings, goodwill — you accumulate steadily where flashier careers spike and crash, and time is always on your side.
- Your friction: you defend positions past their expiry date. A role, rate, or method that once meant security can become a wall you refuse to climb over, and the market moves while you stand firm.

Once this season, price out what staying put is actually costing you — in money or growth — and let the number, not the comfort, make the call.
