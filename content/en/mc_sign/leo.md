title: Midheaven in Leo
---

Your career wants a stage, and it will build one out of whatever materials the job provides. You are known by name, not by department: work only satisfies you when your signature is visible in it, and you instinctively turn roles into performances — the presentation everyone remembers, the launch that felt like an event. Authority tends to like you, because you make the whole enterprise look better lit.

- Your strength: presence. You confer importance on whatever you champion, and your visible pride in the work raises the standard of everyone attached to it.
- Your friction: credit is your pressure point. Shared wins can feel like losses, and when recognition goes elsewhere you may sulk in ways your audience can see, which dims the very shine you trade on.

This month, publicly hand one spotlight to a colleague who earned it, and watch what that does for your own standing.
