title: Midheaven in Aries
---

Your public arc is a series of first moves. Whatever field you enter, you gravitate toward the front edge of it — launching, pioneering, taking the shot nobody else will take — and your reputation is built on the moments you moved while others were still deliberating. Authority figures read you as bold before they read you as anything else, and they either promote you fast or brace against you.

- Your strength: ignition. You are at your professional best at the beginning of things, when a career, project, or fight needs someone willing to go first.
- Your friction: maintenance bores you. The middle of any long campaign tempts you toward a new battlefield, and a trail of promising unfinished starts can quietly cost you the standing your courage earned.

Choose one professional commitment this quarter and decide in advance what finishing it actually looks like, in writing, before the next shiny start appears.
