title: A Libra releasing period
---

This is a short chapter — eight years — and Venus runs it through her airy, social sign. Partnership, negotiation, fairness, and taste become the organizing themes. From the Lot of Fortune it shapes the people and agreements that arrive in your life and the balance or imbalance you feel within it; from the Lot of Spirit it shapes the alliances you form, the contracts you sign, and the work you do with others.

The strength is cooperation. You read people well, find terms that suit both sides, and open doors through relationships that would stay shut to effort alone. The friction is dependence — deferring decisions to keep the peace, measuring yourself by a partner's approval, or weighing options so long that the moment passes.

Eight years moves quickly. Choose your key collaborators deliberately, put agreements in writing, and when a choice is truly yours to make, make it without waiting for consensus.
