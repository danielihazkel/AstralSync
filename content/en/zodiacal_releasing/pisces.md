title: A Pisces releasing period
---

For twelve years the releasing moves through Pisces, and Jupiter sets a quieter, more inward agenda than in Sagittarius: compassion, imagination, faith, and the dissolving of rigid boundaries. From the Lot of Fortune, the period colors what befalls you — sensitivity in the body, situations that call for caretaking, events that seem arranged by chance or grace. From the Lot of Spirit, it colors what you do — creative, healing, or spiritual work and service that does not fit a neat job title.

The strength is receptivity. Intuition sharpens, art and devotion come more easily, and you can help others in ways that matter deeply to them. The friction is diffusion — vague plans, porous limits, escape through fantasy or substances, and being taken advantage of by people who mistake kindness for a lack of edges.

Pisces needs a vessel. Give your imagination a regular practice and a defined form, and set clear terms wherever your generosity is being spent.
