title: A Cancer releasing period
---

At twenty-five years, a Cancer period is one of the longer chapters, and the Moon runs it. Home, family, belonging, and the care you give and receive become the organizing themes. From the Lot of Fortune it describes what surrounds you — household changes, the health of the body, the people who depend on you or whom you depend on. From the Lot of Spirit it describes what you do — building a family, a community, or work that nourishes others.

The strength is rootedness. You learn to make a secure base and to protect what is yours, and your instincts about people sharpen. The friction is tide: moods that rise and fall with circumstances, overprotection, and a reluctance to leave a familiar shore even when the current has moved on.

The Moon changes constantly, so expect the period to have phases. Make your home a place you can work from, not just retreat to, and notice when caring for others has quietly replaced caring for yourself.
