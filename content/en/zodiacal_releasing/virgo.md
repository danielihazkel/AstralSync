title: A Virgo releasing period
---

For twenty years the releasing moves through Virgo, and Mercury sets a precise, practical agenda: skill, service, analysis, and the daily routines that keep things running. From the Lot of Fortune, the period colors your circumstances — work conditions, health and diet, the small demands that fill an ordinary day. From the Lot of Spirit, it colors your actions — the expertise you develop, the systems you improve, the problems people hire you to solve.

The strength is competence. You become the person who knows how things actually work, and careful effort compounds into a reputation for reliability. The friction is narrowing — perfectionism, worry, criticism turned inward, and a tendency to serve other people's plans while your own stays in draft.

A period this long can make you indispensable, which is not the same as fulfilled. Keep a short list of what your skill is ultimately for, and look after the body's routines as carefully as the job's.
