title: A Gemini releasing period
---

For twenty years the releasing moves through Gemini, and Mercury sets the agenda: learning, trading, writing, talking, and moving between several things at once. Read from the Lot of Fortune, the period colors your circumstances — busy surroundings, siblings and neighbors, news that keeps arriving, a restless nervous system. Read from the Lot of Spirit, it colors your actions: the skills you acquire, the deals you broker, the messages you put into the world.

The strength is range. You become fluent in more than one field, collect useful contacts, and adapt quickly when plans change. The friction is scatter — two jobs where one would do, half-finished projects, and a mind so occupied with options that it rarely settles on a choice.

Twenty years is long enough to master something properly. Keep your curiosity, but choose one craft to deepen through the whole chapter, and review every few years whether your many threads still lead somewhere.
