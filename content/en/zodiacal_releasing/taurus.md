title: A Taurus releasing period
---

This is a short chapter — eight years — and Venus runs it through her earthiest sign. The themes are resources, comfort, beauty, and the slow accumulation of things that hold value. From the Lot of Fortune it shapes your material circumstances and your body's appetites and ease; from the Lot of Spirit it shapes what you build, earn, and produce with your own hands.

The strength is steadiness. Money, skills, and relationships can be consolidated now, and pleasure stops feeling like a distraction from the work and starts sustaining it. Things you tend patiently tend to grow. The friction is inertia — clinging to a comfortable arrangement after it has stopped serving you, or letting possessions and routines decide for you.

Because the period is brief, treat it as a harvest window rather than a launch pad. Name what you want to own, outright and in good condition, by the time it ends, and spend accordingly.
