title: A Leo releasing period
---

For nineteen years the releasing passes through Leo, and the Sun sets the agenda: visibility, leadership, creative authorship, and the question of what you stand for. Released from the Lot of Fortune, the period colors your circumstances — recognition or scrutiny that arrives unasked, the vitality of the body, encounters with people in charge. Released from the Lot of Spirit, it colors your actions: the roles you step into, the work you sign, the stage you choose.

The strength is radiance. People notice you, and a confident, generous presence can earn you authority others spend decades seeking. The friction is pride — needing applause before you act, taking criticism as an insult, or guarding a position so fiercely that you stop growing in it.

The Sun rules what is central, so decide what belongs at the center. Commit to one body of work you are willing to be known for, and let feedback refine it rather than wound you.
