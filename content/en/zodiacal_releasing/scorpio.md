title: A Scorpio releasing period
---

For fifteen years the releasing runs through Scorpio, and Mars sets a deeper, more strategic agenda than in Aries: endurance, control, shared resources, and what lies beneath the surface. Released from the Lot of Fortune, the period colors what befalls you — crises, inheritances, debts, and physical tests that ask what you can survive. Released from the Lot of Spirit, it colors what you pursue — investigations, high-stakes work, and commitments made all the way down.

The strength is depth. You develop focus, resilience, and the nerve to face what others avoid, and you can transform situations that seemed fixed. The friction is intensity turned sour: suspicion, power struggles, secrets kept too long, and grudges that hold you hostage.

Scorpio rewards honesty with yourself. Name the one thing you are willing to change completely during this period, and watch carefully how money and trust are shared with the people closest to you.
