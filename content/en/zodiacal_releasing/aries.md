title: An Aries releasing period
---

For fifteen years the releasing runs through Aries, and Mars sets the agenda: initiative, contest, and cutting loose from whatever holds you back. Released from the Lot of Fortune, the period colors what happens to you — sudden openings, physical strain, situations that demand a fast answer. Released from the Lot of Spirit, it colors what you start: the ventures you launch, the fights you choose, the work you claim as your own.

The strength is momentum. You stop waiting for permission, and a decisive move made now can reset the direction of a whole decade. The friction is heat — impatience, burnout, quarrels that cost more than they win, and a habit of opening fronts you never close.

Mars rewards a clear target. Pick the one campaign this period is for, and before each new start, ask what you are finishing to make room for it.
