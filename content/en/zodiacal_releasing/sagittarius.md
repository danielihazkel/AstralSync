title: A Sagittarius releasing period
---

For twelve years — one full Jupiter cycle — the releasing passes through Sagittarius, and Jupiter sets the agenda: travel, study, belief, teaching, and widening the horizon. From the Lot of Fortune, the period colors your circumstances — foreign places, mentors and institutions, opportunities that come from far off. From the Lot of Spirit, it colors your actions — the degrees you pursue, the causes you champion, the knowledge you go on to pass along.

The strength is reach. You think bigger, recover your optimism, and find teachers or audiences that make your world larger. The friction is overextension — promising more than you can deliver, preaching instead of listening, or chasing the next horizon while the present one goes unfinished.

Jupiter expands whatever you feed. Choose the conviction or field you want to grow in, test it in real-world use rather than in theory, and check periodically that your plans still fit your actual resources.
