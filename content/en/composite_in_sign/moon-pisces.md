title: Composite Moon in Pisces
---

The emotional climate of this bond is porous. Moods pass between the two of you like weather through an open window: one person's melancholy becomes shared by dinnertime, one person's peace calms the whole room. Comfort arrives through atmosphere — music, water, rest, the relief of not having to explain anything.

The gift is mercy; this bond absorbs each other's bad days without keeping accounts, and its sympathy feels almost telepathic. The friction is absorbed static. Because feelings merge so easily, it gets hard to tell whose sadness this is, and both of you may drift or self-soothe separately rather than name what is wrong.

When the fog rolls in, ask one sorting question — "is this yours, mine, or ours?" — and let the answer decide who gets cared for first.
