title: Composite Jupiter in Pisces
---

This bond grows through faith and compassion. Together you believe in kindness, imagination, spirituality, and the unseen threads connecting people; the relationship expands through art, retreat, music, and care for those who are struggling. Generosity here is boundless and sincere.

The gift is grace: between you, forgiveness flows easily, and the bond can feel like a refuge from a harsh world. The friction is boundlessness. The relationship can give away more than it has, rescue people who don't want rescuing, and drift from practical reality into hope or escape. Faith can blur into avoidance.

Pair every generous impulse with one practical check — a budget, a time limit, a clear yes or no — so your compassion stays sustainable for both of you.
