title: Composite Mercury in Aries
---

Conversation in this bond moves at sprint pace. Together you think out loud, interrupt with enthusiasm, and decide things in the time it takes others to open a calendar. Plans get made on the spot, and the first idea usually becomes the plan because neither of you wants to wait for a second.

The gift is directness: between you, nothing important stays unsaid for long, and a disagreement aired at breakfast is often settled by lunch. The friction is that speed reads as combat. Debates turn into contests, sentences get finished for each other — sometimes wrongly — and whoever processes more slowly can end up agreeing just to stop the volley.

Before any decision that costs real money or time, sleep on it once; and in heated talks, let each person finish two full sentences before the other answers.
