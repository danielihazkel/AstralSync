title: Composite Mercury in Gemini
---

Between you, talk is the native element. Together you trade ideas, links, gossip, and half-formed theories at a pace that makes the hours vanish; you can plan a trip, solve a friend's problem, and start an argument about a film in the same car ride. This bond learns fast and juggles a lot of information without breaking stride.

The gift is mental chemistry — you sharpen each other, and there is almost always something new to say. The friction is scatter. Decisions stay open because a fresh option keeps appearing, logistics fall through the cracks between two people who each assumed the other was tracking them, and clever wording can stand in for actually agreeing.

When a plan matters, write it down in one sentence with one owner per task, and read it back to each other before you move on.
