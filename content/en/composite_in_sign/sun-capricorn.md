title: Composite Sun in Capricorn
---

This bond means business. Cardinal earth gives the two of you an identity of ambition and structure: goals set, ladders climbed, a relationship that takes itself seriously and expects to be taken seriously. Together you accomplish things — the pairing has a public spine and a long-range plan, spoken or not.

The gift is endurance; this bond shows up on the hard days and compounds like an investment. The friction is austerity. Duty can crowd out delight, achievements can substitute for affection, and either of you may hesitate to bring weakness into a connection that prizes competence.

Schedule pointless fun as deliberately as you schedule obligations, and let each other be tired sometimes — the bond is strong enough to hold what isn't impressive.
