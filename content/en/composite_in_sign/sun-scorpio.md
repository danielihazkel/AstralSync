title: Composite Sun in Scorpio
---

This bond runs deep or not at all. Fixed water gives the pairing an intense, private identity: shared secrets, unflinching honesty, loyalty that survives what would end lighter connections. Its purpose is transformation — the two of you change each other, and the relationship keeps shedding old skins.

The gift is depth; nothing about this bond is casual, and its trust, once earned, is close to unbreakable. The friction is control. Intensity can curdle into testing, jealousy, or strategic silence, and the pairing can treat vulnerability as a resource to ration.

When something feels off, say the true thing early and plainly — this bond can withstand any honest conversation, but it keeps score of the ones that never happened.
