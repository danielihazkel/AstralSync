title: Composite Mercury in Capricorn
---

This bond talks business, even about love. Together you plan carefully, speak with purpose, and make decisions with the long view in mind — five-year horizons, sensible budgets, clear roles. Conversations tend to end with something decided.

The gift is reliability: between you, words carry weight, promises get kept, and practical problems get solved without fuss. The friction is dryness. Talk can shrink to agenda items, playfulness gets postponed until the work is done (and the work is never done), and criticism can come out as a cool assessment that stings more than heat would. One of you may hesitate to raise a feeling that doesn't seem productive.

Hold one conversation a week with no logistics allowed — no calendar, no money, no to-do list — and see what the two of you talk about when nothing needs solving.
