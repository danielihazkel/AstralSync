title: Composite Mars in Virgo
---

This bond gets things done properly or not at all. Together you channel energy into refinement — systems, checklists, the craft of doing a shared task well — and the pair is at its best with a concrete problem to solve. Anger here is rarely explosive; it is itemized.

The gift is competence: this connection fixes what it touches, and its quiet productivity compounds impressively over time. The friction is the critique loop — frustration leaks out as correction, correction reads as attack, and soon the pair is debugging each other instead of the problem, with perfectionism stalling anything that can't be done flawlessly.

Practice aiming the red pen outward: when tension rises, restate the shared problem in one sentence and put it on the table between you, so you are both editors of it rather than of each other.
