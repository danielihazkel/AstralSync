title: Composite Mercury in Taurus
---

This bond thinks slowly and means it. Together you talk things through at an unhurried pace, prefer the concrete over the hypothetical, and trust a plan only once you can picture it working — the budget written down, the route mapped, the furniture measured. Once the two of you reach a decision, it tends to stay made.

The gift is steadiness: conversations here feel safe and practical, and shared logistics rarely collapse into chaos. The friction is stubbornness in stereo. When you disagree, each of you can dig in and repeat the same point more slowly, and new information gets treated as an intrusion rather than an update. Some talks never happen simply because the old arrangement is comfortable.

Schedule a periodic review of one settled habit — a routine, a budget line, a standing plan — and ask honestly whether it still serves you both.
