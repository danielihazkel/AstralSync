title: Composite Mercury in Pisces
---

This bond communicates by intuition. Together you pick up moods without explanation, speak in images and shared references, and understand each other in ways that are hard to put into words. Creative talk, late-night musing, and imagined futures come easily.

The gift is empathy: between you, even clumsy speech gets received with compassion, and very little needs to be spelled out. The friction is vagueness. Plans dissolve in loose phrasing, each of you can assume the other understood when neither actually did, and hard topics get avoided by drifting toward something softer. Details — dates, numbers, who said yes to what — quietly go missing.

After any conversation that ends in a decision, one of you restates it in a single clear sentence, and the other confirms it out loud or in writing.
