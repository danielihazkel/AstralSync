title: Composite Moon in Cancer
---

This bond feels like home even when it is nowhere near one. Its emotional climate is tidal and tender: the two of you read each other's states without asking, keep an archive of shared memories, and instinctively feed, tuck in, and look after whoever is flagging. Safety comes from ritual — the usual spot, the usual call, the usual comfort.

The gift is nurture that runs bone-deep; few connections care this thoroughly. The friction is moodiness and memory. Hurts get stored rather than resolved, needs get hinted instead of asked, and the bond can retreat into its shell together, feeding each other's worries.

Trade hints for requests: once a day, one of you asks plainly for what would help, and the other simply provides it.
