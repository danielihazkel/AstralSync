title: Composite Moon in Scorpio
---

The emotional undercurrent of this bond is deep water. Feelings between the two of you are rarely casual: trust is everything, privacy is sacred, and what passes in an ordinary evening can carry the intensity other pairings reserve for crises. The connection reads subtext fluently — each of you is seen here, sometimes more than is comfortable.

The gift is emotional courage; this bond can sit with the unbearable and not flinch, and its loyalty in dark seasons is absolute. The friction is suspicion. Silences get interpreted, tests get set without being announced, and withheld feelings can turn magnetic attention into surveillance.

When the urge to probe arises, ask the direct question instead — and when asked one, answer it whole, not in installments.
