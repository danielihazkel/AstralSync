title: Composite Mercury in Virgo
---

This bond thinks in checklists. Together you notice details others miss, enjoy solving problems methodically, and handle logistics — bills, bookings, repairs, schedules — with a quiet competence that keeps life running. Conversation often takes the form of improving something.

The gift is usefulness: between you, plans are realistic, information is accurate, and help arrives in exactly the form needed. The friction is the editing reflex. Feedback flows a little too freely, small flaws get more airtime than large successes, and worry can masquerade as planning — circling the same contingencies late into the night. Either of you may start to feel more audited than heard.

For every correction you offer each other, name one thing that already works; and when a worry has been discussed twice, put it on a list with a date and stop rehearsing it.
