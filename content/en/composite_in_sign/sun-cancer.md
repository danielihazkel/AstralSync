title: Composite Sun in Cancer
---

The heart of this bond is belonging. Cardinal water gives the two of you a shared purpose of making a home — literal or emotional — a place where each of you is fed, remembered, and defended. The relationship instinctively takes people in, and its history matters: anniversaries, first meetings, the stories you retell.

The gift is real shelter; few pairings protect their own the way this one does. The friction is the shell. Under stress the bond can close ranks against the world, turn indirect, and communicate through moods instead of words, with old hurts resurfacing at low tide.

When one of you goes quiet, name the feeling out loud before it hardens — sideways sulking is this bond's one bad habit.
