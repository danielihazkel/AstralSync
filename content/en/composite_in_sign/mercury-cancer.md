title: Composite Mercury in Cancer
---

This bond talks from the gut. Together you remember how a conversation felt long after the details fade, and you communicate as much through tone, food, and timing as through words. Decisions get made by asking what will keep the household — and the people in it — feeling safe.

The gift is attunement: between you, a sigh across the room is information, and you can comfort each other with very few words. The friction is indirect speech. Hurt gets expressed as a mood rather than a sentence, old conversations resurface during new ones, and plans stall because neither wants to say the thing that might upset the other. Memory here is long and not always fair.

When one of you is hurt, name the specific moment and the feeling in plain words within a day — and let the other answer that, not the history behind it.
