title: Composite Mercury in Leo
---

Communication in this bond has a sense of occasion. Together you tell good stories, make bold announcements, and talk about plans as if they were already happening; friends tend to hear your ideas performed as a duet. Decisions come from the heart and get defended with conviction.

The gift is warmth in the way you speak to each other — praise is generous, and the two of you can talk each other into courage. The friction is pride in the conversation. Once an opinion is declared it becomes hard to retract, disagreements turn into a contest over whose version gets the spotlight, and listening can shrink to waiting for your turn on stage. Being wrong in front of each other feels costlier than it should.

Practice the graceful reversal: when one of you changes your mind, say so out loud, and let the other respond with appreciation rather than a victory lap.
