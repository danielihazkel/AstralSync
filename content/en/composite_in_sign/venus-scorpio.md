title: Composite Venus in Scorpio
---

Nothing about this bond is casual. Together you value depth over pleasantness: real confessions, fierce loyalty, the sense that the connection knows things about each of you that no one else does. Affection here is intense and selective — the pair invests totally or not at all.

The gift is devotion that survives what would end lighter relationships. The friction is control: trust between you is tested rather than assumed, and hurt can turn strategic — silences with intent, generosity kept on ledger, jealousy over each other's other attachments. Shared resources carry emotional charge; money and loyalty get tangled.

Practice voluntary transparency: tell each other the small uncomfortable truth before it becomes a secret, and let a confidence be given freely at least as often as it is extracted.
