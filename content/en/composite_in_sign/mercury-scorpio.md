title: Composite Mercury in Scorpio
---

Conversation in this bond goes straight underneath. Together you read between the lines, sense what is unsaid, and prefer one real talk to a dozen pleasant ones. You keep each other's confidences fiercely, and as a pair you can research, investigate, or strategize with uncanny focus.

The gift is depth: between you, nothing has to stay on the surface, and honesty can reach places most relationships never visit. The friction is suspicion and withholding. Information gets treated as leverage, questions can feel like interrogations, and silence becomes a strategy whenever either of you feels exposed. A remark can be stored for months and then produced as evidence.

Agree on a no-ammunition rule: what is shared in a vulnerable moment is never used later in an argument — and when you notice yourself keeping score, say so instead.
