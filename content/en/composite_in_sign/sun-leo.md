title: Composite Sun in Leo
---

This bond wants to be seen. Fixed fire gives the pairing a warm, theatrical identity: together you throw the party, tell the story, take up space in a room in a way neither of you might solo. The relationship's purpose is creative — making things, celebrating things, treating shared life as something worth staging well.

The gift is generosity; this bond praises loudly and stays loyal. The friction is pride. When the pairing feels ignored it can perform instead of connect, and inside the bond, both parties can compete for the spotlight or keep score of appreciation.

Make a habit of applauding each other in private, where no audience benefits — that is where this connection proves it is more than a show.
