title: Composite Venus in Libra
---

This bond has natural grace. Together you instinctively balance each other — turn-taking in conversation, fairness in decisions, a shared taste for beauty and civility that makes the connection pleasant to be inside and pleasant to witness. Others often describe the pair as easy company.

The gift is diplomacy: disagreements get softened, framed, and negotiated rather than detonated. The friction is the same softness — between you, honest displeasure can be deferred so long in the name of harmony that resentment files itself away quietly. Decisions as a unit can also stall, each waiting politely for the other to choose.

Watch for peacekeeping that is really avoidance. Once in a while, let one of you state a preference bluntly and the other simply accept it — fairness includes taking turns being direct.
