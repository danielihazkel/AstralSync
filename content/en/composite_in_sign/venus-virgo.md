title: Composite Venus in Virgo
---

Affection in this bond is practical and precise. Together you show care by noticing — the refilled prescription, the calendar reminder, the small fix made before anyone complained. Love here is a verb performed in details, and the pair's shared aesthetic leans clean, useful, and quietly well-made.

The gift is reliability: this connection actually does what it says, and its help is genuinely helpful. The friction is critique — the same sharp eye that spots what a person needs also spots what they got wrong, and steady correction can start to feel like conditional approval. Money as a unit is managed carefully, sometimes anxiously.

Practice a ratio: for every improvement one of you suggests, name two things the other did well — and let some imperfections simply stand as part of the charm.
