title: Composite Mars in Libra
---

This bond acts in tandem or not at all. Together you instinctively strategize as a pair — weighing options, consulting each other before moving, fighting battles with charm and negotiation rather than force. The connection's power is social: as a unit you can persuade rooms that neither could sway alone.

The gift is fairness under fire: even in conflict, this bond argues in complete sentences and genuinely hears the other side. The friction is stalled ignition — anger gets diplomatically postponed until it ferments into politeness with an edge, and decisions can orbit endlessly while each defers to the other.

Watch for the courteous stalemate. When a choice has circled twice, flip for who decides and let that decision stand; and when something angers one of you, say so within the hour, gracefully or not.
