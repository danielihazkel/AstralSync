title: Composite Venus in Leo
---

This bond loves out loud. Together you celebrate each other with a warmth that spills over — public praise, grand gestures, an instinct to make ordinary occasions feel like occasions. The relationship has a stage presence of its own; people notice when the two of you walk in.

The gift is loyalty with flair: this connection defends its own and rarely does anything halfway. The friction is pride — both the pair's shared vanity, which can overspend on appearances, and the quiet contest over who gets the spotlight or the last word of appreciation. Neglect wounds this bond faster than conflict does.

Keep the applause flowing in both directions: make a point of praising each other privately, where no audience benefits, and watch how quickly warmth returns after any chill.
