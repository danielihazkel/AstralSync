title: Composite Mercury in Libra
---

This bond talks in order to agree. Together you consider every angle, ask each other's opinion before deciding, and phrase hard things with care. Socially you make a smooth pair — good hosts, good mediators, quick to read a room and say the right thing to it.

The gift is fair-minded conversation: between you, both sides get heard, and arguments rarely turn cruel. The friction is indecision. Choices bounce back and forth while each of you defers to the other, honest objections get softened until they vanish, and a pleasant tone can paper over a disagreement that never actually resolves. Small decisions — where to eat, which sofa — can take a surprising toll.

Set a simple rule for minor choices: whoever cares more decides, and if neither cares, the first answer wins. Save real deliberation for the decisions that deserve it.
