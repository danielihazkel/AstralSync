title: Composite Mars in Aries
---

This bond acts first and deliberates later. Together you are ignition: projects start on a dare, obstacles get charged head-on, and the pair moves at a pace that leaves committees blinking. When the two of you want something, the wanting itself is loud.

The gift is courage — this connection makes both parties braver than they are alone, and its conflicts, though quick to spark, are honest and quick to burn out. The friction is collision: two accelerators, no designated brake, so arguments can escalate in seconds and shared plans can outrun shared planning.

Agree on one rule before the next flare-up, not during it — for instance, either of you can call a ten-minute pause that the other honors without argument. Speed is your gift; the pause protects it.
