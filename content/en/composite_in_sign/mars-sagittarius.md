title: Composite Mars in Sagittarius
---

This bond charges toward horizons. Together you act on conviction and appetite — booking the trip, launching the venture, defending the principle — and the pair's energy multiplies whenever the goal is bigger than everyday logistics. Enthusiasm is the engine; meaning is the fuel.

The gift is buoyant momentum: this connection recovers from setbacks with a shrug and a new plan, and its arguments, though loud, are rarely bitter. The friction is overreach and crusading — promises outpace capacity, details get left for a future that keeps not arriving, and disagreements can inflate into philosophical trials where each of you argues the principle instead of the point.

Before the next big leap, spend twenty unglamorous minutes together on logistics — dates, costs, who does what. The adventure survives contact with a checklist; this pair should prove it regularly.
