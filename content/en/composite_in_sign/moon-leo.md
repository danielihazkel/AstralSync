title: Composite Moon in Leo
---

The private weather of this bond is sunny and dramatic. Affection here is expressed, not implied: big greetings, standing ovations for small wins, an emotional life with color in it. Together you feel safest when you feel special to each other — celebrated, chosen, remembered on the days that matter.

The gift is warmth that never has to be guessed at; this bond makes both parties feel like somebody. The friction is the wounded-lion sulk. When appreciation goes quiet, hurt pride can masquerade as coldness, and either of you may dramatize a small slight rather than admit it simply stung.

Keep the applause habit alive on ordinary days, and when pride gets nicked, say "that hurt" instead of staging the silence.
