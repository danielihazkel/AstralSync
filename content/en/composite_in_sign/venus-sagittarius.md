title: Composite Venus in Sagittarius
---

This bond expresses affection through adventure and honesty. Together you value the horizon — trips planned at midnight, big questions argued cheerfully over food, a shared allergy to anything that smells like a cage. Generosity between you is expansive and a little reckless, given in experiences more than objects.

The gift is optimism: this connection genuinely enlarges both lives and laughs its way out of moods that would sink heavier pairs. The friction is commitment to the unglamorous parts — follow-through, budgets, the tenth repetition of an ordinary week can make the bond restless, and blunt truths sometimes land harder than intended.

Keep one shared future plan alive on the calendar at all times, and practice delivering your honesty with the same warmth you'd want it delivered to you.
