title: Composite Moon in Aries
---

The emotional weather of this bond is fast and bright. Feelings arrive loudly, get expressed immediately, and pass like summer storms; between the two of you there is little brooding and even less pretending. The connection feels safest when it is doing something — moving, competing, deciding — and it recharges through action rather than long processing sessions.

The gift is honesty in real time: no one wonders where they stand. The friction is heat. Small irritations become skirmishes, and impatience can trample the quieter feeling underneath the flare. Neither of you likes to be the first to soften.

Agree in advance on a truce word for arguments, and use it — this bond forgives fast once someone lowers the sword first.
