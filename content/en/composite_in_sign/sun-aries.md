title: Composite Sun in Aries
---

This bond exists to start things. Together you generate momentum that neither of you quite manages alone: plans get made in an afternoon, dares get taken, and the relationship feels most alive at the beginning of something new. Cardinal fire gives the pairing a pioneering identity — you are known, as a pair, for going first.

The gift is courage on tap. The friction is follow-through: a bond built for ignition can stall once a project stops being novel, and disagreements tend to flare fast, with each side pushing to win rather than to understand.

Practice finishing one shared thing before launching the next, and when a spark of conflict hits, take one breath before answering.
