title: Composite Venus in Gemini
---

Between you, talk is affection. This bond runs on messages, in-jokes, articles forwarded at midnight, and conversations that fork three times before reaching a point. What the pair values most is interest itself — being amused, informed, and surprised by each other.

The gift is lightness: this connection can discuss almost anything, which makes repair after awkwardness easier than for most. The friction is skimming — feelings can get narrated instead of felt, and difficult emotional weather may be joked past rather than sat with. As a unit, money and taste are changeable: many small enthusiasms, little patience for one big commitment.

Practice one exchange a week where you name what you feel without a punchline, and let the silence afterward count as part of the conversation.
