title: Composite Mars in Cancer
---

This bond acts from the gut and fights sideways. Together you are fiercely protective — of each other, of home ground, of anything the pair has decided is family. When the connection is emotionally fed, its drive is formidable; efforts here are personal, never abstract.

The gift is devotion in motion: this pair will do practical battle for its own without being asked. The friction is indirect anger — irritation rarely announces itself, arriving instead as sulking, door-adjacent noise, burnt toast diplomacy, and grievances that surface weeks after the fact, tide-like and confusing to both of you.

When something stings, set a house rule of naming it within a day, however clumsily. A blunt "that hurt" spoken early spares this bond a week of mysterious weather.
