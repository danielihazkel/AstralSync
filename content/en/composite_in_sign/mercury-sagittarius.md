title: Composite Mercury in Sagittarius
---

This bond thinks big and talks freely. Together you love ideas, debates about meaning, plans for trips, and conversations that wander from a news story to philosophy in five minutes. You are frank with each other, and humor keeps even serious talks moving.

The gift is perspective: between you, problems shrink once they are placed inside a larger story, and honesty comes without much fear. The friction is carelessness with detail and with tact. Plans get announced before they are checked, dates and logistics slip, and blunt verdicts land harder than either of you intended. Big-picture agreement can hide the fact that nobody booked the tickets.

When a plan excites you both, assign the details before the conversation ends — who, by when — and before delivering a hard truth, ask whether the other wants it now.
