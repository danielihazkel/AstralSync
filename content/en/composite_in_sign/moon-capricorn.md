title: Composite Moon in Capricorn
---

The emotional climate of this bond is reserved but load-bearing. Care is demonstrated more than declared: showing up on time, handling the hard errand, standing quietly beside each other at funerals and deadlines. Together you feel safest when the structure holds — commitments kept, roles clear, no drama for drama's sake.

The gift is dependability that words can't fake; this bond is who each of you calls when it actually matters. The friction is drought. Feelings get managed rather than shared, tenderness waits for a task to hide inside, and both of you can mistake the other's composure for not needing anything.

Once a week, let one feeling out with no achievement attached to it — spoken plainly, received without advice.
