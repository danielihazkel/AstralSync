title: Composite Jupiter in Cancer
---

This bond grows by nurturing. Together you believe in family, home, and the people you take care of; the relationship tends to widen its circle of belonging — relatives, friends, pets, anyone who needs a seat at the table. Generosity here is emotional and deeply felt.

The gift is shelter: between you, warmth comes easily, and others often feel safer just being near the two of you. The friction is overprotection. The bond can take on more caregiving than it can sustain, cling to tradition when change would help, and keep too many other people's needs at the center. Comfort-seeking can also show up as overeating, overspending on the home, or retreating from the outside world.

Every few months, list who and what you are currently caring for, and decide together what to hand back so you have something left for each other.
