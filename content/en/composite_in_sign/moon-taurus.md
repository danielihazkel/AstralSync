title: Composite Moon in Taurus
---

In private, this bond is a warm kitchen. Its emotional climate is calm, physical, and loyal: comfort comes through food, touch, familiar places, and the deep reassurance of things staying put. Together you soothe each other simply by being around, and the connection's moods change slowly, like seasons rather than weather.

The gift is steadiness — this is a bond people rest inside. The friction is stubbornness in stereo. When feelings do get hurt, both digging in can freeze the pairing for days, and the love of comfort can make either of you avoid necessary emotional disruption entirely.

When a standoff sets in, let touch or a shared meal reopen the door before words do — this bond negotiates best with its shoes off.
