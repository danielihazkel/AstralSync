title: Composite Mars in Scorpio
---

This bond runs on concentrated force. Together you pursue what matters with strategic patience — researching, waiting, then committing everything at once. The pair does not do halfhearted; whatever this connection decides to want, it wants with the whole engine.

The gift is transformative grit: this bond can survive crises, keep confidences, and finish undertakings that would break looser alliances. The friction is submerged warfare — anger goes underground, resurfacing as tests, silences, and precision strikes on known soft spots, and power struggles can smolder for months beneath a calm surface.

Institute an amnesty ritual: at regular intervals, each of you surfaces one buried grievance with no retaliation allowed in response. What this pair names openly loses its poison; what stays hidden compounds interest.
