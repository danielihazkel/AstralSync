title: Composite Mercury in Aquarius
---

This bond thinks independently and talks like old friends. Together you love unusual ideas, technology, social causes, and conversations that question how things are usually done. You give each other room to disagree, and you often arrive at solutions nobody else would have tried.

The gift is mental freedom: between you, no topic is off-limits and no opinion has to be defended to the death. The friction is detachment. Feelings get analyzed instead of felt, a stubborn principle can outlast a simple compromise, and one of you may offer something clever where something kind was needed. Logistics drift because both of you assume the system will run itself.

When a conversation turns emotional, set the theory aside and ask a plain question — what do you need from me right now? — and then answer it literally.
