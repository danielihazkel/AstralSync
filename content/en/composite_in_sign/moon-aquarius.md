title: Composite Moon in Aquarius
---

This bond keeps its emotional thermostat set to cool and clear. Feelings between the two of you are handled with a certain airiness: talked about with intelligence, given space rather than pressure, allowed to be odd. Comfort here is freedom — the friend-like ease of a connection that doesn't cling, monitor, or demand performance.

The gift is acceptance; each of you can be fully strange here, and panic rarely spirals because someone always finds the wider view. The friction is remoteness. Cool can shade into absent, and a bond this good at giving space may fail to notice when what was actually wanted was closeness.

Check the thermostat directly now and then: ask "do you want space or company?" instead of assuming the answer is always space.
