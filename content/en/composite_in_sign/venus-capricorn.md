title: Composite Venus in Capricorn
---

Affection in this bond proves itself over time. Together you value commitment you can build on: showing up when it's inconvenient, keeping promises small and large, treating the connection as something with a future worth planning for. The pair's tastes run classic; its money sense as a unit is disciplined and long-range.

The gift is durability — this bond weathers seasons that dissolve flashier connections, and its respect runs deep. The friction is austerity: warmth can get scheduled, tenderness postponed until the work is done, and duty may quietly replace delight until the relationship feels more like a well-run institution than a pleasure.

Watch for the slide from devoted to merely dutiful. Budget for joy the way you budget for everything else: put something purely fun, with no productive purpose, on the calendar this month.
