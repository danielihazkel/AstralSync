title: Composite Mars in Taurus
---

This bond moves like a glacier — slowly, and through whatever is in the way. Together you pursue goals by outlasting them: steady effort, patient saving, an unhurried rhythm that finishes what flashier pairs abandon. Provocation takes a long time to register between you.

The gift is stamina: this connection can carry heavy, boring, multi-year loads without complaint. The friction is the standoff — when the pair does fight, both positions set like concrete, and the quarrel becomes a siege of silence and stubbornness where nobody remembers the original issue, only the refusal to yield first.

Watch for the early signs of digging in. When you feel the concrete setting, one of you name it out loud — "we're entrenching" — and trade one small concession each before continuing.
