title: Composite Mars in Capricorn
---

This bond climbs. Together you convert ambition into schedule: goals get defined, milestones get hit, and the pair's combined effort has an executive quality that makes long campaigns — degrees, businesses, houses, recoveries — genuinely achievable. Anger here is controlled, dry, and deployed with timing.

The gift is disciplined power: few connections execute like this one, and its word is good. The friction is grimness — the partnership can become a job, with performance reviews instead of affection, and frustration expressed as cold professionalism that leaves both parties managed rather than met. Rest can start to feel like falling behind.

Watch for the moment you are colleagues before you are companions. Declare regular off-duty hours where progress talk is banned, and count keeping them as its own kind of achievement.
