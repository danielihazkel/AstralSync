title: Composite Sun in Taurus
---

The purpose of this bond is to build something that lasts. Fixed earth gives the two of you a steady, unhurried identity: shared meals, shared resources, routines that accumulate into real security. People around you experience the pairing as reliable — the connection that still stands when flashier ones have moved on.

The gift is durability and a talent for making life comfortable together. The friction is inertia: what starts as loyalty can harden into rut, and the bond can resist change even when change is overdue. Money and possessions are where your values get tested most directly.

Watch for the moment "we always do it this way" becomes a wall, and deliberately try one small new thing together each month.
