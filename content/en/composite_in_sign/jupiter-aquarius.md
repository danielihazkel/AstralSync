title: Composite Jupiter in Aquarius
---

This bond grows through ideals and community. Together you believe in progress, friendship, and the future; the relationship expands through networks, causes, groups, and unconventional plans. Generosity here is collective — often aimed at many people at once.

The gift is vision: between you, new possibilities seem natural, and the bond can become a meeting point for interesting people and ideas. The friction is abstraction. The relationship can give more to humanity than to its own daily life, overcommit to causes, and hold principles so firmly that personal warmth gets left behind. Freedom can become an excuse for distance.

Once a week, turn the vision inward: plan one small, specific improvement to life between the two of you, and carry it out before the next cause calls.
