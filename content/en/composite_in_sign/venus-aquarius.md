title: Composite Venus in Aquarius
---

This bond is built on friendship first, whatever else it becomes. Together you value freedom, ideas, and each other's oddities — the connection thrives on being unlike other people's, and it usually is. Affection here is expressed sideways: through loyalty to each other's independence, shared causes, and humor that outsiders don't quite get.

The gift is spaciousness: nobody owns anyone, and the bond stays interesting because both parties remain fully themselves. The friction is distance — cool analysis can stand in for comfort, and in tender moments one or both of you may reach for a theory when a hand was needed.

Practice occasional old-fashioned warmth on purpose: say the sentimental thing plainly, without irony, and let it be awkward. This bond can afford the experiment.
