title: Composite Jupiter in Scorpio
---

This bond grows through depth. Together you believe in transformation, loyalty, and getting to the truth of things; the relationship expands each time it faces something hard and comes out changed. Shared resources — money, intimacy, trust — tend to multiply when handled with care.

The gift is regenerative power: between you, crises become turning points, and the bond often emerges from difficulty stronger than before. The friction is intensity as a habit. The relationship can overinvest in secrecy, control, or high stakes, mistaking drama for depth and suspicion for insight. Generosity can arrive with invisible strings attached.

When something has grown heavy between you, ask whether it needs to be transformed or simply put down — and practice letting one small grievance go without excavating it.
