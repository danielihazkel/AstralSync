title: Composite Mars in Gemini
---

This bond fights and works with words. Together you pursue goals through conversation — brainstorms that spawn five plans before breakfast, debates that sharpen both minds, a shared reflex to talk any problem into submission. Energy between you is quick, plural, and easily rerouted.

The gift is agility: this connection adapts fast and rarely stays stuck, because there is always another angle to try. The friction is scatter and score-keeping — five plans can mean zero finished, and arguments can turn into fencing matches where clever points replace real ones and old quotes get weaponized.

Practice two disciplines: finish one shared project before opening the next, and in conflict, ban the greatest-hits archive — argue only about the thing that happened this week, in plain sentences.
