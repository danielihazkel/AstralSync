title: Composite Mars in Leo
---

This bond pursues its aims with theater and heart. Together you go after big, visible goals — the kind worth telling stories about later — and the pair's combined will has genuine command presence. Effort here wants an audience, or at least applause from each other.

The gift is generous fire: this connection motivates, defends, and celebrates its own with full-bodied loyalty, and its enthusiasm recruits others to the cause. The friction is pride in conflict — arguments become performances, apologies feel like abdications, and both of you would sometimes rather be magnificent than be reconciled.

In your next disagreement, watch for the moment winning the scene replaces solving the problem. Let one of you deliberately go first with "I was wrong about part of this" — in this bond, that line is the bravest role available.
