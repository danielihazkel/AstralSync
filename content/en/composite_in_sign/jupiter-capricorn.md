title: Composite Jupiter in Capricorn
---

This bond grows by building. Together you believe in structure, reputation, and earned success; the relationship expands through careful plans, long commitments, and achievements that last. Generosity here is measured but dependable — help that comes with a strategy.

The gift is durable progress: between you, ambition is grounded, and what you build together tends to hold. The friction is joyless growth. The bond can measure itself only by milestones, postpone pleasure until some future security that never feels secure enough, and dismiss optimism as naïve. Faith in hard work can crowd out faith in each other.

Set aside a small budget and a free weekend each quarter for something with no return on investment — pure enjoyment — and protect it as seriously as any goal.
