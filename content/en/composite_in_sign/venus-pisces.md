title: Composite Venus in Pisces
---

Between you runs an unusual tenderness. This bond values empathy above almost everything — moods are absorbed without explanation, forgiveness comes easily, and the connection often feels fated, musical, slightly outside ordinary time. Generosity here is boundless and unbookkept; the pair gives without tallying.

The gift is compassion deep enough to hold each other's worst days. The friction is fog: boundaries blur, sacrifices go unnamed until they curdle, and the pair can prefer the beautiful version of the story to the accurate one. Money as a unit tends to leak — generously, but it leaks.

Practice gentle realism as an act of love: once a month, say out loud what each of you actually needs and what each has quietly given up, before devotion drifts into martyrdom.
