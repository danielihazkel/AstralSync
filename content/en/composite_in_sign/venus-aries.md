title: Composite Venus in Aries
---

Affection in this bond arrives fast and unfiltered. Together you show you care through action — spontaneous plans, blunt compliments, jumping to each other's defense before anyone asks. The connection feels young no matter how long it lasts, and boredom is the one thing it refuses to tolerate.

The gift is heat: enthusiasm regenerates quickly between you, and forgiveness tends to be as swift as the flare-ups. The friction is impatience — tenderness can get rushed, and whoever needs slower reassurance may feel steamrolled. Money as a unit follows the same pulse: bold, impulsive, occasionally sheepish afterward.

Practice pausing for one beat before acting on a shared impulse, and make a habit of lingering — in conversation, at the table, in appreciation — a little longer than feels natural.
