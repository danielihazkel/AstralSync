title: Composite Jupiter in Taurus
---

This bond grows through comfort and accumulation. Together you believe in good food, beautiful things, financial security, and the slow pleasure of building something that lasts. Generosity here is tangible — gifts, hospitality, a home others love to visit.

The gift is abundance with roots: between you, resources tend to grow steadily, and the relationship itself feels like a well-tended garden. The friction is excess of the senses. Indulgence can become the default reward, spending on comfort can quietly outpace the plan, and contentment can harden into resistance to any change that disrupts the routine. More is not always better, even when it feels that way.

Once a season, pick one shared indulgence to scale back on purpose, and put what you save toward an experience neither of you has had.
