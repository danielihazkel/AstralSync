title: Soul Urge 11
---

Your name's vowels sum to 11, a master number — a 2's longing for connection tuned to a finer frequency. What you privately want is meaning with a current in it: the conversation that turns numinous, the intuition that proves true, contact with something larger that ordinary life keeps promising and postponing.

The gift is that you actually receive on that channel; hunches, dreams, and first impressions bring you information you can't source but can verify. The cost is the same open antenna: crowded rooms drain you, other people's moods arrive as your own, and the gap between what you sense and what you can prove leaves you doubting your best signal.

Sensitivity is an instrument, and instruments need calibration. Keep a log of your strong hunches and their outcomes — let the record, not the anxiety, tell you how much to trust.
