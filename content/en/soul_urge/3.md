title: Soul Urge 3
---

The vowels of your name want to be heard. Your deepest satisfaction is not applause exactly — it is the instant something inside you lands outside you: the story told right, the feeling finally phrased, the room laughing at the true thing.

This urge keeps you warm company and makes ordinary days generative; you turn commutes into bits and setbacks into material. The friction is that performance can replace disclosure. You can be the funniest person at the table and go home unmet, because the polished version of your feelings got expressed while the unpolished version stayed backstage, growing lonelier per encore.

Charm connects rooms; candor connects people. Tell one trusted person something true this week before you have found the entertaining way to say it.
