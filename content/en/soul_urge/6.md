title: Soul Urge 6
---

Your name's vowels want to be needed. The moments that actually satisfy you are domestic in spirit even when they happen at work: the friend fed, the crisis soothed, the circle of people whose well-being visibly improves because you are in it.

This urge makes you the person others call first, and there are worse legacies than a long list of people you steadied. The friction is the exchange rate you hide inside the giving. Care given to be needed carries an invoice — for gratitude, for centrality, for never being left — and when the invoice goes unpaid you feel wronged by people who never saw a bill.

You are allowed to matter without being useful. Spend time with someone you love this week doing something that helps no one, and let that be the whole point.
