title: Soul Urge 1
---

The vowels of your name carry a private craving for sovereignty. Whatever your role looks like from outside, what actually satisfies you is the moment a choice is entirely yours — your call, your risk, your name on it.

This hunger is a clean fuel: it gets you out of dead-end arrangements faster than most people admit they are in one, and it makes your yes mean something. The friction is that it keeps score in private. Praise for teamwork lands hollow, being managed feels faintly insulting even by good managers, and you can grow quietly resentful in situations you chose.

Notice where the resentment pools; it is a map of surrendered decisions. This month, reclaim one — small is fine — and make it visibly, accountably yours.
