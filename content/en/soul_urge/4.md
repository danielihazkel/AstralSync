title: Soul Urge 4
---

Your name's vowels ask for solid ground. What you privately want is not excitement or acclaim but order you can trust: the paid-off debt, the stocked pantry, the calendar that holds, the relationship that does not require decoding.

This desire makes you the steady point in other people's storms, and it gives your life a compounding quality — savings, skills, and trust all accrue to people who want stability enough to build it. The friction is that safety can quietly become the only vote. You decline the move, the venture, the person who might rearrange things, and call it prudence when some of it is fear wearing prudence's clothes.

Security is a base camp, not a destination. Schedule one deliberate, bounded risk this season — and notice that the ground holds.
