title: Soul Urge 9
---

The vowels of your name ache for the whole. Your private measure of a day is not what you gained but whether it connected to something larger — a stranger helped, an injustice named, the sense of being a working part of the human project.

This longing gives your inner life unusual reach; you grieve headlines other people scroll past, and your empathy crosses lines of tribe and distance that stop most hearts at the border. The friction is closer to home. Loving humanity in general can crowd out the specific humans in your kitchen, and idealism disappointed too often curdles into a quiet conviction that you care more than everyone.

The whole is made of nearby pieces. Give the person in front of you today the undivided attention you wish the world would give itself.
