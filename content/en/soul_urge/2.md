title: Soul Urge 2
---

Your name's vowels ask for closeness. Underneath whatever you achieve, the experience you are actually after is being deeply paired — one person, one friend, one collaborator with whom nothing needs translating.

This want makes you a rare kind of intimate: attentive, loyal, alert to the small weather in a partner's voice. The friction is what you trade for the closeness. You edit your preferences before anyone asks, agree to dinners you dread and plans you doubt, and then feel obscurely lonely inside the very harmony you built — because the person being loved is the accommodating version, not you.

Peace bought with self-erasure never quite pays out. Once a day, voice one genuine preference — where to eat, what to watch — before asking anyone else's.
