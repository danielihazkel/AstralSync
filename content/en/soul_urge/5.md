title: Soul Urge 5
---

The vowels of your name crave the open door. What actually feeds you is not any particular experience but the guarantee of a next one — new tastes, new streets, the felt sense that your life has not been decided yet.

This appetite keeps you younger than your peers and impossible to bore; you say yes to the detour and come back with the story everyone retells. The friction is that the same appetite reads every commitment as a shrinking room. Good relationships, good jobs, and good cities get abandoned not because they failed but because they succeeded into familiarity, and the craving mistook comfort for a cage.

Freedom you never spend on anything is just loitering. When the itch to bolt arrives, try changing something inside the commitment before you change the commitment.
