title: Soul Urge 7
---

The vowels of your name want quiet and the truth, in that order. What restores you is not company or achievement but the sealed hour — reading, walking, turning a question over until it confesses — and no external success compensates for a week without it.

This inward pull gives you an interior life most people only visit; you are never bored alone, and your understanding of things has a hand-built quality that borrowed opinions can't match. The friction is that the door you close for solitude also locks from inside. Skepticism becomes a reflex, invitations lapse, and you can analyze a feeling so thoroughly that you never get around to having it.

Depth was never meant to be solitary confinement. Keep your sealed hours — and keep one standing appointment with a person who is allowed past the analysis.
