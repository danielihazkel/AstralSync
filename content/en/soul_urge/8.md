title: Soul Urge 8
---

Your name's vowels want weight. Privately, what satisfies you is consequence: resources you control, respect you did not have to ask for, the sense that when you speak, things afterward are different.

Owned honestly, this desire is a gift — it makes you frank about money and ambition where others are coy, and people trust a person whose motives are legible. The friction begins when the wanting goes underground. Disowned hunger for status leaks out as contempt for other people's success, or as control disguised as standards, and the appetite that could have built something starts auditing everyone instead.

Wanting power is not the problem; pretending you don't is. Write down the position, number, or authority you actually want, and take one open step toward it this month.
