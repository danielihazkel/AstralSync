title: Soul Urge 22
---

The vowels of your name sum to 22, a master number — a 4's wish for solid ground expanded until it wants to pour foundations for other people too. What would actually satisfy you is not comfort but legacy: knowing something durable exists in the world because you insisted on it.

This gives your inner life a long horizon; you think in decades while friends think in weekends, and delayed gratification costs you less than it costs almost anyone. The master number's tax is that the present tense goes unlived. Nothing you finish feels finished enough, rest registers as theft from the mission, and the person building the cathedral forgets to stand inside it.

A legacy you never enjoyed is someone else's inheritance and nothing more. Once a week, walk through something you have already built — a room, a habit, a team — purely to feel that it stands.
