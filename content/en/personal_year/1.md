title: Personal Year 1
---

This is the first year of a new nine-year cycle, and it tends to feel like a door opening after the clearing of the year before. Ideas you shelved come back with more urgency, and you may notice a pull to start something under your own name: a project, a direction, a version of yourself that hasn't had room yet. Decisions made now set the tone for the years that follow.

The gift is momentum. You have more initiative than usual, and other people tend to respond to a clear "I'm doing this" rather than a request for permission. The friction is impatience and isolation. A 1 year can make you push alone when help is on offer, or start five things and finish none because the starting felt better than the second week.

Seeds need choosing before they need watering. Early in the year, write down the one thing you want to begin and take the first concrete step within a week, even a small one. Then protect that thing from the next shiny idea until spring is over.
