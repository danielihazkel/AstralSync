title: Personal Year 6
---

After the movement of a 5 year, attention turns back to the people and places you are responsible for. Home, family, partnership, and community tend to take center stage: someone needs care, a household needs reorganizing, a commitment asks to be made or renewed. You may feel the weight of other people's needs more than usual, and also how much those bonds hold you up.

The gift is belonging. Effort spent on the home front and on close relationships is well rewarded this year, and you can become a steadying presence others rely on. The friction is overload. A 6 year can turn care into control, or into quiet martyrdom, where you carry everyone's problems and then resent being the only one who carries anything.

Responsibility is sustainable only when it has edges. Make a short list of what you are genuinely responsible for this year and what belongs to someone else, then hand back one thing that isn't yours, kindly and clearly, before the summer.
