title: Personal Year 7
---

After a year spent looking after others, this one turns inward. A 7 year tends to bring questions rather than answers: what you actually believe, what the last six years have been for, what you want the next stretch to mean. Study, research, therapy, spiritual practice, or simply more time alone often become important, and outward ambition may feel quieter than usual.

The gift is depth. Insight comes more easily when you give it space, and what you learn now tends to shape the decisions of the strong, outward years that follow. The friction is withdrawal. A 7 year can slide into isolation, overthinking, or a skepticism that dismisses help before hearing it, and the quiet can be mistaken for failure when it is really the cycle doing its work.

Reflection needs a regular appointment. Set aside one protected block each week for study or solitude, and keep a notebook for the questions that surface. By the end of the year, read it back and underline what you now know.
