title: Personal Year 3
---

The quiet groundwork of the 2 year gives way to something louder. This year tends to bring more invitations, more conversations, and more of an urge to make things: writing, music, a talk, a redesigned room, a new way of showing up socially. What began two years ago starts wanting an audience, and you may find yourself saying what you think with less hesitation.

The gift is expression. Ideas flow more easily, people find you good company, and joy becomes a legitimate source of energy rather than a reward for finishing work. The friction is scatter. A 3 year can spread you across too many plans and too many evenings out, and words can run ahead of follow-through. Emotional honesty can also tip into saying things more sharply than you meant.

Creativity needs a container to become more than a mood. Choose one creative or communicative outlet and give it a fixed weekly slot for the whole year, then share something you made at least once a month, finished or not.
