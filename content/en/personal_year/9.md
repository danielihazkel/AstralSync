title: Personal Year 9
---

This is the last year of the nine-year cycle, and it tends to be about completion. Projects reach their natural end, relationships clarify, and habits or roles you have outgrown become harder to ignore. Some endings you choose and some arrive on their own; either way, the year is clearing space for the new cycle that begins next January.

The gift is release. Letting go is easier now than at almost any other point in the cycle, and generosity, forgiveness, and finishing things often bring an unexpected lightness. The friction is clinging. A 9 year can feel like loss if you hold on to what is already leaving, or it can tempt you to launch something big too early, before the ground has been cleared for it.

Endings are easier when they are deliberate. Go through your commitments, possessions, and obligations once this year and choose a few to finish, give away, or close for good. Leave the big new beginnings on a list for next year.
