title: Personal Year 5
---

At the midpoint of the cycle, the walls you built last year start to feel like they need windows. This year tends to bring movement: travel, a change of job or role, new people, new interests, or simply a restlessness that won't settle for the usual routine. Plans can shift quickly, and some of the most useful developments arrive unannounced.

The gift is freedom. You adapt faster than usual, and experiments that would have felt reckless two years ago now feel natural and often pay off. The friction is impulsiveness. A 5 year can tempt you to break things that were working just because they feel confining, to overspend on novelty, or to keep moving so you never have to sit with a decision.

Change goes better with a few fixed points. Decide which two or three commitments from last year are not up for renegotiation, then give yourself real permission to experiment with everything else, including at least one trip, class, or skill you have never tried.
