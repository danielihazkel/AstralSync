title: Personal Year 4
---

After the openness of a 3 year, this one gets down to work. The ideas and connections of the past three years now ask for structure: budgets, schedules, training, repairs, the unglamorous systems that let a plan survive contact with real life. Health routines, housing, and workload often come up, and you may notice how much of the year is spent laying foundations you won't fully use until later.

The gift is solidity. Effort compounds this year, and what you build carefully tends to hold for a long time. The friction is heaviness. A 4 year can feel like all obligation and no reward, and the risk is either grinding yourself down or resenting the work so much that you cut corners on the very things that would make next year freer.

Discipline is easier when it is small and visible. Choose one structure your life is missing, such as a savings habit, a sleep schedule, or a weekly planning hour, and keep it every week of the year. Track it somewhere you can see.
