title: Personal Year 22
---

A 22 year is a heightened 4, so it carries the 4 year's work of structure and discipline, scaled up. Following the expressive 3 year, it tends to ask you to build something larger than yourself: an organization, a body of work, a home or system that other people will rely on. Opportunities may arrive that feel too big for you, along with the practical demands that make them real.

The gift is the ability to turn vision into architecture. Effort this year can produce something that lasts well beyond the cycle, and you may find you can hold the big picture and the details at the same time. The friction is pressure. A 22 year can feel crushing if you try to carry the whole structure alone, and the fear of not living up to it can stall you before the first brick is laid.

Big work is still built one piece at a time. Write a one-page plan for what you want to build, break it into quarterly milestones, and recruit at least one person to share the load before the first milestone is due.
