title: Personal Year 2
---

After the push of a 1 year, this one asks you to slow down and let what you planted take root. Progress tends to happen underground: in conversations, in negotiations, in relationships that need tending more than leading. Partnerships of every kind come into focus, and you may find that what you want depends on someone else's timing as much as your own.

The gift is sensitivity. You read people more accurately this year, and small acts of cooperation open doors that force would have jammed. The friction is waiting. A 2 year can feel stalled if you measure it by visible results, and the temptation is either to dig up the seeds to check on them or to disappear into keeping the peace while your own needs go unspoken.

Patience works better with something to do. Pick one relationship, at work or at home, that matters to the plan you began last year, and invest in it deliberately: a regular check-in, an honest conversation you have been postponing, a favor offered without keeping score.
