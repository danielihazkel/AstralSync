title: Personal Year 8
---

After the inward turn of a 7 year, this one faces the world. An 8 year tends to bring questions of money, authority, and results: promotions, negotiations, investments, a business decision, or a chance to take charge of something that matters. Much of what you have built across the cycle becomes visible now, and the harvest usually reflects the effort that went in.

The gift is traction. You can make things happen at a larger scale this year, and people are more willing to trust you with resources and responsibility. The friction is the pull of power itself. An 8 year can tip into overwork, control, or measuring your worth by the bank balance, and it can be unforgiving to plans that were never properly grounded.

Achievement lands better when you know its terms in advance. Write down one concrete financial or career goal for the year, with a number attached, and review your progress on it monthly. Alongside it, name one thing you won't trade away to reach it.
