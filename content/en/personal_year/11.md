title: Personal Year 11
---

An 11 year is a heightened 2, so it carries the 2 year's lessons of patience and partnership, with the volume turned up. Following the new start of a 1 year, it tends to bring flashes of insight, vivid dreams, sudden clarity about a person or a direction, and a sharper sense of what you are here to contribute. Relationships still need tending, but they may also become the place where something larger is revealed.

The gift is illumination. Your intuition is unusually reliable this year, and you may inspire people simply by saying what you see. The friction is nervous intensity. An 11 year can run your system hot, with restlessness, poor sleep, and a sensitivity that makes ordinary friction feel personal, and the gap between your vision and the slow pace of real life can be hard to bear.

Insight needs grounding to be useful. Keep a simple record of the hunches and ideas that arrive, and test one of them each season with a small, practical step. Protect your sleep as if it were part of the work, because this year it is.
