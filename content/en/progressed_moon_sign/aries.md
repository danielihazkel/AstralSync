title: Progressed Moon in Aries
---

After the soft, porous Pisces months, your feelings wake up hungry. For about two and a half years the progressed Moon in Aries makes you crave movement, independence, and a fresh start; restlessness at home is often the first sign the chapter has begun. You want room to act on an impulse without explaining it, and your body asks for exertion — a sport, a long walk, a reason to sweat.

The strength is emotional honesty at speed: you feel something and say it, and old fogs lift quickly. The friction is a short fuse. Irritation flares over small household things, and you may mistake needing space for needing to leave.

Notice where you feel cramped, and give yourself one physical outlet every week before the anger finds a person instead.
