title: Progressed Moon in Leo
---

After the inward Cancer years, your heart wants to come out and play. The progressed Moon in Leo makes you crave warmth, recognition, and the feeling of being seen for who you are. Romance, children, creative projects, and anything that lets you perform — even for a small audience — become emotional fuel, and home turns into a place for hosting rather than hiding.

The strength is generosity. You give affection freely, encourage others, and rediscover pleasure and pride in your own work. The friction is the need for applause: sulking when you go unnoticed, dramatizing ordinary setbacks, or measuring your worth by how much attention a room pays you.

Make something with your hands or voice this year purely for the joy of it, and watch how much you need anyone else's verdict on it.
