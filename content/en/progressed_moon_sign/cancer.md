title: Progressed Moon in Cancer
---

The Moon is at home in Cancer, and for these two and a half years so are your needs. After the busy Gemini chapter, attention gathers around family, memory, and the people you would call at three in the morning. Moves, renovations, pregnancies, and caring for a parent often cluster here, and you may find yourself cooking more, nesting, or returning to old places.

The strength is depth of feeling. You remember what matters, you nurture well, and your instincts about people are sharp. The friction is sensitivity turned defensive: taking offense where none was meant, retreating into the household, or caring for everyone so thoroughly that you forget to ask for care yourself.

Choose one person you trust and tell them plainly what you need from them this season.
