title: Progressed Moon in Sagittarius
---

After the deep Scorpio years, your emotions want air and horizon. The progressed Moon in Sagittarius makes you restless for travel, study, open spaces, and ideas large enough to live by. You may move further from home, go back to school, take up a faith or philosophy, or simply need more time outdoors to feel like yourself.

The strength is optimism. Heavy feelings lift, you laugh more easily, and you trust that life has meaning beyond the daily grind. The friction is escape: leaving before a feeling is finished, promising more than you can deliver, and preaching your new answers to people who did not ask.

Plan one real journey or course of study for this chapter, and before every big leap check whether you are running toward something or away from it.
