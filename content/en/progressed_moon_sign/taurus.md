title: Progressed Moon in Taurus
---

The Aries sprint is over, and your emotional life wants to settle. With the progressed Moon in Taurus, comfort stops being a luxury and becomes a need: good food, a steady income, a bed you like, routines that do not change every week. Many people put down literal roots in this chapter — buying furniture, tending a garden, staying put.

The strength is calm. You recover from upsets by returning to the body, and your steadiness reassures the people around you. The friction is inertia: holding on to a job, a place, or a habit long after it has stopped feeding you, and treating spending or eating as the answer to every mood.

Build one daily ritual that genuinely restores you, and check each season whether your comforts still serve you or simply keep you still.
