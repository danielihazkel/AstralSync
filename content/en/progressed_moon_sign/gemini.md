title: Progressed Moon in Gemini
---

After the steady Taurus years, your inner life wants company and conversation. The progressed Moon in Gemini makes you hungry for information, errands, short trips, new acquaintances, and the feeling that something interesting is always arriving. Siblings, neighbours, and the people you message daily matter more than usual, and home may fill with books, devices, and visitors.

The strength is lightness. You can talk your way through a feeling, learn a skill quickly, and adapt when plans change. The friction is scatter: so many inputs that you never sit with a single emotion long enough to know what it is, and nerves that fray from too much screen and too little sleep.

Write a few lines each evening about what you actually felt that day, not just what happened.
