title: Progressed Moon in Libra
---

After the busy Virgo years, your needs turn toward other people. The progressed Moon in Libra makes you crave partnership, harmony, and beauty in the space around you. Relationships begin, deepen, or get renegotiated in this chapter, and you may redecorate, seek out art, or simply find you cannot settle in a room that feels ugly or tense.

The strength is grace. You become good at listening, at seeing the other side, and at making home a place where people feel welcome. The friction is dependence on balance: swallowing your own feelings to keep the peace, struggling to decide anything alone, and judging your mood by whether the people around you are pleased.

When a choice comes up this season, decide what you want before you ask anyone else what they think.
