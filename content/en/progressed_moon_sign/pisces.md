title: Progressed Moon in Pisces
---

After the cool Aquarius chapter, your feelings grow tidal again. The progressed Moon in Pisces closes the whole twenty-eight-year cycle, and it often feels like a long exhale: dreams are vivid, empathy is wide open, and you need more solitude, music, water, and sleep than usual. Things that no longer belong to you tend to slip away in this chapter, sometimes gently, sometimes not.

The strength is compassion. You understand people without words, your imagination is rich, and spiritual or creative practice comes easily. The friction is porousness: absorbing everyone's moods, drifting from practical life, and reaching for escapes that blur rather than heal.

Protect a daily quiet hour, let go of one thing you are done carrying, and keep a notebook by the bed — the next fresh start is close.
