title: Progressed Moon in Scorpio
---

After the polite Libra chapter, your feelings drop beneath the surface. The progressed Moon in Scorpio wants intensity, truth, and closeness that costs something. Old grief, jealousy, desire, and family secrets can rise in these two and a half years, and shared money, inheritance, or intimacy often become the stage where they play out.

The strength is emotional courage. You stop settling for surface comfort, you can stay with what is hard, and real healing becomes possible. The friction is control: guarding yourself so tightly that no one gets in, testing loyalty, and holding grudges long after the injury has faded.

Pick one buried feeling to look at directly with a trusted person or a therapist, and notice when you are keeping score instead of telling the truth.
