title: Third Quarter
---

The Moon is half lit again, now waning, rising around midnight and hanging in the morning sky. This third quarter, also called the last quarter, is the second square of the lunar month, and it tends to bring a turning point of a different kind: not building, but reassessing.

These days favor review and course correction. You can see what this cycle produced and what it didn't, and there is enough energy left to make real changes — dropping a commitment, restructuring a habit, admitting a plan needs a different approach. The friction is a crisis of meaning, where frustration with what didn't work spills into judging the whole effort as wasted.

Do an honest audit of the month so far: what to keep, what to change, what to stop. Make one decisive cut or adjustment this week, and be specific about it. Clearing the deck now leaves room for the next new Moon.
