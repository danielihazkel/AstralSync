title: Waning Crescent
---

Only a thin sliver of Moon remains, rising just before the Sun and fading into the dawn. This is the balsamic phase, the last few days of the lunar month, and its mood is quiet, tired, and quietly wise. The cycle is closing, and the body and mind often ask for less rather than more.

These days favor release and rest: finishing loose ends, letting go of what didn't take, sleeping more, and making space for whatever the next cycle wants to become. Intuition and dreams can be vivid. The friction is pushing against the ebb — launching something big on empty reserves, or feeling guilty for a lull that is entirely natural.

Treat this as a deliberate fallow period. Clear one small thing you've been carrying, whether a task, an object, or a grudge, and keep your calendar lighter where you can. The new Moon is close, and it will go better if you arrive rested.
