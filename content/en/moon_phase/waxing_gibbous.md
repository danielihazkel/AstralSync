title: Waxing Gibbous
---

The Moon is more than half full and swelling toward the full, rising in the afternoon and lighting most of the night. These are the refining days of the lunar month — the structure is in place, and what's needed now is polish, patience, and an honest look at the details.

This phase favors editing, rehearsing, and adjusting. You can see the shape of what's coming, which makes it a good time to improve it rather than start anything new. The friction is perfectionism and anxious anticipation: fiddling endlessly with the last ten percent, or worrying about how the finished thing will be received before it exists.

Pick the two or three refinements that would genuinely make a difference and let the rest go. Ask one trusted person for feedback now, while there is still time to use it, rather than after the full Moon has already put your work on display.
