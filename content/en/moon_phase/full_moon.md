title: Full Moon
---

The Moon is opposite the Sun, fully lit, rising at sunset and riding the whole night. These are the culminating days of the lunar month, when what was seeded around the new Moon comes into view — sometimes as a result, sometimes as a revelation about why it isn't working.

Feelings run high and visible now. Sleep can be lighter, conversations more charged, and things that were simmering tend to surface. The gift is clarity: what was hidden is illuminated, and you can see the full picture of a situation, including other people's side of it. The friction is overreaction, especially in relationships, where the Sun–Moon opposition can turn a difference of view into a standoff.

Let the full Moon show you something rather than decide something. Note what has come to light, celebrate what has ripened, and wait a day or two before acting on any strong emotional conclusion.
