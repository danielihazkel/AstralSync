title: Waning Gibbous
---

The Moon has passed full and is starting to shrink, rising later each evening and still bright through the small hours and the morning sky. Traditionally this is the disseminating phase: the harvest is in, and the days favor digesting what you learned and passing it on.

This is a good stretch for sharing, teaching, explaining, and thanking. Results from the full Moon can be put to use, and lessons are fresh enough to articulate clearly. The friction is preachiness — treating your hard-won insight as the answer everyone else needs — or simply coasting on the high of the culmination without integrating it.

Take what came to light at the full Moon and put it into words for someone else: a note, a debrief, a conversation, a thank-you. Explaining what happened is often how you discover what it actually meant, and it lets the energy of the month circulate rather than stall.
