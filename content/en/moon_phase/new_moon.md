title: New Moon
---

The Moon is sitting with the Sun right now, dark to the eye and starting over. These are the quiet days at the very top of the lunar month, when the previous cycle has fully drained away and the next has not yet shown a sliver. The mood tends to be low-lit and inward: energy can feel thin, but intentions land with unusual clarity.

This is seeding time. Traditionally it is the moment to name what you want the coming month to hold, not to push it into the world yet. The friction is impatience — expecting visible results from something that has only just been planted, or mistaking a tired body for a lack of direction.

Write down one intention for the month in a single plain sentence, and take the smallest possible first step toward it. Then let it sit in the dark a few days before you judge it.
