title: First Quarter
---

The Moon is half lit and squaring the Sun, standing high in the afternoon and evening sky. This is the first real test of the lunar month: the easy excitement of beginning has worn off, and whatever you started now meets its first obstacle, schedule clash, or hard decision.

These days favor building and committing. There is more energy available than at the crescent, and it wants an outlet — clearing a blockage, choosing between two paths, doing the unglamorous structural work. The friction is crisis mode: treating every snag as a sign that the plan was wrong, or forcing things through out of pure frustration.

Name the one thing currently in the way and deal with it directly this week. If you need to adjust the plan, adjust it, but don't abandon it at the first wall. Pushing through a quarter-moon problem is often how a project earns its shape.
