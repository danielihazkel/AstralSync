title: Waxing Crescent
---

A thin sliver of Moon is showing in the evening sky after sunset, and the light is growing night by night. These days carry the feeling of something newly sprouted: tender, hopeful, and easy to trample. What you set in motion around the new Moon is starting to ask for small, practical commitments.

This phase favors gathering — resources, information, allies, the first few hours of actual work. Momentum is real but fragile, so the friction is doubt. Old habits and other people's skepticism can talk you out of a plan before it has had the chance to prove anything.

Feed the intention with one concrete action each day, however modest: a call, a page, a purchase, a booked slot. Keep it mostly to yourself for now, and notice which resistance is genuine information and which is simply the discomfort of beginning.
