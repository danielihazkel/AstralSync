title: No mutable planets
---

None of your ten planets sit in Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, or Pisces. The mutable mode is about adapting — adjusting plans, letting go of a finished chapter, absorbing new information mid-course. Without it, you may start strongly or hold steadily, but changing direction once you have committed can feel costly, even when the facts have shifted.

This often shows up as a hunger for flexibility. You may admire easygoing, versatile people, or seek work and travel that force you to improvise. Sometimes the gap is overcompensated by sudden dramatic changes, scrapping everything at once because gradual adjustment never felt available.

Your decisiveness and consistency are valuable; people know where they stand with you. To loosen the joints, practise small revisions on purpose. When a plan meets new information this week, change one detail rather than defending it or abandoning it, and notice that adjustment does not have to mean defeat.
