title: A southern emphasis
---

Most of your planets sit above the horizon, in houses seven through twelve, which in the chart's layout is the southern half. Traditionally this is the public hemisphere: partnerships, shared resources, travel and learning, career, community, and the wider world. Your life tends to unfold in view, through what you contribute and achieve out there.

The gift is reach. You are drawn toward a stage, a mission, or a larger context, and you often find meaning by engaging with society rather than retreating from it. The friction is outward drift: measuring yourself by recognition, neglecting home and body, or running so long on public energy that your private reserves quietly empty.

The northern half of your chart, below the horizon, is the side of roots and inner life, and it is a natural place to grow toward. Protect one regular, unshared ritual that is just for you, and treat it with the same seriousness as any public commitment.
