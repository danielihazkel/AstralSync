title: An eastern emphasis
---

Most of your planets sit on the eastern side of the chart, around the Ascendant, in the half where the sky is rising. Traditionally this is the hemisphere of self-direction. You tend to set your own course and build your life from the inside out, more comfortable acting first and explaining later than waiting for circumstances or other people to open the door.

The gift is initiative. You don't need permission to begin, and you often recover from setbacks by simply choosing a new direction. The friction is self-reliance taken too far: doing everything yourself, missing the help that's on offer, or assuming that what you decide is what everyone else has agreed to.

The western half of your chart, the side of partnership and response, is quieter, and that makes it a good place to grow toward. Once in a while, before you decide, ask someone what they would do, and let their answer actually change your plan.
