title: A western emphasis
---

Most of your planets sit on the western side of the chart, around the Descendant, the half of the sky that is setting. Traditionally this is the hemisphere of relationship. Your life tends to take shape through other people: partners, clients, collaborators, rivals, and the opportunities they bring to your door.

The gift is responsiveness. You read situations well, adapt gracefully, and often do your best work in answer to someone else's need or invitation. The friction is waiting — letting others set the agenda, deferring decisions until the room has spoken, or feeling vaguely unsure what you want when nobody is asking.

The eastern half of your chart, the side of self-direction, is quieter, and that makes it a good place to grow toward. Practice starting one thing that no one asked you for, on your own timetable, and notice how it feels to be the person who makes the first move.
