title: A northern emphasis
---

Most of your planets sit below the horizon, in the first six houses, which in the chart's layout is the northern half. Traditionally this is the private hemisphere: self, resources, home, creativity, and daily work. Your energy tends to go into inner development and personal foundations rather than public roles or reputation.

The gift is depth. You build things that last because you take care of the roots first, and your sense of worth often comes from private satisfaction rather than outside applause. The friction is invisibility: your best work can stay unseen, and you may underplay ambitions or hesitate to step into roles that would put you in view.

The southern half of your chart, above the horizon, is the side of public life and outward reach, and it is a natural place to grow toward. Choose one thing you've been quietly perfecting and share it this year, even if it doesn't feel finished.
