title: Mars opposition Jupiter
---

The tug between doing and dreaming plays out in your relationships. You tend to meet your missing half in other people: partners who push you to go bigger while you handle the fight, or promoters whose grand talk you end up executing. Collaborations feel electric at the start — someone finally matches your energy — and then the bill arrives for promises made at that voltage.

The strength is range: paired with the right person, you can both start the fire and feed it, and your joint output dwarfs what either of you does alone. The friction is projection. You over-promise on someone else's behalf, resent them for the pace you set, or pick philosophical fights that are really contests about whose ambition steers.

When a plan doubles in size mid-conversation, pause and ask who will actually do the added work — and get the answer said out loud.
