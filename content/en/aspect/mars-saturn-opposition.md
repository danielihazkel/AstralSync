title: Mars opposition Saturn
---

The war between push and permission gets staged with other people. You meet your own brakes wearing someone else's face: the boss who slows your best idea, the cautious partner who audits your plans, the rulebook that materializes exactly when you build momentum. Or you flip roles and become the restrainer, tightening procedures around someone whose recklessness secretly speaks for your own throttled drive.

The strength is that this push-pull, once owned, makes you superb at pacing shared work — you can hold ambition and constraint in one field of view and negotiate schedules both sides can survive. The friction is the blame loop: resenting others for limits you would impose on yourself anyway, or dumping all the discipline on your own shoulders while others get to be the free ones.

Next time someone blocks you, write down which of their objections you privately share — argue with the rest, but act on those.
