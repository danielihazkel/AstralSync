title: Saturn conjunct Pluto
---

You build as if the inspectors were ruthless and the weather permanent. Endurance and intensity are fused in you: commitments are taken with sworn-oath seriousness, work is pursued with miner's patience, and when something must be cut away — a habit, a role, an entire life chapter — you can do it with surgical finality and start again from bedrock.

The strength is obvious: almost nothing outlasts you. The cost hides in the assumptions. You tend to run on scarcity logic — brace for loss, trust no shortcut, hold power close — and can turn discipline into self-punishment or leadership into quiet domination. Rest feels like negligence; delegation feels like exposure. People near you may experience your standards as a weather system.

Once a month, deliberately accept help or ease you have not earned, and notice that the structure holds anyway.
