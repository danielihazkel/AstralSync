title: Moon square Jupiter
---

Your need for comfort and your appetite for more grind against each other. Enough never quite lands: the cozy evening turns into overcommitting to three plans, the reassuring snack becomes the whole sleeve, the warm impulse becomes a promise larger than your actual bandwidth. Restlessness shows up precisely when things get settled, and you can mistake emotional agitation for a sign that something bigger is calling.

The productive side of the grind is genuine emotional range. Because your feelings keep pushing past their container, you outgrow stale households, small self-concepts, and comfort zones that would have quietly shrunk you. People with smoother charts often stay put; you keep enlarging what home and care can mean.

The corrosive side is soothing by inflation — spending, eating, promising, moving — which leaves the original need unmet and bigger. When the urge for more hits, name the specific feeling underneath it before you act on the appetite.
