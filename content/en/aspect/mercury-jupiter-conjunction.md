title: Mercury conjunct Jupiter
---

Thinking and meaning-making are fused in you: every fact arrives already reaching for the bigger story it belongs to. You explain things well because you genuinely see the shape of the whole, and people leave conversations with you feeling smarter and more hopeful than when they arrived.

The strength is synthesis — you connect fields, translate jargon, and teach almost by reflex. The friction is inflation. Enthusiasm rounds your numbers up, a plausible pattern gets stated as settled truth, and the fine print bores you at exactly the moment it matters. When someone corrects a detail, it can feel like an attack on the whole vision rather than a small repair.

Before you publish or promise, ask one deflationary question: what is the strongest version of the claim I can actually back? Say that version, and let the grandeur live in your delivery instead.
