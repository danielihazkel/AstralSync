title: Moon sextile Jupiter
---

There is an easy channel in you between feeling and faith. When you choose to use it, moods recover, hospitality comes naturally, and you can find the wider frame around a bad day without faking anything. Friends notice that a walk or a meal with you leaves them lighter.

The catch with this kind of ease is that it waits for you to reach for it. The channel doesn't open itself: on flat days you can default to low-grade gloom or grazing comfort, forgetting you own a reliable exit. Untended, the gift shrinks to mere pleasantness — you cheer others while skipping the practices that would actually restore you.

The friction is subtle: because uplift is available, you may never build it into structure, treating your resilience as a mood rather than a skill. Schedule the things that reliably raise you — the people, the places, the rituals — instead of waiting to feel like them.
