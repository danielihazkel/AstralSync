title: Mars conjunct Uranus
---

Your drive runs on live current. Decision and action arrive in the same instant: you see the gap, you are already through it, and only later does the explanation catch up. Routine physically hurts, restriction reads as a dare, and your reflexes — mental and bodily — fire faster than most people can track.

The strength is genuine originality of action. In a crisis you are superb, because the improvised move is your native move, and you will attempt what no one else has thought to attempt. The friction is that the same wiring quits jobs at noon, ends relationships mid-sentence, and takes physical risks for the voltage alone. Your breakthroughs and your breakages come off the same reel.

Install a twenty-four-hour delay on irreversible moves — the genuinely brilliant impulse will survive a day's wait, and the merely restless one will not.
