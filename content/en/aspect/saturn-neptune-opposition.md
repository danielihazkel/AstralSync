title: Saturn opposition Neptune
---

Realism and reverie face off across your relationships. You tend to hold one pole and hire someone for the other: the dreamer partner whose finances you manage, the pragmatist boss who makes you feel like the office poet, the flaky friend you resent and envy at once. Whoever stands opposite you carries the half you have declined to live.

Held consciously, this polarity makes you an unusual builder of meaning: you can give form to someone's vision or soul to someone's institution, and you spot both fraud and burnout early because you know the smell of each. Held unconsciously, it runs a disappointment engine — idealizing the practical people, then finding them cold; funding the visionaries, then finding them careless — while your own dream-plus-discipline project stays unstarted.

Take back one duty you outsourced to a realist or one dream you outsourced to a dreamer, and carry it yourself this season.
