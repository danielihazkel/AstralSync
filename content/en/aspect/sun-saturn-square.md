title: Sun square Saturn
---

Ambition and doubt share a wall in you, and they pound on it daily. You want to matter — visibly, on the record — while an inner examiner keeps returning your work marked insufficient. Authority figures make it worse: you bristle at their judgment and crave their approval in the same breath.

The productive side of this grind is that nothing you build is hollow. Where luckier charts skate, you re-check, rehearse, and earn, so your competence has foundations that hold under weight. The corrosive side is the deferral loop: waiting for permission that never comes, hiding finished work, mistaking the examiner's voice for the truth about your ceiling.

This square converts friction into mastery, but only through repetitions in public. Submit the work while it still scares you slightly — that discomfort is the aspect doing its job.
