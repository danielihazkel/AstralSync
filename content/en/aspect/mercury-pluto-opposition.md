title: Mercury opposition Pluto
---

Power struggles find your conversations, or your conversations find them. You repeatedly draw interlocutors who seem to interrogate, dominate, or withhold — or you discover, mid-argument, that you have become that figure yourself, cross-examining someone who only wanted to chat. The intensity feels like it belongs to the other person; the aspect suggests half of it is yours.

Used consciously, this polarity is a gift for high-stakes dialogue: negotiation, therapy-grade listening, editorial confrontation. You can hear what an opponent is protecting and say the unsayable thing cleanly, and people trust you with secrets because you visibly can handle them. The friction is projection — assuming hidden agendas until you generate real ones, escalating discussions into loyalty tests, meeting suspicion with the exact evasiveness that confirms it.

In your next charged exchange, name your own agenda out loud first. Putting your cards face-up usually reveals whether the other hand was ever actually hidden.
