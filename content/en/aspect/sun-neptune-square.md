title: Sun square Neptune
---

Who you are and what you long for keep slipping out of each other's grasp. You build an identity, then a fog rolls in — disillusionment with the goal, a glamour that seduces you sideways, a fatigue that no schedule explains. Others sense the shimmer and project onto it, praising a you that doesn't quite exist or blaming one that never did.

The friction is real, and so is its yield: artists, healers, and visionaries are made from exactly this grind between ego and ocean. Because clarity never comes free, you learn to test it, and your eventual discernment — about people, promises, and your own motives — runs deeper than most. Untended, though, the square drifts toward evasion, idealized entanglements, and quiet self-deception.

When a plan feels destined, delay the commitment by a week and re-read it in dry light.
