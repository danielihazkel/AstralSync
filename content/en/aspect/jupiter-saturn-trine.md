title: Jupiter trine Saturn
---

Growth and structure cooperate in you without being asked. You size opportunities accurately, pace yourself well, and rarely mistake a spike of luck for a trend — the builder's temperament, running quietly in the background. People hand you responsibility early because your ambition reads as trustworthy rather than hungry.

The strength is steady, cumulative progress: careers, savings, reputations that thicken with each year. The risk of a trine is that you never notice the machinery. Because moderate plans succeed reliably, you may stop testing what you could actually carry, and a life of comfortable competence can substitute for the larger work you were built for. Ease becomes a ceiling no one imposed but you.

Once a year, deliberately take on a project one size too big — this aspect gives you the suspension to handle rougher roads than you've been choosing.
