title: Venus conjunct Saturn
---

Love and duty occupy the same seat in you. Affection is never casual: when you commit, you build — showing up in illness, remembering the unglamorous anniversaries, treating a promise as architecture rather than mood. People who win your loyalty hold something that does not wobble, and your taste runs the same way: spare, durable, allergic to glitter.

The cost of the fusion is a chill at the threshold. You audit affection before letting it in, suspect warmth that arrives too easily, and quietly assume you must earn what others are given free. That can read as coldness when it is actually caution, and it teaches partners to stop offering the tenderness you most want.

Your bar is a gift; the moat around it is not. Once someone has proven steady, say the warm thing out loud instead of assuming your reliability speaks for you.
