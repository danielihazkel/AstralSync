title: Mercury trine Neptune
---

Image and idea arrive together in you, already married. You explain by evoking: the right story, the right comparison, the tone that makes a hard truth hearable. People confide in you because you seem to understand what they mean before they finish meaning it, and often you do.

Because this channel is frictionless, its risks are quiet ones. You can rely on impressionistic understanding so habitually that you stop verifying — the gist is always available, so why check the text? Over time this breeds a soft spot for beautiful explanations over correct ones, and your gift for making anything sound plausible can lull you along with your audience. The empathy and eloquence are the strength; unaudited plausibility is the leak.

Pick your highest-stakes belief this month and trace it to a primary source. Keep the poetry, but make it a habit to know which of your images are load-bearing.
