title: Moon square Mercury
---

Your head and your heart file separate reports, and they rarely agree on the details. What you feel resists the words you have for it; what you say in the moment often is not what you meant an hour later, and the gap can make you doubt your own testimony.

The gift hiding in this friction is a bilingual mind. Because translation never comes free, you work at it, and over time you develop a precision about inner life that people with easier wiring never build. Untrained, though, the square shows up as arguing when you are sad, going quiet when you are hurt, or editing feelings until they sound reasonable and no longer sound true.

When words and mood disagree, report both: say *the logical answer is X, but it lands wrong*. Naming the split is the skill.
