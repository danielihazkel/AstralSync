title: Mercury square Mars
---

You finish other people's sentences, and not always kindly. Mercury square Mars wires impatience directly into your speech: a slow explanation feels like a personal tax, and by the third meandering minute you have interrupted, corrected, or mentally left the room. Small disagreements escalate fast because your temper travels through words before you notice it moving.

Handled raw, this aspect makes enemies out of colleagues. Trained, it is a genuine weapon: the same friction that makes you snap also gives you mental stamina, and you can hold a hard debate long after politer minds fold. You think best under verbal pressure — the argument is where your ideas get good.

When you feel the interruption rising, write the objection down instead of saying it; if it still matters when they finish, deliver it once, at half volume.
