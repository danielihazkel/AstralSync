title: Moon sextile Pluto
---

You have a working staircase into your own depths. When you choose to use it, you can sit with heavy material — yours or someone else's — without flinching, name the thing under the thing, and come back changed rather than damaged. Friends in real trouble seek you out because you don't need their pain sanitized before you'll look at it.

The staircase, though, requires descending on purpose. Left unused, this sextile settles for depth-flavored conversation: you know the difficult topics exist, gesture at them knowingly, and never actually book the therapy session, open the family question, or have the conversation that would move something. That's the friction — insight rich enough to feel like progress while everything stays exactly where it was.

Your strength is transformation at low cost; changes that require crisis for others require only honesty from you. Pick the one emotional pattern you've named the most and done the least about, and take a first concrete step this month.
