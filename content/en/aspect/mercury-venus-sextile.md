title: Mercury sextile Venus
---

Words cooperate with you when you invite them to. You have a reliable knack for the graceful phrase — the toast, the tactful reply, the sentence that turns a tense thread friendly — but it works on request rather than by reflex, like a good instrument that still has to be picked up.

Picked up, it is quietly powerful: you write the note that mends the friendship, name the compromise both sides can live with, put language around feelings other people only gesture at. Left in its case, the gift shrinks to small talk. You settle for pleasant instead of persuasive, and conversations that could have deepened stay agreeable and forgettable.

Fluency this available deserves scheduled use. This week, write the one message you have been telling yourself you would phrase properly someday — the thanks, the apology, the ask.
