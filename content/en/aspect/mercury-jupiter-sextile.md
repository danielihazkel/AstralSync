title: Mercury sextile Jupiter
---

There is an easy channel in you between the detail and the big picture. When you sit down to learn, subjects open up quickly; when you explain, the right analogy tends to be nearby. Editors, teachers, and mentors like working with you because you take feedback as material rather than verdict.

But this is an on-request talent, not an automatic one. The sextile offers opportunity without insistence: the course you never enroll in, the book outline that stays an outline, the smart observation you keep to yourself in the meeting. Left idle, the gift shrinks to being pleasant company at dinner. The lesser friction is breadth — you sample many topics and can stall before depth in any of them.

Pick one subject this season and push it past the interesting stage into the competent stage — write it up, present it, teach it once.
