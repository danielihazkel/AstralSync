title: Jupiter conjunct Saturn
---

Expansion and consolidation are fused in you, so every opportunity arrives with a blueprint attached. You don't just want more — you want more that lasts, and you instinctively test each opening for load-bearing capacity before you commit. Growth, in your hands, looks less like a leap and more like a well-poured foundation.

The strength is obvious over time: what you build compounds, because optimism and realism sign off on the same plan. The friction is that the two drives fire together, so enthusiasm and doubt arrive in the same instant. You can talk yourself out of a good bet while drafting it, or hedge a vision until it shrinks into a chore, mistaking caution for wisdom.

When a plan excites and scares you equally, set a start date before you finish perfecting it.
