title: Moon opposition Mars
---

Conflict has a way of finding you through other people. You keep meeting the angry boss, the combative sibling, the partner whose temper flares at exactly your softest spots — as if the fight keeps happening one seat over from where your own anger should be sitting.

The pattern usually means the assertion is yours, outsourced. Owned, this axis gives you real fire: the capacity to defend your needs and stay independent inside closeness without abandoning either one. Disowned, you oscillate — accommodating until you erupt, or provoking others into expressing the frustration you will not, then feeling wronged by the explosion you quietly commissioned.

Next time someone's anger lands on you, check for your own unsent version of it. Say the smaller true thing early, and the big fight often never books the room.
