title: Sun opposition Uranus
---

Freedom keeps showing up in your life wearing other people's faces. You attract mavericks, flakes, and brilliant disruptors — partners who upend your plans, colleagues who change everything without warning — and through them you meet the wildness you hesitate to claim as your own. What you disown, they act out, usually at maximum inconvenience.

The strength of this polarity is perspective: you understand both the person who needs the anchor and the person who needs the exit, which makes you a rare translator between stability and change. The friction is the seesaw — clinging until you suddenly bolt, or playing the steady one until resentment turns you unpredictable overnight.

When someone's erratic behavior enrages you, ask which of your own suppressed freedoms they are performing, and reclaim a piece of it deliberately.
