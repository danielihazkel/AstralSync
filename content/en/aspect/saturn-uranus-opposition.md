title: Saturn opposition Uranus
---

Order and rebellion split themselves between you and the people across from you. Play the responsible one, and you attract free spirits who scramble your plans; play the free spirit, and rule-keepers materialize to fence you in. Workplaces cast you in whichever role is vacant — the steady hand among mavericks, the disruptor among bureaucrats — and you argue with your counterpart about a conflict that is really internal.

The strength here is genuine bilingualism: you can translate between institutions and innovators, which makes you valuable in any transition, merger, or reform. The friction is projection — resenting others' recklessness while envying it, or mocking their caution while depending on it, and swapping sides without noticing.

When you catch yourself furious at someone's rigidity or chaos, name the same trait's location in your own life before you answer them.
