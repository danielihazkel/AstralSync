title: Mars trine Saturn
---

Your energy runs through good plumbing. Where others oscillate between binges of effort and weeks of collapse, you meter your force: steady output, realistic pacing, tools maintained, promises kept. You instinctively know how long things actually take, which makes you the person projects quietly depend on.

The strength is durable execution — you finish, and finishing compounds into skill, savings, and a reputation that opens doors force never could. The taken-for-granted risk is that control this smooth stops feeling like a choice. You may never test your ceiling, because nothing ever pushes back hard enough to reveal it, and your comfort with restraint can read to others as coolness or make you impatient with people whose engines run hotter and messier than yours.

Once a season, take on something with a real chance of failure — the trine's machinery stays honest only under genuine load.
