title: Moon opposition Mercury
---

You tend to live one end of a tug-of-war at a time: either the analyst who wants feelings explained, or the feeler who finds explanation beside the point. Often the other end shows up as a person — a partner who dissects your moods, a friend whose relentless logic you keep defending your heart against.

The reward of this polarity is range. You can genuinely do both — build the argument and read the room — and when you manage to hold the two ends yourself, you make an unusually fair witness to other people's conflicts. Unheld, it becomes debates about feelings: you explain when someone needs comfort, or flood with mood when someone needs a plan.

In your next tense conversation, ask one question before offering one interpretation. The order matters more than the content.
