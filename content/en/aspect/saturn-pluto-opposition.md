title: Saturn opposition Pluto
---

Power and authority confront you through other people. Your working life fills with formidable figures — the boss whose control is absolute, the partner whose will meets yours like rock, the institution that seems built to grind you specifically — and every standoff poses the same question: submit, dominate, or find the third thing. You rarely get easy opponents, which is the aspect's strange compliment.

Lived well, this polarity forges negotiators of real substance. You learn where legitimate authority ends and coercion begins, how to hold a line without cruelty, and how to be trusted with other people's leverage; survivors of hard systems often seek your counsel. Lived blindly, it cycles between endurance and eruption — years of compliance, then a rupture that costs the pension, the partnership, or the reputation.

In your current standoff, write down what a clean fifty-fifty settlement would look like before deciding it is impossible.
