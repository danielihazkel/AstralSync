title: Moon trine Saturn
---

Composure comes to you unforced. Your moods have a floor: bad news lands, you absorb it, and within hours you're doing the next sensible thing. You keep confidences, keep schedules, and keep households running, and people file you — accurately — under unshakable. Emotional maturity that others sweat for decades to build seems to have shipped with you.

Here is the tax on effortlessness: because containment costs you nothing, you may never learn to open. Your steadiness reads as self-sufficiency, so friends stop asking how you are; you don't correct them, and years can pass with you known by everyone and disclosed to no one. Ease this smooth can also make you quietly impatient with messier people, mistaking their turbulence for weakness.

The strength is a calm that steadies whole rooms; the risk is solitude wearing calm's uniform. Regularly volunteer one unpolished feeling to someone you trust — before you've processed it into something presentable.
