title: Sun sextile Mars
---

Your drive is on good terms with your sense of purpose. When you decide something matters, energy shows up to serve it — not in a flood, but in a steady, usable supply. You assert yourself without drama, defend your ground without holding grudges, and recover from exertion faster than most people around you.

The sextile's fine print: this cooperation must be invoked. Left idle, the talent shows up as pleasant busyness — mild ambition, mild effort, nothing that would embarrass you and nothing that would define you either. You are capable of far more push than your comfortable baseline suggests.

Pick one aim this month that requires actual assertion — asking, negotiating, competing — and spend the ease you were given on purpose.
