title: Venus conjunct Uranus
---

Attraction and electricity are the same current in you. What you love has to surprise you — the unconventional face, the friendship that starts mid-argument, the aesthetic nobody else has caught onto yet. You fall fast, originally, and without reference to anyone's approval, and your taste often turns out to be five years ahead of the room.

The fusion has a cost: the moment love becomes routine, something in you starts checking the exits. You can confuse freedom with distance, keep partners slightly off-balance to keep yourself interested, and end good things simply because they became predictable. What you call independence sometimes lands on others as weather.

The strength is that no one bores you into betraying yourself; the friction is that steadiness reads as a threat. Practice announcing your need for space before you act on it — a stated orbit keeps what a sudden exit would break.
