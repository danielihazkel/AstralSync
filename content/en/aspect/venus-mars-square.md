title: Venus square Mars
---

What you want and what you love refuse to sign the same treaty. Venus square Mars sets desire and affection at cross purposes: the steady, kind person leaves you strangely unmoved, while friction — the difficult one, the almost-argument, the maybe — lights you up completely. Hot-and-cold is not something that happens to your relationships; it is often the current you bring to them.

Lived unconsciously, this pattern manufactures drama on schedule: you provoke a quarrel to feel the spark, then resent the quarrel. Worked with, it becomes passion that survives the honeymoon — because you never expected love to be frictionless, you do not quit when it stops being smooth.

Notice the moment you pick a fight out of boredom rather than grievance, and redirect that charge into wanting your partner instead of testing them.
