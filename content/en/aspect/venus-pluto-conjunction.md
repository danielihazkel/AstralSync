title: Venus conjunct Pluto
---

You don't do casual. Attraction arrives in you as gravity — total, private, and slightly alarming — and what you love, you want to know all the way down. People sense the intensity before you say a word: you have a magnetism that pulls confessions out of strangers and makes lukewarm company feel like a waste of a body.

The fusion's shadow is that depth can shade into grip. Merging feels like safety, so distance feels like danger, and you may find yourself monitoring, testing, or quietly steering a partner you'd swear you only adore. Losses cut you deeper than most, and you can nurse them into identities.

The strength is love with an all-in loyalty few people ever receive; the friction is the fist it closes into under threat. When fear of losing someone spikes, name the fear to them directly — surveillance is the question you were too proud to ask.
