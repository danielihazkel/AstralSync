title: Mercury conjunct Uranus
---

Your mind runs on a faster clock. Conclusions arrive before you can show your work; conversations feel like waiting for others to load. The insight and the impulse to say it are welded together, so you interrupt, leap, and occasionally drop a genuinely original idea into a room that was not asking for one.

The strength is authentic invention — you see the crack in the standard model because you never fully installed the standard model. The friction is transmission. Skipped steps read as arrogance, contrarian reflexes fire even when the consensus happens to be right, and your attention can shear off a project the moment it stops sparking. Brilliant openings, abandoned middles.

When you deliver a leap, retrace two of the steps you skipped out loud — the idea lands twice as often, and you sometimes catch the leaps that were only static.
