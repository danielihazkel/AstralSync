title: Mercury trine Uranus
---

Original thinking is your resting state. Patterns connect across domains without strain; you absorb new tools quickly, predict where a trend bends, and deliver the unexpected angle so smoothly that people assume you prepared it. There is no war between your intuition and your logic — they trade notes constantly.

The hazard of this flow is casualness. Insight that costs nothing gets spent like it is worth nothing: you toss out ideas in conversation that others would build companies on, and because novelty is always available, you rarely stay with one insight long enough to compound it. The strength is effortless invention; the friction is that nothing effortless gets defended — your best thinking scatters as charming remarks instead of accruing into a body of work.

Start capturing what you casually generate — a running document, reviewed weekly — and commit to developing one idea per quarter past the point where it stops being fun.
