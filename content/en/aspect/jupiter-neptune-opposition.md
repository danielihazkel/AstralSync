title: Jupiter opposition Neptune
---

Your idealism tends to arrive wearing someone else's face. You meet people who seem luminous — the visionary partner, the wise teacher, the cause that finally deserves everything — and you enlarge them with your belief until the pedestal cracks. Or you play the luminous one, absorbing others' outsized hopes and quietly panicking about the altitude.

The strength on this axis is range: you understand both the hunger to believe and the cost of being believed in, which can make you an unusually compassionate judge of gurus, causes, and promises. The friction is the projection cycle itself — over-giving to the idealized other, then swinging to cynicism when they turn out to be human, and calling that discernment.

When admiration for someone spikes toward worship, list two of their ordinary flaws the same day; loving the real person is the assignment.
