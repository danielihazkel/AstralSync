title: Moon opposition Saturn
---

Softness and severity face off across your relationships, and one of them is usually standing in the other person. You may keep finding partners, bosses, or parents who are withholding, exacting, or perpetually busy — leaving you the one who needs. Or you flip poles: becoming the stern, dutiful one whose people strike you as endlessly, unreasonably needy. The coldness in the room always seems to belong to someone.

The gift of living this polarity is range: you understand both the child waiting at the door and the adult who couldn't get there, and when you integrate the two you make an exceptional parent, mentor, or steward — warm without indulgence, firm without cruelty.

The trap is the frozen standoff, where you withhold need to punish someone's withholding. When a bond starts feeling like a negotiation between duty and hunger, say the vulnerable thing first instead of waiting for the other side to thaw.
