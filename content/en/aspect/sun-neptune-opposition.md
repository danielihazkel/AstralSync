title: Sun opposition Neptune
---

Neptune meets you in the mirror of other people. You draw dreamers, sufferers, and enchanting question marks — partners you idealize until the focus sharpens, charismatic figures who turn out to be fog machines, or wounded souls who elect you rescuer. Each one carries a piece of your own unlived longing for transcendence.

Lived consciously, this polarity gives you extraordinary attunement: you sense what others cannot say and can meet illusion with compassion instead of contempt, which makes you a gifted counselor, artist, or ally to the lost. Lived unconsciously, it runs the disappointment cycle — enchantment, sacrifice, betrayal, repeat — with your identity dissolving a little further each round while you call it love.

Before merging with anyone's dream or drama, name in writing what you actually observe versus what you hope, and act on the observed column.
