title: Mars square Jupiter
---

Your drive and your sense of possibility argue about scale. Mars wants the win now; Jupiter wants it enormous; between them you commit to more than any week can hold. You start strong, discover the true size of what you promised, and either sprint yourself ragged or quietly abandon the thing and reach for a fresher, shinier goal.

The gift inside the grind is real: this square generates more raw initiative than almost any other contact, and when you finally aim it at one target, you outwork rooms full of people. The cost is chronic overreach — inflated estimates, crusades picked in anger, the conviction that enthusiasm can substitute for logistics. It cannot, and each collapse teaches you to distrust your own fire a little more.

Halve your estimate of what you can do this month, then actually finish that half; completed swings rebuild the trust that overpromising spends.
