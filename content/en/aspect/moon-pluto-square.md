title: Moon square Pluto
---

Needing someone feels, to you, like handing them a weapon. So your attachments run on a grinding double program: pull them closer, then tighten your grip on how close is allowed; extract reassurance, then distrust it because you extracted it. Small slights detonate at a depth that surprises even you — because the feeling isn't about the missed call, it's about every abandonment the missed call rhymes with.

The grind's yield is formidable. You have crossed emotional territory most people spend their lives avoiding, and it made you impossible to shock, hard to manipulate, and genuinely useful to anyone in a crisis. Depth is not a theory to you; it's your home terrain.

The corrosion is control wearing love's clothing — the tests, the jealousy, the silent audits of loyalty that eventually create the abandonment they feared. When you feel the grip tightening, say the fear underneath it plainly instead of engineering proof against it.
