title: Mercury conjunct Neptune
---

You think in pictures, atmospheres, and undertones. Where others process a conversation's words, you absorb its weather — the unsaid thing under the agenda item, the mood behind the email. Language, when you use it well, carries this extra cargo: your descriptions make people feel places they have never been.

The strength is imagination fused with speech: storytelling, metaphor, and an almost porous empathy for how ideas land. The friction is the porousness itself. Facts blur at the edges; you remember the meaning of what was said rather than what was said, and you can hear a promise where there was only politeness. Wishful listening and accidental embellishment are the twin leaks in an otherwise beautiful vessel.

For anything with consequences — dates, money, commitments — write it down at the moment of agreement and read it back. Let the poetry roam everywhere else.
