title: Mars square Uranus
---

Pressure builds in you until something arcs. You can run disciplined and cooperative for weeks, then one more pointless rule lands and you detonate — quitting, swerving, saying the unsayable — and the people around you never saw the charge accumulating. Your relationship to freedom is not a preference; it is a physical requirement with a bad temper.

The productive face of this square is courage nobody can rent: you will make the abrupt, correct move — leave the failing plan, call out the broken process — while others wait for permission that is not coming. The cost is timing. The rupture often fires at the worst moment, torching work that needed only two more weeks, and accidents cluster when you act at the speed of irritation.

Track your own pressure gauge: when everything starts feeling intolerable at once, schedule the change deliberately before it schedules itself.
