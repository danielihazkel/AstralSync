title: Mars opposition Neptune
---

Desire and dream face each other through other people. You are drawn to the dreamers, the wounded, the elusive — partners whose potential you can fight for, colleagues whose vagueness you compensate with drive — or you play the drifting one and let someone else supply the push. Either way, one person holds the sword and one holds the fog, and the roles harden.

The strength is real: you make an extraordinary champion for people and causes that cannot fight for themselves, and the right creative partner can give your energy a direction worth its force. The friction is the exchange rate — rescuing becomes resenting, inspiration becomes disillusion, and you can spend years of effort on someone's imagined future self while the actual person stays put.

Before you fight another battle on someone's behalf, ask them to name what they will do for it this week — and let their answer size your effort.
