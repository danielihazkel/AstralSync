title: Moon square Saturn
---

Your needs and your inner supervisor are in a standing argument. The moment a feeling rises, something in you audits it: is this reasonable, is this earned, is this convenient for everyone else? Usually the audit wins, the feeling gets filed, and you carry on — dependable, composed, and quietly running a deficit. Comfort often arrives late in your life, if you let it arrive at all.

The grind produces something real: emotional durability that isn't performance. Because nothing soothing was handed to you, you know exactly how to build stability — routines, savings, promises kept — and people lean on that. What luckier charts call security, you have actually constructed.

The corrosive side is treating the audit as truth: deciding your needs are the problem rather than the supervisor's tone. Deprivation starts to feel like virtue. When you catch yourself postponing rest or comfort until you've earned it, take it now, on purpose, and notice that nothing collapses.
