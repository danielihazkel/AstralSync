title: Venus opposition Mars
---

Your love life runs on a live current of push and pull. With Venus opposing Mars, harmony and desire sit at opposite ends of a rope, and you tend to hand one end to a partner: you play the peacemaker while they carry the heat, or you bring the hunger while they insist on calm. Closeness increases the charge — the nearer someone gets, the more the tug shows.

The strength here is range. You understand both sides of every romantic standoff from the inside, which makes you, at your best, an unusually fair partner and a genuinely exciting one. The friction is the swing itself: pursue, retreat, appease, provoke — a rhythm that can exhaust two people who actually love each other.

When you catch yourself retreating or pressing, say so out loud; naming your end of the rope is what stops the tug.
