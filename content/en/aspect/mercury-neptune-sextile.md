title: Mercury sextile Neptune
---

Your rational mind and your imagination are on friendly speaking terms. When you invite it, intuition supplies the image that makes an abstract point land, the hunch about what a client actually wants, the phrase that turns a dry report humane. Colleagues notice that your explanations have warmth without losing the thread.

The gift is optional, though, and that is its weakness. Under deadline pressure you can default to purely literal communication and leave the poetry unopened for months; the sextile never insists. And when you do lean on intuition without checking it, the mild version of Neptune's blur appears — a paraphrase that drifted from the source, a vibe presented with slightly more certainty than it earned.

Give the imagination standing work: a weekly page of freewriting, or one metaphor deliberately built into every important document. Invited regularly, this faculty strengthens; ignored, it stays a party trick.
