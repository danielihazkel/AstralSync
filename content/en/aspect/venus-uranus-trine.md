title: Venus trine Uranus
---

Freedom and affection get along in you without being introduced. You love originally and lightly: friends stay friends for decades because you never cage them, partners get genuine room, and your taste — a little strange, confidently your own — draws people who are tired of the standard menu. Novelty refreshes your bonds instead of threatening them.

The hazard of this ease is that detachment can pass for enlightenment. Because space costs you nothing, you may offer it where presence was needed, keep everything pleasantly open-ended, and drift past the moment a connection wanted to become a commitment. Nothing breaks; things just stay agreeably unfinished.

The strength is love without possessiveness; the untested edge is love without weight. Choose one bond and volunteer some constancy the trine never demanded of you — a recurring date, a promise with a horizon — and notice that freedom survives it.
