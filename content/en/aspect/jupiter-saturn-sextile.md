title: Jupiter sextile Saturn
---

Your optimism and your discipline are on speaking terms, which is rarer than it sounds. When you decide to grow, you can usually find the structure to support it — a schedule, a mentor, a budget that makes the vision survivable. Elders and institutions tend to open doors for you once you knock.

That last clause is the catch: you have to knock. A sextile is a standing offer, not a delivery service, and this one quietly expires when unused. Your strength is sustainable ambition — plans that mature instead of collapsing. Your friction is a comfortable patience that shades into postponement, letting "the timing isn't right yet" become a permanent address.

Pick one long-range goal and give it a concrete next step with a date; this aspect pays out only after you move first.
