title: Venus square Jupiter
---

Your taste and your appetite argue over the same wallet. Part of you wants the refined, chosen thing; another part wants more of everything — more dessert, more romance, more beautiful objects in the cart. The grind shows up as extravagance you later have to explain: the trip that ran double, the flirtation that inflated into a promise.

The productive side of this friction is real largeness of heart. You give without keeping score, celebrate other people's wins loudly, and refuse the stinginess that shrinks lesser charts. Worked consciously, the square teaches discernment the expensive way — every overreach sharpens your sense of what is actually worth wanting.

The corrosive side is treating excess as proof of love, then resenting the bill. Notice the moment "this would be nice" becomes "I deserve this" — that pivot is where the overshoot begins, and where you can pause.
