title: Mercury trine Pluto
---

Seeing through things is your default setting. Structures, sales pitches, group dynamics — the underlying machinery presents itself to you without effort, and you can name it in language that makes others feel the click of recognition. Research that would grind others down feels to you like following a scent.

The risk of this ease is what it does to your relationship with surfaces. Because depth costs you nothing, you can start treating everyone's stated reasons as decoration and your decoded version as the only truth — forgetting that sometimes the surface is the truth, and that people feel handled when they are permanently seen through. Your persuasiveness compounds the hazard: insight this smooth can slide into quiet manipulation without ever feeling like a choice.

Practice taking one person per day at their word, no decoding. Reserve the X-ray for problems that ask for it, and disclose your read instead of steering with it.
