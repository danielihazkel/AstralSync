title: Venus opposition Pluto
---

The intensity usually shows up as the other person. You attract partners with gravity — magnetic, consuming, sometimes controlling — and find yourself in relationships that feel less like companionship than like weather systems. Opposite them you play the reasonable one, the accommodating one, while the deep currents you disown in yourself run the whole household from their side of the bed.

The gift of the polarity is that you learn what desire actually is, not the greeting-card version: your bonds strip you honest, and you emerge from each one knowing more of your own underworld. The friction is the tug-of-war itself — power struggles over money, sex, or loyalty in which surrender and domination keep trading masks.

Balance begins when you claim your half of the intensity. In the next standoff, state what you want nakedly, without strategy — wanting out loud is the power you keep assigning to them.
