title: Mercury square Jupiter
---

Your mouth writes checks your calendar has to cash. The visionary in you keeps outbidding the reporter: a modest update swells into a manifesto, a rough estimate hardens into a promise, and three tabs into researching one question you are suddenly planning a book. The grind is between saying it big and getting it right.

Worked honestly, this square builds real intellectual range. Because your reach keeps exceeding your grasp, you are forced to actually learn — to backfill the claims, read the counterarguments, and develop judgment about what you know versus what you merely find exciting. People who never overreach never acquire that discipline. The cost when you skip the backfill is credibility: listeners start quietly discounting your numbers.

Adopt a personal fact-check lag: let any sweeping claim sit for a day, then restate it with one source and one caveat before it leaves the house.
