title: Mercury trine Saturn
---

Order comes naturally to your thoughts. You file information as it arrives, keep commitments you make verbally, and produce plans other people can actually execute. In chaotic meetings you are the one who quietly restates the decision and the deadline, and the room relaxes because someone is holding the structure.

The flow is real, and so is its blind spot. Because structure costs you nothing, you can mistake tidiness for truth — preferring the well-organized answer over the messy correct one, dismissing intuitive or half-formed ideas from yourself and others because they arrive without documentation. A mind that never strains against its own categories slowly stops noticing what the categories exclude. The reliability is the strength; the unexamined conservatism is the tax.

Once a month, deliberately entertain one idea that offends your sense of rigor — follow it three steps before you file it — and see what your system was leaving out.
