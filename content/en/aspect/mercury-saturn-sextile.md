title: Mercury sextile Saturn
---

Discipline is available to your mind on friendly terms. When you decide to master something, you can build the scaffolding — notes, schedules, drills — without the self-war other people report, and your explanations have a calm, load-bearing quality that makes colleagues trust your summaries.

The catch with this ease is that it waits to be used. The sextile does not force study habits on you; it merely makes them cheap when you choose them, and it is entirely possible to drift for years on decent-but-unstructured thinking, vaguely aware you could be rigorous if you bothered. The lesser friction is dryness: when you do engage the discipline, your delivery can turn cautious and hedged, understating ideas that deserved conviction.

Choose one skill worth certifying and put real structure under it now — a course, a syllabus, a weekly review — while the terms are this good.
