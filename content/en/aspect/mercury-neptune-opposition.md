title: Mercury opposition Neptune
---

Clarity and enchantment sit at opposite ends of your seesaw, and other people usually occupy the far seat. You play the fact-checker to a dreamy partner, exasperated by their fog; or you are the dreamer, feeling flattened by someone's literal-minded questions. Either way, the conversation is really between two halves of your own mind.

The strength here is interpretive range: you can hear both what people say and what they wish, which makes you a gifted counselor, editor, or negotiator when you use both channels deliberately. The friction is projection and drift — idealizing a speaker and hearing wisdom that is not there, or dismissing genuine intuition because it arrived from an unrigorous source. Misunderstandings with this aspect tend to be sincere on both sides.

When you and someone else remember an agreement differently, resist assigning blame; reconstruct it in writing together, and notice which half of the seesaw you were sitting on.
