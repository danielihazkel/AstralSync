title: Sun sextile Moon
---

Your conscious aims and your emotional habits sit at a friendly angle: they cooperate whenever you bother to introduce them. Deciding what you want does not cost you inner peace, and taking care of yourself does not derail your ambitions — a quiet advantage many people spend years trying to build.

The catch with a sextile is that it is an opportunity, not a guarantee. Because the harmony is available rather than automatic, you can drift through stretches where will and feeling simply do not talk, and wonder why life feels flat despite nothing being wrong.

Treat the alignment as a muscle. Once a week, check whether your current project still feeds you emotionally — and adjust while adjustments are still cheap.
