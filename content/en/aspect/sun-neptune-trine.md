title: Sun trine Neptune
---

Imagination irrigates your identity without flooding it. You move between practical life and inner life on a well-worn path: inspiration arrives when called, empathy flows without draining you dry, and people relax around you because your presence carries a faint benediction. Creative or compassionate work feels less like effort than like remembering.

The hazard of water this smooth is that you drift on it. Grace that costs nothing gets spent on nothing — pleasant moods, tasteful consumption, spirituality as ambience rather than practice. You can float years downstream on almost-projects and generalized goodwill, never testing whether your vision holds weight, because nothing forces the question. Effortless compassion can also blur into avoiding every necessary conflict.

Choose one dream and give it deadlines and witnesses; flow becomes a gift only when it turns a wheel.
