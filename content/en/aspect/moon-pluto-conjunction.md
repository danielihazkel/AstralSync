title: Moon conjunct Pluto
---

You feel at archaeological depth. Where others have moods, you have strata: every attachment reaches down into questions of survival, loyalty, and who can truly be trusted with the soft parts. When you love, you love totally; casual affection strikes you as a contradiction in terms, and you can read the undercurrents of a room before anyone speaks.

The fusion of need and survival instinct has a shadow protocol. Because vulnerability once felt dangerous, you manage it — testing people covertly, withholding the real feeling while monitoring theirs, keeping one hand near the eject lever of any bond that matters. Intimacy becomes intelligence work, and the people closest to you sense they're being vetted long after they've passed.

The strength is regenerative: you can survive losses that would hollow others and come back stranger and stronger. Practice offering one unguarded feeling before you've confirmed it's safe — trust built by disclosure, not surveillance.
