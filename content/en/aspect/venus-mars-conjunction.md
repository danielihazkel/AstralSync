title: Venus conjunct Mars
---

Wanting and charming are the same gesture in you. Venus fused with Mars means desire does not wait politely behind affection: when someone interests you, they usually know within the hour, because your attention has warmth and momentum at once. People read you as magnetic partly because there is nothing coy to decode — the appetite and the tenderness arrive together.

That directness wins you beginnings other people only rehearse. The cost shows up after the chase: once the pursuit ends and the prize is simply present, a restlessness can set in, and you may mistake the quiet of security for the death of feeling. Some things you abandoned were not finished — they were just no longer running from you.

When boredom stirs in something good, plan a pursuit inside it — court the person you already have before scanning the horizon.
