title: Moon square Neptune
---

What you need and what you long for keep missing each other. The actual requirement might be sleep, dinner, and a direct conversation; the longing proposes instead a rescue, an escape, or a person glimpsed through such flattering fog that disappointment is pre-installed. You soothe yourself with fantasy the way others use food, and reality collects the tab: leaks in your boundaries, martyred caretaking, the recurring discovery that you've been pouring into someone who mostly needed to be poured into.

Ground rules turn this grind into art. Worked honestly, the square refines raw sensitivity into discernment — compassion that sees people accurately and helps anyway, imagination sturdy enough to make things rather than just yearn. Your empathy, once fenced, is the real kind.

Unworked, it's a cycle of enchantment, depletion, and disillusion. Before you commit care to anyone, list three things you concretely know about them — not sense, not hope, know — and let that list vote.
