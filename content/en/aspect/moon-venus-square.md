title: Moon square Venus
---

What soothes you and what you find beautiful pull in different directions. You crave the person who unsettles you, feel restless in the relationship that feeds you, buy the striking thing and live with the comfortable one — appetite and taste keep filing competing claims.

This tension is why your emotional life never goes stale: the square keeps renegotiating what *enough* means, and it can make you unusually honest about the difference between wanting and needing — a distinction most people never learn to feel from the inside. Unexamined, it leaks out as moody dissatisfaction: nothing is quite right, and the people who love you hear it as criticism they cannot answer.

When restlessness hits, ask which one is hungry — the need or the want — and feed that one first.
