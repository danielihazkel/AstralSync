title: Saturn square Uranus
---

Your life keeps staging the same argument: the structure you built versus the freedom it costs. You commit hard — to jobs, systems, obligations — then feel the walls closing and want to kick them down; or you break free and immediately start missing the scaffolding. Authority triggers both your compliance and your defiance, sometimes in the same meeting.

This grind, worked honestly, produces real reformers. Because you have lived both the cage and the chaos, your changes are load-bearing: you know exactly which rule is dead weight and which one holds up the roof. Unworked, the square becomes a lurch cycle — years of dutiful grinding punctuated by demolitions you later pay for, with the tension discharged as irritability at whoever currently represents the rules.

When the urge to blow something up arrives, write down what you would build in its place; if the page stays blank, wait.
