title: Sun opposition Mars
---

Your drive lives across the aisle from your identity, and it tends to show up wearing other people's faces. You attract the forceful, the competitive, the domineering — and the friction with them is often a negotiation with your own disowned assertiveness.

The strength is that you genuinely rise under opposition. A rival, a deadline, an unfair call: resistance clarifies you, and you frequently do your best work with someone pushing back. The trap is needing the enemy — drifting when nothing opposes you, or escalating small disagreements into arena matches because conflict is where you feel most alive.

Next time someone's aggression rankles you, ask what they are doing that you will not let yourself do. The answer is usually the trait you need to reclaim.
