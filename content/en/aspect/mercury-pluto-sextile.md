title: Mercury sextile Pluto
---

Depth is available to you without the usual dread. When you choose to dig — into a dataset, a person's real concern, a subject's ugly history — you can go far down and come back with the goods, and your findings land persuasively because you deliver them without the paranoid edge harder Pluto contacts carry.

The limitation is that choosing is required. This aspect offers a key to locked rooms but never pushes you through the door, and many people with it settle for capable surface work, vaguely aware they could have been the investigator, the analyst, the one who understood the thing completely. There is also a softer friction: because probing comes easily, you can slip into extracting more from people than you disclose, a one-way intimacy that eventually gets noticed.

Assign yourself one real excavation — a research question, a difficult conversation — each month, and match every probing question you ask with a disclosure of your own.
