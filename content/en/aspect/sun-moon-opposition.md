title: Sun opposition Moon
---

You were born under a full moon, with identity and needs facing each other across the sky. Life often feels like a seesaw: honor your ambitions and your inner life protests; tend your inner life and your ambitions feel abandoned. Frequently the tension gets outsourced — a partner, parent, or boss carries one end of the pole while you carry the other.

The strength here is perspective. Living between two poles teaches you to see both sides of nearly everything, which makes you a natural mediator and an honest self-critic. The friction is decision paralysis, and relationships that turn into proxy wars over your own unresolved split.

When you feel torn between what you want and what someone close to you needs, pause and check whether the conflict is actually between you two — or entirely yours.
