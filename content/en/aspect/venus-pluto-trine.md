title: Venus trine Pluto
---

Intensity comes to you domesticated. You bond deeply without drama: intimacy that terrifies other charts feels like home to you, and people open their vaults in your presence because your attention neither flinches nor exploits. Your loves tend to be few, total, and quietly transformative, and you handle shared resources — money, secrets, grief — with steady hands.

The risk of this flow is assuming everyone swims at your depth. Because closeness costs you nothing, you can under-credit how exposed others feel, or drift into a low-grade possessiveness so comfortable you'd never call it control. Ease can also make depth passive: you wait in the deep end rather than inviting anyone down.

The strength is fearless intimacy; the untested edge is what happens when it's contested. Check occasionally that your closest people still have doors you don't hold keys to — and that you'd honestly celebrate them using one.
