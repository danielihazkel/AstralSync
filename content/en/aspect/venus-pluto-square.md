title: Venus square Pluto
---

Love and power grind against each other in your chart. Wanting someone feels dangerously close to being at their mercy, so part of you seeks leverage the moment attachment forms — testing loyalty, keeping score, reading phones in your imagination if not in fact. Jealousy isn't a mood here; it's a pressure system, and it can turn tenderness into a siege.

The productive face of the friction is that you are incapable of shallow bonds. Every relationship becomes a crucible that burns off pretense, and people who survive your intensity get a loyalty that would walk through walls. You also regenerate: after each ending you'd swear was fatal, you rebuild a truer version of your wanting.

The corrosive face is control dressed as devotion. Catch the moment you start managing a partner's freedom to soothe your own fear — then sit with the fear for ten minutes before you act on it.
