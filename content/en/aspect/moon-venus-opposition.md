key: aspect/moon/venus/opposition
title: Moon opposition Venus
---

Your needs and other people's happiness sit at opposite ends of a seesaw, and you usually notice yours only after theirs are handled. You read a partner's disappointment from across the house, adjust before being asked, and call the adjustment love — which it partly is.

The strength here is genuine relational intelligence: few people track the emotional ledger of a relationship as precisely as you do. The distortion is that the ledger goes unspoken. You give at your own expense, expect the deficit to be noticed, and grow quietly resentful when it is not — then over-correct with a withdrawal nobody saw coming.

Practice asking before the account is overdrawn. Once a week, request something small and specific, out loud, and let someone else do the accommodating.
