title: Venus sextile Saturn
---

Steadiness is available to your affections whenever you reach for it. You have a quiet talent for the long game in relating — keeping confidences, honoring plans, letting trust accumulate in small deposits. Your taste matures well too: you're drawn to quality over novelty, and what you buy or make tends to last.

But this is an offer, not an installation. The sextile hands you the materials for durable love and asks you to do the assembling — the standing arrangement, the difficult conversation held calmly, the budget actually written down. Left passive, the gift shrinks to mere reserve: pleasant, dependable, and never quite committed to anything.

The strength is craftsmanship in relationships; the friction is treating restraint as the finished product. Choose one bond that matters and formalize something in it this month — a ritual, a plan, a promise with a date on it.
