title: Moon sextile Mercury
---

Between your moods and your vocabulary runs a well-paved road that you do not always use. The connection is friendly rather than automatic: when you choose to put feelings into words, they come out clear, proportionate, and easy for other people to receive.

That is a genuine skill — you can defuse a tense room with one accurate sentence, and friends leave conversations with you feeling understood rather than managed. The catch with easy roads is that nothing forces you onto them. Because expressing yourself is comfortable, it never feels urgent, and small resentments can sit unspoken for weeks while everyone assumes your silence means peace.

Treat articulation as maintenance, not emergency response. Once a week, tell someone one true sentence about how you actually are — before anything is wrong.
