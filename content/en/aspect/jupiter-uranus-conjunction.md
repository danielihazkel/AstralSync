title: Jupiter conjunct Uranus
---

In you, growth and breakthrough are the same event. You don't expand by increments; you expand by lightning — the sudden insight, the pivot nobody saw coming, the door that opens the week you stopped waiting for it. Your faith attaches most easily to the untested, and your best opportunities tend to look, at first, like disruptions.

The strength is genuine originality of direction: you find routes that aren't on anyone's map, and your timing for leaps is often uncanny. The friction is that plateaus feel like suffocation. You may detonate a perfectly good situation just to feel the air move, or chase novelty past the point where the last breakthrough would have paid off if tended.

Before you leap again, ask whether you're escaping stagnation or just discomfort with consolidation — then let one experiment run long enough to mature.
