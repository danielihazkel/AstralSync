title: Sun conjunct Mars
---

Your will and your drive are welded together: deciding and acting are one motion. You bring heat to whatever carries your name — projects, arguments, workouts — and people feel the wattage the moment you enter a room. Hesitation is barely in your vocabulary, and that decisiveness wins you ground others spend years asking permission for.

The cost of fusion is that you cannot see your own force. Pushback registers as obstruction rather than information, and what feels to you like healthy directness can land on others as a shove. Anger and identity share a fuse box, so criticism of your work feels like an attack on you.

You also burn hot rather than long. Pick fights and projects worth your fuel, and when someone flinches at your intensity, treat it as data — not weakness.
