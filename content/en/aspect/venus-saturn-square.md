title: Venus square Saturn
---

Wanting and deserving grind against each other in you. You long for closeness while an inner assessor tallies reasons you haven't earned it yet — not accomplished enough, not composed enough, not ready. So you ration your warmth, arrive guarded to what should be tender, and sometimes choose partners who confirm the suspicion that love is billed by the hour.

The productive side of the grind is that your affection, once given, is structural. You do not flake, flatter, or vanish; people build lives on your loyalty. The corrosive side is the transaction loop — proving instead of relating, withholding until it feels safe, reading a partner's ordinary distraction as the verdict you always feared.

This square matures love the slow way, through evidence. Watch for the moment you start performing worthiness instead of expressing want — then say the want plainly, before the assessor can edit it.
