title: Moon trine Neptune
---

Empathy flows through you without effort. You read rooms the way others read headlines, animals and small children gravitate to you, and your presence has a softening effect people can't quite name. Imagination and feeling cooperate so smoothly that beauty — music, light on water, a well-made sentence — genuinely nourishes you.

Effortless attunement carries two quiet risks. First, no membrane: because absorbing others costs you nothing in the moment, you don't notice the accumulating load until you're inexplicably exhausted or sad on someone else's behalf. Second, you assume attunement runs both ways — you know what everyone needs, so you never practice saying what you need, and mostly nobody asks.

The strength is a compassion that makes people feel completely accompanied; the drift is a self that goes underdescribed, even to you. Keep one practice that is purely yours — no audience, no one comforted — and state one plain need out loud each week.
