title: Sun trine Saturn
---

You keep your word so consistently that people have stopped thanking you for it. Deadlines, budgets, the long middle stretch of projects where enthusiasm dies — you handle these without drama, and your life accumulates structure the way other lives accumulate clutter.

Reliability this natural has a shadow: it under-asks. You take the role one size smaller than you could carry, let flashier colleagues narrate work you quietly anchored, and confuse being indispensable with being recognized. Because endurance costs you little, you assume it is worth little, and you price yourself accordingly — in salary, in credit, in ambition.

Steadiness is your surplus; spend some. Name one responsibility you have outgrown and formally ask for the larger version of it this quarter, in writing.
