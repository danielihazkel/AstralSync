title: Mercury square Uranus
---

Your attention has a mutiny scheduled at all times. Mid-sentence, mid-task, mid-commitment, a more interesting thought seizes the controls, and you are suddenly arguing a position you adopted forty seconds ago with total conviction. Routine mental work feels physically difficult; you would rather rebuild the system than maintain it.

The productive side of this jangle is genuine breakthrough. Because your mind refuses to run on rails, you catch what the orderly thinkers miss, and pressure often triggers your best ideas — the deadline improvisation that becomes the standard method. The cost is wreckage: half-finished threads, contradicted yesterday-selves, collaborators who stopped relying on your focus, and contrarianism that fires on reflex rather than on evidence.

Externalize the mutiny — keep a capture list where every shiny tangent gets written down and exactly one gets promoted to action per week. The rest must earn their turn.
