title: Moon trine Pluto
---

Depth is your natural buoyancy. You move through intense emotional territory — grief, rage, other people's crises — the way a strong swimmer moves through surf, and you metabolize experiences that leave others permanently marked. People confess to you within an hour of meeting you, sensing correctly that nothing they say will frighten you off.

The ease deserves scrutiny on two counts. First, because intensity is comfortable, you can mistake it for intimacy — bonding through catharsis and heavy disclosure while the mundane tenderness of daily life goes underdeveloped. Second, your effortless read on people's insides gives you real influence you rarely audit: you steer moods and outcomes with a word, and it works so smoothly you can forget to ask whether you should.

The strength is a resilience that makes you a harbor in other people's storms. Check periodically that your closest bonds contain lightness too — and that your emotional power is being used with consent, not just skill.
