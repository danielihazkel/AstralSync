title: Jupiter square Uranus
---

Your appetite for growth and your appetite for freedom keep interrupting each other. Just as a plan starts compounding, a voltage builds in you — the urge to tear up the script, relocate, reinvent, escape the very structure that was working. You expand in jolts: sudden enrollments, abrupt exits, wagers placed at 2 a.m.

The productive side is real: this friction generates ideas steadier charts never touch, and your willingness to gamble on the untested occasionally pays spectacularly. You innovate because staying put is harder for you than leaping. The costly side is the pattern of premature detonation — quitting three chapters before the payoff, mistaking restlessness for insight, confusing any cage-rattling with progress.

Institute a personal waiting period: when the urge to blow something up arrives, give it seventy-two hours, and act only if the reasons survive.
