title: Mercury trine Mars
---

Words and will run on the same track for you, with no switching delay. Mercury trine Mars gives you a naturally quick tongue: you explain clearly under pressure, make decisions mid-sentence, and rarely feel the gap between knowing what you think and being able to say it. In a crisis, you are the person who can still narrate.

The hazard of a gift this smooth is that it never asks to be trained. Because you win most exchanges on raw fluency, you may skip the preparation, the evidence, the second draft — and then meet the one opponent who did the homework. Effortless can quietly become shallow.

Pick a subject where fluency alone will not carry you and study it properly; your delivery is already excellent, so every hour of substance you add compounds.
