title: Venus sextile Mars
---

Affection and initiative are on friendly terms in your chart, which is rarer than it sounds. Venus sextile Mars lets warmth carry a little engine: you can flirt without menace, compete without coldness, and turn a pleasant acquaintance into something real with one well-timed invitation. Nothing about your desire is at war with your kindness.

The catch with a friendly opportunity is that it waits to be taken. This aspect gives you the opening line, not the compulsion to use it — so the connection you felt at the party stays a feeling, the person you liked gets asked out by someone bolder, and your ease becomes a spectator sport. Chemistry unclaimed reads, from the outside, exactly like indifference.

The next time interest sparks, act within a day — send the message, name the plan — because for you the door is open but never opens itself.
