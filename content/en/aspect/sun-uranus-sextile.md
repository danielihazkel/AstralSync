title: Sun sextile Uranus
---

Originality sits within easy reach for you — an open door between who you are and what could be different. You improvise gracefully, absorb new tools quickly, and can update your self-image without the identity crisis that costs others years. People experience you as current, curious, lightly electric.

The catch with an open door is that you still have to walk through it. This aspect offers opportunities rather than compulsions: the odd invitation, the unconventional contact, the idea that arrives while you are doing something else. Left passive, it decays into being merely interested — a collector of possibilities who never files the patent, quirky in conversation but conventional on paper.

When a strange option flickers past this month, act on one deliberately; the sextile pays only on initiative.
