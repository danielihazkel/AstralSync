title: Moon sextile Neptune
---

A quiet channel runs between your emotions and something wider — call it imagination, empathy, or the sense that ordinary moments occasionally go translucent. When you engage it, you comfort people with uncanny accuracy, dreams and images speak usefully to you, and creative work draws directly on your inner tides.

But this channel is a well, not a spring: nothing rises unless you lower the bucket. Left unused, the sensitivity shows up only as vague melancholy, background yearning, or an evening dissolved into scrolling — longing without form. That's the friction: a gift subtle enough to spend your whole life almost using, while the drift quietly eats the hours that practice needed.

The strength is that small acts open it wide — twenty minutes of piano, a journal page, sitting with a grieving friend costs you little and yields much. Give the sensitivity one scheduled outlet a week, and treat that appointment as non-negotiable.
