title: Sun opposition Pluto
---

Intensity finds you through other people. You attract magnetic, controlling, or transformative figures — the partner whose moods rearrange your week, the rival who knows exactly where to press, the mentor whose approval costs too much — and each confrontation is really a negotiation over who holds the power you have not fully claimed as yours.

The gift of this polarity is X-ray honesty about relationships: once burned, you learn to see manipulation, projection, and desire with unusual clarity, and you can help others out of dynamics you have survived. The liability is the loop itself — alternating between surrendering your will to someone compelling and staging a total revolt, with little practice at the middle register where two strong people simply share a room.

When someone feels overwhelmingly powerful, list what power you handed them and take one piece back this week.
