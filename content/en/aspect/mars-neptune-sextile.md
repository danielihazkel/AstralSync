title: Mars sextile Neptune
---

You can put muscle behind a dream, provided you pick the dream up on purpose. When you point your effort at something with soul — a craft, a cause, a person who needs the help — the work gains a glide it never has when you grind for grinding's sake, and your instinct for timing and nuance quietly does half the pushing.

The strength is graceful effectiveness: you compete without cruelty, persuade without pressure, and read the undercurrents of a situation while others argue with its surface. The friction is that inspiration waits politely instead of seizing you, so your most meaningful projects tend to sit in the someday pile while ordinary demands eat the calendar, and the gap slowly registers as unexplained flatness.

Reserve one recurring block each week for the work you find meaningful, and defend it like a client meeting rather than a hobby.
