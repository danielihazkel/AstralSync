title: Moon opposition Pluto
---

Intensity, in your life, tends to arrive wearing someone else's face. You keep encountering partners, mothers, bosses who are consuming, controlling, or magnetically bottomless — while you play the reasonable one just trying to keep the peace. Or the poles trade: with certain people you become the deep one, the jealous one, the one whose need has gravity. Every close bond eventually poses the same question: who holds the power here?

Lived consciously, this opposition confers rare relational courage. You are not afraid of what's buried in people, so you can stay present through conversations and crises that scatter everyone else, and the intimacy on the far side of those reckonings is the real, tempered kind.

Unlived, it's serial entanglement with controlling figures, each escape leading to the next. When someone's hold on you feels overwhelming, look for the matching intensity you've disowned — reclaiming your own depth is what breaks the casting cycle.
