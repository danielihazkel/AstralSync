title: Saturn conjunct Uranus
---

The engineer and the anarchist share one desk in you. Discipline and disruption are fused, so your instinct is not to smash systems or to obey them but to rebuild them from inside — the colleague who rewrites the process, the founder who makes the radical idea pass an audit. Your work carries both rigor and surprise, a rare combination employers and collaborators come to rely on.

The tension never fully resolves. You can swing between over-structuring your life until it suffocates and torching routines the moment they deliver, and you judge yourself by two contradictory standards: not disciplined enough, not free enough. Under stress, the fusion jams — rigid about the wrong things, chaotic about the ones that needed rules.

Decide explicitly which parts of your life run on rails and which stay open country, and revisit that treaty every year.
