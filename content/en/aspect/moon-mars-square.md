title: Moon square Mars
---

There is a short fuse between your moods and your temper, and it was installed before you had a say. Hurt converts to heat almost instantly: a canceled plan, a dismissive tone, and you are spiky before you have located the softer feeling underneath the spike.

This wiring, mastered, becomes something rare — emotional courage. Because your feelings arrive armed, you do not flinch from confrontation the way gentler charts do, and once you learn to aim, you will say the necessary hard thing everyone else keeps swallowing. Unmastered, it is friendly fire: needs asserted as accusations, apologies issued on a schedule, loved ones learning to flinch first and listen second.

Anger is your dashboard light, not your steering wheel. When it flares, ask what got hurt — then bring *that* to the conversation instead.
