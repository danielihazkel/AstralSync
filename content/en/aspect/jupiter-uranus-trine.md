title: Jupiter trine Uranus
---

Change is kind to you. Upheavals that flatten other people's plans tend to reroute yours upward — the layoff that led to the better field, the cancelled plan that opened the luckier one. You trust the new almost by reflex, and the new usually rewards you for it, so your life advances through graceful pivots rather than grinding climbs.

The strength is a rare combination of optimism and flexibility: you ride waves you didn't predict and land upright. The taken-for-granted risk is exactly that ease. Because escape hatches keep appearing, you may never build anything that requires staying — skimming across interesting surfaces, subsidized by luck, while depth accrues to people who endured their plateaus.

Choose one commitment to keep through its boring middle stretch; your gift for reinvention means more when there's a finished thing behind it.
