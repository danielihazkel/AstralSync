title: Venus opposition Jupiter

This polarity plays out across the table from you. You tend to meet your own excess in other people — the partner who overspends while you budget, the friend whose grand promises you quietly discount, the lover whose enthusiasm feels wonderful and slightly untrustworthy. Whichever role you take, the other half of the seesaw is usually yours in disguise.

The gift is range: you understand both restraint and abundance from the inside, which makes you a generous host and a forgiving companion. The friction is the swing itself — lavishing too much on people, then feeling owed; expecting a beloved to supply the sense of plenty you won't claim directly; mistaking bigness of gesture for depth of bond.

Balance comes from owning both ends. When someone's extravagance or stinginess irritates you, ask where you've outsourced that quality — then practice a small dose of it yourself.
