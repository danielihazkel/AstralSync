title: Mars conjunct Jupiter
---

Your drive and your enthusiasm fire as one engine. When something catches your interest, you do not deliberate — you charge, and the sheer size of your effort often carries the day. People read you as lucky; mostly you are simply willing to swing harder and sooner than anyone else in the room.

The strength here is momentum that recruits others: your confidence is contagious, and half-built plans become real because you start before doubt arrives. The friction is scale. You promise three projects while finishing one, bet the surplus instead of banking it, and treat every open door as an obligation to walk through. Anger, too, arrives oversized — righteous, theatrical, and gone before the cleanup.

Keep the boldness and add a budget: before each new commitment, name the one existing project it will cost you, out loud.
