title: Venus opposition Saturn
---

You tend to meet Saturn in the person across from you. The pattern draws partners who are older, stricter, withholding, or simply serious — people whose approval feels like a prize with a rules committee. Opposite them you play the warm one, the giving one, and slowly start negotiating for affection that should have been freely offered.

The gift of the polarity is that you understand both sides of love's ledger: tenderness and terms, romance and reliability. Relationships that survive your scrutiny are genuinely durable, and you make an honest, steadfast partner yourself. The friction is projection — handing your own high standards to the other person, then experiencing them as rejection; or supplying all the discipline while they supply all the need.

Notice which half you've assigned away. When a partner seems cold, check first whether you've been auditioning instead of asking — then make the direct request.
