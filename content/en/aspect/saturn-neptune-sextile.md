title: Saturn sextile Neptune
---

Your pragmatism and your imagination are on speaking terms. You can bring a soft idea to a hard deadline without either one breaking: the charity gets an actual accounting system, the creative practice gets an actual calendar slot, the spiritual insight gets tested against Monday morning. People trust you with delicate things because you handle them with competent hands.

The quiet risk is that a channel this civil never gets used at volume. Nothing compels you to build the dream, so you can spend years doing sensible work with a faint watercolor wash of meaning over it — supportive of other people's visions, vaguely intending your own. The bridge between form and inspiration exists in you, but traffic must be sent across it on purpose.

Name the ideal you actually hold, then give it one concrete deliverable this month, however small.
