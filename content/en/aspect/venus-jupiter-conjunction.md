title: Venus conjunct Jupiter
---

Affection and appetite are welded together in you. When you love something — a person, a fabric, a city — you love it largely, and people feel expanded in your company. That warmth is real currency: hosts want you at the table, and strangers confide in you within minutes, because being near you feels like good luck.

The friction is that abundance blurs your edges. You overspend to match a mood, promise affection you meant only as enthusiasm, and let the pleasure of the moment stand in for actually choosing it. When everything is delightful, nothing gets vetted, and both your budget and your calendar pay for the drift.

Generosity works best with a container. Before the next grand gesture, ask whether you are giving from intention or from momentum — then give deliberately.
