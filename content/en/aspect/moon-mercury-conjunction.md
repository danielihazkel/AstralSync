title: Moon conjunct Mercury
---

Your feelings arrive with subtitles. The moment a mood forms, a sentence forms with it, and you often discover what you feel by hearing yourself say it. Journals, long phone calls, thinking out loud on a walk — these are not habits so much as digestion.

The strength is fluency: you can name an emotional weather system while others are still squinting at the sky, which makes you the friend people call when they cannot explain themselves. The friction is that fluency feels like accuracy. A mood, described vividly enough, starts to sound like a fact, and you can talk yourself into certainty about things you merely feel.

When a conclusion arrives on a bad night, write it down and re-read it after breakfast before you act on it.
