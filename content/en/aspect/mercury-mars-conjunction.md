title: Mercury conjunct Mars
---

Your thoughts arrive armed. With Mercury fused to Mars, you treat every question as a starting gun: you see the point, say the point, and are three sentences into the argument before slower speakers have cleared their throats. Wit, sarcasm, and diagnosis come naturally, and you can find the weak joint in any plan within a minute of hearing it.

The strength is decisiveness in language — you say hard things plainly, and people leave your meetings knowing exactly where they stand. The friction is that the same blade cuts allies: you argue to win when the moment called for listening, and your first draft of a hard truth often lands as an attack.

Before your next pointed reply, ask one clarifying question first — it costs three seconds and saves whole friendships.
