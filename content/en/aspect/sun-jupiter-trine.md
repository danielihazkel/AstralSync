title: Sun trine Jupiter
---

Things tend to work out for you, and you have mostly stopped noticing. Buoyancy is your baseline: recovery from disappointment comes fast, strangers warm to you quickly, and a plausible next chapter always seems to present itself before the current one fully closes.

Because this luck feels like weather rather than skill, you undervalue it — and you rarely push it. The trine's quiet failure mode is coasting: taking the good offer instead of the great one, staying genial where conviction is required, letting talent stand in for preparation because it usually covers the gap. Grace that is never spent on anything difficult slowly becomes mere pleasantness.

Your ease is capital, not scenery. Pick one goal that honestly seems slightly beyond you and spend some of that easy confidence there, deliberately.
