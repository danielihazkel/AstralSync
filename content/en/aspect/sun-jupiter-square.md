title: Sun square Jupiter
---

There is a standing argument between who you are today and how big you feel you are supposed to be. Enough is a word you understand in theory. In practice you take the second helping, the third project, the stretch goal on top of the stretch goal, because staying at your current size feels like a slow kind of failing.

That restlessness is an engine — you outwork steadier people simply because you cannot sit still inside a finished thing. The friction shows up as overcommitment: calendars that require luck to survive, promises made at the pitch and regretted at the deadline, a tendency to raise the stakes right when consolidating would serve you better.

The appetite is not the problem; the timing is. When you feel the urge to expand, finish one existing promise first and let that be the entry fee.
