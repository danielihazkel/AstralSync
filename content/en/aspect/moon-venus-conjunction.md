title: Moon conjunct Venus
---

Comfort and affection are fused in you: to feel safe is to make things pleasant, and making things pleasant is how you say *I love you*. You notice when the light is wrong, when a guest's cup is empty, when a sentence could be softened — and you fix it before anyone asks.

This makes you a natural host of other people's feelings, and rooms genuinely improve when you enter them. The cost runs on the same circuit: because harmony soothes you, conflict feels like personal bad weather, and you will trade your actual preference for a peaceful afternoon, then wonder why the peace feels thin.

Watch for the moment you agree just to end a discomfort. Once a day, state one small preference plainly — the room will survive it.
