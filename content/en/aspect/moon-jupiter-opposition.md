title: Moon opposition Jupiter
---

Need and abundance sit at opposite ends of your relationships, and other people usually hold one pole for you. You may play the modest homebody who attracts extravagant, promise-everything partners, or the lavish provider whose generosity quietly obligates everyone at the table. Either way, the seesaw is the tell: someone is over-giving, someone is over-needing, and the roles trade places under pressure.

The strength here is real: lived from both ends, this opposition makes you an unusually gracious host and an unusually gracious guest, someone who understands both hunger and plenty from the inside. Few people can make others feel welcome and expansive at once the way you can.

The friction is inflation by proxy — expecting others to supply the optimism, or resenting that your giving isn't matched. When you notice the seesaw tipping, name what you actually need out loud instead of giving more and waiting.
