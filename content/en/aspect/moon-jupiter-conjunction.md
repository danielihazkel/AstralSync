title: Moon conjunct Jupiter
---

Your moods and your optimism run on the same fuel line. When you feel something, you feel it generously — comfort wants to be shared, a good evening wants six more people at the table, and your instinct when someone hurts is to feed them, fund them, or promise them the world will improve. People relax around you because your care arrives without an invoice.

The fusion has a cost side. Because soothing and expanding are one gesture for you, excess becomes your comfort language: one more helping, one more purchase, one more commitment made warmly at ten p.m. that daylight can't honor. Your buoyancy can also steamroll smaller feelings — yours and others' — by rushing everything toward the bright side before it has been heard.

Before you promise care out loud, pause and check whether next week's version of you can deliver it.
