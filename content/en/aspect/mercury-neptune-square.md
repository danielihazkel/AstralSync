title: Mercury square Neptune
---

Precision and reverie fight for your microphone. You reach for the exact word and a fog rolls in; you commit to the plan and a daydream quietly rewrites it. Others sometimes leave conversations with you holding different versions of what was agreed, and you are genuinely surprised — you heard what you hoped, they heard what you implied.

The grind has a real yield. Because you cannot trust language to be automatic, you work at it, and writers with this square often become the most precise stylists of all — precision won from fog is sharper than precision that came free. Your imagination, once harnessed, gives arguments a resonance that pure logic cannot. Unharnessed, the cost is chronic: missed details, slippery commitments, and a reputation for being lovely but vague.

Close every important conversation by stating the concrete next step aloud and confirming it in writing — make the fog pass through one narrow, well-lit gate.
