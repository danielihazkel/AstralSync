title: Mars sextile Pluto
---

Depth of effort is on tap for you, though the tap must be opened deliberately. When you commit to a demanding overhaul — retraining, rebuilding finances, rehabilitating after injury — you find reserves under the reserves, and your persistence has a calm, unforced quality that outlasts flashier wills.

The strength is regenerative power without the melodrama: you can face hard truths about a situation, cut what is dead, and rebuild, all while staying on speaking terms with everyone involved. You read power dynamics accurately and rarely get played. The friction is that this depth stays theoretical until summoned; you can spend years in comfortable half-effort, distantly aware of an intensity you never invoice, and mildly envious of people who seem to burn hotter by default.

Pick one area of life due for honest demolition and rebuilding, and start it while conditions are calm — do not wait for a crisis to unlock the deeper gear.
