title: Mercury opposition Uranus
---

You keep meeting your own wildness in other people. Around conventional thinkers you become the disruptor, unable to resist poking the agreed-upon answer; around genuine radicals you turn oddly careful, the one asking how any of this would actually work. Which role you play depends on the room, which suggests neither role is the whole of you.

Lived well, this polarity makes you a rare bridge: you can translate the visionary's leap into terms the committee can approve, and smuggle rigor into the revolution. Your thinking is genuinely sharpened by electric, unpredictable interlocutors. The friction is projection — dismissing your own strangest ideas until someone else says them, or partnering with chaotic minds so you can keep playing the reasonable one, then resenting the chaos.

Notice which role you are performing in your next disagreement, and deliberately voice one sentence from the opposite side — the room usually needs it, and so do you.
