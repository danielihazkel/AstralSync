title: Sun conjunct Jupiter
---

Your sense of self and your sense of possibility fire as a single circuit. Where other people ask whether something can be done, you ask how big it could get, and your confidence tends to arrive well before your evidence does.

The gift is warmth at scale: you make rooms feel larger, back people before they have proven themselves, and treat setbacks as plot points rather than verdicts. The cost is inflation. You promise in an afternoon what takes a quarter to deliver, and your optimism can talk you past details that later demand payment with interest.

The growth you are built for is real, but it compounds only when something contains it. Before your next commitment, halve the promise and keep all of the enthusiasm.
