title: Venus square Uranus
---

Two hungers argue inside your attractions: one wants a hand to hold, the other wants the window open. You reach for closeness, then feel the walls move in; you claim your space, then miss the warmth within days. The result is a stop-start pattern — sudden beginnings, abrupt cool-offs, and partners who never quite know which of you is arriving.

The productive edge of this friction is honesty about desire. You cannot fake contentment, so your relationships stay negotiated rather than assumed, and the ones that last are genuinely chosen, over and over. You also bring real inventiveness — in affection, in style, in how a life together can be arranged.

The corrosive edge is manufacturing crises to feel free. When restlessness spikes, treat it as data, not a verdict: change something small inside the relationship — a routine, a room, a role — before concluding you must change the relationship itself.
