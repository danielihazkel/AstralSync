title: Mercury opposition Jupiter
---

You tend to meet your own big picture in other people. In one room you play the precise one, puncturing someone else's grand theory with inconvenient specifics; in another you are the enthusiast, irritated by a pedant who cannot see past the footnotes. The debate feels external, but both voices are yours.

Lived consciously, this polarity makes you an excellent translator between visionaries and detail people — you understand what each side is protecting. The strength is real dialectical skill: your ideas improve dramatically under challenge. The friction is projection and seesaw: you may outsource optimism to a partner and then resent their carelessness, or outsource rigor and feel chronically corrected, swinging between overclaiming and nitpicking without settling.

Next time you catch yourself arguing a hard line, argue the other side aloud for one minute — the position you are resisting usually holds your missing half.
