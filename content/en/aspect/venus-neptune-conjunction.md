title: Venus conjunct Neptune
---

Love and dream are fused in you, and it shows in everything you find beautiful. You don't just like a song or a face; you dissolve into it a little. This gives you rare gifts — an ear for the sublime, compassion that asks nothing back, and a devotion that can make an ordinary partner feel consecrated.

The same fusion blurs your vision. You fall for the person you can imagine rather than the one at the table, supply their missing virtues from your own reserves, and call the invoice romance. Disappointment then feels like betrayal, though the contract was signed with someone who never existed. Money can go soft-focus the same way.

Your tenderness is real; aim it at real people. Before deepening any bond, list three flaws you can name in them without flinching — if you can't find any, you're still looking at the projection.
