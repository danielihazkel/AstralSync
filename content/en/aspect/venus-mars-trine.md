title: Venus trine Mars
---

Attraction has never required effort from you, which is both the gift and the problem. Venus trine Mars blends charm and drive so smoothly that connection simply happens: you set people at ease, desire flows without drama, and your relationships tend to begin warmly and stay civil even when they end. Where others negotiate between love and appetite, you have a standing agreement.

Ease this reliable breeds coasting. Because affection comes cheap, you may never learn to fight for it — the grand gesture stays theoretical, the difficult conversation gets charmed around rather than had, and partners can feel pleasantly held but never truly pursued. What flows effortlessly also drifts effortlessly.

Choose one relationship this month and spend on it — the awkward apology, the extravagant effort, the risk your charm would normally excuse you from taking.
