title: Mars conjunct Saturn
---

Your accelerator and your brake are welded to the same pedal. Every surge of drive arrives with its own inspector: is this worth it, is it allowed, will it hold? The result is a compressed, deliberate force — you move less often than others, but when you move, the action is load-bearing.

The strength is endurance under conditions that break enthusiasts. You can drill the same skill for years, work through discouragement, and deliver on schedules that require grim consistency. The friction lives in the compression itself: anger that gets swallowed instead of spoken, ambitions postponed until conditions are perfect, and a body that stores the tension of every charge you cancelled. Frustration with your own slowness can curdle into harshness toward everyone else's.

Give the engine scheduled full-throttle time — a hard workout, a deadline sprint — so the pressure drives something instead of merely building.
