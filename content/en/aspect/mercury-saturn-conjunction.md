title: Mercury conjunct Saturn
---

Your thinking and your inner editor occupy the same desk. Nothing leaves your mouth or your keyboard without inspection, so what you say tends to be structured, defensible, and worth writing down. You learn slowly by classroom standards and permanently by every other standard.

The strength is authority: over time you become the person whose word settles the question, because you never claimed what you had not checked. The friction is the toll the editor charges. You rehearse remarks until the moment passes, delete good paragraphs for imaginary flaws, and read silence after you speak as a graded exam you failed. In young years this can masquerade as slowness when it is actually caution wearing a disguise.

Set a shipping rule the editor cannot veto — a fixed deadline, a word count, a send time — and let accuracy live in revision rather than in refusal.
