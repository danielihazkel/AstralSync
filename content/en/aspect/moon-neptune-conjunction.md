title: Moon conjunct Neptune
---

Your feelings and your imagination share one bloodstream. You don't observe other people's moods — you catch them, the way skin catches weather, and often you know what someone needs before they've admitted it to themselves. Music, water, and dim rooms restore you; harsh lighting and hard bargaining drain you at double rates.

The fusion makes you a gifted comforter and, at times, a missing person. Because the membrane between your emotions and everyone else's is thin, you can spend a week carrying a sadness that was never yours, or pour devotion into someone you've partly invented — nursing the person they could be while the actual one exhausts you. Your own needs go blurry in the fog of everyone else's.

The strength is compassion that reaches where words don't; the risk is dissolving into it. At the end of each day, ask one sorting question: which of today's feelings actually started in me?
