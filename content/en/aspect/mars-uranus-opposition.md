title: Mars opposition Uranus
---

Volatility keeps arriving from across the table. Your life fills with wildcards — the erratic partner, the colleague who upends the plan at the deadline, the friend whose emergencies become your sprints — and you play the steady one who absorbs the shocks. Or the poles swap: around especially controlled people, you become the disruptor, acting out an unpredictability you never claim when alone.

The strength is that you handle other people's lightning better than almost anyone; crisis partnerships are where your courage and reflexes look their best. The friction is the disowned charge: as long as excitement is something others inflict on you, you get the turbulence without the authorship, and your resentment of disruptors hides an envy of their freedom.

Notice which risks you only take when someone else forces the moment — then take one of them on your own initiative, at a time you choose.
