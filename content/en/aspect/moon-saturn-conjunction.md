title: Moon conjunct Saturn
---

Feeling and duty are welded together in you. Somewhere early you learned that needs are best handled quietly, so your care arrives as competence: showing up on time, fixing the thing, holding the household steady while everyone else emotes. People trust you in a crisis precisely because your feelings come pre-organized.

The welding cuts both ways. Because comfort and responsibility are one substance, you ration tenderness — for others, and especially for yourself — as if warmth were a budget line that could run out. You can sit in a room full of people who love you and still report, honestly, that asking for help never occurred to you. Loneliness becomes a habit that looks like maturity.

The strength is a loyalty that outlasts weather; the risk is a guardedness that outlasts its reason. Once a week, tell someone what you need before you've solved it yourself.
