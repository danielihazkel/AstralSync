title: Mars square Neptune
---

Your willpower and your imagination fight over the steering wheel. You launch toward a goal and the goal dissolves mid-pursuit — the job loses its point, the plan grows fog, and energy that was surging on Monday is unaccountable by Thursday. Anger is equally slippery: it rarely comes out straight, preferring sulks, martyrdom, or a mysteriously missed deadline.

The productive side of this friction is uncommon sensitivity in action. Once you learn to verify your inspirations, you can execute visions other people cannot even perceive, and your indirectness matures into genuine strategy and craft. The costly side is chasing mirages — glamorous projects, rescue missions, causes that evaporate on contact — and the escapist detours that follow each disillusionment.

Test every inspiring plan with one concrete, unglamorous first step within forty-eight hours; the real visions survive contact with logistics and the mirages do not.
