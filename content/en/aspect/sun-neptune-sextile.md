title: Sun sextile Neptune
---

You carry a quiet side door into the imaginal. Intuition supports your identity rather than swamping it: you read undercurrents accurately, soften hard situations with well-timed empathy, and can borrow from dreams without losing the plot. Creative and spiritual pursuits suit you, and kindness comes without the martyrdom surcharge that heavier Neptune contacts collect.

But a side door only matters if you use it. This aspect whispers instead of insisting, so its offers — the image worth painting, the hunch worth trusting, the person worth helping — are easy to file under someday. Unexercised, your sensitivity flattens into vague pleasantness, and the inspired work stays a private daydream you describe better than you deliver.

Give the intuition a container: one scheduled hour a week where imagination must produce something you can show another person.
