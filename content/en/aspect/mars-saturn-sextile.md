Discipline is available to you at a discount. When you choose to harness your energy — a training plan, a savings target, a long apprenticeship — the structure holds without the white-knuckle drama other people report. Effort and patience negotiate quietly in you, and deadlines tend to be met a day early rather than an hour late.

The strength is credibility: people learn that your commitments are load-tested, and responsibility flows toward you because of it. The friction is the low hum of this gift — because sustained effort never feels heroic, you can undervalue it, settle into competent maintenance, and let years pass without pointing the machinery at anything genuinely ambitious. Steady is only a virtue when it is steady toward something.

Set one goal that currently feels a size too big, and put your reliable engine on it before comfort talks you down.
