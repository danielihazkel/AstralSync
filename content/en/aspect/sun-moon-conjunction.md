title: Sun conjunct Moon
---

You were born near a new moon, with your sense of self and your emotional needs occupying the same patch of sky. What you want and what you need rarely argue: when you commit to a direction, your whole system comes along, which gives you unusual consistency and a self-possession people can lean on.

The friction is blindness. Because identity and instinct speak with one voice, you have no internal second opinion — your moods feel like facts, your preferences like principles. You can mistake comfort for correctness and defend a habit as though it were a conviction.

Other people are your missing counterweight. When someone challenges a choice you feel certain about, resist the reflex to double down; ask them to say more, and listen for the angle you cannot generate alone.
