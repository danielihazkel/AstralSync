title: Sun trine Moon
---

Your will and your feelings run in the same current. Deciding something rarely triggers an inner argument; you tend to want what you need, so your energy arrives undivided and people read you as steady, natural, at home in yourself.

The gift is real but easy to squander. Because self-agreement came free, you may underrate how rare it is — and underuse it. No inner friction means no inner alarm, so you can tolerate mediocre situations that a more conflicted person would have clawed their way out of years ago.

Your calm also makes you a stabilizer for others in crisis; let yourself be borrowed. But set one goal that genuinely stretches you, because comfort will never volunteer the suggestion.
