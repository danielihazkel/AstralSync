title: Mercury square Saturn
---

Speaking up costs you something it does not seem to cost others. An internal examiner audits every sentence before release — is it precise, is it defensible, will someone smarter object — and the audit often runs long enough that the conversation moves on without you. You may have a history of teachers or elders whose corrections still echo when you draft an email.

The grind pays real wages. Because you cannot get away with glibness, you build arguments that survive hostile review; your writing, when it finally ships, has a density people quote. That is the strength. The corrosion sets in when the examiner is mistaken for the truth — when you conclude you are slow rather than thorough, and start outsourcing your voice to more confident, less careful people.

Track your hit rate for a month: note each time you finally spoke and were right. Let the record, not the dread, set your threshold.
