title: Mercury sextile Mars
---

There is a persuasive punch sitting in your toolkit, and it only works when you pick it up. Mercury sextile Mars means your mind and your drive cooperate willingly: when you decide to make the case, the words sharpen, the pace quickens, and you argue with an energy that surprises people who know your usual easygoing register.

Because the gift is optional rather than compulsive, you rarely wound anyone by accident — a real advantage. The trade-off is that opportunity left idle simply expires. You draft the bold email and soften it, rehearse the negotiation and then accept the first offer, hold the winning point until the meeting ends.

Once a week, pick one conversation you would normally coast through and deliberately press your case — the muscle is there, but only reps make it reliable.
