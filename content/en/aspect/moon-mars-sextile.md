title: Moon sextile Mars
---

Your anger and your needs are on speaking terms — a quieter blessing than it sounds. When something bothers you, you can usually say so at volume two instead of waiting for volume nine, and your energy rises to meet what you care about without burning the furniture on the way.

Used deliberately, this is emotional traction: you convert irritation into repairs, restlessness into exercise, protectiveness into actually showing up. But sextiles run on invitation, and unclaimed, this one idles — you know you *could* raise the issue, so it never feels urgent, and mild frustrations accumulate into a fatigue you do not connect to its source.

Keep the channel warm through use. When something small bothers you, say it within a day, precisely because it is small.
