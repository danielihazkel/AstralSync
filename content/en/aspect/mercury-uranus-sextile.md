title: Mercury sextile Uranus
---

You have a side door to the unconventional. Fresh angles come to you readily when you go looking — the workaround nobody tried, the question that reframes the meeting — and unlike harsher wirings, your originality arrives without the static; people find your odd ideas approachable rather than alarming.

But the door only opens if you walk to it. This ease can idle as mild cleverness: you notice the better way, mention it once, and let it die politely when nobody bites. The gift needs deliberate provocation — new inputs, strange collaborators, problems outside your lane — or it settles into producing witty comments about a status quo you never actually disturb. The mild friction is follow-through: novelty excites you more than implementation does.

Book a standing appointment with the unfamiliar — a monthly deep dive into a field you know nothing about — and bring one stolen idea back to your real work each time.
