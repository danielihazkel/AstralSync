title: Sun square Mars
---

Your will and your temper answer to different masters. You know exactly where you are going, but the energy meant to carry you there keeps firing at the wrong moments — impatience with slow colleagues, irritation at red tape, a sharp word that costs you a week of repair.

Handled well, this square is a career. The friction between purpose and drive generates more raw horsepower than any easy aspect, and people who master it often outwork everyone in the room once they find a target worthy of the heat. Handled badly, it is a pattern of self-sabotage: picking fights on the way to victories, quitting in anger inches from the finish line.

Learn your fuse length. When irritation spikes, buy sixty seconds before responding — nearly everything this aspect breaks gets broken inside that first minute.
