title: Moon trine Venus
---

Being pleasant costs you nothing, which is rarer than it sounds. Warmth, tact, and a feel for what makes a moment agreeable come to you the way grammar comes to a native speaker — you do not construct charm, you simply have it switched on.

People trust you quickly and forgive you easily, and you can steady a fraying group with nothing but tone. The hazard of the gift is that it works too well: harmony this cheap makes hard conversations look optional, and you can glide past real problems for years on likability, leaving the difficult truths to whoever loves you less gracefully.

Spend some of the goodwill you generate. Use your next easy moment with someone to raise the one thing you have been smoothing over.
