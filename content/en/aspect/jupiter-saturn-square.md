title: Jupiter square Saturn
---

Your accelerator and your brake are wired to the same pedal. Part of you sees the bigger version of your life and wants it now; another part audits the plan and finds the funding, the timing, or your credentials lacking. The result is a stop-go rhythm — surges of expansion followed by contractions that feel like penalties.

The grind is productive when you let it be. Few people can both dream and stress-test the dream, and ventures that survive your internal audit tend to survive the market too. The corrosion sets in when the phases fight instead of alternate: over-promising in the optimistic swing, then over-correcting into austerity, so progress happens in lurches you then blame on luck.

Watch for the moment you cancel a commitment right after making it — pause there, and renegotiate the size of the step instead of abandoning the direction.
