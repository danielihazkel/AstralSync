title: Mars sextile Uranus
---

You have easy access to the unconventional move, though you have to reach for it. When you deliberately break your own routine — a new tool, a reversed sequence, an odd training method — energy floods back into work that had gone flat, and your experiments have a knack for landing usefully rather than just strangely.

The strength is adaptability without chaos: you can absorb sudden change, pivot fast, and stay steady while others scramble, all without needing to blow anything up to feel alive. The friction is dormancy. Because novelty is optional for you, whole seasons can pass on autopilot, and the inventive streak that would distinguish your work sits unused while you execute other people's standard playbooks perfectly well.

When a task starts feeling mechanical, treat that as a signal, not a mood — change one variable in your approach that same day.
