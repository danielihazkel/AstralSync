title: Saturn square Neptune
---

Duty and dream grind against each other in you, and neither will surrender. The responsible life you maintain can feel like a stage set — convincing, joyless, faintly unreal — while the meaningful life you imagine dissolves whenever you reach for it with a plan. You oscillate: months of grim structure, then a slide into drift, doubt, or beautiful procrastination, then guilt marches you back to the desk.

Worked through, this square builds something rare: disillusionment literacy. You learn to strip the glamour off institutions, gurus, and your own excuses, and what survives that stripping — the practice you keep, the commitment that still means something — is genuinely yours. Unworked, it yields chronic low-grade despair dressed as realism, or escapism dressed as sensitivity.

When meaninglessness fogs in, do not renegotiate your whole life; do the next small task and reassess in daylight.
