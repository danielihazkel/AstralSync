title: Moon conjunct Mars
---

In you, feeling and doing share a nervous system. Hurt becomes a reply already sent; worry becomes a task already started; someone threatens a person you love and you are standing up before you have decided to stand. Emotion does not visit you — it deploys.

That speed is a real asset: you are the one who acts in emergencies, defends the absent, and never leaves people guessing where they stand with you. The bill arrives as reactions that outrun understanding — you can be mid-argument before you know what you are actually upset about, fighting for territory when what you needed was comfort.

Build one beat of delay into the circuit. When heat rises, name the feeling out loud before doing anything with it; action taken after naming is twice as accurate.
