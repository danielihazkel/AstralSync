title: Jupiter trine Pluto
---

Influence accrues to you without visible strain. You read power structures fluently — who decides, what they fear, where the leverage hides — and your ambitions tend to be funded, promoted, and protected by people who sense you'll multiply whatever they invest. Recovery is a talent too: setbacks that end other careers become your origin stories.

The strength is depth married to optimism; you transform situations rather than merely occupying them, and usually with clean hands. The taken-for-granted risk is unexamined power. Because doors open easily, you can stop noticing you're carrying keys others lack, mistaking structural advantage for pure merit — or you coast, spending accumulated leverage on comfortable wins instead of the consequential work you're built for.

Once a year, aim your influence at something that doesn't benefit you directly, and track it as seriously as your own ventures.
