title: Venus sextile Uranus
---

You carry an easy openness to the unusual in love and taste. Unconventional people don't alarm you; they interest you, and you can befriend across every line — age, culture, subculture — without strain. Given room, your relationships breathe well: you grant freedom naturally and rarely mistake possession for devotion.

The sextile's fine print is that this originality stays theoretical until you act on it. It's entirely possible to hold delightfully odd tastes and still date the same safe profile, decorate in beige, and keep your most interesting affections at the acquaintance level. The talent for fresh connection needs deliberate outings or it idles.

Your strength is friendship woven into attraction; your weakness is letting novelty stay hypothetical. Say yes to one invitation this month that your predictable self would decline — the aspect only pays out on experiments actually run.
