title: Mercury conjunct Pluto
---

Your attention does not skim; it drills. A casual question from you is rarely casual — you are tracking the discrepancy in someone's story, the motive under the mission statement, the one loose thread in a hundred-page document. When you speak, people feel addressed at a depth ordinary conversation does not reach.

The strength is investigative power fused with persuasion: you find what is buried and you can make others care that you found it. The friction is that the drill has no off switch. You interrogate small talk, read menace into neutral emails, and hold conversational ground like territory — needing not just to be right but for the other person to concede it. Intensity that illuminates can also exhaust the room.

Before pressing a point past the second exchange, ask yourself what winning it actually buys. Save the drill for questions that deserve excavation.
