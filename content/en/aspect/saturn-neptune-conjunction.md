title: Saturn conjunct Neptune
---

You are built to pour concrete around a dream. Structure and imagination run as one process in you, so your ideals refuse to stay abstract — they demand budgets, deadlines, and load tests — and your practical work refuses to stay soulless. At your best you are the one who ships the vision: the composer who finishes, the founder whose mission statement is also the operating manual.

The fusion has a damp side. Doubt seeps into your dreams and dreaminess into your duties: you can undermine inspired projects with premature realism, or quietly resent obligations for lacking magic. There are seasons when everything solid feels illusory and everything beautiful feels unaffordable, and fatigue masquerades as pragmatism.

Keep one project on the bench at all times that is both idealistic and scheduled, and let its progress arbitrate between the skeptic and the dreamer.
