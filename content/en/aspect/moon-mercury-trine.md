title: Moon trine Mercury
---

Talking about feelings costs you almost nothing. Mood flows into language without a toll booth, so you can narrate your inner weather in real time — a gift teachers, counselors, and friends recognize quickly and come to rely on.

That fluency comforts everyone, including you, and there is the catch: a feeling smoothly described can be a feeling quietly skipped. Because the words arrive so fast, you sometimes trade the experience for the summary, filing grief or joy under a tidy label before it has finished happening. People may also mistake your articulateness for resolution and stop checking on you.

The strength needs occasional silence to stay honest. Once in a while, let a feeling stay unnamed for a full day and notice what it does when nobody is narrating it.
