title: Sun opposition Jupiter
---

You meet your own bigness through other people. An audience makes you expand: in front of the right listeners you become more generous, more visionary, and noticeably more committed than you were an hour earlier, alone, reading the same plan.

That responsiveness is a genuine social gift — you rise to occasions, and groups borrow courage from your scale. The seesaw tips when the audience starts writing your promises for you. You say yes to match the room's excitement, size your ambitions to others' expectations, and later meet the gap between what was announced and what you privately intended, usually with the same people watching.

The corrective is a delay between applause and commitment. When a room loves your idea, thank it, leave, and confirm the promise only after you have re-read it in private the next morning.
