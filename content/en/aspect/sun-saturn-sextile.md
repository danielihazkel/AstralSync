title: Sun sextile Saturn
---

Discipline is available to you on unusually friendly terms. Structure does not feel like punishment; routines hold once you set them, elders and institutions tend to take you seriously, and you can defer gratification without the theatrical suffering it costs most people.

Because the machinery is cooperative rather than demanding, it waits to be switched on. That is the quiet risk: you are capable of ten-year projects but drift through two-year moods, keeping your considerable steadiness in reserve for a serious occasion that never formally announces itself. Willing hands, no assignment.

The sextile rewards deliberate enrollment — a craft, a body of work, a qualification with a long arc. Choose one commitment measured in years, not weeks, and give your patience something worthy of its size.
