title: Saturn sextile Pluto
---

Depth and discipline cooperate in you without melodrama. You can face hard truths about work, money, and power and respond with process rather than panic: quietly paying down what others deny, restructuring before the crisis, holding authority without needing it noticed. In a group, you are the one who can be told the real numbers.

The soft spot in this steadiness is under-ambition. Because the aspect offers capacity rather than compulsion, you may apply your considerable depth to maintenance — keeping things solvent, stable, and small — while transformations you could easily lead go unattempted. Prudence can become its own comfort zone, and your talent for handling power can atrophy into merely avoiding its misuse.

Identify one structure in your life that deserves demolition rather than repair, and schedule the rebuild deliberately; this aspect finishes whatever you dare to start.
