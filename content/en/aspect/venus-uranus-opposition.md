title: Venus opposition Uranus
---

The lightning tends to arrive in other people. You draw the brilliant and unpredictable — partners who electrify a room and cancel at the last minute, lovers whose independence you admire right up until it stings. Opposite them you often play the steady one, holding the calendar while they hold the excitement, wondering why you keep casting this film.

The polarity's gift is genuine tolerance: you can love difference without needing to fix it, and your relationships escape the scripts that suffocate more conventional pairs. The friction is disowned restlessness — outsourcing your own hunger for freedom to a volatile partner, then resenting the turbulence, or bolting from stable love the moment it's finally offered.

The pattern steadies when both charges live in you. When a partner's unreliability flares, ask what change you've been refusing yourself lately — and make one small, honest bid for your own freedom instead.
