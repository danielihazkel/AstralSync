title: Sun conjunct Mercury
---

Your mind and your identity share a bloodstream — a fusion so common (Mercury never strays far from the Sun) that its effects hide in plain sight. You think in the first person: opinions are not things you hold, they are things you are, and articulating an idea feels like presenting yourself.

This makes you a vivid communicator with real conviction; people rarely wonder where you stand. The friction is that disagreement lands as personal insult, and you can defend a position long past its expiry date because retreating feels like self-erasure. Listening suffers when every conversation doubles as a performance of who you are.

Practice one deliberate separation: say "I currently think" instead of "I am someone who thinks." The gap that phrase opens is where your mind gets to change.
