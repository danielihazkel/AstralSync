title: Moon trine Jupiter
---

Emotional buoyancy is your factory setting. Moods right themselves, strangers confide in you within minutes, and your instinct for hospitality — the right food, the right word, the extra chair — operates without effort. In a crisis you're the person whose steadiness has warmth in it, and homes you make tend to feel larger than their square footage.

The risk of anything effortless is that you stop noticing it's a resource. Because recovery has always arrived, you can skate over grief instead of metabolizing it, wave off real problems with reflexive silver linings, and assume the reservoir refills forever without maintenance. Others may lean on your abundance harder than you register, and you rarely think to ask for care in return.

Generosity given automatically can also go unexamined — who it serves, what it avoids. Once a season, check what you're smoothing over with optimism, and let one trusted person return the caretaking.
