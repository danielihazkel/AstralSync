title: Mars square Pluto
---

Your will meets resistance and doubles, meets it again and doubles again. Life keeps arranging contests — the territorial manager, the rigged process, the rival who will not yield — and something in you cannot leave a power struggle unanswered. Losing is not merely disappointing; it feels existential, which is exactly what makes the fights so hard to exit.

Forged properly, this square produces formidable people: survivors of real ordeals, workers of last resort, the one who finishes the project everyone else abandoned at the crater stage. The danger is the scorched-earth setting — escalations that cost more than the prize, grudges nursed into strategy, and the slow discovery that opponents were partly mirrors, fighting the ruthlessness you had not admitted was yours.

In your next conflict, write down what winning is actually worth in hours and relationships — and hold yourself to spending not one unit more.
