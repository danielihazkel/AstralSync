title: Jupiter sextile Uranus
---

Opportunity reaches you through the side door. New technologies, unusual people, and left-field ideas keep drifting into range, and when you engage them, they tend to upgrade your prospects — a chance conversation becomes a collaboration, a hobby becomes a credential. Your mind is genuinely open, so luck has more surface area to land on.

The strength is adaptability without chaos: you can absorb change and profit from it while others are still objecting to it. The friction is passivity. This aspect supplies encounters, not outcomes, and the interesting stranger or odd invitation does nothing if you file it under "someday." Curiosity that never converts to action becomes a scrapbook of near-misses.

When something unconventional catches your attention this month, follow it for one concrete step — a message sent, a prototype built — within the week.
