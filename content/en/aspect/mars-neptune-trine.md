title: Mars trine Neptune
---

Your effort has an easy poetry to it. Whatever you do carries atmosphere — the meal is presented, the code is elegant, the workout flows like choreography — and you pursue what you want with such soft-footed timing that people rarely register being pursued. Helping others energizes rather than depletes you, and your intuition routinely spares you fights that never needed to happen.

The strength is action aligned with meaning as a default setting: you seldom waste force on hollow prizes. The taken-for-granted risk is drift. Because grace papers over the absence of a plan, you can float from one pleasant, vaguely purposeful chapter to the next and only notice at forty that the current, not you, chose the destinations. Comfort with the indirect route can also become an allergy to any direct confrontation at all.

Once a quarter, name your heading in plain words — what you are building and by when — so the flow serves a course you actually set.
