title: Jupiter opposition Saturn
---

You tend to meet this polarity in other people. When you're in an expansive mood, someone across the table plays the auditor — a cautious partner, a skeptical boss, a lender reading fine print. When you're the careful one, you attract big talkers whose confidence both irritates and tempts you. The seesaw is really internal: optimism and prudence, each waiting for the other to blink.

Held well, the opposition makes you a superb negotiator between vision and viability; you can represent either side because you contain both. Held badly, it outsources half your judgment — you let the doubter veto your dreams or let the promoter spend your reserves, then resent whoever played the role you disowned.

Next time someone's caution or confidence provokes you, ask what that voice is saying on your own behalf before you argue with it.
