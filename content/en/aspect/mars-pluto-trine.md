title: Mars trine Pluto
---

Power moves through you without grinding its gears. You apply sustained, concentrated force the way others apply attention — steadily, precisely, without visible strain — and difficult undertakings that intimidate most people simply read to you as long. Your presence settles rooms; conflicts tend to resolve before they fully form, because few care to test you.

The strength is quiet, immense capacity: whatever you decide to transform, from a business to your own habits, tends to get transformed. The taken-for-granted risk is unexamined dominance. Influence this frictionless invites you to steer people and outcomes without noticing you are steering, and because you rarely face real resistance, you can go years without discovering where your will is actually wrong. Ease can also make you underestimate what struggle costs everyone else.

Invite pushback on one live decision this month — ask someone unafraid of you to argue the other side, and listen to the end.
