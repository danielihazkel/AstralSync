title: Mercury square Pluto
---

Your mind grips. A stray remark from Tuesday is still under interrogation on Friday; a question without an answer becomes a splinter you cannot stop touching. You do not really browse information — you pursue it, and you tend to assume the important part is being withheld.

This grind forges formidable strengths. You out-research everyone, catch the buried clause, and argue with a force that reorganizes rooms; obsession, pointed at a worthy problem, is simply called dedication. The corrosive side is the suspicion tax: reading betrayal into brevity, turning discussions into contests of will, and spending nights prosecuting conversations that the other party forgot by dinner. The mind that will not let go also cannot rest.

Give each obsession a container: one page where the suspicion gets written as a testable claim, with the evidence for and against. What survives the page deserves pursuit; what does not, you release on purpose.
