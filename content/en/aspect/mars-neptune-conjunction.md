title: Mars conjunct Neptune
---

Your drive and your imagination share one bloodstream. You cannot push for something you do not believe in — money and status alone leave the engine cold — but hand you a cause, a craft, or a performance and you summon stamina that surprises everyone, yourself included. Your pursuit style is tidal rather than frontal: you arrive at goals sideways, on timing and atmosphere, and opponents rarely see the approach.

The strength is inspired, almost artistic action, plus real gifts for service and for moving a crowd. The friction is leakage. When the meaning drains out of a task, so does your energy — sometimes as fatigue, sometimes as escapism — and your dislike of open combat can slide into evasions that confuse people who needed a plain yes or no.

Before committing your effort anywhere, name what it serves in one sentence; if you cannot, expect the fuel to run out early.
