title: Jupiter square Neptune
---

Your hopes have a habit of outrunning your evidence. A plan starts at reasonable size, then the fog machine kicks in: this job becomes the calling, this person becomes the answer, this idea becomes the thing that changes everything. When reality invoices the difference, the deflation feels less like a correction than a small bereavement.

The productive side of this friction is that you keep asking the biggest questions — what it all means, who it serves, whether more is actually better — and your disillusionments, metabolized honestly, refine faith into something sturdier than optimism. The costly side is the cycle itself: inflation, escape, disappointment, and the temptation to soothe the crash with the next glittering promise or a convenient blur.

When something new feels like salvation, write down what you concretely expect from it in ninety days, and check the note when the date comes.
