title: Saturn sextile Uranus
---

You have a working handshake between order and innovation. New methods do not threaten your structures; they upgrade them, and you can introduce change at a pace institutions actually absorb. In practice this looks like being the person who modernizes the workflow without breaking it, or who keeps a stable life that still has room for the strange and new.

Because the handshake is quiet, it is easy to leave unused. Nothing forces you to modernize, so you can settle into competent maintenance for years — mildly interested in the new tool, the certification, the restructure — while the opportunity to lead change passes to louder colleagues. The gift only compounds when exercised deliberately.

Once a quarter, pick one proven routine and one experiment, and formally graft the experiment onto the routine before the impulse fades.
