title: Sun sextile Pluto
---

Depth is available to you on friendly terms. You can look at what others flinch from — motives, money, mortality, the unsaid thing in the room — without being swallowed by it, and people confide in you because your attention feels sturdy. Reinvention comes as an option you can exercise rather than a crisis that exercises you.

The limitation is that options politely offered are politely declined. This aspect never forces the descent, so you can skim along using your psychological acuity for commentary instead of change — diagnosing everyone's patterns, including your own, while altering none of them. Insight without stakes curdles into cleverness, and your considerable power stays theoretical.

Pick one entrenched habit or stalled ambition and commit to transforming it end-to-end; the sextile funds the journey, but only after you book it.
