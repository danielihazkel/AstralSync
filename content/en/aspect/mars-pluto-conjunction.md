title: Mars conjunct Pluto
---

Your will has a second, deeper gear that most people never install. When you truly decide on something, the pursuit is total: obstacles get studied rather than cursed, setbacks feed the resolve, and you keep advancing long after any reasonable person would have negotiated a surrender. People sense the reactor even when you are being pleasant, and some are magnetized while others quietly step back.

The strength is transformative stamina — you can rebuild a body, a career, or yourself from the ground up on sheer sustained intent. The friction is proportionality. Small disputes receive siege engines, control creeps into places that needed trust, and buried anger, denied ordinary exits, surfaces as intensity that scorches allies along with obstacles.

Match your force to the actual stakes: before engaging, ask whether this fight deserves the whole reactor or a fraction of one percent.
