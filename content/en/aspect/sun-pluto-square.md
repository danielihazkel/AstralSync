title: Sun square Pluto
---

Somewhere early you learned that power decides things, and you have been negotiating with that lesson ever since. Your will collides with forces — bosses, institutions, your own compulsions — that seem determined to remake you, and your instinct is to meet pressure with more pressure. Life keeps scheduling confrontations you did not put on the calendar.

The grind produces genuine formidability. You develop stamina, strategic patience, and a nose for hidden agendas that makes you nearly impossible to manipulate; what you rebuild after each demolition is stronger than the original. The danger is becoming the thing you fought: controlling, secretive, scorched-earth in conflicts that deserved a shrug, or locked in power struggles that are really old wounds wearing new faces.

In your next standoff, ask what you are actually afraid of losing — then fight for that, not for the win.
