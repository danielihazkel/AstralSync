title: Venus square Neptune
---

Your longing and your eyesight are in a running argument. Some part of you wants love to be transcendent — rescue, redemption, the film score swelling — and it will quietly retouch whoever is available until they fit the poster. Then reality files its correction, and the crash lands on you as disillusionment you swear you'll never repeat, until the next luminous stranger.

The grind is productive because it forces discernment the hard way. Every idealization that collapses teaches you to separate the glow from the person, and few charts emerge with a subtler eye for beauty or a deeper well of compassion. Your art, if you make any, is fed directly by this ache.

The corrosive form is martyrdom — giving to be needed, staying to finish the fantasy. Watch for the phrase "they'd be perfect if" in your own head; when it appears, evaluate what's actually in front of you, not the potential.
