title: Sun trine Mars
---

Effort comes naturally to you — so naturally you may not recognize it as a trait. Confidence, physical energy, and the nerve to act arrive without internal negotiation, and you quietly assume everyone's body says yes this easily. It does not.

The gift is durable vitality: you can sustain output that exhausts other people, and your assertiveness rarely curdles into aggression because nothing inside you is fighting itself. The friction is complacency. Winning small has always been easy, so you may never have tested where your actual ceiling sits — the trine hands you a sports car and no reason to leave the neighborhood.

Seek competition slightly above your weight class. You were built for a harder game than the one you have been playing.
