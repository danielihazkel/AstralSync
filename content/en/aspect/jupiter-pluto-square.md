title: Jupiter square Pluto
---

Enough is a word your ambition struggles to pronounce. Each summit reveals the next one, and the drive that got you here immediately re-arms for a bigger campaign. You push, consolidate, push again — and somewhere in the cycle, growth stops being expansion and starts being conquest.

The productive side of this grind is force: you achieve at scales that astonish steadier temperaments, and your refusal to accept limits has broken ceilings that deserved breaking. Pressure turns you strategic where it turns others brittle. The corrosive side is escalation — doubling down when folding is wiser, treating rivals as enemies, letting ends-justify-means reasoning creep into places you'd once have called off-limits. Burnout and blowback are this square's tuition.

Audit your methods quarterly, not just your results: list what this quarter's wins cost, and name one tactic you won't use again.
