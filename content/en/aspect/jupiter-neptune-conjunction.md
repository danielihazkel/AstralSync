title: Jupiter conjunct Neptune
---

Faith and imagination are welded together in you, and both run large. You don't just hope; you envision — whole futures, causes, redemptions — and your generosity flows toward anything that carries that shimmer. People feel enlarged in your presence, because you see the best version of them and lend against it.

The strength is real vision: you can inspire, comfort, and give meaning to efforts that would otherwise be mere transactions. The friction is that your enthusiasm and your discernment use the same eyes. A cause, a teacher, or an investment that glows with promise may simply be well lit, and you tend to notice the difference only after the check clears. Boundless giving can quietly become boundary-less giving.

Before committing money, time, or belief to anything that feels destined, ask one skeptical friend to read the fine print with you.
