title: Venus trine Saturn
---

Loyalty runs through you like grain through wood. Commitment doesn't frighten you; it organizes you. You keep the people you choose, handle money with unhurried sense, and prefer one proven pleasure to five promising ones. Partners relax around your steadiness, and your taste — restrained, well-made, quietly expensive-looking — rarely embarrasses you in hindsight.

The trap in all this ease is mistaking stability for aliveness. Because devotion costs you so little effort, you may never check whether the arrangement still contains desire, or whether you settled early because settling was simple. Comfortable bonds can calcify politely for years while both people call it contentment.

Your strength is love that holds weight; your risk is furniture that no one sits in anymore. Once a season, ask your steadiest relationship for something new — a trip, a truth, a shared project — and see what wakes up.
