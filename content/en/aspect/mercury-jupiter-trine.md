title: Mercury trine Jupiter
---

Ideas move through you without turbulence. You read fast, retain the gist, and reframe other people's tangled problems in a sentence or two. In groups you often become the explainer — the one who can make the plan sound coherent and the setback sound survivable — and this fluency wins you trust early.

Because it all comes so easily, the risk is that you never test it. Effortless articulacy can coast for years on first drafts and half-read sources; the trine will keep making you sound right whether or not you are. The strength is genuine perspective; the quiet friction is that fluency and understanding start to drift apart, and you may be the last to notice the gap.

Regularly explain your reasoning to someone qualified to push back, and treat the first hard question you cannot answer as the syllabus.
