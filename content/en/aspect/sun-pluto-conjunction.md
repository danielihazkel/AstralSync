title: Sun conjunct Pluto
---

You do not do anything lightly, including exist. Identity and intensity are welded together in you: every project becomes an excavation, every relationship a depth sounding, every setback a death you are expected to survive and metabolize. People feel the gravity before you say a word, and they respond with fascination, deference, or flight.

The strength is regenerative force — you can dismantle yourself to the studs and rebuild, more than once, and your focus can carry work through conditions that break others. The shadow is control: because you have felt how much power moves under the surface, you grip situations and people tighter than you admit, and you may confuse being influential with being safe.

Notice where you are managing outcomes covertly this week, and release one of them out loud.
