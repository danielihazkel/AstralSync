title: Sun trine Pluto
---

Power runs through you like current through a well-grounded wire. You concentrate, persist, and regenerate without drama: crises that flatten others seem to reorganize you instead, and your influence accumulates quietly because you rarely need to force anything. People sense reserves in you and follow accordingly.

The risk of strength this frictionless is that you never learn its edges. Because doors open and resistance melts, you can underestimate your own impact — steamrolling gently, deciding for people who never voted, mistaking your ease with power for everyone's comfort with it. And since transformation comes cheap to you, you may hoard it, cycling through private reinventions while committing deeply to nothing outside yourself.

Attach your endurance to a purpose that outlives your interest in it, and invite someone to tell you honestly how your force lands.
