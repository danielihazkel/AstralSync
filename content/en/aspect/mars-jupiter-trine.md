title: Mars trine Jupiter
---

Action comes easily to you and usually goes well. Effort and opportunity feed each other in a loop most people have to build by hand: you act, doors open, the wins make you bolder, and boldness finds more doors. Physical vitality tends to run generous, and your anger, when it comes, burns clean and finishes fast.

The strength is honest confidence — you expect effort to pay, so you volunteer for the hard part and often carry it. The taken-for-granted risk is that ease breeds shallowness. Because a decent result arrives on the first push, you rarely stay for the second and third pushes that turn talent into mastery, and you can mistake a lucky streak for a strategy.

Choose one arena where a decent result is no longer enough, and deliberately practice past the point where it stops being easy.
