title: Moon trine Uranus
---

Emotional flexibility is native to you. Moods pass through without lodging, change of any kind — new city, new roommate, upended plan — costs you less than it costs most people, and your intuition delivers quick, accurate reads that arrive as certainty rather than analysis. Friends bring you their crises because you respond with fresh air instead of alarm.

The effortless part is exactly what to watch. Because feelings release so easily, you can mistake moving on for working through: griefs get filed as "handled" while they're merely postponed, and your talent for detachment lets you exit situations a beat before they would have deepened you. People can experience your lightness as never fully landing anywhere — or with anyone.

The strength is resilience without rigidity; the risk is a life of interesting weather and no climate. Choose one relationship or place and let it bore you occasionally on purpose — depth is what your ease will never hand you for free.
