title: Mars sextile Jupiter
---

Effort and optimism cooperate in you, but on request rather than by default. When you decide to pursue something, energy shows up cheerfully and in quantity: you train harder without resenting it, ask for bigger opportunities without choking, and recover from setbacks with your appetite intact.

The strength is that risk does not frighten you into paralysis — you can size a bold move and take it with a steady pulse. The friction is that nothing forces the move. Because this ease never nags, you can coast for months on potential, vaguely confident that the big push will happen someday. Opportunities favor you, but they still wait for a first step you have not taken.

Pick one ambition you have been saving for later and schedule its opening move this week — the aspect pays only after you initiate.
