title: Saturn trine Neptune
---

Making dreams practical comes naturally to you. Where others split into cynics and fantasists, you quietly do both jobs: the meditation practice that survives busy seasons, the creative discipline that never feels like discipline, the career choice that pays rent and still means something. Your idealism has good boots, and people bring you their visions to be sanity-checked without being crushed.

The trap in this ease is modest scale. Because integrating spirit and structure costs you so little, you may never notice how much more the combination could carry — settling for a meaningful-enough life administered smoothly, your compassion budgeted, your imagination housebroken. Effortless synthesis can also make you patient with situations that deserve holy dissatisfaction.

Ask yourself what you would build if the dream were allowed to be inconveniently large, then draft its first real step this week.
