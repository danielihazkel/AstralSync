title: Sun sextile Jupiter
---

Confidence and opportunity are on friendly terms in your chart. Doors tend to open when you knock, mentors respond when you write, and your optimism reads as credible rather than salesy — people extend you the benefit of the doubt without quite knowing why.

The catch with easy access is how quietly it can go unused. Because nothing forces the issue, invitations sit unanswered, half-drafted proposals age in folders, and you assume the door will still be there next year. Sometimes it is. The interesting ones usually are not, and you rarely see the growth you skipped.

This aspect pays out on initiative, not on standing near the doors. Once a month, make one concrete ask you would normally postpone — the pitch, the introduction, the application.
