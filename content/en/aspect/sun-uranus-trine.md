title: Sun trine Uranus
---

Being unusual costs you nothing. Where others agonize over standing out, you simply do — your individuality flows without friction, and change tends to arrive as opportunity rather than rupture. You adapt to new technology, new cities, and new versions of yourself with an ease that looks like luck and is really native wiring.

That very ease is the trap. Because your freedom is never seriously threatened, you rarely fight for it, and gifts nobody fights for stay half-grown. Your originality can idle at the level of charming eccentricity — great stories, clever workarounds — while people with harder aspects out-innovate you through sheer necessity. Effortless also means forgettable if it never meets resistance.

Pick one unconventional idea and push it to a finished, public form; borrowed pressure will do what the trine won't.
