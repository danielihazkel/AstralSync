title: Moon opposition Neptune
---

The longing in this aspect lives across the table. You keep meeting people who need saving — the wounded artist, the almost-sober, the brilliant one who just needs someone to believe — or you play the elusive dream while a partner tries to rescue you into solidity. Either way, one person is the sea and one is the shore, and the roles feel fated rather than chosen.

There is real treasure in the polarity: you understand devotion. When you stop projecting the mystic onto partners and claim your own imagination, you can love actual flawed people with unusual tenderness — and forgive them things that would end other relationships. Compassion is not your weakness; it's your craft.

The friction is chronic mirage: falling for potential, staying for pity, then waking to a stranger and calling it betrayal. When a new person feels like destiny, wait until you've seen them tired, wrong, and ordinary before you hand over your heart's keys.
