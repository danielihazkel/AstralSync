title: Sun conjunct Uranus
---

Your identity and your urge to break things open are the same engine. You do not rebel occasionally; being yourself is the rebellion. Rooms reorganize around your arrival because you refuse the script on principle, and your best ideas land sideways, ahead of schedule, from angles nobody briefed.

The gift is authenticity with voltage — you can reinvent yourself overnight and give others permission to do the same. The cost is that fusion leaves no observer: you cannot always tell the difference between a genuine breakthrough and a reflex to shock, and steadiness can feel like death even when it is exactly what your work needs.

Consistency is not conformity. Before you detonate a commitment, ask whether you are escaping a cage or just an ordinary Tuesday.
