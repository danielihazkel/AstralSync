title: Sun square Uranus
---

You are a settled life's least reliable tenant. Just as an identity starts to fit — the role, the title, the relationship everyone approves of — something in you reaches for the ejector seat. The grind is internal: a self that wants recognition versus a disruptor that despises the terms recognition is offered on.

Worked well, this square makes you genuinely inventive rather than decoratively different. You break patterns because you have felt their cost from inside, and your reinventions carry earned authority. Worked badly, it becomes sabotage on a timer: quitting at the brink of success, picking fights with any structure that dares to hold you, mistaking restlessness for vision.

The skill to build is scheduled disruption — change something real every season so the pressure has a valve, and your revolutions become choices instead of explosions.
