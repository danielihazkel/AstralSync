title: Venus sextile Pluto
---

Depth is on offer in your relationships, one honest move away. You're comfortable where small talk ends: people trust you with their undersides, and intimacy deepens smoothly when you let it. You also read desire and money with a strategist's eye — who wants what, and what it's really worth — without the obsession that haunts harder Pluto contacts.

The quiet catch is that the door only opens if you knock. This ease never forces a reckoning, so you can spend years in agreeable mid-depth — trusted by everyone, fully known by no one — mistaking your capacity for intimacy for the practice of it.

The strength is transformative closeness without the melodrama; the weakness is leaving it in the box. In one safe relationship this month, volunteer a truth you'd normally file under later — the aspect compounds every time you go first.
