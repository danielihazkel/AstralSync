title: Moon conjunct Uranus
---

Your emotional weather changes on a fast clock. Feeling and lightning are fused in you: a mood arrives complete, reverses without ceremony, and delivers sudden intuitive readings of people that turn out to be uncannily right. You need closeness the way everyone does — but with a window open, an exit visible, and room to be a slightly different person tomorrow.

The strength of this fusion is emotional honesty without sentimentality. You can't fake a feeling for long, you refuse to rot in stale arrangements, and your household — however unconventional — is actually alive. The friction is that the same voltage trips the breaker: when intimacy presses in, you flick to detachment mid-conversation, and people experience your weather as whiplash. What feels like freedom to you can land as abandonment on them.

When the urge to bolt from a close moment hits, say "I need an hour" out loud instead of just going remote.
