title: Mars square Saturn
---

You drive with one foot on the gas and one on the brake, and the smell of that friction follows you through every project. Desire says go; an internal timekeeper says not yet, not like that, not you. Efforts stall at the ninety-percent mark, authority figures seem stationed at every gate, and your anger — throttled too long — escapes in cold, cutting bursts you later regret.

Yet this is a mastery aspect. The grind forces you to learn technique where luckier people rely on flair, and what you finally build is bolt-tight; late bloomers with this square routinely outlast the early stars. The corrosive side is quitting in disgust just before the payoff, or grinding joylessly at goals you chose only to prove the timekeeper wrong.

When you hit the wall, drop to a smaller repetition instead of stopping — this aspect rewards continued motion at reduced speed.
