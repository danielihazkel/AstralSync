title: Moon trine Mars
---

Wanting something and going after it feel like one motion to you. Your needs come with their own engine: hunger finds food, loneliness picks up the phone, ambition starts the project — no committee meeting between impulse and act.

This gives you an emotional metabolism that looks almost unfair from outside: you rarely stew, sulk, or archive resentments, because feelings get worked off through motion the same day they arrive. The gift's blind spot is assuming everyone runs this way. You can steamroll slower processors without malice, mistake their deliberation for weakness, and grow impatient with grief — yours or anyone's — that refuses to become a to-do list.

Some feelings are not fuel; they are weather. When someone near you is stuck, offer company before solutions, and notice which one they take.
