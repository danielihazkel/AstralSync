title: Saturn trine Pluto
---

You wear pressure well. Responsibility deepens you instead of flattening you: given the hard project, the turnaround, the estate to settle, you become calmer as stakes rise, and your career tends to compound like well-managed capital — slow, unglamorous, unusually durable. Authority accrues to you because you never seem to be reaching for it.

The hazard of strength this quiet is unexamined weight. Because enduring costs you comparatively little, you may keep carrying loads that should be renegotiated or refused, and let others normalize your overcapacity. There is also a subtler stagnation: transformation on tap can mean transformation deferred, steadily optimizing a life whose basic architecture you have not questioned in a decade. Competence becomes a place to hide.

Audit what you are currently carrying, name one burden you hold purely from habit, and set it down where someone can see you do it.
