title: Jupiter trine Neptune
---

Grace runs in the background of your life. Help arrives unrequested, strangers extend trust, and your instinct for meaning turns even detours into chapters that make sense later. You give easily, forgive readily, and carry a quiet conviction that things work out — which, around you, they often do.

The strength is genuine: compassion without agenda, imagination that soothes and elevates, a faith that steadies other people in rough weather. The taken-for-granted risk is drift. Because the current is kind, you may stop steering — trusting providence to choose your direction, letting vagueness stand in for decisions, giving to whoever asks rather than where it counts. Unexamined ease can dissolve a decade before you notice it went anywhere.

Name one specific thing your faith is for — a work, a practice, a person — and direct this week's generosity there on purpose.
