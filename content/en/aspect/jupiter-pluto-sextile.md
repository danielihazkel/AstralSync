title: Jupiter sextile Pluto
---

You have a workable channel between ambition and depth. When you choose to dig into something — a skill, a market, a buried problem — resources and backers tend to materialize, and your influence grows in quiet increments rather than dramatic grabs. People sense you can be trusted with leverage, so they keep handing you more of it.

The strength is transformative competence without the scorched earth: you improve what you touch and usually leave the bridge standing. The friction is under-use. Because power doesn't obsess you, you can settle for modest stakes, declining the deeper renovation — of career, finances, or self — that this aspect would actually support. Opportunity here rewards deliberate excavation, not waiting.

Pick one area where you've been skimming and commit to going disproportionately deep for six months; watch what compounds.
