title: Venus opposition Neptune
---

The dream tends to wear someone else's face. You attract the luminous and the lost — artists, mystics, charmers with unfinished stories — and in their company you become the anchor, the patron, the one who sees what they could be. Or the roles flip, and you play the muse while someone loves an idealized portrait you can't keep posing for.

The gift of this polarity is real: you experience romance at a depth most people only stream, and your empathy across the table is genuine, not performed. The friction is the exchange rate — clarity for enchantment. Deception, self-deception, and slow-leak sacrifices flourish exactly where you most want to believe.

Wholeness means keeping the dream and the ledger in the same room. When a new connection feels fated, slow the tempo deliberately: let three ordinary, unglamorous weeks pass before you promise anything, and watch who is still standing there.
