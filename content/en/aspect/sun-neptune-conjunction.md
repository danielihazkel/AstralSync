title: Sun conjunct Neptune
---

Your sense of self has no hard shoreline. Imagination, atmosphere, and other people's feelings pour straight into your identity, which makes you unusually compassionate, artistically porous, and hard to pin down — sometimes even for yourself. You can become whatever a room needs, and often do before noticing you agreed to it.

The gift is vision: you perceive the ideal version of things and can transmit it through art, healing work, or sheer presence. The cost is dissolution — commitments blur, boundaries leak, and when reality disappoints the dream, the exits marked fantasy, avoidance, or numbing start looking like doors home. People may also project their saviors and scapegoats onto your blank shimmer.

Anchor the vision in one repeatable practice with visible output, and check weekly that your yes still belongs to you.
