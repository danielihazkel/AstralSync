title: Jupiter in exaltation
---

In Cancer, generosity is not just present — it is honored. Your abundance takes the form of nurture: the open table, the spare room, the instinct to feed people before they know they are hungry. Growth, for you, means growing a place where others can grow, and communities tend to form wherever you settle.

The exalted gift has a performance mode: providing as identity. When being the nourisher is the applauded role, giving can slide into overfeeding — help that creates dependence, hospitality that will not let guests leave, protection that reads as smothering. And the provider's own hunger goes unmentioned. Watch for gifts that come with invisible strings of belonging. Once in a while, let someone feed you — and take the seat at the table instead of the kitchen.
