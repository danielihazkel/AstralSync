title: Venus in detriment
---

Opposite its homes in Taurus and Libra, Venus in Scorpio or Aries loves against the grain. Charm is not your native tool: in Scorpio, connection must be total or it feels like nothing; in Aries, desire announces itself bluntly and wants the chase more than the settling. You do not do polite, decorative affection — and everyone around you knows exactly where they stand.

That is the hard-won strength: your bonds, once made, are forged rather than arranged, and your honesty spares people years of pleasant confusion. The friction is intensity management — testing loyalty instead of asking for it, or torching a good thing because it got comfortable. When closeness feels dull or suspicious, say what you need plainly before you turn up the heat.
