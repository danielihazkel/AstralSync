title: Jupiter in detriment
---

Opposite its homes in Sagittarius and Pisces, Jupiter in Gemini or Virgo grows against the grain. The grand vision keeps getting interrupted by particulars: in Gemini, a hundred fascinating side-doors; in Virgo, the flaw in paragraph three. You cannot swallow a philosophy whole — you have to take it apart first, and most philosophies do not survive the disassembly.

That skepticism is the hard-won gift. Your faith, once assembled from verified parts, is sturdier than any inherited creed, and you make an honest guide precisely because you have checked the map. The friction is meaning death by a thousand details — so busy fact-checking hope that you never get around to having any. Periodically zoom out on purpose: ask what all these well-audited pieces are adding up to.
