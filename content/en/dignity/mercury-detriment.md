title: Mercury in detriment
---

Opposite its homes in Gemini and Virgo, Mercury in Sagittarius or Pisces thinks against the grain. Details slip; the big picture doesn't. In Sagittarius your mind leaps to meaning before the evidence is in; in Pisces it absorbs whole atmospheres and struggles to show its work. Either way, you reach conclusions by routes you cannot always retrace — and you are right more often than your method suggests.

That is the compensation: a mind that trades inventory for vision often sees what the careful catalogers miss. The friction is credibility — hand-waving where others want footnotes, promises of "roughly" that turn out to matter. Keep one external system for the small stuff — a list, a calendar, a checking friend — and let your mind do what it is actually for.
