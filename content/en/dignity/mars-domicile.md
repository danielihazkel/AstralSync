title: Mars in domicile
---

Your drive runs on home ground. In Aries, Mars is a sprinter — desire converts to action in one motion, and hesitation barely exists; in Scorpio, it is a submarine — force applied with patience, strategy, and total commitment to the target. Either way, you do not need to be motivated; wanting something and moving toward it are the same event in you.

The friction of a self-fueling engine is that it does not idle well. Combat becomes the default mode: obstacles get attacked when some should be walked around, and quiet stretches feel like failure, so you manufacture battles to stay warm. Keep a real project in front of you at all times — this Mars is magnificent with a worthy target and corrosive without one.
