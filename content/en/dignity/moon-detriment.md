key: dignity/moon/detriment
title: Moon in detriment
---

In Capricorn, opposite the Moon's home in Cancer, feeling works against the grain. The sign wants competence, and so your emotions get managed like projects: triaged, scheduled, handled. You are the person who functions in a crisis, who shows love by fixing the boiler and paying the bill, and whose care is more durable than most people's declarations.

That management is a hard-won skill, not a defect — but the friction is dryness. Needs get reclassified as weaknesses, comfort gets deferred until the work is done, and the work is never done. Practice reporting a feeling before it becomes a problem statement: "I'm sad" rather than "I've identified an issue." The people who love you can only respond to what you actually file.
