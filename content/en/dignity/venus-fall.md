title: Venus in fall
---

In Virgo, opposite its exaltation in Pisces, Venus swaps rose-tinted devotion for clear-eyed care. You love in verbs: the lunch packed, the appointment remembered, the flaw gently flagged before it costs someone. Where exalted Venus adores the dream, you attend to the actual person — and that attention, sustained over years, is a deeper vow than most poetry.

The friction is that a caring eye and a critical eye share a socket. Noticing what could be improved shades into cataloging what is wrong — in partners, and mercilessly in yourself — until affection feels like an inspection. The skill to practice is expressed appreciation without a footnote. Once a day, name something you love about someone exactly as it is, and let the sentence end there.
