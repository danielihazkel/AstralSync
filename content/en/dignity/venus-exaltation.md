title: Venus in exaltation
---

In Pisces, love is not just at ease — it is exalted into something close to devotion. You give without keeping score, forgive without being asked, and find beauty in people precisely where they are broken. At its best this is the rarest relational gift there is: affection with no conditions attached.

The exalted virtue has a stage version, though. Boundless compassion can become a performance of saintliness — loving an idealized person rather than the actual one, rescuing because rescuing feels transcendent, calling it devotion when it is really avoidance of your own needs. The skill to build is discernment inside generosity. Before you give again, check one thing: is this what they asked for, or what the beautiful story required? Give to the real person, not the halo.
