title: Mercury in fall
---

In Pisces, opposite its exaltation in Virgo, Mercury gives up the ledger for the dream. You think in images, moods, and associations rather than bullet points; answers surface whole, the way a shape rises out of fog, and your best ideas arrive while you are doing something else entirely. This is a genuinely creative intelligence — metaphor, empathy, and pattern where others see noise.

The friction is translation. Knowing something and being able to state it are different acts for you, and under pressure your explanations can blur exactly when precision is demanded. The earned skill is patience with your own process: capture the image first, structure it second. Keep a note within reach, write the thought down raw, and tidy it only after it has landed.
