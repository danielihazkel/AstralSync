title: Sun in exaltation
---

In Aries, your core identity is not just at ease — it is celebrated. Courage, initiative, and the willingness to go first are the traits this placement gets applauded for, and you can summon them fast: a decision, a direction, a visible act of nerve. Starting things is where you feel most like yourself.

The catch with an exalted planet is that praise can become the fuel. There is a difference between being brave and performing bravery, and this Sun feels the pull toward the second — picking fights or launches mainly because boldness is expected of you. Before your next big first move, ask whether you would still do it with nobody watching; if the answer is yes, go.
