title: Mars in exaltation
---

In Capricorn, your drive is not just strong — it is celebrated for its discipline. Raw heat becomes engineered force: you pick targets that matter, build the plan, and apply effort in cold, repeatable strokes until the mountain moves. Endurance is your signature; others burn hot for a week, and you are still climbing in year three.

The exalted version has its trap: performing the machine. Relentlessness becomes identity, so rest reads as weakness, and you keep executing plans whose purpose expired because stopping would spoil the image of the one who never stops. The check worth running is simple. Every so often, ask what the summit is actually for — and make sure at least one thing you grind toward would still matter if no one saw you reach it.
