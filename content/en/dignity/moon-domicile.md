title: Moon in domicile
---

In Cancer, your feeling life runs on home ground. Moods arrive, are felt fully, and pass through like weather over water — you do not need anyone to explain your inner life to you, and you read the emotional temperature of a room before a word is spoken. Caring for people comes as naturally as breathing; your instincts about who needs what are usually right.

The friction of a self-fueling tide is that it can close the harbor. Protectiveness becomes a shell: familiar comforts, familiar people, familiar hurts, all defended equally. Notice when you are guarding a feeling instead of feeling it. Once a week, let something unfamiliar past the seawall — a new place, person, or plan — and see what the tide does with it.
