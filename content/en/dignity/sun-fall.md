title: Sun in fall
---

In Libra, opposite its exaltation in Aries, the Sun learns selfhood the slow way: through other people. Your identity keeps checking the mirror of relationship — how a choice lands, whom it affects, whether it is fair — before it commits. This makes you genuinely good at collaboration; you can lead without steamrolling because you never forgot the other chairs at the table.

The hard-won skill here is a self that includes others without dissolving into them. The friction is deferral: polling everyone, averaging the answers, and calling the result your decision. When something matters, try writing down what you want before you ask a single person's opinion — then notice how far the final call drifts from that first honest line.
