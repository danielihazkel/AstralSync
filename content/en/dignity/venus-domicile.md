title: Venus in domicile
---

Your capacity for pleasure and connection runs on home ground. In Taurus, Venus enjoys through the senses — food, touch, music, the deep comfort of things made well; in Libra, through people — grace, fairness, the choreography of getting along. Either way, you attract rather than chase: you make rooms, relationships, and afternoons that others want to stay in.

The friction of ease is that it defends itself. Comfort can become inertia, harmony can become avoidance, and a life arranged around what feels good quietly edits out anything that doesn't — including necessary conflict. Notice what you have been keeping pleasant instead of honest. Once in a while, choose the awkward conversation over the smooth evening; real sweetness survives friction, and only the fake kind needs protecting.
