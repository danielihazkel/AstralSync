title: Mercury in domicile
---

Your mind runs on home ground here. In Gemini, Mercury connects — ideas, people, headlines, half a dozen open tabs woven into conversation; in Virgo, it refines — sorting signal from noise with a craftsman's patience. Either way, thinking and speaking are frictionless for you: words arrive when you reach for them, and learning feels less like effort than appetite.

The friction of fluency is that it fuels itself. Talk can substitute for thought, analysis can substitute for action, and the sheer pleasure of processing keeps the wheels spinning past the point of usefulness. Before your next explanation, try compressing it to one sentence and then stopping. A mind this quick proves itself best by what it chooses to leave unsaid.
