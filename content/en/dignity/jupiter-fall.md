title: Jupiter in fall
---

In Capricorn, opposite its exaltation in Cancer, Jupiter's abundance is put on a budget. Optimism does not come free for you; it must be earned, evidenced, and stress-tested. You distrust windfalls, discount hype automatically, and grow the way institutions grow — slowly, structurally, with reserves. What you build tends to still be standing when the boom-and-bust crowd has moved on.

That is the compensation: hope with an engineering margin is the most reliable kind. The friction is austerity as reflex — deferring joy until conditions are met that keep receding, treating celebration as an expense rather than a return. The practice is scheduled abundance: when something succeeds, mark it — a dinner, a toast, a day off — before filing it under "expected performance" and moving the goalpost.
