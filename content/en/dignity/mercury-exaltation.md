title: Mercury in exaltation
---

In Virgo, your mind is not merely at home — it is honored. Precision is the celebrated gift: you see the flaw in the plan, the typo in the contract, the step everyone skipped, and you can turn a vague mess into a working procedure. People bring you their tangles because you actually untangle them.

Exaltation's shadow is performing the virtue instead of using it. Correctness becomes a stage: correcting people to display sharpness, polishing details on things that do not matter, mistaking criticism for contribution. The discipline worth practicing is triage — deciding what deserves your full precision and letting the rest ship rough. Ask of each task: does this need to be right, or does it just need to be done?
