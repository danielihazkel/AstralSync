title: Saturn in exaltation
---

In Libra, Saturn's judgment is not merely competent — it is celebrated. Fairness is your load-bearing virtue: you weigh both sides slowly, honor commitments to the letter, and can be trusted with the verdict precisely because you take no pleasure in delivering it. People hand you the tiebreak, the contract, the difficult mediation, and they are right to.

The exalted judge has a performance mode: impartiality as a pedestal. Weighing can become a way of never landing, and visible even-handedness can matter more than the outcome being actually just — especially when someone close needed you to take their side. Notice when deliberation has become a residence. When the evidence is in, rule; a fair decision delivered on time beats a perfect one delivered too late.
