title: Jupiter in domicile
---

Your faith runs on home ground. In Sagittarius, Jupiter expands outward — travel, philosophy, the conviction that the horizon is an invitation; in Pisces, it expands inward — compassion wide enough to hold whoever wanders in. Either way, optimism is not something you practice; it is the water you swim in, and luck seems to follow because you keep saying yes to doors.

The friction of native abundance is that it never checks the tank. More can become the answer to everything: more plans, more promises, more generosity than the calendar or the bank account can honor. Growth without pruning is just sprawl. Before adding the next commitment, name one thing you will finish or release first — expansion counts double when something actually ripens.
