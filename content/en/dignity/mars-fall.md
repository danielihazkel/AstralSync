title: Mars in fall
---

In Cancer, opposite its exaltation in Capricorn, Mars fights with the tide instead of the hammer. Your drive is moody and protective: ferocious when someone you love is threatened, elusive when the cause is abstract. You will not march on schedule — but when the fight touches home, you become the most dangerous person in the room, because you are fighting for something real.

That is the earned strength: motivation rooted in care runs deeper than motivation rooted in ambition. The friction is indirectness — anger that comes out as sulking, retreat, or a snap at the wrong person hours later, because naming it head-on feels unsafe. When something stings, try saying so within the hour, in one plain sentence, before the tide carries it somewhere less honest.
