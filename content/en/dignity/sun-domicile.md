title: Sun in domicile
---

Your sense of self runs on home ground here. In Leo, the Sun does not have to borrow confidence from anyone: purpose, warmth, and visibility come from the same internal furnace, and people tend to orient around that steadiness the way a room orients around a fireplace. You know what you are for, and you rarely need a committee to confirm it.

The friction is that a self-fueling fire can forget it is not the only source of light. Confidence slides into assuming the spotlight is yours by default, and generosity can become performance. Watch for moments when you are broadcasting instead of listening; the surest sign your radiance is working is who it warms, not who applauds it.
