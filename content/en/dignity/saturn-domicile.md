title: Saturn in domicile
---

Your relationship to limits runs on home ground. In Capricorn, Saturn builds — structure, mastery, the long apprenticeship willingly served; in Aquarius, it architects systems — rules understood so thoroughly they can be redesigned. Either way, discipline is not imposed on you from outside; you carry your own scaffolding, and responsibility sits on you like a well-cut coat.

The friction of native structure is that it keeps building past the blueprint. Standards harden into severity, prudence into pessimism, and the load-bearing walls multiply until there is no room left to live in. You will meet your obligations — that was never in doubt. Audit them instead: once a season, find one rule you enforce on yourself and ask who it is actually serving.
