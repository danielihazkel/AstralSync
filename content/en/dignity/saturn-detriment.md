title: Saturn in detriment
---

Opposite its homes in Capricorn and Aquarius, Saturn in Cancer or Leo sets boundaries against the grain. The sign wants warmth — family feeling in Cancer, open-hearted shine in Leo — while Saturn wants walls, and you live at the checkpoint between them. Duty and tenderness keep arriving at the same door: you protect what you love by structuring it, and sometimes the structure is all anyone can see.

The compensation is care that holds weight — you are the one who shows up, keeps the household standing, takes responsibility for other people's safety without being asked. The friction is armor over the heart: love expressed as rules, praise withheld until it is overdue. Say the warm thing out loud while it is still current; the roof you built already proves the rest.
