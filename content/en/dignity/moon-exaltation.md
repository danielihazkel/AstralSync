title: Moon in exaltation
---

In Taurus, your emotional nature is not just comfortable — it is prized. Steadiness is your signature: you soothe yourself and others through the body and the senses, a good meal, a warm room, a predictable rhythm. People treat your calm as a public utility, and mostly it is; you are the one who does not panic.

Exaltation's catch is that the celebrated virtue can become a costume. Performing serenity — staying placid because that is your role — lets real feelings settle like sediment until they harden into stubbornness no one can move. When something bothers you, say so within the day instead of absorbing it. Your calm is most valuable when it is chosen fresh each time, not maintained like a monument.
