title: Mars in detriment
---

Opposite its homes in Aries and Scorpio, Mars in Libra or Taurus asserts against the grain. In Libra, the urge to act must first survive a fairness review — every push weighed against how it lands on others; in Taurus, force moves like a glacier — slow to start, nearly impossible to stop. Direct combat is simply not your first language.

The compensation is a rarer weapon than speed: in Libra, the ability to win people instead of fights; in Taurus, staying power that outlasts every flashier opponent. The friction is anger with nowhere to go — it leaks sideways as passivity, stalling, or a sudden avalanche after months of "fine." Track your irritations in the small sizes; a grievance voiced at volume two never needs to reach ten.
