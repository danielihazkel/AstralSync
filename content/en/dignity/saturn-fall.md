title: Saturn in fall
---

In Aries, opposite its exaltation in Libra, Saturn's patience is yoked to a sprinter. The sign wants to go now; Saturn wants the permit, the plan, the proof — so your starts carry a built-in brake. You feel the friction as hesitation at the exact moment boldness is called for, or as harsh self-judgment for every rough first draft.

But a brake on a fast engine is a skill nobody else gets to learn. Over time you develop what pure impulse never does: timing — the ability to act decisively and deliberately in the same motion. Your later starts are better than most people's, because each one carries the debugging of the last. When you stall, shrink the move: commit to the smallest irreversible step today, and let momentum handle its own paperwork.
