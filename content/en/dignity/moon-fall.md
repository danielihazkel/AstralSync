title: Moon in fall
---

In Scorpio, opposite its exaltation in Taurus, the Moon trades placid comfort for depth. Your feelings do not graze on the surface; they dive, and they surface with things other people never notice — motives, undercurrents, the sentence someone almost said. This gives you rare emotional courage: you can sit with intensity that would send most people out of the room.

The friction is trust. A Moon this deep learns early to armor its softness, so vulnerability gets rationed, tested, sometimes withheld as leverage. The compensating skill — loyalty that survives anything, honesty that goes to the bone — only pays off when someone is allowed close enough to receive it. Choose one person and tell them a small true thing before it has been fully processed.
