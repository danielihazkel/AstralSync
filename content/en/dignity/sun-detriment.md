title: Sun in detriment
---

In Aquarius, opposite the Sun's home in Leo, identity works against the grain. The sign favors the group, the system, the long view — so your sense of self is not handed to you; it is assembled, questioned, and rebuilt with unusual honesty. That makes you a sharp observer of ego in others and refreshingly free of the need to dominate a room.

The compensation is real skill: a selfhood chosen on purpose tends to be sturdier than one inherited by default. The friction is distance — standing so far outside yourself that you feel like a spectator at your own life, or letting the collective decide what you want. Practice claiming one preference a day out loud, without a rationale, just because it is yours.
