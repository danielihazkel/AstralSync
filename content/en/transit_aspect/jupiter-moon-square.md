title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Moon
---

Emotions run larger than the occasion during these weeks. Small slights feel operatic, minor comforts feel essential, and the urge to soothe yourself — with food, purchases, reassurance, or grand romantic gestures — gets ahead of what you actually need. Domestic plans may balloon too: the simple renovation becomes a remodel, the visit becomes an extended stay.

The friction is useful if you read it correctly. An exaggerated feeling is still pointing at a real need, just at the wrong volume, and this square often reveals exactly where your emotional life has been undernourished. The failure mode is answering that need with sheer quantity — more of everything — and ending the transit overextended, overfed, and oddly unsatisfied.

When a craving spikes this month, pause and name the need underneath it before you spend anything on it. Half-size the indulgence and double the attention, and the square becomes instructive rather than expensive.
