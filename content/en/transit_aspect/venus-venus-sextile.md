For a few days, your own taste gets a light, friendly echo from the world. The market has good tomatoes, the secondhand shop has the exact green you like, and the people you're fond of are easy to be fond of. Affection moves in small denominations — a squeeze of the hand, a shared joke, a well-chosen emoji that actually says something.

The snag is passivity. This transit is so undemanding that it can pass entirely unnoticed, a pleasant week you never touched. Mild pleasure, unclaimed, just evaporates.

Claim a little of it with your hands. Rearrange one shelf until it pleases you, put the good cup where you'll use it daily, and tell one person a specific thing you like about them — not "you're great," but the actual detail. Small beauty, deliberately placed, outlasts the transit.
