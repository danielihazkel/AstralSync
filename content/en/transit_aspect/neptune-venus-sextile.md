title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Venus
---

For a year or so, tenderness comes easier. Forgiveness that used to require effort arrives on its own, aesthetic pleasure deepens — music, light, gardens land with more weight — and relationships benefit from a gentle idealism that sees the best in people without demanding they perform it. Creative and romantic gestures made now tend to be received in the spirit intended.

The sextile asks so little that its gifts often go uncollected. Compassion stays a mood instead of becoming a phone call; the artistic impulse stays an evening of appreciation instead of a practice. And a soft focus, left entirely unexamined, can let small relationship drifts continue because everything feels fine at this resolution.

Turn the tenderness into one concrete act per month — the letter of appreciation, the donation, the shared unhurried afternoon — so the season leaves deposits, not just weather.
