title: Transiting Mars trine natal Sun
---

Will and vitality line up, and for a week or two action feels frictionless. You recover faster, decide faster, and carry a natural authority that does not need to raise its voice. People tend to follow you now because you visibly know where you are going.

The power is momentum with low resistance. The hazard is subtler than with hard aspects: trines can coast. Easy energy spent on errands and busywork leaves nothing to show when the current recedes, and overconfidence can tempt you to skip due diligence.

Aim the flow at something with weight — the ambitious proposal, the physical goal, the leadership moment you would normally rehearse into oblivion. Act while the machinery runs warm; do the double-checking anyway.
