title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Mercury
---

Words find their footing this week. Mars sextile your Mercury lends conviction to your voice without tipping it into aggression — you can ask directly, disagree cleanly, and follow a plan through to its finish while the details still hold your interest.

This is one of the better short windows for anything that mixes nerve and language: interviews, negotiations, cold outreach, technical problem-solving under deadline. The power is persuasive momentum. The hazard is simply neglect — a sextile does not shout, and the sharpened focus dulls quietly if you spend the week on routine correspondence.

Choose the conversation with the highest stakes and have it now. Make the ask specific, put a number and a date on it, and write down what you agreed before the energy moves on.
