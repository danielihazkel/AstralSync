title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Neptune
---

For several months, likely with a retrograde revisit, Saturn opposes your natal Neptune, and the confrontation between duty and dream arrives from outside. The world's demands — deadlines, ledgers, other people's realism — line up across from the ideals your generation stamped into you, and each side files complaints about the other: your obligations feel meaningless, while your longings look irresponsible to everyone holding a schedule.

The risk of the season is a bad trade in either direction — abandoning a workable life to chase a mirage, or letting a genuine calling be talked down by people who only respect what invoices. The reward is calibration. Oppositions offer full visibility: you can finally see what your dream costs and what your duties starve, and negotiate between them like adults. Arrangements made now — the day job that funds the studio, the sabbatical with an end date — tend to hold.

Put your dream and your duties in one honest budget, and fund the dream with a fixed, defended share.
