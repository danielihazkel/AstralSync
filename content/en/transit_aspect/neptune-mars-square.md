title: Transiting Neptune square natal Mars
---

For a year or more, in several passes, your will and your clarity are out of phase. Motivation ebbs without notice, goals that organized whole years look suddenly arbitrary, and efforts misfire in unglamorous ways — the initiative launched at the wrong moment, the fight picked with the wrong person, the energy spent defending against threats that turn out to be mist. Discouragement here has a peculiar quality: not defeat, but evaporation.

The square is dissolving a false version of your ambition — the goals adopted for appearance, the fights inherited rather than chosen — and the listlessness marks exactly where. What survives the season is drive you can trust. The hazards are compensatory: forcing action to feel solid again, or numbing the uncertainty instead of listening to it.

Downshift honorably: keep commitments small and concrete, protect sleep and the body's basics, and let the question "what do I actually want to fight for?" stay open until it answers itself.
