title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Mercury
---

This is your Mercury return: once a year, in ordinary years, the moving Mercury comes home to the degree it held when you were born. Think of it as the first page of a new mental year — the notebook flips over, and the questions you carry for the next twelve months get their opening lines here.

In retrograde years the return can happen three times over a couple of months, which reads less like one fresh page and more like a draft revised twice. Either way, what you pay attention to now tends to set the syllabus.

So choose deliberately. Write down the three questions you most want to be smarter about a year from now. Then do one small act of intake maintenance: unsubscribe from something, subscribe to something better, and clear the read-later pile's top layer.
