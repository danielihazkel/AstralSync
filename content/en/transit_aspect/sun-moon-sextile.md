title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Moon
---

Twice yearly, for a couple of days each time, the Sun forms a sextile to your natal Moon, and will and feeling stop arguing. What you want to do and what you need line up closely enough that ordinary tasks feel less like effort. Domestic errands, family conversations, and small acts of self-maintenance go unusually well.

Because nothing is wrong, it is easy to spend the window on autopilot and notice only afterward that you felt good.

Put the ease to work on something emotional you normally defer: a check-in with someone you have been vaguely worried about, or an honest look at your sleep. Gentle windows are for gentle repairs.
