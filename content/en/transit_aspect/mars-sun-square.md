title: Transiting Mars square natal Sun
---

This is a classic short-fuse week. Mars squares your Sun and everything feels like a test of who is in charge — especially encounters with bosses, officials, or anyone grading your work. Criticism lands hotter than usual, and your first draft of a reply is rarely your best.

The friction is real fuel. Squares force output: the same charge that wants to slam a door can push a stalled project over a hill. If Mars is retrograde, this pressure can simmer for a couple of months instead of days, which rewards pacing over eruption.

Choose the hill deliberately. Put the fight into a deadline, a workout, or a hard repair, and give authority conflicts a 24-hour cooling rule before you answer.
