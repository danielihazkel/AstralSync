title: Transiting Mars square natal Neptune
---

For a week or two, Mars cuts across your natal Neptune at a hard angle, and effort starts missing its target. You push and nothing firms up: instructions come back garbled, allies are vague, and you may find yourself furious at a situation that, on inspection, you cannot quite name. Fighting shadows is the signature move here — and shadows do not lose.

The strange power of this transit is discernment under pressure. Whatever collapses when you push on it this week was already hollow, and better to learn that now. Sensitivity to others is heightened, which makes gentle action unusually effective. The hazards are deception, self-deception, and depletion: scams, guilt-trips, glorified exhaustion.

Defuse before you fight. Get every important agreement in writing, name your suspicion to a blunt friend before acting on it, guard your sleep and immunity, and channel the restlessness into water, music, or craft instead of pursuit.
