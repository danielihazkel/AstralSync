title: Transiting Uranus square natal Jupiter
---

For a year or more, with multiple exact passes as Uranus retrogrades back over the degree, your appetite for growth collides with a craving for escape. Plans that seemed generous suddenly feel like cages; you may want to quit, relocate, or double your ambitions overnight. Events cooperate by disrupting the very ventures you thought were settled, forcing the question of whether your idea of a bigger life is actually yours.

The breakthrough available is a genuine upgrade of belief, trading an inherited philosophy or a stale five-year plan for something you actually mean. The danger is pure overreach: impulsive gambles, sudden exits, promises made in a rush of conviction that sober Tuesday cannot fund.

Before any dramatic leap, run the honest arithmetic, then change the plan deliberately rather than detonating it.
