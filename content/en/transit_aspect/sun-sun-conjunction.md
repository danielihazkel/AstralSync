title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Sun
---

This is your solar return: the Sun comes back to the degree it occupied at your birth, and a new personal year begins. For a couple of days your vitality gets a factory reset. Whatever you seed now — a resolution, a project, a change of direction — carries the charge of the whole cycle ahead.

The friction is noise. Birthdays fill with other people's plans, and it is easy to spend the reset performing gratitude instead of choosing anything. Fatigue can also run high just before the return, so do not read the tired week behind you as a verdict on the year.

Find one quiet hour, name the single wish that matters most, and give it first place. One clear intention beats a list of ten.
