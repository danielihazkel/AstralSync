title: Transiting Venus square natal Saturn
---

Venus squares your natal Saturn for a few days, and wanting bumps into ought. The evening out competes with the early meeting; the gift you would love to give argues with the number in the account; affection itself can feel like one more thing you are being graded on.

There is a hidden sweetness here: the friction shows you exactly which loyalties you keep from love and which from fear of disappointing someone. That is worth knowing. The snag is the reflex to earn tenderness through compliance — saying yes to be safe, then resenting the yes.

When you feel the pinch, name the real limit out loud instead of performing cheerfulness around it. One honest "I can't this week, but I want to" mends more than three dutiful appearances.
