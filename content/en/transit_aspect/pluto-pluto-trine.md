title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Pluto
---

Arriving for most people in the sixties or seventies and unfolding over a couple of years with several exact passes, this trine is the harvest of a lifetime's transformations. The losses, reinventions, and reckonings you have already survived resolve into something like mastery: you know what regenerates and what merely repeats, and younger people begin to seek out that knowledge. Power no longer needs to be displayed to be real.

The empowerment is generativity, mentoring, stewarding resources, presiding over endings in family and community with a steadiness others can borrow. The risk is the elder's shadow: using accumulated insight and leverage to quietly manage heirs and successors, or clinging to control of institutions and inheritances because letting go still feels like dying.

Choose where your depth is most needed, give it away deliberately, and practice releasing one form of control each year while release is still a choice.
