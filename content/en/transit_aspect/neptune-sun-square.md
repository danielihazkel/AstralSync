title: Transiting Neptune square natal Sun
---

Through the year or so this square is in effect, including retrograde returns, a fog settles over questions that used to have answers. What do you want? Where is this career going? Who are you when nobody needs you? Motivation can thin out, energy may dip without medical explanation, and you become easier to mislead — by others, and by your own wishful readings of ambiguous situations.

The square is dissolving a self-image that has quietly expired, and the confusion is the solvent working. People who treat this period as an interrogation to sit with, rather than a problem to outrun, tend to emerge with truer aims. The failure modes are escapism in its many flavors and premature reinvention — grabbing any new identity just to end the uncertainty.

Double-check facts before committing to anything large this year, and let the question of who you are becoming stay honestly open. Fog lifts on its own schedule.
