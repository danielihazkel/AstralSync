title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Moon
---

Across the year or more of this transit, emotional power struggles arrive through the people you depend on. A partner or family member may become controlling, secretive, or consumed by their own crisis; someone may probe your soft spots with unsettling precision; ultimatums surface in relationships that once ran on unspoken agreement. Care and control keep trading masks.

The opposition externalizes your relationship with need itself. Whatever you believe about depending on others — that it is dangerous, humiliating, or owed to you — gets embodied by someone across the table until you look at it. Bonds that can renegotiate their terms honestly come through transformed and sturdier. The failure modes are matching manipulation with manipulation, or purchasing peace through appeasement that quietly hollows you out.

Name the actual terms of your key relationships this year — what is given, what is expected, what is off-limits — and refuse to trade in guilt, yours or theirs, as currency.
