title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Jupiter
---

A door opens between small talk and the larger story. For a few days, casual exchanges — a hallway chat, a reply to an old email — can connect you to opportunities you would normally file under "someday." Your optimism sounds credible right now instead of naive.

The catch is that easy aspects are easy to waste. Nothing forces the conversation, so the window can pass while you tidy your inbox. There is also a mild pull to agree to interesting things without checking what they cost.

Pick one growth-flavored topic — a course, a trip, a collaboration — and ask one specific person one specific question about it. A single well-aimed message does more here than a week of vague enthusiasm.
