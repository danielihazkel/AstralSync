title: Transiting Mercury square natal Mars
---

For a couple of days, minor communications carry a static charge. The square from transiting Mercury to your Mars means the terse email reads as hostile, the interruption lands as an insult, and traffic, printers, and hold music all seem to be conducting a personal campaign. Your fuse for verbal friction is short, and so is everyone else's.

The energy itself is not the problem; it is combustible thinking looking for a target. Given a hard problem, a deadline, or a workout, it burns clean. Given an inbox, it burns bridges.

Schedule the demanding task for the irritable hours and let it absorb the charge. Do not conduct conflict by text — voices carry tone that keystrokes lose. If provoked, ask one question before you counterattack: "What outcome do you want here?" And check the address line before you hit send.
