title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Jupiter
---

About three years after your Jupiter return, and again around nine, transiting Jupiter squares its own natal place and asks how the cycle's plans are actually growing. This stretch tends to expose the gap between what you committed to and what has taken root: one venture is thriving and cramped, another is politely dying, and your calendar still treats them as equals.

The temptation is to solve friction with more — more projects, more promises, more spending — which only enlarges the problem. The opportunity is a working adjustment: prune what stalled, feed what proved itself, and update the plan you made when everything looked equally possible.

Choose one overgrown commitment to cut back this month so the genuine growth has room.
