title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Uranus
---

For a couple of years, through several exact passes, Pluto sextiles your natal Uranus and quietly reopens negotiations with your buried rebellion. Interests you set aside as impractical, a technology, an art form, a cause, a way of living, start tugging again, and this time there is depth and stamina behind the pull rather than mere restlessness. Change becomes available without requiring rupture.

The empowerment is steady reinvention: experimenting inside a stable life, updating one outdated arrangement at a time, and discovering that your originality can carry real weight in the world. The risk is milder here but present, cultivating an identity around being the maverick, or stirring low-grade disruption in groups and workplaces because intensity has started to feel like aliveness.

Choose one genuine departure from your routine and commit to it for a full season, so the rebel gets a real job instead of a hobby.
