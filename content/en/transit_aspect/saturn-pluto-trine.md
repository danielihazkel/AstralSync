title: Transiting Saturn trine natal Pluto
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn trines your natal Pluto, and depth and discipline cooperate. The way your generation metabolizes power — persistence, intensity, an instinct for what is really going on — becomes available to you as a steady working asset. Long-term projects that require ruthlessness about priorities move well now: eliminating debt, consolidating a business, ending an arrangement that has been dying politely for years.

The strength of the period is quiet, legitimate power. Influence accrues to you through demonstrated reliability rather than maneuvering, and structural changes you make are the kind that do not reverse. The shadow is subtler than a crisis: with this much traction, efficiency can shade into coldness, and you may prune people and possibilities that deserved more patience simply because cutting feels so clean right now.

Aim this stamina at one entrenched problem you have been managing instead of solving, and dismantle it completely before the season ends.
