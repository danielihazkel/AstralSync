title: Transiting Saturn square natal Jupiter
---

For several months, including a likely retrograde revisit, your plans for more collide with a structure that says not yet. Budgets tighten, approvals stall, and the growth story you have been telling gets cross-examined by someone — a lender, a manager, a spreadsheet — who wants evidence instead of vision. It is easy to read this as the universe blocking your luck.

What the square is actually locating is the gap between your appetite and your capacity. Overcommitments surface precisely so you can renegotiate them; inflated projections deflate so the real number can emerge. The friction is genuine — you may have to shelve something you love — but plans that survive this pressure come out fundable, schedulable, and far harder to argue with. Plans that only survive on enthusiasm do not, and better to learn that now.

List your current commitments, cut the one you are keeping out of pride, and reinvest that time in the plan with the strongest evidence.
