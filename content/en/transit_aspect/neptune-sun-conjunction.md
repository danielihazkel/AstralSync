title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Sun
---

Over the year or more this rare transit stays in range, the hard outline of who you are goes soft at the edges. Ambitions that once organized your life may stop making sense; imagination, compassion, and longing for something beyond the daily grind swell to fill the space. You are more porous now — to beauty, to spirit, to other people's moods, and unfortunately also to their agendas.

This dissolving is preparation, not malfunction. Old identities are being soaked off so a subtler one can form, and creative or spiritual work undertaken now can carry unusual depth. The failure modes are drift and glamour: waiting passively for a sign, or pouring yourself into a person, cause, or substance that promises transcendence at retail prices.

Keep your practical machinery running on rails this year — bills automated, commitments modest, big irreversible decisions deferred where possible — so the inner tide can do its work without sinking the ship.
