title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Jupiter
---

Over roughly two years, with several exact passes as Pluto stations and retrogrades across the degree of your natal Jupiter, your entire growth agenda goes through the crucible. The beliefs, plans, and opportunities you use to feel expansive are examined for hollowness. Teachers lose their glow, a philosophy stops explaining your life, a promising venture demands more of you than optimism can supply.

What survives this excavation comes back concentrated. Ambition that once felt inflated becomes strategic and durable, and your capacity to influence outcomes rises sharply. The shadow is plutocratic appetite: the creeping conviction that more money, more reach, or more certainty is the point, and that a large enough vision justifies any means of getting there.

Each time an opportunity swells, ask what it would cost your integrity, and pursue only the growth that costs nothing there.
