title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Jupiter
---

Roughly two years after your Jupiter return, and again near the ten-year mark, transiting Jupiter sextiles its natal place and the cycle offers you a friendly checkpoint. Over these weeks, growth comes through modest openings: an introduction, a small grant, an invitation slightly beyond your current circle. Nothing forces itself on you, which is exactly the catch.

Sextiles reward initiative and give nothing to those who coast. The opportunity is real but quiet, and the main risk is letting a period of easy expansion pass while you wait for something more dramatic to prove the moment matters.

Pick one opening that advances what you began at the return, and act on it while the door is still ajar.
