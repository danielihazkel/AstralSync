title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Sun
---

Life runs smoother than usual under this transit. For a few weeks, authority figures are well disposed toward you, your health and morale tend to lift, and efforts you made months ago quietly bear fruit. It is the astrological equivalent of a tailwind: you still have to pedal, but every push travels further.

The shadow of a Jupiter trine is complacency. Because nothing is wrong, there is no pressure to act, and many people let this window pass in pleasant idleness — then wonder later why the good mood produced nothing durable. Ease is capital, and capital unspent simply expires. This is also a forgiving climate for asking: raises, favors, and second chances land better now than under harsher skies.

Use the grace period deliberately: make the request you have been postponing or ship the project that is nearly done, while conditions are still leaning your way.
