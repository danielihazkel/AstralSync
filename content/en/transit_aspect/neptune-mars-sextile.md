title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Mars
---

For a year or so, action and imagination cooperate quietly. Work done in service of something felt — a craft, a cause, another person — carries you further than equivalent effort spent on obligation, and your timing acquires an intuitive edge: you start things when conditions are subtly right and step back just before pushes would have been wasted.

The sextile never conscripts. Its invitation — to put muscle behind an ideal, to train the body with attention instead of punishment, to volunteer the skill somewhere it matters — expires politely if unaccepted, and the season passes as ordinary busy time. The only friction is the soft kind: inspiration available and unclaimed.

Attach your effort to one thing you actually believe in this year — a project, practice, or service — and let that alignment, not discipline alone, set the pace.
