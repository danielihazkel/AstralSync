title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Neptune
---

During this stretch, transiting Jupiter opposes your natal Neptune, and your ideals get projected onto people and prospects at full size. A teacher, partner, investment, or cause may arrive looking like everything you have hoped for; alternatively, someone may cast you in their dream and expect you to play the part.

The opportunity is real discernment. With your imagination this visible, you can finally examine what you keep hoping others will be — and start supplying some of it yourself. The overreach risk is enchantment: giving money, loyalty, or years to a beautifully lit picture, then resenting the person for being human when the lighting changes.

Before committing to whatever glows brightest now, ask it for specifics in writing and watch how the glow responds.
