title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Sun
---

Once a month the Moon stands across the sky from your Sun, and for an evening you see yourself a little from the outside. Other people's reactions land with unusual weight; you notice how you come across, not just how you feel. Used well, that distance is clarifying — a small personal full moon that shows you what your usual self-story leaves out.

Used badly, it turns into tug-of-war: their needs versus yours, with someone cast as the problem. Feelings you have not owned tend to show up wearing someone else's face tonight.

When a reaction to another person spikes, pause and ask what it reflects back about you; the answer is often worth writing down.
