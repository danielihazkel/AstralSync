title: Transiting Sun trine natal Venus
---

With the Sun trine your natal Venus, life briefly acquires better lighting. Affection is easy to give and easy to receive, your aesthetic judgment is sharp and confident, and money conversations lose their usual edge. People agree to things because you asked pleasantly, which is its own kind of power.

The risk is that pure enjoyment leaves no trace. A lovely, frictionless few days can evaporate without moving anything that matters to you.

Convert some of the ease into a fixture: book the trip, frame the picture, have the pricing talk, tell the person plainly that you value them. Pleasant words spoken now get remembered.
