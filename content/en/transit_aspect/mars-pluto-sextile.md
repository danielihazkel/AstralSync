title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Pluto
---

For about a week on the two-year Mars cycle, transiting Mars offers your natal Pluto a controlled feed of power. Depth becomes usable: you can concentrate longer than usual, face an uncomfortable truth without flinching, and work near the roots of a problem instead of trimming its leaves.

The power is quiet leverage. Research pays off, difficult questions get honest answers, and a calm, private conversation can shift a dynamic that public effort never moved. It is a strong window for therapy, negotiation, strategy, and steady strength training. The hazard is mild but real: intensity without an assignment turns inward as brooding, or sideways as subtle maneuvering you would not want read back to you.

Give the depth a job. Open the file you have avoided, ask the pointed question kindly, consolidate the loans, start the program that rebuilds something from the base. Small, deliberate pressure at the root moves more than force at the surface.
