title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Neptune
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter trines your natal Neptune and your imagination gets generous weather. Creative work deepens without strain; empathy comes easily and is well received; faith in your long arc quietly returns. Meaningful coincidences may cluster, and helping others feels less like duty than like current.

The opportunity is to produce from the vision rather than only bask in it. The friction is exactly that comfort: trines let you drift, and a season that could yield a body of work, a healed relationship, or a real spiritual practice can dissolve into pleasant vagueness with nothing to show.

Set one finishable creative or compassionate goal for this period and let the inspiration prove itself in the completed thing.
