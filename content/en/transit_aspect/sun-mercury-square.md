title: Transiting Sun square natal Mercury
---

The Sun squares your natal Mercury and, for a few days, will outruns words. You know what you want; explaining it is the problem. Conversations turn into corrections, instructions get half-heard, and your quickest replies are the ones you later want back.

The friction is diagnostic. Where communication keeps snagging this week is where your thinking is genuinely unfinished — the square is pressing on a plan that has a hole in it.

Slow the channel down. Write before you speak, read messages twice before answering, and treat every misunderstanding as a prompt to sharpen the idea rather than repeat it louder.
