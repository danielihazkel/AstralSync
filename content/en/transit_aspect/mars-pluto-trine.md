title: Transiting Mars trine natal Pluto
---

Twice in the two-year Mars cycle, a week-long trine to your natal Pluto puts ruthless focus on your side. Stamina deepens; distractions lose their pull; you can hold one aim in mind for days without strain. Others sense the concentration and tend to step aside rather than test it.

This is demolition-and-rebuild weather at its most cooperative. The power is transformation on schedule: eliminating a bad habit, renegotiating from strength, cutting a dead project loose cleanly, doing the physically or emotionally heavy lift you have deferred. Because the force flows easily, people rarely resist it — which is also the hazard. Getting your way without pushback can slide into steamrolling, and you may not notice the silence around you until later.

Aim the drill at matter, not people. Finish the purge, close the account, master the hard skill's ugliest section. Then check in with someone affected and ask, honestly, how the week felt from their side.
