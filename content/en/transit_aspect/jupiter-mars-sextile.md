title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Mars
---

Effort compounds nicely for a couple of weeks: workouts build on each other, work sessions end further along than planned, and asserting yourself — asking for the resource, claiming the assignment — meets less resistance than you braced for. Momentum is available at a discount.

The sextile's weakness is that it never insists. Nothing external will force you to move, and energy that is not given a job tends to leak away as busyness — errands done briskly, nothing advanced. The opportunity here is specifically for initiative you were already contemplating: the door is unlocked, not open.

Commit to one concrete push this fortnight — the application sent, the training block started, the difficult task attacked first thing — and let the ease do its quiet multiplying from there.
