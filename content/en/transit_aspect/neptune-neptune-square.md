title: Transiting Neptune square natal Neptune
---

Around age forty-one, Neptune squares its own natal place, and for a year or more, across several exact passes, the dream you have been living inside is held up to the light. This is one of the classic midlife passages. The goals that organized your adulthood, the career, the image, the story of where it was all heading, can suddenly look thin, and a quiet question moves in underneath everything: is this actually what I meant?

The disillusionment is not a malfunction; it is the mechanism. Illusions are being dissolved so that a truer dream can be chosen by the person you have become rather than inherited from the person you were at twenty. The risks are the famous ones: numbing the ache with escapes, chemical, digital, romantic, or torching a workable life because the fog made it look like a cage.

Grieve the old dream without abandoning your post. Change nothing irreversible this year; instead keep a daily page of honest writing and one steady physical routine, and let the new dream announce itself when the water clears.
