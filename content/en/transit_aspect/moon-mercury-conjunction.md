title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Mercury
---

The Moon slides over your Mercury and the talking-and-thinking part of you gets flooded with feeling. Words come faster and warmer; conversations that were stuck on logic suddenly loosen because you are speaking from experience instead of argument. It is a fine few hours for journaling, voice notes, or telling someone what something meant to you.

The flip side: objectivity thins out. You may read tone into a flat text message, or send a heartfelt reply you would edit tomorrow. Your mind is persuasive right now, but it is arguing for your mood.

Draft freely, and let anything emotionally loaded sit for an hour before you hit send.
