title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Venus
---

The Sun lights up your natal Venus once a year, and for a few days your taste, charm, and affections take center stage. People warm to you with less effort on your part; it is a strong window for a date, a launch of anything beautiful, or asking for what something is worth.

The heat has a spending problem. Whatever pleases you now pleases you loudly — carts fill, desserts multiply, flattery convinces. Wanting is amplified along with being wanted.

Enjoy one deliberate indulgence rather than ten drifting ones, and use the goodwill for a concrete ask: the price, the second meeting, the overdue compliment said out loud.
