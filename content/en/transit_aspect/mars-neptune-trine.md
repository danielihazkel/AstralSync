title: Transiting Mars trine natal Neptune
---

Twice in Mars's two-year circuit, a week-long trine to your natal Neptune softens the border between effort and flow. Work feels like current rather than combustion: you lose track of time in the good way, and actions taken for something larger than yourself carry surprising force. Charisma runs quiet and high; people trust your motives.

This is one of the best windows for art, healing, mentoring, and any campaign that runs on meaning instead of adrenaline. Physical practices that blend breath and movement — swimming, yoga, long easy miles — restore more than they cost. The hazard is dissolution of aim: with no resistance to push against, the week can evaporate into pleasant vagueness, promises made in a glow and forgotten by the weekend.

Choose one devotion and serve it daily while the angle holds. Finish the song, sit with the person who needs company, train softly and long. Write down what you promised anyone, including yourself.
