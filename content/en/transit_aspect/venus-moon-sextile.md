title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Moon
---

For a few days, home and heart cooperate quietly. The kitchen feels friendlier, the neighbor's greeting lands warmer than usual, and small acts of care — yours and other people's — come without effort or ceremony. It's a good stretch for mending soft things: a frayed routine, a slightly neglected friendship, the plant on the windowsill.

The only real snag is inertia dressed as coziness. The ease makes staying in so pleasant that you can skip the very connection the transit is offering, and let the window close with the curtains.

Put the kettle on for someone. Invite a friend over without tidying first, or bring soup to the person who's been running on empty. Care given casually, in slippers, is the specialty of these few days — use it before it fades.
