title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Mars
---

For a year or so, boldness and timing cooperate. Unconventional moves that would normally read as reckless land as visionary; your energy favors self-direction and rewards it, and you can take genuine risks — the launch, the relocation, the public bet on your own method — with better-than-usual odds and less-than-usual drama. Physical daring sharpens too, with reflexes and confidence arriving together.

The trine's quiet failure mode is adventure as consumption. With excitement available cheaply, the season can be spent on serial small thrills — trips, toys, side quests — while the one structural leap it was built to fund goes untaken because comfort never forced the question. Freedom is being offered wholesale; retail purchases waste it.

Identify the boldest reversible move on your list and make it this year, while your nerve and your luck are running on the same clock.
