title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Saturn
---

For a year or more, across a few exact passes, Neptune forms a soft supporting angle to your natal Saturn. This is one of the kinder combinations of dream and discipline: your structures loosen just enough to breathe. Work you once did purely from duty can start to feel meaningful again, and imaginative or spiritual interests find practical containers, a class, a practice, a schedule, instead of staying wishes.

The opening is quiet integration, building something modest that your ideals can actually live in. The drift is subtler: because nothing is forcing the issue, you can let the invitation pass, keeping your routines rigid and your dreams unscheduled, or you can loosen commitments a little more each month without noticing the slide.

Use the season deliberately. Choose one responsibility you already carry and ask what would make it feel worth carrying; then change one concrete thing about how you do it, and keep that change for ninety days.
