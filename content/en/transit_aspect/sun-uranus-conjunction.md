title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Uranus
---

Once a year the Sun lights up your natal Uranus, and for a few days the part of you that refuses to be ordinary gets the microphone. Routines feel suddenly absurd, unconventional ideas arrive fully formed, and you may catch yourself wanting to quit something on principle rather than for a reason.

The opportunity is genuine insight. Under this brief light you can see which of your constraints are real and which are just habit wearing a uniform. Experiments started now — a new tool, a strange schedule, an honest conversation about needing more room — carry unusual voltage.

The friction is impulse. The urge to shock, to cut ties, to reinvent everything by Thursday, tends to outlast its own wisdom by about a week.

Change one thing that is reversible and note the irreversible changes you were tempted by; if any still appeal in a month, they were real.
