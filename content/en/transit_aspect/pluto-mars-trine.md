title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Mars
---

For two or three years, power and self-command run in the same direction. Sustained effort feels almost frictionless: you can work at depth for long arcs without burning out, confront hard situations without tipping into aggression, and carry authority in a way others accept rather than resist. Rebuilds — of a body, a career, an organization — succeed now that would have stalled in any other season.

The trine's danger is invisible: force this well-lubricated rarely triggers the feedback that keeps power honest. You can dominate rooms while experiencing yourself as merely competent, and steamroll quieter people with complete sincerity. Ease also permits a subtler waste — spending a transit built for transformation on the maintenance of an empire already won.

Aim at the largest constructive target you can name, and institutionalize dissent around you — one person paid or trusted to say no — while everything is going your way.
