title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Saturn
---

For a few days, Venus sextiles your natal Saturn and warmth cooperates with duty instead of fighting it. Obligations you usually grind through — the family check-in, the budget, the standing commitment — take on an unexpected pleasantness, like a chore done in good light.

This is a fine window for unglamorous relationship maintenance. The affection available now is not fireworks; it is the satisfaction of a promise kept and noticed. The only snag is that modest openings are easy to ignore. The chance to firm something up will not insist on itself.

Use the window practically. Set the date that keeps getting postponed, put the shared expense in writing kindly, or thank someone specifically for a duty they perform so well you had stopped seeing it.
