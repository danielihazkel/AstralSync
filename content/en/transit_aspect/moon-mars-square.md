title: Transiting Moon square natal Mars
---

Twice a month the Moon squares your Mars and the friction shows up as static electricity: small things spark. The printer, the driver ahead of you, the way someone chews — irritation finds a target fast, and your reactions arrive about a half-second before your judgment does.

Underneath the crackle is real information. Anger this reflexive usually marks a boundary that has been leaned on too long, and this recurring hour is when you can catch it in the act. The cost of ignoring that is snapping at a bystander and spending the evening apologizing.

Buy yourself the missing half-second: feel the spark, exhale once before responding, and later note what actually lit it — the pattern across months is the real finding.
