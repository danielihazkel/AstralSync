title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Mercury
---

Your mind gets a tailwind for a few days. The current Mercury trine your own means the ambient pace of information — conversations, reading, traffic, queues — happens to match your natural processing speed. Tasks that usually take three sittings take one. The right word is just there.

Tailwinds flatter you into overbooking. It is tempting to schedule this ease away on routine correspondence and feel busy rather than use it on the one piece of thinking that actually needs your best hours.

Protect a block for the hardest cognitive task on your list — the analysis, the outline, the decision memo — and do it first, before the inbox. Then, while comprehension is cheap, read the dense thing you have been avoiding: the contract clause, the documentation, the fine print. Note one question it raises.
