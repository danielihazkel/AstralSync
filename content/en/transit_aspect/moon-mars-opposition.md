title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Mars
---

Once a month the Moon opposes your Mars, and for an evening conflict seems to come looking for you. Other people appear pushier, more demanding, quicker to challenge — though at least half of that heat is your own assertiveness arriving from across the table, wearing someone else's face.

The opportunity is a clean airing: tension that has been circling a relationship can finally be named and released, and you may discover the other person was waiting for the chance. The hazard is the reflex counterpunch, where you win the exchange and lose the hour.

When you feel pushed tonight, ask yourself what you have been wanting to push for; say that instead, plainly.
