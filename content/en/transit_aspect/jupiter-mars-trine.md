title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Mars
---

Action flows without friction for a few weeks: your timing improves, competence is visible to the people who decide things, and effort produces results at a rate that makes work feel almost like play. Physical confidence rises too — the body cooperates, and risks you take tend to be the calculated kind that come off.

The catch with this much ease is that it rewards the current direction, whatever it is. A trine will happily accelerate a project that deserved to be questioned, and its pleasant productivity can crowd out the harder strategic pause. There is also a quieter loss available: coasting on talent through a window that could have funded a genuine leap.

Spend this transit on your most ambitious pending move — the one that needs both nerve and luck — rather than on polishing what already works.
