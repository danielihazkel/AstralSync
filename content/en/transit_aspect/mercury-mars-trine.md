title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Mars
---

Decisiveness comes cheap for a few days. Transiting Mercury trine your Mars aligns the analysis with the nerve: you can size up a situation, pick a course, and say so without the usual second-guessing tax. Meetings end with owners and dates. Difficult calls get made before lunch.

The catch is that a trine lowers the felt cost of commitment, not the real cost. Deciding is so comfortable that you can commit yourself three weeks of work in one fluent afternoon — everything feels equally doable at the moment of saying yes.

Spend the clarity on the decision you have genuinely been postponing: name it, choose, and tell the person who is waiting on you. Cap yourself at two new commitments. And before the transit fades, write down why you chose what you chose — future you will want the reasoning, not just the verdict.
