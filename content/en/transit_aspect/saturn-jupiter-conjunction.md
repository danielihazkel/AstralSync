title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn sits directly on your natal Jupiter, and every growth plan you have been running on optimism gets called in for audit. Commitments you stacked up in more expansive years now show their true carrying cost: the side project, the extra course, the generous promise you made when everything felt possible.

The friction is real. Opportunities seem to arrive with strings attached or not at all, and your usual confidence can feel rationed. But this is consolidation, not punishment. Expansion that survives a Saturn audit becomes infrastructure; the pruned branches were taking sap from the ones that bear fruit. People who use this season to pick one growth direction and build it properly tend to spend the next years compounding instead of scattering.

Choose the single opportunity you would defend under questioning, and put a schedule and a budget behind it this month.
