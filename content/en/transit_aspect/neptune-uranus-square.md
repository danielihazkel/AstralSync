title: Transiting Neptune square natal Uranus
---

For upwards of a year, including retrograde revisits, Neptune squares your natal Uranus and blurs your rebel streak. The part of you that knows exactly what it will not tolerate loses its edge: you feel restless without a cause, tempted to disrupt your life in ways you cannot quite justify, or seduced by movements and ideologies that promise liberation in beautifully vague terms.

There is an opening beneath the static. Old defiance, positions you have held since youth mostly out of identity, can dissolve now, making room for ideals that are actually yours. Inspiration is available, but it arrives mixed with noise, and the classic errors of this square are sudden exits made in a fog, technology or online rabbit holes as escape hatches, and confusing agitation with awakening.

Hold the impulse to detonate anything. When restlessness spikes, change something small and reversible, a route, a room, a routine, and reserve the irreversible decisions for a month when you can name, in one plain sentence, what you are actually seeking.
