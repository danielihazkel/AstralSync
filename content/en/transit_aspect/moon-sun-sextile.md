title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Sun
---

For a few hours the Moon offers your Sun a friendly hand: mood and purpose are on speaking terms, and it is slightly easier than usual to be warm, visible, and unhurried. This is a good pocket of time for the mildly daunting task — the email you have been circling, the favor you meant to ask.

Because the aspect is gentle, its weakness is that it slips by unnoticed. Nothing pushes you to use it, so it often gets spent on scrolling.

Pick one small thing that would make you feel more like yourself — a walk, a call, ten minutes on your own project — and do it while the window is open.
