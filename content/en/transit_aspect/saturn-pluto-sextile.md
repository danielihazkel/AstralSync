title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Pluto
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, Saturn sextiles your natal Pluto, and your generation's relationship to power — as it operates in you — gets a chance to be quietly reorganized on favorable terms. Depth work becomes practical: the therapy that finally sticks, the debt-payoff plan that holds, the honest conversation about control in a partnership that upgrades it instead of ending it.

The strength of this period is stamina with steering. You can dismantle an entrenched pattern deliberately, one structural change at a time, without the crisis that usually forces such work — and reforms made this way tend to be permanent. The weakness is that entrenched patterns do not volunteer for dismantling. Nothing will hurt enough to demand this excavation, so the season can pass with the leverage unclaimed and the buried arrangement intact.

Choose one long-standing pattern of control or avoidance, and start the slow, scheduled work of replacing it now, while you are choosing freely.
