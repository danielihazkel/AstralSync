title: Transiting Uranus square natal Mars
---

For roughly a year, in several passes, your fuse shortens and the world seems to strike matches. Interruptions land mid-stride, plans are overturned by other people's sudden moves, and anger arrives fast, electric, and slightly out of proportion. The urge to do something — quit, confront, floor the accelerator — can be nearly physical. Accident risk is genuinely elevated when speed and irritation share a moment; haste around vehicles, tools, and stairs costs extra this year.

Underneath the static, the square is confronting you with every place your will is jammed: obligations accepted under duress, aggression swallowed for so long it now discharges randomly. The explosive impulses are crude drafts of a legitimate demand for room, and the season goes best for people who renegotiate loudly on purpose rather than erupting by accident.

Move the voltage through your body daily — hard effort, on schedule — and when fury spikes, name the specific trapped thing it points to before you act on anything.
