title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Mars
---

The midpoint of your two-year drive cycle. Transiting Mars stands opposite its natal place, and what you set in motion at your last Mars return now faces you from maximum distance — often wearing the costume of an opponent, a rival, or a wall of external resistance. Energy runs high but feels contested, as if every push meets an equal push back.

That is the point. The opposition is the cycle's review: you can finally see your own campaign from the outside, including the parts that were bravado. Conflicts this week are frequently mirrors — the trait that infuriates you in your adversary is usually on your own roster.

Hold the line on what still deserves defending; formally drop what does not. Then spend the leftover fight in honest competition — a race, a match, a deadline — where opposition is the game, not the problem.
