title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Mercury
---

For about a year, disruption arrives through other voices. A colleague's radical proposal upends your roadmap, a new arrival talks a language your training never covered, or the consensus you relied on dissolves in public. The pressure is to pick a pole: dig into your established view and defend it, or capitulate to the exciting new one wholesale.

The opposition is staging a genuine debate your thinking needs. The unsettling voice is usually right about something specific and wrong about its scale, and your job is the unglamorous middle work of extracting the true part without buying the whole manifesto. Handled honestly, this season leaves your position renovated; handled defensively, it leaves you fluent in rebuttals to a future that arrives anyway.

When a disruptive idea confronts you, steelman it back to its holder until they agree you understand it — then decide what to adopt on your own schedule.
