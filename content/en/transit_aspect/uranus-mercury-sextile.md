title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Mercury
---

For a year or so, novelty is available on friendly terms. New methods, unusual people, and left-field information keep drifting into range, and your mind is unusually willing to play with them — this is a fine long window for learning a technology, joining a community of interesting strangers, or renovating how you think about a stale subject.

The sextile's openings are easy to admire and easy to ignore. Nothing will force the experiment; the interesting course stays bookmarked, the odd idea stays a dinner anecdote, and the season can close having merely entertained you. The friction here is mild but real: growth requires choosing disruption no one is imposing.

Say yes to one genuinely unfamiliar thing per season — the tool you don't need yet, the conversation outside your field — and give it enough hours to change your mind.
