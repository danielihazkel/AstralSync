title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Jupiter
---

Once a year the Sun stands directly across the sky from your natal Jupiter, and for a few days other people hold up a mirror to your beliefs and appetites. A partner questions the plan you thought was obviously good; an advisor doubles the estimate you hoped was generous. What you call vision, someone across the table calls a gamble.

The opportunity is perspective. Seen from the far side, your long-running growth story — the business, the degree, the move abroad — gets an honest outside audit it rarely receives, and the criticism usually contains one genuinely useful correction.

The friction is defensiveness and one-upmanship: the urge to answer doubt by promising even more. Grand gestures made in these few days age badly.

Let the pushback land before you reply. Ask the most skeptical person available one sincere question about your plan, and write their answer down.
