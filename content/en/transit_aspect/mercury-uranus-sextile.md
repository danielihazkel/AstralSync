A small window for friendly lightning. For a few days, the workaround you could not see becomes obvious, often through an offhand remark — a colleague's joke, a stranger's question, a forum post from years ago. Your curiosity is contagious and low-stakes experiments come easily.

The only friction is dilution. With attention this quick, you can sample ten novelties and finish none, ending the transit entertained but empty-handed. Interesting is not the same as useful, and this window offers plenty of both.

Aim the spark at one stuck thing. Take a problem you have been solving the respectable way and ask the deliberately naive question: what if we skipped this step entirely? Then message the most unconventional person you know and ask how they would do it.
