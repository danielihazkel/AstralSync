title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Mars
---

For a few days, disagreement finds you. Transiting Mercury opposite your Mars casts other people as the challengers: the pushback in the meeting, the driver leaning on the horn, the reply that opens with "Actually." Your instinct is to meet force with force, and the argument is usually winnable — which is exactly the trap.

Winning the exchange can cost the afternoon, the mood, and occasionally the relationship. But a worthy opponent is also the fastest test a plan can get. Someone is pressure-testing your reasoning for free.

Separate the challenge from the challenger. Take the strongest objection you heard this week and answer it on paper before you answer the person. If a Mercury retrograde is in play, the same dispute may return for a second and third round, so aim for a durable answer, not a fast one. Then ask your critic: "What would change your mind?"
