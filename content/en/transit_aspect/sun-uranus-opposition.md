title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Uranus
---

Once a year the Sun stands opposite your natal Uranus, and for a few days other people become unpredictable on your behalf. Someone changes the plan without asking, a colleague goes rogue, a partner announces a need for space — or you find yourself cast as the stable one while everyone around you improvises.

The opportunity is honest renegotiation. The disruptions of this window reveal where a long-standing arrangement has quietly gone rigid; the person acting out across from you is often voicing a freedom you have also been suppressing. Handled calmly, these days can loosen a relationship that needed air.

The friction is reactive ultimatums. Meeting someone else's sudden swerve with your own dramatic counter-move turns a weather event into a wreck.

When the surprise lands, ask what the other person actually needs more room for, and see whether you can grant a piece of it without blowing anything up.
