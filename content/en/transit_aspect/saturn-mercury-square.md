title: Transiting Saturn square natal Mercury
---

For several months, likely including a retrograde revisit, your words meet resistance: the proposal comes back with objections, the paperwork stalls, the audience that used to nod now asks for evidence. An inner critic often joins in, second-guessing sentences until writing anything feels like defending a thesis. Communication itself — devices, schedules, logistics — may add its own petty obstructions.

The square is not telling you your thinking is worthless; it is stress-testing it, and the places that crack are the places that were held together by fluency rather than substance. Arguments you reinforce now become permanently harder to knock down. The real hazard is silence — abandoning the idea because rigor was demanded of it.

When your work is challenged this season, treat each objection as an assignment rather than a verdict: answer it in writing, one at a time, and keep the answers.
