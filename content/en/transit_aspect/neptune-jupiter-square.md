title: Transiting Neptune square natal Jupiter
---

For upwards of a year, including retrograde revisits, Neptune squares your natal Jupiter and quietly inflates your hopes past the point where judgment can follow. Plans grow grander each time you describe them. Promises, yours and other people's, sound more certain than they are. The line between faith and wishful thinking, usually visible, goes soft.

There is a real opening here: this square can crack open a belief system that had become too small, and reconnect you with a sense of meaning that rules and credentials cannot supply. But the same fog that dissolves cynicism also dissolves discernment. Overcommitment, overspending, and charismatic certainty, in gurus, salespeople, or your own inner voice, are the classic traps of this period.

When something feels destined this year, that is your cue to slow down, not speed up. Run every large promise past a skeptical friend and a written budget before you say yes.
