title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Neptune
---

Intuition and intellect pass notes to each other for a few days. You read rooms accurately, sense what a friend is not saying, and find gentler phrasings for hard things. Creative work benefits from a light touch: the right word floats up if you stop chasing it.

The friction is mild but real — this current is subtle enough to ignore completely. A day of errands and screens can drown it out, and you will not know what you missed. There is also a soft temptation to keep impressions vague because naming them feels like breaking a spell.

Give one hunch a body. Take the wordless sense you have about a person or project, and write it as a single plain sentence beginning "I suspect that..." Then check it, kindly, by asking the person an open question.
