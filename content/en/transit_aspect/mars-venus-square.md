title: Transiting Mars square natal Venus
---

Wanting and having grind against each other under this square. Desire spikes, but so does friction around it: mixed signals in romance, jealousy where there was ease, and a cart abandoned online three times before the fourth, unwise checkout. Money burns a hole; so does unspoken attraction.

The friction is honest, though. This transit exposes where your relationships and pleasures have gone passive — where you have been waiting to be chosen, paid, or entertained. The itch is energy for renegotiation. A retrograde pass can keep the theme active for a couple of months, replaying the same tension until you handle it differently.

Convert craving into a concrete ask: for clarity, for the fair price, for the kind of time together you actually want. And move the impulse money into savings before it decides for you.
