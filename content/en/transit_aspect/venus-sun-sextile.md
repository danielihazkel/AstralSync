title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Sun
---

For a few days, small doors open pleasantly. An invitation arrives, a colleague mentions your work to someone useful, a stranger holds the elevator and means it. Nothing dramatic — just a mild tailwind at your back, the sense that being yourself is quietly working in your favor.

Because a sextile asks so little, its gifts are easy to waste. The pleasant offer sits unanswered, the friendly opening closes on its own, and the days pass agreeably but leave no trace. There is also a temptation to treat the ease as a reason to spend on treats you barely notice consuming.

Pick one opening and walk through it. Say yes to the invitation you'd normally postpone, or introduce yourself to the person who already seems to like you.
