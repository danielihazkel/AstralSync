title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Neptune
---

Every couple of years, Mars spends a week or two immersed in your natal Neptune, and the throttle goes soft. Motivation blurs: you sit down to work and drift, start errands and forget the middle one, feel tired without having done much. Willpower behaves like a flashlight in fog — the beam is on, but it scatters.

The gift is that action and imagination briefly share a body. Work done for a cause, a craft, or another person can feel effortless and inspired in a way brute ambition never does. The hazard is leakage: energy siphoned into rescue missions, vague crushes, screens, or a fight with something that turns out not to be there.

Lower the stakes and raise the meaning. Schedule creative or compassionate work in short blocks, verify facts before acting on suspicion, avoid alcohol and heavy machinery when foggy, and defer contracts and confrontations until the picture sharpens.
