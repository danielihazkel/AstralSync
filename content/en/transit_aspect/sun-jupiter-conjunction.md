title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Jupiter
---

Once a year, on roughly the same date, the Sun crosses your natal Jupiter and turns the lights on in the part of your life where you naturally expect things to work out. For two or three days your confidence runs warmer than usual, people say yes more easily, and plans that felt too big look suddenly reasonable.

The opportunity is visibility: this is a good window to ask, pitch, apply, or launch, because you present as generous and credible. The friction is inflation — the same warmth tempts you to promise more than the rest of the year can deliver, or to celebrate a win before it exists.

Treat it as an annual review of your growth story rather than the growth itself. Make one bold request while the window is open, and write down every commitment you make so you can check it against reality next week.
