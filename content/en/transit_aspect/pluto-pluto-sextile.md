title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Pluto

Arriving for most people somewhere between the late twenties and thirties, and lasting a couple of years across several exact passes, this sextile is a quiet deepening of your relationship with your own power. Nothing dramatic is required of you. Instead, psychological material that once ran you invisibly, old survival strategies, inherited fears, the family's unspoken rules, becomes available for inspection, often through therapy, a consuming project, or the first real losses of adult life.

The empowerment is durable self-possession: you learn what you can survive, what you actually want, and how to pursue it without theatrics. The risk is subtle, mistaking intensity for depth, cultivating control over your image or intimates, or deferring the inner work because nothing is forcing it yet.

Open one sealed room voluntarily this year, an honest conversation, a pattern examined, and let the deepening happen on your terms.
