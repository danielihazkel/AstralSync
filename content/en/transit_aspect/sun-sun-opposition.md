title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Sun
---

Your half-birthday: the Sun stands directly opposite the degree it held when you were born. You are as far from your solar return as you can get, which makes this the year's best vantage point. For two or three days you can see your own trajectory the way an outsider would — what is working, what was abandoned, what quietly took over.

Objectivity has a cost. Seen from this distance, the year's efforts can look smaller than they felt, and the temptation is to judge rather than assess. Feedback from partners and rivals lands loudly now too.

Write a plain mid-year review: three things on track, one thing to stop. Save it to reread at the next birthday.
