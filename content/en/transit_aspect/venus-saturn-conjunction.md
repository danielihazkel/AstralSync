title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Saturn
---

Venus sits on your natal Saturn for a few days, and affection turns toward what has weathered. You feel the pull of the friend of twenty years over the acquaintance of twenty minutes, the plain gold ring over the trend, the quiet evening over the loud one.

The sweetness is steadiness made visible: love expressed as reliability, and the deep comfort of people who have already seen your worst. The snag is austerity mistaken for virtue — you may withhold warmth to seem serious, or trade compliance for approval, being agreeable to someone whose respect you should simply ask for.

Do one durable thing. Repair the shoe rather than replace it, call the elder who shaped you, or tell a long-standing friend, in plain words, that they are load-bearing.
