title: Transiting Moon trine natal Mercury
---

Words flow downhill for a few hours. Feeling and phrasing arrive together, so you explain yourself with unusual grace and listen without rehearsing your reply. Writing that has been stiff turns supple; a delicate subject can be raised without anyone flinching. Memory is warmer too — the right story or example surfaces just when you need it.

The mild hazard is glibness. Because everything sounds good right now, you can talk fluently past a problem rather than into it, and charm yourself into thinking a thing is settled when it is only well described.

Capture the fluency: spend ten minutes writing the paragraph, apology, or idea that has resisted you all week.
