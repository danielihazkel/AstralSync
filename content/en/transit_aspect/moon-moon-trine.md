title: Transiting Moon trine natal Moon
---

Your inner tide and the sky's are moving together, and for a few hours feelings flow without snagging. You know what you need and it is mostly within reach; empathy comes easily, and old worries lose some of their grip. People often sleep well, cook well, and forgive small things under this weather.

The only real hazard is passivity. Everything feels fine enough that you may float past a conversation or task that would land beautifully right now, precisely because you are so settled.

While the water is calm, say the tender thing you keep postponing — appreciation travels furthest when nothing in you is braced.
