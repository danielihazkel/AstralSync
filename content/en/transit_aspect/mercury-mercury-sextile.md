title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Mercury
---

The sky's Mercury makes a friendly angle to your own, and for a few days other minds are unusually compatible with yours. Explanations you give get received the first time. Someone else's offhand comment hands you the missing piece of a problem you had shelved.

The limitation is that sextiles reward initiative and punish nothing. If you keep your thinking to yourself these days, they pass unremarked. The trade routes are open, but you have to load the cart.

Take one half-formed idea out of your head and show it to a person who thinks differently than you do — the colleague who asks annoyingly practical questions, the friend who reads other books. Ask them, "What am I not seeing here?" and write down the first thing they say, especially if it stings a little.
