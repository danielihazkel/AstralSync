title: Transiting Sun trine natal Neptune
---

Twice a year the Sun flows in easy aspect to your natal Neptune, and for a few days the world is gentler than usual. Empathy comes without effort, aesthetic pleasure is everywhere — light on a wall can stop you mid-step — and forgiveness, of others and of yourself, is briefly within reach.

The opportunity is restoration. This is a natural window for art, rest, prayer or meditation, water in any form, and reconciliations that pride has been blocking. Creative work touched now often gains a quality you cannot manufacture on demand, so the long project of your imagination deserves an hour of it.

The friction is dissolution of the to-do list. Under this weather everything urgent feels optional, and three days of bliss can quietly become a missed deadline.

Guard your two or three fixed obligations, then give the rest of one day fully to the thing that refills you.
