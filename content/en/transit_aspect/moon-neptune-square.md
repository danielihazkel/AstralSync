title: Transiting Moon square natal Neptune
---

A few hours each month, fog rolls across your schedule. The Moon squares your natal Neptune: keys go missing, instructions read two ways, a vague unease hums under the to-do list, and effort feels like walking uphill through weather.

The gift is honest exposure — the blur tends to gather exactly where you've been running on fumes, wishful math, or a story that no longer holds. What goes soft in these hours was already soft. The snag is deciding anything from inside the mist, or sliding into self-pity because the day won't sharpen.

Double-check the one appointment or figure that matters, then lower your standards for a few hours on the rest. Note what felt blurry. The same blur returning monthly is a message, not a mood.
