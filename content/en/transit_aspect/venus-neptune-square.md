title: Transiting Venus square natal Neptune
---

Venus squares your natal Neptune for a few days, and desire fogs the windshield. You want the mood more than the facts — the version of the person, the purchase, the plan that exists mostly in the trailer you have cut for it. Disappointment during this transit is usually the bill for last week's embellishment.

The sweetness is a heightened ache for beauty and connection that, aimed at art, produces your most affecting work. The snag is aiming it at people: reading a text three times for hidden tenderness, or being vague yourself because clarity feels unromantic.

Keep the longing, check the invoice. Before you commit money or heart, ask one concrete question and let the answer count. Then give the fog a canvas — an evening of film, poetry, or rain on the glass, enjoyed as fiction.
