title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Mercury
---

For a year or so, imagination is available as a working tool. Metaphors arrive when you need them, explanations soften without losing truth, and you can hear the feeling underneath what people say — useful equally in teaching, selling, counseling, and writing. Dreams and idle reveries may carry more signal than usual.

The gentleness of this transit is also its limit: inspiration mists in quietly and evaporates just as quietly if nothing catches it. Ideas that felt luminous at midnight are gone by breakfast, not because they were false but because mist does not file itself. There is also a mild drift toward pleasant vagueness in commitments — everything agreeable, nothing scheduled.

Keep a capture habit — notebook, voice memos, morning pages — and convert one inspired fragment per month into something finished enough to show someone.
