title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Mercury

For a few days, honey gets into your sentences. Emails come out gracious on the first draft, small talk turns into actual talk, and you find the tactful phrasing for things that usually arrive blunt. People enjoy listening to you, and you can hear it in how long they stay on the line.

The snag is that pleasant words are also the easiest place to hide. It becomes tempting to tell people what they'd like to hear, to wrap a "no" in so much softness it sounds like "maybe," or to be charmed by a pitch because the sentences were beautiful.

Use the eloquence on something that deserves it. Write the thank-you note you've owed for months — the real one, specific, three sentences about exactly what they did. Sweet language spent on true things doesn't spoil.
