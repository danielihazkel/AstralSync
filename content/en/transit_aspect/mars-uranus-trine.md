title: Transiting Mars trine natal Uranus
---

Twice per two-year Mars lap, a week-long trine to your natal Uranus turns your reflexes brilliant. Courage and originality cooperate: the unconventional move that usually feels too risky now feels obvious, and your timing for it is unusually good. You improvise well, adapt fast, and enjoy your own strangeness.

The power is the clean break that costs nobody blood — changing course, updating an identity, adopting the new method while others are still debating it. Bodies respond well to new training stimuli; minds respond well to new problems. The hazard is treating the window as a mood instead of a chance: freedom enjoyed as restlessness, then business as usual.

Make one forward-only change while the angle holds. Switch the workflow, debut the new direction, take the spontaneous trip, ask the unusual question in the meeting. Choose something you will keep after the week ends — the trine opens the door; walking through is still your job.
