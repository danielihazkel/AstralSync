title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Jupiter
---

Over a year or more, with the usual retrograde returns to the exact degree, Uranus and your natal Jupiter cooperate instead of collide. Experiments succeed with suspicious ease: the odd side project finds an audience, the unconventional application gets the interview, travel or study drops you among people who change how you think. Your beliefs stretch without tearing.

Because trines feel effortless, their breakthrough is easy to waste. The liberation on offer is a real widening of your world through the untried; the quiet risk is treating good fortune as a spectator sport, or growing careless because everything keeps landing on its feet. Streaks like this reward consolidation, not just momentum.

Launch the experiment you have been postponing while conditions favor it, and bank part of every win instead of rolling all of it forward.
