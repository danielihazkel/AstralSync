title: Transiting Neptune square natal Pluto
---

For upwards of a year, including retrograde revisits, Neptune squares your natal Pluto, and your strategies of control stop working the way they used to. Situations you would normally master by force of will turn to water in your hands: the harder you grip, the less there is to hold. Old compulsions may flare precisely because their grip is being pried loose, and the psyche's basement sends up fog instead of clear signals.

The opening inside the frustration is surrender as a skill. What dissolves under this square is mostly what was costing you the most: vigilance, secrecy, the exhausting project of managing everyone's perceptions. The dangers are equally deep, escapist undertows, fascination with manipulative people or your own manipulations, paranoia blooming in ambiguity, and power struggles fought against opponents made mostly of mist.

Assume your read on hidden motives is distorted this year, including your read on your own. Before acting on any suspicion or compulsion, take it to one trusted outside voice, and steady the body daily, sleep, movement, water, so the depths have a shore.
