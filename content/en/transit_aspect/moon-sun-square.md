title: Transiting Moon square natal Sun
---

Twice a month the Moon squares your Sun, and your mood briefly stops cooperating with your plans. You want to push forward and you also want to be left alone; neither impulse fully wins, so the hours can feel scratchy and self-critical for no obvious reason.

The gift hidden in the friction is information: whatever chafes right now is usually something your daily routine genuinely neglects. The snag is that the irritation feels bigger than it is, and you may be tempted to rewrite your life before dinner.

Hold off on big conclusions until tomorrow, and simply name — out loud or in your journal — the one need that felt squeezed today.
