title: Transiting Mars square natal Uranus
---

For a week or two, Mars crosses wires with your natal Uranus, and the mood is a static charge looking for a doorknob. Interruptions multiply, plans jump the rails, and a caged feeling builds until part of you wants to kick the cage just to hear it rattle. Sudden exits and sharp words feel righteous in the moment.

There is real signal under the static: the constraint that most infuriates you now is probably one you have genuinely outgrown, and this friction supplies the nerve to change it. The hazard is haste — this is a textbook window for the impulsive rupture, the reckless overtake, the strain injury from moving faster than your setup allows.

Slow your body and free your schedule. Drive gently, double-check ladders and blades, and build slack into the week. Then defuse the pressure at its source: name the one constraint to renegotiate deliberately, in daylight, not by detonation.
