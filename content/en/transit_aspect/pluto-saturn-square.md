title: Transiting Pluto square natal Saturn
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto squares your natal Saturn and puts your load-bearing structures under compression. Careers, contracts, institutions, and long-held rules about how life must be run are tested by forces that do not negotiate: reorganizations, audits, authority confrontations, or an internal exhaustion that refuses one more year of the same grind. What is structurally sound survives; what was propped up by fear or habit begins to buckle.

The empowerment on the far side is a life re-founded on what you actually authorize, with an authority that comes from within rather than from a title. The danger is fighting the demolition, gripping harder, over-controlling subordinates or family, treating every challenge to your position as an enemy, until the structure fails anyway and takes more with it.

Where you feel pressure mounting, dismantle voluntarily what you can no longer honestly defend, and rebuild only what deserves your name.
