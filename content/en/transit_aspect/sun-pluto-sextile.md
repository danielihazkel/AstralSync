title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Pluto
---

Twice a year the Sun offers your natal Pluto quiet cooperation, and for a few days depth becomes accessible without drama. Conversations go one level truer than usual, you can look at an old wound without flinching, and focused effort compounds — an afternoon of decluttering, editing, or debt-paying accomplishes what usually takes a week.

The opportunity is voluntary transformation in small doses. This is the window to close the account, delete the archive, ask the direct question, or drop the habit you have already half-released. Changes chosen now tend to hold because nothing forced them.

The friction is that nothing forces them: the sextile presents power politely, and politely offered power is easy to decline. The heavy lifting stays optional right up until it doesn't.

Pick one thing you have outgrown and end it cleanly today, while ending things is this easy.
