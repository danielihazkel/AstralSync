title: Transiting Sun square natal Saturn
---

Twice a year the Sun grinds against your natal Saturn for a few days, and you meet the wall where your will runs into your limits. Plans hit red tape, a supervisor asks for proof, your body reminds you it is not a machine. Whatever you have been putting off tends to send an invoice this week.

The friction is obvious: delay, criticism, the sense of being tested by someone with a clipboard. Less obvious is the opportunity — the obstacle is specific, and specific obstacles can be worked. This annual pressure point shows you exactly which support beam in your long-term structure is undersized.

Resist the twin temptations of quitting and of arguing with the referee. The test is brief; the structure it examines is not.

Pick the requirement you have been resenting and simply meet it — one form filed, one standard reached — before the pressure lifts.
