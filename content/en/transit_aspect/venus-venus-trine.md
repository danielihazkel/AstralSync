title: Transiting Venus trine natal Venus
---

For a few days, the world agrees with your taste. What you cook comes out balanced, what you wear hangs right, and affection flows at exactly the temperature you like it — warm, unhurried, nobody keeping score. You are easy to please and easy to be pleased by, which makes you genuinely good company.

The snag is that when everything tastes good, you stop tasting. Indulgence slides into accumulation: three treats where one would have sung, another episode, another glass, pleasure piled until it goes numb. A trine's abundance is best met with a small plate.

So choose one nice thing and give it your whole attention — the single square of excellent chocolate eaten slowly, the one album played start to finish with the lights low. Depth over volume. Let enough be a flavor too.
