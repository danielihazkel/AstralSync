title: Transiting Moon square natal Mercury
---

For a few hours your mood and your mind talk past each other. You feel something clearly but explain it clumsily, or you say the technically correct thing in exactly the wrong tone. Misreadings multiply on both sides: expect a text taken the wrong way, an instruction half-heard, a small crossed wire with someone you usually understand.

There is a gift in the static — it exposes where your habitual phrasing does not match what you actually mean, which is worth knowing. The trap is doubling down and arguing your way deeper into the tangle.

When a conversation starts to knot, stop explaining and ask one clarifying question instead; note in your journal which topic keeps knotting at this monthly touch.
