title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Pluto
---

Once a month the Moon sextiles your natal Pluto, opening a few hours of quiet depth. Nothing churns; you simply find you can look at hard things without flinching — the old grievance, the money truth, the habit you defend — and they hold still for inspection.

The gift is low-cost honesty. Insights that would sting on a raw day arrive matter-of-factly, and letting go of something small feels almost administrative. The snag is that nothing forces the descent: with no crisis knocking, it's easy to stay in the shallows and let the window close.

Ask yourself one honest question — what am I still pretending about? — and answer it in your journal. These monthly entries, read in sequence, show you exactly what keeps surfacing when the water is calm.
