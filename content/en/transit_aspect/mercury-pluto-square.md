title: Transiting Mercury square natal Pluto
---

For a few days, conversations carry a current. An innocent question lands on a nerve, a negotiation turns into a contest of wills, and your own mind loops on one thread — the remark, the slight, the unanswered message — replaying it at 2 a.m. with fresh commentary. Something half-hidden may surface at an inconvenient time.

The value is in what the intensity points at: you do not obsess over things that do not matter to you. The loop is a crude instrument for locating buried stakes. A retrograde pass can bring the same charged exchange around again over a couple of months, usually with more of the truth available the second time.

Break the loop on paper. Write the obsessive thought once, completely, then beneath it write what you are actually afraid of. Say that second sentence to the person involved — or to no one, yet — but say it in words.
