title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Neptune
---

In your early eighties, Neptune reaches the opposition to its own natal place, a transit of a year or more, with several exact passes, that arrives like moonlight on the whole span of a life. The boundary between past and present grows thin: memory, dream, and the day at hand mingle freely, and the question of what it all meant stops being abstract and becomes the actual weather of the mind.

The opening is a rare one: seen from this far shore, old injuries can dissolve into understanding, faith can become experiential rather than argued, and the imagination that has accompanied you since birth shows itself whole. The risks are gentler but real, disorientation, retreat into fog, susceptibility to confusion or to people who exploit it, and a sadness that mistakes dissolution for loss.

Stay tethered while the veil thins. Keep regular human contact and simple daily rhythms, tell the stories out loud to someone who writes them down, and let each day close with one concrete act of order, a watered plant, a tidied table, a letter sent.
