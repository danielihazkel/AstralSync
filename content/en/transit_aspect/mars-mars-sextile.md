title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Mars
---

A smooth stretch in your own energy cycle. Transiting Mars sextiles its natal place, and for about a week your effort meets the world at a favorable angle — training progresses, work compounds, and initiative feels affordable rather than costly. Whatever you set in motion at your last Mars return gets willing weather here.

The power is traction: this is a maintenance-and-advance window, ideal for adding a rep, a client, a habit, a skill. The hazard is only that ease breeds vagueness — energy this cooperative will happily disperse into a busy week that advances nothing in particular.

Reconnect with the goal you named at the cycle's start and push it one measurable notch forward. If you never named one, do it now, mid-stride; the cycle accepts late entries.
