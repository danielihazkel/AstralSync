title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Uranus
---

Across a year or more of slow passes, Neptune trines your natal Uranus, and vision and invention run in the same current. Your unconventional side gets bathed in meaning: experiments feel guided rather than reckless, intuition and intellect trade information freely, and changes you have wanted for years can happen almost without friction, dissolving into place instead of being forced.

The invitation is to update your life in the direction of your ideals while the water is calm, to modernize a practice, join the community you have circled, or let a creative or technological project carry spiritual weight. The drift is passivity: the trine applies no pressure, so its openings can slide by as pleasant moods, or restlessness can scatter into a dozen inspirations with no follow-through.

Choose deliberately what the ease will fund. Name one overdue change that serves your actual values, schedule its first concrete step within the month, and keep a simple weekly log so a year of gentle current leaves a visible wake.
