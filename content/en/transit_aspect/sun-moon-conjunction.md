title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Moon
---

Once a year the Sun crosses your natal Moon, and for a few days your inner life stands in full light. Needs you usually manage quietly — for rest, food, family, familiar ground — become visible, to you and to everyone around you. Home matters ask for attention and reward it.

The friction: visibility cuts both ways. Moods you would normally keep private leak into meetings and messages, and other people may notice you are tired or tender before you do.

Feed the need instead of hiding it. Cook the meal, call the parent, fix the corner of the house that has been nagging at you. What you tend now stays tended.
