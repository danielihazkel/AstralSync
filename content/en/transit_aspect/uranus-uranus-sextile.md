title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Uranus
---

The Uranus-to-Uranus sextile marks the flowing chapters of your individuation cycle, arriving first in the mid-teens, when a distinct self starts announcing its preferences, and again around seventy, when reinvention becomes available without the pressure of crisis. For a year or more, across a retrograde revisit or two, change cooperates with you rather than ambushing you.

The breakthrough on offer is low-cost experimentation: trying an identity, a scene, a skill, or a freedom on for size while the stakes stay manageable. The risk is twofold, letting the window pass because nothing forces you through it, or sampling novelty so restlessly that nothing new ever takes root.

Pick one genuine departure from your routine self, give it a real trial this year, and keep whatever version of you it reveals.
