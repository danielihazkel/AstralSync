title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Moon
---

For about a week, courage and feeling work as allies instead of rivals. It becomes noticeably easier to say what you need without either apologizing for it or weaponizing it — the emotional equivalent of a clean throw.

The power of this window is warm initiative: repairing a strained tie, asking for support, tackling the household project that guilt has been circling. The hazard is mild but real — because nothing is forcing the issue, the easier path is to let the mood pass unspent and keep the old silence.

Use the traction on one deferred emotional errand. Make the call you have been rehearsing, set the boundary gently but out loud, or put real effort into your physical home. Acting on instinct lands well now; waiting wastes it.
