title: Transiting Pluto square natal Mars
---

Across two to three years, in repeated passes, your will collides with forces that do not yield to effort. Obstruction arrives with teeth: the rival who fights dirty, the institution that grinds, the circumstance that takes something you were not prepared to lose. Anger under this square runs deep and slow, more magma than spark, and the temptation is total war — or its inverted twin, a defeated numbness.

The square is forging your relationship to power itself. It exposes where your force has been borrowed, performative, or aimed at targets too small, and it teaches — expensively — the difference between intensity and effectiveness. People emerge from this transit either harder and narrower, or genuinely stronger: the fork is whether the fight becomes the identity.

Choose battles by what victory builds, not by what provocation demands; discharge the pressure physically and often, and keep one counselor who is allowed to tell you when you have become the aggressor.
