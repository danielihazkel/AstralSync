title: Transiting Moon square natal Moon
---

For a few hours the current emotional weather runs cross-grain to your baseline needs. You may feel restless in your own routines — the usual comforts taste flat, the usual people slightly grate — without being able to say what would actually help. It is the inner equivalent of a lunar quarter: a small, recurring pinch, not a crisis.

Its usefulness is diagnostic. What irritates you at this monthly touch is often a habit that has quietly stopped feeding you. The risk is snapping at whoever happens to be nearby, or eating your way past the signal.

Rather than fixing anything tonight, just note which comfort failed you; if the same one fails next month, that is worth acting on.
