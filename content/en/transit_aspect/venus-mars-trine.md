title: Transiting Venus trine natal Mars
---

For a few days, attraction flows without forcing. What you want moves toward you at roughly the speed you move toward it — the glance is returned, the collaboration clicks, effort and pleasure stop being opposites. Even mundane pursuits, the errand, the workout, carry a low pleasant hum.

The snag is assuming the current is permanent. When wanting is this frictionless, it's easy to bank nothing — to enjoy being wanted without saying you want back, to let the mutual pull idle because surely it will still be there next month. Trines reward participation; they just never demand it.

Say the pull out loud while it's easy. Tell the person you enjoy them, in words, today. Or ride the aligned energy into something physical you love — dancing, swimming, the long walk — and let your body spend what the sky is handing out.
