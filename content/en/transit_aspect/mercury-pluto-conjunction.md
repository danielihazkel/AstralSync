title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Pluto
---

For a few days, your mind acquires X-ray vision. Small talk feels unbearable; you want the real conversation, the one under the one being had. Research goes deep fast — you can find the buried email, the pattern in the accounts, the sentence in an old argument that explains everything since.

The friction is grip. One thread can swallow the whole day, and questions can start sounding like interrogations to people who have nothing to confess. What you unearth also asks for judgment: not every discovered thing needs to be deployed.

This depth returns about three times a year, so you can afford precision over urgency. Choose the one question that actually matters, ask it plainly of the one person who can answer it, and then — this is the discipline — stop digging and listen.
