title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Moon
---

Feelings run hot this week. Mars sits on your Moon, and moods that usually simmer under the surface come up fast and loud — often at home, often at the people closest to you. Small domestic frictions carry disproportionate charge.

The gift here is honesty. Needs you have swallowed for months finally have enough force behind them to be spoken, and instinct arrives with unusual clarity about what actually bothers you. The hazard is scorching the messenger: family and housemates catch heat that belongs elsewhere, and comfort eating or doomscrolling can masquerade as self-soothing.

Move the energy through your body and your house. Rearrange the room, scrub the kitchen, take the hard walk — then name the real need in one plain sentence while you are calm enough to mean it.
