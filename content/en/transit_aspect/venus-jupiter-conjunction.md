title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a few days each year, Venus crosses your natal Jupiter and turns the volume up on welcome. Invitations multiply, portions grow, and your generosity feels effortless — people sense you are in a mood to say yes, and they arrive with their own yes ready.

The sweetness is real abundance: warmth in company, luck that shows up through other people, a table that seems to set itself. The snag is that everything delicious wants an encore. A second dessert, a third round, one more purchase that felt festive in the moment — Jupiter rarely signals when enough has arrived.

Host something small before the transit passes. One good bottle shared beats three saved, and the toast you make now will be quoted back to you later.
