title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Mars
---

For a few hours courage comes at a discount. There is a modest, usable current of get-up-and-go: enough to make the slightly awkward phone call, open the tricky email thread, or begin the task whose first step is the hard part. Assertion feels like plain speech rather than confrontation, which is exactly when it works best.

Modest is the operative word. This weather will not carry a marathon, and if you ignore it entirely it drains away into fidgeting or one more episode of something.

Spend it on a single decisive first move — starting is the whole gift here, and momentum can take it from there.
