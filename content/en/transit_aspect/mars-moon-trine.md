title: Transiting Mars trine natal Moon
---

Gut and engine agree this week. What you feel like doing and what would actually be good for you overlap more than usual, so acting on instinct carries less risk than it normally does. Emotional stamina runs high; things that would ordinarily drain you merely tire you.

The power is embodied confidence — cooking, training, caretaking, and hands-on work all go unusually well, and people find your directness reassuring rather than sharp. The hazard is spending the whole window on maintenance: pleasant momentum can dissolve into a very productive week of nothing important.

Direct the ease at one thing with emotional weight. Host the gathering, start the fitness habit you actually enjoy, have the frank talk that hard weeks make impossible. Comfort and courage rarely coincide; use the overlap.
