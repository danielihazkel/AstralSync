title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Pluto
---

Venus sextiles your natal Pluto for a few days, and depth becomes approachable. The real conversation — the one about money, grief, desire, or what actually happened — can be had now without the room going dark. People are willing to be honest with you because you are visibly able to bear it.

The sweetness is intimacy at a workable temperature: loyalty affirmed, an old knot loosened, a friendship quietly upgraded from pleasant to trusted. The snag is subtle — knowing people's soft points this clearly is a power, and even gentle steering of someone by their tender places leaves a residue.

Open one honest door. Ask the question you have been circling, or answer one you have been dodging, and let whatever is said stay unweaponized, permanently.
