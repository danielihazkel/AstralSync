title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Mercury
---

Over the better part of a year, in several passes, your mind gets rewired. Ideas arrive like static shocks — sideways, fully formed, indifferent to schedule — and subjects you had filed as settled reopen with new evidence. Many people change how they work under this transit: new tools, new fields, new circles whose conversation runs faster than the old one.

The brilliance is real and so is the noise. Insight and impulse arrive on the same channel, and this transit does not label which is which; you can revolutionize a project on Tuesday and blow up a workable plan on Thursday with equal conviction. Attention scatters, sleep may thin, and patience for slow explanations drops toward zero.

Keep an idea log and let entries age two weeks before you act on them — the genuine breakthroughs survive storage; the static does not.
