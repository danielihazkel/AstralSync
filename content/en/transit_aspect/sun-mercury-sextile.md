title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Mercury
---

For a couple of days the Sun sextiles your natal Mercury, and communication runs on greased rails. Emails get answered, meetings end early, misunderstandings untangle with one clarifying sentence. Learning is quicker too; material that resisted you last week goes in cleanly now.

Nothing about the window shouts, which is why it usually passes unnoticed — a pleasant, forgettable Tuesday, twice a year.

Aim the fluency at a backlog. Clear the correspondence you have been dodging, outline the document that never gets started, or ask the question you felt too slow to phrase. Small verbal debts are cheapest to pay this week.
