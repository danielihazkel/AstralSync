title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Pluto
---

For a week or two, Mars faces your natal Pluto across the axis, and power struggles come looking for you. Someone pushes — a boss tightening control, a partner testing a boundary, a stranger who simply will not yield — and something old and unbending in you pushes back. Stakes inflate: it stops being about the parking spot or the deadline and becomes about who can make whom move.

The power is that pressure reveals structure. You learn precisely where your boundaries actually are, and standing calmly at one of them, without theatrics, can settle a question that years of accommodation never did. The hazard is the death-grip: refusing to lose a point that does not matter, or meeting manipulation with manipulation until nobody remembers the original issue.

Sort your battles into two piles — boundary or preference. Defend the boundary in plain words, once. Release every preference visibly, and spend the freed-up force on hard exercise or hard work.
