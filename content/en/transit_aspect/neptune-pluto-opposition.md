title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Pluto
---

For a year or more, through multiple exact passes, Neptune opposes your natal Pluto, and your relationship to power gets renegotiated through other people. Figures of intensity, the controller, the mesmerist, the wounded healer, the cause that demands everything, appear across from you wrapped in glamour, and something old in you either grips harder or longs to dissolve into them entirely.

The opening is real: seen at this distance, your own survival patterns become visible in a way they never are from inside, and compassion can finally reach the part of you that learned to control because it was once unsafe not to. The deception risk is proportionally deep, idealizing someone whose power is actually appetite, ceding your will in the name of devotion, or fighting shadowy battles with people who are mostly mirrors.

Keep your sovereignty while the boundary blurs. Before merging money, secrets, or loyalty with anyone this year, wait a full season, keep independent counsel, and hold a daily grounding habit, breath, gravity, plain food, that reminds your body where you end.
