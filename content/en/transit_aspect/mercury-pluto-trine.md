title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Pluto
---

For a few days, depth comes without drama. You can name a difficult truth in a steady voice, and people confide in you — the seatmate, the client, the cousin — as if you were wearing a sign. Research, therapy, editing, and any work of finding-what-is-really-there flows.

The friction is subtle: being trusted with the levers. When you understand people this well, persuasion gets easy, and easy persuasion is worth double-checking. Make sure the outcome you are steering toward is one the other person would choose with full information.

Use the window to finish an excavation. Reread the hardest document in your life right now — the diagnosis, the settlement, the old fight in text messages — and write the one-sentence version of what it means. Naming it is how it loses its weight.
