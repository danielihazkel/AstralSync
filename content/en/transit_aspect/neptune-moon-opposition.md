title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Moon
---

Through the months this opposition remains active, confusion enters through your closest bonds. Someone you rely on may become elusive, idealized, or quietly deceptive; equally, you may be the one projecting — reading rescue or betrayal into a person who is simply human. Caretaking can blur into martyrdom, and requests for support may come from people whose needs are bottomless by design.

The transit is teaching discernment in the one domain where you least want to practice it: among people you love. Its gift is a subtler heart, capable of compassion without fusion. Its failure mode is the boundary collapse — covering for someone's problem, funding someone's fantasy, or abandoning your own needs so gradually you never notice the transaction.

When someone's story tugs at you this year, help in ways that are concrete, bounded, and verifiable — a ride, a meal, a specific sum — and let vagueness be your signal to pause.
