title: Transiting Neptune square natal Saturn
---

For a year or more, including retrograde returns to the exact degree, Neptune squares your natal Saturn and erodes the ground you stand on. Plans that once felt certain develop leaks. Authorities disappoint, timelines blur, and the rules you have lived by can seem suddenly hollow, leaving a low hum of anxiety that is hard to attach to any single cause.

Underneath the discomfort is a necessary question: which of your structures are load-bearing, and which are just old fear wearing a suit? The square dissolves false certainty so that something truer can be founded on meaning rather than obligation. The dangers are real, though: escapism dressed as flexibility, quitting solid commitments in a fog of vague dissatisfaction, or exhausting yourself trying to re-solidify what is meant to soften.

Do not make irreversible exits while the fog is thick. Hold your key commitments lightly but hold them, and steady yourself with one physical daily practice, walking, stretching, cooking, that puts your body back in charge of the ground.
