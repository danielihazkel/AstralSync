title: Transiting Pluto square natal Sun
---

For a year or more, with retrograde returns, this square applies pressure to your will like depth applies pressure to a hull. Power struggles find you — with bosses, institutions, a partner, or your own compulsions — and ambitions you thought were settled reveal obsessive undercurrents. Efforts to simply push harder tend to meet immovable resistance, which is the transit's signature.

The purpose under the pressure is purification of intent. Goals pursued for approval, revenge, or inherited expectation start to rot in your hands, while aims rooted in something authentic gain ferocity. People who let this period burn off false ambition come out concentrated and formidable. Those who fight every battle as if survival were at stake exhaust themselves and scorch their alliances.

When you find yourself locked in a contest this year, ask what winning would actually get you. Fight only where the answer survives that question, and yield everywhere else without shame.
