title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Jupiter
---

Across a year or more of slow passes, Neptune trines your natal Jupiter and the current runs with you. Faith comes easily now, not as doctrine but as atmosphere: a settled sense that life is larger and kinder than your worries suggest. Creative work deepens, compassion widens, and doors tend to open through imagination, teaching, travel, or spiritual community rather than through effort.

The trine's danger is its comfort. With everything flowing, you may float on good feeling instead of building with it, or assume that because your optimism is genuine, every optimistic idea is sound. Grand plans still need arithmetic; generous impulses still need boundaries. Ease can quietly become drift, and a year of inspiration can evaporate without leaving anything behind.

Anchor the gift while it is here. Choose one meaningful project and give it a fixed weekly appointment, treating the inspired mood as fuel for scheduled work rather than a substitute for it.
