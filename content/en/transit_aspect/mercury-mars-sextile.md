title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Mars
---

Thinking and doing cooperate for a few days. The sextile from transiting Mercury to your Mars gives words traction: the plan you talk through actually gets started, the errand list actually shrinks, and asking directly for what you want feels less like a confrontation and more like efficiency.

The soft spot is that this energy rewards whoever engages it. Left alone, the transit passes as a mildly productive stretch you barely notice — a few emails, some tidying, nothing advanced.

Choose the request you have been rehearsing and make it out loud: the raise conversation, the deadline extension, the favor with a due date. Direct beats elaborate this week. One sentence of context, one clear ask, one proposed next step. Then convert one lingering "we should really" into a booked slot on an actual calendar.
