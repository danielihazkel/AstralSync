title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Uranus
---

Once every two years or so, Mars spends a week or two on your natal Uranus, and your tolerance for the usual drops to zero. Routines you have endured for months suddenly feel unbearable. Impulses arrive fully formed: quit, leave, cut it off, try the wild version. Your reflexes are quick, your patience is not.

The genuine gift is the liberating break — this transit can snap a chain in an afternoon that you have been filing at for a year. The hazard is the accident-by-haste: the burned bridge, the slipped knife, the reply-all sent at speed. Electricity does not distinguish between freeing you and shocking you.

Give the voltage a safe conductor. Experiment where the downside is small — a prototype, a new route, a radical draft — and impose one deliberate pause: twenty-four hours between any irreversible impulse and the send button. Mind your hands, your speed, and hot tools.
