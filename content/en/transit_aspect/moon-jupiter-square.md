title: Transiting Moon square natal Jupiter
---

For a few hours each month, your appetite for more chafes against the day you actually have. The Moon squares your natal Jupiter and everything on the calendar suddenly feels cut too small — the desk, the town, the to-do list.

The gift hides inside the itch: this restlessness points, fairly precisely, at where your life could genuinely use more room. The snag is how you spend it — impulse purchases, grand pronouncements, cancelling solid commitments in a fit of wanderlust that will have passed by dinner.

Don't rebuild your life in these hours; take notes instead. Write down exactly what pinched. If the same pinch shows up at this monthly touch again and again, that's not weather anymore — that's information.
