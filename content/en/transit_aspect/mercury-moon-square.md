title: Transiting Mercury square natal Moon
---

Head and heart file separate reports for a couple of days. Transiting Mercury square your Moon can make you argue logic when the real subject is a feeling, or snap over a scheduling detail when the actual grievance is older and softer. Practical talk — chores, plans, who said what — carries more static than the content deserves.

The gift inside the friction is diagnostic: whatever topic keeps going sideways is pointing at something unspoken underneath it. Irritation is information here, not a verdict on the other person.

Before replying to the message that stung, name the feeling to yourself in one word. In the conversation, trade a complaint for a statement: "I felt overlooked when the plan changed" gets further today than a review of the timeline. Double-check dates before you commit to anything.
