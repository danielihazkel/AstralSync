title: Transiting Pluto square natal Pluto
---

Depending on the eccentric rhythm of Pluto's orbit and the cohort you were born into, this square arrives anywhere across the long middle of life, and when it does, it is the great mid-life power reckoning. Over roughly two years and several exact passes, everything you have built your sense of potency on, career leverage, physical stamina, control over how life unfolds, is weighed by a force that does not flatter. Mortality stops being theoretical. Compulsions you thought you had outgrown resurface to show you what still owns you.

The regeneration offered is immense: shedding the false powers, borrowed ambitions, and armored identities of the first half of life, and locating the authority that survives their loss. The compulsive route is to grip, dominating at work, controlling loved ones, chasing potency substitutes, and to call the gripping strength.

Whatever this period takes, let it teach you what cannot be taken, and reorganize your life around that.
