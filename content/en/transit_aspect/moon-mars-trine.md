title: Transiting Moon trine natal Mars
---

Drive and mood pull in harness for a few hours. Effort feels good instead of grim: exercise flows, work has traction, and you can stand up for yourself so smoothly that nobody experiences it as a fight. Confidence sits in the body rather than the ego, which makes this some of the month's best weather for doing rather than deliberating.

The quiet risk is overreach. Because nothing resists you, it is easy to commit tonight's easy strength to next week's calendar — signing up for more than ordinary days can carry.

Use the surge on something finishable today, and notice how far you get without forcing anything; that is your natural pace, worth remembering.
