title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Jupiter
---

Over the course of a year or more, with several exact passes, Neptune forms a quiet, supportive angle to your natal Jupiter. This is a gentle tide rather than a flood: your beliefs soften at the edges, your imagination grows more generous, and opportunities tend to arrive through compassion, art, or shared ideals rather than through pushing.

Because the sextile whispers instead of insists, its opening is easy to waste. The invitation is toward inspired growth with your feet still on the ground; the drift is toward pleasant vagueness, where you feel expansively hopeful about plans you never quite start, or extend faith to people and schemes that have not earned it.

Treat this as a season for apprenticing your dreams. Pick one aspiration that keeps resurfacing and give it a small, scheduled, weekly practice, so the inspiration has somewhere concrete to land each time it returns.
