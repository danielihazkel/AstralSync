title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mars
---

For a couple of years, concentrated power is available in civilized doses. Efforts you choose to intensify respond dramatically — the training program compounds, the business initiative finds leverage, the difficult reform you champion actually moves. Authority tends to accrue to you quietly, because you visibly finish what you start and do not waste force on display.

The sextile supplies capacity, not direction, and unclaimed capacity simply idles. The deeper workout, the harder negotiation, the ambitious rebuild of how you work — all remain available and optional, and choosing comfort forfeits them without ceremony. The only notable hazard is small: strength exercised only in low-stakes arenas can breed a private conviction of power that has never been tested.

Pick one domain to genuinely transform this season — body, work, finances — and apply steady, escalating pressure there until the change is structural.
