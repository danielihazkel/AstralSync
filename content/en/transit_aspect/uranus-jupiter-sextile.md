title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Jupiter
---

Across a year or more, including a retrograde revisit or two, Uranus feeds your natal Jupiter a steady current of interesting accidents: a stranger who mentions the right name, a course that opens a field you had not considered, a small experiment that outperforms the plan. This is one of the friendlier windows for growth through novelty, but a sextile only pays when you move toward it.

The breakthrough on offer is a wider life reached by an unconventional route; the risk is scattering, saying a shallow yes to six opportunities and finishing none, or mistaking restlessness for vision. Luck here favors the person already in motion, not the one waiting to be discovered.

Pick the one unusual invitation that genuinely stretches you, act on it this season, and let the merely novel pass by.
