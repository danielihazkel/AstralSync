title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Saturn
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter sextiles your natal Saturn and growth cooperates with structure instead of straining it. Plans that need patience find sponsors; senior people take your ambitions seriously; the unglamorous groundwork you have been doing quietly becomes an asset someone wants to build on.

Because the aspect is gentle, its opportunity is easy to miss. The risk is not overreach but underreach: staying safely inside routines while a period made for measured expansion — a certification, a proposal, a raise conversation — passes without a single ask.

Make one concrete, well-prepared request of an authority figure this month, and let your track record do the persuading.
