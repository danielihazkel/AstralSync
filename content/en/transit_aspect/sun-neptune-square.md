title: Transiting Sun square natal Neptune
---

Twice a year the Sun rubs against your natal Neptune at a hard angle, and for a few days your clarity leaks. Motivation goes soft, instructions come out garbled, and you may feel a vague disappointment you cannot attach to anything specific — as if the week were slightly out of focus.

The friction is confusion and its cousins: escapism, small deceptions, the attractive shortcut that is not quite honest, the extra glass that is not quite necessary. Whatever you idealize — a person, a plan, your own stamina — gets tested against fact, and the gap can ache.

The opportunity is a truer picture. This annual blur exposes where you have been squinting on purpose, and one honest look now saves months of drift in a long-running illusion.

Double-check times, numbers, and promises before sending anything, and name to yourself the one thing you have been preferring not to see.
