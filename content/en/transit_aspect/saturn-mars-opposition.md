title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Mars
---

For several months your initiative answers to an opposing authority: the boss who slows the project, the regulation that blocks the move, the senior rival whose caution reads as obstruction. Every push meets a policy. The characteristic mood is a simmering, procedural anger — you are not forbidden to act, just required to justify each step to someone unhurried.

The opposition trains timing and legitimacy. Acting rashly against this counterweight loses on the counterweight's terms; the wins available now go to whoever masters the rules better than the rule-keepers, builds the case before making the move, and chooses confrontations where the paperwork is already on their side. Some of the resistance, examined honestly, is your own discipline arriving from outside because it was not yet installed within.

Pick the one battle that matters this season, prepare it to institutional standard, and let the others pass; a single authorized victory outperforms a year of skirmishes.
