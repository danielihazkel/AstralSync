title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Mars
---

Words pick up an edge for a day or two. Transiting Mercury on your Mars turns thought into speech with almost no buffer: decisions come fast, arguments come armed, and the reply you would normally sand down for an hour goes out in ninety seconds. In a debate, a pitch, or a deadline sprint, this is a genuine advantage.

The same wiring sends the message you cannot unsend. Sarcasm reads as attack today; a "quick question" can arrive like a summons. And speed can masquerade as certainty — a fast decision is not automatically a considered one.

Point the blade at tasks, not people. Knock out the backlog of small decisions you have been circling. For anything addressed to a human, draft hot, then wait twenty minutes before sending — and delete the last sentence, which is usually the jab.
