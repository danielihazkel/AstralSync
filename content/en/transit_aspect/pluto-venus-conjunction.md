title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Venus
---

Across two to three years, love stops being decorative. An attraction may arrive with a gravity you have not felt before — consuming, fated-seeming, indifferent to convenience — or an existing bond descends through its polite layers into the rooms where power, jealousy, and real need live. Casual connection becomes almost impossible; whatever you love now, you love with the volume all the way up.

This is the transit under which people discover what they actually require from intimacy, usually by colliding with it. Relationships either deepen into something unprecedented or reveal a foundation that cannot bear weight; both outcomes are the transit working. The hazards are possession mistaken for passion, tests administered instead of trust extended, and the slow conversion of desire into control.

When intensity surges, ask what it is trying to protect; loving fiercely and holding lightly are compatible, but only on purpose.
