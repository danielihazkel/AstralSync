title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Neptune
---

Across a year or more, with the usual retrograde returns, Uranus feeds your natal Neptune a clean current: intuition sharpens, synchronicities multiply, and the imaginative life gets reliably interesting without getting destabilizing. Ideas that once seemed too strange or too idealistic now find footholds, an artistic experiment lands, a contemplative practice deepens, technology and imagination cooperate instead of quarreling.

The breakthrough is an awakened but grounded idealism, faith refreshed by direct experience rather than borrowed belief, and creative work that carries a signature only you could produce. The soft risk of the trine is pleasant passivity: enjoying the glow, narrating the insights, and building nothing, or floating past practical duties because the inner weather is so good.

Anchor this period with one regular practice, creative or contemplative, and let the rest of the inspiration organize itself around that keel.
