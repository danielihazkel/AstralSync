title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Neptune
---

Venus opposes your natal Neptune for a few days, and someone across the table wears borrowed light. You may idealize them — the mentor, the new interest, the friend going through something — or discover you are the screen someone else is projecting onto, adored for a self you do not recognize.

The sweetness is genuine enchantment: sympathy runs deep both directions, and being moved by another person is nothing to cure. The snag is the exchange rate — rescuing, being rescued, and mistaking either for intimacy; the sacrifice that was never requested and will later be invoiced.

Enjoy the shimmer and keep one hand on the actual person. Ask them something mundane and delight in the ordinary answer. Compassion that survives the mundane is the real thing; note where yours does.
