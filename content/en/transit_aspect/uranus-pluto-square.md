title: Transiting Uranus square natal Pluto
---

For a year or more, with multiple exact passes, Uranus grinds against your natal Pluto, and pressure builds along your oldest fault lines. Control strategies that have worked since childhood, holding on, staying silent, managing everyone, start failing loudly. Power struggles flare at work or at home, and your own reactions can surprise you with their intensity, as if something long compressed has found a vent.

The breakthrough is a genuine jailbreak: naming the compulsion, quitting the manipulation game from either side, and reclaiming force you had spent on containment. People often exit this square visibly freer. The risk is the eruption itself, ultimatums, purges, burned alliances, or a rebellion so total it hands the wreckage to the very pattern you meant to escape.

Vent the pressure in small honest increments, early and often, so nothing has to explode to be heard.
