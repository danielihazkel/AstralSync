title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Pluto
---

For several months, likely with a retrograde revisit, Saturn opposes your natal Pluto, and questions of power play out face to face. The control strategies your generation stamped into you meet an external authority that will not blink — an employer, a bureaucracy, a creditor, a person with real leverage over your circumstances. The confrontation tends to surface old material: whatever you learned early about domination and survival reappears wearing a current face.

The danger of the season is entrenchment, a standoff in which both sides spend everything on not losing, and the collateral damage lands on the life around the battle. The gift is an accurate map. Oppositions show you exactly where your power ends and someone else's begins — knowledge most people only buy through defeat — and settlements negotiated from that clarity tend to be both fair and final. You cannot control the other side; you can control your terms.

Name the fight you are in, decide what outcome is actually worth its cost, and negotiate for that instead of for victory.
