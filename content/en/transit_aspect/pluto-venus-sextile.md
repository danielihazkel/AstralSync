title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Venus
---

For a couple of years, depth in relationships is available on gentle terms. Conversations willingly go beneath the usual floor; loyalties clarify themselves without crisis; and you can renegotiate an old pattern — the chronic imbalance, the unspoken ledger — with less resistance than it would cost in any other season. Attractions formed now tend to be substantial rather than merely bright.

As always with sextiles, the invitation expires unused. Intimacy can stay at its comfortable depth all season, values can go unexamined, and the offered transformations — of a relationship, of your relationship to money and worth — remain brochures. The only real friction is the opportunity cost of politeness.

Initiate one deepening on purpose: the conversation beneath the conversation, the financial truth finally shared, the friendship allowed to matter as much as it already does.
