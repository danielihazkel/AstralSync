title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Sun
---

Once in a lifetime, Uranus crosses your Sun, and for the year or so it stays in range — retrogrades included — the person you have been agreeing to be comes up for renegotiation. Restlessness spikes. Roles that fit last year now pinch. You may shock people who thought they knew you, or life may do the shocking for you, clearing a path you would never have chosen but secretly wanted.

The energy is genuinely liberating and genuinely destabilizing, often in the same week. Authentic changes made now — the career pivot, the coming-out, the departure from a script you inherited — tend to stick. The failure mode is burning structures for the thrill of the flame, then standing in the ashes wondering what the insight was.

Before each dramatic move this year, ask whether you are running toward a truer version of yourself or just away from boredom. Act decisively on the first; sit with the second.
