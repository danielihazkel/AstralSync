title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Mars
---

For several months your drive runs against a governor. Projects advance at half the expected speed, authority looks over your shoulder, and the direct route — the one that has always worked — is precisely the one that is blocked. Energy that cannot move forward tends to turn inward as frustration or a bone-deep tiredness that sleep does not fix.

What Saturn is doing here is converting raw force into skill. Athletes call it strength under load: effort applied against resistance builds capacity that free movement never does. The seasons of your life when you learned discipline, timing, and the difference between intensity and effectiveness mostly look like this one from the inside. The danger is rage at the governor — or quitting the race because the pace was insulting.

Choose one long objective, cut your daily effort to a sustainable dose, and repeat it without negotiation; what you build under this weight, you keep.
