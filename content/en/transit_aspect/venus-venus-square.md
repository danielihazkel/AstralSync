For a few days, your own taste chafes against itself. The jacket you loved looks wrong on the hook, the playlist grates, the person you adore does the thing they always do and today it lands differently. Nothing is broken; you're just seeing your preferences from an unflattering angle, and the itch says buy something, change something, fix it now.

The sweetness hidden here is discernment waking up — dissatisfaction is taste sharpening. The snag is treating a vague ache with a specific purchase. The new lamp will not resolve a mood; it will just arrive after the mood has left, slightly embarrassing.

Put the thing in the cart and leave it there for a week. Write down what actually feels off instead — one honest sentence. If the want survives the wait, it was taste. If not, it was weather.
