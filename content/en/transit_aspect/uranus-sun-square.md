title: Transiting Uranus square natal Sun
---

For the better part of a year, on and off with retrogrades, this transit rattles the cage of your established identity. Sudden urges to quit, bolt, or reinvent arrive with startling force, and external disruptions — reorganizations, breakups, technological upheavals in your field — may force flexibility you did not volunteer for. The consistent theme: whatever is rigid in your self-definition gets stress-tested.

The square's intelligence is real. It targets the places where you have traded aliveness for predictability, and the discomfort is proportional to the trade. But its timing is impulsive, and the classic casualty is a good structure destroyed to escape a feeling that would have passed. Freedom seized in a spasm often recreates the old cage with new bars.

When the urge to detonate something arrives, write it down and revisit it in two weeks. What survives the delay deserves action; what evaporates was weather.
