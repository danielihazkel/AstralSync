title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Neptune
---

Once a year the Sun faces your natal Neptune across the full width of the sky, and for a few days other people become screens for your projections. Someone seems more wonderful, more wounded, or more disappointing than they actually are; requests for your help arrive with strings you cannot quite see; a hero wobbles.

The opportunity is disillusionment in its literal, useful sense — the removal of an illusion. This annual check-in on your long romance with an ideal shows you where you have been relating to a picture instead of a person, and what remains after the glow fades is usually sturdier than the glow.

The friction is gullibility and its mirror, cynicism. Both are ways of not looking carefully.

Before agreeing to anything this window, restate the other person's request in plain words and ask if you have it right; the answer will tell you most of what you need.
