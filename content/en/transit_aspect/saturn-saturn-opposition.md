title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Saturn
---

Fourteen to fifteen years from your Saturn return, you stand at the midpoint of the 29.5-year cycle: the accountability review, held at maximum distance from where you started. For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, life presents the results of the structure you chose back then — career, commitments, the shape of your days — and asks, in full view, whether it is actually working.

Because oppositions arrive through other people, the review often comes personified: an employer reassessing your value, a partner naming what the arrangement costs them, a younger colleague holding the ambition you shelved. The friction is exposure; there is nowhere to hide from your own track record. The payoff is perspective you cannot get anywhere else in the cycle — a clear sight line back to the return and forward to the next one, while there is still time to correct course.

Review your central commitment as if you were an outside assessor, and act on the finding you would give someone else.
