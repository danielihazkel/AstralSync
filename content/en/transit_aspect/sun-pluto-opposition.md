title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Pluto
---

Once a year the Sun stands directly opposite your natal Pluto, and for a few days the deep end of a relationship comes into view. Someone with leverage — financial, emotional, institutional — exercises it, or you notice how much of yourself you have handed to another person's keeping. Conversations carry undertow; silences say more than sentences.

The opportunity is to see a long entanglement clearly. Oppositions externalize: the control, dependency, or buried resentment that usually operates offstage briefly performs in daylight, and what is visible can finally be discussed. Some of the most honest exchanges of the year happen in this window.

The friction is escalation and score-keeping. Treating the other person as an opponent guarantees they behave like one.

If a confrontation surfaces, hold your ground on the substance and give ground freely on face; state your one non-negotiable and let everything smaller go.
