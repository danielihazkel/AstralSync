title: Transiting Venus trine natal Saturn
---

For a few days, Venus trines your natal Saturn and love wears its work clothes comfortably. Old friendships feel like well-broken-in tools; commitments feel chosen rather than imposed. Your eye goes to quality — the sturdy, the classic, the thing that will still be good in ten years.

This is a graceful window for decisions with long tails: agreeing on terms, choosing the durable option, formalizing something that has been informal too long. The snag is mildness itself — the transit will not push, so a good moment for a serious conversation can pass in agreeable small talk.

Pick one lasting thing and tend it. Rebook the standing dinner with the oldest friend, oil the wooden handle, or say the committed sentence you have been meaning to say calmly.
