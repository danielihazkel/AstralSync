title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Neptune
---

For a year or more, through several exact passes, Uranus confronts your natal Neptune from across the chart, and the collision tends to arrive embodied: a movement, a mentor, a technology, or a charismatic stranger challenges what you hold sacred, or dangles a transcendence that looks suspiciously convenient. Old ideals and new awakenings face off, and both claim to be the truth.

The breakthrough is discernment earned in public, learning to tell genuine inspiration from glamour, real teachers from performers, your own longing from the crowd's contagion. What survives this opposition is faith you have actually tested. The risk is projection: handing your vision to someone electric and following them off a cliff, or dismissing every new current as a scam and sealing the windows.

Stay curious about what confronts you, verify before you enlist, and keep custody of your own ideals throughout.
