title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Venus
---

Appetite outruns budget under this square — financially, romantically, and calorically. For a few weeks more feels like the answer to everything: more comfort, more company, more of whatever felt good last time. Spending drifts upward in small, defensible increments, and in relationships you may promise a level of warmth or availability that your actual week cannot fund.

The square's useful pressure is that it exposes where your pleasures have quietly become obligations, and where you are using generosity to buy approval rather than express it. The overindulgence is real, but it is also information about a hunger that smaller, steadier portions have not been feeding.

Track what you actually spend — money, evenings, attention — for two weeks, and cut the one indulgence that reliably feels worse afterward, keeping the ones that hold up.
