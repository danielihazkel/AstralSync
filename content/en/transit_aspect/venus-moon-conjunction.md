title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Moon
---

For a few days each year, Venus settles over your Moon and everything domestic turns velvety. The apartment looks better at dusk, the blanket is exactly heavy enough, and the people who care for you seem easier to let in. You want to be tended, and for once asking doesn't feel like weakness.

The snag is that comfort this good can become a substitute. A hard feeling arrives and you feed it pastry, or soothe it with a small online order, and the feeling waits politely underneath. Nothing terrible — just sweetness doing the job of attention.

Cook something slow tonight, something that fills the rooms with smell while it works. Let someone bring you tea without apologizing for needing it. If a mood shows up, name it before you sugar it.
