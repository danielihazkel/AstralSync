title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Pluto
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn sits on your natal Pluto — the place where your generation's relationship to power, survival, and transformation is stamped into you. This transit restructures how you handle control. Situations arise in which your deepest strategies for staying in charge — the vigilance, the leverage, the refusal to depend on anyone — meet a limit that does not negotiate, often through work, institutions, or money.

The pressure can feel tectonic: slow, impersonal, and total. Old power arrangements in your life compress until something either consolidates or cracks — a dynamic with a boss, a debt, a family pattern of who controls whom. The reward for meeting it honestly is formidable: durable authority built on competence rather than grip, and the retirement of survival tactics that were costing more than they protected. The danger is doubling down on control precisely where release is being asked.

Identify the one thing you are white-knuckling, and either commit to it with full structure or set it down deliberately.
