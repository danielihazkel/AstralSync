title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Mercury
---

For a year or more, the imaginative and rational halves of your mind stop competing. Complex feelings become sayable, symbols and stories open easily, and your words acquire a resonance that makes people feel understood rather than merely informed. It is a superb long window for creative work, gentle teaching, and conversations that heal old misreadings.

The trine's hazard is a beautiful passivity. Understanding flows so freely that expressing it can feel redundant — why write the book when the insight is already so satisfying to have? Meaning arrives unearned, and unearned things are easy to leave unbuilt. There is also a mild anesthetic effect: unpleasant facts can be reframed into acceptance a little faster than they should be.

Give the inspiration a container with a deadline — the manuscript, the course, the recorded piece — and let one hard fact stay hard this year instead of dissolving it in perspective.
