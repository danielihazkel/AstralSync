title: Transiting Mercury square natal Venus
---

Taste and tact rub against each other for a couple of days. Under this square, honest feedback comes out blunter than intended, prices and preferences become oddly touchy subjects, and a passing remark about someone's choices — the paint color, the restaurant, the budget — can land as a judgment on the person.

The friction is real, but so is the information underneath it. If a comment about money or style keeps nettling you, there is usually an unspoken agreement in that relationship that needs actual words: who pays, who decides, whose taste wins where.

Delay purchases made mainly to soothe a mood; the cart will keep until the transit passes. If you must give critical feedback on someone's work or taste, separate the two sentences: what works, then what to change. And double-check the invoice before you dispute it.
