title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Pluto
---

A quiet chance to say the deeper thing without the room going cold. For a few days, honesty and tact are unusually compatible: you can raise a loaded subject — money between friends, an old hurt, a power imbalance at work — and have it land as care rather than attack.

The friction is that nothing will make you do it. Sextiles knock softly, and the loaded subject is easy to leave in its drawer for another season. There is also a faint pull toward using your insight strategically — steering people you could simply ask.

Choose asking. Pick the one relationship where something true has gone unsaid, and open with curiosity instead of a verdict: I have been wondering about something between us — can I ask you about it?
