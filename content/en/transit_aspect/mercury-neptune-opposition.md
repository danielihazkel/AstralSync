title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Neptune
---

For a few days, other people are hard to bring into focus. Someone's story does not quite add up, a colleague's enthusiasm may be sincere or may be sales, and you cannot tell whether you are perceiving them accurately or projecting a fog of your own. Meanwhile, they may be idealizing or misreading you in return.

The opportunity is unusual receptivity: you can hear the longing underneath what people say, which is often the truest part. The friction is that longing and fact wear the same clothes this week, and decisions made on vibes alone tend to age poorly.

Split the channel. Listen generously in the room, then verify separately: ask for the details in writing, sleep on any pitch, and before agreeing, ask one clarifying question you feel slightly awkward asking.
