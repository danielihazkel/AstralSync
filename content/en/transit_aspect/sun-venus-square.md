title: Transiting Sun square natal Venus
---

The Sun squares your natal Venus, and for a few days wanting and doing pull apart. Your agenda says produce; your appetite says browse, buy, linger, be adored. Purchases made now tend to be mood repair in disguise, and small social slights can feel disproportionately pointed.

Underneath the friction is useful information: whatever you crave this week marks a pleasure your regular routine has been rationing too hard.

Delay any purchase over a modest threshold by three days — past the transit's edge — and instead schedule the cheap version of the craving: the walk, the playlist, the meal cooked properly. Feed the want without billing your future.
