title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Mars
---

This is your Mars return — roughly every two years, transiting Mars comes home to the degree it held at your birth, and your engine turns over on a fresh cycle. Whatever you commit your drive to during this week or two tends to set the tone for the next two years of effort, appetite, and fight.

The power is a clean slate for ambition: energy runs high and unusually pure, uncolored by other agendas. The hazard is spending the ignition on whatever happens to be irritating you that day — a return squandered on a quarrel is still a return spent.

Treat it like a launch date. Name the one campaign — physical, professional, personal — that deserves two years of your fire, take its first irreversible step now, and write down what "done" looks like.
