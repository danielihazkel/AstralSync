title: Transiting Sun trine natal Uranus
---

Twice a year the Sun makes an easy angle to your natal Uranus, and originality flows without a fight. For a few days your odd ideas land well: the unconventional suggestion gets adopted in the meeting, the shortcut works, the thing you do differently from everyone else briefly looks like genius instead of stubbornness.

The opportunity is to advance your independence with everyone smiling. Ask for the flexible arrangement, publish the weird project, automate the tedious task — changes proposed now meet unusually little resistance, and this annual window rewards those who arrive with something prepared.

The friction is that ease breeds noncommitment. Inspiration under a trine is plentiful and cheap, and it evaporates just as cheaply if nothing is captured.

Before the window closes, take your best unusual idea and give it a fixed form — a document, a prototype, a sent proposal — that will still exist next week.
