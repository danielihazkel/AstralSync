title: Transiting Sun trine natal Sun
---

About four months from your birthday in either direction, the transiting Sun trines its natal place. Vitality runs smooth: your body cooperates, confidence feels unforced, and people respond to you as the person you mean to be. It is one of the easiest few days of your personal year.

The shadow of a trine is drift. When everything works, nothing demands review, and momentum can carry you comfortably in a direction you never quite chose.

Use the ease deliberately. Tackle the task you have been avoiding while your energy is at its most forgiving, or have the conversation that would feel heavier in a harder week.
