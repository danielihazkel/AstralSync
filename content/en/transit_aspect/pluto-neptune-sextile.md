title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Neptune
---

For a couple of years, through several exact passes, Pluto sextiles your natal Neptune and offers a gentle composting of your ideals. Beliefs and dreams you have carried since youth can be turned over quietly, the sentimental parts breaking down, the living parts feeding something sturdier. Meditation, art, service, or therapy done in this period tends to go unusually deep, and compassion starts to come with clearer boundaries.

The empowerment is a faith that matures without crisis: you lose some naivety about people, causes, and your own fantasies, and gain a discernment that makes devotion safer to give. The risk stays subtle, drifting into comfortable vagueness, or savoring intense inner experiences until the pursuit of depth itself becomes a soft compulsion that postpones ordinary life.

Give one ideal a concrete body this year, a practice, a commitment, a scheduled act of service, and let the rest stay weather.
