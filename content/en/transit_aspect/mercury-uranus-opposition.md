title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Uranus
---

For a few days, other people are the plot twist. Someone contradicts your carefully reasoned position with an idea that is either brilliant or unhinged — possibly both — and ordinary exchanges keep veering off script. Group chats, comment threads, and team calls all run hotter and weirder than usual.

The opportunity is genuine cross-pollination: the view from the far side of the table contains the piece your thinking is missing. The friction is contrarianism for sport, yours or theirs, where positions get taken just to feel electric and abandoned by Friday.

Test before you adopt or dismiss. Take the strangest suggestion you hear and steelman it in writing for one paragraph. Then ask its author the grounding question: what would we see in a week if you were right?
