title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Neptune
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto opposes your natal Neptune and your ideals are composted through encounters. A charismatic figure, a movement, or a suffering person carries your dream for a while, and the relationship gradually reveals what the dream was made of: how much genuine devotion, how much longing to be rescued or to rescue, how much refusal to see clearly.

The empowerment is discernment earned in relationship, faith that survives seeing the other whole, compassion that no longer requires illusion, and the recovery of imaginative power you had handed to someone else. The risk is enthrallment or its mirror: obsession with an idealized other, martyrdom to a cause, or a cynical crusade against everything you once found sacred.

When someone begins to glow with impossible promise, slow down, verify, and keep your practical commitments intact while the enchantment clarifies into truth.
