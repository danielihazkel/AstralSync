title: Transiting Sun trine natal Mars
---

With the Sun trine your natal Mars, strength and steadiness arrive together. You can work long without fraying, assert yourself without bristling, and take a calculated risk with your judgment intact. Athletic efforts, launches, and difficult first moves all benefit from these few days.

The quiet danger is undershooting. Because nothing is straining, you may not notice how much extra capacity you have, and spend a rare high-output window on routine maintenance.

Pick the boldest reasonable item on your list — the ask, the entry, the attempt — and take a real run at it now. This much power with this little static comes around only twice a year.
