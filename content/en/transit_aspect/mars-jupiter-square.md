title: Transiting Mars square natal Jupiter
---

For a week or two, Mars picks a fight with your natal Jupiter, and the result feels like a foot on the gas with no map. You want more — more scope, more speed, more territory — and you want it now. Irritation flares at anyone who counsels patience.

Used well, this friction is a growth spurt: it exposes exactly where your current life is too small and gives you the aggression to do something about it. Used badly, it is the classic overreach transit — the impulse purchase, the grand promise, the argument about principles that is really about restlessness.

Before you commit to anything bigger, double the estimate of what it costs in time and money. Then spend the surplus energy physically: train harder, walk farther, build something with your hands while the appetite burns off.
