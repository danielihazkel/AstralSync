title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Pluto
---

Across a year or more, with the usual retrograde returns, Uranus and your natal Pluto cooperate, and transformation loses its usual tax. Old patterns release with strange ease: a grudge you carried for a decade simply sets down, an inherited fear stops running the meeting, power flows to you through change rather than resistance. Reinventions attempted now tend to reach bedrock instead of repainting the surface.

The breakthrough is regeneration on your own initiative, shedding an outlived identity, taking real authority in your work, converting old wounds into competence. Because nothing forces the issue, the risk is leaving the depths untouched while enjoying the surface weather, or wielding your newly fluid influence carelessly, forgetting that ease for you can still be upheaval for others.

Use this window to finish one deep piece of unfinished business, and be generous with the power that follows.
