title: Transiting Pluto square natal Venus
---

Across two to three years, in repeated passes, your attachments are subjected to pressure that finds every fault line. Jealousy, financial entanglement, a triangle, an obsession, a partner's transformation you did not order — some agent of depth intrudes on what was comfortable, and the relationship question stops being "are we happy?" and becomes "what is actually holding us together?"

The square excavates the deals beneath your bonds: affection traded for security, desirability used as currency, closeness maintained by managing another person. Whatever was load-bearing and unspoken gets spoken, or breaks. This is brutal and clarifying in equal measure — many durable intimacies date their real beginning from this transit, and many merciful endings too. The hazard is fighting for control of the process instead of letting the truth restructure it.

When you feel the grip — yours or theirs — name the fear beneath it out loud; what survives honest naming is yours to keep.
