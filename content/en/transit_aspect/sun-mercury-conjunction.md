title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Mercury
---

The Sun crosses your natal Mercury once a year, and your ideas step into the light. Words come faster, plans crystallize, and people are unusually willing to hear you out. If you have been sitting on a proposal, a pitch, or a difficult email, this window gives it maximum visibility.

Visibility is also the hazard. Everything you say now carries extra wattage, including the careless remark, and enthusiasm can talk over the very people you need to persuade.

Ship one piece of thinking while the light is on — send the memo, book the meeting, publish the post — and let listening take up half of every conversation you start.
