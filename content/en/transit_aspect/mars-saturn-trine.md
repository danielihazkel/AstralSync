title: Transiting Mars trine natal Saturn
---

Twice in its two-year round, Mars trines your natal Saturn for roughly a week, and you get the rare combination of hot engine and steady hands. Energy arrives pre-disciplined: you can sustain effort for hours, estimate honestly, and say no without drama. Authority figures read you as reliable, because right now you are.

This is master-craftsman weather. The power is finishing — clearing backlogs, executing the plan exactly as written, doing precise physical work without injury. The hazard is smallness: it is easy to spend the whole window polishing what already works and never touch the harder structure that actually needs building.

Aim the steadiness at something with a deadline and a load-bearing role in your life. Negotiate the long-term commitment, pour the foundation, formalize the agreement. Then close the week by writing down the routine that made it work, so you can rebuild it later on demand.
