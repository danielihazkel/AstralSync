title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Venus
---

For a year or more, love is refracted through a finer and less reliable lens. Beauty affects you physically, art you make or witness carries unusual depth, and romance — new or long-standing — acquires a devotional quality, as if the person in front of you were also a window onto something larger. Compassion widens; so does the longing for a love without edges.

The refraction is the risk. You are partly in love with what you can imagine, and this transit makes imagining effortless: the new acquaintance becomes a soulmate before the second coffee, the struggling partner becomes a project, the unavailable one becomes a quest. Real people, offered these roles, eventually decline them — and the disappointment belongs to the lens, not the person.

Enjoy the enchantment as art, and test it as evidence: keep dates concrete, promises small, and give any major romantic or financial decision a sober second reading in daylight.
