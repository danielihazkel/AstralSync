title: Transiting Moon square natal Saturn
---

For a few hours each month, the Moon squares your natal Saturn and you feel the wall. Blocked, judged, behind schedule — the inner critic finds a microphone, and whatever obligation you resent most starts throbbing.

The gift is precision: the heaviness lands exactly on the duty or standard you've been quietly fighting, which is worth knowing. The snag is what you do while it hurts — snapping at whoever happens to stand near the obstacle, or making a dramatic decision from a mood that will lift by evening.

Press nothing send-shaped. Instead, name the wall in one plain sentence: what exactly feels blocked. If your journal shows the same wall at this touch month after month, that's a structure, not a feeling.
