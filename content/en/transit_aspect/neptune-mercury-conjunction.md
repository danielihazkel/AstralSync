title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Mercury
---

For a year or more, the boundary between thinking and imagining thins. Intuition improves dramatically — you read rooms, subtexts, and unspoken moods with new accuracy — and creative or spiritual material that once seemed opaque begins to speak. Writers, artists, and anyone who works with images or empathy often does career-defining work under this transit.

The same permeability degrades ordinary precision. Details slip, appointments blur, and wishful listening becomes a genuine hazard: you hear what was meant, or what you hoped, rather than what was said. This is the classic season for signing things misread and trusting explanations that were mostly atmosphere. The fog is not a malfunction — it is the price of the open channel — but contracts do not accept poetry.

Route all facts through paper this year: write agreements down, confirm plans twice, and reserve your intuition for what it is newly brilliant at — people, art, and meaning.
