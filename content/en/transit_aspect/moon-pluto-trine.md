title: Transiting Moon trine natal Pluto
---

Each month the Moon trines your natal Pluto and, for a few hours, you get depth without drama — access to your own basement with the lights on. Difficult truths feel speakable, intimacy feels safe, and something you've been gripping is suddenly willing to be released.

The gift is easeful honesty: conversations can go one level deeper than usual and land softly, and private reckonings settle without the usual bracing. The snag is smoothness itself. Because nothing aches, it's easy to stay upstairs and never use the open door.

Have the slightly deeper conversation you'd normally defer — one true sentence past your usual stopping point. Or write it, if no one's around. Notice which subject you reach for whenever depth comes this cheap; that's the one that matters.
