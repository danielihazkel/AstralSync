title: Transiting Mars trine natal Mercury
---

Thinking and doing synchronize. For a week or so, the plan in your head and the steps in front of you match up: you can outline in the morning and execute by evening without the usual drag between intention and action. Your speech carries quiet authority — direct, but not barbed.

The strength of this trine is clean decision-making at speed, which makes it ideal for scheduling, strategy, tough conversations, and work that needs both nerve and precision. The hazard is glibness: because everything sounds convincing now, including your own reasoning, weak assumptions can slide through unexamined.

Spend the window on the decision with the longest shadow — the route, the contract terms, the honest conversation about direction. Say it plainly, commit it to writing, and stress-test the one assumption you like most.
