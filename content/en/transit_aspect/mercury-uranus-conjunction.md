title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Uranus
---

For a few days, your mind runs on a faster clock. Ideas arrive sideways — in the shower, mid-commute, in the middle of someone else's sentence — and the odd angle you usually keep to yourself suddenly has language. Conversations with unusual people go unusually well.

The friction is landing gear. Insights outpace follow-through, notifications shred your attention, and you may blurt the true thing at the least diplomatic moment. Not every live wire needs to be grabbed today.

The flashes will not wait for you to be ready, so build a net: keep one capture place — a single note, not six — and write every stray idea into it unedited. Tomorrow, reread the note and circle the one entry that still sparks. That one gets an experiment.
