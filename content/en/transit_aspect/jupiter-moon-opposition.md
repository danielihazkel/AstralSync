title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Moon
---

During these weeks, other people's abundance presses against your emotional life. Someone may make a generous offer with strings you can feel but not see, a family member's big plans may crowd your routines, or public and professional demands may swell until home gets the leftovers. The pull is outward, away from the nest, and your feelings register the drain before your calendar does.

The opportunity inside the tension is perspective. Seen from across the table, your needs become visible in a way they rarely are from inside — you learn what you require by watching what its absence costs. The failure mode is overpromising emotionally: agreeing to host, help, or accommodate on a scale you will resent within the month.

Before accepting any large invitation or obligation right now, check it against your baseline needs for rest and privacy, and negotiate the terms out loud rather than absorbing them silently.
