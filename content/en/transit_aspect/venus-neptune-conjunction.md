title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Neptune
---

Venus drifts over your natal Neptune for a few days, and the world acquires a soft focus. Music lands deeper, colors blend kindlier, and your heart opens toward strays, strangers, and old songs. Compassion comes easily; so does longing for something you cannot quite name.

The sweetness is real: this is the year's finest weather for art, tenderness, and forgiving from the bottom of the ledger rather than the top. The snag is the airbrush. Someone ordinary may glow like a painting now, and the halo you lend them will still be your property when the transit ends.

Pour the glamour into vessels that can hold it. Play the album straight through, paint or write badly and happily, light a candle for someone you miss — and postpone any vow inspired by candlelight until next week.
