title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Uranus
---

This is the Uranus return, arriving only around the mid-eighties of a life: the awakener completes its full circuit of your chart and comes home to the degree it held at your birth. Few transits are rarer. Over a year or more, with several exact passes as the planet retrogrades and returns, the whole long arc of your rebellions, reinventions, and departures comes up for review.

The breakthrough available is a distilled authenticity, permission to be exactly as original as you always were, without apology or performance, and to say the true things politeness postponed. The risk is disruption for its own sake: sudden ruptures with family or routine, or restless upheaval in arrangements that genuinely sustain you.

Tell the stories only you can tell, change what still deserves changing, and keep the people who let you be strange.
