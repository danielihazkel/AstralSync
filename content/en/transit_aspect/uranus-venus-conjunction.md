title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Venus
---

Over the better part of a year, what you love gets renegotiated from scratch. Attractions arrive out of category — the person, aesthetic, or lifestyle you would never have chosen suddenly chooses you — and arrangements built on quiet compromise start to feel like costumes. Some people fall abruptly in love under this transit; others abruptly need air inside a good relationship and cannot immediately say why.

The transit's demand is honest: some of your attachments were chosen by an earlier self, and the electricity marks exactly where. But excitement is doing the arguing, and it argues badly — the thrilling option is presented as the authentic one, when sometimes it is only the newest. Ruptures made purely for the voltage tend to be regretted at leisure.

Give the restlessness legitimate outlets — new experiences inside the bond, honest conversations about freedom — before you let it draft any resignation letters.
