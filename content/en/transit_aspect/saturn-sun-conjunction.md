title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Sun
---

Roughly every twenty-nine years, Saturn arrives at your Sun and audits the life you have built. Over several months, often with a retrograde return, energy runs lower, recognition comes slower, and responsibilities land on your desk with your name already on them. Whatever in your identity is genuine gets consolidated; whatever is borrowed or inflated gets quietly repossessed.

This can feel like walking uphill in every area at once, and the temptation is to read the heaviness as failure. It is closer to a commissioning. Commitments made now — to a vocation, a discipline, a role — tend to hold for decades, precisely because they were made without illusions. The failure mode is refusing the weight entirely and drifting, which converts pressure into depression.

Choose the responsibility you are willing to carry for the long haul and formalize it: sign the papers, take the title, set the schedule. Saturn respects structure.
