title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Moon
---

This is one of the soberest transits in the catalog, and it stays for months, usually revisiting by retrograde. Feelings grow quieter and heavier; you may withdraw socially, feel older than your years, or find that home and family present duties where they used to offer refuge. Old griefs sometimes resurface, not to torment you but to be finished properly.

Saturn here is pruning your emotional life down to what actually sustains it. Relationships kept out of habit, comforts that numb rather than nourish, and needs you have outsourced to others all come under review. The failure mode is mistaking the season's loneliness for a verdict on your worth, and hardening into isolation that outlasts the transit.

Keep two or three genuine connections deliberately warm — schedule them, show up tired if necessary — and let the rest of the pruning happen. What survives this period is trustworthy.
