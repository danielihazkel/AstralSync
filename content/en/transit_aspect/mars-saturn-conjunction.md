title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Saturn
---

Every two years or so, Mars spends a week or two sitting on your natal Saturn, and it feels like driving with the handbrake on. Effort meets resistance everywhere: approvals stall, tools break, seniors say no, your own body demands more rest than usual. This is the classic frustration transit, and raging at it only strips the gears.

Its hidden power is compression. Whatever survives this week — the habit, the project, the commitment — has been load-tested, and Saturn rewards work done slowly under pressure with results that keep.

Play the patience game deliberately. Choose one hard, unglamorous task and grind at it daily in short, controlled sessions. Do not launch, confront, or quit anything major; take the delay as inspection time, and fix the weak joint it points to.
