title: Transiting Mars trine natal Jupiter
---

Twice in Mars's two-year cycle, it trines your natal Jupiter for a week or so, and drive and faith stop arguing with each other. You act like someone who expects to win, which changes how rooms respond to you. Stamina runs deep; recovery is quick; generosity comes easily.

The power is clean execution at scale — this is the window to do the big thing you already planned, because coordination problems seem to dissolve. The hazard is complacency dressed as confidence: trines hand you fair winds, and it is tempting to coast, celebrate early, or assume the streak is permanent.

Spend the ease on something that outlasts it. Ship the ambitious version, negotiate while you are warm, do the physical challenge you have been circling — and bank a little of the winnings instead of rolling all of them forward.
