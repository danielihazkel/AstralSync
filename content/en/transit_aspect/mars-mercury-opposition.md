title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Mercury
---

Contradiction is the theme of this week. Mars opposes your Mercury, and it can feel as though everyone has appointed themselves your editor — challenging your facts, talking over your points, answering questions you did not ask. The temptation is to meet every challenge head-on and turn the whole week into a tribunal.

Used well, opposition is peer review. The pushback you receive now, however gracelessly delivered, tends to expose the genuinely soft spots in your argument, and a position that survives this week is a position worth holding.

Pick your debates by consequence, not by irritation. Answer the substantive objection in writing, let the rest go unanswered, and if a reply must wait a day, let it. A walk before the difficult call does more than another rehearsal.
