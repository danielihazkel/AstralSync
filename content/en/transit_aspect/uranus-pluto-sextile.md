title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Pluto
---

Over a year or more, including a retrograde revisit, Uranus offers your natal Pluto a side door out of old patterns. Habits and dynamics that usually require a crisis to shift become negotiable: a therapy insight actually lands, a compulsion loosens when you change one routine, a power imbalance at work or home turns out to be renegotiable once you finally name it. Depth work pays unusually well now.

The breakthrough is voluntary transformation, dismantling a buried pattern while it is quiet instead of waiting for it to detonate on schedule. The risk is subtler than drama: declining the invitation because the pattern is familiar and functional, or making cosmetic changes and calling the excavation done.

Pick the one entrenched dynamic you always said you would deal with someday, and make this the year you actually begin.
