title: Transiting Neptune sextile natal Pluto
---

For a year or more, across a few exact passes, Neptune forms a gentle angle to your natal Pluto, and the deep machinery of control gets a quiet oiling. Patterns you usually defend, the need to manage outcomes, to hold the secret, to win the underground argument, loosen slightly, not through crisis but through perspective. Insight arrives sideways: in dreams, in art, in a conversation that unlocks something ten years old.

The opening is gradual transformation without drama, a rare chance to release a grudge, soften a compulsion, or bring compassion to your own intensity while nothing is on fire. The drift is that gentleness invites neglect: the material surfaces, you notice it, and you let it sink again, or the new porousness leaks into vague fascination with power, mystery, and other people's shadows instead of your own.

Work the seam while it is open. Choose one control pattern you are tired of, name it in writing, and give it a regular practice of release, breathwork, journaling, honest weekly conversation, for as long as this transit lasts.
