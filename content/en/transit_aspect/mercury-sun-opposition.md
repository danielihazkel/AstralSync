title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Sun
---

Transiting Mercury stands across the sky from your Sun for a few days, and other people's readings of you come into sharp focus. Feedback arrives, solicited or not. Someone summarizes you back to yourself, and the summary is slightly off — or uncomfortably on.

The opportunity is perspective you cannot generate alone. The friction is that it can feel like contradiction for its own sake, and the temptation is to correct the record loudly instead of hearing the useful part.

Treat these days as a mirror check rather than a debate to win. If this contact falls in a Mercury retrograde loop, the same conversation may circle back twice more, so leave it open rather than slamming it shut. Ask someone you trust: "How would you describe what I do?" Then just take notes.
