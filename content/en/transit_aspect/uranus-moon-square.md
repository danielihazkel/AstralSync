title: Transiting Uranus square natal Moon
---

Emotional weather turns gusty for the better part of a year under this square, with retrograde passes bringing repeat storms. Sleep may go irregular, moods can swing from numb to electric in an afternoon, and sudden domestic disruptions — a housemate leaving, a family surprise, a home repair that becomes a saga — force adaptations you did not schedule. Closeness itself may feel confining one day and urgent the next.

The square is prying your fingers off comforts that have become constraints. Somewhere your security arrangements are too tight for the person you are becoming, and the agitation marks the spot. The failure mode is discharging the voltage impulsively — abrupt exits, provocations, three a.m. decisions about relationships — instead of letting it renovate the structure.

When agitation spikes, move your body before you move your life: walk, lift, clean. Decisions about home and relationships made after discharge are consistently better than those made during it.
