title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Uranus
---

Around forty-one or forty-two, Uranus reaches the point exactly opposite its place at your birth, the hinge of the whole cycle and the classic engine of the midlife reckoning. For a year or more, through several exact passes, every deferred question reports for duty at once: the career that was supposed to be temporary, the dream shelved for practicality, the self edited down to fit a life that no longer quite fits back.

This is not a malfunction; it is an audit. The breakthrough is reclaiming what is genuinely yours before the second half begins, and lives that feel authentic afterward are usually renegotiated here. The famous risk is the demolition spree, ending marriages, careers, and cities in one convulsion, only to rebuild the same life with different props.

Change what is truly false first, slowly and specifically, and give everything else a year before you judge it.
