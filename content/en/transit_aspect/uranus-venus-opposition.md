title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Venus
---

For about a year, the disruption in your relationships arrives from the other chair. A partner announces a change of direction, a steady friend becomes unpredictable, or someone magnetic and uncontainable walks in and reorganizes your sense of what you want. You may feel like the stable one in a suddenly experimental world — or like the audience to someone else's liberation.

The opposition asks how much freedom your closeness can hold. Clinging harder is the reflex and it reliably backfires; so does matching chaos with chaos to prove you are no one's anchor. What actually works is renegotiation: the other person's bid for air is usually a distorted version of a need you share, and naming your own version of it turns a defection into a redesign.

When someone's sudden change frightens you this season, ask them what they are moving toward rather than arguing with what they are leaving; the answer tells you whether there is a redesign worth making together.
