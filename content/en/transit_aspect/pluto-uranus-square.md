title: Transiting Pluto square natal Uranus
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto squares your natal Uranus and forces the buried rebellion into the open under pressure. Constraints you tolerated for years suddenly feel unbearable, and circumstances seem to conspire: a rigid workplace tightens, a technology or movement upends your field, a role that once fit now pinches everywhere. The friction is real, and it is aimed at every place you traded your originality for safety.

Worked consciously, this square regenerates your independence at a depth restlessness never could. You can dismantle a genuinely obsolete arrangement and rebuild around what is actually alive in you. Worked unconsciously, it becomes compulsive disruption, quitting, severing, and shocking others to discharge pressure, or a bitter power struggle with whoever symbolizes the system.

Before any irreversible break, write down what you are building next; act when the answer is concrete, and wait when it is only escape.
