title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Sun
---

For a week or so, energy and identity cooperate. Confidence comes without swagger; effort feels proportionate to results. Doors that would normally need a shoulder swing open at a knock — but only if you knock.

That is the catch with a sextile: it is an offer, not an event. The power here is easy self-assertion, the kind that reads as competence rather than aggression. The hazard is letting the window pass in comfortable busyness, then wondering later why nothing moved.

Pick one ambition you have been circling and take its first concrete step now — send the application, schedule the difficult conversation, start the training plan. Moderate, steady exertion this week compounds; heroics are not required.
