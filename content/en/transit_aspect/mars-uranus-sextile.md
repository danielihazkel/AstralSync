title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Uranus
---

For about a week on the two-year Mars cycle, transiting Mars feeds current to your natal Uranus at a workable angle. Novelty energizes instead of destabilizes: you are quicker to improvise, readier to try the odd tool, more willing to break a routine on purpose and see what happens.

The power is inventive action — this is one of the better windows for testing an unconventional fix, automating a tedious chore, or approaching a stale problem sideways. Interesting strangers and left-field opportunities respond well to a direct approach now. The hazard is scattering: five clever experiments started, none logged, none finished, and the insight evaporates with the week.

Run it like a lab. Pick two experiments at most, define what would count as success, and try them before the window closes. Keep the one that works and fold it into normal life; that is how a sextile pays.
