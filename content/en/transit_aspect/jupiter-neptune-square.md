title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Neptune
---

During this stretch, transiting Jupiter squares your natal Neptune and inflates your ideals until they strain against reality. Longing gets loud: for escape, for meaning, for a version of your life that shimmers just out of frame. Opportunities arriving now tend to be presented in soft focus, by others or by your own hopeful narration.

The gift inside the fog is that it shows you what you are starved for — imagination, faith, rest — usually because your schedule currently contains none of it. The overreach risk is buying relief in bulk: grand promises, rescues, purchases, or exits that promise transcendence and deliver cleanup.

Feed the longing directly with creative or contemplative time, and verify every glowing claim — including your own — before money or promises move.
