title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a few hours each month, the Moon crosses your natal Jupiter and your mood widens. The room feels bigger, the future looks affordable, and generosity comes easily — you want to treat someone, promise something, plan something.

The gift is real: this is your lifelong appetite for possibility, briefly audible as a feeling instead of an idea. The snag is scale. In these hours you tend to say yes to five things when one would do, or order the second helping in whatever form second helpings take for you.

Enjoy the warmth, commit to one thing only, and park the bigger promises in your journal overnight. What you keep reaching for when hopeful, month after month, is worth knowing.
