title: Transiting Mars square natal Moon
---

Defensiveness is the signature of this transit. For a week or two your emotional skin thins: a neutral comment sounds like criticism, a request sounds like a demand, and the flare-ups cluster at home, where guards are lowest. Old grievances you thought were filed away can reopen with surprising heat.

Underneath the prickliness is useful information. Squares from Mars to the Moon mark exactly where a need has gone unfed — the irritation is a map, not just weather. If a retrograde loop holds this aspect, the pattern can repeat for a couple of months, giving you three chances to respond differently.

Defuse before you discuss. Eat, sleep, and move first; postpone family verdicts until the spike passes; then address one grievance while it is warm enough to matter and cool enough to handle.
