title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Saturn
---

Over a year or more, with several exact passes, Neptune sits on your natal Saturn and slowly dissolves whatever you have been treating as bedrock. Career definitions, long-held duties, financial plans, even your private rules for being a responsible person can lose their outlines, the way a fence disappears in fog. What was solid does not break; it softens, and asks why it was built.

This is disorienting, but it carries a genuine opening. Structures that survive this transit come out re-founded on meaning rather than fear, and obligations you kept only out of habit can be released with surprising gentleness. The risk is the opposite motion: drifting out of commitments without deciding to, letting anxiety masquerade as intuition, or abandoning discipline entirely because it suddenly feels arbitrary.

Rebuild slowly, on purpose. Keep a short daily anchor, the same walk, the same task list, the same bedtime, so that while the walls are wet you still have a floor.
