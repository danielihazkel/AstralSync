title: Transiting Venus trine natal Moon
---

For a few days, being cared for feels uncomplicated. The house holds you kindly, meals taste like someone meant them, and affection arrives in its most digestible form — no tests, no ledger, just warmth. Even your own company is good company now.

The snag is how completely you could disappear into it. Trines don't knock; they let you sink. A whole weekend can pass in soft light and second helpings, pleasant to live and impossible to remember. And gratitude, unspoken, quietly becomes taking things for granted.

Change the sheets and get into them early. Then spend one small effort on the person who tends you most reliably — a call to the friend who always asks how you're sleeping, flowers for the one who never expects them. Ease is best honored by passing some of it on.
