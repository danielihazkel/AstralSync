title: Transiting Saturn square natal Uranus
---

For several months, including a likely retrograde revisit, Saturn squares your natal Uranus, and the friction between obligation and freedom becomes the loudest thing in the room. Your generation carries a signature restlessness; this transit tests how it lives in you specifically. Rules tighten exactly where you bend them, and the urge to blow something up — the job, the routine, the arrangement — arrives right when the cost of doing so is highest.

The trap on one side is capitulation, quietly filing down your edges to fit; on the other, a tantrum dressed as liberation that trades a real future for a dramatic exit. The work is discrimination: some of your rebellions protect something essential, and some are just reflexes against being told anything at all. People who separate the two here win durable autonomy; people who fight everything usually lose the fights that mattered.

Before you break any rule this season, write down what it protects — then break only the ones that protect nothing.
