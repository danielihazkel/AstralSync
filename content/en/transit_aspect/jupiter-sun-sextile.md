title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Sun
---

For a few weeks, Jupiter offers your identity a friendly opening rather than a grand entrance. Useful contacts appear at the edge of your circle, a mentor is unusually receptive, or a small opportunity shows up in exactly the area where you have been wanting to grow. Nothing about it is dramatic, which is precisely why it is easy to miss.

A sextile is an invitation that requires an RSVP. If you send the email, ask for the introduction, or submit the application, this period tends to return more than you put in. If you wait for fate to insist, the window closes quietly and you never know it was there. The only real failure mode here is passivity dressed up as patience.

Choose one door that has been slightly ajar and knock on it this week, while the timing still favors you.
