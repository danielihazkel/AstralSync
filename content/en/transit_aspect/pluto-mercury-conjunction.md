title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Mercury
---

Across two to three years, your mind acquires depth of field. Surfaces stop satisfying: small talk turns unbearable, and you find yourself researching one subject — psychology, money, mortality, a mystery in your own history — with a thoroughness that surprises you. Perception sharpens toward the hidden layer; you notice motives, patterns, and the sentence someone almost said.

The intensity has costs. Thought can tip into obsession, circling one theme long after it has yielded its insight, and conversations can become interrogations without your noticing the temperature change. Secrets — keeping yours, uncovering others' — take on more weight than is always healthy, and dark conclusions feel truer than bright ones simply because they are heavier.

Give the depth a worthy assignment — the research project, the therapy, the investigation that matters — and keep one relationship where you still talk about nothing, on purpose.
