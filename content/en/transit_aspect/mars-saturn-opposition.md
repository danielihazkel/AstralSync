title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Saturn
---

For a week or two, Mars stands opposite your natal Saturn, and the brakes are usually applied by someone else: a boss slows your project, a landlord enforces the fine print, an older colleague questions your pace. You feel tested — pushed to prove yourself against a judge who does not seem to be rooting for you.

The power in this standoff is definition. Meeting hard limits face to face shows you exactly what the standard is and where your effort actually falls short, which is knowledge flattery never provides. The hazard is the cold war: resentment, malicious compliance, or exhausting yourself trying to force respect on schedule. Classically this is a frustration transit; the patience play wins it.

Do not resign, sue, or ultimatum this week. Instead, ask the blocking party for their exact criteria, meet one of them visibly, and rest more than pride says you need.
