title: Transiting Moon trine natal Jupiter
---

A few hours of buoyancy arrive each month when the Moon trines your natal Jupiter — so smooth you might credit the coffee or the weather. Perspective comes easily. Problems keep their size instead of swelling, and your humor works.

The gift is effortless breadth: this is the widest, most forgiving version of your outlook, on tap for an afternoon. The snag is coasting. Trines ask nothing, so it's tempting to glide through the good mood and let it gloss over something that actually needs your attention.

Put the lift to work. Pick the one task you've been dreading and do it while everything feels lighter than it is. Notice how differently the same chore lands in these hours — that contrast is the lesson.
