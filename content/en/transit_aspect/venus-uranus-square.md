title: Transiting Venus square natal Uranus
---

For a few days, Venus squares your natal Uranus and comfort starts to itch. The standing arrangement feels suddenly airless, the reliable person suddenly predictable, and part of you wants to knock something over just to hear a new sound.

The sweetness inside the static is honest information: whatever bores you most sharply right now is telling you where a relationship or routine has stopped being chosen and started being assumed. The snag is acting on the itch with scissors — canceling, provoking, or flirting with disruption for its own sake, then missing what you cut once the mood passes.

Change the variable, not the equation. Take the usual person somewhere unusual, swap the ritual for one night, and let the restlessness redecorate before you let it demolish.
