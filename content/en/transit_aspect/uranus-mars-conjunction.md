title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Mars
---

Over the better part of a year, your will runs on a higher, less stable voltage. Courage arrives in surges — the resignation drafted at midnight, the move finally made, the risk that had waited years taken in an afternoon. Physical energy spikes and crashes, reflexes quicken, and tolerance for being managed, scheduled, or slowed drops to a career low.

Some of these surges are the real thing: acts of self-liberation you will still be glad of in a decade. Others are static discharges wearing conviction's clothes, and the transit does not label them. Accident-proneness is the body's version of the same problem — force released faster than aim can catch up. Machines, traffic, and sharp tools deserve extra margin this year.

Honor the energy with real outlets — hard exercise, bold but planned moves — and give any irreversible act a 72-hour cooling period; genuine liberations keep their nerve after the wait.
