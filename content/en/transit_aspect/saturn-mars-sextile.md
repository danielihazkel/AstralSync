Effort and structure cooperate for a few months. Routines that usually decay hold their shape, incremental work compounds visibly, and people in charge notice reliability — this is the stretch where showing up daily quietly outperforms talent. Physical training, debt paydown, certification grinds, and any campaign won by attrition all run ahead of schedule.

The transit asks initiative of you and offers stamina in return; it will not supply the spark itself. Its low drama is genuinely a limitation — nothing here forces the issue, so the whole season can pass in competent maintenance of what already exists rather than patient construction of what does not.

Start the long program now — the training cycle, the savings plan, the apprenticeship — and make it boring on purpose: small units, fixed schedule, progress tracked somewhere you can see it.
