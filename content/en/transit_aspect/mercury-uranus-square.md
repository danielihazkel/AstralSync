title: Transiting Mercury square natal Uranus
---

Plans develop plot twists for a few days. The meeting moves, the file will not open, the person you counted on changes their mind mid-thread, and your own attention pinballs between five urgent-feeling tabs. Words come out more abrupt than you intend.

Underneath the static there is information: whatever breaks under this jolt was brittle already. The disruption is showing you which arrangement depended on nothing changing — a bad assumption in any month. During a retrograde loop, the same disruption can rehearse itself two or three times over a couple of months until the fragile part is actually fixed.

Do not fire off the reactive reply; park it in drafts for three hours. Then write one sentence starting with "If this falls through, then..." and finish it honestly. That sentence is your real plan.
