title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Saturn
---

Across a year or more of slow exact passes, Neptune trines your natal Saturn, and dream and duty stop arguing. This is the craftsman's transit: imagination flows into existing structures instead of dissolving them. Long-term work gains a quiet sense of vocation, spiritual or creative life accepts routine without resentment, and you can make ideals concrete, a body of work, a practice, a well-run home, with unusual patience.

The invitation is to consecrate what you have already built, letting meaning soak into your commitments the way water soaks into stone. The drift is complacency: the trine asks nothing, so you can spend it on autopilot, mistaking low friction for progress, or let pleasant vagueness soften your standards until deadlines and boundaries quietly slip.

Give the ease a job. Pick the one long-term project that most deserves your faith and set a realistic weekly quota for it, measured in finished pieces rather than hours, and let the year compound.
