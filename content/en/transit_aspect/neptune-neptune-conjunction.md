title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Neptune
---

Neptune takes so long to circle the zodiac that it only returns to its own natal place at the far edge of a very long life; most people feel this contact, if at all, as the slow planet hovering near its birth degree in the earliest or latest years. However it arrives, the theme is the same: renewed contact with the source of your imagination, the particular quality of longing, music, and dream you were born under.

When Neptune touches its own natal region, the veil that normally separates daily life from that source thins for a year or more, across several passes. Inspiration, nostalgia, and spiritual openness rise together, and so do their shadows: sentimentality, confusion about what is memory and what is wish, and a pull toward retreat that can shade into withdrawal from the people who need you present.

Receive it as a visitation rather than a directive. Keep one grounding ritual through the whole period, a morning walk, a made bed, a shared meal, so the tide can move through you without carrying you out.
