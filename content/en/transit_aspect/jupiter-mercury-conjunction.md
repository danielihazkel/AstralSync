title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Mercury
---

For a few weeks your mind runs bigger than usual: ideas arrive in clusters, conversations open doors, and subjects that once seemed dry suddenly look like maps of something larger. This is one of the better windows for pitching, publishing, enrolling in a course, or starting the book — the appetite to learn and the confidence to speak tend to peak together.

The friction is inflation. Jupiter makes every plan sound feasible while it is still a sentence, so you may promise timelines you cannot keep, skim where you should study, or talk past the person you meant to persuade. Enthusiasm is doing the editing that judgment should.

Capture everything, but before you commit to anything, translate the big idea into one concrete next step and check whether you would still want it at half the size.
