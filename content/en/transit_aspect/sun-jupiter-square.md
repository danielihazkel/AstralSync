title: Transiting Sun square natal Jupiter
---

For a few days the Sun sits at a hard angle to your natal Jupiter, and your appetite outruns your capacity. You want more — more scope, more recognition, more dessert — and the wanting itself feels like justification. Bosses, clients, or family may read your enthusiasm as overreach, because some of it is.

The friction is real but useful: this annual check-in shows you exactly where your optimism pads the numbers. The overcommitted calendar, the budget that assumes the best case, the promise made to sound impressive — all of it becomes visible under this light.

The opportunity hides inside the excess. Whatever you are exaggerating points at something you genuinely want and have been too casual about pursuing properly. Before the transit passes, take one inflated plan and cut it down to a version you could actually start this month.
