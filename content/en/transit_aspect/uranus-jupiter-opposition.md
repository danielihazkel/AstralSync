title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Jupiter
---

For a year or more, across several exact passes as Uranus swings retrograde and direct, disruption approaches through other people and outside events: a partner announces a sudden change of direction, a market shifts under your venture, an offer from far away asks you to reconsider everything. Your natal Jupiter wants to say yes to all of it at once.

The opposition's breakthrough is perspective, seeing your beliefs and ambitions reflected back by circumstances that refuse to flatter them, and discovering which growth is real and which was inflation. The recklessness risk is equally clear: abandoning sound commitments for a glittering elsewhere, or overextending in every direction because staying put feels unbearable.

When the dramatic offer arrives, negotiate rather than lunge, and keep one stable commitment intact while you explore.
