title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Mars
---

Across two to three years, you contend with power that faces you directly. An adversary, authority, or circumstance with real leverage contests your right to act — blocking, out-waiting, or out-muscling your initiatives — and ordinary assertiveness bounces off. Confrontations during this transit have a heavier atmosphere than their subjects justify: both sides sense that the real question is who must yield.

The opposition teaches strategy as survival skill. Force-on-force loses against a stronger opponent by definition, so the season rewards what desperation usually discovers late: preparation, alliance, timing, the patience to let an overextended power defeat itself. It also holds a mirror — the domination you feel from outside is often your own unowned will, met at last at full size.

Never escalate on the opponent's schedule; build your position quietly, choose the ground yourself, and treat every urge toward a final showdown as a request to be reviewed in thirty days.
