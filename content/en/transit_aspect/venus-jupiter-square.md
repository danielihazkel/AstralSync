title: Transiting Venus square natal Jupiter
---

For a few days, Venus squares your natal Jupiter and appetite argues with judgment. Everything looks a little more beautiful than it is — the coat, the getaway, the grand romantic gesture. You want to be lavish, and the wanting itself feels like proof you should be.

There is genuine warmth in this: you are more open-handed, quicker to celebrate, easier to be around. The friction is overcommitment. You promise two dinners for the same evening, or buy the gift that says more than you meant to say, and the bill for enthusiasm arrives after the mood has moved on.

Enjoy the expansiveness, but put a beat between impulse and checkout. Treat someone modestly and specifically — one perfect pastry says more than a hamper.
