title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Pluto
---

For a year or more, with several exact passes as Uranus loops retrograde and direct, the awakener sits on your natal Pluto, the vault where old power struggles, survival strategies, and unspoken compulsions are stored. Things buried do not stay buried under this transit: a family pattern surfaces in daylight, a long-suppressed ambition demands air, a dynamic you always tolerated becomes suddenly, physically intolerable.

The breakthrough is liberation at the root, breaking a compulsion rather than managing it, walking out of a power arrangement you were born into, converting old intensity into honest force. The risk is voltage without a ground wire: obsessive crusades, scorched-earth confrontations, or seizing power in ways that recreate the very dynamic you escaped.

When the buried thing surfaces, deal with it deliberately and with support, not in a single midnight purge.
