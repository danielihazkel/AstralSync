title: Transiting Sun trine natal Jupiter
---

This is one of the friendliest sky contacts you get, arriving twice a year like clockwork and lasting only a few days. The Sun flows easily to your natal Jupiter, and things that normally require pushing simply roll: schedules align, moods lift, strangers are helpful for no clear reason.

The opportunity is momentum. Anything already in motion — a project, a negotiation, a recovery — tends to advance further with less effort now. It is also a natural window for generosity; a favor done under this transit tends to be remembered.

The friction is complacency. Ease this smooth encourages you to coast, and a trine spent entirely on the couch leaves nothing behind when it fades. The luck is real but it is not policy; it is a weather window.

Move one stalled thing forward today, while the pushing is cheap, and thank someone who helped you get here.
