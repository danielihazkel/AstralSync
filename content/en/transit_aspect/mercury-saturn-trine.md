title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Saturn
---

Your thoughts arrive pre-organized for a few days. Complicated matters — estates, schedules, technical debt, a difficult family logistics question — break into steps almost on their own. People experience you as reliable when you speak, and serious topics can be raised without the room tensing.

The quiet cost is range. In this mood you prefer the answerable question, and you may deflect a conversation that matters precisely because it cannot be reduced to steps. Efficiency can become a polite way of not going deeper.

Spend the clarity on something with a long shelf life. Draft the document you will still be using next year — the process note, the will, the maintenance checklist — and ask one experienced person to read it and tell you what is missing.
