title: Transiting Venus square natal Sun
---

For a few days, the wish to be liked rubs against the need to be yourself. You may catch yourself softening an opinion mid-sentence, laughing at something that isn't funny, or reaching for your wallet to smooth a moment that words could have handled. The affection on offer is real, but it seems to come with a dress code.

The sweetness here is genuine attention — people are looking at you. The snag is what you'll trade for it: agreement you don't feel, purchases meant to buy belonging, flattery accepted at face value because it's pleasant.

Let one small disagreement stand without rushing to repair it. And resist the thing you're about to buy mostly so someone else will approve; if you still want it next week, it was yours anyway.
