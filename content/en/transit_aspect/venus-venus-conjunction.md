title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Venus
---

This is your Venus return — once a year, the sky's Venus comes home to the degree she occupied when you were born. For a few days, your taste resets to factory settings and then updates itself: colors you loved at twenty look tired, a song you'd filed away starts playing in your head, and the way you want to love and be loved comes up for quiet review.

The sweetness is a fresh appetite for beauty. The snag is mistaking the reset for a shopping list — celebrating the new eye with a cart full of things chosen by the old one, or declaring a whole new love style by Friday.

Instead, take inventory. Walk through your rooms and notice what you've grown out of — one object, one habit, one way of showing affection. Retire it gently. Let the year's taste begin with subtraction.
