title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Pluto
---

For a few weeks, sometimes doubled by a retrograde revisit, transiting Jupiter crosses your natal Pluto and magnifies your relationship to depth and power. Ambitions you usually keep submerged surface with force; research, therapy, or strategy work goes unusually far; you can see levers of influence you normally pretend not to notice.

Handled cleanly, this is empowerment: claiming authority you have earned, ending what is finished, transforming a resource — money, skill, reputation — into something larger. Handled greedily, it is the classic overreach transit: obsession dressed as focus, control dressed as leadership, and wins that cost you trust you will want later.

Pursue the one deep goal that matters most, and let witnesses see your methods while you do.
