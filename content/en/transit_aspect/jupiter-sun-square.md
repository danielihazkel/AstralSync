title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Sun
---

This transit inflates your sense of what is possible faster than your circumstances can accommodate it. For several weeks you may feel restless inside your current role, convinced you are meant for something bigger, and tempted to force the upgrade now. The urge to expand is real and worth honoring; the timing and proportions are what need scrutiny.

Squares from Jupiter rarely bring disaster — they bring excess. Taking on too much, spending ahead of income, or making sweeping declarations you later have to walk back are the typical potholes. Yet this same friction can push you out of a rut that comfort alone never would have, if you let the restlessness inform a plan instead of detonating one.

Before saying yes to anything sizable this month, ask what it costs in time and energy, not just what it promises, and get the numbers in writing.
