title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Moon
---

Through the months this opposition is active, unpredictability approaches through the people closest to you. A partner's sudden schedule change, a family member's rebellion, a landlord's surprise — the domestic ground shifts because someone else moved it, and your security instincts sound the alarm. You may feel torn between clinging harder and matching their volatility with your own.

The transit is exposing how much of your emotional stability was outsourced to other people's constancy. That is uncomfortable knowledge and valuable: needs that can name themselves can be met on purpose, by you, rather than hostage to whoever shares your roof. The failure mode is control — tightening rules on people who are changing, which reliably accelerates the very departures you fear.

For each disruption someone hands you this season, respond by strengthening your own base — savings, friendships, routines that need no one's cooperation — before renegotiating theirs. Stability you generate cannot be revoked.
