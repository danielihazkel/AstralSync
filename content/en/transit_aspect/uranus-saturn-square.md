title: Transiting Uranus square natal Saturn
---

For a year or more, with multiple exact passes as Uranus stations and returns, the part of you that keeps rules collides with a force that breaks them. Restrictions you tolerated for years become intolerable overnight; meanwhile external shocks, a reorganization, a cancelled arrangement, an authority changing the terms, rattle the structures you counted on. The friction is the point: it locates exactly where your order has hardened into rigidity.

The breakthrough is a working structure that bends, renegotiated obligations, updated methods, rules you keep because they serve you rather than because they are old. The recklessness risk is real too: quitting in a fury, defying the wrong authority at the wrong moment, or white-knuckling the status quo until it snaps on someone else's schedule.

List what genuinely cannot flex and what merely has not, then start renovating the second list before events do.
