title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Mars
---

For a few days, desire arrives from across the room. Someone wants something of you, or you of them, and the space between fills with signals — approach, retreat, a step forward answered by a half step back. The push-pull can be delicious; it can also become the whole relationship if nobody breaks pattern.

The sweetness is being genuinely met: attraction with a live person on the other end, not just a fantasy. The snag is turning it into a contest — playing hard to get, keeping score of who texted first, withholding warmth as strategy. Games measure power; they never manage to measure affection.

Instead of testing, name it. Say what you want and ask what they want, in that order, and let the answer be the answer. Whatever happens next, you'll have traded suspense for something warmer: knowing.
