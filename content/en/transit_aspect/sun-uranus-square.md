title: Transiting Sun square natal Uranus
---

For a few days the Sun clashes with your natal Uranus, and restlessness spikes. Plans get jolted — a cancellation, a reversal, a piece of news that rearranges your week — or, if the sky stays quiet, you supply the disruption yourself by picking a fight with a routine that was working fine.

The friction is obvious: you are twitchy, allergic to instruction, and tempted to mistake irritation for a revelation. Decisions made purely to feel free again tend to look strange once the static clears.

The opportunity is diagnostic. Whatever makes you want to bolt during these days is pointing at a real, slow-burning constraint in your life that deserves adult attention — just not a demolition charge on a Tuesday.

Write down exactly what you wanted to escape and why, delay any dramatic exit for one week, and act then only if the reason survives on paper.
