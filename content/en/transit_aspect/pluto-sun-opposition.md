title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Sun
---

Throughout the year or more this opposition takes to complete, power confronts you from outside. A controlling partner or employer, a legal or institutional battle, a rival who seems to have studied your weaknesses — someone or something meets your will with equal and opposite force. It is common to feel that another party is trying to remake you on their terms, and sometimes that reading is accurate.

The deeper current is projection: qualities you have disowned — ruthlessness, ambition, rage — tend to appear in the people opposing you, magnified. Reclaiming your share of those forces is what converts this siege into initiation. The failure modes are capitulation dressed as virtue and total war over stakes that were never worth it.

In every standoff this year, name precisely what is yours to control and release the rest out loud, in writing if needed. Opponents lose most of their leverage when you stop needing them to change.
