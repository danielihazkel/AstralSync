title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Uranus
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, Saturn offers steady support to your natal Uranus — the imprint of your generation's push for change as it lives in you, your particular brand of independence and invention. Under this sextile, the establishment is unusually willing to make room for it: a manager open to your unorthodox method, a flexible arrangement formalized, an experiment granted a modest budget.

The strength of the period is legitimized originality. Innovations you propose now tend to be judged on their merits rather than their strangeness, and freedoms you negotiate calmly tend to stick. The weakness is that the offer whispers. No crisis forces the conversation, so the remote-work clause goes unrequested, the patent unfiled, the prototype undemonstrated — and the window closes without anyone noticing it was open.

Pick one unconventional practice you already rely on, and get it formally sanctioned while the gatekeepers are receptive.
