title: Transiting Moon trine natal Saturn
---

Each month brings a few hours when the Moon trines your natal Saturn and you feel composed — not happy exactly, but capable. Your feet are under you. Decisions hold still long enough to be made.

The gift is quiet competence: things that normally require bracing — the difficult email, the boundary, the appointment you keep rescheduling — can be handled without drama, and they stay handled. The snag is that this steadiness is so undramatic you may not notice it's there, and spend it rearranging icons instead.

Use it deliberately. Sign the thing, file the thing, book the thing — one act of adult follow-through while it costs half its usual price. Notice, afterward, how rarely you regret what you settle in these hours.
