title: Transiting Moon square natal Pluto
---

For a few hours each month, an undertow pulls against the ordinary day. The Moon squares your natal Pluto: a small slight tugs a thick cable, a routine disagreement suddenly feels like a power struggle, and your thoughts loop with unusual grip.

The gift is the map. The trigger is rarely about its surface — what hooks you hardest in these hours points straight at the buried theme your chart has carried all along. The snag is escalation: turning a passing intensity into a standoff, an ultimatum, or a three-hour excavation of someone's tone.

Delay any confrontation until the undertow releases — it will, within hours. Log the trigger instead: what tiny thing pulled, and how hard. Three months of those logs read like a map with an X on it.
