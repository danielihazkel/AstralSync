title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Mercury
---

Concentration comes easier than usual for a few months. Plans made now tend to be realistic, advice you receive skews genuinely useful, and older or more experienced voices are unusually willing to teach if you ask. It is a quietly excellent stretch for organizing systems, drafting the long document, or putting structure under an idea that has lived too long as inspiration.

Nothing about this transit will announce itself, and that is its weakness: the season passes as easily unbuilt as built. The discipline on offer is real but optional, and low drama can read, wrongly, as low opportunity.

Choose one piece of mental scaffolding — a filing system, an outline, a study schedule, a mentorship — and set it up now; it will still be holding weight years after the transit is forgotten.
