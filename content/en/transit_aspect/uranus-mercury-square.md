title: Transiting Uranus square natal Mercury
---

For roughly a year, in several passes, your mental settings are jolted without your consent. Plans get overturned by late-breaking information, schedules fracture, devices and logistics misbehave at the least convenient moments, and conversations veer into disagreements nobody scheduled. Your own opinions may swing hard — certainty one month, contrarian reversal the next.

Under the disruption, something necessary is happening: assumptions you have not examined in years are being stress-tested by sheer interruption, and the ones that shatter were brittle. People often emerge from this square having changed their stance on something they would have sworn was permanent — and better for it. The hazards are impulsive announcements, rash signatures, and burning a bridge to feel decisive.

When the urge to declare, quit, or reverse arrives, write it down and wait one week; act only on the ones that keep their force without their adrenaline.
