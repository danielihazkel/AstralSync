title: Transiting Uranus square natal Uranus
---

Uranus squares its own natal place twice: around age twenty-one, when the first real claim of independence is staked, and again in the early sixties, when the script you have followed loosens its grip. Either way, for a year or more with multiple exact passes, an itch spreads through everything settled. The life you built, or the one built for you, suddenly reads like someone else's plan.

At the first square, the breakthrough is separation, choosing a direction your upbringing did not pre-approve. At the second, it is subtraction, dropping roles kept alive by habit and reputation. The shared risk is rupture for its own sake: burning bridges, quitting without a next thing, mistaking irritation for destiny.

Change something structural this year, chosen soberly, so the restlessness has somewhere real to go.
