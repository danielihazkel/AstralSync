title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Pluto
---

For a few days, Venus opposes your natal Pluto and someone else carries the intensity toward you. A partner presses for more than you offered, an old flame resurfaces trailing history, or you feel studied — wanted with a thoroughness that is flattering and slightly airless at once.

The sweetness is being desired seriously: no small talk, no shrugs, a bond that insists it is not disposable. Fights under this sky are often love checking its own pulse. The snag is the tug-of-war — matching possessiveness with possessiveness, or going strategically cold to regain the upper hand, until closeness becomes a siege.

Loosen your half of the rope first. Answer intensity with one plain truth about what you can give, then demonstrate trust concretely — leave the plans unmonitored and let the evening prove itself.
