title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Sun
---

Twice a year the transiting Sun sextiles its own natal place — about two months after your birthday, and again two months before the next one. It is a mild, friendly checkpoint: energy is steady, doors open with a light knock, and the year's intentions are still close enough to remember.

The catch is that nothing forces action. Sextiles offer opportunity but never insist, so this window slides by unused more often than not.

Pick one thing you promised yourself at your last birthday and give it a small, concrete push this week — an email sent, a session booked, a first draft opened. Ease is the gift; motion is your part.
