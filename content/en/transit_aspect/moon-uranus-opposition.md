title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Uranus
---

A few hours each month, the surprises come from other people. The Moon opposes your natal Uranus: someone cancels, contradicts, acts wildly out of character, or drops an idea that knocks your afternoon sideways. The disruption feels external, but it's tuned to your own frequency.

The gift is the mirror. What jolts you most usually points at a place where your own life has grown cramped — the person who bails on plans annoys you most in the months you secretly wanted out too. The snag is rash exits: provoking a break, or being provoked into one, over a charge that lasts till bedtime.

Sit on the surprising message for an hour before replying. And keep a private tally of who reliably unsettles you at this touch — the list is short and interesting.
