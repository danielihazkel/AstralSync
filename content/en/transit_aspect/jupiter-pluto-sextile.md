title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Pluto
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter sextiles your natal Pluto and offers quiet access to your own depths. Difficult subjects become discussable; a negotiation, investigation, or piece of inner work that has been stuck finds movement; people with real influence are unusually open to backing you.

The opportunity favors deliberate effort, because sextiles hand you a key rather than an open door. The risk is leaving the depth untouched because the surface of life is running fine, or spending the influence on small maneuvering instead of one transformation actually worth making.

Pick the conversation or project you have been avoiding for being too heavy, and open it now while your nerve and your leverage are both up.
