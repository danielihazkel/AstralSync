title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Moon
---

This extended transit lays a gentling hand on your inner life. Sleep may fill with meaningful dreams, old resentments lose their charge almost without effort, and an unforced sweetness enters your dealings with family and close friends. Many people feel closer to the sacred during this period, however they define it — through nature, art, prayer, or simple quiet.

Because the trine never pushes, its depth is optional, and that is the only real risk: a year of soft feelings that leaves no trace. The same waters that can merely relax you can, if entered deliberately, heal specific things — a strained parental bond, a harshness toward your own needs, a creative gift abandoned in adolescence. The transit will not choose for you.

Pick one old wound that seems slightly less tender than it used to be and move toward it now — a letter, a visit, a therapy intensive — while the current is with you.
