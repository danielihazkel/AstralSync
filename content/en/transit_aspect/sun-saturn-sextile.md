title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Saturn
---

For a couple of days, twice a year, the Sun offers your natal Saturn quiet support, and discipline comes cheaper than usual. Routines feel satisfying instead of confining; the boring, structural work — filing, budgeting, maintenance, practice drills — goes down easily and actually gets done.

The opportunity is consolidation. This is a window to shore up a long-running effort: renegotiate a payment plan, ask a senior person for guidance, put a habit on the calendar where it can survive. Elders and institutions tend to respond helpfully to reasonable requests made now.

The friction is invisibility. Nothing dramatic marks these days, so they are easy to spend on trivia and only notice afterward that steadiness was on offer and went unused.

Choose the most unglamorous task on your list and finish it completely; that small completed thing is what this transit is for.
