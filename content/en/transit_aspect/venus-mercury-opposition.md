title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Mercury
---

For a few days, other people's preferences arrive fully worded. Everyone has a suggestion — where to eat, how to phrase it, what you should really do about the thing — and most of it is offered pleasantly, which makes it harder to sort. Conversation becomes a gentle tug-of-war over whose version of nice wins.

The sweetness is genuine dialogue; you'll hear perspectives worth having. The snag is agreeing out loud while disagreeing inside — nodding along to keep the exchange pleasant, then carrying the unsaid half home. Politeness that silences you isn't politeness, it's postponement.

When you feel the automatic "sure, sounds good" rising, trade it for a question: "what makes you prefer that?" It keeps the talk warm, buys your real opinion time to surface, and turns a contest of charm into an actual conversation.
