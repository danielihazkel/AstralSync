title: Transiting Sun trine natal Saturn
---

For a few days the Sun flows smoothly to your natal Saturn, and competence feels good. Concentration deepens, your word carries weight, and the people who control resources — managers, lenders, landlords, editors — are inclined to take you seriously. This contact returns twice a year, a reliable appointment with your own reliability.

The opportunity is to move the heavy furniture of your life while it is briefly light: sign the sensible contract, present the long-range plan, commit to the training schedule. Work done now tends to hold its shape after the transit ends.

The friction is dryness. In this weather you can undervalue everything that is not productive — rest, affection, play — and schedule the joy out of your own week without noticing.

Advance one long project a real step, then deliberately leave an evening unstructured as proof that the discipline serves you and not the reverse.
