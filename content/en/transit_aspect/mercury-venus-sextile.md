title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Venus
---

A mild, pleasant angle links the day's Mercury to your Venus, smoothing the social machinery for a few days. Small talk turns into actual rapport. The awkward follow-up — after the party, the interview, the first date — is easier to send and easier to receive. Money conversations lose some of their usual chill.

As with all sextiles, nothing happens by itself. The friction here is passivity dressed as patience: waiting to be approached, priced, invited, when the window favors the one who opens it.

Pick one relationship or one deal and make the first move in writing. Ask the shop about the discount, ask the client about the budget, ask the person to coffee. Keep it to three sentences, and end with a question that is easy to answer with yes.
