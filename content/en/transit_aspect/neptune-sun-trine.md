title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Sun
---

This long, gentle transit thins the membrane between you and the sublime without tearing anything. Music lands harder, sympathy flows easier, and your presence acquires a quality people describe as calming or magnetic without being able to say why. Creative and spiritual pursuits begun now tend to feel less like effort and more like remembering.

The trine asks so little that its gifts often go uncollected. Idealism without a deadline becomes daydreaming; compassion without a commitment becomes mood. There is also a soft temptation to coast on charm — the transit makes you easy to believe, which is pleasant and slightly dangerous if your plans stay vague. What you consecrate this year, through steady practice, can become a permanent room in your life.

Attach the inspiration to a discipline: the same hour each week for the art, the meditation, the cause. Regularity is what converts this grace from weather into architecture.
