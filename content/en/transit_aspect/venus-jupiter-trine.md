title: Transiting Venus trine natal Jupiter
---

Venus trines your natal Jupiter for a few days, and social gravity works in your favor. Conversations run long in the good way, strangers are friendlier than the city usually allows, and your taste and your optimism agree with each other for once.

This is one of the year's most pleasant windows: charm without effort, celebration without occasion. Its only snag is drift. Ease this smooth can slide past you unmarked — a lovely weekend you barely remember because nothing demanded anything, and the goodwill on offer was never collected.

Spend it on purpose. Reconcile with someone over a meal, ask the favor you have been sitting on, or simply tell a friend what they mean to you. Grace received should be grace passed along while it is still warm.
