title: Transiting Pluto square natal Mercury
---

Across two to three years, in repeated passes, your ideas are subjected to power. Information is withheld from you, or weaponized around you; a view you have long held is challenged by someone with leverage, or by evidence that will not stay buried. Arguments during this transit are rarely about their stated topic — they are about who controls the narrative — and you can feel your own mind hardening in response, thinking in cases and counterattacks.

The square's legitimate work is the demolition of a mental structure that has become a fortress: a belief defended so long it stopped being examined. What cracks it open is usually painful and usually true. The hazards are the siege mentality — treating every disagreement as war — and rhetorical scorched earth you cannot take back.

When you feel the grip tighten in a conversation, name the stakes to yourself before replying; concede facts fast, hold values deliberately, and never say in victory what you would not survive in defeat.
