title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Uranus
---

A light crackle runs through a few hours each month when the Moon sextiles your natal Uranus. Curiosity comes cheap: odd questions occur to you, unfamiliar things look inviting instead of tiring, and small experiments feel low-stakes.

The gift is playfulness with your own habits — you can bend a routine without needing to break it, and sometimes the bend teaches you something the routine was hiding. The snag is scatter. The same lightness that makes experiments easy makes it easy to open eleven tabs of novelty and finish none.

Run one deliberate experiment: a different tool, a different hour, a different first step for a familiar task. Note the result somewhere. A month of these small trials adds up to real knowledge about how you work.
