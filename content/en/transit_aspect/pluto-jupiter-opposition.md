title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Jupiter
---

For about two years, across several exact passes, Pluto opposes your natal Jupiter and the crucible arrives through other people. A rival with deeper pockets, an institution that controls your access, a partner whose ambitions dwarf or dismiss yours: something across the table tests how much of your confidence is substance and how much is inflation.

The regenerative work is to reclaim the power you have projected. When you stop treating the formidable other as the one holding your growth, negotiations change and your own convictions get sharper for having been contested. The risk is a battle of appetites, escalating stakes, legal or financial brinkmanship, or clinging to a belief precisely because someone powerful attacks it.

When a standoff over vision or resources forms, name what you actually need, concede what is merely pride, and bargain from that clean position.
