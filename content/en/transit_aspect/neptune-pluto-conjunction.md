title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Pluto
---

Over a year or more, with several exact passes, Neptune drifts across your natal Pluto and begins dissolving your deepest control patterns. Pluto marks the place where you grip: survival strategies, buried intensities, the compulsions you manage rather than discuss. Neptune does not confront any of it. It simply softens the grip, and material you have held down for decades starts surfacing as dreams, moods, and strange tides of feeling.

The opening is profound: obsessions can lose their charge without a fight, old power struggles can be forgiven, and surrender, the real kind, chosen rather than collapsed into, becomes possible. The hazard is that dissolving defenses can leave you porous, prone to escapism, magnetic but murky people, or a fascination with the depths that becomes its own quicksand.

Let go on a schedule, not in freefall. Keep one steadying container through this period, therapy, a twelve-step room, a trusted confidant met regularly, and end each day with something physical and ordinary that returns you to the surface.
