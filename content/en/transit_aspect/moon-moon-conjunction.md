title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Moon
---

This is your lunar return: once a month the Moon comes home to the place it held when you were born, and your emotional cycle quietly resets. For a few hours your feelings run undiluted — comfort feels more comforting, hunger more insistent, tiredness harder to argue with. Whatever you actually need tends to surface without disguise.

The gift is honesty with yourself; the snag is absorbency. You soak up the atmosphere of whatever room you are in, so a tense household can masquerade as your own bad mood.

Treat today as the first page of a fresh emotional month: write down how you feel and what you are craving, and compare it with last month's entry.
