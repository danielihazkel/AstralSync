title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Saturn
---

An authority stands opposite your ideas for a few days — a supervisor, an institution, a parent, or simply the rules as written. You propose; they cite policy. You explain; they ask for it in writing. The temptation is to read every "no" as personal.

Mostly it is not. Oppositions expose a real negotiation that was always latent: what you owe, what you are owed, and who decides. Handled plainly, this is one of the better windows all year for renegotiating terms, because the other side is finally at the table and paying attention.

Skip charm; bring specifics. Before the meeting, write the three terms you actually need, in order. Then ask the older question out loud: what would it take for this to be a yes?
