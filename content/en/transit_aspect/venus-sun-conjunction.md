title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Sun
---

Once a year Venus passes directly over your Sun, and for a few days the light around you softens. People warm to you without your working for it. Compliments land closer to the truth than usual, and you may notice you like yourself a little more too — your face in the mirror, your own handwriting, the way you laugh.

The snag is coasting. It is easy to let charm do the talking and skip anything that might dent the glow, or to celebrate the mood with a purchase you'll shrug at next month. Being liked feels so good that honesty can start to seem rude.

Enjoy this openly. Wear the thing you save for better occasions, accept the compliment without deflecting it, and let someone see you pleased with your own company.
