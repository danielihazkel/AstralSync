title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Neptune
---

For a few hours each month, the fog stands opposite you wearing someone's face. The Moon opposes your natal Neptune, and other people turn hard to read: mixed messages, a friend who suddenly needs rescuing, a colleague whose silence you fill with your own imagination.

The gift is rare visibility — what you project becomes easier to see once it's hanging on somebody else. The longing or dread you assign to them in these hours is usually yours, briefly on loan. The snag is misreading and quiet drain: guessing at motives, absorbing a mood that was never yours to carry.

Ask one plain clarifying question instead of decoding. Afterward, a journal line about who went blurry — and what you assumed — builds a map of your own projections, one month at a time.
