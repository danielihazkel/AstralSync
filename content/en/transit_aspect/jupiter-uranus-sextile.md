title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Uranus
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter sextiles your natal Uranus and quietly enlarges the room your originality gets to operate in. Experiments meet encouragement instead of eye-rolls; a side interest, an unusual method, or a technology you tinker with can connect to people who see its value.

Because nothing disruptive forces the issue, the main risk is letting the window close unused — keeping the inventive work private out of habit while a friendly audience is briefly assembled. A smaller risk is novelty for its own sake, chasing every shiny variation instead of shipping one.

Show one unconventional piece of your work to people outside your usual circle before this period ends.
