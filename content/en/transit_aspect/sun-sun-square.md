title: Transiting Sun square natal Sun
---

The Sun squares its own natal position at the quarter points of your personal year — roughly three months after your birthday and three months before the next. Think of it as a seasonal course correction: for a few days, the gap between what you intended and what you are actually doing becomes hard to ignore.

Friction shows up as restlessness or a vague sense of being behind. That discomfort is the useful part; it marks exactly where the plan and the life have drifted apart.

Rather than scrapping the year's agenda, adjust it. Choose one commitment to renegotiate — drop it, shrink it, or double down — and make the change before the window closes.
