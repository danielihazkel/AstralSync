title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Saturn
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter trines your natal Saturn and ambition and discipline pull in the same direction for once. Long projects advance; authorities approve; growth arrives in the sturdy, unglamorous form of better terms, firmer foundations, and plans that survive scrutiny. This is one of the best climates in Jupiter's cycle for building something meant to last.

The risk is undervaluing it. Because the progress is steady rather than dramatic, you may bank it as an ordinary season and skip the bigger commitments it could support — or drift toward flashier prospects that ignore the structure quietly working for you.

Commit to one long-horizon goal now, in writing or in contract, while patience and luck are briefly the same thing.
