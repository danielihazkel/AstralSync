title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Neptune
---

Over a year or more, including a retrograde revisit, Uranus sends intermittent sparks through your natal Neptune, and the veil between imagination and workable idea gets thin. Creative solutions arrive whole in the shower; a meditation practice, a piece of music, or an unfamiliar philosophy suddenly opens; compassion finds practical outlets it lacked before. Your ideals become something you can prototype.

The breakthrough is gentle awakening, updating your dreams without trauma, noticing which longings are alive and which are nostalgia wearing perfume. The risk is characteristic of easy aspects to Neptune: pleasant vagueness, collecting inspirations like souvenirs, drifting through spiritual novelty while committing to none of it, until the window closes with the visions still unbuilt.

Take the most persistent inspiration of this period and give it form, a draft, a demo, a scheduled practice, before the current moves on.
