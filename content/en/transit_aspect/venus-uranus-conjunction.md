title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Uranus
---

For a few days, Venus lights up your natal Uranus and your taste jumps the fence. The person who catches your eye is nobody's obvious choice; the color you want is the one you have never worn; the plan that excites you was invented an hour ago.

The sweetness is genuine refreshment — attraction to the original, delight in people who surprise you, a social spark that makes strangers easy. The snag is that the familiar suddenly looks beige. Restlessness under this transit can misread a perfectly good bond as a cage simply because it is known.

Give the electricity a small conductor. Rearrange one room, go somewhere in your own city you have never been, or let an odd new acquaintance become an actual plan — novelty spent on the day, not on the relationship's foundations.
