title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Neptune
---

For a year or more, with several exact passes as Uranus stations retrograde and direct, the awakener crosses your natal Neptune, the seat of your ideals, longings, and cherished fogs. Illusions do not survive this transit intact: a guru disappoints, a fantasy meets daylight, a belief you never examined turns out to be a mood you inherited. What replaces them can feel like waking up mid-dream, disorienting and strangely electric.

The breakthrough is an honest spirituality and an imagination you can actually build from, inspiration arriving in jolts rather than mists. The risk is the rebound: cynicism that discards genuine ideals along with the false ones, or chasing sudden visions, causes, and enlightenments with more voltage than discernment.

Test each new revelation against a month of ordinary life before you reorganize anything around it.
