title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Neptune
---

Once a year the Sun drifts across your natal Neptune, and for a few days the boundary between what is and what you wish softens. Music hits harder, sleep fills with vivid material, and you feel unusually porous to other people's moods — the stranger's sadness on the train can follow you home.

The opportunity is imaginative and compassionate. This is a strong window for creative work, meditation, or quietly helping someone, because your access to the non-literal parts of yourself is briefly wide open. A long, slow story about your ideals gets its annual page.

The friction is fog. Facts blur, energy dips, and both flattery and self-pity become very persuasive. It is a poor window for contracts, big purchases, or judging your own worth.

Make something or tend someone while the veil is thin, and postpone every decision involving money or fine print until the sky clears.
