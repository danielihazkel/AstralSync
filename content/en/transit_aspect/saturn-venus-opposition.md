title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Venus
---

For several months the bill for a relationship arrives from the other side of the table. A partner asks for definition, a family elder weighs in on your choices, a financial entanglement demands terms — someone else is setting the conditions, and their requirements feel heavier than the affection that is supposed to justify them. Even solid bonds can feel like negotiations conducted across a wide desk.

The opposition's work is to replace assumed agreements with explicit ones. What the other party is really asking, usually, is whether you are in or out — and your own resentment at the question is information about which answer you have been avoiding. Relationships renegotiated honestly now tend to last; those held together by vagueness rarely survive the season unchanged.

Hear the demand fully before you answer it, then give a real answer with terms of your own; the respect this earns outlives the discomfort.
