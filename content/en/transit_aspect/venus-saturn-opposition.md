title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Saturn
---

Venus opposes your natal Saturn for a few days, and someone across from you seems to hold the scorecard. A partner's practicality reads as coldness, an authority's reserve reads as rejection, or you become the strict one while somebody else asks for softness you are not sure you can spare.

The sweetness is clarity about terms: relationships show their actual structure now — who gives, who counts, who keeps the books. Seen kindly, that is useful architecture, not an indictment. The snag is bargaining for warmth, offering obedience where the honest currency is candor.

Notice one place where you are paying for approval in compliance, and stop the payment gently. Then offer real warmth free of charge — a note of thanks with no request attached travels far under this sky.
