title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Jupiter
---

About every twelve years, Jupiter returns to the place it held at your birth, and this stretch opens a new chapter of growth. Themes from twelve years ago may resurface as fresh invitations: a field of study, a kind of travel, a scale of ambition that suddenly feels available again. Doors that were closed swing loose on their hinges.

The risk of a Jupiter return is not failure but dilution. Optimism runs high enough to say yes to everything, and a cycle that could fund one substantial venture gets spent on six promising starts. Retrograde revisits often show you which commitments were enthusiasm and which were direction.

Before these weeks pass, name the one or two pursuits you would be proud to have grown for the next twelve years, and give them your first yes.
