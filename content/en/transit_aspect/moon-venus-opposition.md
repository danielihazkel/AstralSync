title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Venus
---

Once a month the Moon faces your Venus, and questions of affection get mirrored back through other people. You become keenly aware of who wants what from whom — whether you feel appreciated, whether the give-and-take balances — and someone else's ease or beauty may set off a quiet flicker of comparison or longing.

The useful side is candor: for an evening you can see a relationship's ledger clearly, including generosity you have been taking for granted. The ungenerous side is keeping score aloud, or fishing for a compliment instead of asking for what you want.

Notice one thing someone regularly gives you that you rarely acknowledge, and acknowledge it before the night ends.
