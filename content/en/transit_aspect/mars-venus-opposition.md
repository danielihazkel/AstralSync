title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Venus
---

The heat comes at your relationships from outside this week. Mars opposes your Venus, and someone else's urgency presses on what you value: a partner pushing for more than you are ready to give, a rival circling something you love, a bill arriving with an attitude. Attraction itself can feel adversarial — intense, but edged.

The clarifying power of the opposition is that it reveals price tags. You learn quickly what a relationship, a luxury, or a compromise actually costs you, and whether you are still willing to pay. Passion recovered through friction can be more durable than passion that was never tested.

Refuse the tug-of-war framing. Where someone pushes, state your actual terms instead of counter-pushing, and settle shared-money questions on paper, in daylight, before feelings set the exchange rate.
