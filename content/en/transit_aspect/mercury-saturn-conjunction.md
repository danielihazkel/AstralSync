title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Saturn
---

For a few days, your mind sits down at the adult table. Long-avoided subjects — money, boundaries, the thing you agreed to years ago — become discussable, and your words carry unusual weight. It is a strong window for contracts, reviews, and the conversation you keep rescheduling.

The friction is tone. Sober thinking can slide into harsh self-talk, where every draft looks amateurish and every plan looks late. That voice sounds like realism but measures nothing accurately.

Since this contact recurs about three times a year, you do not have to settle everything now. Choose the single most necessary conversation and open it with the older question: not "is this okay?" but "what are we actually committing to?" Then write the answer down, dated.
