title: Transiting Mars square natal Mars
---

A checkpoint in your own two-year drive cycle, and a gritty one. Transiting Mars squares its natal position, and your energy fights itself: you are impatient with your own pace, irritable without an obvious target, prone to forcing things that need finesse. Strains, snapped tools, and snapped tones cluster in weeks like this.

The friction is the test built into the cycle. Whatever you launched at your last Mars return meets resistance now precisely so you can find out whether the method holds. If a retrograde loop parks Mars here, the examination can run for a couple of months — a marathon checkpoint, not a sprint.

Adjust the method, not the goal. Fix your form before adding load, in the gym and everywhere else, and burn the surplus aggression on effort you chose rather than friction you found.
