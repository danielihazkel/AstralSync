title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Moon
---

Someone else puts your feelings into words before you do, and for a couple of days that can be a relief or a trespass. Transiting Mercury opposite your Moon pulls private weather into shared conversation: a partner asks what is really going on, a colleague reads your face, a message lands right on a sore spot.

The opportunity is being genuinely understood, which requires letting the other person be partly right. The friction is defensiveness — dismissing an accurate observation because you did not choose the moment it arrived.

You do not owe anyone a full excavation on their schedule. But pick one observation you received and test it instead of deflecting it: "You said I seemed withdrawn this week — tell me what you noticed." Listening to the answer costs less than a day of silent correction.
