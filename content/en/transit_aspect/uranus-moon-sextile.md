This months-long transit loosens emotional habits without breaking anything. You may notice you can talk about feelings you used to route around, that unconventional people suddenly feel like kin, or that a small change at home — rearranging a room, shifting a routine — refreshes your mood out of proportion to its size. The nervous system is open to updates and taking them gracefully.

Sextiles wait to be used. If you experiment now — new social circles, a different way of dividing domestic labor, honest conversations tried in a lighter key — the changes tend to integrate smoothly and stay. If you change nothing, nothing changes, and the window closes without complaint. The only cost of passivity is continuity with a slightly outgrown emotional life.

Alter one household or relational routine this season on purpose, as an experiment with a review date, and keep whatever version leaves you breathing easier.
