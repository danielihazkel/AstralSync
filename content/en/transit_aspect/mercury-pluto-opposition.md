title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Pluto
---

For a few days, someone across from you seems to hold the cards. A negotiator who will not show their reasoning, a partner whose silence says more than the argument, an institution asking questions it will not answer itself. Information becomes currency, and you can feel the exchange rate in every pause.

The opportunity is that hidden agendas — including yours — are close enough to the surface to name, and naming them changes the game entirely. The friction is escalation: matching secrecy with secrecy, probing with counter-probing, until nobody remembers what the conversation was for.

Defect from the poker game first. Say the thing you were saving as leverage: here is what I want and here is what I am afraid of. Then ask them the disarming question — what does a good outcome look like from your side?
