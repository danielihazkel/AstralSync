title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Venus
---

This is one of the friendliest transits the sky offers: for a few weeks relationships breathe easier, your sense of what is beautiful sharpens, and even money matters tend to resolve on decent terms. Reconciliations come cheap, celebrations land well, and you are likely to be seen — accurately — as better company than usual.

The only real hazard is passivity. A trine this comfortable invites you to receive and never build: the good dinner happens, the warm conversation happens, and nothing is planted that outlives the mood. Ease can also paper over a relationship problem that deserves attention while goodwill is high, since raising it now would spoil a lovely evening.

Use the surplus deliberately — host the gathering, commission the piece, have the tender-but-necessary conversation — so something durable exists when the weather turns ordinary again.
