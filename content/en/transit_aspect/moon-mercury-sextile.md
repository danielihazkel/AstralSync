title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Mercury
---

Head and heart are trading notes politely for a few hours. You can put feelings into words without drama and take in information without defensiveness, which makes this a quietly excellent window for the practical conversation: scheduling, clearing up a misunderstanding, asking the question you felt awkward about.

Because the mood is light, it favors breadth over depth — you may chat pleasantly around the edges of a topic and never quite land on it. Nothing forces the point.

If there is a message you have been composing in your head for days, write the plain version now, while the words are not sticking to your throat.
