title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Jupiter
---

Roughly six years after your Jupiter return, transiting Jupiter opposes its natal place and the cycle reaches its full moon. Over these weeks you see, at maximum size, what the visions of the return actually became. Some results are larger than you planned; others are revealed as enthusiasm that never grew roots. Either way, the harvest is visible, and other people are looking at it too.

The opportunity is an honest reckoning while the evidence is fresh. The overreach risk is classic Jupiter: inflating what worked into promises you cannot fund, or defending what failed because you announced it loudly six years ago.

Sort this cycle's results into what deserves consolidation and what deserves a graceful ending, and act on one item from each list.
