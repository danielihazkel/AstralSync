title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Saturn
---

Every year around the same date, the Sun lands on your natal Saturn and shines a work light on your obligations. For a few days the weight of what you have signed up for is unusually visible: the mortgage, the deadline, the role you carry for other people. Energy can feel lower, and authority figures seem to notice you more than usual.

The opportunity is sober clarity. Long projects respond well to attention now; an hour of honest planning under this transit is worth three in a giddier week. Commitments made here tend to stick.

The friction is heaviness sliding into gloom. It is easy to read a temporary lighting condition as a verdict on your whole life, and to say something bleak you will retract by Friday.

Do one durable thing — pay the bill, fix the schedule, have the overdue serious conversation — and postpone judging yourself until the transit passes.
