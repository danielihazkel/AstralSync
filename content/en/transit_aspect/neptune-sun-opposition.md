title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Sun
---

For the extended season this opposition lasts, other people become screens for projection — yours and theirs. Someone may cast you as savior or disappointment; you may idealize a partner, guru, or opportunity until reality files its correction. Deals and alliances formed now have a way of looking different in a year's light, and the common thread is that clarity about others is at a cyclical low.

There is genuine gold here: empathy deepens, and encounters during this period can dissolve a too-rigid sense of where you end and others begin, which is the raw material of real intimacy and real art. The failure mode is signing binding agreements — romantic, financial, professional — with the idealized version of a person rather than the actual one.

Slow every major interpersonal commitment this year to a crawl: verify claims, watch behavior over months, and invite a clear-eyed friend to review anything that feels destined.
