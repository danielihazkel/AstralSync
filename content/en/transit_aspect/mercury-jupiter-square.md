title: Transiting Mercury square natal Jupiter
---

Your mouth and your beliefs are briefly out of alignment. You may catch yourself preaching, exaggerating, or turning a simple status update into a manifesto. Others might do the same to you — expect at least one conversation where someone's certainty runs well ahead of their facts.

The useful part is that inflation makes assumptions visible. When you hear yourself say "obviously" or "everyone knows," you have found a belief worth examining. The friction lasts only a few days, but a retrograde loop can bring the same argument back for a second and third pass over a couple of months.

Before you send the long message, cut it by half. Then ask the person you disagree with one genuine question: what would change your mind?
