title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Mars
---

For a few weeks your gas pedal sticks. Impulses arrive with a mission statement attached: the argument feels like a cause, the risky shortcut feels like boldness, the fourth commitment of the week feels like momentum. Physical restlessness runs high, and patience for slow processes — traffic, bureaucracy, other people's caution — runs correspondingly low.

The square's genuine gift is courage you would not otherwise summon; some of what it urges is exactly the leap you have been over-preparing for. The failure mode is indiscriminate escalation: burning energy on winnable-but-worthless fights, promising speed you will have to borrow from your health to deliver, and mistaking irritation for conviction.

Before acting on any surge this month, ask whether you would still want the fight or the project after a night's sleep — then act fully on the ones that pass.
