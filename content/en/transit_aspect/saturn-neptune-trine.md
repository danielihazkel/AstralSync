title: Transiting Saturn trine natal Neptune
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn trines your natal Neptune, and the dreaming and building parts of you work the same shift. Your generation's idealism, as it lives in you, finds patient hands: the novel accumulates chapters, the charity gets a bank account and bylaws, the meditation practice becomes as regular as breakfast. Imagination and discipline, usually rivals, pass work between them without friction.

This is among the best seasons for making something real out of something felt — art, service, and contemplative practice all consolidate now, and what you build carries an integrity that survives the transit. The catch is that the trine demands nothing. Its grace can be spent entirely on a mood — a pleasant sense that life has meaning — while the manuscript stays a folder of notes. Ease is the resource here, and unused resources expire.

Commit to finishing one embodiment of your ideals this season, and measure the season by whether it exists at the end.
