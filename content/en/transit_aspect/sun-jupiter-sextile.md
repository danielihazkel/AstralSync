title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Jupiter
---

Twice a year the Sun forms this easy angle to your natal Jupiter, and for a few days doors open a crack that usually stay shut. Nothing forces itself on you; instead you notice invitations, introductions, and small pieces of luck that reward a little initiative.

The opportunity is low-cost expansion — a conversation that could grow into a collaboration, a course that could grow into a skill. The friction is passivity: sextiles do not shout, and it is entirely possible to spend these days scrolling and let the window close unused. There is also a mild tendency to say yes to everything pleasant and follow through on none of it.

While this contact is live, pick one opening and take the first concrete step — send the message, book the ticket, register the name — before the Sun moves on.
