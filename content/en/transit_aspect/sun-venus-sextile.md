title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Venus
---

For a couple of days the Sun sextiles your natal Venus and social friction drops. Invitations arrive, negotiations soften, and rooms you walk into seem pre-warmed. Money matters benefit too: it is easier to talk terms when nobody feels cornered, and this window keeps things uncornered.

The limitation is mildness. This transit opens doors politely and closes them again without complaint if you stay home.

Accept one invitation you would normally decline, or issue one — coffee with the colleague you want on your side, a note to the friend gone quiet. Connections made under a sextile cost little and keep well.
