title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a few days, your everyday voice merges with your biggest ideas. The plan you have carried around half-formed suddenly has sentences, and people lean in when you describe it. Teaching, pitching, publishing, and long-view conversations all come easier than usual.

The friction is scale. Enthusiasm inflates estimates, and you may promise a timeline your calendar cannot honor. Notice when "we could" quietly becomes "I will."

This contact returns about three times a year, so treat it as a drafting window rather than a deadline. Write the one-page version of the big plan — the honest version, with costs included — and say one sentence of it out loud to someone who will remember you said it.
