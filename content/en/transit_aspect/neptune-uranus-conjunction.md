title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Uranus
---

Over a year or more, with several exact passes, Neptune crosses your natal Uranus and pours fog over the part of you that breaks rules. Your instinct for freedom, usually sharp and sudden, becomes oceanic: instead of one clear rebellion, you feel a diffuse longing to be elsewhere, otherwise, unbound. Flashes of genuine inspiration arrive, often through music, technology, dreams, or chance encounters that feel fated.

The opening is a marriage of vision and invention, where your originality is softened by compassion and aimed at something larger than shock value. The hazard is aimless restlessness: quitting things at random, chasing novelty that dissolves on contact, or mistaking a mood of transcendence for an actual plan. Change made in this fog tends to evaporate with it.

Let the ideal clarify before you act on it. Keep a running list of every liberation fantasy this year, date each entry, and only pursue the ones that are still on the list, unchanged, three months later.
