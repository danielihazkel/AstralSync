title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Venus
---

For several months your affections are audited. Relationships that run on habit or hope get weighed for their actual substance, pleasures start presenting their invoices, and you may feel less lovable — not because anything changed, but because Saturn dims the flattering light everything was standing in. Casual connections tend to cool; serious ones tend to formalize.

This is commitment weather, in both directions. Bonds with real foundations often deepen into something explicit now — the shared lease, the engagement, the honest renegotiation — while arrangements that were mostly atmosphere quietly end. The friction is a loneliness that argues you must either settle for less or need no one, both of which are the transit talking.

Judge every relationship this season by how it behaves, not how it feels on a given evening — and give the ones that behave well something concrete: your time, your word, your plans.
