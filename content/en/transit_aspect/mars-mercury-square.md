title: Transiting Mars square natal Mercury
---

Arguments find you easily under this square. Words come out faster than judgment can screen them, and other people's words hit a nerve that is suddenly very close to the surface. Expect crossed wires, interrupted sentences, and the peculiar urge to win exchanges that do not matter.

The productive face of the friction is decisiveness. Questions you have chewed on for months can be settled in an afternoon, because impatience finally outweighs perfectionism. Hasty commitments are the tax: this is a poor week to sign anything you read at speed, and if Mars is retrograde the argumentative weather can stretch across a couple of months.

Channel the charge into finishing hard mental work — audits, debugging, difficult letters written and held overnight. In live conflict, slow your speech deliberately; the pause is the win.
