title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Saturn
---

For a year or more, through several exact passes as Uranus reverses and returns, disruption faces your natal Saturn from across the axis, usually wearing someone else's face. A boss rewrites the rules, an institution withdraws its guarantee, a partner demands freedom from an arrangement you thought was settled. Your instinct is to hold the line; the transit's job is to test which lines deserve holding.

The breakthrough is a cleaner contract with the world: obligations kept by choice, authority you respect rather than merely obey, structures flexible enough to survive surprise. The dual risk is rigidity that turns every negotiation into a standoff, or a reactive rebellion that torches durable commitments to win a temporary argument.

When the external shakeup comes, ask what the demand is right about before deciding what it is wrong about, and renegotiate from there.
