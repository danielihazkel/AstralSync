title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Pluto
---

During this stretch, transiting Jupiter opposes your natal Pluto, and questions of power play out through other people. Someone with resources or authority may enlarge your world dramatically — an investor, an institution, an intense ally — or may seem to hold your growth hostage to their terms. Either way, the real negotiation is about how much control you trade for expansion.

The opportunity is a clear-eyed view of your own depths: what you envy, what you fear losing, what you are willing to owe. Seen honestly, that is potent self-knowledge. The overreach risk is entering a compelling alliance so hungry for scale that you sign away leverage you cannot get back.

Before accepting any powerful offer this season, calculate what it would cost to walk away later, and keep that price payable.
