title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Jupiter
---

Someone across the table holds the big picture while you hold the details — or the reverse. For a few days, conversations polarize between vision and logistics, promise and fine print. A mentor, editor, advisor, or confident stranger may challenge how you have framed a plan.

The opportunity is perspective you cannot generate alone; the friction is a contest over who is "really" right, where both sides talk past each other at increasing volume. Overcommitting to end the argument is the classic mistake here.

Instead of defending your version, borrow theirs for one honest minute. Ask them to describe your plan back to you in their own words, and write down the difference between what you meant and what they heard.
