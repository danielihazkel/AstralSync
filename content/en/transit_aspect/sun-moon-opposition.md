title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Moon
---

Once a year the Sun opposes your natal Moon, stretching you between the public day and the private evening. Work wants your full presence at exactly the moment home does too, and someone — a boss, a partner, a child — may feel shortchanged whichever way you lean.

The gift of the opposition is perspective: with the two poles fully lit, you can see your actual balance, not the one you claim. Notice which side you reflexively sacrifice; that reflex is the real finding.

For these few days, schedule the neglected side first and let the other fit around it. Treat it as an annual audit of where your hours truly go.
