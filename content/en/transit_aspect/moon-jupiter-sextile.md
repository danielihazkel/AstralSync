title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Jupiter
---

Once a month the Moon sextiles your natal Jupiter, and for a few hours a door stands ajar. Nothing dramatic — just a mild, workable optimism, the sense that a small stretch would probably go fine.

The gift is usable confidence: asking feels easier, learning feels lighter, people seem more receptive because you are. The snag is that pleasant hours like these slide by unused. A sextile invites; it doesn't insist. If you spend the window scrolling, it closes without complaint.

So spend it on purpose. Send the one hopeful message you've been postponing, or take the first small step on the plan you keep shelving. Later, notice whether these easy hours tend to favor the same kind of reaching.
