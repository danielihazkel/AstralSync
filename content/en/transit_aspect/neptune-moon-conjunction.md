title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Moon
---

During the long season this transit is in orb, your emotional skin thins to translucency. You absorb the moods of rooms, dream vividly, cry at commercials, and feel homesick for places you cannot name. Longings surface that ordinary comforts cannot answer, and the family past may return in softened, sometimes distorted, memory.

This permeability is a genuine opening: compassion, artistic sensitivity, and spiritual hunger all run rich now, and many people find their inner life more vivid than it has been in decades. The dangers are absorption and anesthesia — losing track of which feelings are actually yours, or quieting the ache with whatever numbs fastest. Boundaries do not disappear during this transit; they just stop announcing themselves, so you must check for them manually.

Institute a daily clearing practice — journaling, solitude, a walk without your phone — to sort your own emotions from the ones you picked up secondhand.
