title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Mars
---

For a few days, charm and drive work the same shift. Flirtation comes easily and lands lightly; asking for things — a favor, a date, a better deal — feels less like exposure and more like conversation. You can pursue without pushing, which is the rarest register there is.

The snag is vagueness. Because nothing is urgent, it's tempting to keep everything at the level of pleasant maybe — the "we should hang out sometime" that never picks a day, the raise you deserve mentioned only as a joke. Low-stakes ease is exactly when directness costs the least, and exactly when people skip it.

Ask directly for one thing you want this week. Name a day, a number, a plan. The warm air around you will cushion the ask; all you have to supply is the sentence.
