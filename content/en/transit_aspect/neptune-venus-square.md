title: Transiting Neptune square natal Venus
---

For a year or more, in several passes, your heart's accounting goes soft. Longing outruns its objects: the relationship you have feels insufficient in ways you cannot name, while someone or something just out of reach glows with imported light. Sacrifices feel romantic before they feel expensive. Financially the same blur applies — money leaks toward whatever promises transcendence, comfort, or rescue.

The square is dissolving an illusion about what you love or how you love, and the ache is the dissolving. Common versions: discovering you loved a potential rather than a person; noticing a devotion that was really an escape; watching an idealized figure turn out to be human at scale. What remains after the tide goes out is smaller and real, which is the point.

Name the longing precisely instead of medicating it with the nearest glitter — and make no binding romantic or financial commitments while the water is still moving.
