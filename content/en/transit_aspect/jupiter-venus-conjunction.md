title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Venus
---

Affection, beauty, and appetite all get the volume turned up for a few weeks. People warm to you with less effort on your part, romance that has been idling may accelerate, and money wants to be spent on things that feel like celebration. Socially this is harvest weather: invitations multiply, and generosity — given and received — comes easily.

The friction is that pleasure makes a persuasive advisor. Under this conjunction you can commit to a person, a purchase, or a lifestyle upgrade because the moment feels abundant rather than because it fits your life. What you say yes to now will still be on the books when the mood normalizes.

Enjoy the season openly, but put a one-week pause on any decision with a price tag or a promise attached; whatever is genuinely good will survive the wait.
