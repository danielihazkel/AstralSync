title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Venus
---

Once a year the Sun stands opposite your natal Venus, and relationships hold up a mirror. What you value — in people, in beauty, in money — is reflected back through someone else's choices, and the reflection is not always flattering. A partner's spending, a friend's taste, a client's counteroffer: each one shows you your own standards from outside.

Friction runs through comparison. It is easy to feel underappreciated now, or to notice you have been doing the underappreciating.

Instead of keeping score, name one thing the other side genuinely does better and say so. Balance in the ledger of affection starts with the entry you control.
