title: Transiting Venus trine natal Mercury
---

For a few days, the right words show up on time. Diplomacy comes naturally — you can disagree without anyone bristling, deliver feedback that lands as a favor, and make a request sound like an invitation. Even your typing seems to have better manners.

The snag with fluency is that it can skate. Charm carries a conversation so well that substance never has to show up, and you can leave a meeting where everyone felt wonderful and nothing was decided. Graceful vagueness is still vagueness.

Spend the gift where stakes are real: have the delicate conversation you've been scheduling around — the money talk, the roommate talk, the "where is this going" talk. You will not get a smoother runway for it this season. Say the hard part early, kindly, and let your ease do the landing.
