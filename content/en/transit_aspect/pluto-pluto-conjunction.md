title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Pluto
---

Pluto returns to its own natal degree only once in roughly two and a half centuries, so this conjunction belongs to the beginning of life and to the archetypal long cycle rather than to any age you will mark on a calendar. What it describes is Pluto reactivating the very ground of your chart: the degree region that holds your deepest instincts about power, survival, loss, and renewal becomes freshly charged whenever Pluto's motion stirs it.

When this territory is lit, themes you were born carrying, the family's unspoken material, the compulsions and the resilience that came with your generation, rise for conscious ownership. The empowerment is inheritance claimed: transformative capacity used deliberately. The risk is inheritance repeated: enacting the old survival script as if it were fate.

Study what your line and cohort did with power, and choose consciously which parts of that legacy you will carry forward.
