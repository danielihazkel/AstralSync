title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a year or more, across several exact passes, Neptune drifts over your natal Jupiter and turns faith into weather. Your sense of what is possible expands like fog at dawn: visions arrive fully formed, generosity comes easily, and spiritual or creative longing that has been dormant for decades wakes up asking to be fed.

The gift and the hazard are the same substance. Jupiter under Neptune inflates whatever it touches, so a genuine calling and a beautiful delusion can feel identical from the inside. Discernment thins exactly when the promises get biggest, which makes this a poor season for speculative investments, sweeping vows, or teachers who discourage questions.

Follow the inspiration, but let it prove itself slowly. Before you commit money, time, or belief to any grand vision this year, write it down, wait two weeks, and reread it on an ordinary tired morning.
