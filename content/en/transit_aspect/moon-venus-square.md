For a few hours your appetite and your comfort argue. You want something — sweetness, attention, a purchase, a reply — and whatever you reach for is slightly off-target, so the wanting continues. Under this recurring pinch people snack without hunger, shop without need, or fish for reassurance in ways that do not quite land.

The gift is that the mismatch points at a real longing your routine papers over; monthly, it names what you are actually missing. The trap is soothing the signal instead of hearing it, and feeling faintly overdrawn afterward.

Before satisfying the next craving tonight, pause ten seconds and ask what it stands in for — the answer is worth a line in your journal.
