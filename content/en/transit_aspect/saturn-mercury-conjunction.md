title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Mercury
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, your mind is asked to grow up in some specific way. Vague plans stop surviving contact with paper; conversations turn consequential; the study, certification, or documentation you have deferred becomes unavoidable. Thought slows down — not because you are duller, but because every idea is suddenly being weighed before it is allowed out.

The heaviness has a purpose. Saturn here builds the mental infrastructure — the finished manuscript, the mastered skill, the airtight agreement — that quicker seasons never sit still long enough to pour. The friction is a pessimistic narrator who mistakes rigor for gloom and starts pronouncing verdicts on your whole intelligence from one slow week.

Pick the one demanding mental project this season is clearly asking for and work it in small daily units; keep a written record, because progress this gradual is invisible without one.
