title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Jupiter
---

Across a couple of years and several exact passes, Pluto trines your natal Jupiter and puts real power behind your growth. Efforts to study, publish, teach, invest, or expand meet unusually little resistance, and influence tends to accrue to you without much campaigning. Beliefs mature during this period; what you understood in theory becomes something you have lived and can transmit.

Because the trine is frictionless, its main risk is unexamined appetite. Expansion feels so natural that you may not notice ambition shading into acquisitiveness, or conviction shading into the assumption that your worldview should simply prevail. Power gained easily is easy to misuse casually.

Choose one long-range ambition worthy of this support and build it with deliberate ethics, so that when the trine ends you hold something regenerated rather than merely enlarged.
