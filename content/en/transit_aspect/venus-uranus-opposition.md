title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Uranus
---

For a few days, Venus opposes your natal Uranus and other people bring the lightning. Someone close acts out of character; a newcomer with strange opinions is suddenly the most interesting person in the room; plans get remade by hands other than yours.

The sweetness is the jolt itself — being surprised by people is a form of being seen freshly, and a partner's unpredictability can remind you they were never furniture. The snag is whiplash: you may swing between clinging to the familiar and romanticizing whatever disrupts it, judging the known bond against the unknown stranger's highlight reel.

Let the surprise be interesting without making it a verdict. Ask the out-of-character person what changed, genuinely listen, and save one evening this week for something neither of you has done before.
