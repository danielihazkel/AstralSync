title: Transiting Moon trine natal Venus
---

Affection moves without friction for a few hours. You like people more easily and they return the favor; taste is sure-footed, so it is a good window for choosing colors, arranging a space, or planning anything meant to delight someone. Tenderness that usually feels risky feels ordinary now, which is the whole point.

The soft spot in this weather is agreeableness. Everything and everyone seems fine, so you may nod along to plans or purchases you would question on a plainer day, simply to keep the pleasantness going.

Enjoy freely, but keep decisions with price tags for tomorrow; tonight, just tell one person plainly why you enjoy them.
