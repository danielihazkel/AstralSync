title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Moon
---

Emotional regeneration proceeds gently through the long life of this transit. Feelings gain depth without gaining menace: you can revisit painful history and find it informative rather than radioactive, and attachments strengthen at the root. Many people find their instincts uncannily accurate now — a quiet knowing about who is trustworthy and what a situation really is.

The ease is the only trap. Because nothing hurts, the profound housecleaning on offer can be left undone; a trine opens the depths but leaves attendance optional, and unclaimed depth simply closes over again. This is among the best periods in decades for deliberately re-founding your emotional life — ending a numbing habit for good, metabolizing an old loss, building a home that reflects who you have become rather than who you were managing.

Choose the one piece of emotional deep-cleaning you have always assumed would be agonizing and start it now, while your inner weather makes hard things unusually bearable.
