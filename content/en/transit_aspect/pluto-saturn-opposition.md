title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Saturn
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto opposes your natal Saturn and the confrontation with structure arrives from outside. An employer, regulator, landlord, or patriarch, someone who holds real leverage, presses against the commitments and defenses you have spent years building. The encounter exposes exactly which of your load-bearing walls stand on merit and which stand on someone else's permission.

The empowerment is a re-founding of authority on your own terms: boundaries renegotiated with the powerful, obligations rewritten as agreements between adults, a career rebuilt around what you can actually stand behind. The danger is a war of attrition, matching control with control, resenting every institution, or capitulating completely and calling it duty, either of which leaves the old structure condemned but still inhabited.

In each standoff, distinguish the demand you must honor from the domination you must refuse, and answer the two differently.
