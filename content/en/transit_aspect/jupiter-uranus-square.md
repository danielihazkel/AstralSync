title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Uranus
---

During this stretch, transiting Jupiter squares your natal Uranus and inflates your appetite for freedom past its usual settings. Routines feel twice as confining as they did last month; sudden exits, impulsive purchases, and dramatic reinventions all present themselves as overdue liberation.

There is real signal in the restlessness. The parts of your life that genuinely cramp your independence get loud now, and that information is worth having. The overreach risk is acting on all of it at Jupiter speed — treating every irritation as a cage and every impulse as authenticity, then spending months repairing what one week overturned.

List what the restlessness is pointing at, sit on it for two weeks, and act only on the item that still looks true.
