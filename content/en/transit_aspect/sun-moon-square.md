title: Transiting Sun square natal Moon
---

For a few days the Sun squares your natal Moon and your agenda argues with your appetite. The day's demands — deadlines, visibility, other people's schedules — press directly against your need for rest, comfort, and familiar rhythm. Irritability here is usually hunger or tiredness wearing a costume.

The opportunity inside the scratchiness: this transit shows you precisely which need you have been shortchanging. The thing you snap about is the thing that wants tending. It happens twice a year on schedule, so nothing is uniquely wrong with this week.

Before reacting to anyone, name the unmet need out loud — sleep, food, quiet, home — and meet the cheapest version of it today.
