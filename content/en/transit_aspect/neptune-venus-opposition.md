title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Venus
---

For a year or more, enchantment approaches from outside. Someone arrives trailing glamour — a person, a cause, an aesthetic world — and invites a devotion that feels destined; or a partner grows misty, harder to read, present in body and elsewhere in attention. Either way, the relationship field fills with projections, and you cannot be sure which longings are yours and which are being reflected at you.

The opposition teaches the difference between compassion and absorption. The glowing other is genuinely carrying something you need to reclaim — usually your own unlived tenderness or artistry, outsourced long ago — and the relationship works only when that carrying is noticed and taken back. Rescuing, being rescued, and mutual beautiful vagueness are the three classic dead ends.

Keep your feet when the tide comes in: maintain your own practices and friendships, ask small factual questions of large romantic claims, and love what is actually there.
