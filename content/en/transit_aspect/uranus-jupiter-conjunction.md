title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a year or more, often with three exact passes as Uranus stations retrograde and direct, the planet of sudden change sits on your natal Jupiter, the part of you that believes, bets, and grows. Opportunity arrives sideways under this transit: an offer from nowhere, a field you never considered, a door that opens only because you happened to be standing somewhere unusual.

The gift is expansion through the untried; the hazard is recklessness dressed up as faith. Windfalls here are real, but so is the urge to gamble the mortgage on a hunch or inflate a promising idea past what it can carry. Long-held beliefs get shaken too, and a philosophy that fit you a decade ago may burst its seams now.

Say yes to the unfamiliar opening, and cap what you stake on any single roll.
