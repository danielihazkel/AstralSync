title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Sun
---

For a few days, your voice and your intentions run on the same current. Explanations come out in the right order. Meetings where you have to represent yourself — interviews, reviews, first calls — tend to go smoother, because you sound like the person you actually are.

The risk is coasting. Trines make things frictionless, and frictionless days are easy to spend on chatter and inbox grooming. The fluency fades with the transit; what you put it into stays.

Use the ease on something with a longer shelf life: the bio you keep postponing, the cover note, the difficult-but-not-hostile explanation someone deserves. Write the paragraph that says what you want next, in your own plain words, and send it while the sentences are still cooperating.
