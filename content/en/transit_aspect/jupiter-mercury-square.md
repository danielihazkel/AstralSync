Your thinking wants to skip steps for a few weeks. Plans balloon between the first draft and the second telling, small commitments multiply into a calendar that cannot hold them, and the fine print — contracts, estimates, itineraries — is exactly where this square hides its bill. You may also find yourself arguing a position further than you actually believe it, carried by the momentum of your own rhetoric.

The gift inside the friction is genuine reach: you are seeing implications and audiences your everyday thinking misses. The failure mode is not the big idea but the skipped verification underneath it — the number rounded up, the assumption never tested, the reply sent before the reread.

Before you sign, send, or announce anything this month, have one detail-minded person check the part you are most confident about.
