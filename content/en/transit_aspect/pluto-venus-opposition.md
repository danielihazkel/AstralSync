title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Venus
---

Across two to three years, transformative pressure enters through your closest bonds. A partner undergoes changes that renegotiate the whole arrangement; a magnetic figure arrives whose effect on you feels less like attraction than like weather; or a relationship's finances, family stakes, or old secrets surface with force. The recurring sensation is being changed by proximity to someone else's depths.

The opposition's question is whether you can stand in full contact with a powerful other without surrendering yourself or armoring against them. Merging and fleeing are the twin evasions; the work is staying — present, boundaried, honest about the pull. Often the intensity opposite you is carrying your own disowned wanting, and the relationship becomes the place you finally meet it.

Keep your center under strong influence: hold your friendships, money, and judgment as your own, and let the encounter deepen you at a pace you choose rather than one imposed.
