title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Pluto
---

Across a year or more of slow passes, Neptune trines your natal Pluto, and the deep water and the tide move together. Transformation that would ordinarily require crisis happens by seepage: entrenched habits lose their necessity, old wounds drain quietly, and you find yourself able to look at the controlling, hungry, secretive parts of your own nature with something close to tenderness.

The opening is regeneration without demolition, a season to do the inner work you have postponed because it always seemed to require a breakdown to justify it. Depth psychology, contemplative practice, and honest intimacy all pay unusual dividends now. The drift is the trine's usual tax: because nothing hurts enough to force the issue, the invitation can pass unused, or the new permeability can spend itself on moody atmospheres and occult window-shopping.

Volunteer for the surgery while the anesthetic is free. Pick the one buried pattern you most want to be done with, engage it through a sustained practice or a skilled guide, and mark the months on a calendar so the depth work has edges.
