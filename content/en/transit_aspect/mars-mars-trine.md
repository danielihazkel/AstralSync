title: Transiting Mars trine natal Mars
---

Your engine hums. Transiting Mars trines its own natal place, and for a week or so output rises while strain falls — the athletic weeks, the shipping weeks, the weeks when your body and your schedule finally cooperate. Stamina, coordination, and nerve all run a notch above baseline.

Within your two-year drive cycle this is a harvest lane: efforts seeded at the Mars return move forward almost on their own. The power is sustainable intensity, the kind you could keep up without burning down. The hazard is coasting on talent — trines reward whatever you actually do, including nothing.

Schedule your heaviest lifts, literal or otherwise, inside this window: the long haul of work, the physical test, the bold-but-planned move. Bank progress now against harder weather later in the cycle.
