title: Transiting Mercury square natal Neptune
---

Static on the line for a few days. Messages arrive with pieces missing, tones get misread, and a plan everyone "agreed to" turns out to exist in three incompatible versions. You may also be tempted to blur the truth yourself — not lying exactly, just leaving the inconvenient sentence unsaid.

The gift inside the fog is a map of where your communication runs on assumption. Every misunderstanding this week marks a place where you were transmitting and hoping rather than confirming. A retrograde loop can replay the same confusion across a couple of months until the assumption is finally spoken.

Practice boring clarity. After any important exchange, send a three-line summary: what I heard, what I will do, by when. And say the inconvenient sentence — softly, but say it.
