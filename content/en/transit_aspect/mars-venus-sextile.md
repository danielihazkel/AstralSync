title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Venus
---

Warmth gets a spine this week. Mars sextile your Venus makes affection easier to act on: the compliment gets said instead of thought, the invitation gets sent instead of drafted, and social effort returns more than it costs. Creative work benefits from the same blend — enough drive to finish, enough taste to finish well.

The opportunity is initiative in matters you usually leave to chance: romance, friendship, aesthetics, and the gentler side of money, like asking politely for what something is worth. The hazard is only inertia; the window closes quietly if you wait to be approached.

Make one deliberate overture — the date proposed with a time and place, the collaboration pitched, the underpriced work repriced. Small courage in pleasant directions goes unusually far right now.
