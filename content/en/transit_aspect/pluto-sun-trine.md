title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Sun
---

Personal power consolidates almost silently under this long transit. Stamina runs deeper than usual, focus holds, and you may find that people defer to you more readily — not because you demand it, but because you have stopped leaking authority through self-doubt. Old fears lose mass; projects that require sustained intensity, from physical rebuilding to organizational overhauls, go unusually well.

The trine's hazard is invisibility: transformation this smooth can pass unclaimed. Without friction to mark the change, you may keep operating at your old, smaller scale out of habit, declining challenges you are now fully equipped to win. There is also a quieter temptation to use the ease manipulatively, since reading and steering people comes easier now.

Take on one undertaking this year that would have intimidated you two years ago — the leadership role, the physical feat, the honest reckoning. Capacity confirmed by action becomes permanent; capacity merely felt drains away with the transit.
