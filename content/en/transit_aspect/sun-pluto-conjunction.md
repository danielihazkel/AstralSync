title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Pluto
---

Once a year, on nearly the same date, the Sun crosses your natal Pluto and briefly floodlights the basement. For a few days you feel everything at higher intensity: ambition, jealousy, desire, the need to know what is really going on beneath the polite surface of a situation.

The opportunity is depth on demand. Research cuts to the core, therapy sessions go somewhere, difficult truths can finally be said because you have the nerve to say them. One layer of a decades-long transformation becomes visible, and what you see now tends to be accurate.

The friction is control. Under this light the urge to manage outcomes — and people — hardens quickly, and a small disagreement can escalate into a contest nobody remembers starting.

Aim the intensity at a problem rather than a person: spend it finishing, purging, or investigating one thing all the way to the bottom.
