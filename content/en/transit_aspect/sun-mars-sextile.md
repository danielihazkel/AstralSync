title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Mars
---

For a couple of days the Sun sextiles your natal Mars, and effort pays out at a better exchange rate. You get more done per unit of push: exercise builds, chores clear, and assertiveness comes across as confidence rather than aggression. Asking directly for what you want works unusually well now.

The soft spot is that nothing compels you. Without an external deadline, this efficient little window happily hosts a nap.

Front-load the day with the task that needs muscle or nerve — the gym session, the firm phone call, the messy garage — and let the momentum carry the afternoon.
