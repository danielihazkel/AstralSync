title: Transiting Saturn trine natal Jupiter
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, discipline and opportunity stop arguing and start cooperating. Long-range plans that once felt grandiose now look merely ambitious: the mortgage application, the graduate program, the expansion into a second market. Authority figures are unusually receptive to your proposals because, for once, your optimism arrives with paperwork attached.

The strength of this period is that steady effort converts almost directly into visible progress — what you build now tends to still be standing years later. The risk is the trine's signature vice: ease breeds coasting. Because nothing is pushing back, you may not push forward, and this window can close having been merely pleasant. Favorable conditions reward the person who shows up with a plan, not the person who waits for the plan to arrive.

Choose the long-term goal you would pursue if you trusted yourself, and lock in its first milestone before this season ends.
