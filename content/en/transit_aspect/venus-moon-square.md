title: Transiting Venus square natal Moon
---

For a few days, wanting comfort and wanting to be liked pull in different directions. You crave the couch but agreed to the dinner; you want to be held but keep the conversation light and pleasant instead. There's a low hum of appetite under everything — sugar, softness, reassurance — that no single snack quite answers.

The sweetness is real: people are being kind, and home still soothes. The snag is soothing sideways — buying candles for a mood that wanted company, eating past pleasure, saying "it's fine" in a voice that even you don't believe.

Before you feed the feeling, ask it one question: what would actually help? Sometimes the answer is still the pastry, and then enjoy it fully. But resist the third comfort purchase of the week; it's carrying a message, not a price tag.
