title: Transiting Saturn square natal Moon
---

For a stretch of months, with a probable retrograde encore, your needs and your duties grind against each other. Obligations at work or to family may crowd out rest; the people you lean on may be less available; moods can turn flat, pessimistic, or simply tired. The feeling that no one is taking care of you is common under this square, and it is data, not destiny.

The friction is exposing where your emotional support system depends on luck rather than arrangement. That discovery is unpleasant and genuinely useful: this period builds self-reliance in people who respond to it, and quiet resentment in people who only white-knuckle through. Guard against reading temporary heaviness as permanent truth about your relationships.

Treat your own maintenance as a scheduled obligation this season — sleep, meals, one protected evening a week — with the same seriousness you give everyone else's demands.
