title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Moon
---

For a few days, the social world and your private world make competing offers. Out there: pleasant company, invitations, people at their most charming. In here: the lamp, the quiet, the deep wish to be off duty. You can feel pulled to host, entertain, and please precisely when your reserves ask for stillness.

The sweetness is that both offers are genuine — the company really would be lovely, and so would the early night. The snag is trying to accept both at once: showing up hollow, smiling on schedule, then resenting people who only wanted your presence, not your performance.

Decline one thing this week, kindly and without a backup excuse — "I'm staying in" is a complete sentence. Then give the evening you saved its full flavor: warm light, good food, phone in the other room.
