title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Mercury
---

For a few weeks other people's grand plans arrive at your desk, and your own ideas keep meeting someone with a bigger counter-proposal. Advisors, salespeople, and enthusiasts all seem certain, and the pressure is to either swallow their vision whole or dismiss it entirely. Meanwhile your inbox and calendar swell with more input than one mind can honestly process.

The opposition's real work is calibration: your detailed, local knowledge is being confronted with a wider view that genuinely contains something you lack — along with a good deal of padding. Taken well, this is the season you upgrade a plan by arguing with someone who thinks in decades. Taken badly, it is the season you agree to things in meetings you regret by evening.

When a big claim lands, ask for one specific number or example before responding; how the claim survives that question tells you most of what you need.
