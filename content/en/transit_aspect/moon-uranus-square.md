title: Transiting Moon square natal Uranus
---

For a few hours each month the day develops a jangle. The Moon squares your natal Uranus: plans get poked, interruptions multiply, and a restless voltage builds against whatever your schedule insists on. You feel like a lid on a pot.

The gift is diagnostic. The urge to smash rarely picks at random — it lands on the routine, role, or agreement most overdue for honest change. The snag is acting on the voltage directly: quitting sideways, snapping at structure, breaking whatever happens to be nearest rather than what actually confines you.

Let the hours pass before you resign from anything. Write down precisely what you wanted to smash. When the same target shows up at this monthly touch three times, believe it.
