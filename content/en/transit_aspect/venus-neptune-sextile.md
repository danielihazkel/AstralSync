title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Neptune
---

For a few days, Venus sextiles your natal Neptune and gentleness becomes practical. Sympathy finds words that actually help; beauty shows up in modest places — steam off a cup, light on a wet street — and small kindnesses cost you almost nothing to give.

The sweetness is quiet attunement: you read what people need before they ask, and a little imagination graces the ordinary day. The snag is faint but real — soft-heartedness can slide into soft-headedness, agreeing to vague plans and vague favors that were never quite defined and never quite end.

Do one small merciful thing with edges. Donate a fixed amount, listen to one friend for one hour with your phone face-down, or frame the photograph that has lived in your camera roll for a year.
