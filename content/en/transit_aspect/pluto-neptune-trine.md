title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Neptune
---

Across a couple of years and several exact passes, Pluto trines your natal Neptune and deepens your inner life with unusual grace. Ideals compost gently: youthful fantasies break down into workable devotion, spirituality sheds its costume without a crisis of faith, and imagination gains enough gravity to become craft. Creative and healing work undertaken now can reach people at a level that surprises you.

The regeneration is quiet authority in the invisible realms, intuition you can trust, compassion that empowers rather than rescues, meaning that no longer depends on perfect circumstances. The trine's risk is seduction by depth itself: retreating from ordinary obligations into practice, art, or altered states, or using your growing psychological insight to steer others more than you admit.

Anchor the inspiration in one finished, visible offering, a body of work, a service, a teaching, so this depth circulates instead of pooling.
