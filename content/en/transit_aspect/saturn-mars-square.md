title: Transiting Saturn square natal Mars
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, obstruction is the theme: rules tighten exactly where you need slack, gatekeepers multiply, and the harder you push, the more precisely the world pushes back. Anger under this square runs hot but has nowhere clean to go, which is how it ends up discharged at bystanders, or turned inward as harsh self-assessment, or somatized as tension the body keeps.

The square is a forced audit of how you use force. Methods that rely on speed, charm, or momentum fail on schedule here, and what replaces them — preparation, paperwork done right, strength built slowly — is the durable version of your will. Most of what feels like sabotage is the cost of skipping steps finally coming due.

When you hit the wall this season, stop ramming and map it: find the one requirement you have been refusing to meet, meet it thoroughly, and watch the gate change its mind.
