title: Transiting Saturn square natal Neptune
---

For several months, including a likely retrograde revisit, Saturn squares your natal Neptune, and hard facts press on the softest parts of your worldview. An ideal you have organized your hopes around — a person, a calling, an institution, a version of the future — gets audited against its actual performance, and the numbers rarely flatter. Disillusionment is the signature feeling: foggy fatigue, a suspicion that the magic has drained out of things.

The cost is real grief for what turns out to have been partly illusion. The gain is uncontaminated vision. This square dissolves the inflated parts of a dream so the load-bearing part can be found, and what survives — the faith, the project, the devotion that still stands when romanticized hope is subtracted — is yours in a way the fantasy never was. The danger is numbing the process with escape instead of letting it finish.

When disappointment surfaces this season, ask what specifically was untrue, keep what remains, and act on that smaller, truer thing.
