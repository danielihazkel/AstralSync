title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Mars
---

For about a year, other people's volatility keeps crossing your path. Competitors change the rules mid-game, collaborators combust or defect on short notice, and strangers seem unusually ready to escalate. Your steady push forward keeps being interrupted by someone else's sudden swerve, and the temptation is to answer unpredictability with force — which reliably converts static into open conflict.

The opposition is a masterclass in reading energy you do not control. The skills it pays for are a fighter's: distance management, patience under provocation, refusing the bait that is only bait. Some interruptions carry information — the rebel opposite you is often acting out a freedom your own plans quietly need — but you can only hear that from a stance that is alert without being armed.

When someone erupts across your path this season, hold your line and lower your speed; respond to the pattern of their moves, never to the single spark.
