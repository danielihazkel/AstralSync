title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Neptune
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, Saturn gently supports your natal Neptune — the imprint of your generation's ideals and imagination as it lives in you. The practical world becomes quietly hospitable to your inner one: a creative practice fits into a real schedule, a spiritual discipline stops being aspirational and becomes a habit, a compassionate impulse finds an organization that can actually use it.

The payoff is craft. Inspiration that usually evaporates can be caught in form now — pages written, canvases finished, retreats booked and attended — and the structures you build for your imagination this season tend to serve it for years. The friction is faintness: both planets are working softly, and the invitation to give your dreams scaffolding can lose out to another season of pleasant vagueness where nothing is refused and nothing is made.

Attach one recurring appointment to your inner life — a weekly writing hour, sitting practice, or studio session — and keep it like a work meeting.
