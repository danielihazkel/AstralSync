title: Transiting Sun sextile natal Neptune
---

For a couple of days, twice a year, the Sun gives your natal Neptune gentle backing, and inspiration becomes practical. The daydream you carry in the background — the book, the garden, the cause — briefly connects to ordinary means: a helpful person appears, a small grant or free hour opens up, the right image arrives while you are washing dishes.

The opportunity is to give one soft thing a hard edge. Sketch the idea, book the volunteering shift, set up the quiet corner where the practice can live. Kindness offered now tends to land exactly where it is needed.

The friction is mistiness: sextiles whisper, and Neptune whispers in metaphor, so the whole window can pass as a pleasant mood that changes nothing.

Catch one intuition in writing before it dissolves, and take a single mundane step — an email, a purchase of supplies — toward the dream it belongs to.
