title: Transiting Pluto square natal Neptune
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto squares your natal Neptune and pressure-tests everything you take on faith. Illusions do not dissolve politely here; they are dredged. A cause reveals its machinery, a rescuer wants payment, a fantasy you have run on for decades stops producing comfort, and disenchantment can feel at first like loss of meaning itself.

It is not. It is the composting of meaning: the naive layer breaks down so a deeper, tougher faith can grow, one that includes the shadow and no longer needs perfection from its objects. That is the empowerment. The compulsive route is to grip the dissolving dream harder, through idealizing a person or movement, self-medicating the disillusion, or spinning conspiracies that keep the enchantment alive as dread.

When you catch yourself defending a belief from evidence, let the evidence in deliberately, and rebuild your faith from whatever remains standing.
