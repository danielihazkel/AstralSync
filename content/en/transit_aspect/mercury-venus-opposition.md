title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Venus
---

Someone whose taste differs from yours gets a speaking part for a few days. Transiting Mercury opposite your Venus stages small negotiations everywhere: what the room should look like, what the trip should cost, whether the gift is thoughtful or the quote is fair. The other side of each question is unusually well-argued.

The friction is feeling talked out of what you like. The opportunity is discovering which preferences survive being questioned — those are the real ones — and which you were holding out of habit.

Do not sign anything aesthetic or financial in the first flush of someone's persuasive case; sleep on it once. In matters of shared taste, propose a trade rather than a debate: "You choose this one, I choose the next." And ask the question that reopens stuck negotiations: "What would make this feel fair to you?"
