title: Transiting Jupiter square natal Pluto
---

During this stretch, transiting Jupiter squares your natal Pluto and inflates your themes of power and control under pressure. Ambition intensifies; so does the temptation to force outcomes — pushing a decision through, escalating a conflict, spending or leveraging more than the situation honestly needs. Power struggles with authorities or partners can flare over stakes that later look smaller than they felt.

The opportunity inside the pressure is honest ambition: this square shows you what you truly want enough to fight for, which is information worth the discomfort. The overreach risk is winning crookedly — securing the outcome while quietly torching the trust the outcome depends on.

Name the thing you are trying to force, and pursue it in the open, at a cost you would accept if the roles were reversed.
