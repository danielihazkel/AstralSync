title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Neptune
---

Once a month the Moon sextiles your natal Neptune and lends you a few gently hazed hours. Imagination cooperates instead of interrupting: the right word floats up, the hunch about a friend turns out accurate, the mundane task takes on a pleasant glow.

The gift is usable intuition — soft focus you can actually point at creative work, caregiving, or simply listening better than usual. The snag is drift. Hours this pleasant dissolve quietly, and an afternoon of agreeable fog can leave nothing behind but the vague sense of having been somewhere nice.

Give the mood ten minutes on paper: sketch the image, hum the phrase into a recorder, write the dream fragment down. Small captures like these, made monthly, become a surprisingly coherent archive of what moves you.
