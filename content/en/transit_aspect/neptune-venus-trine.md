title: Transiting Neptune trine natal Venus
---

For a year or more, love and imagination collaborate instead of colliding. Existing bonds soften — old grievances lose their charge, affection finds wordless channels — and new connections begin with an unusual quality of recognition. Creative work flows, charity feels natural, and beauty seems to seek you out. It is among the kindest long transits for the heart.

The kindness carries a mild sedation. With everything glowing, discernment naps: flattering company is mistaken for deep company, generosity flows without checking its recipients, and the relationship questions that deserved asking get deferred because the light is so good. Nothing breaks under a trine — but nothing is forced to become real, either.

Choose where the grace goes: deepen one bond deliberately, finish one piece of heartfelt work, and let the season's tide leave at least one thing permanently ashore.
