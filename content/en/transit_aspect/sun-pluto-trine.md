title: Transiting Sun trine natal Pluto
---

Twice a year the Sun makes a flowing angle to your natal Pluto, and for a few days you have stamina with a purpose. Concentration turns tidal: you can work, train, or study past the point where you would normally quit, and people sense the extra gravity — your requests get taken seriously, your presence steadies a room.

The opportunity is regeneration. Anything that rebuilds from the roots thrives now: physical conditioning, financial restructuring, the honest talk that resets a relationship's foundations. Influence offered to you this window is usually safe to accept, because you are unlikely to abuse it while the aspect flows.

The friction is quiet excess. Power that meets no resistance keeps pushing, and you can steamroll a gentle person or overwork a willing body without hearing a single complaint until later.

Choose one rebuilding project and pour the surplus into it, checking once a day that everyone involved is still consenting.
