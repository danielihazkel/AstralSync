title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Mars
---

For a few days, wanting and pursuing sit in the same chair. Attraction sharpens — to a person, yes, but also to projects, fabrics, food, the whole textured surface of things. You're magnetic in the specific way of someone who clearly wants something, and people respond to that before you've said a word.

The snag is spending the charge sideways: flirting in every direction and committing to none, sending signals so mixed they cancel out, or converting desire into a shopping burst because buying is the easiest form of pursuit. Heat this good deserves a target.

Make the move plainly. Tell the person you'd like to see them — a sentence, not a campaign. If the wanting isn't about a person, give it a project instead: start the thing you've been circling, tonight, while the appetite is honest.
