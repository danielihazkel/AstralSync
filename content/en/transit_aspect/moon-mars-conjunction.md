title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Mars
---

The Moon crosses your Mars and lights the pilot flame. For a few hours you have more push than usual: chores that sat for a week get done in one burst, and it is easier to say the direct thing, ask the bold question, or start the workout. Your body wants motion and your temper is closer to the surface — those are the same current wearing two faces.

The snag is the shortened fuse. Interruptions feel like insults, slow traffic feels personal, and a flash of irritation can spend the whole burst on a pointless skirmish.

Give the energy a physical job early — move, lift, clean, walk fast — and the rest of the mood tends to behave.
