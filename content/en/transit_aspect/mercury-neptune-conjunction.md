For a few days, language turns porous. Images, hunches, and half-remembered dreams drift into your sentences, and you can finally describe feelings that usually live below the waterline. It is a rare window for poetry, apology, prayer, lyrics — anything where precision matters less than truth.

The same fog that softens your words blurs the facts. Numbers swim, instructions get misheard, and you may hear promises no one actually made. This is a poor week to sign, quote, or diagnose from memory.

Keep two notebooks running, in effect: one for the images, one for the errands, and never let them merge. Tonight, write the metaphor down exactly as it came to you, unexplained. Tomorrow, confirm the meeting time in writing, exactly as agreed.
