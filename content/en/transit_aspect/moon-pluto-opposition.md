title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Pluto
---

A few hours each month, intensity faces you from across the table. The Moon opposes your natal Pluto, and someone else seems to carry the charge — probing questions, a jealous edge, a magnetic pull, a conversation that feels like chess.

The gift is disclosure by reflection: what compels or repels you most in them is usually a disowned piece of your own wanting, easier to study at arm's length than in the mirror. The snag is the tug-of-war — matching pressure with pressure, or reading manipulation into every pause until you find some.

Notice your body: the jaw, the grip, the held breath — and notice it loosen as the hours pass, proof this was weather. Then note who reliably pulls this hard. The roster repeats, and it's instructive.
