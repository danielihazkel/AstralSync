title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Uranus
---

Over roughly two years, with several exact passes, Pluto crosses your natal Uranus and excavates the rebel you buried to get along. Old defiance, the causes, inventions, and refusals you shelved for respectability, resurfaces with compulsive force. You may feel sudden intolerance for arrangements you accepted for years, or find yourself electrified by ideas your younger self abandoned.

The regenerative promise is integration: reclaiming your originality as usable power rather than adolescent protest. People who work with this transit often reinvent a career, break a genuinely dead pattern, or finally build the unconventional thing they kept postponing. The risk is rebellion as compulsion, burning structures merely to feel free, mistaking provocation for authenticity, or being controlled by the very urge to be uncontrollable.

When the urge to break something arises, wait one deliberate beat and ask whether this is liberation or just detonation, then act only on the first.
