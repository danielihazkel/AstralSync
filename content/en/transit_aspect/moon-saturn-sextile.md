title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Saturn
---

Once a month the Moon sextiles your natal Saturn and hands you a few quietly steady hours. The mood is unglamorous and slightly cool — and perfectly suited to paperwork, tidying, budgets, and the small maintenance your life keeps deferring.

The gift is patience you didn't have to manufacture: realism sits comfortably instead of heavily, and finishing things feels mildly satisfying rather than grim. The snag is that these hours are boring enough to waste. Nothing announces them; they pass as an unremarkable stretch of afternoon.

Claim one of them. Do the fifteen-minute chore you've dodged all month — the form, the filing, the phone call. Over time, notice which chore is always waiting when these steady hours come around.
