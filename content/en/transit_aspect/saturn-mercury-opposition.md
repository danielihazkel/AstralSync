title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Mercury
---

For several months your ideas answer to an external examiner: an institution, a regulator, a senior skeptic, a contract's exact wording. Communication feels like testimony — statements get quoted back, casual estimates harden into commitments, and the burden of proof sits, fairly or not, on your side of the table. Negotiations run long and cold.

What the opposition builds, if you let it, is precision under pressure. Positions that survive an unsympathetic reading are the only ones worth holding, and the discipline of writing for a hostile audience upgrades your thinking permanently. The trap is either capitulation — adopting the examiner's view to end the discomfort — or a defensive crouch that stops listening exactly when listening is the advantage.

Put everything important in writing this season, ask for the same in return, and concede quickly on the points where the other side is simply right.
