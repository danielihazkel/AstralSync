title: Transiting Sun square natal Mars
---

The Sun squares your natal Mars, and for a few days the world seems staffed entirely by slow walkers and bad drivers. Friction between what you will and what circumstances allow generates real heat: arguments spark faster, machines misbehave, and forcing an outcome becomes tempting precisely when it works worst.

The energy itself is clean — high-grade fuel arriving faster than your usual engine burns it. Anger this week is mostly surplus power with nowhere to go.

Burn it on purpose: hard exercise, heavy lifting, a demanding physical chore. Postpone confrontations by a day or two when you can; the same words will land better after the square separates.
