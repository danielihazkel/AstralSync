title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Jupiter
---

For a few days, Venus opposes your natal Jupiter and other people become the mirror for your abundance. Someone else's celebration pulls you in, someone else's generosity obliges you, and you may find yourself matching a bigness you did not order.

The sweetness is being swept up: a party you did not plan, praise you did not fish for, a companion whose enthusiasm doubles your own. The snag is escalation. Oppositions love a seesaw, and two generous people can talk each other into a weekend neither can afford, or a promise neither will love keeping.

Say yes to the occasion and no to the arms race. Bring flowers instead of matching the champagne, and notice who in the room is being lavish because they are happy — and who because they are nervous.
