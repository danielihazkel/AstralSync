title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Moon
---

Over the year or more this once-in-a-lifetime transit takes, the basement of your emotional life is opened for inspection. Feelings arrive with archaeological layers attached: a present-day slight excavates a childhood wound, a family event unearths dynamics everyone had agreed to forget. Attachments intensify, and so does your awareness of the strings attached to them — who feeds whom, and at what price.

This is a demolition-and-rebuild of your security system, and it is thorough. Bonds capable of honesty deepen into something nearly unbreakable; bonds sustained by pretense tend not to survive, and their endings, however painful, are diagnostic. The failure modes are emotional blackmail — using the new intensity to control — and burying the surfacing material under compulsive busyness.

Get skilled support for the excavation: a therapist, a wise elder, a rigorous practice. What you process consciously this year becomes strength; what you refuse becomes symptom.
