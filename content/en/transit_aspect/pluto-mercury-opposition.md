title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Mercury
---

Across two to three years, you negotiate with minds that want to win. A controlling communicator — a boss, an institution, a relentless adversary, sometimes a loved one — contests your version of events, and the contest is asymmetric: they seem to have more information, more patience, or fewer scruples. You may find your words quoted selectively, your questions answered with questions, your confidence eroded by expert insinuation.

The opposition trains intellectual sovereignty. Its gift, bought expensively, is the ability to stay inside your own judgment while under persuasion pressure — to be influenced by evidence and unmoved by force. The failure modes mirror each other: capitulating to the stronger frame, or becoming as obsessed with winning as the opponent and losing years to the duel.

Keep records, verify independently, and decide in advance which questions are yours to answer; power fades surprisingly fast when it cannot set the agenda.
