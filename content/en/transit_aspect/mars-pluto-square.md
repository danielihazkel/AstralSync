title: Transiting Mars square natal Pluto
---

For a week or two, Mars grinds against your natal Pluto, and ordinary friction starts feeling like war. Small slights read as threats; a disagreement about logistics becomes a contest about who yields. You may notice a compulsive quality in your own pushing — unable to drop it, checking again, needing to win rather than to finish.

The buried gift is that this pressure X-rays your power arrangements. Wherever you feel most provoked is where control is genuinely contested, and seeing that clearly is worth the discomfort. The hazards are the classic ones: the scorched-earth email, the grudge nursed into policy, the injury from training or working past every warning signal.

Defuse deliberately. Delay any confrontation you can, and script the one you cannot — three sentences, no history lesson. Burn the surplus intensity on heavy, honest labor: lifting, digging, scrubbing, clearing. Decide in advance what winning would even mean, and stop there.
