title: Transiting Saturn square natal Pluto
---

For several months, including a likely retrograde revisit, Saturn squares your natal Pluto, and the structures of your life press hard on your deepest strategies for control. Your generation carries a particular stamp around power and survival; this transit finds where it lives in you and applies load. Authority may feel heavier than usual — a demanding institution, a financial squeeze, a power struggle at work or home in which neither side can simply win.

The cost of this season is honest: it can feel grinding, and the temptation to respond with force — ultimatums, secrecy, scorched earth — is strong precisely because you feel cornered. The payoff is a purge. Squares expose which of your control mechanisms are actually strength and which are armor rusted into the skin. What you release under this pressure — a grudge, a manipulation, an unwinnable fight — stops draining you permanently.

When you feel the urge to tighten your grip this season, ask what you are afraid will happen if you loosen it, and test that fear once.
