title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Mars
---

For a year or more, your directness meets dissolving targets. Opponents will not fight cleanly: commitments soften when tested, blame diffuses through systems no one owns, and the person undermining your project does it with such sincerity that confrontation makes you look like the aggressor. Efforts to pin things down produce apologies, delays, and fog.

The opposition trains a subtler assertiveness. Force fails against mist by design — the lesson is to stop supplying the collision the confusion feeds on. What works is documentation, patience, and letting outcomes rather than arguments expose what words keep blurring. It is also worth checking the mirror: under this transit your own commitments can be the vague ones, promising an availability your energy cannot honor.

Put shared undertakings in writing with dates attached, deliver your own promises at the size you can actually keep, and let anything that only exists as atmosphere miss its deadline without you.
