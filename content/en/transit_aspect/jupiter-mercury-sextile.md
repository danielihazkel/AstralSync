title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Mercury
---

Useful information finds you easily for a couple of weeks: the right article, the mentor who answers the email, the meeting where your suggestion lands. Learning feels lighter than usual, and small acts of outreach — a question asked, a draft shared — tend to return more than they cost.

Sextiles pay only on participation, and that is the catch here. The opportunity is an open door, not a delivery; if you keep your ideas in the notebook and your questions unasked, the window closes without a trace and the period reads, in hindsight, as merely pleasant.

Pick one conversation you have been postponing — the pitch, the inquiry, the request for advice — and have it while the listening is this good.
