title: Transiting Mercury square natal Sun
---

For a couple of days, what people hear is not quite what you meant. The square from transiting Mercury to your Sun puts a slight torque on self-expression: your tone reads sharper in text than it sounded in your head, or a casual comment gets taken as a position.

The useful side is that friction reveals fuzzy thinking. If explaining your choice to a skeptic feels hard right now, part of the explanation may genuinely be missing. Pressure on the wording is free editing.

Reread anything important before sending, especially anything about yourself, your role, or credit for work. If a message annoys you, wait a few hours to answer. Then ask one clarifying question — "what did you take from what I said?" — before you defend anything.
