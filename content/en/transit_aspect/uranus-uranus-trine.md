title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Uranus
---

The Uranus-to-Uranus trine opens the smooth reinvention windows of the cycle, first in the late twenties, as you refine the independence claimed earlier into something workable, and again in the mid-fifties, when experiments no longer threaten the foundations. For a year or more, across the usual retrograde returns, change and stability stop fighting: you can alter course without capsizing.

The breakthrough is voluntary evolution, updating your work, your look, your circles, your stance, from curiosity rather than emergency. Because nothing pushes, the risk is drift: letting a fertile window close with the same routines intact, or scattering the ease across a dozen shallow novelties instead of one real renewal.

Choose the reinvention you would want a crisis to force on you eventually, and begin it now, while it can be done gracefully.
