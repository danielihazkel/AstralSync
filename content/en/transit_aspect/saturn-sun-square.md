title: Transiting Saturn square natal Sun
---

For several months, including a likely retrograde revisit, you may feel tested by circumstances that refuse to move: a boss who blocks the promotion, a body that demands rest, a project that reveals every corner you cut. Saturn squares have a way of locating the exact gap between the person you present and the competence underneath, and pressing on it.

This is discouraging by design, not punitive by nature. The square forces a choice between doing the unglamorous work of closing that gap and quietly resenting whoever seems to be holding you back. People who use this period to upgrade their skills or restructure their obligations usually look back on it as a turning point; people who only endure it tend to meet the same wall at the next square.

Identify the criticism that stings because it is partly true, and spend this season answering it with practice rather than argument.
