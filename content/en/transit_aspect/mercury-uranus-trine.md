title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Uranus
---

For a few days, your originality is easy to explain. The perspective that usually earns you puzzled looks suddenly translates: you can show people the future you see without frightening them. Old problems reorganize themselves the moment you describe them out loud to someone new.

The gentle risk is treating the ease as a mood instead of a tool. Insights that feel obvious now will be genuinely hard to reconstruct next week, and nothing about this transit forces you to preserve them.

So preserve them. Record yourself talking through your best current idea for five unpolished minutes, or type the explanation as fast as you can without fixing typos. Then send it to one future-minded friend with a single question attached: what is the first thing you would build with this?
