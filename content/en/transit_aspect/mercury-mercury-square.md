title: Transiting Mercury square natal Mercury
---

For a couple of days the world's information arrives in a format your mind finds slightly wrong. Instructions skip the step you needed. Meetings run at the wrong speed. People who summarize when you want detail give you summaries; people who ramble when you want the point, ramble.

This is friction, not failure. A square between the current Mercury and your own is a workout for translation: restating, asking again, converting someone's spreadsheet brain to your story brain or back. The skill you build here outlasts the transit, which returns in some form a few times a year.

Budget extra time for anything procedural — forms, bookings, handoffs — and confirm the details in writing. When confused, resist bluffing. The most productive sentence available is: "Can you walk me through that once more, slower?"
