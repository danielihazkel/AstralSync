title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Sun
---

Once every twelve years, Jupiter returns to your Sun and opens a new chapter of growth. During these few weeks, confidence rises, doors seem to swing open with less effort, and people respond to you as if you were slightly larger than life. It is one of the friendliest windows the sky offers for launching something that carries your name on it.

The catch is inflation. Jupiter magnifies whatever it touches, including your blind spots, so overcommitment, overspending, and promising more than you can deliver are the classic missteps. The transit rewards genuine ambition and quietly punishes grandiosity, and the difference is often invisible from the inside while your optimism is running hot.

Pick the one venture you would still be proud of a year from now and pour the extra energy there, rather than scattering it across everything that glitters.
