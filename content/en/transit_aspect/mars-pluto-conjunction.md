title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Pluto
---

Roughly once every two years, Mars spends a week or two fused to your natal Pluto, and the volume of your will turns up past its usual limit. You want what you want with unusual intensity, and you can suddenly see the levers — who holds power, what they fear, where the pressure point is. Obsession feels like clarity.

Used consciously, this is surgical strength: the deep clean, the debt attacked at the root, the confrontation you have needed for months, done once and done completely. Used unconsciously, it is the vendetta week — control disguised as help, escalation disguised as honesty, exhaustion disguised as dedication.

Pick one worthy target and empty the intensity into it, preferably something that cannot fight back: the renovation, the hardest training block, the archive purge, the addiction you are ready to starve. Watch your grip in conversations, and let others keep their exits.
