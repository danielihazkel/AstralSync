title: Transiting Sun square natal Pluto
---

For a few days, twice a year, the Sun grinds against your natal Pluto and power struggles find you. A gatekeeper digs in, a rival tests your position, or a compulsion you thought was handled — checking, spending, controlling — taps you on the shoulder. Stakes feel higher than the situation justifies.

The friction is the fight itself. Under this pressure you want to win more than you want to be right, and tactics get heavier than the moment deserves. Whatever battle you choose this window, expect to be remembered for how you fought it.

The opportunity is X-ray honesty about your own motives. The annual return of this aspect lights the same deep drive each time; noticing what you reach for under pressure tells you what the long transformation underneath is actually about.

When you feel the grip tighten, name what you are afraid of losing — out loud if possible — before you make a single move.
