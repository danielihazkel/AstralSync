title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Neptune
---

For a few days, you speak the language of the tide. Empathy comes without effort, symbols make sense, and you can comfort someone with words you did not know you had. Music, imagery, and memory braid into your thinking; even routine writing picks up warmth.

The friction is faint edges. In this current, you agree easily to keep the mood soft, and details you glossed over will still be waiting when the water recedes. Compassion that says yes to everything helps no one for long.

Point the gift at something dormant. Reread an old journal, letter, or piece of your own creative work from years ago — this transit reveals what it was actually about. Then write one page in that same voice, and tell one person the kind thing you have been thinking but not saying.
