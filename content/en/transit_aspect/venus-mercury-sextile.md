For a few days, conversations go down easy. Negotiations soften at the edges, the awkward coworker turns out to be funny, and messages you'd been dreading answer themselves in two friendly lines. Words and goodwill are trading at a favorable rate.

The snag is pleasant filler. Talk flows so smoothly that you can spend the whole transit on weather, plans, and podcasts while the one real sentence — the appreciation, the apology, the ask — stays comfortably undelivered. Ease loves a detour.

Send the kind message you've been half-drafting in your head: the overdue reply, the "I keep thinking about what you said," the note to someone whose work quietly helped you. It will land better this week than most, and it costs you two minutes and one small moment of sincerity.
