title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Saturn
---

For a couple of years, through several exact passes, Pluto sextiles your natal Saturn and offers steady power for structural renovation. This is the rare season when foundations can be rebuilt without the building falling down around you: debts restructured, a career path quietly re-founded, a discipline deepened until it becomes genuine mastery. Authority figures tend to respond well to your seriousness, and long-term plans laid now have unusual staying power.

The empowerment lies in voluntary demolition, letting go of one outgrown duty or defense at a time, before circumstances force the issue. The risk is consolidation for its own sake: tightening control over schedules, money, or people because thoroughness feels virtuous, until prudence hardens into a quiet compulsion to manage everything.

Choose one structure that must carry the next decade and renovate it deliberately this year, while the pressure is low.
