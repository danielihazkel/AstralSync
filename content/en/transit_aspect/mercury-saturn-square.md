title: Transiting Mercury square natal Saturn
---

For a few days, every sentence meets a wall. Replies come back slow or cold, a gatekeeper asks for the form you did not fill out, and your own inner editor rejects drafts before they reach a second line. It can feel like being graded by someone who never smiles.

The wall is doing you a favor: it shows exactly where your thinking is unsupported. What survives this scrutiny is genuinely solid. What does not was going to fail later, more expensively. If Mercury is in its retrograde loop, the same objection may return twice more over a couple of months — same wall, better answers each time.

Do not argue with the critic in your head; interview it. Write down its exact objection, then answer only that, in one paragraph, with evidence.
