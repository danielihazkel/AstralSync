title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Jupiter
---

For a week or two, roughly once every two years, Mars lights the fuse on your natal Jupiter. Whatever you believe in most gets an engine: the pitch you have been sitting on, the trip you keep postponing, the cause you defend at dinner tables. Your appetite for risk climbs, and confidence arrives before evidence does.

The power here is momentum — doors open because you finally push them. The hazard is overreach: promising three things when one would land, betting the whole stake because the first bet paid off, turning enthusiasm into a lecture.

Channel it into one bold, bounded move. Launch the thing, book the meeting, sign up for the race — then write down what you promised, so the version of you two weeks from now can actually deliver it.
