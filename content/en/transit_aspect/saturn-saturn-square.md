Roughly seven years from your last Saturn return, this transit is the structural test of the 29.5-year cycle — the moment the building you commissioned at the return gets its load inspected. For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, strain shows up exactly where your commitments were vague: the role you accepted but never renegotiated, the routine that carries more weight than it was designed for, the promise you have outgrown.

The friction is that this rarely feels philosophical; it feels like fatigue, deadline pressure, and a creeping doubt about choices you once felt sure of. The payoff is precision. Squares locate weak joints while they are still repairable, and people who renegotiate honestly here — adjusting the job, the schedule, the terms — carry a sounder structure into the next quarter of the cycle instead of hauling cracks forward.

Find the commitment that costs the most quiet resentment, and renegotiate its terms out loud this season.
