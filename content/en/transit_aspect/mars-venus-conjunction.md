title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Venus
---

Desire stops being polite. With Mars on your Venus for a week or two, attraction turns active: you want to pursue rather than be found, and passivity in love, art, or pleasure feels suddenly unbearable. Charisma runs hot, and people notice.

The power is the courage to want out loud — to make the first move, name the attraction, ask for the raise your work deserves, buy the instrument and actually play it. The hazards travel in the same package: impulse spending dressed up as self-love, come-ons that skip consent for enthusiasm, and pleasure chased hard enough to become its own chore.

Pursue one desire deliberately. Make the invitation, start the creative piece, book the experience — and put a 48-hour hold on any purchase over an amount you name before the week starts.
