title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Venus
---

For a few days your words wear better clothes. Transiting Mercury on your Venus lends charm to ordinary speech: compliments come out specific instead of generic, requests sound like invitations, and negotiations about price, taste, or terms tilt toward the person who can phrase things pleasantly — which is currently you.

Charm has a shadow: agreeableness. It is easy this week to say yes to keep the music playing, or to soften a boundary until it no longer holds anything. A beautifully worded concession is still a concession.

Use the silver tongue where it serves you. Send the appreciative note you keep composing in your head — to the mentor, the vendor, the friend — and make it concrete: name the exact thing they did. Then, in any negotiation, state your actual number before the pleasantries dissolve it.
