title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Mars
---

Once a year the Sun opposes your natal Mars, and your assertiveness comes back at you from across the table. Other people seem pushier, deadlines and rivals press, and open opposition — an argument, a standoff, a competitor's move — is likelier in this window than most.

The advantage hidden in the confrontation: adversaries show you your own blind spots for free. What someone opposes hardest this week is often the exact place your plan or your manner needs reinforcing.

Do not duck the contest, but choose your ground. Fight for the point that matters, concede the ones that do not, and note precisely which criticism you found hardest to hear.
