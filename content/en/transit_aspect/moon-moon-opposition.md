title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Moon
---

Once a month the Moon faces your natal Moon from the opposite shore, and your needs become visible mainly through contrast. Someone else's way of seeking comfort — clingier, cooler, louder than yours — throws your own style into relief. For a few hours you can see your emotional habits almost as a stranger would, which is rare and genuinely useful.

The snag is polarization: it is tempting to decide the other person is needy and you are reasonable, when really you are two halves of the same seesaw.

Tonight, notice which of someone else's needs annoys you most, then ask whether it is one you deny in yourself; the honest answer belongs in your journal.
