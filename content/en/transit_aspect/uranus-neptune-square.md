title: Transiting Uranus square natal Neptune
---

For a year or more, with multiple exact passes, Uranus grinds against your natal Neptune, and the friction lands on whatever you have been idealizing: a hero, a cause, an escape, a version of the future you use to soothe yourself. Sudden events puncture the fog, or a new obsession floods in with suspicious brightness, and either way the difference between vision and evasion becomes urgent.

The breakthrough is disillusionment in its literal, useful sense, the removal of illusion, leaving ideals that can bear weight. People often emerge from this square with a sturdier faith and fewer fantasies. The risk is trading one dream for a flashier one, sudden conversions, magical schemes, or numbing the disturbance with whatever dissolves it fastest.

When certainty surges or collapses this year, delay irreversible decisions until you have slept on them many times.
