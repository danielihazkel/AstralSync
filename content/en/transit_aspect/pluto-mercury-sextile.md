title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Mercury
---

For a couple of years, depth is available without turbulence. Your concentration can bear down when you choose — research, analysis, and difficult reading yield more per hour — and your words carry a quiet penetrating quality: people bring you their real questions because you visibly do not flinch. Studies of the hidden — psychology, finance, systems, the past — reward you unusually well now.

The sextile leaves the choosing to you. Depth on offer is not depth achieved; the transformative course of study, the honest conversation series, the archive finally opened all wait politely and forever. There is also a mild occupational hazard for good listeners: becoming the keeper of everyone's secrets while disclosing none of your own.

Commit to one deep inquiry with a deliverable — the certification, the family history written down, the hard book actually finished — and let it change what you think.
