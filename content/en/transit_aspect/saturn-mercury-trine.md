title: Transiting Saturn trine natal Mercury
---

Your judgment is unusually sound for a few months. Details stay organized without heroic effort, long projects advance on schedule, and what you say carries a weight people respond to — this is the season colleagues start treating your estimates as the reliable ones. Work requiring sustained precision, from research to contracts to code, goes uncommonly well.

The shadow is conservatism on autopilot. With structure feeling this natural, novel ideas can get filed under "later" simply because they are untidy, and you may build excellent systems around assumptions that deserved to be questioned first. Steadiness is a tool here, not a worldview.

Use the window for the long-haul intellectual commitment you could not sustain in noisier seasons — and deliberately let one unruly, unproven idea into the plan before you seal it.
