title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Saturn
---

Over roughly two years, with several exact passes, Pluto crosses your natal Saturn and inspects every load-bearing structure in your life: career scaffolding, financial commitments, the rules you inherited about duty and worth. Whatever is genuinely sound gets stress-tested and reinforced. Whatever is hollow, a role outgrown, a fear masquerading as prudence, an authority obeyed out of habit, starts to crack, sometimes through direct confrontation with bosses, institutions, or your own limits.

This is demolition in the service of re-founding. People who cooperate with it rebuild on bedrock and emerge with an authority that no longer needs external endorsement. People who cling to condemned structures exhaust themselves defending walls that are already coming down, or turn rigid and controlling to mask the fear underneath.

Inspect your obligations honestly, keep the ones you would choose again today, and begin dismantling the rest on your own schedule.
