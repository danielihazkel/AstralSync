title: Transiting Sun trine natal Mercury
---

With the Sun trine your natal Mercury, thought and intention move as one. Explanations land on the first try, decisions that stalled for weeks resolve in minutes, and your memory serves up exactly the fact you need mid-sentence. It is a fine few days for negotiation, drafting, and thinking out loud.

Fluency has a quiet cost: because everything sounds right, weak arguments slip through unexamined, yours included.

Use the window for the hardest verbal task on your list — the contract review, the tricky conversation, the plan that needs a final shape — and stress-test one conclusion you reached too smoothly before you act on it.
