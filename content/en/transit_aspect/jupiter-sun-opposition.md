title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Sun
---

Opportunity arrives through other people during these weeks, and so does exaggeration. A partner, client, or rival may present an offer that looks larger than life, or you may find yourself in negotiations where both sides are overstating their case. The expansion is real, but it sits across the table from you rather than in your own hands, which makes discernment the whole game.

The failure mode is twofold: dismissing a genuine opening because it comes wrapped in someone else's agenda, or swallowing a pitch whole because your own optimism is running high. Oppositions externalize what conjunctions internalize, so watch for people who mirror your ambitions back at you in flattering, slightly distorted form.

Take every promising proposal seriously and none of them at face value: sleep on the big decision, check one independent reference, and negotiate as if the glow will fade — because in a few weeks, it will.
