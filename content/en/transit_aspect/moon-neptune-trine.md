title: Transiting Moon trine natal Neptune
---

Each month the Moon trines your natal Neptune and grants a few soft-focused hours where the world seems kinder than the news suggests. Empathy is effortless, beauty is everywhere at eye level, and your inner critic dozes off mid-sentence.

The gift is gentle inspiration: ideas arrive without strain, and people open up around you because you've briefly stopped bracing. It's your imagination working at body temperature. The snag is the flattering light — in these hours you idealize easily, forgive prematurely, and let small important things slide because they'd spoil the glow.

Enjoy it fully; this is one of the month's genuinely sweet windows. Just reread anything consequential before you send it, and notice afterward which people and projects looked most golden in this light.
