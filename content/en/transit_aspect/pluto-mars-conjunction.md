title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Mars
---

Across two to three years, your will is taken down to the studs and rebuilt. Ambition intensifies past comfortable levels: you want outcomes, not activities, and you discover reserves of stamina and ruthlessness you did not know were on the premises. Work capacity can become genuinely formidable — this is a transit under which people build companies, bodies, and reputations from bedrock.

The same current is dangerous unmanaged. Drive can become compulsion, competition can become vendetta, and the refusal to lose can spend things — health, relationships, scruples — that were not yours to spend. Rage, when it comes, comes from deeper strata than the provocation explains. Power struggles sought out now tend to escalate to the bottom.

Yoke the force to one worthy long project and physical training that empties the tank regularly; decide your ethical lines before you are tested, because you will be.
