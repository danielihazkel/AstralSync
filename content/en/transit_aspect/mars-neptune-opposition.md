title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Neptune
---

For a week or two, Mars stands opposite your natal Neptune, and other people become hard to read at exactly the moment you feel pushed to act. Someone's story does not add up; a collaborator goes passive just when you accelerate; you may be cast as the aggressor in a script you never agreed to. Direct force seems to pass through the problem like a hand through mist.

The power here is compassion with a spine: this transit can teach you to defend your energy and your facts without cruelty, and to spot the difference between someone who needs help and something that merely drains. The hazards are projection and seduction — attacking a phantom motive, or being quietly steered by flattery, pity, or a beautiful vagueness.

Slow every commitment down. Ask clarifying questions twice, confirm plans in writing, keep your substances minimal and your sleep long, and give the urge to rescue or accuse a day of quiet before you act on it.
