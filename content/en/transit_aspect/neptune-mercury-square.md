title: Transiting Neptune square natal Mercury
---

For a year or more, in several passes, your information supply is unreliable. Facts arrive incomplete, instructions change after you have acted on them, and at least one narrative you trusted — about a job, a person, a plan — develops holes. Concentration runs foggy in stretches, and decisions feel like navigating by instruments you are not sure are calibrated.

The square's deeper work is disillusionment in the literal sense: a story you were living inside stops holding, and the confusion is the gap between its collapse and the truer account that has not yet formed. Rushing to replace one certainty with another is the classic error; so is filling the gap with whoever sounds most confident. Deception, including self-deception, finds its best market in exactly this weather.

Defer major irreversible decisions where you can, verify claims independently when you cannot, and treat "I don't know yet" as a legitimate position rather than a failure.
