title: Transiting Uranus square natal Venus
---

For roughly a year, in several passes, your attachments are tested by voltage. Restlessness spikes inside settled arrangements, a disruptive attraction may cut across an existing commitment, and other people's sudden moves — the friend who relocates, the partner who changes course — rearrange your emotional furniture without consulting you. Money can jolt in sympathy: windfalls, impulse purchases, surprise expenses.

The square is separating what you genuinely value from what you were merely used to, and it does this crudely, by shaking everything and seeing what holds. Bonds with living roots usually survive and often improve, once the demand for more honesty and room is spoken instead of acted out. The classic casualty is the good relationship detonated for an excitement that had a half-life of months.

When the urge to break something arrives, ask what specific freedom you are missing — then request that freedom explicitly before treating escape as the only door.
