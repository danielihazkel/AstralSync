title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Sun
---

For a few days, the messenger sits right on your Sun, and words about who you are come easier than usual. Introductions land. If you have been meaning to describe your work, your project, or yourself in one clean sentence, this is the window to draft it.

The friction is speed: with self-expression this fluent, you can talk past people, announce before you have decided, or polish the pitch instead of the plan. Say it once, then stop and listen for what comes back.

This contact returns about three times a year, so treat it as a check-in on your own headline. Write the two-line version of what you are building right now, and read it aloud to one person who will tell you the truth.
