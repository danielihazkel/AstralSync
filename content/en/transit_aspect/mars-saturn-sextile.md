title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Saturn
---

For about a week on its two-year circuit, transiting Mars offers your natal Saturn cooperation instead of combat. Drive and discipline shake hands: you can work steadily without burning out, and the boring parts of your ambitions — paperwork, budgets, maintenance, training — suddenly feel doable rather than deadening.

The power is durable progress. Anything you build now tends to hold, because you are laying brick with patience instead of speed. Elders and gatekeepers are more receptive to a well-prepared ask than usual. The hazard is underuse: nothing forces your hand, so the week can pass in tidy routine while the structural work stays untouched.

Schedule the unglamorous move you keep deferring — the contract review, the debt payment plan, the strength program, the conversation with the boss. Start it now, small and on a calendar, and let repetition do the rest.
