title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Sun
---

Over the long stretch this aspect stays active, depth becomes available on friendly terms. You can look at your own machinery — motives, patterns, inherited scripts — without the crisis that usually forces such honesty. Influence accrues quietly too: people sense a new substance in you, and doors open to roles with real leverage rather than mere visibility.

A Pluto sextile is a standing invitation to voluntary transformation, and invitations can be declined by simple inaction. Therapy, serious training, strategic career moves, taking command of your finances — undertakings like these, begun now, draw on unusual stamina and tend to reach bedrock. Skipped, the period passes as ordinary time, and the same depths wait for a harder aspect to open them.

Choose one area where you have been operating at half power and commit to a sustained, structured deepening of it this year. The endurance for long projects is temporarily doubled; spend it.
