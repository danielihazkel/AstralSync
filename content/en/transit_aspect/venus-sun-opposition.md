title: Transiting Venus opposition natal Sun
---

For a few days, other people act as your mirror. A partner, friend, or even a pleasant stranger shows you how you come across — mostly kindly, sometimes with an accuracy that stings a little. Relationships want balancing now: whose turn, whose choice, whose evening.

The sweetness is real company and the relief of not having to run the show. The snag is dimming yourself to keep things smooth — swallowing your preference, then feeling faintly cheated by an evening everyone else enjoyed. Peace bought that way has a short shelf life.

Let someone else choose the restaurant or the film, and enjoy being a guest in their taste. But state your own preference once, plainly, before you defer — being agreeable is lovelier when it's a gift rather than a disappearance.
