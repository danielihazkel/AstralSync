title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Moon
---

Other people seem to know exactly which button to press this week. Mars opposes your Moon, and provocation arrives from outside — a coworker's tone, a partner's timing, a stranger's carelessness — while the reaction wells up from somewhere old and deep. The tug-of-war is usually between what others demand and what home requires.

The power hidden in the discomfort is diagnosis: whoever angers you most is pointing, crudely, at a need you have been outsourcing or ignoring. Met consciously, this transit ends with a clearer contract between you and the people who rely on you.

Do not litigate feelings at peak temperature. Say "not now, but soon," mean it, and schedule the conversation. In the meantime, defend your recovery time — sleep and solitude are the pressure valves here.
