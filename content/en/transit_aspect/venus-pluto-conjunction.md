title: Transiting Venus conjunction natal Pluto
---

For a few days, Venus sits on your natal Pluto and affection stops being casual. Whatever you like this week, you want; whoever holds your attention holds all of it. Conversations go from small talk to confession in one drink, and you can feel who in the room is actually alive to you.

The sweetness is depth on demand: loyalty declared, honesty that would be too much any other week and is exactly right now. The snag is grip. Wanting can shade into monitoring — rereading messages for tone, testing devotion, needing to know rather than trusting.

Spend the intensity where it deepens rather than surveils. Tell one person the true weight they carry in your life, retire one small jealousy deliberately, and let one thing you desire remain unpursued, just to prove you can.
