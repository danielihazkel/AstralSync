title: Transiting Venus trine natal Uranus
---

Venus trines your natal Uranus for a few days, and being yourself becomes socially frictionless. The quirk you usually sand down is exactly what charms people now; the unconventional preference gets a warm reception; the mixed crowd that shouldn't work does.

The sweetness is freedom without a fight — you can experiment, in style or company or plan, and the world meets it with curiosity rather than raised eyebrows. The snag is only that the ease is easy to waste: with nothing pushing, you may spend these open days on the default settings you didn't even want.

Try the thing you have quietly wondered about. Wear it, cook it, attend it, say it. And notice which unusual person makes you feel more like yourself, not less — that is data worth keeping past the transit.
