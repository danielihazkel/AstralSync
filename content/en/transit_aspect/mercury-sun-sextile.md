title: Transiting Mercury sextile natal Sun
---

A light, cooperative angle between the day's Mercury and your Sun opens small doors for a few days. Emails get answered. The person you have been meaning to reach is suddenly reachable. Nothing dramatic, just a lower cost for speaking up on your own behalf.

The catch with easy aspects is that they wait for you to move. The door opens; it does not pull you through. If you spend these days only replying, the window closes with nothing new started.

Pick one conversation that would advance something you actually care about, and open it deliberately. Send the message that begins, "I've been meaning to ask you about..." and name the thing plainly.
