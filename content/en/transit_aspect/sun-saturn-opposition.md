title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Saturn
---

Once a year the Sun faces your natal Saturn from across the zodiac, and for a few days authority stands opposite you in person: a boss, a parent, a bank, a rulebook. Requests get answered slowly or with conditions. You may feel blocked, judged, or simply tired of being accountable to anyone.

The opportunity is an accurate reading of where you stand. Oppositions clarify by contrast — the no you receive now maps the real boundary of a long negotiation, which is more useful than a vague maybe. Sometimes the figure across the table is right, and hearing it stings precisely because you suspected it already.

The friction is taking the standoff personally and either capitulating entirely or burning a bridge over a three-day mood.

Ask the blocking party what, specifically, would change their answer, note it without arguing, and revisit the plan once the Sun has moved on.
