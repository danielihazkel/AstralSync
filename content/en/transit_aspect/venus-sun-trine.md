title: Transiting Venus trine natal Sun
---

For a few days, life runs on oiled hinges. Requests get granted, meetings end early and well, and people seem predisposed to give you the benefit of the doubt. You feel appreciated without performing, which is rarer than it sounds.

Trines are generous and undemanding, and that is their trap. Nothing pushes, so nothing gets done; the ease can dissolve into a long soft afternoon of scrolling and snacks, or a second dessert that was really about the mood, not the dessert. Comfort this available is worth choosing deliberately rather than drifting into.

Spend the goodwill on purpose. Take the slow lunch with someone whose company genuinely pleases you, and pass a compliment along — tell someone's boss what they did well. Praise given while you're shining costs nothing and lands twice as far.
