title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Mercury
---

Once a month the Moon opposes your Mercury and stages a small debate between how you feel and how you think. In conversation this often plays out with a partner: one of you holds the facts, the other holds the feelings, and each is sure the other is missing the point. For an evening, dialogue itself is the weather.

Handled well, this is how you hear the counterargument your own mind never makes — other people are carrying half of your thought tonight. Handled badly, it is bickering over wording while the real subject escapes.

Try repeating the other person's point back before answering it; notice how often your summary surprises them.
