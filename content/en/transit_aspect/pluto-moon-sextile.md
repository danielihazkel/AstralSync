title: Transiting Pluto sextile natal Moon
---

For the long stretch this aspect remains in range, your emotional depths become accessible without becoming overwhelming. Conversations reach honest layers with less flinching; family patterns you could never see clearly come into focus at a survivable distance; intimacy tolerates more truth than usual. It is a favorable climate for the kind of inner work people usually postpone until crisis forces it.

Being a sextile, it forces nothing. The trapdoor to the basement stands unlocked, but you must walk down yourself — into therapy, into the overdue conversation with a parent, into an unblinking look at what you actually need from closeness. Descents made voluntarily now tend to go smoothly and pay off for decades. Declined, the door simply closes, and the material keeps its appointment with a later, harsher transit.

Open one sealed topic on purpose this year, in a setting with support built in, and let the honesty compound from there.
