title: Transiting Pluto trine natal Uranus
---

Across a couple of years and several exact passes, Pluto trines your natal Uranus and puts deep power behind your originality. The rebellious streak you once buried to keep the peace resurfaces without drama, and this time the world cooperates: unconventional ideas find backing, a reinvention of your work or lifestyle proceeds in steps rather than explosions, and you discover you can be radical and effective at once.

The regeneration is a durable integration of the maverick, independence that no longer needs an antagonist, innovation that survives contact with institutions. The risk of the trine is drift: because change comes easily, you may chase novelty compulsively, upgrading, relocating, and reinventing as a way of avoiding whatever intensity is asking to be felt rather than outrun.

Direct this ease at your oldest constraint, the one you assumed was permanent, and dismantle it while dismantling is this cheap.
