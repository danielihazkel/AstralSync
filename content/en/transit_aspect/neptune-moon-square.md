title: Transiting Neptune square natal Moon
---

For a year or so, counting retrograde passes, your emotional readings lose calibration. Moods arrive unattached to causes; nostalgia and vague dread trade shifts; you may misread the people closest to you, sensing rejection where there is only distraction, or safety where there is quiet erosion. Home can feel unaccountably wrong without anything being nameably broken.

The square is dissolving an outdated map of what security means — often one drawn in childhood — and the disorientation is the map coming apart before its replacement exists. Handled honestly, this period refines your emotional perception and deepens compassion for your own history. The failure modes are self-soothing through fog (drink, scrolling, fantasy relationships) and making major domestic decisions to escape a feeling that would have followed you anyway.

Postpone permanent choices about home and family where you can this year, and test your emotional readings against a trusted outside view before acting on them.
