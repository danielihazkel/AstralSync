title: Transiting Saturn sextile natal Saturn
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, you reach a quiet maintenance window in the 29.5-year Saturn cycle, arriving roughly five years to either side of your Saturn return. Nothing dramatic demands attention; instead, life offers small, practical chances to reinforce what the last hard checkpoint taught you — a skills refresh, a savings habit, a boundary restated calmly before it needs defending.

The payoff is cheap insurance: adjustments made now are minor, voluntary, and largely painless, and they decide how rough the next square or opposition will be. The cost is subtlety. Because nothing is breaking, it is easy to postpone the inspection entirely and coast on structures that are quietly aging — the certification lapsing, the pension unexamined, the friendship running on old goodwill.

Do one deliberate act of structural upkeep this season: renew, review, or repair something before circumstances make it urgent.
