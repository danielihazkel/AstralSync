title: Transiting Mars square natal Saturn
---

For a week or two, Mars grinds against your natal Saturn at a hard angle, and every push seems to summon a wall: rules you did not write, a supervisor's veto, a timeline that will not budge. Anger and duty argue inside you. This transit has an old reputation for frustration, and it earns it.

The wall, though, is a diagnostic. Where you feel most blocked is where your method, not your will, needs upgrading — and the discipline forged here outlasts the transit by years. The hazard is forcing it: snapping at authority, working through pain, cutting a corner that later cuts you.

Make patience an active strategy. Slow the pace on purpose, honor the safety margin, and pick one obstacle to dismantle by procedure — appeal, paperwork, practice reps — rather than by force. Let the wall teach before you climb it.
