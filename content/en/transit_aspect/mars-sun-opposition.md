title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Sun
---

Mars faces your Sun from across the sky, and the heat tends to arrive wearing someone else's face: a competitor, a demanding client, a partner who suddenly pushes back. For a week or two you may feel challenged to defend your position — or provoked into overdefending it.

The power of an opposition is clarity through contrast. Opponents show you exactly where your boundaries and blind spots are, and a well-met challenge can sharpen your case better than a month of solo planning. The hazard is the standoff: two parties escalating over a point neither can define.

Treat confrontations as negotiations, not duels. State your terms once, listen fully, and burn the leftover adrenaline somewhere physical — a run settles this transit better than a rebuttal.
