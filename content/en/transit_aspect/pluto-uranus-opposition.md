title: Transiting Pluto opposition natal Uranus
---

For roughly two years, across several exact passes, Pluto opposes your natal Uranus and your buried rebellion comes back through other people. Disruptors, defectors, and iconoclasts enter your orbit, or someone close makes the sudden break you never permitted yourself, and their electricity is strangely magnetic precisely because it is yours, projected. Alternatively you may face a power that tries to suppress your freedom just as you begin to reclaim it.

The regenerative task is to withdraw the projection and integrate the voltage: let the rule-breakers show you where your own life has calcified, then make your changes deliberately rather than vicariously. The risk is polarization, becoming the controller who must contain every wildcard, or the compulsive rebel who detonates relationships to prove no one owns you.

When someone's defiance provokes you, ask what frozen part of you it is thawing, and free that part yourself before you fight them.
