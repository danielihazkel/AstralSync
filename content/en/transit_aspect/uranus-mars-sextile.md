title: Transiting Uranus sextile natal Mars
---

For a year or so, initiative and innovation are on speaking terms. Trying the unconventional route costs less than usual — the experimental workflow, the sport you never considered, the independent project run alongside the day job — and your energy responds well to variety, running higher when the work is novel and self-directed.

Like every sextile, this one is patient to a fault: it will keep the door open all year and never once push you through it. Restlessness stays comfortable, expressed as browser tabs and gear purchases rather than launched attempts. The mild friction worth noticing is between the freedom you say you want and the routines you keep choosing.

Run one real experiment this season with a start date and a finish line — a prototype, a race, a trial month of the different schedule — and judge it by what you learned, not whether it worked.
