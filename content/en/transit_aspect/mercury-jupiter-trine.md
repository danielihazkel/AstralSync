title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Jupiter
---

Words and meaning travel together for a few days. Explanations land, jokes land, and the audience you usually have to win over seems already half-persuaded. It is a good window for anything that requires generosity of mind: mentoring, negotiating, writing the introduction rather than the footnotes.

The soft risk is coasting. Because everything you say sounds reasonable, you may not notice which parts are actually untested. Fluency is not the same as accuracy, and this transit hands out fluency freely.

Use the ease deliberately. Reread something you wrote when you were stuck — a stalled proposal, an abandoned draft — and fix it now, while the right phrasing comes cheap. Then tell one person the plan in plain words and watch which sentence makes them nod.
