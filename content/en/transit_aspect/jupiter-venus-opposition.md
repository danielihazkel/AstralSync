title: Transiting Jupiter opposition natal Venus
---

Other people's wants arrive oversized for a few weeks: a partner's plans expand, a friend's celebration requires more from you than advertised, a persuasive someone offers exactly the indulgence you were trying to ration. You may feel pulled between your own settled tastes and a social current that insists the bigger, brighter option is obviously better.

The opposition is genuinely tempting rather than simply false — some of what is offered would enlarge your life. The friction is discernment under charm: flattery reads as connection, luxury reads as love, and it becomes hard to tell whether you want the thing or the wanting is being performed at you. Agreements made to keep the mood festive are this transit's classic debt.

When an appealing offer lands, name out loud what it will cost you in a normal week; if the answer draws resistance instead of respect, that is your data.
