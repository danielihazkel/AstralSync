title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Neptune
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter sextiles your natal Neptune and gently enlarges your imaginative and idealistic side. Creative projects find flow with less forcing; spiritual or charitable interests attract kindred people; the difference you want to make in the world feels less naive and more workable than usual.

The risk with so soft an opening is that it stays a mood. Inspiration visits, feels pleasant, and leaves nothing behind — or generosity flows toward whoever asks first rather than where you actually mean it to go.

Choose one ideal you keep deferring and give it a scheduled, recurring slot — an hour of craft, a standing donation, a weekly practice — before this window closes.
