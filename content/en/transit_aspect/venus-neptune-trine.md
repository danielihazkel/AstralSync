title: Transiting Venus trine natal Neptune
---

For a few days, Venus trines your natal Neptune and the membrane between you and beauty thins. Empathy flows without effort, grudges feel heavy and slightly absurd, and ordinary rooms take on the kindness of good lighting. People confide in you; you find it easy to let them.

This is a window for reconciliation and for art — both run on the same willingness to see generously. The snag is dissolving too far: boundaries go quiet under this sky, and you may give away an afternoon, a secret, or a loan simply because refusing felt like a wrong note.

Choose where the softness goes. Forgive one specific debt you have privately decided to release, visit water if you can get to any, and make something with your hands that has no purpose except loveliness.
