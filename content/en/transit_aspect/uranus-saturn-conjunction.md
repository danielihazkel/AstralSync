title: Transiting Uranus conjunction natal Saturn
---

For a year or more, with several exact passes as Uranus loops retrograde and direct, the awakener sits directly on your natal Saturn, the load-bearing wall of your life. Jobs, contracts, routines, and long-standing rules come under sudden pressure, and the structures that were quietly obsolete tend to be the first to shake. What felt like security last year may reveal itself as habit.

The breakthrough here is renovation: releasing a duty you outgrew, modernizing how you work, replacing a rule you inherited with one you chose. The risk is the swing between extremes, either clamping down harder as the ground moves or demolishing in a week what took a decade to build and cannot be rebuilt on impulse.

Decide which commitments still hold you up, reinforce those deliberately, and let the rest be dismantled on your terms.
