title: Transiting Mars sextile natal Jupiter
---

This is an opportunity window, open for a week or two on Mars's two-year lap. Transiting Mars offers your natal Jupiter a friendly nudge rather than a shove: energy and optimism line up, and effort feels lighter than usual. People say yes more easily. Small asks travel further.

The power is leverage — a modest push now compounds, because you are pushing in the direction things already want to move. The hazard is drift: sextiles do not force anything, so it is easy to feel great, talk about plans, and let the window close with nothing started.

Pick one growth project and give it a real first action this week — send the application, ask the mentor, put money in the fund. The luck here belongs to whoever moves first.
