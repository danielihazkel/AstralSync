title: Transiting Mercury opposition natal Mercury
---

Halfway around your mental year, the moving Mercury faces your natal Mercury, and your habitual way of thinking meets its opposite in the wild. The person across the table reasons differently — from examples where you use principles, from feelings where you use figures — and for a few days those differences are loud.

The friction is obvious: crossed wires, talking past each other, the maddening sense that a perfectly clear point is not landing. The opportunity is rarer: a full-length view of your own thinking style, visible only by contrast.

Do not spend the whole transit converting anyone. Instead, borrow the other lens once, deliberately: take a decision you have already reasoned through and re-argue it the other person's way. Then ask them to summarize your position back to you, and check what got lost in transit.
