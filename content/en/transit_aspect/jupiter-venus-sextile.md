title: Transiting Jupiter sextile natal Venus
---

For a couple of weeks the social weather is mild and favorable: introductions go smoothly, small kindnesses are returned with interest, and tastes you usually defend feel affirmed. It is a good window for a first date, a gallery visit that becomes a friendship, a modest purchase that turns out to be exactly right.

Because nothing forces the issue, the whole period can slide by as pleasant background. Sextiles offer openings, not events; the connection at the edge of your circle, the collaboration someone half-suggested, the invitation you nearly declined — these are the transit's actual content, and they expire unanswered.

Accept one invitation you would normally skip, or extend one yourself; the low-stakes yes is where this transit pays.
