title: Transiting Neptune conjunction natal Mars
---

For a year or more, your engine runs on a subtler fuel. Raw push — deadlines, competition, willpower — loses its grip: efforts made from pure force seem to dissolve mid-swing, and stamina comes and goes on a tide you do not control. What works instead is inspired action, effort aligned with something you find genuinely meaningful, which can flow for hours without depletion.

The transit's danger is drift wearing the costume of surrender. Ambitions can quietly liquefy; projects continue out of habit while the will that drove them is elsewhere; and escapes — screens, substances, elaborate daydreams — offer themselves precisely where drive used to be. Physical energy needs gentler handling too: this is a season for swimming and long walks more than personal records.

Follow the energy honestly: keep acting where meaning still burns, formally pause what has gone hollow, and treat any new fatigue that persists as information worth a check, not a character flaw.
