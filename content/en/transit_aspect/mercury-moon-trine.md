title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Moon
---

For a few days, memory and mood are on speaking terms. Transiting Mercury trine your Moon favors the low-stakes, high-value conversations: reminiscing that turns out to matter, a talk with a parent or old friend that flows past its usual stopping point, writing that sounds like you on a good day.

The flip side of comfort is drift. This transit makes agreeable conversation so easy that hard subjects politely wait outside, and the window passes with everyone soothed and nothing decided.

Spend the ease on purpose. Have the mildly awkward domestic conversation now — money for the household, plans for the holiday, the ongoing thing with the neighbor — while the tone is naturally kind. Start with "this isn't urgent, but I'd like us to figure out..." and let the trine do the rest.
