title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Saturn
---

This is your Saturn return, the famous maturation threshold that arrives roughly every 29.5 years — near 29 the first time, near 58 the second. For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn stands exactly where it stood at your birth and audits everything you have built since: career, commitments, the adult identity you have been trying on. Structures that fit get reinforced; structures you inherited or drifted into start to buckle.

It can be a heavy season. Endings cluster here — jobs, relationships, versions of yourself — and the pressure to get serious can feel relentless. But the return is a commissioning, not a demolition. The first one asks you to choose your own foundations instead of your family's or your peer group's; the second asks what your accumulated mastery is actually for. What you commit to now tends to define the next three decades.

Name the life you are willing to be responsible for, and let go of one obligation that belongs to somebody else's script.
