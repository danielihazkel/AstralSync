title: Transiting Mercury trine natal Venus
---

For a few days, diplomacy costs you nothing. Transiting Mercury trine your Venus is the classic window for conversations that need both accuracy and warmth: the reference letter, the toast, the apology that has been waiting for the right register, the negotiation where you want the relationship intact afterward.

The hazard is mistaking pleasant for finished. Under a trine, everyone leaves the meeting feeling good, and it is only later you notice nothing was actually agreed — no number, no date, no name next to the task.

So enjoy the tone and still close the loop. Have the graceful conversation, then follow it with one plain-prose message: "To confirm, we agreed on X by Y." If there is someone whose work you have quietly admired for months, this is the week to tell them exactly why.
