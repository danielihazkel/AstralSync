title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Sun
---

This transit happens at most once in a life, and it takes a year or more to complete its passes. During that time, the version of you that has been running the show is dismantled down to load-bearing walls. Power becomes a live issue everywhere: who has it over you, what you will do to get it, what you have buried to keep the peace. Encounters with formidable people and non-negotiable circumstances are typical.

What survives this period is essential; what does not was costume. Many people emerge with startling new force and focus — but only after surrendering an identity they defended hard. The failure modes are tyranny and paranoia: gripping control of everything within reach, or reading every setback as persecution.

Cooperate with the excavation. When something in your life insists on ending this year, grieve it properly and study what it was built on, rather than rebuilding the same structure in fresh paint.
