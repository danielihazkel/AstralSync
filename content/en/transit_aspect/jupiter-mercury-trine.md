title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Mercury
---

Words cooperate under this transit. For a few weeks explanations come out cleaner than they went in, negotiations tilt friendly, and study sessions cover twice the ground at half the strain. It is a strong window for writing that must persuade, exams that must be passed, and conversations that need both honesty and tact.

The risk is that ease breeds thin output. Trines remove the resistance that usually forces depth, so you can produce a great volume of fluent, agreeable material that says less than it seems to. Ideas win nods they have not earned, and you may mistake a receptive audience for a finished argument.

Aim this fluency at the hardest piece of thinking on your desk — the proposal, the difficult letter, the concept you keep failing to explain — rather than spending it on what was already easy.
