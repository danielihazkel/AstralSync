title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Sun
---

Through the months this transit holds, disruption tends to wear other people's faces. A partner suddenly wants a different life, a collaborator goes rogue, an institution you counted on changes its rules mid-game. It can feel as though the world has become erratic while you have stayed the same — which is usually half the story. Oppositions project: some of that hunger for change is yours, delivered by courier.

The confrontation clarifies what you actually stand on. Relationships and roles flexible enough to renegotiate come through renewed; those held together by routine alone may not, and their departure is information. The failure mode is polarizing — clamping down harder as others break free, or matching every provocation with a counter-detonation.

When someone destabilizes an arrangement this season, ask what part of their rebellion you secretly envy before you respond. Negotiating from that honesty keeps you an author of the change rather than its casualty.
