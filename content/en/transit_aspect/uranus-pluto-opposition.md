title: Transiting Uranus opposition natal Pluto
---

For a year or more, through several exact passes, Uranus faces your natal Pluto across the chart, and buried power themes arrive through other people. Someone controlling meets your sudden refusal to be controlled; or you play the immovable one while a partner, colleague, or institution demands radical change. Either way the confrontation is a mirror: what you fight in them is usually a pattern you have been carrying underground.

The breakthrough is emancipation from the whole game, seeing the old dynamic clearly enough to step outside it, ending arrangements built on leverage, and meeting others as equals for the first time. The risk is escalation: power struggles that consume the year, dramatic severances that feel like freedom but are only the same struggle relocated.

In each standoff, ask what you are actually defending, and release the position that only pride is holding.
