title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Uranus
---

Over these weeks, transiting Jupiter trines your natal Uranus and your originality gets an easy, well-lit runway. Experiments that usually meet resistance glide; lucky accidents cluster around your most inventive work; changes you have wanted to make can happen now with unusually little breakage.

The opportunity is genuine reinvention at low cost. The friction is that trines never insist: it is entirely possible to feel refreshed and interested for a month and change nothing, or to scatter the ease across a dozen novelties instead of one real upgrade to your independence — income of your own, skills that travel, room to decide.

Make one structural change toward autonomy now, while circumstances are unusually willing to cooperate.
