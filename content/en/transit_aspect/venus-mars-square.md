title: Transiting Venus square natal Mars
---

For a few days, wanting to be wanted and wanting to win get their wires crossed. There's a static charge in your close relationships — teasing that runs a degree too hot, a bickering match that's really about attention, irritation at the very person you most want to reach for. Desire is present; it's just wearing armor.

The sweetness underneath is real appetite, for touch and for life. The snag is discharging it badly: picking the fight that's secretly an invitation, or soothing the frustration with a purchase that has the shape of desire and none of its warmth.

When the quarrel starts assembling itself, resist it — and say the vulnerable version instead: "I've missed you," "I want more of your attention." It's harder than sparring and much shorter. The friction was only ever asking for that.
