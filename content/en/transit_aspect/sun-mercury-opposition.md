title: Transiting Sun opposition natal Mercury
---

Once a year the Sun opposes your natal Mercury, and your thinking meets its audience. Plans you developed in private now draw questions, pushback, or a competing version from someone across the table. It can feel like resistance; it is closer to peer review.

The strain is real — being contradicted in this window stings more than usual, and defending a position can matter more to you than testing it.

Let one specific person poke holes in your current plan and write down every objection without rebutting in the moment. The ideas that survive these few days are the ones worth carrying forward; revise the rest.
