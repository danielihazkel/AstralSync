title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Uranus
---

Static in the body: for a few hours each month the Moon crosses your natal Uranus and routine becomes unbearable. You want to rearrange the furniture, quit something, take the other road home. Everything settled suddenly smells stale.

The gift is a genuinely fresh angle — this is your lifelong wiring for reinvention switched on loud, and an old problem may crack open sideways in these hours. The snag is that the mood can't tell renovation from arson. Bridges burned before dinner are still burned at breakfast, long after the charge has drained.

Change something small and reversible right now — your route, your desk, the order of your morning. Save the irreversible ideas in your journal and see which ones still spark next month.
