title: Transiting Sun conjunction natal Mars
---

The Sun crosses your natal Mars once a year and plugs your drive straight into the mains. Energy, nerve, and impatience all surge together: workouts feel great, hard tasks feel possible, and waiting feels intolerable. Whatever you have lacked the courage to start becomes startable for a few days.

The same current heats your temper. Fuses shorten, driving gets pushier, and minor obstacles read as personal insults. Accidents in this window usually trace back to hurry.

Give the energy a job before it picks one — the heavy project, the tough conversation, the physical push — and add ten seconds of pause before responding to anything that makes your jaw tighten.
