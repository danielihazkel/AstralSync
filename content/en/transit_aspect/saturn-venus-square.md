title: Transiting Saturn square natal Venus
---

For several months, often with a retrograde revisit, love and duty pull against each other. Work eats the evenings a relationship needed, money limits what affection wants to give, or a partner's legitimate requirements start to feel like a cage inspection. Warmth is not gone, but it has to be scheduled, and being scheduled makes it feel conditional — which quickly becomes the fear that you are only loved conditionally too.

The square is exposing which of your bonds can metabolize honest limitation and which require constant subsidy. That is painful but clarifying: relationships that survive a season of budgets and hard conversations come out with load-bearing walls. The trap is bleak arithmetic — concluding from a cold stretch that connection itself is a bad investment.

Name the constraint out loud to the people it affects, propose a concrete arrangement, and watch who negotiates in good faith; those are your people.
