title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Neptune
---

For a few hours each month, your edges soften. The Moon crosses your natal Neptune and the membrane between you and everything else thins: music lands deeper, strangers' moods soak in, and time goes loose around the middle of a daydream.

The gift is real permeability — compassion without effort, imagination without prompting. This is your lifelong longing for something beyond the literal, briefly running through your body instead of the back of your mind. The snag is fog around facts. Numbers blur, memories improve themselves, and other people's feelings arrive labeled as yours.

Postpone contracts and verdicts for a few hours; they'll still be there when the tide goes out. Instead, note what you drifted toward. The recurring daydream at this monthly touch is trying to tell you its name.
