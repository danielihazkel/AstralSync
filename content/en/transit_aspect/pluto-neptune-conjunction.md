title: Transiting Pluto conjunction natal Neptune
---

Over roughly two years, with several exact passes, Pluto crosses your natal Neptune and composts your ideals. Dreams, faiths, and images of the perfect life that have quietly guided you are turned over and examined for rot: a guru disappoints, a cherished fantasy meets its costs, a spiritual practice is stripped of its decoration. What decays here is not your capacity for meaning but its naivety.

The regeneration is a faith with roots, compassion that has seen the shadow and stayed, imagination that can build as well as console, devotion you choose with open eyes. The risk is compulsive escape or fixation: numbing through substances, screens, or romance, or attaching obsessively to a savior, cause, or vision precisely when discernment matters most.

Whenever a longing intensifies this year, test it gently against reality before you surrender to it, and keep only the dreams that survive daylight.
