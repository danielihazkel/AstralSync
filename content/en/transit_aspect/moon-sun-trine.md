title: Transiting Moon trine natal Sun
---

Mood and identity glide in the same direction for a few hours. You feel at home in your own skin: energy is steady, confidence is quiet rather than loud, and other people find you easy to be around. It is a good stretch for anything that benefits from you being relaxed — a first meeting, a creative session, a hard conversation you want to keep kind.

The catch with ease is drift. Trines ask nothing of you, so the afternoon can dissolve into pleasant nothing while the opportunity coasts by.

Spend some of the smoothness deliberately: choose one thing you have been avoiding while tense, and start it while you are not.
