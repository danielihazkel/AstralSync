title: Transiting Mars trine natal Venus
---

Pursuit and pleasure agree for a week or so. What you go after tends to respond, and what responds tends to be good for you — a rare alignment in matters of attraction. Social courage feels natural rather than performed, and creative work has both heat and polish.

The power is magnetic follow-through: dates that actually happen, projects that ship looking the way you imagined, negotiations where charm and firmness share the table. The hazard is the trine's usual one — abundance without direction. A week of easy pleasure can pass leaving nothing begun, and indulgence expands to fill unstructured time.

Give the ease a keel. Plan the memorable evening rather than drifting into an average one, finish and show the creative piece, or make the financial move that mixes enjoyment with return.
