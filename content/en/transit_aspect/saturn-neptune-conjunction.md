title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Neptune
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn sits on your natal Neptune — the place where your generation's shared dreams, ideals, and escapes live in you personally. Reality moves in with a clipboard. The vision you have cherished vaguely for years is asked for a budget; the cause you believe in wants hours, not sentiment; the habit you use to soften the world starts presenting its invoice.

This can feel like disenchantment, and some of it is: a hero shrinks, a fantasy version of the future quietly dies, and low-grade melancholy or fatigue may shadow the season. But Saturn is not here to kill the dream — it is here to see whether the dream can hold weight. Ideals that accept structure become vocations, disciplines, and finished works; ideals that refuse it are revealed as anesthetic. That sorting is this transit's real gift.

Take your most persistent dream and give it a deadline, a cost estimate, and a first working session this month.
