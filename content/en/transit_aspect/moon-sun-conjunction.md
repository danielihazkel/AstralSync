title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Sun
---

Once a month the Moon crosses your Sun, and for a few hours your feelings and your sense of who you are speak with one voice. Things that usually take effort — asking for what you want, taking up space — come more naturally, and people tend to read you clearly because you are not performing.

The snag is that the mood can turn the volume up on yourself. Small slights feel personal, and praise can go to your head a little. Either way, it is an afternoon's weather, not a verdict on your character.

Before it passes, jot down what felt most like you today; over several months those notes sketch a surprisingly honest self-portrait.
