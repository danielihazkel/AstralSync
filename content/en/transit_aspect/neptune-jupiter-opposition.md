title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Jupiter
---

For a year or more, through multiple exact passes, Neptune opposes your natal Jupiter, and the grand visions tend to arrive wearing other people's faces. A teacher, a partner, an opportunity, or a cause appears luminous with promise, offering exactly the meaning or abundance you have been hungry for. Your own beliefs get stretched by the encounter, which is partly the point.

The opening is real: this opposition can free you from a shrunken worldview by showing you, in living form, how much more is possible. The risk is projection. Whatever shimmers across the table is carrying your inflated hopes, and the gap between the glow and the actual person or offer is where disappointment, overpromising, and financial fog live.

Let others inspire you without letting them define your faith for you. Before accepting any glowing invitation this year, ask for specifics in writing, and sleep on it at least twice before you answer.
