title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Mars
---

Drive and confidence peak together for a few weeks. Projects that intimidated you look startable, physical energy runs high, and the gap between deciding and doing shrinks to almost nothing. This is a launch window in the plainest sense: campaigns, ventures, competitions, and bold asks all get an extra tailwind while it lasts.

The friction is scale without brakes. Jupiter amplifies whatever Mars was already doing, including its bad habits — you can overtrain the same week you overcommit, pick a winnable fight and then three more, or bet the surplus on the least examined plan in the drawer. Righteousness is the subtle trap: conviction this strong feels like proof.

Choose the one initiative that deserves this fuel and start it decisively; write the others down for later, and schedule the rest day before you think you need it.
