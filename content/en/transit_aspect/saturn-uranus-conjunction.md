title: Transiting Saturn conjunction natal Uranus
---

For several months, usually with a retrograde revisit, Saturn sits on your natal Uranus — the place where your generation's restlessness lives in you personally as your need for freedom, originality, and room to differ. Structure presses directly on that need. Schedules tighten around your most independent habits, institutions ask your unconventional side to show credentials, and the parts of your life you kept deliberately loose get asked to sign something.

The friction is obvious: it can feel like being domesticated. The payoff is less obvious but larger. Rebellion that never takes form stays a mood; this transit offers to turn your differences into something with a roof and a rent — the unusual skill certified, the side experiment incorporated, the independence funded rather than merely declared. What refuses all structure now tends to get structured by someone else.

Choose the freedom that matters most, and build the boring container — money, schedule, paperwork — that lets you keep it.
