title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Sun
---

For several months, authority stands across from you and pushes back. Employers, institutions, partners, or simply the accumulated weight of prior commitments now demand an accounting, and you may feel judged, limited, or boxed in by forces outside your control. This is typically a culmination point: whatever you began building around seven years ago gets stress-tested by external reality.

The gift inside the confrontation is clarity about which limits are legitimate. Some walls you meet now are load-bearing — contracts, health, other people's rights — and maturity means honoring them. Others are merely someone's preference wearing a uniform. The failure mode is treating them identically: capitulating everywhere out of fatigue, or rebelling everywhere out of pride, both of which waste the lesson.

When you hit resistance this season, ask which kind of wall it is before you push, and put your remaining strength only into the doors that are actually hinged.
