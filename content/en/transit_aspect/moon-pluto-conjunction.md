title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Pluto
---

A few hours each month, your mood drops below the waterline. The Moon crosses your natal Pluto and feelings arrive with unusual density — wanting becomes wanting badly, irritation acquires a history, and small exchanges feel loaded with subtext.

The gift is honest contact. In these hours you can feel what you actually want and actually fear, without the daytime padding; it's your deepest natal current briefly at the surface. The snag is brooding and control — replaying conversations, tightening your grip on people or plans, treating a passing intensity as a permanent state.

Name the feeling in one precise sentence, then set it down. If the same sentence writes itself at this monthly touch again and again, you've found a theme worth working with in daylight.
