title: Transiting Moon trine natal Uranus
---

Each month the Moon trines your natal Uranus and, for a few hours, you get pleasant altitude. A cool, curious detachment settles in: you can look at your own life like an interesting map, and ideas arrive sideways — the shortcut, the reframe, the connection nobody at the meeting saw.

The gift is easy originality without the usual static; problems loosen when you stop gripping them. The snag is that the same detachment makes follow-through optional. Sideways ideas evaporate fast, and by tomorrow the clever solution is a vague memory of having been clever.

Catch the idea while it's live: one voice memo, one journal line, one sketch. Then notice which subjects your mind keeps circling back to whenever it's given this monthly slack.
