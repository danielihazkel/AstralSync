title: Transiting Pluto square natal Jupiter
---

For roughly two years, through several exact passes, Pluto squares your natal Jupiter and applies pressure to everything you believe about growth. Plans built on optimism meet a force that demands substance: a market turns, an institution you trusted shows its machinery, a conviction you preached collides with evidence. The friction is designed to separate genuine faith from convenient inflation.

Handled consciously, this is a profound empowerment. You emerge with fewer beliefs held far more deeply, and with ambitions that can survive scrutiny. Handled unconsciously, it breeds overreach, doubling down on a failing bet, moralizing your appetite for power, or fighting anyone who questions the size of your claims. The plutocratic temptation is loudest at the square.

When you feel compelled to expand, pause and audit the motive; act only on the ambitions that survive honest questioning.
