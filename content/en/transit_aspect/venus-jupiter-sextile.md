title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Jupiter
---

Venus spends a few days offering your natal Jupiter an easy opening. Nothing dramatic — just doors that swing lighter than usual. A friend of a friend turns out to be useful, a casual compliment lands well, an outing you almost skipped becomes the best part of the week.

The pleasure here is low-stakes plenty: good humor in groups, small windfalls, a sense that people are rooting for you. The catch is that sextiles reward participation. If you stay home and wait, the transit passes politely and takes its introductions with it.

Accept one invitation you would normally decline, or extend one yourself. The luck of these days travels through company, so put yourself where the company is.
