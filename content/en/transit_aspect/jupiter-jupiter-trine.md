title: Transiting Jupiter trine natal Jupiter
---

Around four years after your Jupiter return, and again near eight, transiting Jupiter trines its natal place and the cycle moves into easy air. Over these weeks, what you planted grows with little resistance: collaborators appear, timing cooperates, and progress that usually costs effort arrives at a discount.

The friction here is subtle. Trines ask nothing, so it is easy to bank the good luck as mood rather than momentum — to enjoy the season and build nothing that outlasts it. Growth that is not reinvested tends to evaporate by the next hard angle of the cycle.

Take the smoothest-running part of your life right now and make one durable investment in it — training, savings, a stronger commitment — while the current is with you.
