title: Transiting Moon sextile natal Venus
---

A small door to warmth stands open for a few hours. Compliments come out sounding natural, minor social friction smooths itself, and modest pleasures — a coffee outside, a shared joke, fresh flowers — hit above their weight. This is easy weather for mending a slightly frayed connection, since goodwill needs only a nudge.

Being gentle, it asks nothing, and that is its weakness: the opening closes quietly if you spend it alone with a screen, and you will not even notice what was missed.

Send one appreciative message you would normally just think and not type, and see what comes back.
