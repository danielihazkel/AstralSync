title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Mercury
---

For a year or so your thinking runs ahead of the room in a way the room enjoys. Original ideas land as refreshing rather than threatening, new tools fit your hands quickly, and you can translate between the innovators and the skeptics — a rare and marketable position. Insights arrive with unusual frequency and, unlike harder Uranus weather, they arrive usable.

The trine's gentleness is its trap: because nothing is breaking, nothing demands follow-through. Clever observations get spent in conversation instead of built into anything, and the season can end with a reputation for brilliance and a drawer of unstarted prototypes. Ease of insight is not the same as a finished thing.

Pick the single best idea this period hands you and ship a version of it — a draft, a demo, a published piece — before the window's fluency moves on.
