For a few days, someone else's taste sits directly across the table from yours. Their apartment, their playlist, their way of loving — all suddenly vivid, and hard not to measure yourself against. Admiration and envy can wear the same outfit this week; you may not know which one you're feeling until later.

The sweetness is exposure: other people's beauty genuinely expands what you can imagine wanting. The snag is the two cheap responses — copying them wholesale, or keeping the peace by pretending their preferences are yours. A relationship where only one aesthetic survives gets thin.

Let their taste be theirs, out loud: "that's beautiful and I'd never choose it" is a complete, friendly sentence. Then offer them something from your side of the table — a dish, a song, a place you love — and let the difference be the pleasure.
