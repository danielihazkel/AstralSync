title: Transiting Moon conjunction natal Saturn
---

A few hours each month, the Moon sits on your natal Saturn and the light goes gray. Duties get louder, comfort gets quieter, and an old familiar voice starts auditing your life with a red pen.

There's a gift under the weight: sober clarity. In these hours you can see the real numbers — what a plan actually costs, what a promise actually requires — without wishful rounding. The snag is mistaking the mood for a verdict. This is your lifelong relationship with limitation made feelable for an afternoon, not a final report on your worth.

Write down the harshest thing your mind says, with a timestamp. Reread it tomorrow. Noticing which criticism returns at this monthly touch tells you where the real work is.
