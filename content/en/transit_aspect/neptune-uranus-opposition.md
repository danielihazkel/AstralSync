title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Uranus
---

For a year or more, through multiple exact passes, Neptune opposes your natal Uranus, and the call to freedom comes at you from outside. Disruptive people, glamorous movements, sudden opportunities to reinvent everything: the world seems full of exits, each lit softly enough that you cannot see where it lands. Your own independent streak reacts, either electrified or unsettled, to every one of them.

The opening is genuine cross-pollination: encounters this year can dissolve stale certainties and reconnect your originality to a larger vision, especially through collaboration with people unlike you. The deception risk is borrowed rebellion, adopting someone else's revolution because it shimmers, idealizing the disruptor across the table, or letting collective churn convince you that your steady commitments are the problem.

Test every liberation on offer against your own history. Before joining, quitting, or following anyone this year, write down what you wanted before you met them, and act only on the desires that predate the glow.
