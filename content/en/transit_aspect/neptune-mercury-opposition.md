title: Transiting Neptune opposition natal Mercury
---

For a year or more, the fog is on the other side of the table. People bring you inspiring visions with missing arithmetic, sincere promises that dissolve on contact with calendars, and accounts of events that feel rehearsed. Your clear questions seem to slide off; the more precision you request, the more atmosphere you receive. Meanwhile your own certainty can waver simply from prolonged exposure to eloquent vagueness.

The opposition trains discernment without cynicism — the rarest communication skill. Some of what arrives shrouded is genuine vision that has not yet learned to speak in numbers, and some is fog concealing emptiness; dismissing everything mystical and believing everything beautiful are the twin failures. The tell is rarely the content and usually the response to gentle verification.

Ask soft, specific questions — who, when, how much — and watch what happens: vision welcomes the grounding, and mirage resents it.
