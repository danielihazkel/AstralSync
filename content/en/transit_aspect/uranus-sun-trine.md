title: Transiting Uranus trine natal Sun
---

Reinvention comes without rupture under this long transit, which stays in range for many months. Changes that would normally require a crisis — updating your image, adopting new tools, stepping into a more original version of your work — slide into place with surprising ease. People experience you as fresher and more interesting, and you may attract allies who value exactly the quirks you used to sand down.

The gentle hazard is that ease breeds shallowness. With no friction to force commitment, you can sample novelty endlessly and integrate none of it, ending the transit entertained but unchanged. Trines hand you the materials for an upgrade; they do not perform the installation.

Pick the one experiment from this period that has actually energized you and build it into your routine — a weekly slot, a public commitment, a piece of equipment. Turning a whim into a fixture is what makes this transit permanent.
