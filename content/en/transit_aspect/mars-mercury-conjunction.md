title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Mercury
---

Your mind gets a blade this week. Mars on your Mercury speeds thought, sharpens speech, and collapses the gap between deciding and saying. Ideas arrive with momentum; hesitation feels physically uncomfortable. It is a superb window for pitching, debating, negotiating, and cutting through a backlog of decisions.

The edge cuts both ways. Sarcasm lands harder than you intend, emails written in thirty seconds read like ultimatums, and the same impatience shows up behind the wheel and at the keyboard. Being right and being persuasive briefly feel identical; they are not.

Give the blade real material: draft the proposal, make the case, decide the thing that has sat undecided for weeks. Then re-read anything heated once before sending, and drive like the other lane also has a Mars transit.
