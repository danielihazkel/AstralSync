title: Transiting Venus square natal Pluto
---

For a few days, Venus squares your natal Pluto and the ledger of love comes out. Who owes whom attention, who liked whose news, who was invited and who found out later — small currencies acquire absurd gravity, and an offhand slight can feel like evidence.

The sweetness hiding in the churn is passion itself: you care this much, which means the bond is live, not decorative. Underneath most jealousy this week is a wish to matter that has forgotten how to ask. The snag is letting the wish go underground — the cold shoulder, the test, the strategic silence that manufactures the distance it fears.

When the surge comes, trade tactics for a sentence: say "I want to matter to you" to the person it is true of, and delete the draft that keeps score.
