title: Transiting Moon opposition natal Jupiter
---

Each month, for a few hours, expansiveness comes at you from across the table. The Moon opposes your natal Jupiter, and it's usually someone else who carries the theme — their big plans, their invitation, their insistence that you should be more excited than you are.

The gift is perspective by contrast: watching enthusiasm from the outside shows you what your own hope actually looks like. The snag is borrowed appetite. In these hours you're prone to matching someone else's scale, agreeing to weekends, budgets, or projects sized for their life rather than yours.

Before you say yes, check whose optimism you're holding. A quick journal line — who invited, what you agreed to, how it aged — makes the monthly pattern easy to spot.
