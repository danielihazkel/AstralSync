title: Transiting Venus square natal Mercury
---

For a few days, tact and truth elbow each other in your mouth. You know what needs saying, but every phrasing sounds either too harsh or too sugared, so you keep revising and end up saying nothing. Meanwhile everyone around you sounds suspiciously smooth — the sales call, the beautifully worded offer, the compliment that's really an opening bid.

The sweetness is real charm in the air; talk is warmer than usual. The snag is what the sugar coats. You can sweet-talk yourself past a doubt, agree in a meeting to keep the tone nice, or buy something because the description read like a poem.

Resist signing anything today mostly because it was prettily pitched — reread it tomorrow in flat morning light. And say the awkward true thing in plain words. Kindness doesn't require frosting.
