title: Transiting Venus sextile natal Uranus
---

Venus sextiles your natal Uranus for a few days, and pleasant surprises come in through the side door. An out-of-the-blue message from someone you liked and lost track of, an unplanned detour that turns into the highlight, a friend's odd idea that turns out to be fun.

The sweetness is low-voltage novelty: your routines loosen just enough to let something interesting in, without anything needing to break. The snag is mild — you may greet the surprise with a raincheck reflex, filing the spontaneous invitation under "someday" until the window closes.

When the unexpected option appears, take it while it is still warm. Reply to the strange message today, say yes to the last-minute thing, or introduce two friends from different corners of your life and watch what sparks.
