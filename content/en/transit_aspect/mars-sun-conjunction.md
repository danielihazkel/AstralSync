title: Transiting Mars conjunction natal Sun
---

Roughly every two years Mars crosses your Sun and relights the pilot. For a week or two your vitality runs high, your patience for waiting runs low, and you want your name on something. This is one of the best windows in the cycle to start what requires nerve.

The power is initiative: asking, launching, leading before you feel fully ready. The hazard is heat without aim — picking fights with bosses, overtraining, mistaking irritation for conviction. Authority figures may feel like obstacles simply because they are in front of you.

Give the surge one worthy target. Begin the project, book the pitch, take on the physical challenge — and let the small provocations burn off in the effort rather than in the meeting room.
