title: Transiting Pluto square natal Moon
---

For upwards of a year, with retrograde reprises, emotional life runs at compulsive intensity. Jealousy, dread, and old grief can erupt through routine days; family dynamics turn volcanic over seemingly minor triggers; the need for closeness and the fear of being controlled by it wrestle openly. Home itself may become an arena — power struggles over space, money, or loyalty that are never really about the stated subject.

The square is forcing toxins to the surface where they can finally be treated. Every disproportionate reaction this period hands you is a map coordinate for buried material, and people who track them honestly emerge with far cleaner emotional machinery. The failure modes are acting the intensity out on loved ones or swallowing it until the body files a complaint.

When a reaction outsizes its trigger this year, write down what the moment reminded you of before responding. The archive, not the argument, is where this transit does its healing.
