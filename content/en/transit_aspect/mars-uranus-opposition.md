title: Transiting Mars opposition natal Uranus
---

For a week or two, Mars opposes your natal Uranus, and disruption tends to come at you from outside: someone else's sudden reversal, a cancellation that scrambles your week, a person whose unpredictability provokes your temper. Plans built on other people's steadiness wobble. You may feel baited into reacting fast.

The power is agility under fire — handled well, this transit teaches you to hold your aim while conditions jump around, and sometimes the disruption breaks you out of an arrangement you were too loyal to leave. The hazard is the reflex counterstrike: matching someone's chaos with your own, or making a permanent decision to end a temporary annoyance.

Protect your reaction time. When surprised, name it aloud, take one breath, and choose a response instead of firing one. Keep this week's plans modular, confirm the important appointments, and save your genuinely rebellious energy for a change you would still want next month.
