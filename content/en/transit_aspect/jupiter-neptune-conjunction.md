title: Transiting Jupiter conjunction natal Neptune
---

For a few weeks, longer if a retrograde brings a second pass, transiting Jupiter crosses your natal Neptune and turns the volume up on your ideals and imagination. Creative work runs vivid; compassion widens; a cause, a practice, or a dream you have carried quietly can suddenly feel like the most important thing in your life.

Some of that feeling is true and some is tide. Jupiter magnifies Neptune's fog along with its vision, so this is also a season of overpromising, idealizing people, and mistaking beautiful plans for funded ones. Big commitments made now can look different once the glow fades.

Give the inspiration a container — pages, sketches, a pilot, a savings target — and postpone irreversible decisions until you have tested it there.
