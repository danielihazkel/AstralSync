title: Transiting Saturn opposition natal Moon
---

During these months, the demands of the outer world sit directly opposite your need for comfort, and other people tend to play Saturn's part. A partner may seem colder or more critical than usual, an employer may treat your personal life as an inconvenience, or caretaking duties may arrive from the family itself. You can feel emotionally rationed — as if warmth were being weighed before it is given.

The transit is testing the boundary between healthy obligation and self-abandonment. Some of the coolness you perceive is real and worth addressing; some is projection, your own unmet needs read onto other faces. Sorting one from the other is the work. The failure mode is withdrawing into injured silence and calling it strength.

Say your needs out loud, plainly and without accusation, at least once during this period. Requests made explicit can be negotiated; requests left implicit can only be disappointed.
