title: Transiting Mercury conjunction natal Moon
---

For a day or two, feelings that usually stay wordless come with captions. Transiting Mercury on your Moon narrows the gap between what you feel and what you can say about it — which makes this a good window for the conversation that needed the right words, or for a journal entry that finally names the mood.

The friction: everything gets a caption, including passing weather. A three-hour irritation can get written up like a manifesto. Not every feeling that becomes articulate has become important.

Say the tender thing to the person it belongs to, in one or two sentences, without the essay. If it is too raw to send, write it down anyway; this transit returns in a few months, and you will want the record of what this one was actually about.
