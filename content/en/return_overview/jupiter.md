title: The Jupiter return
---

Roughly every twelve years, Jupiter arrives back at the degree it held when you were born — near ages 12, 24, 36, 48, and onward. A chart cast for that moment opens a new growth chapter: it sketches where your life is ready to expand, what you now believe is possible, and which door luck is most likely to use.

Read it by house above all: the house holding your Jupiter return shows the arena asking for a bigger yes — study, travel, publishing, a role you would have called too large a year earlier. The emphasis is opportunity, and these seasons reward the risk you take in Jupiter's direction.

The caution is appetite. Expansion is not discrimination, and a Jupiter year can inflate commitments, spending, and promises as cheerfully as it inflates confidence. Say one large, well-chosen yes near the return — and let it crowd out the five glittering maybes.
