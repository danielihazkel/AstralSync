title: The lunar return
---

Every 27.3 days or so, the Moon comes back to the exact place it occupied at your birth. A chart cast for that moment is your lunar return: a snapshot of the emotional month ahead — what your moods will orbit, where you will seek comfort, and which needs will knock loudest until the next return.

Its usefulness is rhythm. Read the return Moon's house for the life area that will color your inner weather, and treat the roughly four-week span as one emotional chapter with a theme, which makes patterns visible that daily horoscope-checking never catches.

The caution: lunar returns are subtle. They whisper where a solar return states, so expect texture rather than events, and do not over-plot a month from a chart that mostly describes feelings. Note the theme in your journal on return day, and check at month's end whether the tide behaved as advertised.
