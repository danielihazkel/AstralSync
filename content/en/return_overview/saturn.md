title: The Saturn return
---

About every 29.5 years, Saturn comes home to its natal degree — first near ages 28 to 30, again near 58 to 60. A chart cast for that moment describes a maturation threshold: the point where provisional adulthood ends and you are asked to commit, in concrete terms, to the life that is actually yours rather than the one you inherited or drifted into.

Read it as an audit. The house of your natal Saturn shows where the pressure concentrates, and the return season tests every structure there — job, relationship, address, identity — for load-bearing honesty. Its gift is authority: what survives this passage becomes the foundation you can build on for decades.

The caution: the Saturn return consolidates what is real rather than punishing you, so the losses are usually scaffolding coming down, not the building. List the commitments you are still keeping out of habit, and formally renew or release each one.
