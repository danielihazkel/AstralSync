title: The solar return
---

Once a year, the Sun returns to the exact degree and minute it held when you were born — usually on or within a day of your birthday. A chart cast for that instant is your solar return: a portrait of the year ahead, describing its dominant flavor, the arenas that will demand your energy, and the tone your vitality takes until the next return.

Read it practically: find the house the Sun occupies, because that life area becomes the year's main stage, then note the return Ascendant for how the year wants you to show up. Its strength is orientation — one glance tells you where the plot is likely to thicken.

The caution is to treat it as a weather map, not a verdict; it describes climate, and you still choose what to build in it. Before your next birthday, jot three intentions that match the return chart's emphasis and revisit them at the half-year mark.
