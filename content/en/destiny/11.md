title: Destiny 11
---

Your name sums to 11, a master number — the diplomat's 2 run at higher voltage. You are built to inspire: to stand at the front of the room, or the page, and make people feel a possibility they could not articulate alone.

Your strength is antenna-grade intuition about groups; you sense the mood shift before it has words, which makes you a natural teacher, counselor, or voice for an idea. The cost of the wiring is that it never switches off — the same sensitivity delivers stage fright, second-guessing, and nervous exhaustion, and master-number careers famously start late because the confidence takes years to catch up to the signal.

Voltage needs insulation. Keep an unglamorous daily anchor — exercise, a set bedtime, a craft with your hands — and treat it as the equipment your inspiration runs on.
