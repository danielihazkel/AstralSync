title: Destiny 4
---

Your name sums to the builder's number. You are made for work that outlasts the meeting where it was proposed: systems, schedules, foundations, the load-bearing parts nobody photographs but everybody stands on.

Your strength is reliability compounded over time — you show up, you document, you finish, and after a few years people discover that half the organization quietly runs on things you built. The friction is rigidity. A process that once served the goal can become the goal, and you defend it long after conditions have changed, mistaking your discomfort with revision for a defect in the new plan.

Structures serve best when they stay inspectable. Once a quarter, pick one rule or routine you enforce and ask what it protects now — if the answer is only habit, retire it yourself before someone else has to.
