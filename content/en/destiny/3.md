title: Destiny 3
---

The letters of your name total to expression. You are built to communicate — to take the shapeless thing everyone half-understands and give it words, color, or a punchline. Whatever your job title says, your actual function is translation into delight.

Your strength is generative speed: drafts, pitches, and ideas come out of you at a rate that embarrasses careful people. The friction is finishing. The first eighty percent of anything is a party; the last twenty percent is a chore, and you tend to answer a chore by starting a new party.

A talent for beginnings needs a scaffold for endings. Keep a visible list of your open projects, cap it at three, and refuse yourself a new one until something ships.
