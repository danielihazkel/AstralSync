title: Destiny 2
---

Your name's full sum builds you for the work between people. You are the negotiator, the editor, the second chair who makes the first chair sound good — talent expressed through partnership rather than solo performance.

You read a room faster than most people read a sentence, and you can hold two opposing positions long enough to find the third one nobody saw. That is rare and billable. The friction is credit and conviction: because your best work is invisible glue, it goes unnamed in reviews, and because you see every side, deciding can feel like betraying someone.

Diplomacy without a position eventually becomes furniture. In your next disagreement at work, state your own preference out loud before you start mediating everyone else's.
