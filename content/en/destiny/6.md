title: Destiny 6
---

Your name totals to the number of stewardship. You are built for work where someone or something is in your care: teams, clients, students, households, standards. Responsibility does not land on you by accident — you reach for it, and you carry it well.

Your strength is that things flourish under your hands; you notice what a person or project needs before it asks, and you make environments where others do their best work. The friction is over-reach. Helping shades into managing, managing shades into hovering, and you can end up resented for repairs nobody requested while your own work sits neglected.

Care is only a gift when it was wanted. Before your next rescue, ask the person one question — do you want help or just a witness — and honor the answer.
