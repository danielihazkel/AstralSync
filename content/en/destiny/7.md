title: Destiny 7
---

The full sum of your name is the analyst's number. You are built for depth work: research, diagnosis, strategy, any craft where the answer hides under three layers of noise and someone has to be willing to sit with the problem longer than is comfortable.

Your strength is that your conclusions hold. While others repeat what they read, you have actually checked, and over time people learn that your quiet "I'm not sure that's true" is worth more than a confident deck. The friction is the moat you dig around the work. Collaboration feels like interruption, sharing drafts feels like exposure, and expertise hoarded in one head helps no one and promotes no one.

Insight completes itself in transmission. Set a standing rhythm — a memo, a talk, a weekly explanation to one colleague — so the depth you mine actually reaches the surface.
