title: Destiny 8
---

Your name adds up to the executive number. You are built to run things at scale — budgets, organizations, deals — and to be comfortable with money and authority in a way that makes other people either relax or bristle.

Your strength is judgment under stakes: you can read a balance sheet, a negotiation, and a person's real motive in the same meeting, and you deliver outcomes rather than efforts. The friction is the throttle. Everything becomes a performance metric, including rest, relationships, and your own worth, and a life optimized like a portfolio starts returning numbers instead of meaning.

Power holds its value only if something is exempt from it. Choose one domain — a day, a relationship, a hobby — where you refuse to measure results, and guard it like an asset.
