title: Destiny 22
---

Your name totals 22, the master builder — a 4's discipline scaled up to institutions. You are built to take a vision most people would frame as a dream and turn it into something with an address: a company, a school, a body of work that employs and outlives you.

Your strength is the rare pairing of blueprint and shovel; you can hold the ten-year picture and still care about this week's load-bearing detail. The master number's cost is the standard it sets. Anything less than monumental can feel like failure, so you either grind toward a crushing target or refuse to start at all, and both modes read from outside as the same stalled ambition.

Monuments are laid in courses. Break the big vision into a stretch of work you could finish in ninety days, and let completing it count.
