title: Destiny 33
---

Your name sums to 33, the master teacher — a 6's caretaking raised to a vocation. You are built to lift people: mentoring, healing, leading communities, doing work whose product is other humans becoming more capable than they believed.

Your strength is that you teach by presence as much as content; people leave your orbit steadier, and institutions put you wherever morale is bleeding. The master number's cost is self-erasure. Because the work is noble, your own needs get filed as indulgences, and you can give from the principal instead of the interest for years before the crash announces the accounting error.

A depleted teacher instructs everyone in depletion. Book the appointment, the day off, or the ask for help you have been deferring — this week, before the next person who needs you calls.
