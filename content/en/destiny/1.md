title: Destiny 1
---

The full sum of your name points at origination. You are built to be the first mover — the one who names the project, breaks the tie, and works best when the outcome traces back to your own decisions.

Your strength is momentum under uncertainty: where others wait for permission or precedent, you act, and teams quietly reorganize around whoever is willing to decide. The friction is the handoff. Delegation feels like dilution, feedback feels like drag, and left unchecked you end up leading a team of one, doing work that deserved ten hands.

Your contribution scales only when others can carry it. On your current project, pick one piece you consider yours alone and hand it over with real authority — then watch how much faster the whole thing moves.
