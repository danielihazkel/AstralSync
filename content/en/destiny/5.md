title: Destiny 5
---

The full count of your name is wired for motion. You are built to work across borders — between fields, formats, cities, and audiences — and your natural contribution is bringing back what you found there before anyone else knew it existed.

Your strength is range: you learn new tools in an afternoon, talk to anyone, and thrive in exactly the ambiguity that makes specialists seasick. The friction is depth. Careers reward people who stay past the interesting part, and your résumé can read as ten brilliant first chapters with no book, because you leave when mastery starts demanding repetition.

Range plus one deep root beats range alone. Choose a single skill worth boring years and protect it while you keep everything else moving.
