title: Destiny 9
---

The full sum of your name points at the wide angle. You are built for work that serves more than the people in the room: teaching, healing, advocacy, art with a conscience — vocations where the deliverable is somebody else's better life.

Your strength is perspective; you connect your daily tasks to their human consequences, which gives your work a gravity colleagues notice and causes people to trust you with things that matter. The friction is the boundary line. Because every request sounds like the mission, you say yes past your capacity, and the person who serves everyone eventually serves everyone badly.

A cause needs a worker who still exists next year. Before accepting the next worthy ask, name out loud what current commitment it will displace — if you can't, decline it.
