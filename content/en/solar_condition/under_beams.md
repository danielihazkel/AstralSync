title: Under the beams
---

This planet sits within about seventeen degrees of your Sun — *under the beams*, close enough to be veiled by the light but not burned by it. Its work is real and ongoing, but it tends to happen out of sight: in preparation, in private, or in support of goals that others credit elsewhere.

The gift is quiet competence. This part of you develops without an audience, which can make it more honest and less performative, and it often matures into something reliable long before anyone notices. The friction is under-recognition — including your own. You may undervalue what this planet gives you, or wait too long for permission to show it.

As the planet's own cycle carries it out from the Sun's glare, this side of you tends to emerge; the obscuring is a stage, not a sentence. Keep a simple record of what this planet quietly achieves, and decide on one place where it deserves to be seen.
