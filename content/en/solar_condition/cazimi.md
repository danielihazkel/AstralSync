title: Cazimi
---

This planet sits within a fraction of a degree of your Sun — *in the heart of the Sun*, as the old astrologers put it. Instead of being burned by the light, it is enthroned in it. Whatever this planet signifies is fused with your core purpose and backed by your full will, so it tends to work powerfully, visibly, and with unusual authority.

The gift is integration: no gap between who you are and how this planet acts through you. People often sense it as a signature talent or a defining trait. The friction is that you may struggle to see it at all. What sits at the centre is hard to step back from, so you can mistake this planet's way for the only way, or take its strength for granted.

Ask someone who knows you well how this planet shows up in your life. Their answer will often name a gift you have stopped noticing — and that is worth using deliberately.
