title: Combust
---

This planet sits close to your Sun — within about eight and a half degrees — and is, in the traditional word, combust: burned by the light. Its significations are real but hard to see. They get absorbed into your larger solar will, so this part of you tends to act in service of your identity rather than on its own terms, or to stay hidden until something forces it out.

The gift is intensity and fusion. What this planet does, it does with your whole ego behind it, and in private it can be remarkably strong. The friction is visibility and perspective: others may not see this side of you, and you may find it hard to separate its needs from your sense of self, so it gets overruled or overheated.

Give this planet some independent room. Once a week, do one thing that belongs to its domain purely for its own sake — not to prove anything, just to let it breathe outside the glare.
