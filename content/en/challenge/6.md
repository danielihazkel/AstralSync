title: Challenge 6
---

This challenge gathers around responsibility and the people you love. You may feel that every family problem is yours to fix, that a good home must match an ideal picture, and that caring means managing. It shows up as doing too much for others, offering advice nobody asked for, and quietly resenting the load you volunteered to carry. High standards can turn into perfectionism aimed at yourself and everyone near you.

The overcorrection is withdrawal: deciding nobody appreciates you, refusing responsibility you truly hold, or letting things fall apart to prove a point. Both extremes keep love tied to control. Mastering this challenge gives you a generous, grounded kind of care. You can support people without running their lives, accept a home and a family that are good enough, and let others carry their own share.

This week, notice one moment when you are about to step in and fix something for someone capable of handling it. Ask, "Do you want help with that?" and accept the answer.
