This challenge names no single obstacle, which sounds like good news until you live it. With no one weakness pointing the way, the difficulty is choosing at all. Every path looks reasonable, every lesson seems to apply a little, and you can spend a long stretch of life sampling options without committing to any of them, waiting for a sign that never arrives.

The overcorrection is grabbing the first direction that offers relief and defending it long after it stopped fitting, simply to escape the discomfort of open doors. Either way, the choice is being made by default rather than by you. Mastering this challenge gives you something rare: the ability to look at a wide field, decide which few things actually matter, and give them your full weight, because you picked them on purpose.

Write down the three areas of your life asking most for attention right now. Choose one, name it out loud to someone, and let the other two wait for a set date.
