title: Challenge 2
---

This challenge lives in your nervous system. You may register a shift in someone's tone before they know they have shifted, and take it personally. Conflict can feel physically unsafe, so you smooth things over, agree too early, or apologize for what was not your fault. In close partnerships you can slowly erase yourself, keeping the peace by leaving out the parts of you that might cause friction.

The overcorrection is a hard shell: deciding you will not care, keeping people at a careful distance, or bracing for slights before they happen. Neither the sponge nor the wall is real closeness. Mastering this challenge turns your sensitivity into a strength. You become a skilled mediator who can stay present in disagreement, read a room accurately, and still keep your own outline intact while caring deeply about someone else.

The next time you feel the urge to say "it's fine" when it is not, pause and offer one honest sentence instead: what you noticed, and what you would prefer.
