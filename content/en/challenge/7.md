title: Challenge 7
---

This challenge turns on trust. You may often feel misunderstood, as if the people around you are speaking a slightly different language, and the natural response is to pull inward. Skepticism becomes a habit: you test people before letting them close, doubt what cannot be proven, and keep your inner life private enough that nobody quite gets to know it.

The overcorrection is swallowing a belief whole, attaching to a teacher, a system, or a person and handing over your judgment just to stop feeling alone with your questions. Total doubt and blind faith both skip the harder work of trust that is earned and tested over time. Mastering this challenge gives you real depth. Your solitude becomes a source of insight rather than a hiding place, your faith rests on your own experience, and you can let a few people see how you actually think.

Choose one person you trust more than most and share a question you have been turning over privately. Let them think it through with you rather than solving it alone.
