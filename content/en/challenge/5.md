title: Challenge 5
---

This challenge asks what you do with freedom. Restlessness is the usual first symptom: the job, the city, or the relationship starts to feel small just as it begins to deepen. You may chase stimulation through too much food, spending, travel, or novelty, and treat commitment as a door closing rather than a room opening.

The overcorrection is just as limiting. Afraid of where your appetites might lead, you can lock yourself into routines, stay in situations long past their expiry, and resist change because change once went badly. Running from everything and holding on to everything are both ways of avoiding the real question of what is worth your freedom. Mastering this challenge gives you adaptability with a center. You can move, experiment, and recover from surprises while still keeping the commitments that make your freedom mean something.

Before your next big change, wait seventy-two hours and write down what you are moving toward, not only what you are leaving. If you cannot name it, stay put a little longer.
