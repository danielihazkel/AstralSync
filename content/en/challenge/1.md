title: Challenge 1
---

This challenge is about standing on your own feet. It often shows up as quietly waiting for permission: checking whether others approve before you speak, letting someone else's plan become yours because proposing your own felt presumptuous, or sensing that your needs only count once they have been endorsed. The cost is a life that runs slightly to one side of what you actually want.

The overcorrection swings the other way, into insisting on your way to prove you can, steamrolling a conversation, or refusing help you genuinely need. That is dependence in disguise, still organized around other people's reactions. Mastering this challenge gives you a steady, self-directed confidence: you can state what you want plainly, hear disagreement without collapsing or attacking, and lead when leading is needed without needing everyone to follow.

Once this week, make a small decision that affects only you without asking anyone first, and notice how the urge to check in feels. Then let the decision stand.
