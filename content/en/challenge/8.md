title: Challenge 8
---

This challenge is about money, power, and control, and how much weight you give them. It can show up as measuring your worth by income or status, chasing the next promotion past the point where it makes you happier, or needing to control every outcome. It can also appear as an uneasy relationship with authority, whether you resent the people who hold it or bristle when it is handed to you.

The overcorrection is fearing success altogether: underpricing your work, deciding ambition is shallow, or turning down responsibility because power feels corrupting. Both sides give money and authority more control over you than they deserve. Mastering this challenge gives you a clear, practical command of the material world. You can earn well, lead fairly, negotiate without guilt, and use resources as tools for what you value rather than as a scoreboard.

Look at your finances this week with plain curiosity and no judgment. Write down one number you have been avoiding, and one thing you would like money to make possible.
