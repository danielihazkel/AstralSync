title: Challenge 3
---

This challenge concerns your voice. You probably have more to say, make, or share than you let out, and the bottleneck is an inner critic that edits everything before it reaches the air. Drafts stay in folders, jokes stay unspoken, and a creative idea gets judged against a finished masterpiece while it is still a sketch. Underneath is a fear of being seen and found wanting.

The overcorrection looks like the opposite of shyness: talking constantly, starting ten projects and finishing none, scattering your energy across so many outlets that nothing gets enough of you to become real. It keeps attention moving so no single effort can be judged. Mastering this challenge gives you a voice that is both free and focused, the ability to express something honestly, let it be imperfect, and follow it through to an audience.

Pick one small piece of work you have kept private, whether a paragraph, a sketch, or a song, and show it to one person this week without apologizing for it first.
