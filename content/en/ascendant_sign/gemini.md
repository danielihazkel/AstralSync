title: Gemini Rising
---

Words are your handshake. You open conversations easily, notice everything, and can find common ground with almost anyone inside two minutes. People leave a first meeting with you feeling *interesting* — that is a real skill, and you do it without thinking.

The friction is that quickness can read as lightness. Because you pivot topics fast, others may assume you pivot loyalties fast too, and the steadier person underneath — your Sun or Moon may crave far more depth than your banter suggests — goes unseen.

You do not have to slow your mind, only your exits. In your next conversation, ask one follow-up question about the thing the other person clearly cares about, and stay there.
