title: Virgo Rising
---

You show up prepared. Before you have said much, people register the noticing: you clock the typo, the loose hinge, the gap in the plan, and your first instinct in any new situation is to be useful in some specific way. That earns trust fast — competence is your calling card.

The cost is that constant appraisal leaks. Others can feel measured around you even when your inner commentary is aimed entirely at yourself, and the easy, generous person underneath — which your Sun or Moon may well be — stays hidden behind the clipboard.

Offer one observation today that is pure appreciation, with no improvement attached, and say it out loud.
