title: Cancer Rising
---

You read the room before the room reads you. First meetings with you feel oddly personal — you remember names, notice who is tired, and adjust your tone without being asked. That attunement is your strength: people confide in you quickly because your face signals safety.

But the shell is real. You approach new situations sideways, testing the warmth before committing to it, and what you experience as caution others can read as moodiness or withdrawal. Your surface tracks the emotional weather closely, even when your Sun or Moon is far more even-keeled underneath.

Notice the next time you deflect a personal question with a question of your own — answer it straight once, and see whether the sky actually falls.
