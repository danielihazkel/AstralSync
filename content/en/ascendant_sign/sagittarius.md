title: Sagittarius Rising
---

Your default setting is open. You talk to strangers in queues, say the obvious thing everyone else is politely avoiding, and treat most first meetings as the start of a possible adventure. That candor is magnetic — people trust someone who visibly is not managing them.

Bluntness is the bill for it. The remark you consider honest weather-reporting can land as a verdict, and because your delivery is cheerful, others do not always feel entitled to say it stung. Meanwhile whoever you are underneath — a more careful Sun or Moon, perhaps — rarely gets credit for its tact.

Before your next hard truth, ask whether the person wants your read or just your company, and answer accordingly.
