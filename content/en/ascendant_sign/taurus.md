title: Taurus Rising

Steadiness is the first thing people notice about you. You move at your own tempo, speak when the sentence is ready, and hold a room by refusing to be rushed. Others relax around you because you are visibly hard to knock over.

That calm is a surface, though, and the person underneath — whatever your Sun and Moon are doing — may run much hotter than your face admits. The friction shows up as stubbornness: what you call consistency, others experience as a door that never reopens once it has closed.

Your presence builds trust; your pace can also stall things when a situation genuinely needs speed. This week, pick one small decision you have been sitting on and make it in under a minute.
