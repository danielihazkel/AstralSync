title: Leo Rising
---

Warmth precedes you. You greet people as if the gathering was incomplete until they walked in, and that generosity of attention is why strangers remember you. Whether your inner life matches the shine — your Sun or Moon may be shy, anxious, or intensely private — the doorway version of you performs confidence beautifully.

- Your strength: you make occasions out of ordinary moments, and people rise to meet the version of themselves you reflect back.
- Your friction: when a room withholds acknowledgment, you push the performance harder instead of dropping it, and the effort starts to show.

Once this week, let someone else hold the spotlight for a full story without adding your matching one.
