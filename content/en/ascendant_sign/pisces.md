title: Pisces Rising
---

Your edges are soft in the best sense: people project onto you easily, confide quickly, and often walk away feeling understood without noticing you barely spoke. You absorb the mood of whoever you are with and hand it back gentler. That receptivity is a genuine gift.

It is also porous. In new company you shape-shift toward what the moment seems to want, so commitments blur, timelines drift, and others may call you flaky when the truth is that you never actually said yes — or no. The self underneath, whatever your Sun and Moon build, can go unrepresented at its own front door.

At the next request that reaches you, give a plain answer with a date in it, and let the silence sit afterward.
