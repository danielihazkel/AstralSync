title: Aquarius Rising
---

You come across as the interested outsider — friendly, curious, faintly untouched by the social rules everyone else is obeying. People find you refreshing on first meeting: you ask unusual questions, skip the small-talk script, and address the intern and the director in the same level voice.

That evenness is a strength and a screen. Because you are equally warm to everyone, no one is sure they matter to you in particular, and the loyal, feeling person underneath — your Sun or Moon may be far more sentimental than your cool interface admits — goes unclaimed.

Tell one person, plainly, that you specifically like them. Not people in general, not humanity: *them*.
