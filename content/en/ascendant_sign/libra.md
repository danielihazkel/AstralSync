title: Libra Rising
---

You are easy to meet. Something in your manner rounds off the edges of a first encounter — you mirror pace, find the shared reference, make the other person feel reasonable. Hosts seat you next to the difficult guest for a reason.

The strength is genuine: you lower the temperature of rooms. The friction is that smoothing becomes reflex. You agree in the doorway and object in the car, and people who only ever meet your pleasant surface are startled when the firmer person underneath — the one your Sun or Moon actually runs on — finally speaks.

Watch for the moment today when you say **either is fine** while privately preferring one. Name the preference instead.
