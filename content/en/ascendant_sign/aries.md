title: Aries Rising
---

You arrive like a door swinging open. People read you as direct, quick, and a little impatient — someone who would rather start wrong than wait right. That first impression is usually accurate about your pace, if not about your depth: underneath, your Sun or Moon may be far more deliberate than the face you lead with.

Your strength is ignition. Rooms stall until someone moves first, and you are almost always that someone. The friction is that speed reads as pressure; colleagues and partners can feel steamrolled before you have said anything unkind.

The next time you feel the urge to jump in, count the beat between someone's sentence ending and yours beginning — stretch it by two seconds and watch what changes.
