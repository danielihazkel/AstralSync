title: Scorpio Rising
---

You give away nothing at the door. People meet a level gaze, a few precise words, and the distinct sense of being assessed — which they are. This containment is a strength: you are hard to manipulate, hard to rush, and when you do commit your attention, it lands with real weight.

It is also a wall. Strangers often read your reserve as suspicion or disapproval when, underneath, your Sun or Moon might be sunny, chatty, even soft. You control the interface so tightly that warmth has trouble getting out, not just in.

Try volunteering one unprompted personal detail in your next new conversation — small, true, freely given — and let someone in a half-step early.
