key: ascendant_sign/capricorn
title: Capricorn Rising
---

People take you seriously before you have asked them to. Your bearing suggests competence and self-control: you arrive on time, dressed one notch sharper than the occasion, already scanning for what the situation requires. Authority gets handed to you at doors where others have to request it.

The tax is warmth. Your composure can read as distance, your dry humor arrives too deadpan to be caught, and newcomers may assume you are older, sterner, or less playful than the person your Sun and Moon actually make you.

You do not need to perform lightness — just signal it. Smile in the first thirty seconds of your next introduction, before you have decided whether the meeting is worth it.
