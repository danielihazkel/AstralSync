title: Jupiter square Midheaven
---

Jupiter presses on the angle person's vertical axis from the side, stirring both their public ambitions and their private home life. The Jupiter person wants more for the angle person's career and has strong views about how the household should support it — or about how home should be bigger, freer, more open to guests and plans. The gift is momentum: the angle person stops settling for a job or a life that is merely adequate.

The cost is a growth agenda that never pauses. The Jupiter person's encouragement can sound like a verdict that the angle person's current path is too small, and the push upward can come wrapped in moralizing about purpose and potential. The angle person may overextend — taking the bigger role, the bigger mortgage, the bigger commitment — to prove the Jupiter person right.

Before any major career or household expansion, both of you name what it will cost in time and money, not only what it promises.
