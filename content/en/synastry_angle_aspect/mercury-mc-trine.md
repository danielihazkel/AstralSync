title: Mercury trine Midheaven
---

The angle person's ambitions and the Mercury person's mind run on compatible tracks. Career talk between you is genuinely productive: the Mercury person grasps the industry politics on first hearing, remembers the names, and frames the angle person's work so well that the angle person sometimes quotes them in interviews. Plans made together tend to be smarter than plans made alone.

The comfortable groove has a cost. The Mercury person becomes the default sounding board, and the angle person may stop testing ideas anywhere else — narrowing their inputs to one clever voice. Meanwhile the Mercury person's own projects can get crowded out of the conversation entirely, reduced to a footnote after an hour on the angle person's promotion.

Split the airtime on purpose: for every planning session about the angle person's path, spend equal time on what the Mercury person is building.
