title: Sun trine Ascendant
---

The Sun person's core self and the angle person's outward style run in the same current. You look right together, and more importantly you feel right together in public — the angle person's natural manner seems to express something the Sun person deeply values, so approval flows without being asked for. Friends comment on the chemistry before you have named it yourselves.

The gift is mutual amplification: the angle person shines more easily, and the Sun person feels flatteringly reflected. The friction hides in the ease. Because being seen together is so comfortable, the pair can live at the surface — great entrances, warm appearances, little said about what is underneath. The angle person may also lean on the Sun person's approval instead of their own compass.

Keep some of your best moments private, so the bond is built on who you are and not only on how well you appear.
