title: Mercury conjunct Midheaven
---

The Mercury person becomes the in-house strategist for the angle person's career. They think about this person's professional life almost as fluently as their own — drafting the tricky email, rehearsing the negotiation, spotting the angle in a job posting the angle person skimmed past. When the angle person needs to explain what they do and why it matters, the Mercury person hands them the words.

The friction is authorship. Advice arrives whether or not it was requested, and the angle person can start to feel their career is being ghostwritten — every plan annotated, every decision pre-briefed. The Mercury person, for their part, may feel unthanked for labor the angle person never actually assigned.

Make the consulting explicit: the angle person names the problems they want help with, and the Mercury person saves their brilliance for that list.
