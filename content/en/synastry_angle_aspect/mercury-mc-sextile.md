title: Mercury sextile Midheaven
---

Shop talk is easy and useful here. The Mercury person asks good questions about the angle person's work — the kind that untangle a problem rather than just filling air — and the angle person finds that describing their ambitions to this listener makes the ambitions clearer. Ideas, contacts, and well-worded introductions flow toward the angle person's career without either of you forcing it.

Because the help is low-effort, it can also be low-commitment. The Mercury person offers three clever options and moves on, leaving the angle person to do the choosing and the labor; the angle person may enjoy the brainstorms but quietly never act on them, which slowly teaches the Mercury person to stop offering.

Close the loop deliberately: when a suggestion lands, the angle person reports back on what they did with it, so good ideas keep coming.
