title: Saturn conjunct Midheaven
---

The Saturn person takes the angle person's ambitions more seriously than almost anyone else in their life — sometimes more seriously than the angle person does. They become the unofficial mentor: asking about the five-year plan, insisting on the boring fundamentals, holding the ladder steady while the angle person climbs. Careers touched by this contact tend to gain structure, credibility, and staying power.

The shadow is gatekeeping. Saturn's seriousness can harden into judgment about which of the angle person's ambitions are "realistic," and the mentor's voice starts sounding like a supervisor's. The angle person may find themselves working for the Saturn person's approval instead of their own goals, or hiding the wilder dreams entirely.

Keep the roles honest: the Saturn person offers structure only for goals the angle person has chosen, and once a year the angle person names one ambition that is not up for review.
