title: Mercury square Midheaven
---

The Mercury person's questions land crosswise on the angle person's whole vertical axis — probing the career and the home life in the same breath. "Why are you still at that job?" arrives alongside "why do we never talk at dinner?", and the angle person can feel audited from both directions at once. The gift is real: this is the one voice that will not let the angle person's public story and private reality quietly drift apart.

The friction is that insight delivered as interruption sounds like doubt. The angle person hears their competence questioned; the Mercury person hears their honest curiosity treated as sabotage, and starts editorializing to friends instead.

Give the hard questions a container — a monthly check-in where anything about work or home can be asked — and keep ambush-free zones the rest of the time.
