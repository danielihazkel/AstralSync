Talk comes easily here, and it flatters the angle person without trying to. The Mercury person notices how this person comes across and can articulate it lightly — a well-timed observation, a phrase the angle person borrows for their own bio, a joke that lands because it is precise. The angle person, in turn, gives the Mercury person a lively audience; ideas sharpen when spoken in their direction.

Ease is the gift and the limit. The banter can stay pleasant and glancing, circling the angle person's surface without ever asking what is underneath it, and the Mercury person's sharpest insights may go unshared because the moment never demands them.

Every so often, trade one observation that costs something — what each of you actually sees changing in the other — instead of another round of comfortable wit.
