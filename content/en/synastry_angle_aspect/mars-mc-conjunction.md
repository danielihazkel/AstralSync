title: Mars conjunct Midheaven
---

One of you has landed their Mars squarely on the other's Midheaven, and the angle person's career becomes a shared campaign. The Mars person sees where this person could go and pushes — making the introduction, drafting the pitch, saying "ask for more" when the angle person hesitates. Ambitions that drifted for years suddenly have an engine.

The friction is ownership. Mars can champion so hard it starts steamrolling: choosing battles the angle person never wanted to fight, or treating their professional turf as its own. The angle person may feel energized one week and managed the next, and disagreements between you about their work can get visibly heated.

Agree on a simple rule: the Mars person proposes and provokes, but the angle person makes the final call on their own career — out loud, every time.
