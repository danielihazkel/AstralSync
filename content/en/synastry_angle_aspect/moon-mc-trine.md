title: Moon trine Midheaven
---

The Moon person's care and the angle person's calling flow together without competing. The Moon person genuinely enjoys watching the other work toward something, and the angle person finds their ambition easier to sustain with this steady warmth behind it — home life and public life feed each other instead of fighting for the same hours.

The gift is effortless backing: the angle person never has to justify the dream at their own kitchen table, and the Moon person gets the quiet satisfaction of being part of what gets built. The friction is invisibility. Support this smooth is easy to stop noticing, and the Moon person's own aspirations can slip into the background while both of you orbit the angle person's trajectory.

Take turns being the one with the dream — ask regularly what the Moon person is building, and back it with the same warmth.
