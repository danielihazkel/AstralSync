title: Sun opposition Midheaven
---

Opposing the Midheaven, the Sun person lands on the angle person's IC — the private floor of the chart, where family, home, and origins live. Whoever brings the Sun here brightens the other's inner world: the angle person feels unusually at home with this person, willing to show the unpolished self that never appears on their résumé.

The gift is roots. You build a hearth together quickly — shared spaces, family feelings, a sense that this bond is where the angle person recharges from public life. The friction is the pull between hearth and summit: the Sun person can anchor the angle person so warmly at home that ambition dims, or feel excluded from the polished, public self the angle person shows the world.

Bring the two halves together on purpose — let the Sun person witness the angle person's public life sometimes, and treat home as a base camp, not a hiding place.
