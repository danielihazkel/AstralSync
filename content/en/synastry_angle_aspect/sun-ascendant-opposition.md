title: Sun opposition Ascendant
---

Opposing the Ascendant means the Sun person is sitting right on the angle person's Descendant — the classic signature of "this is the partner I was describing before we met." The angle person tends to recognize the Sun person instantly as their kind of other half; the Sun person, in turn, feels unusually welcomed into the role of counterpart, ally, and equal.

The gift is genuine partnership gravity: you naturally face each other, negotiate as a pair, and fill in each other's missing pieces. The friction is projection. The angle person can hand the Sun person the job of being confident, decisive, or radiant for both of them, then resent how much space that takes; the Sun person can enjoy being needed a little too much.

Keep it a partnership of two whole people — regularly ask what each of you wants separately, before deciding anything together.
