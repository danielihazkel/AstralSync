title: Saturn sextile Midheaven
---

The Saturn person lends quiet ballast to the angle person's career. Their support is practical and unglamorous — reviewing the contract, remembering the deadline, being the reason the angle person saves before they leap. Nothing about it is dramatic, which is exactly why it works: the angle person's ambitions gain a keel, and their bolder moves succeed more often because someone insisted on the groundwork first.

The friction here is undersupply of celebration. The Saturn person marks progress by moving the goalpost — good work is met with the next requirement — and the angle person can end up feeling that no milestone will ever be enough to earn a toast. Meanwhile the Saturn person's steady labor goes unthanked because it never announces itself.

When something is achieved, stop and mark it together — dinner, a toast, a day off — before either of you mentions what comes next.
