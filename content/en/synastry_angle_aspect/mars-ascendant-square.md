title: Mars square Ascendant
---

The Mars person gets under the Ascendant person's skin — sometimes deliciously, sometimes just under it. The very way this partner walks, talks, and presents themselves seems to provoke Mars into commentary or contest, and because the square also clips the Descendant, the sparks fly straight into how you relate: sudden quarrels that neither of you quite chose, over tone rather than substance.

The gift is honesty with a pulse. Nothing stays politely buried between you; irritation surfaces fast, gets handled, and the attraction underneath it stays lively for years. The friction is wear — being reflexively challenged about how you come across is tiring, and the Ascendant person may start armoring their whole manner before the Mars person even speaks.

Fight the issue, not the delivery: when heat rises, each of you names the actual grievance in one sentence before responding to tone.
