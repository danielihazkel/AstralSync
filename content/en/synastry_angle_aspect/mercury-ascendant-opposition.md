title: Mercury opposition Ascendant
---

Opposing the Ascendant, the Mercury person sits directly on the angle person's Descendant — the partnership point — and becomes the designated interlocutor. This is a bond built face to face and sentence by sentence: the angle person thinks best out loud with this particular listener, and big decisions do not feel decided until the two of you have talked them through. As a pair you negotiate, plan, and debrief unusually well.

The friction is that everything becomes a conversation. The Mercury person can turn dinner into a seminar; the angle person may feel they cannot simply sit with something before it gets discussed, dissected, and filed. Words can also stand in for contact — analyzing the relationship instead of having it.

Protect some wordless time together each week, and let one decision a month be made by feel, reviewed afterward instead of debated first.
