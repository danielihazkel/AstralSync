title: Mars trine Midheaven
---

Backing flows toward the angle person's ambitions without much translation. The Mars person instinctively fights for this person's public standing — talking them up in rooms they are not in, clearing obstacles before they are asked, treating the angle person's wins as shared victories. Under that steady advocacy, the angle person tends to aim higher than they would alone.

The ease has a blind spot. Because support arrives automatically, the angle person may stop steering — letting the Mars person's enthusiasm choose the next move — while the Mars person can pour energy into a direction the angle person quietly outgrew. Momentum without a destination is still drift, just faster.

Every few months, sit down and let the angle person restate the goal in their own words before the Mars person commits another ounce of fuel to it.
