title: Sun sextile Ascendant
---

The Sun person warms the angle person's self-presentation without crowding it. There is an easy, encouraging current here: one of you carries a clear sense of purpose, and the other finds it pleasantly simple to show up as themselves alongside it. Compliments land well. Introductions go smoothly. You like being seen together.

The gift is low-effort confidence — the angle person often experiments with new looks, new openings, new first impressions because the Sun person's approval feels safely available. The friction is mildness: because nothing is demanded, the encouragement can stay vague. The Sun person nods along rather than truly investing, and the angle person settles for pleasant instead of memorable.

Turn the goodwill into fuel — have the Sun person name one specific thing the angle person does brilliantly, and build on it together.
