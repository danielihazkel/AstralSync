title: Jupiter square Ascendant
---

The Jupiter person pushes the angle person toward a larger life, and the push is felt. They challenge the angle person's caution, question their modest self-image, and keep raising the ceiling on what this person should want. When it works, the angle person outgrows habits that were keeping them small and discovers appetites they had talked themselves out of.

When it misfires, encouragement turns into management. The Jupiter person's vision of the angle person can be more about Jupiter's own philosophy than about the actual person in front of them, and the advice arrives with a faint sermon attached. The angle person may feel steered upward against their own pace, or start over-promising to match the hype, then resent the gap between who they are and who they were sold as.

Before offering a bigger plan, the Jupiter person should ask one question: "Do you want this, or do I want it for you?"
