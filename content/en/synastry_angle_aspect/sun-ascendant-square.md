title: Sun square Ascendant
---

The Sun person's sense of identity sits crosswise to how the angle person presents themselves — and, because a square touches both ends of the axis, to how they relate one-on-one as well. One of you walks in radiating a direction; the other feels subtly asked to adjust their style, their tone, even their entrance to accommodate it. Neither is wrong. The angles are simply different.

The gift is friction that sharpens: the angle person cannot coast on autopilot charm around the Sun person, and often discovers a stronger, more deliberate self because of it. The trouble is a low hum of image conflict — public moments where one of you feels upstaged, edited, or dressed down in front of others.

Agree on this rule: challenge each other in private, present as allies in public, and revisit any moment that broke it.
