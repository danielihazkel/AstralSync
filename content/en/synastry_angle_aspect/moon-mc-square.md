title: Moon square Midheaven
---

The Moon person's emotional needs run crosswise to the angle person's career axis, pulling at both the public summit and the private foundation. One of you needs closeness and domestic rhythm at precisely the seasons the other's ambitions demand absence and output. Neither appetite is wrong; they simply peak on conflicting schedules.

The gift is that this square keeps the angle person honest about cost — no promotion arrives without someone lovingly asking what it will take from the home you share, which saves the pair from victories that hollow out the relationship. The friction is the recurring standoff: the Moon person feels deprioritized by the career, the angle person feels guilted for having one, and both keep score quietly.

Put the tradeoff on the calendar — when a demanding stretch begins, name its end date together and plan the reunion before it starts.
