title: Sun square Midheaven
---

The Sun person's identity runs crosswise to the angle person's career axis — pulling at both the public summit and the private base of their life. One of you has a strong sense of direction that does not automatically fit the other's professional path or home rhythm; plans that suit the Sun person's purpose can complicate the angle person's ambitions, and vice versa.

The gift is honest stress-testing. The angle person's goals get questioned by someone whose opinion actually matters, and the goals that survive come out clearer and more truly their own. The friction is a recurring tug-of-war: career decisions that feel like loyalty tests, and moments where one of you suspects the other's success comes at their expense.

Name the competition out loud when you feel it, then decide one concrete way each of you will back the other's next milestone.
