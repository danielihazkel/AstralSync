title: Saturn trine Midheaven
---

The Saturn person backs the angle person's climb the way a good foundation backs a house — invisibly and constantly. They respect this person's profession without needing it explained, absorb the late nights without keeping score, and their advice, when it comes, is the durable kind: reputations, savings, and skills compound in this partnership. The angle person often looks back and realizes their steadiest career years happened alongside this ally.

The trap is quiet conservatism. Because what you have built together works, the Saturn person may greet any pivot — a resignation, a retraining, a risk — with reflexive gravity, and the angle person may pre-shrink their ambitions to fit the existing structure. Duty keeps the machine running while nobody asks whether it is still pointed anywhere.

Once a year, plan as if starting over: each of you says what you would build now if nothing were already built, and take one piece of it seriously.
