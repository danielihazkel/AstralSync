title: Sun conjunct Ascendant
---

Whoever brings the Sun here lands directly on the other's front door. The Sun person sees the angle person's style, mannerisms, and way of entering a room — and lights all of it up. The angle person tends to feel more visible, more confident, more like themselves in this company, as if someone finally turned the spotlight toward the version of them they always meant to project.

The gift is vitality: you make a striking pair, and the angle person often grows bolder simply by proximity. The friction is glare. The Sun person's presence can be so bright that the angle person starts performing for an audience of one, or feels overshadowed at their own party.

Trade the spotlight deliberately — let the angle person headline some rooms while the Sun person applauds from the wings.
