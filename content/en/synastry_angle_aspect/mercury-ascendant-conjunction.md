title: Mercury conjunct Ascendant
---

The Mercury person keeps a running commentary on how the angle person moves through the world — and it is often startlingly accurate. They can put words to this person's style, mood, and mannerisms before the angle person has found them, which makes the angle person feel genuinely seen. Conversation between you starts fast and rarely stalls; the angle person's presence seems to switch the Mercury person's mind on.

The catch is that observation shades into editing. A remark about the outfit, the phrasing, the way they entered the room — each one small, together a steady stream of unsolicited notes. The angle person can begin performing for the commentary or bracing against it.

Let the angle person set the channel: the Mercury person asks "do you want my read on this?" before offering one, and honors the no.
