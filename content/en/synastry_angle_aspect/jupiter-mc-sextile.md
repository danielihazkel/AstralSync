title: Jupiter sextile Midheaven
---

The Jupiter person is a natural source of career openings for the angle person. Introductions, a timely piece of advice, a contact in the right field, a course worth taking — help arrives in practical, low-pressure form, and the angle person's public reputation benefits from being associated with someone so well-regarded and well-connected. Work talk between you is easy and usually hopeful.

Because none of it is urgent, little of it gets acted on. The Jupiter person may offer leads generously but loosely, and the angle person may nod along without committing to any of them. Over time the relationship can collect a long list of good ideas about the angle person's future that nobody actually tests.

Once a quarter, sit down together and choose one of the Jupiter person's suggestions to pursue properly — with a date, a first step, and a check-in.
