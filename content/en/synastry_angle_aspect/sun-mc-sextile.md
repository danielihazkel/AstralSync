title: Sun sextile Midheaven
---

The Sun person takes an easy, genuine interest in the angle person's public life. Career talk flows naturally between you — one of you describes an ambition and the other's instinct is to encourage it, sketch a next step, or mention a useful contact. Support here is offered lightly, never imposed.

The gift is steady professional warmth: the angle person feels backed without feeling managed, and often takes small risks — a pitch, an application, a raise conversation — because someone believes in the arc of their work. The friction is that light support can stay theoretical. The Sun person cheers from a comfortable distance, and opportunities that needed a real push drift by half-encouraged.

When a genuine chance appears in the angle person's career, convert the goodwill into one concrete act of help, not just applause.
