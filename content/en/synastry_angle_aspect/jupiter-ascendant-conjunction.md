title: Jupiter conjunct Ascendant
---

The Jupiter person sees the angle person as bigger than they see themselves, and says so. In this company the angle person takes up more room: they laugh louder, pitch the idea they were sitting on, and walk into rooms as though they were expected. The Jupiter person enjoys this expansion as much as the one expanding, and the relationship often has a buoyant, lucky feel from the first meeting.

The inflation runs both ways. Praise this generous can drift from accurate to promotional, and the angle person may start performing the larger self the Jupiter person describes instead of growing into it. The Jupiter person, meanwhile, can turn every conversation into a pep talk or a lesson about how to live, mistaking enthusiasm for listening.

When the Jupiter person offers encouragement, make it specific: name the actual thing the angle person did well, not the grand thing they could become.
