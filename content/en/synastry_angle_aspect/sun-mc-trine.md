title: Sun trine Midheaven
---

The Sun person's purpose and the angle person's public direction flow the same way. What one of you is trying to become fits naturally beside what the other is trying to build, so career support here rarely needs negotiation — the Sun person's pride in the angle person's work is real and unforced, and the angle person tends to rise faster with that warmth behind them.

The gift is an ally who never treats your ambition as a threat. Mentorship, referrals, and honest strategy talk come easily, and outsiders often read you as a power pairing. The friction is complacency: because approval is guaranteed, the angle person may stop seeking harder feedback, and the Sun person may endorse plans they have not actually examined.

Once in a while, ask the Sun person to critique the plan like a stranger would — then enjoy how well you build together.
