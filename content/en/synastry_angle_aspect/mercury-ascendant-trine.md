title: Mercury trine Ascendant
---

Words and presence cooperate between you. The Mercury person reads the angle person fluently — catching the shift in mood behind the polite face, translating this person to friends and colleagues who find them opaque. The angle person relaxes into being understood without having to explain, and conversations between you tend to leave both of you clearer than you started.

The risk is ventriloquism. Because the Mercury person articulates the angle person so well, the angle person can drift into letting them do it — nodding along to summaries of their own feelings, outsourcing the hard sentences. Over time the portrait, however flattering, replaces the sitter, and the Mercury person mistakes fluency for full knowledge.

Keep the angle person in first person: when something matters, they say it in their own words before the Mercury person polishes it.
