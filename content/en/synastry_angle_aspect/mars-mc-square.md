title: Mars square Midheaven
---

The Mars person's drive cuts across the angle person's whole vertical axis, tugging at their public life and their private one at the same time. Pressure about the career shows up at the dinner table; tension from home follows the angle person to work. The gift inside the abrasion is honesty — the Mars person will not politely watch this person coast, and their challenges often name exactly where the angle person has gone soft.

But challenge delivered at the wrong angle reads as attack. The angle person can feel that nothing is safe from critique — not the job, not the household — and start defending turf instead of hearing the point. Arguments about "your career" are frequently arguments about respect.

Pick the arena before you engage: name whether tonight's conversation is about work or about home, and keep it there until it is finished.
