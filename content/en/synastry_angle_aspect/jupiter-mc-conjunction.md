title: Jupiter conjunct Midheaven
---

The Jupiter person treats the angle person's career as a calling worth backing. They talk up the angle person's work to anyone who will listen, see promotions and pivots before the angle person does, and supply the confidence to apply for the role that seemed out of reach. Public standing often grows during this relationship, and the angle person may credit this partner with the turn in their professional luck.

The shadow is a reputation inflated faster than the skills behind it. The Jupiter person can oversell the angle person to colleagues or push them to accept titles they are not ready for, and the angle person may feel that every achievement is immediately treated as a stepping stone to something grander. Ambition gets borrowed rather than owned.

Agree on one professional goal that belongs to the angle person alone, and let the Jupiter person cheer it on without enlarging it.
