title: Moon conjunct Midheaven
---

The Moon person cares — personally, viscerally — about the angle person's ambitions. Career wins for one feel like family news to the other; setbacks land in the Moon person's stomach as if they were their own. Whoever brings the Moon here becomes the emotional home base of the other's public climb: the first call after the interview, the face scanned for in the audience.

The gift is ambition with a heartbeat. The angle person aims higher knowing someone genuinely tends the dream, and the Moon person finds purpose in nurturing something the world will eventually see. The friction is entanglement: the Moon person's moods can start tracking the angle person's career like a stock ticker, and professional decisions get weighed down by fear of disappointing each other.

Separate the person from the résumé — celebrate and grieve outcomes together, but make clear the caring never depended on them.
