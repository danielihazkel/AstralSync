title: Mercury opposition Midheaven
---

Opposing the Midheaven, the Mercury person sits on the angle person's IC, and the conversation moves indoors. This bond reaches into home and family life: the Mercury person is the one the angle person tells about the childhood story, the sibling tension, the thing they have never said at work. Around this listener, the angle person's private world finally gets language, and the household itself gets more talkative — plans, memories, running jokes.

The friction is porousness. The Mercury person can narrate what was told in confidence, or analyze the angle person's family with a briskness that feels like trespass. And a home where everything is discussed can leave the angle person nowhere to be simply quiet.

Agree that the private channel stays private, and let the angle person mark some topics "not yet" — held, not hidden — until they are ready to talk.
