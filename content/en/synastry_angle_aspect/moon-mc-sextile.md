title: Moon sextile Midheaven
---

The Moon person offers the angle person's ambitions a kind of gentle weather report — sensing when the work is going well, when a goal has quietly lost its shine, and when encouragement would land better than advice. The support is instinctive and undemanding: a good dinner after a hard week, the right question at the right time.

The gift is sustainable ambition. With the Moon person tending the emotional side of the climb, the angle person burns out less and recovers faster, and the pair develops a comfortable rhythm between work talk and real life. The friction is that comfort can mute urgency: the Moon person soothes where a push was needed, and the angle person mistakes being consoled for being championed.

When a setback hits, ask which is wanted first — comfort or strategy — and give that one before the other.
