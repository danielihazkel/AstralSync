title: Mars sextile Midheaven
---

The Mars person's energy is easy for the angle person to borrow. When a deadline looms or an opportunity opens, the Mars person supplies momentum without taking over — a nudge to apply, a practice run before the interview, an hour of help that turns a stalled project loose. The angle person's ambitions move faster with this ally nearby, and the help rarely feels heavy.

Because the support is comfortable, it can also stay shallow. The Mars person cheers from the sidelines but may never learn what the angle person actually wants long-term, and the angle person can start treating that energy as free fuel, drawing on it without asking what the Mars person is building for themselves.

Once a season, trade real updates: each of you names one professional goal, and the other commits to one concrete push toward it.
