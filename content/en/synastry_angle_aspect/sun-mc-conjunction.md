title: Sun conjunct Midheaven
---

The Sun person stands at the summit of the angle person's chart — squarely inside their career, reputation, and public direction. Whoever brings the Sun here instinctively champions the other's ambitions: opening doors, talking them up, treating their goals as worthy of real light. The angle person often achieves more, and more visibly, with this person in their corner.

The gift is a shared sense of mission; you can feel like a team building something the world will see. The friction is authorship. The Sun person's ideas about what the angle person should become can quietly replace the angle person's own, or the Sun person may start absorbing credit that belongs to the climb itself.

Check in on whose dream is being built — let the angle person state the goal in their own words, and let the Sun person power it rather than steer it.
