title: Mars opposition Ascendant
---

Opposing the Ascendant, the Mars person lands on the other's Descendant — the partnership angle — and brings a sword to it. The angle person experiences this partner as magnetic in the most literal sense: impossible to ignore, arousing, and just as capable of pulling them into an argument as into an embrace. Relationships with this signature are rarely lukewarm.

The gift is engagement: the Mars person treats the partnership as something worth fighting for, and their directness drags real issues into the open where they can actually be solved. The friction is that the same directness turns the relationship itself into the arena — the angle person can start to feel that being close to this person means being perpetually opposed by them.

Agree on rules of engagement while you are calm: what is fair in a fight, what is out of bounds, and how you signal that a duel should become a truce.
