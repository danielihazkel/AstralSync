title: Mars opposition Midheaven
---

Opposing the Midheaven, the Mars person's fire lands on the angle person's IC — the base of the chart, the door to home and family. This bond reaches past the resume into private life: the Mars person energizes the household, tackles the move, the renovation, the difficult relative, and makes the angle person's foundations feel defended rather than fragile.

The same heat can scorch indoors. Mars stirring the domestic sphere means restlessness at home, doors closed a little too hard, and career frustrations detonating in the one room meant to be safe. The angle person may feel their sanctuary has acquired a motor that never quite idles.

Give the energy a domestic job — a shared project with a finish line — and agree that work grievances get aired on a walk, not in the kitchen.
