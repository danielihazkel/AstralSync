title: Moon sextile Saturn
---

Between you, feelings get handled with quiet competence. One of you brings the emotional weather; the Saturn side of this pairing brings the shelter — routines, patience, and a calm that makes hard days smaller. Trust builds gradually here, and it builds solid.

The gift is dependable care: you can fall apart in front of each other without fear that the other will flinch or leave. The friction is understatement. Because comfort arrives in useful forms — errands run, plans made, problems solved — the tender words can go unsaid, and one of you may start wondering whether the devotion is felt or merely performed on schedule.

Pair the practical care with plain declarations. Saying "I'm doing this because I love you" once in a while keeps the steadiness from being taken for granted.
