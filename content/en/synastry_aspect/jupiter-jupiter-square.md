title: Jupiter square Jupiter
---

Your ideas of growth pull at an angle. This contact usually links people about three or nine years apart in age, formed by different cultural moods about faith, success, and what a generous life looks like. One of you may value risk while the other values principle, or one seeks meaning through expansion while the other seeks it through belief.

The friction is mostly about values, not warmth. Each of you can find the other's convictions excessive, naive, or preachy, and discussions about money, religion, politics, or ambition can escalate into competing sermons. Your expansive seasons also arrive on different clocks, so one is ready to leap while the other wants to consolidate. The gift is stretch: you widen each other's sense of what is possible once you stop trying to convert.

Agree to disagree on the big questions and cooperate on the specific ones. Decide together on one shared goal and let each of you justify it in your own terms.
