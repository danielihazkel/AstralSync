title: Saturn sextile Saturn
---

Your rulebooks differ just enough to be useful. Coming from nearby but distinct Saturn cohorts, you hold compatible ideas about responsibility with slightly different emphases — one of you stricter about money, perhaps, the other about time or family duty. The differences read as perspective, not threat.

The gift is easy structural cooperation: you can divide obligations, plan years ahead, and trust each other's follow-through without needing identical philosophies. Each of you also lightly modernizes the other's habits. The friction is complacency about the whole system. Because your standards mesh so smoothly, you may never seriously question them together, and shared duties can quietly expand until they crowd out everything unscheduled.

Review the load, not just the split. Twice a year, ask together which obligations still deserve you — and retire one that no longer does.
