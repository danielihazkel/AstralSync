title: Venus conjunct Saturn
---

Love gets serious fast between you. One of you brings affection, charm, and the wish to please; the Saturn side of this pairing brings commitment, caution, and a deep distrust of anything easy. The result is a bond that would rather prove itself than announce itself — fewer grand gestures, more kept promises.

The gift is loyalty of an old-fashioned kind: this aspect is famous for relationships that last, because both of you treat love as something built rather than found. The friction is scarcity. Saturn can ration affection like a budget, and the warmer one of you may begin working for tenderness that should be given freely, mistaking drought for depth.

Guard against earning each other. Give one unearned, unprompted gesture of affection every day, and let it be received without commentary.
