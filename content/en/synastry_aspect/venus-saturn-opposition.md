title: Venus opposition Saturn
---

Attraction and restraint face off across this pairing. One of you offers openness, pleasure, and the wish to be adored; the Saturn side answers with seriousness, boundaries, and a wariness about being owed anything. The pull between you is strong precisely because each embodies what the other holds at arm's length.

The gift is ballast: this bond resists shallowness, and when you commit, you commit with eyes open, each tempering the other — indulgence gains judgment, discipline gains sweetness. The friction is the withholding cycle. One of you reaches, the other steps back to assess, the reach becomes a plea, the assessment becomes a wall, and both of you end up lonely inside a loyal relationship.

Interrupt the cycle early. When one of you notices the distance opening, name it that day — closeness here must be chosen out loud, not assumed.
