title: Sun conjunct Jupiter
---

This is one of the most generous contacts two charts can make. One of you brings identity, will, and a wish to be seen; the Jupiter person meets it with instant, wholehearted belief. In each other's company the Sun person feels larger and more capable, and the Jupiter person gets to do what they love most — champion someone worth championing.

The gift is momentum: plans get bigger, risks feel reasonable, and confidence is contagious in both directions. The friction is inflation. Praise this easy can outrun the facts, and the pair can talk each other into ventures, purchases, or promises that neither would have signed alone. The Jupiter person may also start coaching when the Sun person only wanted applause.

Keep one honest checkpoint before every big yes — a night's sleep, a budget, a friend's opinion — so the encouragement stays fuel rather than fog.
