title: Venus sextile Jupiter
---

Kindness flows easily between you, and it tends to open doors. The Venus person's charm and sense of what is pleasing meet the Jupiter person's optimism and generosity, and together you notice opportunities — a trip, a friendship, a creative project — that neither of you would have chased alone.

The gift is good-humored goodwill: you speak well of each other, forgive small lapses quickly, and enjoy introducing each other to people and places. The friction is that this aspect offers rather than insists. The openings it creates need someone to act on them, and two comfortable people can let a promising idea drift into "someday" because nothing is pushing.

Treat your good ideas as appointments. When one of you says "we should," the other picks a date before the conversation ends, and you both protect it as you would any other commitment.
