title: Venus opposition Mars
---

This is polarity in its classic form: pursuit facing receptivity across the full width of the axis. The Mars person wants and shows it; the Venus person attracts and knows it; and the space between you crackles with a tension neither quite wants resolved. Few aspects generate more immediate chemistry.

The gift is magnetism that does not wear off on schedule — the charge renews because the poles stay poles. The cost is the same distance that creates the charge: wanting and being wanted are different jobs, and over time the roles can calcify into pursuer and prize, effort and evaluation, until desire starts keeping score. Timing mismatches — advance meeting withdrawal — are this aspect's signature quarrel.

Rotate the poles: the one who is always wanted must sometimes do the wanting, openly. Polarity stays electric only when both of you have held each end.
