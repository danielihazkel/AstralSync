title: Mercury sextile Mars
---

Words turn into action easily between you. One of you thinks it, the other is already reaching for shoes — and the reverse works too, with the doer's energy prodding the thinker to stop polishing and commit. Your conversations tend to end with something actually happening.

The gift is productive candor: you can be direct with each other without wounds, and planning together feels brisk and alive rather than tedious. The friction is pace pressure. The quicker current between you can rush past reflection — half-discussed plans get executed, and the one who wanted to think longer learns to just go along.

Before acting on a good conversation, add one deliberate pause: sleep on anything irreversible, and let the slower thought catch up to the faster feet.
