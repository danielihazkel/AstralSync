title: Mercury sextile Venus
---

Wit and warmth flirt easily here. One of you says something clever, the other receives it with delight, and the exchange keeps looping upward — banter, in-jokes, long comfortable talks about what you both find beautiful. Compliments come naturally, and so does laughing at the same moment.

The gift is social ease: this connection smooths rough days, makes shared plans pleasant to negotiate, and gives affection a reliable verbal channel. The friction is lightness — the conversational current between you prefers the agreeable surface, so grievances tend to arrive dressed as jokes, or not at all, and real preferences can hide behind politeness.

Once in a while, trade one charming exchange for an unvarnished one: say what you actually want, without a punchline attached.
