title: Mars conjunction Mars
---

Your engines are the same make. Pace, temper, appetite for challenge, and style of pursuit all match: when you move together, the combined momentum is formidable, and each of you finally has a partner who neither lags nor flinches. Shared effort — work, sport, building anything — is this pairing's love language.

The gift is force multiplication: what either of you attempts alone, you attempt together at double strength and half hesitation. The cost is that identical drives also collide identically — same trigger, same fuse length, same refusal to back down — so when you clash, there is no built-in damper, and disagreements escalate symmetrically until something external stops them.

Point the engines at shared targets relentlessly, and adopt one asymmetric rule for conflicts: whoever noticed the escalation first calls the pause, and the other honors it as a win for both.
