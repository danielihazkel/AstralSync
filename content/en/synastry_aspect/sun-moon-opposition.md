title: Sun opposition Moon
---

This is the classic full-moon pairing: one of you embodies direction and daylight, the other embodies mood and memory, and you face each other across that divide with unusual mutual fascination. Each of you makes the other feel witnessed from an angle no one else quite occupies.

The gift is complementarity with real gravity — many lasting bonds carry this aspect, because each partner holds what the other reaches for. The cost is a recurring seesaw: the Sun person's plans can eclipse the Moon person's needs, the Moon person's tides can swamp the Sun person's momentum, and both can feel the relationship demands choosing between selfhood and closeness.

Treat the divide as a shared horizon rather than a fault line: schedule both agendas — the outward push and the inward tending — and honor whichever one is not yours.
