title: Mars trine Saturn
---

Effort flows efficiently between you. One of you brings the push, the Saturn side of this pairing brings the pacing, and together you get the rarest thing in shared work: sustained momentum without burnout. You start what you can finish and finish what you start, and other couples often envy how little you argue about who does what.

The gift is endurance — this pairing can carry long campaigns, tight years, and big builds without breaking stride. The friction is the grind's comfort. Discipline that comes this easily can become the whole personality of the relationship: always another project, always a next phase, effort standing in for intimacy because effort is what you are best at.

Finish something and then actually stop. Celebrate completions extravagantly and take real rest together, so the machine you have built stays in service of the love.
