title: Moon opposition Venus
---

Comfort and charm face each other across the table. The Venus person is drawn to the Moon person's emotional realness; the Moon person is soothed and slightly dazzled by the Venus person's grace — and each secretly suspects the other's mode is the superior one. Longing has a built-in role in this pairing: what the other has always looks like what is missing.

The gift is complementary softness — between you, both the polished and the private forms of tenderness exist, and pairs who share them freely feel unusually complete. The cost is a quiet exchange-rate problem: the Moon person can perform depth to earn delight, the Venus person can perform delight to avoid depth, until both feel loved for the act.

Trade places regularly: let the Venus person be moody and unlovely, let the Moon person host and shine — and receive each other's off-role self warmly when it appears.
