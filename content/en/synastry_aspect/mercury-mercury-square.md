title: Mercury square Mercury
---

You process the world at cross angles. One of you thinks out loud, the other thinks before speaking; one wants the headline, the other needs the whole story; interruptions and "that's not what I meant" are regular guests in your conversations. Neither style is wrong — they just cut across each other.

The gift is coverage: between the two of you, a problem gets examined from angles neither would find alone, and your best decisions are genuinely sturdier for the argument. The friction is exhaustion — everyday logistics can turn into translation work, and each of you can start to feel that the other simply refuses to understand.

Match the medium to the mind: put plans in writing for the one who needs to see them, and talk them through for the one who needs to hear them.
