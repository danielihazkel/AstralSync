title: Mars opposition Saturn
---

Force meets wall across this pairing. One of you charges; the Saturn side of this pairing holds the line — and each of you can feel appointed to your post by the other's behavior. The more one pushes, the more the other fortifies; the more one delays, the harder the other pushes. Standoffs here are less shouting match than siege.

The gift is genuine balance when you use it consciously: courage with judgment, urgency with staying power. Big undertakings suit you, because between you nothing is either rushed or abandoned. The friction is chronic timing war — desire and readiness rarely arriving together, and both of you keeping score of who yielded last.

Trade concessions explicitly rather than winning silently. When you disagree on pace, set a date to act and a condition to wait for, and honor whichever comes first.
