title: Venus trine Venus
---

Affection circulates between you in compatible currencies. Your tastes differ enough to stay interesting and agree enough that shared life composes itself — the home, the friends, the rituals all form without committee meetings. Each of you feels that being loved by the other is easy to recognize and easy to return.

The gift is durable social and domestic harmony; this aspect quietly underwrites long partnerships, because the daily texture of being together stays pleasant even when other layers strain. The friction is the trine's usual whisper: harmony this natural invites coasting. Courtship can retire early, and the pair's talent for pleasantness can be spent avoiding rather than enjoying.

Keep spending on each other what you no longer need to — the compliment, the occasion, the effort — and let the ease be a floor you build on, not a hammock.
