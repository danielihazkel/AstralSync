title: Venus sextile Saturn
---

Affection between you is practical, patient, and quietly durable. One of you brings sweetness and taste; the Saturn side of this pairing brings follow-through — the anniversary remembered, the leaky faucet fixed, the plan for next year already sketched. You court each other by being dependable, and it works.

The gift is love that compounds: small, consistent deposits of care that add up to real security, with little of Saturn's usual heaviness. The friction is modesty. This pairing can become so sensible about romance that it stops being romantic — comfortable evenings replacing courtship, gifts becoming logistics, desire filed under someday.

Spend a little foolishly on each other now and then — time, money, or effort with no practical excuse. Impracticality, used sparingly, is how this steady bond stays a love affair.
