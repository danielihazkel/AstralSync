title: Mars opposition Jupiter
---

Each of you sees in the other the energy you are missing. The Mars person brings directness, urgency, and appetite for action; the Jupiter person brings vision, principle, and confidence about where things should be heading. The pull is strong, and together you can cover both the why and the how.

The friction comes from opposing scales. Jupiter can feel that Mars acts without meaning; Mars can feel that Jupiter talks, moralises, or promises without doing. Competitions over who is right can escalate, and both of you are prone to overstatement once heated. The gift is balance once you stop grading each other: action gains purpose, and belief gains traction.

Swap roles on purpose. Let the Mars person explain why a goal matters to them, and let the Jupiter person take the first concrete step — then compare notes on what it felt like.
