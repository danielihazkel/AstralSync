title: Mars square Saturn
---

This is the accelerator and the brake pressed at the same time. One of you moves on instinct and wants action now; the Saturn side of this pairing tests, delays, and demands a plan — usually at the exact moment the other has finally built up steam. The timing friction is real: efforts stall, initiatives get vetoed, and anger can turn cold and stubborn on both sides.

The honest gift inside the grind is tempering. The impatient one learns endurance and precision; the cautious one learns that some doors only open to a shove. Pairs who survive this square often become quietly unstoppable, because their drive has been stress-tested.

Stop fighting over the wheel in real time. Decide in advance who leads which kinds of decisions, and give every veto an expiry date and a stated reason.
