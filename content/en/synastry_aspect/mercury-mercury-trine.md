title: Mercury trine Mercury
---

Talking to each other feels like thinking with a better brain. Your styles mesh without effort — same humor, compatible pacing, an easy rhythm of listening and replying — and hours can disappear into conversation that leaves you both energized rather than drained.

The gift is a friendship of minds that steadies everything else between you. The friction is the echo. Minds this compatible confirm each other quickly, and shared assumptions harden into "obvious" truths no one has actually tested; like any same-planet contact, your sameness is also a shared blind spot with no in-house dissent.

Appoint a rotating skeptic: on decisions that matter, one of you deliberately argues the other side before you settle, even when — especially when — you already agree.
