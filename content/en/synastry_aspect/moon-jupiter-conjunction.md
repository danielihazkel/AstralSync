title: Moon conjunct Jupiter
---

Comfort comes in generous portions here. One of you feels deeply; the Jupiter person responds with warmth, humor, and a reassuring sense that things will work out. The Moon person's moods are met with acceptance rather than analysis, and the Jupiter person feels at home being the open hand.

The gift is emotional abundance: celebrations, shared meals, a welcoming household, and a feeling that there is always enough affection to go around. The friction is overindulgence and glossing. Comfort can become food, spending, or too many plans, and the Jupiter person's optimism may rush past a feeling that needed to be sat with. The Moon person may come to depend on cheerfulness that cannot always be supplied.

Let some moods simply be heard without being fixed or brightened, and keep the treats tied to real occasions rather than every hard day.
