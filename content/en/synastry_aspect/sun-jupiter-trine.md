title: Sun trine Jupiter
---

Goodwill runs easily between you. The Sun person feels respected for who they already are; the Jupiter person feels their faith in people confirmed. Ordinary days tend to go well together, and you bring out each other's humor, generosity, and appetite for learning something new.

The gift is a relationship that assumes the best. Mistakes are forgiven quickly, credit is shared, and both of you tend to grow more confident and more ethical in each other's company. The friction is complacency. When everything feels lucky, effort can slacken; you may coast on goodwill, overlook a real problem because it spoils the mood, or indulge each other's comforts a little too freely.

Use the ease to attempt something difficult together — a course, a trip, a commitment — so the good fortune has somewhere meaningful to go.
