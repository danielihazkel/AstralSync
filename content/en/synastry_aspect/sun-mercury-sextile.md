title: Sun sextile Mercury
---

Talk comes easily between you, and it tends to make both of you brighter. One of you brings presence and direction; the other brings words, questions, and angles. When you compare notes, the quieter ambitions of the Sun side get named, and the ideas of the Mercury side get taken seriously.

The gift is encouragement — you are good at making each other feel heard without much effort. The friction is that ease invites lightness: because conversation flows so pleasantly, you can circle the same enjoyable topics for years and never open the harder ones about needs, doubts, or direction.

Every so often, spend one of your easy conversations on a hard subject on purpose; the rapport you already have will carry it.
