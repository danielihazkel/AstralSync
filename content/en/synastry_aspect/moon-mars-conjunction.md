title: Moon conjunct Mars
---

Feelings run hot between you. One of you stirs the other's emotions just by walking into the room; vulnerability and desire sit unusually close together here, and neither of you can stay neutral about the other for long.

The gift is aliveness — fierce protectiveness, passionate reconciliation, and an instinct to act on each other's behalf the moment something feels wrong. The friction is a short fuse: moods ignite into action before words arrive, and one of you can feel provoked while the other feels smothered, sometimes within the same hour.

Build a shared pause. When heat rises, name the feeling before you act on it, and remember that the same intensity fueling your arguments also fuels your loyalty.
