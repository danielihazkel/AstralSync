title: Mercury sextile Jupiter
---

You are good at thinking together. The Mercury person notices the practical detail; the Jupiter person sees what it could lead to, and the exchange tends to land somewhere useful. Negotiations, joint projects, and shared study go smoothly, and you are often each other's first call with a new idea.

The gift is constructive optimism: neither of you shoots down the other's thinking, and both come away from a talk with more options than you started with. The friction is mild but real — a tendency to keep generating possibilities rather than choosing one, and an occasional drift into opinion where the other wanted information.

End your longer planning talks with a single decision and a single next step, and let whoever has more energy for it own the follow-up.
