title: Moon sextile Jupiter
---

You make each other feel lighter. The Moon person shares a worry; the Jupiter person offers a joke, a broader view, or a practical next step, and the worry shrinks to its actual size. Home life between you tends toward hospitality — friends drop by, plans form easily, and kindness is the default tone.

The gift is steady, friendly encouragement that helps both of you take small emotional risks. The friction is that the ease can stay shallow. Because cheering up comes so naturally, difficult feelings may get a quick lift instead of a real conversation, and the Jupiter person's well-meant advice can arrive before the Moon person has finished speaking.

Ask "do you want ideas or company?" before responding to a hard day, and honor the answer.
