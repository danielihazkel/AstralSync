title: Moon square Venus
---

Care and affection speak related but mistranslating dialects here. What the Venus person offers as love — treats, aesthetics, social warmth — can strike the Moon person as decoration when what they wanted was presence; what the Moon person offers as intimacy — moods shared, needs voiced — can feel to the Venus person like weather interrupting the picnic.

The gift is a real education in the difference between pleasing someone and nourishing them; pairs who do the translation work develop an unusually complete repertoire of care. The cost is a chronic slight mismatch — gifts that miss, comfort that clutters — which each side can tally privately as evidence of not being loved, when the love was arriving mislabeled all along.

Ask, don't infer: "what would feel like love right now?" is the whole technology this square requires, applied in both directions.
