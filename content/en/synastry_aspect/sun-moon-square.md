title: Sun square Moon
---

Between you, one person's natural way of shining tends to rub against the other's need for comfort. What one of you does to feel alive — taking the stage, pushing forward — can leave the other feeling unconsidered, while what the other does to feel safe can read as dampening.

The gift is growth: this friction keeps you honest about the difference between what you want and what you need, and pairs who work it become remarkably self-aware. The cost is a recurring grind of timing — enthusiasm arriving when reassurance is wanted, moods surfacing just as plans take flight.

Name the pattern out loud when it starts. Agree that neither the bright agenda nor the quiet need is wrong, and take turns letting each set the tempo.
