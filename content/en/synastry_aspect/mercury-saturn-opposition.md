title: Mercury opposition Saturn
---

You face each other as speaker and editor. One of you generates ideas, questions, and chatter; the other stands opposite with standards, doubts, and a red pen. Each secretly wants what the other has — the freedom to just say things, the authority to be taken seriously — and each can resent the other for having it.

The gift is a powerful working balance: with the light mind and the heavy one pulling honestly, your shared decisions are both imaginative and sound. The friction is the gatekeeper dynamic — the Saturn side dismissing before understanding, the Mercury side performing or withholding to dodge the judgment, until conversation itself becomes an audition.

Retire the roles on schedule: some days the careful one brainstorms recklessly, the quick one plays editor, and both of you notice how the other half lives.
