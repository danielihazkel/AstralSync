title: Sun sextile Mars
---

Energy backs identity here. The Mars person's drive naturally serves the Sun person's direction — projects, plans, and shared undertakings get an executor and a purpose in the same room — and neither feels diminished by the arrangement. There is a teammate quality to the bond: brisk, capable, mutually energizing.

The gift is productive companionship; this pairing gets things done together and enjoys the doing. The friction is mild and mostly about idleness — with no natural spark of opposition, competitive fire stays banked, and the pair can under-challenge each other, defaulting to comfortable projects when they are jointly capable of ambitious ones.

Pick a goal slightly too big for either of you alone and formally team up on it; this aspect pays best when it is actually put to work.
