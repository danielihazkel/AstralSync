title: Venus opposition Jupiter
---

You attract each other across a gap in values. The Venus person is drawn to the Jupiter person's expansiveness, humor, and big-hearted confidence; the Jupiter person is charmed by the Venus person's grace and sense of what makes life pleasant. Each of you seems to hold something the other lacks.

The gift is real enlargement: the Venus side learns to want more for themselves, and the Jupiter side learns that beauty and comfort matter as much as vision. The friction is a tug over scale and meaning. Jupiter can treat Venus's preferences as small or superficial, and can preach or promise more than they deliver; Venus can feel the generosity is performance rather than attention. Money and indulgence become the battleground.

Translate between your scales. When one of you wants more and the other wants better, ask what each choice is really for, and settle on the version that serves both purposes.
