title: Moon square Saturn
---

This aspect asks you to be honest about coldness. One of you reaches for reassurance at the exact moments the Saturn side of this pairing goes practical, composed, or quiet — and that timing mismatch can read as rejection. Feelings get met with fixes, needs get met with schedules, and hurt hardens into distance on both sides.

Yet couples with this square often stay, because underneath the friction is unusual seriousness about each other. The Saturn side is teaching that care can be structural; the lunar side is teaching that structure without warmth is just weight. Each of you holds the lesson the other needs.

Make comfort a named responsibility, not a mood. When one of you says "I don't need a solution, I need company," let that sentence outrank every other plan.
