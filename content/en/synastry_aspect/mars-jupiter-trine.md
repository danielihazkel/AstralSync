title: Mars trine Jupiter
---

You are natural teammates. The Mars person's drive meets the Jupiter person's faith and generosity in a way that feels easy, so effort rarely turns into strain. You enjoy doing things together — moving, traveling, competing, launching — and each of you tends to bring out the other's courage and good humor.

The gift is a relationship that recovers quickly: arguments burn off fast, losses get laughed at, and there is a shared sense that life is better tackled than avoided. The friction is lack of brakes. Because nothing about your combined energy warns you off, you can take on too much at once, assume luck will cover the gaps, and skip the boring follow-through that makes success stick.

Pair every big yes with a finish line. Before taking on something new together, agree on what has to be completed first, and celebrate endings as loudly as starts.
