title: Sun trine Moon
---

Support flows between you without much translation. One of you sets a direction and the other instinctively backs it; one of you shows a feeling and the other makes room for it. Days together tend to feel smooth, unforced, and quietly restorative.

The gift is a steady base — you refuel each other, and outsiders often notice how relaxed you are as a pair. The friction is subtler: ease can become inertia. Because nothing demands repair, you may stop saying the important things, letting the relationship coast on comfort rather than deepen.

Use the safety you have. Schedule real conversations about wants and worries even when nothing is wrong, so the harmony stays chosen rather than assumed.
