title: Sun opposition Venus
---

Admiration arrives across a distance here. The Venus person sees in the Sun person a radiance they want close; the Sun person enjoys being the object of that regard — and the whole exchange has a faint stage-and-audience geometry, each of you slightly performing the role the other's attention assigns.

The gift is real magnetism and a courtship energy that can persist for years; this pairing rarely goes flat. The cost is the gap between the admired image and the actual person: the Venus side can love a portrait and resent the sitter's departures from it, while the Sun side can grow dependent on applause and hide whatever might dim it.

Retire the portrait deliberately: show each other something unflattering and survive it together. Affection that has met the real face is the only kind that keeps.
