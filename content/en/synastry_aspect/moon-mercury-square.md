title: Moon square Mercury
---

Head and heart run on different clocks between you. One of you reaches for logic, questions, and quick summaries; the other is still inside the feeling and hears all that as being talked over. The Moon side can seem moody or vague to the Mercury side; the Mercury side can seem glib or cold to the Moon side — even when both mean well.

The gift hiding in the friction is range: together you cover both the felt truth and the spoken one, and when you sync up, your conversations reach places neither of you goes alone. The recurring cost is timing — right words, wrong moment.

Agree on a simple rule: feelings get acknowledged out loud first, and analysis waits until the person feeling them says they are ready.
