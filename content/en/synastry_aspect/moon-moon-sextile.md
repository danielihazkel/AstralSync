title: Moon sextile Moon
---

Your emotional weathers are compatible climates. What each of you needs when tired, worried, or tender is different in flavor but similar in grammar, so comfort offered usually fits and comfort requested is usually understood. Domestic rhythms — food, rest, quiet, company — negotiate themselves with little friction.

The gift is easy emotional cohabitation: this aspect makes sharing a home, a family, or a hard season noticeably smoother. The limitation is depth by default: because your moods rarely collide, they also rarely force disclosure, and two people can feel pleasantly attuned while whole chambers of feeling stay privately owned.

Volunteer the deeper layer occasionally — the fear under the mood, the memory under the preference. The attunement between you can hold more than it is usually given.
