title: Moon square Moon
---

Your default comforts contradict. When one of you is depleted, you reach for exactly what the other finds draining — company versus solitude, talking it out versus walking it off, warmth versus space — so the moments you most need care are the moments you are worst at providing it to each other.

The gift is emotional bilingualism, eventually: pairs who learn each other's care language here become genuinely skilled at love, because none of it runs on autopilot. The cost until then is a bruising pattern of mutual disappointment at low moments, each of you giving the comfort you would want and having it received as tone-deafness.

Write each other an actual instruction manual — "when I'm low, do this, not that" — and follow it literally, especially when it contradicts every instinct you have.
