title: Sun trine Sun
---

Your identities run on the same current. Values, humor, and instincts about how life should be approached line up so naturally that being together feels like operating at your own frequency with better company — there is little translation overhead, and each of you tends to validate the other's way of being simply by sharing it.

The gift is deep, restful affirmation: you rarely have to defend who you are here. The friction is subtle and long-range — so much alikeness can build an echo chamber. Weaknesses you share go unchallenged, blind spots overlap, and the pair can drift toward comfortable stagnation because nobody in the room disagrees.

Import difference on purpose: seek out people and experiences that argue with you both, and bring what you learn back into the ease.
