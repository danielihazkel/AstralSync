title: Mercury conjunct Mars
---

Talk between you has voltage. One of you supplies the ideas, the other supplies the fire under them, and conversations accelerate — fast replies, bold plans, debates that feel like sport. Nothing about your communication is sleepy; you wake each other's minds up.

The gift is momentum: decisions get made, projects get launched, and honesty arrives without delay. The friction is heat in the same wire. Words come out sharper than intended, disagreement escalates from zero to argument in seconds, and the Mars side's intensity can make the Mercury side feel interrupted or steamrolled mid-thought.

Adopt a pace rule for hot topics: one of you finishes a full thought before the other answers — the fire stays, but it stops burning the speaker.
