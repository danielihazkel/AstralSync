title: Mercury sextile Mercury
---

Your minds run on friendly, neighboring tracks — similar enough to click, different enough to stay interesting. One of you tends to open a topic and the other extends it, so conversations between you keep building instead of circling. Banter is easy; so is dividing up the practical thinking.

The gift is collaborative momentum: brainstorming, planning a trip, or untangling a problem genuinely goes better with both of you on it. The friction is comfort. Because talking is so pleasant, you can mistake good conversation for full agreement, nodding along in the flow and discovering later that you heard two different plans.

End your best talks with one concrete recap — who is doing what, by when — so the meeting of minds also lands in reality.
