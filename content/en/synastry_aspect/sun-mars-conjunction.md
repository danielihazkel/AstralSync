title: Sun conjunct Mars
---

Energy multiplies when you are together. One of you acts as a spark to the other's sense of purpose; plans get bigger, workouts get harder, and dull afternoons turn into projects. You activate each other in a way few people can.

The gift is momentum — as a team you start things, finish things, and defend each other fiercely. The friction is combustion: the same heat that drives you can flip into rivalry, impatience, or arguments where winning quietly replaces understanding. Neither of you likes to yield, and both of you feel it.

Give the competitive current a track — games, goals, shared challenges — and in disputes, slow your first response. Ask what you are fighting for before you fight.
