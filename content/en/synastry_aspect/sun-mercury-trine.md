title: Sun trine Mercury
---

You understand each other with very little translation. One of you speaks and the other's purpose gets clearer; one of you acts and the other can put it into words. Being seen and being heard, the two hungers that strain many pairs, are quietly fed here every day.

The gift is fluency — plans, jokes, and course corrections move between you without friction, and each of you feels smarter in the other's company. The subtle cost is assumption. Because understanding comes so cheap, you may stop checking whether it is still accurate, and drift apart while still finishing each other's sentences.

Ask each other real questions you think you already know the answers to; let the answers surprise you now and then.
