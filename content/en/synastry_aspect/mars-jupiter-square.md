title: Mars square Jupiter
---

Ambition runs hot and slightly crosswise here. The Mars person wants to act now and on their own terms; the Jupiter person has opinions about the larger purpose, the right principles, the better way — and is not shy about sharing them. Each of you pushes the other further than is comfortable, sometimes into real achievement, sometimes into a contest of who is more right.

The friction is excess on both sides: Mars's impatience meets Jupiter's overconfidence, and risks, spending, or schedules can balloon past sense. Disagreements easily turn moral, with one lecturing and the other rebelling. The gift is genuine stretch. Neither of you lets the other play small, and when your aims align, the combined drive is formidable.

Separate the action from the sermon. Agree on the goal first, then let the Mars person own the method, and keep advice to the moments it is asked for.
