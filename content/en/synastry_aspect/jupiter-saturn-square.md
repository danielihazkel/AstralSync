title: Jupiter square Saturn
---

Expansion and restraint keep colliding. The Jupiter person wants to say yes — to plans, spending, risk, generosity — and the Saturn person instinctively asks what it will cost and whether it is wise. The timing grates: hopes get checked just as they form, and caution gets overridden just when it was needed.

The friction is a clash of values rather than a lack of affection. Jupiter can experience Saturn as pessimistic or controlling, while Saturn sees Jupiter as careless or preachy about faith in things working out. Both can dig in until every decision becomes a referendum on whose worldview is right. The gift is hard-won judgment: plans that survive this square are both inspired and sound.

Build a shared decision rule instead of re-arguing each case. Agree on a threshold — of money, time, or risk — above which both of you must say yes, and below which either can act freely.
