title: Mercury square Saturn
---

Between you, one person's speech keeps meeting the other's scrutiny. The Saturn side hears sloppiness in the other's quick thinking and reaches for corrections; the Mercury side hears disapproval in the other's caution and starts talking less, or talking defensively. Over time, silence can start to feel safer than conversation — which is exactly the wrong lesson.

The hidden gift is rigor: ideas that survive this pairing are genuinely sound, and the Mercury side often becomes a clearer, more careful thinker while the Saturn side learns that timing and warmth are part of being right.

Change the ratio deliberately: for every correction, offer a real acknowledgment first, and let the quicker one of you finish speaking before the verdict — better yet, replace the verdict with a question.
