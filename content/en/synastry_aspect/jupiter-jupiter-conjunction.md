title: Jupiter conjunct Jupiter
---

You share a sense of what makes life worth growing into. Jupiter returns roughly every twelve years, so this contact usually links people close in age or about twelve or twenty-four years apart, and it gives you matching instincts about faith, learning, generosity, and what counts as a good opportunity. You often laugh at the same things and get excited about the same horizons.

The gift is mutual encouragement without translation: your hopes point the same way, so supporting each other feels natural, and your expansive seasons tend to arrive together. The friction is shared blind spots. Whatever you both overestimate — a belief, a risk, a habit of overcommitting — goes unchecked, because neither of you sees it as excess.

Invite a skeptic into your big decisions. Before you commit to a shared venture or conviction, ask someone who thinks differently to poke holes in it, and listen.
