title: Venus square Mars
---

The attraction between you is real but rarely synchronized. One of you tends to advance just when the other wants to be courted; the pace, style, or timing of desire on one side keeps missing the other's way of warming up.

The gift hides inside the frustration: this mismatch generates friction, and friction keeps a spark alive long after smoother matches have gone flat. Many pairs with this contact stay fascinated with each other for years. The cost is hot-and-cold cycles — pursuit, retreat, sudden flare-ups — that can feel like rejection if you take the tempo personally.

Treat timing as a shared puzzle rather than a verdict. Say plainly when you want closeness and when you need space, and let anticipation do some of the work.
