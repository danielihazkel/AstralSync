title: Sun square Mercury
---

Between you, identity and voice sit at cross purposes. The way one of you naturally talks — the pace, the questions, the corrections — can grate on the other's sense of self, while the Sun side's confidence can roll straight over the Mercury side's half-formed ideas. Each of you sometimes leaves a conversation feeling unheard for opposite reasons.

The friction is real, but so is the gift: you cannot coast on assumptions, so when you do slow down and explain yourselves, you understand each other more precisely than easier pairs ever bother to. Misfires force translation, and translation builds depth.

When a talk starts to spark, pause and repeat back what you heard before answering; being accurately restated is what both of you are actually asking for.
