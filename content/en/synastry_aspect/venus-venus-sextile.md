title: Venus sextile Venus
---

Your tastes rhyme without repeating. Each of you enjoys things the other hadn't considered but takes to instantly, so the shared aesthetic life keeps gently expanding — new food, new places, new people, each introduction received with pleasure rather than tolerance. Socially you make each other look good, and know it.

The gift is companionship that stays fresh cheaply: novelty flows in from both sides and affection is fluent in both dialects. The limitation is the sextile's characteristic modesty — none of this deepens by itself. The friendship layer can be so pleasant that the pair never tests whether it can hold weight, and enjoyment stands in for investment.

Put the compatibility under load once in a while: plan something together that can actually fail — a trip, a project, a hosting — and let the working-through bond you past the pleasant.
