title: Sun square Sun
---

You are built on crossing axes: the way one of you naturally shines is, almost point for point, the way the other doesn't. Decisions expose it fastest — pace, priorities, and what counts as a good outcome differ at the root, so even small joint choices can feel like negotiations between two administrations.

The gift is genuine expansion. Each of you embodies an approach to life the other never develops alone, and pairs who respect the difference become far more versatile than either would be solo. The cost is chronic friction over leadership: both identities want the plan to run on their operating system, and neither yields gracefully.

Divide sovereignty explicitly — domains where each of you simply decides — and treat the other's alien logic as a consultant's view, not an obstacle.
