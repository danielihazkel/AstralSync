title: Sun conjunct Mercury
---

One of you thinks and speaks in the very key the other lives in. The Mercury side seems to find words for who the Sun side is, and the Sun side feels genuinely seen when those words land. Conversation between you is constant, personal, and rarely small.

The gift is recognition: you narrate each other well, finish thoughts, and plan easily from shared assumptions. The friction is proximity. With mind and identity fused this closely, commentary can sound like verdict — when one of you critiques a plan, the other may hear a critique of themselves and shut down or overrule.

Keep the closeness, but separate the idea from the person: say "this plan," not "you," so either of you can disagree without it feeling like disloyalty.
