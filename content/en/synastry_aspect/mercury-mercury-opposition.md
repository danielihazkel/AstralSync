title: Mercury opposition Mercury
---

Your minds face each other from opposite ends of the spectrum: big picture versus fine print, intuition versus evidence, wandering versus linear. Each of you finds the other's way of thinking genuinely fascinating — and, on tired days, genuinely maddening.

The gift is that together you hold a complete picture: whatever one of you misses, the other is already staring at, which makes you a formidable team when you actually pool your views. The friction is debate for its own sake — the pull to correct, counter, and win can turn every discussion into a match, until one of you stops sharing ideas just to avoid the rebuttal.

Before answering each other, steelman first: restate the other's point so well they say "exactly" — then, and only then, add your side.
