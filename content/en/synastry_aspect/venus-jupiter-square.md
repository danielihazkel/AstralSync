title: Venus square Jupiter
---

Generosity and taste rub against each other here. The Venus person has a clear sense of what feels lovely and what is enough; the Jupiter person wants more — more plans, more people, more spending, more of a good thing — and can read restraint as stinginess. Each of you means well, yet your values about pleasure, money, and how much is appropriate keep landing at an angle.

The friction shows up as overspending, overcommitting, or one person feeling the other's affection is either too much or not quite sincere. Jupiter's praise can sound inflated, and Venus's preferences can feel like a ruling on the other's style. The gift is real warmth beneath the misfit: you genuinely want each other to enjoy life, and that shared intent is something to work with.

Name your numbers before you indulge. Agree in advance on a limit for gifts, outings, and treats, and say what "enough" looks like to each of you.
