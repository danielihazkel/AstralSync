title: Venus trine Jupiter
---

This is one of the most good-natured contacts two charts can share. The Venus person feels appreciated without having to ask, and the Jupiter person finds that their generosity is received gracefully rather than doubted. Laughter, hospitality, and mutual admiration come naturally, and other people often enjoy being around you as a pair.

The gift is a relationship that builds confidence: you see each other's best qualities and say so, which makes both of you braver and kinder outside it. The friction is softness. Because so much feels easy and pleasant, you may avoid saying anything unwelcome, letting small disappointments go unspoken and comfortable habits — rich food, late nights, spending — slide without either of you calling time.

Use the goodwill to carry honesty. Once a month, each of you names one thing you would like to change, knowing the warmth between you is strong enough to hold it.
