title: Mercury square Mars
---

Between you, discussion keeps turning into combat. The Mars side hears challenge in the other's questions; the Mercury side hears aggression in the other's certainty. Interruptions multiply, voices rise, and you can find yourselves fighting about tone long after forgetting the topic.

There is a real gift under the sparks: neither of you can hide behind vagueness, so the truth gets said here faster than in gentler pairings, and problems that would fester elsewhere get dragged into the open. Handled well, this contact makes you both braver and clearer.

Fight the issue, not each other: when a talk heats up, drop the score-keeping, take a break by agreement rather than by storm-out, and come back to finish the actual question.
