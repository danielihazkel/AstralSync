title: Mars conjunct Saturn
---

Drive and discipline occupy the same room in this pairing. One of you supplies heat — initiative, desire, the urge to act now; the Saturn side of this pairing supplies the engineering — patience, method, and a keen sense of when the moment is actually ripe. Fused well, you are formidable: projects finish, plans survive contact with reality.

The gift is controlled power, the rare ability to turn shared ambition into shared results. The friction is suppression. The braking influence can land on the accelerating one as constant correction — slow down, not yet, not like that — until spontaneity curdles into simmering frustration or effort simply stalls.

Give the fire scheduled right-of-way. Agree on domains where the bolder one of you leads unedited, and let the careful one govern the long game.
