title: Sun opposition Mercury
---

You face each other like a speaker and an author: one of you embodies a way of being, and the other cannot help analyzing, narrating, and questioning it. The pull is strong — the Sun side loves having such an attentive witness, and the Mercury side loves having such vivid material.

The gift is perspective. Each of you shows the other exactly what they cannot see from inside themselves. The friction is the standoff that follows: commentary can start to feel like heckling, and self-assurance can start to feel like refusal to listen, until one of you performs and the other critiques from across the room.

Trade seats regularly — let the analyst be the one who is seen, and the shining one be the one who asks the questions.
