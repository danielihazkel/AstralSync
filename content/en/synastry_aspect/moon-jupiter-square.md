title: Moon square Jupiter
---

Feelings and beliefs keep bumping into each other here. The Moon person needs care in a particular, familiar form; the Jupiter person offers care in a big, idealistic form — a pep talk, a philosophy, a grand gesture — that does not quite fit the need. Both are trying to give, and both can feel missed.

The strength is warmth with growing room. This pairing can widen the Moon person's sense of what is possible and teach the Jupiter person the value of small, specific comforts. The friction is excess and moralizing: overspending, overeating, or overpromising to soothe tension, and moments where one person's emotional reaction is answered with a lesson about how to feel.

Swap one concrete request each week — "this is what helps me" — and practice meeting it exactly, without upgrading it.
