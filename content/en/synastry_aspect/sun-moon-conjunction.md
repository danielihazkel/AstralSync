title: Sun conjunct Moon
---

There is an unusual sense of recognition between you, the kind that makes early conversations feel like reunions. One of you expresses openly what the other feels privately, so each of you experiences the other as a missing half made visible.

The gift is complementarity: one energizes, the other nourishes, and the relationship runs on a shared current. The friction is fusion — you can blend so quickly that neither of you remembers where your moods end and the other's begin, and small emotional weather becomes a joint storm.

Enjoy the closeness, but keep separate friendships and routines alive. Once a week, ask each other what you need as individuals rather than assuming the answer is already known.
