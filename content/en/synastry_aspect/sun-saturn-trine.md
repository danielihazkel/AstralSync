title: Sun trine Saturn
---

You steady each other without trying. One of you brings light and purpose; the Saturn side of this pairing brings patience and structure, and the two fit together like a plan that was always going to work. People around you tend to read you as a serious, durable unit early on.

The gift is longevity through respect: you take each other seriously, keep your word, and build a reputation together. The friction is quiet — with so much mutual reliability, the relationship can turn institutional. Duty gets handled, admiration gets assumed, and the spark that started it all is filed under "settled."

Trade roles now and then. Let the responsible one play, and let the radiant one carry something heavy, so the structure stays alive rather than merely maintained.
