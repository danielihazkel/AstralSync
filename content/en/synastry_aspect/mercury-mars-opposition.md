title: Mercury opposition Mars
---

One of you wields words; the other wields will — and you face each other across that line. The Mercury side can dissect the Mars side's impulses with surgical accuracy, and the Mars side can detonate the Mercury side's careful arguments with one blunt push. Each of you is the other's most stimulating opponent.

The gift is a genuine sharpening: your ideas get stress-tested and your actions get thought through, because the other pole will not let anything pass unexamined or undone. The friction is provocation — you know exactly which sentence lights the other's fuse, and in low moments you use it, turning stimulation into a cycle of jab and eruption.

Notice the moment before the jab: name what you actually need underneath it, and offer that sentence instead.
