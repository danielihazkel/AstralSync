title: Mercury trine Mars
---

You debate the way other couples dance. One of you throws out an idea, the other pushes back with energy, and the exchange sharpens both positions without drawing blood — spirited, fast, and somehow friendly the whole way through. Together you talk each other into motion.

The gift is fearless conversation: hard subjects hold no dread here, because you both trust the exchange to stay fair, and shared projects benefit from a rare mix of quick thinking and quick doing. The friction is competitiveness on autopilot — the sparring habit can crowd out softer registers, so that tenderness, doubt, and worry never find airtime between all the clever thrust and parry.

Keep the matches, but schedule the truce: make room for conversations where nobody argues anything and someone simply gets listened to.
