title: Mercury conjunct Mercury
---

You think in the same dialect. Jokes land on the first try, plans form in shorthand, and each of you regularly says the exact sentence the other was composing. Conversation between you is less an exchange than a shared workspace.

The gift is obvious: almost zero friction in daily logistics, and a rare feeling of being instantly understood. But identical minds share identical blind spots. Whatever your common thinking style overlooks — feelings, risks, other people's perspectives — stays overlooked twice, with no internal counterweight, and your quick agreement can feel airtight while being wrong in stereo.

Borrow outside eyes on purpose: before a big decision, ask someone who thinks nothing like either of you, and take their strangest objection seriously.
