title: Sun trine Mars
---

Courage and confidence feed each other here. The Mars person's energy makes the Sun person bolder; the Sun person's steadiness gives the Mars person's drive somewhere worthy to go. Together you act sooner, dare more, and recover faster than either of you does alone — adventure is this pairing's native language.

The gift is momentum without friction: joint decisions convert to action quickly, and physical activities, travel, and shared enterprises thrive. The hazard is shared blind speed — with both throttles open and no internal brake, the pair can commit to things fast that deserved slower thought, and mistake mutual encouragement for due diligence.

Appoint the brake deliberately: before any big joint leap, one of you argues the case against, out loud, as a formality. Then leap — this aspect was built for it.
