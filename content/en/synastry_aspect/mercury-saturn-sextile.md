title: Mercury sextile Saturn
---

You make each other's words more dependable. One of you thinks quickly and aloud; the other listens carefully and asks the question that makes the plan real — what it costs, when it happens, who does it. Between you, conversations reliably turn into commitments, and commitments reliably hold.

The gift is trust built sentence by sentence: you can hand each other difficult topics and know they will be handled with care rather than drama. The friction is dryness. With so much airtime going to logistics and sensible next steps, the playful, pointless, wandering kind of talk — the kind that keeps a bond warm — can quietly fall off the agenda.

Protect one regular conversation with no agenda at all: no plans, no problems, just talking for the pleasure of it.
