title: Mercury conjunct Jupiter
---

Conversation between you expands. The Mercury person brings a sharp detail or a quick question; the Jupiter person turns it into a theory, a story, or a plan for next year. Talking together feels like learning, and many pairs with this contact share books, courses, travel ideas, or a running stream of jokes.

The gift is intellectual encouragement: each of you feels smarter and more articulate in the other's company, and ideas that might have been dropped get a second, larger life. The friction is overstatement and lost precision. Facts can be stretched to fit the bigger story, promises made in enthusiasm go unchecked, and the Jupiter person may lecture where the Mercury person wanted a dialogue.

Before you commit to anything discussed in an excited moment, let the Mercury person write down the specifics — dates, numbers, who does what.
