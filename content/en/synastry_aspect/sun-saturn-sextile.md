title: Sun sextile Saturn
---

Respect comes easily between you, and it shows up in practical ways. One of you radiates direction and confidence; the Saturn side of this pairing quietly supplies scaffolding — plans, follow-through, a sense that promises here actually get kept. You make each other more credible.

The gift is steady endorsement without heaviness: encouragement that sounds like "I believe you can, and here is how." The friction is mildness itself. Because the support is low-key, it can go unnoticed and unthanked, and the pair of you may settle into competent routine, admiring each other's reliability while forgetting to celebrate anything.

Put the partnership to work on a shared goal with a deadline. This aspect rewards building something together, and the build keeps the respect warm.
