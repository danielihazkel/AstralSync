title: Venus square Venus
---

You love in crossing styles. What reads as romance to one of you reads as excess or austerity to the other; ideas of a perfect evening, a fair split, a beautiful room, or a proper gift come from different aesthetic constitutions. Neither taste is wrong, and neither will convert.

The gift is range: pairs who stop litigating whose style is correct end up with a broader palette of pleasure than same-taste couples ever develop, fluent in two whole languages of enjoyment. The cost until then is chronic low-grade offense — generosity received as miscalculation, preferences heard as verdicts on each other's refinement — and a subtle competition over whose values set the household's tone.

Alternate curatorship rather than compromising everything to beige: whole evenings, rooms, and budgets assigned to one taste at a time, attended by the other in good faith.
