title: Moon trine Moon
---

Being together is restful. Your emotional needs run on compatible clocks — hunger for company and hunger for quiet tend to arrive in the same seasons — and moods pass between you without demanding explanation. Many pairs with this aspect describe a feeling of having known each other longer than the calendar says.

The gift is a home-like ease that makes this one of the best aspects for actually sharing a life; comfort here regenerates rather than depletes. The friction is near-zero, and that is the caveat: emotional agreement this deep can quietly seal the pair off — inside jokes, shared moods, a private climate outsiders find hard to enter — and can let shared melancholies deepen unchallenged, since neither of you breaks the weather.

Keep windows open: maintain friendships and inputs outside the bubble, and when you both sink at once, let one of you be assigned to fetch the ladder.
