title: Saturn square Saturn
---

Your rulebooks clash on principle. Raised under different Saturn seasons — often about seven years apart, or at offset life stages — you carry genuinely different convictions about obligation: what one of you calls discipline, the other calls rigidity; what one calls flexibility, the other calls neglect. Arguments here are rarely about the dish or the bill; they are about whose definition of responsible wins.

The heaviness is real: each of you can feel judged by the other's standards while defending your own, and Saturn seasons hit you on different clocks, so one is often carrying weight the other cannot yet feel. The gift is a stress-tested structure — rules that survive this pairing's scrutiny are actually good rules.

Write the shared rulebook explicitly rather than enforcing two silently. Where you cannot merge standards, assign the domain to one owner and let the other genuinely stand down.
