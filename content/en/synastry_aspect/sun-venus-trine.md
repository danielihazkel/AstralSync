title: Sun trine Venus
---

Warmth flows downhill between you. The Venus person delights in who the Sun person actually is, and that delight reaches its target — the Sun person feels valued at the identity level, not for services rendered. Shared pleasures, social ease, and an instinct for enjoying the same things make time together feel like a standing holiday.

The gift is a bond that photographs well and genuinely is what it looks like: mutual enjoyment with very little performance. The friction is what ease always risks — the relationship can live on charm and pleasant occasions while avoiding every topic with a sharp edge, because neither of you wants to spend the goodwill.

Spend some of it anyway: use the deep reservoir of liking to have the one conversation you have been saving for never. It will go better here than it would anywhere else.
