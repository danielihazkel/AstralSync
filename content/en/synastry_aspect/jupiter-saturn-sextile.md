title: Jupiter sextile Saturn
---

Optimism and realism cooperate well between you. The Jupiter person spots possibilities and believes in them; the Saturn person knows how to turn possibilities into schedules, budgets, and steady steps. Neither feels dismissed by the other, because each contribution plainly makes the other's more useful.

The gift is sustainable growth: you are good at long projects, savings goals, education, or anything that rewards both faith and patience. The Saturn person relaxes a little in Jupiter's company, and the Jupiter person learns that structure can be a form of support. The friction is a slow drift into fixed roles. Jupiter may stop taking responsibility because Saturn always will, and Saturn may stop hoping because Jupiter always does.

Swap jobs now and then. Let the Saturn person propose the next big idea and the Jupiter person draw up its plan, and notice what each of you learns.
