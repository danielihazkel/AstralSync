title: Jupiter trine Jupiter
---

You trust each other's sense of what is worth pursuing. This contact often links people about four or eight years apart in age, who arrived at different but harmonious philosophies of growth, faith, and generosity. Your enthusiasms fit together easily, and you tend to bring out each other's optimism and humor.

The gift is effortless goodwill on the level of meaning: you encourage each other's studies, journeys, and convictions without needing to share every one, and your expansive seasons tend to support rather than compete. The friction is that ease breeds assumption. You may take each other's goodwill for granted, overcommit together because everything feels promising, or skip the hard conversations about money and limits because your values seem aligned.

Use your shared optimism deliberately. Once a year, sit down and choose one big intention for the pair, then write down what you will give up to make room for it.
