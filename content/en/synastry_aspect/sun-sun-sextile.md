title: Sun sextile Sun
---

Your core temperaments are different enough to be interesting and compatible enough to be easy. What each of you is fundamentally about — the way you assert, choose, and take up space — makes intuitive sense to the other, and encouragement flows both directions without effort or envy.

The gift is mutual reinforcement: you tend to leave each other's company slightly more yourselves than you arrived. The limitation is that gentle compatibility never forces depth. This aspect happily supports a pleasant acquaintance for years, and in a close bond it can let you cheer each other on without ever really challenging each other's course.

Don't settle for being each other's audience. Trade honest opinions, not just applause — the harmony between you is sturdy enough to carry real critique.
