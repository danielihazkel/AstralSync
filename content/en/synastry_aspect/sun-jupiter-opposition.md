title: Sun opposition Jupiter
---

You face each other across a wide horizon. The Sun person has a clear sense of who they are; the Jupiter person sees what they could become, and says so. At its best this feels like being believed in by someone standing just far enough away to see the whole picture.

The strength is perspective: each of you widens the other's world, and the attraction often has a touch of the foreign or philosophical about it. The friction is overreach and competing truths. The Jupiter person may promise more than they deliver or offer guidance that sounds like a verdict; the Sun person may hear every big idea as a criticism of their small, real one. Both can inflate a disagreement into a matter of principle.

When views clash, name the specific decision at stake and settle that, leaving the grand worldview debate for a long walk.
