title: Saturn conjunct Saturn
---

You share a rulebook. Born into the same Saturn cohort — close in age, or nearly a full cycle apart — you carry matching assumptions about duty, work, money, and what a responsible life looks like. Between you, obligations rarely need negotiating: you both already know what "the right thing" is, because you learned it under the same sky.

The gift is frictionless partnership on the practical plane — commitments align, timelines match, and you face life's Saturn seasons at the same time, which breeds real solidarity. The friction is sameness as a blind spot. Whatever your generation's rulebook gets wrong, you both get wrong, and neither of you is positioned to catch it.

Borrow outside eyes deliberately. Invite perspectives from people whose obligations look nothing like yours, and audit your shared "obviously" list once a year.
