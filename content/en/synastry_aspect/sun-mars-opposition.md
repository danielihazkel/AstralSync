title: Sun opposition Mars
---

You face each other like sparring partners. The Mars person's assertion aims straight at the Sun person's identity — challenging, provoking, sometimes protecting — and the Sun person's presence draws exactly that intensity out of the Mars person. Attraction and antagonism run on the same wire here, which the early phase usually experiences as chemistry.

The gift is honesty under pressure: neither of you can posture for long, because the other instinctively tests whatever is being displayed. Pairs who harness this become each other's best training — braver, sharper, unillusioned. The cost is escalation: unchecked, the dynamic polarizes into aggressor and defender, roles that harden with each round.

Agree on the tap-out signal before you need it, and when a fight ends, name one thing the other was right about; opposition only builds strength when both people leave the mat respected.
