title: Moon conjunct Mercury
---

Feelings find words quickly between you. What one of you senses, the other can say — moods get named almost as they arrive, and late-night talks slide easily between the practical and the personal. Confiding feels natural here, often from the very beginning.

The gift is emotional fluency: little stays buried, and misunderstandings get talked out before they harden. The friction is that words can crowd the feeling itself. The Mercury side may rush to explain, label, or solve an emotion the Moon side is still having, and the Moon side may hear analysis where they wanted company — accurate commentary, delivered a beat too soon.

When one of you is stirred up, ask "listening or solving?" before responding, and honor the answer.
