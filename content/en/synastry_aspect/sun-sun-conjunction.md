title: Sun conjunct Sun
---

Your core identities run on the same frequency. You recognize each other's motives without explanation, laugh at the same absurdities, and often want the same things at the same time. Being together can feel like finally being understood from the inside.

The gift is effortless alliance — you validate each other's way of being and rarely have to justify who you are. The friction is doubled blind spots: whatever your shared sign avoids, you both avoid, and there is no built-in counterweight. You can also compete for the same spotlight, since two suns want one sky.

Invite difference on purpose — friends, advisors, and habits that think unlike you both — and take turns being the one who shines while the other applauds.
