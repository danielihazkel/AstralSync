title: Moon conjunct Moon
---

You share the same emotional weather. When one of you needs quiet, the other usually does too; comfort foods, moods, and instincts about home line up with uncanny ease, and empathy between you is nearly automatic.

The gift is being understood without translation — few bonds offer this depth of felt safety, and hard days are easier because neither of you has to explain why. The friction is amplification: you sink into the same lows together, reinforce the same coping habits, and can circle a shared rut with no one positioned to pull the other out.

When a heavy mood settles over both of you, let one deliberately play the steady one — a walk, a call, a change of scene — instead of sinking in tandem.
