title: Sun sextile Venus
---

Liking each other is easy here. The Venus person finds the Sun person's essential character attractive — not their performance, their default — and the Sun person blossoms a little under that appreciation. Aesthetics, social style, and ideas of a good time overlap enough that shared plans mostly make themselves.

The gift is a durable fondness that lubricates everything else: criticism lands softer, apologies come easier, and the pair is pleasant company to itself. The friction is faint but worth naming — affection this comfortable can idle at "nice." Neither of you is forced to invest, so the warmth may never be told, shown, or built into anything.

Say the appreciation out loud and put dates on it: the bond responds to explicit affection the way a sextile always does — generously, but only when asked.
