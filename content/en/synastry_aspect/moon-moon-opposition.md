title: Moon opposition Moon
---

Your inner tides run counter-phase. When one of you rises — sociable, expressive, hungry for contact — the other is often ebbing toward quiet, and the mismatch feels personal even though it is structural. Each of you can read the other's different rhythm as rejection or demand.

The gift is coverage: between you, someone is nearly always steady, which makes this a surprisingly resilient pairing in crisis — one keeps the household breathing while the other falls apart, and you trade places without contract. Pairs who see the pattern come to treat it as shiftwork. The cost is loneliness inside proximity when the pattern goes unnamed, both of you privately concluding the other is never there in the right way.

Name the phases like weather — "I'm ebbing, it's not about you" — and plan your closeness for the overlaps instead of grieving the gaps.
