title: Sun square Mars
---

Will meets will at a crossing angle. The Mars person's way of pushing — pace, bluntness, appetite for friction — lands sideways on the Sun person's sense of how things should be done, and the Sun person's self-assured course reads to the Mars person as something to test. Sparks here are genuinely sparks: exciting at first, combustible on repeat.

The gift is vitality — this pair does not bore each other, and each toughens the other in useful ways: the Sun person learns to hold ground under pressure, the Mars person learns that force is not the only instrument. The cost is a rivalry reflex that can turn collaboration into scorekeeping and small disagreements into contests neither meant to enter.

Give the combativeness a sanctioned arena — sport, debate, a shared hard project — and keep explicit rules of engagement everywhere else: no interrupting, no third parties, same day repairs.
