title: Moon square Mars
---

Tenderness and force collide at close range. The Mars person's directness — volume, pace, bluntness — lands on the Moon person's most sensitive instruments, and the Moon person's emotional responses can strike the Mars person as brakes applied mid-stride. Both of you are reacting to something real: this aspect genuinely runs hot.

The gift is passion and candor — nothing between you stays repressed for long, and the Moon person often grows a spine here while the Mars person grows a bedside manner, an education neither would get in gentler company. The cost is a fast escalation cycle: impatience wounds, hurt retaliates as mood, mood reads as manipulation, and suddenly the fight is about the fighting.

Slow the loop at its first beat: the Mars person softens the opening tone, the Moon person names the sting immediately instead of banking it. Fights caught in the first minute stay small here.
