title: Sun conjunct Venus
---

You genuinely like each other, and it shows. One of you finds the other's essential character charming; the other feels appreciated simply for being themselves. Together you become each other's favorite audience, quick to laugh and quick to compliment.

The gift is warmth that smooths daily life — this contact makes affection easy to express and easy to believe. The friction is a taste for pleasantness: because approval feels so good between you, both of you may soften complaints, dodge awkward truths, and let charm stand in for candor until small resentments accumulate.

Protect the honesty. Make it a habit to say one true, slightly uncomfortable thing when it matters, trusting that the fondness between you can absorb it.
