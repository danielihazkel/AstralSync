title: Mercury conjunct Venus
---

Conversation between you has charm built in. One of you brings the words, the other brings the warmth, and together your talk turns graceful — playful teasing, shared taste in books and music, compliments that actually land. People notice that you sound good together.

The gift is affection expressed fluently: this pairing rarely runs out of sweet things to say, and it makes ordinary errands feel like dates. The friction is a preference for pleasantness. Hard truths get softened, and softened again, until they vanish — one of you polishing the phrasing, the other steering toward harmony, both quietly editing out anything that might spoil the mood.

Keep the charm, but ban the third rewrite: when something bothers one of you, say the plain version within a day.
