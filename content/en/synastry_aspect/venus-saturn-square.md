title: Venus square Saturn
---

This square puts a toll gate on affection. One of you expresses love openly and wants it mirrored; the Saturn side of this pairing shows love through duty, restraint, and standards — and may flinch at displays that feel excessive or unearned. Warmth starts to feel conditional: given for good behavior, docked for disappointment.

The honest news is that this dynamic can grind. The affectionate one may feel perpetually auditioned; the reserved one may feel pressured to perform feelings on demand. Yet the same gravity that makes this hard makes it binding — couples with this aspect rarely drift apart casually, and love that survives the audit tends to be unusually loyal and real.

Separate feedback from affection completely. Criticism can wait for a conversation; warmth should never be the thing withheld to make a point.
