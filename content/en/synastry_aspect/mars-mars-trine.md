title: Mars trine Mars
---

Your energies run parallel at compatible speeds. Effort synchronizes without negotiation — you start when the other starts, rest when the other rests, and push at intensities each finds natural rather than imposed. Physical activities, shared work, and even household logistics flow with a veteran team's economy of motion.

The gift is sustainable momentum: this pair can maintain a demanding shared pace for years without the friction burns that wear other couples down, and anger between you tends to discharge cleanly rather than fester. The quiet risk is unexamined direction — moving well together is so satisfying that neither of you may pause to ask whether the road itself is the right one.

Enjoy the stride, but hold a periodic strategy stop — quarterly is enough — where movement pauses and direction gets questioned before the engines rev again.
