title: Sun square Saturn
---

One of you shines; the other checks the shine against a standard. In this pairing, self-expression keeps colliding with rules about how things should be done, and the collision can feel personal. The solar side may feel graded rather than loved; the Saturn side may feel cast as the joyless critic when they meant to help.

There is real material here. Saturn's scrutiny, taken well, forges a sturdier confidence than easy applause ever could, and the solar warmth can soften a rigidity the other has carried for years. But left unmanaged, this becomes a criticism loop: correction, deflation, withdrawal, colder correction.

Agree on when feedback is welcome and when cheering is the only job. Timing the critique — not abolishing it — is what makes this square constructive.
