title: Mars sextile Mars
---

Your energies interlock like gears of compatible size. Working styles differ — one pushes where the other paces, one sprints where the other grinds — but the difference meshes instead of grinding: joint efforts naturally divide along each person's strengths, and you cover each other's dead angles without discussing it.

The gift is effortless teamwork with none of the rivalry that often shadows two capable people; each of you would rather see the other win than keep score. The limitation is that the partnership idles politely when nothing demands it — with no friction to strike sparks, two able people can spend years being vaguely supportive without ever building anything together.

Give the collaboration a standing project — the business, the training partnership, the annual undertaking — because this aspect's value only shows up in motion.
