title: Mercury trine Saturn
---

Your words rest on bedrock. One of you speaks and the other genuinely considers it; one of you commits and the other knows it will stand. Conversations here have an unhurried, grown-up quality — advice is offered without condescension, and hard subjects get discussed calmly, in order, until they are actually settled.

The gift is a rare kind of mental safety: each of you thinks better for having a listener this steady, and outsiders often bring you their problems because your joint judgment is sound. The friction is gravity itself — reliable can slide into routine, and your talk can settle so deeply into the sensible that curiosity, nonsense, and surprise stop visiting.

Keep the foundation, but build something impractical on it now and then: learn a useless skill together, or argue a ridiculous question just for fun.
