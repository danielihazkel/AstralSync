title: Mercury square Venus
---

Words and taste rub each other the wrong way between you. A joke one of you finds sharp and funny can land on the other as unkind; feedback meant as helpful can bruise the other's sense of style; and the Venus side's diplomatic wrapping can strike the Mercury side as evasive. You like each other — you just keep phrasing it badly.

The gift is expansion: the Mercury side learns that delivery is part of the message, and the Venus side learns that honesty can be a form of care. Pairs with this contact often refine each other's manners and each other's candor at once.

When a remark stings, name it in the moment — "that landed harder than you meant it" — and let the retry stand as the real message.
