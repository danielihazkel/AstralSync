title: Venus trine Saturn
---

You love each other like people who intend to stay. One of you brings grace and affection; the Saturn side of this pairing brings constancy, and the two blend into a devotion that feels both tender and load-bearing. Commitment does not frighten this pairing — it flatters it.

The gift is durable romance: loyalty without drama, promises that hold, a shared instinct for building rather than burning. Friends may treat your relationship as proof that steadiness and love can coexist. The friction is the settling instinct itself. Affection can become assumed, rituals can calcify, and you may catch yourselves maintaining the relationship the way one maintains a well-made house — carefully, and without wonder.

Renovate on purpose. Revisit an old date, break one comfortable routine a month, and tell each other what you would still choose today.
