title: Mars sextile Saturn
---

You work well in harness together. One of you generates momentum; the Saturn side of this pairing shapes it — pacing the effort, sequencing the steps, quietly removing obstacles before the other even hits them. Whether the project is a move, a business, or a household, things you attempt as a pair tend to get done.

The gift is productive trust: each of you knows the other will pull their weight, and disagreements about effort are rare. The friction is that the partnership can become all harness. Shared to-do lists crowd out shared adventures, and desire — which needs some wildness — gets managed with the same calm efficiency as everything else.

Protect time where nothing is being accomplished. A pair this capable must practice idleness together on purpose, or the teamwork will quietly replace the romance.
