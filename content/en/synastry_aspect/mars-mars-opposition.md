title: Mars opposition Mars
---

You are natural sparring partners: equal force, opposite corners. Each of you instinctively takes the counter-position to the other's move — push meets brace, advance meets flank — and the contest energizes as much as it exhausts. Respect is rarely the problem here; coexistence of two full-sized wills is.

The gift is honed strength: no one keeps you more honest about your methods than the person built to counter them, and pairs who channel this become each other's best training and fiercest protection — the combativeness pointed outward is devastatingly effective. The cost is that everything can become a contest, including things with no prize: routes, thermostats, whose tiredness is more legitimate.

Ration the contests: compete hard where competition is fun, declare the rest of life a demilitarized zone, and defend each other in public without exception.
