title: Moon opposition Mars
---

Sensitivity and assertion face off here, and both of you feel it as charge. The Mars person is magnetized by the Moon person's depth and can't resist stirring it; the Moon person is drawn to the Mars person's fire and braced against it in the same breath. The dynamic swings between passionate closeness and sudden defensiveness, often within a single evening.

The gift is aliveness — feelings never fossilize in this pairing, and each of you develops the capacity the other embodies: initiative on one side, emotional literacy on the other. The cost is a provoke-and-retreat rhythm that can harden into roles — one always the aggressor, one always the wounded — neither of which is the whole truth of either person.

Break the roles on purpose: the Moon person initiates something bold weekly, the Mars person tends something gently — and both name the pull toward the old script when you feel it.
