title: Moon opposition Mercury
---

You sit at opposite ends of the same conversation: one of you leads with what they feel, the other with what they think, and each secretly relies on the other to supply the missing half. The Mercury side gives shape to the Moon side's tides; the Moon side gives warmth to the Mercury side's words.

The gift is completion — together you can both feel a thing fully and say it clearly. The friction is polarization. Under stress, the talker gets chattier and the feeler goes quieter, until one of you is explaining at a closed door and the other feels interviewed instead of held.

When you notice the split widening, swap roles on purpose: the feeler says one clear sentence, and the thinker simply sits with it, unanswered, for a while.
