title: Sun square Venus
---

Attraction and identity snag on each other here. The Venus person is drawn to the Sun person but keeps wanting to adjust the presentation — the tone, the taste, the manner — while the Sun person enjoys being adored and chafes at being curated. Social choices expose it: what one finds delightful, the other finds a bit much, or not enough.

The gift is refinement through friction: each of you genuinely stretches the other's sense of beauty, pleasure, and style, and couples who metabolize the difference end up with richer shared taste than either brought. The cost is a low hum of criticism — one side feeling improved-upon, the other feeling unappreciated — that erodes warmth if it goes unnamed.

Trade the editing for curiosity: once a month, let the other person plan the evening entirely their way, and attend it as a tourist rather than a critic.
