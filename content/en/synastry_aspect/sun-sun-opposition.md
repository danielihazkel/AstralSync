title: Sun opposition Sun
---

You stand at opposite ends of the same axis — complementary by design, contrary by default. Each of you is strong exactly where the other is unfinished, which is why the connection often carries a magnetic, "my missing half" quality at the start and a tug-of-war quality later, when the same differences stop being charming and start being positions.

The gift is completion: together you cover a spectrum neither spans alone, and decisions made jointly are genuinely wiser than either would make solo. The cost is polarization under stress — each of you retreats to your own pole and recruits the other's traits as evidence, turning a partnership into a debate.

When you feel yourselves polarizing, swap briefs: argue the other's side sincerely before deciding anything. The axis you share matters more than the ends you hold.
