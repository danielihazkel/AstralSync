title: Moon sextile Mars
---

Feeling and action cooperate here. The Mars person's energy tends to protect and mobilize rather than trample — when the Moon person is low, the Mars person's instinct to do something usually helps — and the Moon person's emotional read gives the Mars person's drive better aim. There is a healthy circulation between comfort and courage.

The gift is a bond that handles practical adversity well: this pair moves house, weathers crises, and defends its own effectively, with warmth intact. The limitation is the sextile's usual one — the strengths stay latent unless engaged. The Mars person can under-notice quiet needs that never announce themselves, and the Moon person can under-use the safety to actually ask.

Practice the exchange deliberately: needs spoken plainly on one side, energy offered before it's requested on the other. This aspect improves rapidly with reps.
