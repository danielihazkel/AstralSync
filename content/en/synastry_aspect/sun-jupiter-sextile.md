title: Sun sextile Jupiter
---

You open doors for each other without making a production of it. The Sun person shows up with a goal; the Jupiter person knows someone, suggests a course, or simply says "of course you can" at the right moment. Between you, good ideas tend to find their first small opportunity rather than dying in conversation.

The gift is a friendly, low-pressure optimism that makes growth feel ordinary. Travel, study, and side projects come naturally to this pair. The friction is that a sextile waits to be used: the encouragement is real but mild, and it is easy to enjoy the good feeling of possibility without ever acting on it. The Jupiter person's advice can also drift toward a gentle lecture.

Turn one shared "we should" into a dated plan each season, and let the Sun person choose which one.
