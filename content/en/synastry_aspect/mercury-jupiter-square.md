title: Mercury square Jupiter
---

You think on different scales. The Mercury person wants accuracy, specifics, and a clear line of reasoning; the Jupiter person wants the big meaning and may treat details as beneath the point. Each can find the other's talk exasperating — too nitpicky on one side, too sweeping on the other.

The strength is that this friction sharpens both minds. Arguments here can be lively and genuinely educational, and you are hard to fool together once you combine detail with vision. The friction is exaggeration and preachiness: overpromising, overlooking small print, and debates that turn into sermons about who is right in principle rather than what is true in fact.

When a discussion heats up, pause and ask "what would change your mind?" — then check the facts together before returning to the bigger question.
