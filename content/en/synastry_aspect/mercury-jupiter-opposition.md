title: Mercury opposition Jupiter
---

You see the same subject from opposite ends. The Mercury person asks how it works; the Jupiter person asks what it means. Put together, these views can make a complete picture, and the attraction is often intellectual — debates, shared curiosity, and a sense of being stretched by someone who thinks differently.

The strength is breadth: each of you fills in what the other's mind tends to skip. The friction is polarization. The Jupiter person may sound grandiose or preachy, brushing aside details as trivia; the Mercury person may seem picky or skeptical, puncturing every larger hope with a fact. Plans made between you can also overshoot, with big promises agreed in conversation and never pinned down.

Give each conversation two halves: first the big picture, then the specifics, and do not end until both are on the table.
