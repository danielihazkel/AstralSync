title: Mercury trine Venus
---

Talking together is one of the genuine pleasures of this relationship. One of you gives ideas a lovely shape; the other gives beauty a vocabulary. Museums, menus, playlists, plans — everything becomes better conversation, and even disagreements tend to stay civil and oddly enjoyable.

The gift is a durable friendliness: this aspect keeps the tone kind long after novelty fades, and it makes reconciliation easier because neither of you enjoys staying harsh. The friction is conflict avoidance wearing the mask of grace — with everything so pleasant to discuss, the unpleasant topics wait politely at the door, sometimes for years.

Put the gracious skill to hard use: pick the subject you have both been avoiding and give it the same generous, unhurried conversation you give your favorites.
