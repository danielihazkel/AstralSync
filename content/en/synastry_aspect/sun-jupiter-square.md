title: Sun square Jupiter
---

There is a lot of enthusiasm here, and it does not always point in the same direction. The Sun person wants to lead with their own purpose; the Jupiter person wants to enlarge it — add a bigger vision, a moral, a better plan. Each can experience the other as too much: too sure of themselves, too sure of the answer.

The strength is real stretch. This pairing pushes both of you past cautious defaults, and arguments about meaning, faith, or ethics can be genuinely clarifying. The friction is excess and one-upmanship: bigger promises, louder certainty, and a habit of overcommitting together and then blaming the other for the overreach. Preaching on one side meets pride on the other.

Before you expand any plan, agree out loud on its limit — time, money, or scope — and treat staying inside it as a shared win.
