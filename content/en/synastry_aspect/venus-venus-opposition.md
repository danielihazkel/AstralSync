title: Venus opposition Venus
---

Your ways of loving mirror each other from across a divide. Each of you is fascinated by the other's aesthetic world — the taste, the social ease or the depth, the whole style of pleasure you don't possess — and the attraction carries a trace of envy's friendlier cousin: admiration that wants to borrow.

The gift is expansion through partnership: together your circles, palates, and pleasures roughly double, and each of you gains entry to experiences your own style would never book. The cost is a values seesaw — spending, socializing, beauty, and affection itself keep tipping toward one pole or the other, and whoever's style is currently "losing" can feel subtly unloved in their own home.

Formalize the balance: both styles get real estate — in the calendar, the budget, the house — and neither gets treated as the guest.
