title: Moon sextile Mercury
---

There is a gentle channel between one person's inner weather and the other's way with words. Feelings that usually stay wordless get coaxed into the open here, not by pressure but by good questions and easy timing. Talking things through actually soothes you both.

The gift is comfortable honesty — checking in feels light rather than heavy, and small hurts get aired before they grow. The friction is mildness itself: because this connection never forces the issue, the deepest material can stay politely untouched, with both of you assuming the other would raise it if it mattered.

Use the ease deliberately: once in a while, ask the bigger question — what one of you is afraid of, not just how the week went.
