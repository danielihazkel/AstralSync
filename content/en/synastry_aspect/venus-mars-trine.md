title: Venus trine Mars
---

Desire and affection cooperate easily between you. One of you initiates in exactly the register the other enjoys receiving, so physical warmth, playfulness, and everyday touch come naturally and rarely feel forced or negotiated.

The gift is a durable, low-maintenance spark — the kind that survives busy seasons because reconnecting takes minutes, not campaigns. The friction is complacency: because attraction never has to be earned, you may stop courting each other altogether, and the ease can mask other areas — money, ambition, values — where you actually diverge.

Keep making the effort you technically don't need: plan the date, dress for each other, try something new. Let the easy chemistry fund the harder conversations instead of replacing them.
