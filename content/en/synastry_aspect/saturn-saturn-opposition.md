title: Saturn opposition Saturn
---

You stand at opposite points of the same long cycle — often close to half a Saturn return apart in age, or born under opposing seasons of it. Your philosophies of obligation mirror each other in reverse: where one of you builds walls, the other builds doors, and each of you learned your stance from a world the other did not quite live in.

The gift is completeness: between you, every duty gets seen from both sides, and your Saturn seasons alternate, so one of you is usually steady while the other is being tested. That staggered strength is rare and valuable. The friction is mutual correction. Each of you can treat the other's rulebook as the error to be fixed, turning practical decisions into verdicts on each other's upbringing.

Treat your two systems as a rotation, not a contest. When a burden lands, let the one currently on solid ground carry more — and say thank you in plain words.
