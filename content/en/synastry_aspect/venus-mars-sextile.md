title: Venus sextile Mars
---

Attraction here is friendly before it is anything else. The Mars person's energy engages the Venus person without overwhelming; the Venus person's warmth invites the Mars person without games. Desire and affection meet on level ground, and flirtation between you stays fun rather than fraught — a rarer talent than couples usually credit.

The gift is chemistry with a low maintenance cost: reconnection is quick, playfulness survives routine, and physical affection negotiates itself. The limitation is that a sextile supplies the kindling but not the match — under stress or long familiarity the spark can simply go unlit for stretches, mistaken for gone when it was only waiting to be asked.

Ask it: initiate on purpose, schedule what should never need scheduling, and treat the ease between you as an instrument that stays in tune by being played.
