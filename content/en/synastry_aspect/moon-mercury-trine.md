title: Moon trine Mercury
---

Between you, feelings travel into language with almost no loss. One of you can sense a mood shift and the other can name it kindly; hard conversations that would strain other pairs tend to end with both of you feeling closer. Talking is a form of comfort here.

The gift is trust — being able to say anything, and be met with understanding rather than alarm, makes this bond feel like home. The friction is quiet: fluency can become narration. You may get so good at describing feelings that you stop sitting with them, processing everything into tidy words while the raw, awkward material never quite surfaces.

Leave room for silence sometimes; let one of you be wordlessly upset without either of you rushing to caption it.
