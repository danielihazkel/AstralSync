title: Mercury trine Jupiter
---

Ideas move freely and generously between you. The Mercury person's quick observations meet the Jupiter person's wide perspective, and conversations tend to leave both of you feeling understood and a little wiser. Teaching, travel planning, writing, or simply long talks over coffee come naturally to this pair.

The gift is good faith in communication: you assume the best about each other's words, misunderstandings are rare, and humor smooths over the ones that happen. The friction is a comfortable vagueness. Because agreement comes easily, you may skip the careful details, take on more plans than you can follow through on, or reinforce each other's opinions without testing them.

Use the ease for something with a finish line — a shared project, a trip you actually book, a subject you study to the end.
