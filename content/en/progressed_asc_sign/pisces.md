title: Progressed Ascendant in Pisces
---

Your current interface has lost its hard outline, on purpose. These years teach you to lead with compassion: to meet new situations by feeling into them, to let art, dream, and quiet do work that argument used to do, and to allow identities that once defined you to dissolve without rushing to replace them.

The emphasis is permeability as a gift — empathy that reads rooms other people find opaque, imagination that arrives unbidden, kindness with no ledger attached. The friction is with the natal layer underneath: if that self is precise or driven, this fog can feel like losing the plot, and the caution is drift — boundaries so soft that other people's emergencies, moods, and debts flow in and settle as your own.

Keep one bank of the river solid. Guard a daily hour of practice — music, prayer, water, page — and rehearse one clear sentence of no for the person who most reliably overflows into you.
