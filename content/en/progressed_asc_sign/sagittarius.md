Your interface has thrown its windows open. These years teach you to lead with meaning: to greet new situations as evidence of a bigger pattern, to say yes to the far city and the strange subject, and to let honesty arrive before tact does. Your horizon widens, and people start coming to you for perspective rather than sympathy.

The strength is expansion — teaching, travel, publishing, faith in the long arc — and a laugh that carries further than it used to. The friction is with the natal presentation underneath: if that layer is careful or private, this new candor can feel reckless, and the caution is inflation — promising three continents' worth of plans and preaching conclusions you have not yet lived.

Aim the arrow, then walk to where it lands. Book the course, the trip, or the mentor that your beliefs keep pointing at, and go test them in the open.
