title: Jupiter Retrograde
---

Your sense of meaning is homegrown. Inherited beliefs — the family religion, the school's definition of success, the culture's idea of a good life — never quite fit until you take them apart and rebuild them in your own workshop. Growth for you is less about accumulating more and more about digesting what you already have.

- Your strength: your optimism, when you have it, is earned. People trust your encouragement because you clearly don't hand it out on principle.
- Your friction: you can under-claim. While others announce their luck loudly, you quietly assume opportunities meant for you must have been mislabeled, and you let doors swing shut unexamined.

The next time an opening appears — an invitation, a role, a bit of luck — try acting on it *before* you have finished deciding whether you deserve it.
