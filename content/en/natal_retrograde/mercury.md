title: Mercury Retrograde
---

You were born while Mercury appeared to move backward, and your mind moves the same way — inward first, outward later. You rarely think out loud; you think, revise, test the sentence against your own standard, and only then speak. What you finally say tends to be more considered than the room expects, which is why people quote you back to yourself.

- Your strength: you catch what fast talkers miss — the flawed assumption, the word that means two things, the question nobody asked.
- Your friction: in live conversation you can go quiet at exactly the moment your input matters, and silence gets read as agreement or absence.

This week, when a discussion moves too fast, say *I'm still thinking about that — can I come back to it?* out loud instead of just doing it privately.
