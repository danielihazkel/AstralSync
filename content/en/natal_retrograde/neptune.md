title: Neptune Retrograde
---

You carry a lie detector where others carry rose-colored glasses. Collective dreams — advertising, ideology, the group's favorite comforting story — tend to slide off you, and your imagination points inward instead, toward a private symbolic world that feels more real than most public ones. Your spirituality, if you claim one, is tested firsthand or not at all.

- Your strength: you see through glamour. Friends bring you their shiny new plans precisely because you'll notice the fog machine in the corner.
- Your friction: constant disillusionment can curdle into refusing enchantment altogether — treating every hope as a scam in progress, including your own, and going subtly numb to protect yourself.

Sometime this week, let yourself be moved by something — music, water, a film — without immediately explaining what trick it's playing on you.
