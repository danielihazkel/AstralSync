title: Uranus Retrograde
---

Your rebellion is an inside job. You may look conventional — steady job, ordinary calendar — while holding views that would startle everyone at the table. Rather than performing difference, you *are* different, quietly, and you change your life in sudden private leaps that observers mistake for impulse when they were actually years in the making.

- Your strength: genuine independence of mind. You don't need a movement, a uniform, or an audience to think for yourself.
- Your friction: because the revolution happens internally, the people close to you get no warning. The resignation, the move, the ended relationship — it lands on them as lightning, though you'd been storing the charge for a long time.

When you next feel a big internal shift beginning, tell one trusted person about it *early*, while it is still a weather report and not yet a storm.
