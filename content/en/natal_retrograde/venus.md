title: Venus Retrograde
---

This is a rare birth signature, and it shows in how privately you hold what you love. Your taste, your affections, your sense of what is beautiful — none of it arrived off the shelf. You audit every value you were handed and keep only what survives the audit, so your loyalties, once given, are unusually deliberate and unusually durable.

The friction is timing. You often recognize your own feelings in retrospect — the person mattered more than you admitted, the compliment you deflected was one you wanted. Because you review love instead of performing it, others can experience you as cooler than you are, and old relationships have a way of returning for a second reading.

Once this week, tell someone what they mean to you *while it is still happening*, not after the moment has safely passed.
