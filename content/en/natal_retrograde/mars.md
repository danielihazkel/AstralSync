title: Mars Retrograde
---

Your drive runs on an internal circuit. Where others charge, you coil — assessing the terrain, rehearsing the move, waiting for the moment when effort will actually land. This makes you formidable in long campaigns: you outlast people who burn hot early, and your timing, when you finally commit, is often devastatingly good.

- Your strength: strategic patience. You win by choosing the battle, not by arriving first.
- Your friction: anger you don't express doesn't leave; it composts. It resurfaces as fatigue, sudden withdrawal, or a no delivered months after the yes that should have been a no.

Notice this week the first flicker of *I don't want to* — and say a small, prompt version of it, so the large delayed version never has to be said.
