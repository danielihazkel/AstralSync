title: Pluto Retrograde

Your deepest changes happen underground. Where others process crisis out loud, you take it into the basement workshop and emerge months later visibly altered, with no interest in narrating the middle part. Power, for you, is something to be understood before it is ever used — you study how influence works, in yourself and in others, with a researcher's patience.

- Your strength: you transform thoroughly. When you release an old pattern, it stays released; there is nothing performative about your growth.
- Your friction: secrecy becomes a reflex. You can withhold even harmless truths simply because disclosure feels like handing someone a lever, and intimacy stalls at the door you keep locked.

This week, share one small in-progress struggle with someone you trust — before it is finished, resolved, and safe to mention.
