title: Saturn Retrograde
---

Authority never fully convinced you from the outside. Somewhere early, the people meant to model structure — a parent, a teacher, an institution — were absent, inconsistent, or simply wrong, so you built your own scaffolding. The rules you follow now are rules you wrote, and you hold yourself to them with a rigor no boss could impose.

That self-made discipline is your strength: you don't need supervision, deadlines pressure you less than your own standard does, and you finish things in private that others need an audience to attempt. The friction is that your inner judge works overtime. You often treat ordinary mistakes as verdicts on your character, and you can spend years over-qualifying for permission no one was actually withholding.

This month, notice one place you're waiting to feel *ready* — and ask whether the requirement is real or just yours.
