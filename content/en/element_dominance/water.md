title: Water Dominant
essence: a tide-like attunement to every unspoken mood
---

With water signs carrying most of your chart's weight, feeling is your first language and everything else is translation. You know how the meeting went before the recap, and you carry other people's weather home in your chest whether you invited it or not.

This attunement makes you the confidant, the early-warning system, the one who notices the coworker quietly unraveling. Intimacy that would take others a year takes you an evening. The undertow is porousness: absorbing moods that were never yours to hold, reading rejection into a slow reply, retreating into hurt silence and expecting loved ones to divine why. Sensitivity without boundaries becomes a flood plain.

You cannot un-feel, but you can sort. When a heavy mood arrives, ask one clarifying question out loud — whose is this, mine or theirs? — and set down whatever turns out not to be yours.
