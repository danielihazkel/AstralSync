key: element_dominance/fire
title: Fire Dominant
essence: a spark's readiness to move first and adjust later
---

With so many planets clustered in fire signs, your chart's center of gravity is ignition. You experience wanting and doing as nearly the same event: the idea arrives, the body is already in motion, and hesitation feels like a minor illness.

This temperament gives you courage that other people rent from you. You volunteer for the unproven plan, say the true thing at the awkward moment, and recover from failure with almost suspicious speed. The friction is scorch: interrupting, deciding for the group, promising on Monday what bores you by Thursday. When everything is urgent, the people around you learn to stop telling you which things actually are.

Fire is a fine engine and a poor navigator. Before your next big yes, impose a single overnight delay — if the idea still burns in the morning, trust it.
