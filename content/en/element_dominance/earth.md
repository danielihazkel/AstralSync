title: Earth Dominant
essence: a bedrock steadiness that outlasts every passing storm
---

A chart weighted toward earth signs gives your temperament gravity. You measure ideas by whether they survive contact with rent, calendars, and human fatigue, and you distrust any plan that cannot answer the question "who does this, and by when?"

Around you, things get finished. Friends borrow your calm the way they borrow tools, and employers discover that your unglamorous consistency is what their heroics quietly stand on. The friction is inertia dressed up as prudence: you can keep watering a dead plant — a role, a routine, a relationship — because tending is what you know, and because change proposes a mess you would rather not schedule. Comfort and rightness are not the same measurement.

Once a season, audit your commitments the way you would audit a budget, and let one obligation go before it lets go of you.
