title: Mars conjunct Midheaven
---

Your ambition is not a private matter — it is stapled to the top of your chart for everyone to see. You compete in public, and you are at your best with a summit to take: launches, deadlines, turnarounds, any arena where hunger is an asset rather than a faux pas. Bosses learn quickly that you would rather be given the hard problem than the safe one.

The friction is that the same visibility magnifies your battles. A dispute that would stay private in another career goes on your permanent record in yours, and a reputation for fire can quietly harden into a reputation for volatility. Burned bridges above you are expensive.

Aim the heat at the work, not the hierarchy. Pick one professional summit per season, pursue it loudly, and let your results do the arguing that your temper is tempted to do.
