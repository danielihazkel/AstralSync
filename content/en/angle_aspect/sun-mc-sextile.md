title: Sun sextile Midheaven
---

Who you are and what you're building toward are on speaking terms. Your talents translate into professional currency without heavy conversion costs: mentors notice you, your name comes up in rooms you're not in, and the career ladder tends to have a few rungs pre-climbed.

The strength is alignment — you rarely suffer the grinding split between the job and the self, so your work carries a signature. The friction is that a sextile is an offer, not a delivery. Opportunity arrives as a light tap here, easy to miss or defer, and you can spend years vaguely well-positioned while people with louder aspects actually ship. Being promising is a phase, not a career.

When the next door cracks open, don't just note it — walk through within the week, before the moment cools.
