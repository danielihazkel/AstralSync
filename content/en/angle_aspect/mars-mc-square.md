title: Mars square Midheaven
---

Your drive squares off against both poles of your vertical axis: the public role you are building and the private base you build it from. Career pushes ignite friction at home; domestic demands feel like sabotage of your climb; and authority figures seem to stand precisely where you want to charge. Nothing about your ambition is frictionless.

The compensation is force that never goes soft. People with this square become formidable because every professional inch is contested — you learn to fight for territory, defend your hours, and take hits without folding. The hazard is chronic war on two fronts: battling the boss all day and bringing the leftover heat back to the kitchen at night.

The axis needs a treaty, not a victor. Decide explicitly what the climb may cost your home life and what it may not, and renegotiate that line out loud every time the job escalates.
