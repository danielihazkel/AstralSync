title: Pluto square Midheaven
---

Power struggles run down your vertical axis, straining career and home at once. At work you collide with authority — bosses who feel threatened, institutions that promote you and then police you — and your rises tend to be dramatic, your exits total. Underneath, the pattern has roots: a family story about control, survival, or ambition that you either inherited or swore to escape, still running the show from the basement.

The strength this grinds into you is the capacity to rebuild from zero. You have lost positions, homes, or identities and reconstituted each time with more density, so setbacks that end other careers merely reroute yours. People who have watched you recover once never doubt you again.

Trace your next workplace battle back a generation — ask whose fight this originally was — before deciding how much of yourself to spend on it.
