title: Sun trine Ascendant
---

Vitality reaches your surface without resistance. You look like yourself, sound like yourself, and carry an unforced warmth that makes people assume competence and good faith before you've proven either. Doors that others knock on tend to be ajar for you.

The strength is coherence under pressure: because your presentation isn't a performance, it doesn't crack when things get hard, and people find that steadiness magnetic. The friction is the classic trine tax — ease you never earned is ease you don't notice spending. You can mistake being welcomed for being finished, drift on likability, and wake up years in with presence but no body of work behind it.

Attach the ease to something difficult: pick one ambition that scares you and let your natural welcome be its opening move, not the whole act.
