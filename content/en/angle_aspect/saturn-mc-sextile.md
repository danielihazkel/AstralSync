title: Saturn sextile Midheaven
---

Your ambition has good bones. Discipline and public direction cooperate in you, so career-building feels less like a gamble and more like carpentry: measure, cut, join, repeat. Mentors and institutions tend to open doors when you knock, because you read as someone worth investing in — patient, structured, unlikely to squander the chance.

The steady grade of the climb is also its hazard. Because progress comes from consistency rather than crisis, you can mistake momentum for meaning and spend a decade advancing smoothly up a ladder you never chose, competent all the way. Opportunity keeps arriving; you keep accepting; the question of what the structure is for goes quietly unasked.

Sextiles pay out only when worked deliberately. Once a year, put the ladder itself on trial — then commit hard to the one that survives the review.
