title: Neptune square Ascendant
---

This square pulls fog across the whole relating axis: how you appear and whom you attract are both touched by Neptune's blur. People misread you constantly — they meet a mirage, fall for it or dismiss it, and you are left defending a self you never actually presented. In partnership the confusion doubles: you idealize, or are idealized, and the eventual correction stings on both ends.

The buried strength is compassion with teeth. Because you know intimately what it is to be mistaken for a projection, you extend others a precision of empathy that easy charts never develop. When you do state who you are and what you want, it lands with unusual force, exactly because it cost you something.

Practice declarative sentences about yourself — plain, unhedged, slightly uncomfortable — and let people update their picture of you in real time.
