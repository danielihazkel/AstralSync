title: Sun opposition Midheaven
---

Your center burns at the base of the chart, not the summit. With the Sun on the IC, identity is anchored in the private sphere — home, family, lineage, the rooms with no audience — and that anchor pulls steadily against whatever public arc your life is tracing. Career can feel like an away game; you do it well, then come home to become real again.

The strength is foundation. You build interior lives, households, and family cultures with a depth that publicly-fixated charts never manage, and the people inside your walls know exactly who you are. The friction is the tax on visibility: chances to be seen can feel like exposure, and it's tempting to shrink the public self until resentment starts leaking under the door.

Choose one piece of your private depth and let it go public on purpose this season.
