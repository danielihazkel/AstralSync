title: Venus square Ascendant
---

Wanting to be liked and being yourself keep stepping on each other. This square strains the whole axis of self and other: you can over-groom the first impression and then resent the performance, swing between accommodating everyone and abruptly pleasing no one, or attract people with a persona your private tastes cannot sustain. Relationships begin on charm and hit turbulence when the unedited you shows up.

The friction has a payoff. Because harmony never comes automatically, you actually study it — what fairness costs, where charm ends and honesty must begin — and the affection you eventually earn is sturdier than easy popularity. People with this square often develop real taste precisely because their first aesthetic instincts got argued with. Untended, though, it curdles into approval-hunting: reading every face for a verdict on your worth.

When you feel the urge to edit yourself for a room, name one genuine preference out loud instead — let people adjust to you occasionally.
