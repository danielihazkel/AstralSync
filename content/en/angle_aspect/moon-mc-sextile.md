title: Moon sextile Midheaven
---

Your instincts are professionally useful. The Moon's easy angle to the Midheaven lets gut feel flow into your public work: you sense what an audience, a client, or a team actually needs beneath what they say, and your career choices tend to track your inner tides instead of fighting them.

The strength is a vocation with a pulse — colleagues experience you as humane, timing-savvy, and easy to work for, and that reputation compounds quietly for years. The friction is quietness itself. Because the support is ambient rather than urgent, you may never quite claim it: intuition gets filed as "just a hunch," care work gets done off the books, and the promotion goes to whoever narrated their instincts out loud.

Start saying the hunch in the meeting, with your name attached, before someone else says it slower and gets the credit.
