title: Venus sextile Midheaven
---

Your career runs on smooth bearings. This sextile feeds Venus quietly into your public life: colleagues enjoy working with you, your work has a finish that reads as care, and professional relationships tend to ripen into opportunities without you forcing them. You advance less by conquest than by being the person nobody objects to promoting.

The taken-for-granted risk is exactly that phrasing — nobody objects. Easy goodwill can become a strategy of inoffensiveness: taking the assignments that keep everyone comfortable, letting harmony set your ambitions' ceiling, being warmly recommended for roles slightly smaller than your talent. Pleasant careers can plateau precisely because no one, including you, ever felt friction on your behalf.

Once a year, pursue something professionally that risks real disapproval — a bold pitch, a public stance, a stretch role — and let your accumulated goodwill absorb the turbulence it was saved for.
