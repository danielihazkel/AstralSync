title: Mercury trine Ascendant
---

Articulacy is your natural gait. Thought moves into speech and speech into presence without visible effort: you explain things people actually understand, you are funny at the right moment, and strangers trust you quickly because your words and your bearing agree with each other. Interviews, introductions, and improvised explanations play to you.

The trine's shadow is glibness. Because you can be persuasive without preparation, you may stop preparing; because you can talk your way through, you may never sit with what you cannot yet say. Fluency starts to substitute for depth, and people who adore your company can struggle to say what you actually stand for. Ease that is never challenged quietly becomes a ceiling.

Pick one subject that matters to you and speak about it only when you have done the work — let at least one part of your voice be earned, not just given.
