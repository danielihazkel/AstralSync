title: Pluto trine Midheaven
---

Power flows toward your public role without much friction. Pluto trine the Midheaven makes you the person organizations trust with the loaded things — budgets, secrets, restructurings, other people's careers — and your professional reinventions land cleanly, each chapter visibly deeper than the last. You metabolize institutional crisis as ordinary work, and your reputation for holding pressure builds itself while you simply do the job.

The trine's shadow is unexamined accumulation. Because influence arrives without battles, you may stop noticing you have it: decisions you consider small move other people's livelihoods, and easy authority can quietly become entitlement to outcomes. Power that was never contested is the kind least often audited.

Run your own review once a year — list what you now control that you did not five years ago, and ask who would tell you if you misused it.
