title: Pluto opposition Midheaven
---

Pluto sits at the root of your chart, on the IC, so your power source is buried in the private ground: family history, early survival lessons, the things your household did not say out loud. Home is never neutral for you — it is the crucible where your real transformations happen — and the depth you draw from that hidden territory gives your public work an authority that cannot be faked. People sense you are standing on something dense.

The friction is what the ground contains. Inherited secrets, old alliances and grudges, control patterns learned at the kitchen table keep resurfacing in your closest quarters, and career choices can quietly become ways to avoid going home to them. Foundations you refuse to excavate excavate themselves, usually at worse moments.

Map the family story deliberately — ask the questions, read the records — so your roots become fuel you chose rather than pressure you inherit.
