title: Moon square Ascendant
---

Your inner weather and your outer surface run on different clocks. What you feel and what you show fall out of sync — you come across cooler than you are, or leak more than you meant to — and the mismatch strains both ends of the axis: strangers misjudge your temperature while intimates get the backlog all at once.

The strength is emotional depth under a worked surface. Because expression doesn't come automatically, you become a genuine student of your own moods, and the feelings you do manage to voice land with unusual weight. The corrosive side is the vault-and-flood cycle: containing everything for weeks because the moment never feels right, then releasing it sideways at whoever is closest.

Name feelings while they're still small — a plain "I'm frustrated" today spares everyone the translated version next month.
