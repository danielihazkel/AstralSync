title: Venus trine Midheaven
---

Your reputation seems to groom itself. The trine carries Venus up to the Midheaven on an easy current: your work is remembered as elegant, your presence in professional settings as gracious, and allies accumulate around your career the way regulars accumulate at a good cafe. Mentors take to you, and doors have a way of being already open when you reach them.

The shadow of all this welcome is passivity. When advancement arrives through charm and favor, you can mistake being carried for swimming — waiting to be chosen rather than declaring what you want, accepting the flattering opportunity over the right one, and never learning to fight for a position because you have never had to. Favor is weather; it changes.

Name your actual ambition out loud to the people who like you — give all that goodwill a specific destination instead of letting it drift you somewhere agreeable.
