title: Pluto opposition Ascendant
---

Pluto occupies your Descendant, so transformation arrives wearing another person's face. You do not do casual bonds: the people who matter to you land like weather systems — magnetic, consuming, and impossible to file under acquaintance. At its best this gives you relationships of rare depth, the kind that dismantle who you were and leave someone truer standing; partners become the crucible in which you actually change.

The friction is the power line running through every close bond. Jealousy, ultimatums, financial or emotional leverage, the slow contest over who controls whom — these keep surfacing, and the tempting story is that you simply attract intense people. You do; you also co-author the intensity, often by handing partners power and then fighting to reclaim it.

In your next conflict, name the thing you fear losing before you make a single move to control it.
