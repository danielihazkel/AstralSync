title: Jupiter square Midheaven
---

Your appetite for more strains both poles of the vertical axis: the career keeps expanding past its container, and the home keeps paying the invoice. You say yes to the bigger role, the extra market, the second venture — and each yes quietly withdraws hours and presence from your foundation. Meanwhile your private self dreams on the same grand scale, so even rest turns into renovation plans.

The strength is genuine range. You outgrow ceilings that would satisfy others for life, and your willingness to bet big produces the occasional result that redraws your whole trajectory. The friction is the perpetual oversubscription: commitments in public and private that total more than one person, and a faith that the details will somehow settle themselves.

Growth needs a budget like everything else. Cap your simultaneous major commitments at a number you choose in cold blood, and honor it especially when the next dazzling yes appears.
