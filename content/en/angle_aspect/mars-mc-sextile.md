title: Mars sextile Midheaven
---

Drive feeds your public role through a side door. You can push for the promotion, the budget, or the byline without tripping the alarms that overt ambition usually sets off — colleagues read you as motivated, not menacing. Initiative comes easily in professional settings, and decision-makers tend to hand you the projects that need a self-starter.

The risk of a supportive aspect is that support gets mistaken for destiny. Because career effort flows smoothly, you may settle into competent motion — busy, reliable, respected — without ever forcing the question of what you are actually building toward. Sextiles reward the asker, and they give nothing to the drifter.

Turn ease into trajectory. Name the role you want two steps from now, tell someone with power to help, and let this aspect do what it does best: convert a stated aim into an open door.
