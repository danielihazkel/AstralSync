title: Sun conjunct Midheaven
---

Your identity is stapled to the visible arc of your life. Work is not what you do; it is where you become yourself, in front of witnesses. You track reputation the way other people track weather, and you tend to rise because ambition here isn't a mood — it's structural.

The strength is focus: you know the game is played in public, so you build a record instead of a daydream, and authority sits naturally on you once earned. The friction is that the staple goes both ways. A professional setback lands as an identity wound, praise becomes a nutrient you can't quite live without, and the private, unphotographed parts of life can thin out to a green room for the next appearance.

Schedule something weekly that you're allowed to be mediocre at, with no audience and no résumé value.
