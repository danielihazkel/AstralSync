title: Neptune square Midheaven
---

The fog rolls across both poles of your vertical axis: what you are building toward and what you are standing on. Career direction blurs — you chase callings that evaporate on contact, or inherit ambitions that turn out to be someone else's dream — while home never quite feels like the solid base it should be. You can work hard for years and still struggle to say what, exactly, you are becoming.

The hidden strength is an allergy to hollow ambition. You simply cannot sustain effort toward a goal your soul has not signed, and that refusal, honored consciously, filters out decades of wasted climbing. When direction and meaning finally align, your commitment has a devotional quality colleagues notice.

Test every goal against a plain question — would I want this if no one ever saw it — and drop the ones that fail twice.
