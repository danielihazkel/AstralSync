title: Mars trine Ascendant
---

Vitality pours straight into how you carry yourself. You move with an athlete's economy, decide quickly, and project a physical confidence that makes leadership look unearned — people simply assume you will go first, and mostly you do. Courage is not a performance for you; it is a resting state.

The shadow of that flow is over-reliance. When force is always available, it becomes the default tool, and you can bulldoze situations that wanted patience, subtlety, or silence. Ease also breeds blind spots: because you have rarely had to strategize your way through resistance, a determined opponent who refuses direct combat can leave you swinging at air.

Keep the momentum but widen the toolkit. In your next contested situation, deliberately try the indirect route first — timing, allies, leverage — and save the frontal push for when it is genuinely the best move.
