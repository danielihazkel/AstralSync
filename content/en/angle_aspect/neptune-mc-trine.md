title: Neptune trine Midheaven
---

Your reputation does quiet work on your behalf. Neptune trine the Midheaven surrounds your public name with an attractive haze — people assume depth, taste, and good intentions, and opportunities drift toward you through recommendation and rumor rather than application. Creative, spiritual, or helping work suits you unusually well, and you can move between professional worlds without the friction most people pay at the border.

The taken-for-granted risk is passivity. Because doors open on atmosphere, you may never learn to knock; the career happens to you, pleasant and slightly shapeless, until you look up mid-life and cannot find your own fingerprints on it. Grace without steering is just weather.

Name the contribution you want to be known for in one sentence, and make your next professional yes or no answer to that sentence.
