title: Mercury trine Midheaven
---

Thinking and career run on the same rails for you. Ideas convert into professional currency with unusual ease: you learn the vocabulary of any field quickly, explain your work in ways decision-makers actually absorb, and your reputation grows through memos, talks, and well-timed questions rather than through politicking. People above you tend to assume you are on top of things, largely because you sound like you are.

That last part is the trine's trap. Sounding on top of things is cheaper than being on top of things, and this aspect lets you run a long way on articulate summaries of shallow understanding. The risk is a polished professional surface over an unexamined middle — impressive in the meeting, thin in the crisis.

Keep one hard skill in deliberate training at all times, and let your public fluency report on real depth rather than perform in place of it.
