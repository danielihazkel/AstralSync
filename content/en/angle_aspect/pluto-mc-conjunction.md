title: Pluto conjunct Midheaven
---

You are built for high-stakes work. With Pluto fused to the Midheaven, your vocation keeps steering toward power, crisis, and transformation — turnarounds, depth psychology, investigation, medicine, money, anything where something must die so something better can live. Your reputation carries weight early: superiors either groom you or guard against you, and you rarely get to be professionally invisible even when you want to.

The friction is the price of altitude. Public roles expose you, rivals take your composure personally, and when a chapter ends you tend to end it completely — resignation, relocation, name off the door — leaving allies stunned by the scorched ground. Ambition can also curdle into needing control of outcomes no one controls.

Before your next reinvention, salvage deliberately: choose which relationships, skills, and records cross the fire with you instead of torching the whole era.
