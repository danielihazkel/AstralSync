title: Moon square Midheaven
---

Your needs and your public arc bill each other for the same hours. The Moon square the Midheaven strains the whole home-career axis: ambition tugs while you're resting, belonging tugs while you're working, and whichever end you're standing on, the other sends invoices. Guilt becomes a commute you make in both directions.

The strength is that you refuse to amputate either pole. People with this square, once they stop apologizing, build the rare life where the work is nourishing and the home is ambitious — because they've had to negotiate every inch of it consciously. The corrosive side is chronic half-presence: answering messages at the dinner table, daydreaming of the dinner table at work, and doing neither place justice.

Draw one hard line this week — a work-free hour or a guilt-free deadline — and keep it like a promise to a child.
