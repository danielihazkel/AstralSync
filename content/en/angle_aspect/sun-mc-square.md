title: Sun square Midheaven
---

Your sense of self grinds against the public track you're on. The career that impresses doesn't quite fit who you are; the life that fits doesn't quite impress — and both ends of the axis feel it, because home and vocation keep billing each other for the same hours. You may cycle through roles that look right on paper and chafe in practice.

The strength is that this friction is a compass. Every misfit job teaches you something exact about your actual shape, and identities forged through course corrections end up more durable than ones handed over by a tidy ladder. The corrosive side is perpetual restlessness: quitting the container instead of renegotiating it, or staying and quietly leaking morale.

Before the next pivot, write down what the current role violates in you specifically — then change that one thing first.
