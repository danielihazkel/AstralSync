title: Jupiter opposition Midheaven
---

Jupiter sits on your IC, the root of the chart, so your abundance pools in the private sphere. Home is where you are largest: the generous table, the open guest room, the family culture of books, faith, or laughter that visitors remember for years. Your confidence is fed from beneath — by lineage, land, or a philosophy of belonging that no career setback can repossess.

That deep floor is the strength: you take professional risks calmly because the base always holds. The friction is gravitational. Comfort this rich can make the public climb feel optional, and you may keep your biggest gifts household-sized — the wisdom shared only at your own table, the ambitions deferred because home already feels like enough.

Let the root fund the branch. Take one thing your private world does beautifully — hosting, counsel, tradition, teaching — and give it a public form this year.
