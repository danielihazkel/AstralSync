title: Neptune opposition Ascendant
---

Neptune sits on your Descendant, so you meet the ocean through other people. Partners arrive haloed: the wounded artist, the almost-available mystic, the person who would be perfect if only. You give devotion few can match — patient, imaginative, willing to love what someone could become — and the intimacy you offer at your best has a genuinely soulful register.

The friction is the disillusionment cycle. You fall for the halo, discover the human underneath, and feel betrayed by a promise the other person never actually made. Or you play the elusive one yourself, keeping partners in love with your potential. Rescuing, being rescued, and quietly disappearing are all the same pattern wearing different coats.

Before committing to anyone, list three of their flaws you can live with; if you cannot name any flaws yet, you are not seeing them.
