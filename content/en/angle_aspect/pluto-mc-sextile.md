title: Pluto sextile Midheaven
---

Influence accrues to you quietly at work. Pluto sextile the Midheaven means the difficult assignments migrate your way — the failing project, the delicate negotiation, the conversation nobody else will have — and you handle them with a composure that steadily converts into institutional trust. You understand where real power sits in any organization, usually within weeks, and you can use that map without abusing it.

Because the gear engages so smoothly, you may stay the fixer forever: indispensable, discreet, and never the one whose name is on the transformation. Sextiles wait to be worked; unclaimed, this one leaves you powerful in every room except your own career narrative. Deference can masquerade as patience for decades.

Claim authorship of your next rescue in public — a document, a talk, your name attached — instead of letting the credit dissolve upward.
