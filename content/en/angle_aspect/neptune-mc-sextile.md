title: Neptune sextile Midheaven
---

Imagination and career are on friendly terms in your chart. Ideas arrive when you need them professionally: the humane angle in a technical field, the image that sells the strategy, the instinct for what an audience quietly wants. Colleagues experience you as the one who brings soul into rooms that had settled for process, and your reputation benefits from a warmth you never have to perform.

The sextile's risk is politeness. Because inspiration flows without crisis, you may keep it decorative — a flourish on someone else's agenda — and never test whether the visionary streak could carry a project of its own. Easy gifts are the ones most often left in the drawer.

Once a year, pitch something that is entirely yours: an offering, a piece, a program with your name on it, shipped whether or not anyone asked.
