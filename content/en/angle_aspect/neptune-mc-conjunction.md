title: Neptune conjunct Midheaven
---

Your calling refuses to fit on a business card. With Neptune fused to the Midheaven, you are pulled toward work that heals, imagines, or transcends — art, spirit, care, image-making — and your reputation carries a shimmer people respond to before they can explain it. At your best you become a professional vessel: audiences and clients feel something move through your work that is larger than technique.

The friction is drift. Career plans dissolve mid-decade, titles never quite describe you, and colleagues project wildly — genius one year, flake the next. Waiting for the mist to clear before committing is the trap; the mist is permanent.

Anchor the vision in deliverables. Pick one body of work, give it a name and a deadline, and let the mystery live inside the container rather than instead of one.
