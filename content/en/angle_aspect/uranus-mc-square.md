title: Uranus square Midheaven
---

Restlessness runs through your whole vertical axis. Public direction and private foundation both carry a live wire: careers that detonate just as they stabilize, home arrangements that keep reshuffling, a recurring urge to torch the plan the moment it starts working. You resent being defined by a role, then resent having no role to be defined by.

The gift inside the static is authenticity pressure. This square will not let you succeed at the wrong thing for long — every false vocation gets ejected, sometimes rudely, until the work actually fits. People with your aspect end up in careers no counselor could have predicted and no one else could do. The cost is scorched runway: quitting reads as pattern, allies stop investing, and some explosions destroy structures that only needed remodeling.

Learn to tell renovation from demolition before reaching for the switch. Change the work in deliberate stages, and save the true detonations for structures that deserve them.
