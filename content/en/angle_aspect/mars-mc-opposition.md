title: Mars opposition Midheaven
---

Mars anchors the base of your chart, on the IC, so your fire burns in the foundation rather than on the facade. Home is where your engine lives: you renovate, defend, organize, and champion the people under your roof with an intensity the office rarely sees. Your deepest motivations are private, often inherited — a family story of struggle that still fuels you.

The strength is a defended core. Nobody uproots you easily, and the ambition that grows from that secure base is patient and hard to demoralize. The friction is that heat trapped indoors finds targets indoors: household tempers, sibling rivalries replayed with spouses, door-slamming energy that your colleagues would never recognize in you.

Give the root fire honest work. Take on a demanding physical project at home — building, training, repairing — so the engine spends itself on structure instead of on the people you love.
