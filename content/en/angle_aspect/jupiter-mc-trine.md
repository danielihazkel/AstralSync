title: Jupiter trine Midheaven
---

Professional doors open for you before you finish knocking. This trine wires your public standing to Jupiter's generosity: mentors adopt you, institutions bend their rules in your favor, and your name tends to come up in rooms you have never entered. You are built for vocations that scale — publishing, education, law, anything where reputation itself is the multiplier.

The hazard of a gifted career is sleepwalking through it. When advancement arrives on schedule without crisis, you can wake at mid-career inside a success you never examined, having accepted each opening simply because it opened. Ease also dulls urgency: the book stays unwritten because nothing forces the deadline.

Gratitude is the discipline that keeps this trine honest. Choose one ambition that luck cannot hand you — only sustained work can — and give it the first hour of your day.
