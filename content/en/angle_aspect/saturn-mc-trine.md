title: Saturn trine Midheaven
---

Reputation compounds for you. Effort converts to standing at a favorable exchange rate: you do solid work, the right people notice, and each finished project becomes a rung that holds. Elders and institutions trust you early, often before you have done much to earn it, because you carry the bearing of someone who finishes what they start.

The strength is a career with no wasted motion — few scandals, few collapses, a name that quietly appreciates. The taken-for-granted risk is low altitude. Because the system rewards you for steadiness, you may keep choosing the respectable next step over the remarkable leap, and end up an admired custodian of ambitions smaller than your capacity. Ease never forces the question that squares force daily: is this actually the mountain?

Bank the trust, then spend it. Use your standing to attempt one thing that steadiness alone could never reach.
