title: Mars trine Midheaven
---

Effort converts to advancement at a favorable exchange rate for you. Where others grind against office politics, your push tends to be welcomed: superiors read your aggression as leadership, your impatience as urgency, your appetite for the hard assignment as star quality. Careers with a scoreboard — sales, litigation, athletics, startups, surgery — feel like home.

The taken-for-granted risk is mistaking motion for direction. Because the machine responds every time you press the pedal, you can spend a decade accelerating down a road you never chose, collecting wins that impress everyone but you. Easy traction also tempts you to skip strategy; why plan when pushing works?

Audit the destination, not the speed. Once a year, ask whether the summit you are climbing is still the one you want — and if it is not, spend this trine's political capital on the pivot while it is cheap.
