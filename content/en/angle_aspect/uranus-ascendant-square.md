title: Uranus square Ascendant
---

You short-circuit your own first impressions. The square wires a jolt into how you meet the world: just when rapport settles, something in you swerves — a blurted truth, an abrupt exit, a sudden reinvention that leaves people relating to last month's version of you. Both ends of the axis feel it: you resist being pinned down, and partners complain they cannot predict you.

The voltage is also the value. You are incapable of a stale persona; you shed outgrown selves fast, notice when any relationship has calcified, and force the update everyone else was avoiding. Around you, nothing gets to be quietly dead. The cost is trust-building, which runs on sameness over time — and contrarian reflex, the swerve made just because someone expected a straight line.

Ask before each disruption: is this liberation or just discharge? Break patterns on purpose, on your own timing, and let the harmless ones live.
