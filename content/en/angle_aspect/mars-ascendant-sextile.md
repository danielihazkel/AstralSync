title: Mars sextile Ascendant
---

Assertiveness sits within easy reach of your persona. You can raise your voice, claim a seat, or start the hard conversation without the room flinching, because your drive arrives dressed in good manners. People experience you as energetic rather than aggressive, which lets you push agendas that would get a blunter person shut down.

The catch with ease is that it goes unexercised. Because standing up for yourself costs you little, you may never train the muscle for real resistance — you advocate when it is convenient and go quiet when the pushback has teeth. Confidence that has never been stress-tested can fold at the exact moment it matters.

This aspect is an open door, not a delivered outcome. Once a week, volunteer for the confrontation you could plausibly skip, and let the ease compound into actual courage.
