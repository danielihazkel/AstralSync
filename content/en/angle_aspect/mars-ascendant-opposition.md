title: Mars opposition Ascendant
---

Mars sits on your Descendant, the angle of partnership, so your fire lives in other people. You attract the driven, the competitive, the frankly combative — lovers who spar, business partners who push, rivals who feel strangely intimate. One-to-one is where your adrenaline turns on.

The strength is that you do conflict best in relationship: you negotiate hard, defend the people you are bonded to ferociously, and respect anyone who meets you at full strength. The friction is projection. When you disown your own aggression, it does not vanish — it shows up wearing your partner's face, and you end up baffled by the fights that keep finding your closest bonds.

Every recurring battle with an intimate is partly a mirror. Next time one flares, locate the demand you have not made directly, and make it yourself before assigning it to them.
