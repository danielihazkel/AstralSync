title: Uranus conjunct Midheaven
---

Your public role is the strange one — and it is supposed to be. Uranus fused with the Midheaven marks a vocation that does not fit the org chart: the innovator, the field-changer, the person whose job title has to be invented after they arrive. Conventional career ladders feel like slow suffocation to you, and you have a talent for making that visible.

The strength is genuine pioneering. You spot where an industry is about to break before it does, and your reputation, once it lands, is for being first — the one who did it differently and turned out to be right. The friction is trajectory: your career moves in lightning strikes and lulls, sudden reversals, ruptures with bosses and institutions, and observers may read the zigzag as instability rather than navigation.

Your path only looks erratic from outside; from inside it has a compass. Name the through-line of your work in one sentence, and let every zigzag answer to it.
