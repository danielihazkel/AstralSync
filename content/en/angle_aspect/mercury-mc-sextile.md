title: Mercury sextile Midheaven
---

Your ideas find their way into your work without a fight. This sextile links the way you think to the way you are seen professionally: reports, pitches, and explanations come out clean, you summarize meetings people were lost in, and your competence tends to get noticed because you can describe it. You do not have to campaign for a reputation as capable — your words quietly file the paperwork.

The catch with easy support is that it stays background. You can spend years as the person who writes everyone else's story — the ghostwriter, the explainer, the reliable second voice — because packaging others' ideas costs you so little. The skill is real; the byline goes missing.

Once a quarter, put your own thinking on the record under your own name — a talk, an article, a proposal — so the fluency builds your reputation, not just everyone else's.
