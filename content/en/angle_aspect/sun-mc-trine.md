title: Sun trine Midheaven
---

Vocation comes to you the way accents come to children — absorbed, not studied. What you naturally are happens to be professionally legible: authority reads on you early, gatekeepers wave you through, and your ambitions feel less like a war and more like a river with a known mouth.

The strength is stamina by another name. Because the public arc doesn't fight your grain, you can sustain output for decades where others burn out battling roles that don't fit. The friction is complacency with excellent posture: unearned smoothness invites you to pick the reachable summit instead of the right one, and to confuse a frictionless career with a finished one.

Every year or two, deliberately choose one professional risk that could actually fail in public — the trine will cushion the landing, not the regret.
