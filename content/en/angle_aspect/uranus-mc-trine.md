title: Uranus trine Midheaven
---

Career luck, for you, looks like timing. The trine syncs your public life to the future's schedule: you drift toward fields just before they matter, adopt tools just before they win, and pivot with a smoothness that makes reinvention look like a plan. Where others cling to sinking specialties, you have usually already stepped onto the next ship.

The strength is a reputation for being ahead of the curve without the martyrdom that usually buys it — institutions tolerate, even fund, your experiments. The taken-for-granted risk is shallowness of stake. Because escape is always available, you may never bind yourself to one mission long enough to be identified with it, and a résumé of well-timed exits can mask the absence of anything you would refuse to exit.

Surfing is a skill; building is a legacy. Pick the one wave worth riding all the way in, and put your name on what it carries to shore.
