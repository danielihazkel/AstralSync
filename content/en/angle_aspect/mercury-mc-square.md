title: Mercury square Midheaven
---

Your mind and your public role pull at right angles, straining both ends of the axis — career and home alike. What fascinates you privately is not what your position asks you to talk about; the questions you raise at work read as off-script; family stories and old mental habits keep leaking into professional judgment. You can feel permanently slightly miscast: too curious for the job, too employed for the curiosity.

The productive face of this square is intellectual honesty under pressure. Because agreement never comes cheap, you interrogate the official line, and organizations eventually prize you as the one who asks what everyone avoided. The corrosive face is chronic second-guessing — rewriting the email five times, litigating your career in your head at midnight, sounding scattered exactly when the room wants a verdict.

Give the restless commentary a channel with borders: one notebook, one standing conversation — then let your public statements be short, few, and final.
