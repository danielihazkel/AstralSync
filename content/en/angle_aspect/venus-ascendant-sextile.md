title: Venus sextile Ascendant
---

You make good first weather. This sextile lends your presence an easy pleasantness: you read a room's temperature quickly, find the friendly thing to say, and leave encounters a little smoother than you found them. It is not dazzling charisma so much as reliable warmth — the colleague people want on the interview panel, the guest who makes the dinner work.

Because the ease is quiet, you can undervalue it and let it run unexamined. The risk is defaulting to pleasant everywhere: agreeable in meetings that needed a dissent, diplomatic with people who needed the truth, so consistently nice that your actual preferences blur into the wallpaper. Support that is never steered just maintains the status quo.

Use the goodwill you generate as capital, not camouflage — spend some of it, regularly, on saying the true thing that only a well-liked person could get away with.
