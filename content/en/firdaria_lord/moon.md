title: A Moon firdaria
---

For about nine years the Moon governs your life, and the chapter turns toward the body, the home, and the people you depend on — and who depend on you. Mothers, caregivers, the public, daily routines, and your emotional weather all carry more weight than usual. Night-born people begin life under the Moon; for day-born people it arrives as a later season of nesting and responsiveness.

The gift is adaptability and belonging. You read moods and needs with unusual accuracy, and life rewards you for meeting people where they are. The friction is fluctuation: plans shift with the tide, and it is easy to let other people's needs set your course until you lose track of your own.

Your natal Moon's sign, house, and phase describe how gently or turbulently this chapter moves — a dignified Moon steadies it, a pressured one asks for more conscious care. Build one daily rhythm that nourishes you, and protect it as carefully as you protect others.
