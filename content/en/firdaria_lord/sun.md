title: A Sun firdaria
---

For about ten years the Sun governs your life, and the chapter asks one question again and again: what are you here to stand for? Purpose, visibility, reputation, and your relationship to authority — including fathers, bosses, and your own inner sense of command — move to the centre. Day-born people meet this period at the very start of life; night-born people reach it later, when the question of public identity lands with more weight.

The gift is clarity and presence. You become easier to see and easier to follow, and efforts made with your whole name behind them tend to count. The friction is pride: a solar chapter can make you rigid about being right, or leave you depending on recognition that others control.

Your natal Sun's sign, house, and dignity colour all of this — a well-placed Sun shines steadily, a strained one has to earn its light. Choose one role you are willing to be publicly accountable for, and grow into it on purpose.
