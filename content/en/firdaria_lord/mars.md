title: A Mars firdaria
---

For about seven years Mars governs your life, and the chapter runs hotter than the ones around it. Ambition, competition, courage, and the will to cut through obstacles come forward. You are more likely to start things, fight for things, and leave situations that have stopped serving you. Physical energy, risk, and conflict all feature.

The gift is drive. Problems that once looked permanent become things you can actually tackle, and you discover how much you can do when you stop waiting for permission. The friction is heat without direction — accidents, quarrels, burnout, or battles picked because they were available rather than worth winning.

Your natal Mars's sign, house, and dignity describe how cleanly this fire burns — a well-placed Mars makes a capable warrior, a strained one needs firmer channels. Give this chapter a worthy fight: one concrete goal hard enough to absorb your energy, so it does not spill into the people closest to you.
