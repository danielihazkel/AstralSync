title: A Mercury firdaria
---

For about thirteen years — the longest of the planetary chapters — Mercury governs your life. Learning, trade, writing, skill, and the busy traffic of messages and errands come forward. Siblings, colleagues, students, and teachers tend to matter more, and your livelihood often runs through what you know how to do or explain.

The gift is versatility. You pick things up quickly, make useful connections, and can turn information into income or influence. The friction is scatter: so many open threads that none of them gets finished, and a restless mind that mistakes motion for progress. Nervous strain and second-guessing are the usual costs.

Mercury takes on the colour of whatever it touches, so its natal sign, house, dignity, and aspects matter even more than usual here — they show whether this chapter sharpens you into a craftsperson or keeps you running. Pick one skill to master across the period, and let the rest be supporting work.
