title: A South Node firdaria
---

For two years the South Node governs your life — a brief, transitional chapter without sub-periods, closing one era before the next begins. Its tone is subtraction. Things that have run their course tend to fall away: roles, habits, attachments, sometimes plans you thought were settled.

The gift is release. You can let go of what is heavy with less struggle than usual, and old skills or wisdom you had stopped noticing come quietly back into use. The friction is that loss rarely feels like a gift while it happens; you may feel diminished, nostalgic, or unsure what remains once the clutter is gone.

The sign and house of your natal South Node, and any planets close to it, show where the clearing tends to happen. Work with the tide rather than against it: finish what is nearly done, give away what you no longer use, and let the space stay empty long enough to see what wants to fill it.
