title: A North Node firdaria
---

For three years the North Node governs your life — a short, transitional chapter without sub-periods, wedged between the planetary eras. Its tone is increase. New people, ambitions, and directions tend to arrive, often faster than you can fully explain them, and life seems to lean you toward something you have not done before.

The gift is momentum toward the unfamiliar. Doors open that were not there earlier, and appetite grows for experience you do not yet have the skill for. The friction is that growth can be indiscriminate: the North Node amplifies whatever it touches, so overreach, restless hunger, and taking on too much are common side effects.

The sign and house of your natal North Node, and any planets close to it, show where this growth tends to concentrate. Treat these three years as a doorway rather than a destination — say yes to what genuinely stretches you, and keep a short list of what you are deliberately leaving outside.
